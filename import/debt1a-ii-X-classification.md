# Debt 1, Chunk A-ii: The classification of X

*Working note. Status: the three-way classification (healthy / auxiliary / ghost) is resolved into a sharper statement: X is forced to be a massive spin-2 field, with exactly one known consistent structure available, and the massless option excluded by theorem. One tuning question remains, now precisely posed.*

## 1. The shift symmetry [D — rigorous]

The Möbius action of the rigid Sp(2d,ℝ) contains the subgroup $\begin{pmatrix}1 & B\\ 0 & 1\end{pmatrix}$, B symmetric, acting as

$$
Z \to Z + B: \qquad X \to X + B, \quad Y \to Y.
$$

Constant shifts of X are **exact symmetries** of any S_H built from (ω, H). Consequences: X has no potential and no non-derivative couplings from the geometric sector alone — it is axion-like — and "auxiliary X" (the soft outcome 2) is off the table at the fundamental level: X appears only through derivatives, so it propagates or it doesn't exist.

## 2. The gauge structure and the no-go [D + T]

The local version of these shifts sits inside the ω-generalized diffeomorphisms: dual-diffeomorphism parameters λ_μ(x) act as δX_μν = ∂_(μ λ_ν) — because X is **symmetric**, this is a *second copy of linearized diffeomorphisms*, making X a second graviton-like gauge field. (Contrast DFT: there the doubled partner is the antisymmetric B-field, δB = dλ, a 2-form gauge theory — spin-wise harmless. The symmetry type of the rigid structure — η antisymmetric-pairing vs ω — dictates the symmetry type of the partner field. **This is the structural reason string theory's η-doubling exists and naive ω-doubling is hard: the symplectic partner of the graviton is another graviton.**)

Two consistency facts then close the door on the massless option:

- Without the gauge invariance, no ghost-free two-derivative kinetic term for a Lorentz-covariant symmetric tensor exists (mixed time–space components enter with the wrong sign under any invariant contraction; this is why the Fierz–Pauli structure and its accidental gauge symmetry exist at all). [T — standard]
- With the gauge invariance, X is a second **massless** spin-2 — and massless multi-graviton cross-interactions are inconsistent (Boulanger–Damour–Gualtieri–Henneaux). X must couple (to g through the Siegel structure, to χ through the gate), so decoupling is not available. [T]

**Therefore X must be massive.**

## 3. The mass mechanism and the endpoint [D — structural]

The mass cannot be written by hand (shift symmetry) — but it doesn't need to be. The vacuum ⟨Z⟩ = i·g sits at X = 0 and is *moved* by the shifts: the polarization selection (η-emergence) **spontaneously breaks the local dual diffeomorphisms**. Gravitational Higgs mechanism: the four would-be Goldstones π_μ of the broken λ-gauge are eaten; X becomes a **massive spin-2** with its five polarizations, mass M_X = the polarization-breaking scale. (The Λ(d) moduli, τ included, are not the eaten modes — they carry the compact phase directions, which constant shifts do not generate; verifying τ survives un-eaten is flagged [O].)

Massive spin-2 coupled to gravity has exactly one known consistent structure: the Fierz–Pauli tuning at quadratic order completed by a dRGT/Hassan–Rosen-class potential [T: HR 2011]. So the classification lands here:

> **X is a massive spin-2 field. The geometric sector is healthy iff the potential generated by the η-selection lands in the Hassan–Rosen class; a generic potential reintroduces the Boulware–Deser ghost.**

Restated as a selling line: **BBT's geometric sector is bimetric gravity with the second metric's kinetic structure and couplings dictated by modular (Siegel-domain) symmetry rather than chosen by hand** — *if* the dRGT tuning emerges from the symmetry. That conditional is now the entirety of Debt 1's remaining content: a single, well-posed tuning computation (expand the η-selection sector — the J–Z coupling — and compare its X-potential against the FP/dRGT coefficients). Full circle, incidentally, to the second turn of this whole conversation: bimetric gravity was the right neighborhood from the start; the modular structure explains *which* bimetric.

A signature caveat recorded honestly: the compatibility theorem and Siegel parametrization were derived for positive H. Lorentzian g makes the stabilizer U(1, d−1) and the invariant target metric indefinite — which is precisely the regime where the gauge+mass structure above is not a luxury but the only path to health. The chain of §1–§3 is the Euclidean-rigorous skeleton; the Lorentzian dressing rides on the same FP/HR machinery it lands on. [Flagged, not glossed.]

## 4. Consequences downstream [D — structural, with one honest downgrade]

**Gate (Debt 2).** Integrating out massive X between χ bilinears gives the contact four-χ operator. The relative-momentum dependence enters through the χ quanta's **intrinsic momentum-space extent**: p-polarized quanta are extended objects on the momentum fiber with width μ, and they exchange X only over their fiber overlap — the dual of how x-extended composites acquire form factors F(qR). Wavepacket overlap of Gaussian profiles gives exactly $e^{-(p_1-p_2)^2/2\mu^2}$ — the postulated gate, with **μ = the χ quantum's own p-width**, a physical identification the EFT lacked. *However*: tree-level exchange with generic vertices yields rational (power-law-tailed) gates; the Gaussian requires the Gaussian fiber profile. Honest downgrade to the flagship: **row 1's "exponential cliff" weakens to "sharp knee at v_*, with the tail shape (Gaussian vs rational) a diagnostic of the vertex completion."** The knee's existence and location are robust; its steepness is model-internal information, not a guaranteed exponential.

**Fifth forces.** Massive X ⇒ short-ranged, safe; couplings to x-polarized (SM) matter run through the SM's dual stress, which is unenhanced. [Consistency preserved; quantify with the propagator later.]

**A new door, deliberately left closed today:** homogeneous oscillations of a massive spin-2 X are themselves a dark-matter candidate (cf. bigravity dark matter). BBT may not need χ as a separate field — X-condensate dark matter would unify the geometric sector with the dark sector. Logged as an open direction, not pursued. [O]
