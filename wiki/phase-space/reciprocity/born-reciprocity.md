*Born reciprocity is the map ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x), which exchanges position and momentum, with one sign change, as a quarter-turn of phase space. The rules of quantum kinematics are unchanged by it, and the [Fourier transform](fourier-transform.md) carries it out on wavefunctions. Max Born proposed in 1938 and 1949 that a fundamental theory should respect it. In 1938 he gave momentum space a radius of its own, as Einstein's closed universe has a radius in position, and fixed it at a particle scale; in 1949 he split ℏ into a length and a momentum. The program's paper [#13](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md) describes his hope as a physics with a fundamental mass and a [fundamental length](born-length.md#borns-fundamental-length) on equal footing. Observed dynamics does not respect it — interactions are local in position — and the program's starting assumption, [its hard core](../../program/hard-core.md), is that ρ is an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum breaks.*

## The map

On the coordinates (x, p) of phase space, ρ is the matrix

```text
ρ = [[0, 1], [−1, 0]]          ρ(x, p) = (p, −x)
ρ² = −1:  (x, p) → (−x, −p)    ρ⁴ = 1
```

It turns the phase plane by a quarter-turn: the new position is the old momentum, and the new momentum is the old position with its sign reversed. Two quarter-turns give the parity ρ², which reverses both variables ([#1 §2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core)); four give the identity. So ρ generates a cyclic group of order four, which the [lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) calls "the ℤ₄ generated by the complex structure I" ([#1 §1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#1-postulates), P3). With d positions and d momenta the same matrix acts in d × d blocks ([#1 §2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core)).

### Why the sign

A plain exchange (x, p) → (p, x) reverses the sign of the symplectic form (in the phase plane of one position and one momentum, it reverses the orientation) and turns [x, p] = iℏ into −iℏ [T]. The minus sign repairs this. ρ "is symplectic and squares to −1" ([toy-model note](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md) [§1](../../papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#1-setting-and-prior-work)): it preserves the [symplectic form](symplectic-form.md) ω, and with it [the canonical commutator](canonical-commutator.md). Among the linear maps that preserve ω it is the quarter-turn of the compact circle of rotations. The [popular article](../../history/documents/popular-articles.md) [#12](../../../popsci/universes-other-half.md) says the same in words: exchange position for momentum throughout "(with one sign adjustment) and the formalism is unchanged" ("Two descriptions of one thing"; never a status source).

## A symmetry of kinematics

[#11 §3](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question): "Quantum kinematics is exactly invariant under ρ: (x,p) → (p,−x); the Fourier transform implements it unitarily [1†, 2†]." The sentence holds on ℝ^d, which #11's flat cosmology assumes; on a closed space, such as a three-sphere or a torus, position keeps a continuous spectrum while momentum becomes discrete, so no unitary map exchanges them as wholes, and the flat transform exchanges a wave packet of width σ only locally, to relative accuracy (2/3)(σ/R)² for curvature radius R (flag 47 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md); [reciprocity on a closed space](closed-space-reciprocity.md)). [The v1 paper](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) adds that "the canonical commutator is blind to it" ([#9 §1](../../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md#1-the-question)). The transform F conjugates x̂ into p̂ and p̂ into −x̂ [T], which is ρ written for operators; see [the Fourier transform as a phase-space rotation](fourier-transform.md).

"Kinematics" here means the arena: the operators, their commutation relations and the space of states. It leaves out the Hamiltonian, which says how states change in time. A symmetry of the arena alone is, ordinarily, a change of description, the same physics in rotated variables, rather than a symmetry of a specific action. A withdrawn paper on monopole dark matter states the point: "as usually encountered, rho is a unitary equivalence between representations of the canonical commutation relations, not a Noether symmetry of a specified action" ([its §1](../../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md#1-framework)). See [unitary equivalence versus Noether symmetry](unitary-equivalence.md).

### What breaks it in observed physics

The laws of motion single out position. The toy-model note's abstract opens on this: ρ "is an exact symmetry of quantum kinematics but not of observed dynamics, which is local in position". The v1 paper lists the asymmetries: "Hamiltonians are p²/2m + V(x), interactions are local in x, the mass shell is a momentum-space structure with no positional twin, and general relativity curves x-space while leaving momentum fibers flat" ([#9 §1](../../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md#1-the-question)).

The first item shows the asymmetry plainly. A Hamiltonian p²/2m + V(x) is quadratic in momentum and arbitrary in position; under ρ it becomes x²/2m + V(p), a different law [T]. Born's question, why physics uses only half of the symmetry its kinematics has, is on [Born's question](../../program/borns-question.md); the flat momentum fibers are discussed on [momentum fibers and the cotangent bundle](momentum-fibers.md) and [curved momentum space](curved-momentum-space.md).

## Born's self-dual program

#13 records Born's motive: "This was Born's original hope in 1938: a physics with a fundamental mass and a fundamental length on equal footing" ([§4](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md#4-invariants-the-mass-shell-and-the-length-shell)). What ρ does to relativistic invariants shows why the two scales go together. The invariant of [special relativity](../../conjugate-relativity/special-relativity.md) is the mass shell, p_μp^μ = m²c². Its Born image is a hyperboloid in position space:

```text
x_μ x^μ = λ_χ²
```

#13 calls it an invariant-length shell: "conjugate quanta are characterized by an irreducible extent λ_χ as ours are by mass [D — structural; this recovers Born's 1938–49 program of a self-dual physics with both m and λ fundamental [3†, 4†]]" (§4). #13 lists "Born's self-dual program [3†, 4†]" among its literature tethers ([§7](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md#7-summary-of-tethers)). See [the invariant-length shell and Planck closure](../../conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell.md).

In its relativistic form the map exchanges all four coordinates with all four momenta, time with energy included [T]. #13 works in a "3+1 presentation" that leaves time alone and lists "the fully covariant construction with the four-dimensional Born map (t ↔ E)" as open [O] ([§6](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md#6-what-is-shared-what-is-broken-what-is-open)); see [Open: the four-dimensional Born map](../../conjugate-relativity/four-dimensional-born-map.md).

### What Born wrote

The phase-space-diameter note quotes the two papers as follows ([§4](../../papers/phase-space-diameter.md#4-extent-and-resolution-and-the-published-answers), [T][S]):

- **1938, "A suggestion for unifying quantum theory and relativity".** Born notes that Einstein's static closed universe has "an upper limit for the distance between two points, given by the radius a of the universe", and writes: "Let us transfer this consideration to the p-space." Momentum space receives Einstein-type equations with constants of its own, and "for systems of some kind there is an upper limit for momenta, determined by the radius b of the hypersphere"; the number of states in a volume V is then finite. He declines to connect the two radii: "Whether these nuclear constants are connected with the corresponding molar constants λ, κ cannot be decided yet." (In the wiki's reading, λ and κ are here the constants of Einstein's equations for position space, not the [Born length](born-length.md).) He fixes b from the classical electron radius r₀ by r₀ = h/(πb), so that b c = 2ℏc/r₀ ≈ 1.4 × 10⁸ eV [V] ([the phase-space-diameter script](../../method/verification/phase-space-diameter-script.md)), a particle scale, and leaves open "whether the constant b is universal, or characteristic for each energetically closed system". He opens by rejecting Eddington's attempt "to link the properties of the smallest particles to those of the whole universe".
- **1949, "Reciprocity theory of elementary particles".** Born splits ℏ = ab into a length and a momentum and fixes the length from particle masses.

"Neither paper uses the Planck length" (§4). The note connects the papers to the rest of its argument in three places:

- Born's split ℏ = ab, a length times a momentum, is the choice of units that the map with a Born length needs, x′ = (λ²/ℏ)p, p′ = −(ℏ/λ²)x (§5 [T][V]); "Born's own reciprocity length was a particle scale" (§5), whereas #13 sets the Born length equal to the Planck length.
- Born's open question whether b is universal or set by each system is the choice that Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman, Leigh and Minic later make for the system at hand, taking their cell length to be "not universal but instead … chosen in the context of the system in question" (§4).
- Pairing the size of the universe with an extent in momentum is the question the note itself takes up; for the universe's radiation the two sizes are tied by a fixed product, and for its range of scales one Born length, about 50 micrometres, makes the box exactly self-dual ([the phase-space diameter relation](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md); [the self-dual cosmic box](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-cosmic-box.md)).

The closed-universe note of 2026-10-27 returns to Born 1938 from the other side. Born started from a closed universe and gave momentum space a hypersphere to match; the note asks whether reciprocity makes the universe closed, finds that no source derives closure from Born reciprocity, and finds that a closed position space times a closed momentum space cannot be a phase space at all, though a compact phase space that is not a product, Gr̃(2,5), exists ([`notes/closed-universe.md` §2](../../../notes/closed-universe.md#2-the-premise-and-the-turnaround-it-buys), [§5](../../../notes/closed-universe.md#5-reciprocity-on-a-closed-space); [a closed Comatter universe](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md); [reciprocity on a closed space](closed-space-reciprocity.md)).

#13's description of Born's motive, "a physics with a fundamental mass and a fundamental length on equal footing", and its "self-dual physics with both m and λ fundamental" are #13's words, cited from model memory; the note does not compare them with Born's text, and the comparison belongs to the † literature battery.

## What the program does with it

- **The hard core.** "The program: ρ is exact and spontaneously broken." ([#11 §3](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question); [the hard core](../../program/hard-core.md)). The program argues that the breaking is unavoidable ([mandatory breaking](../../symmetry-breaking/mandatory-breaking.md)); the idea that a preferred [polarization](lagrangian-polarizations.md) breaks the symmetry is credited to Freidel, Leigh and Minic ([broken Born duality](broken-born-duality.md)).
- **The postulate.** "**P3** The action is invariant under the Born rotation generated by I = ω⁻¹H. [P]" ([#11 §4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates); [P3](../../program/postulates/p3-reciprocity.md)).
- **On the three structures.** In the standard frame, "R preserves ω (it is symplectic), preserves H (it is orthogonal), and **flips the sign of η**, hence flips J and K while preserving I" [D] ([#1 §2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core), where R is the matrix of ρ; [the structures ω, η, H](../born-geometry/born-structures.md)). Since η is the program's order parameter, [the calc note](../../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md) reads the flip as "the broken symmetry moving the order parameter" ([#5 §B](../../../notes/geometric-core.md#b-the-inconsistency-and-the-forced-repair-d--structural); [the order parameter](../../symmetry-breaking/order-parameter.md)).
- **On the modular field.** "ρ: Z → −Z⁻¹ (Möbius; d = 1 check), fixed point Z = i" [D] ([#11 §6](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field); [reciprocity as modular inversion](../modular-field/modular-inversion.md)). On Gaussian wave packets the Fourier transform acts on the packet's shape parameter by the same map, with Z = i the one packet it leaves unchanged [T][V] ([phase-space-diameter note §5](../../papers/phase-space-diameter.md#5-what-reciprocity-adds-invariant-area-inverted-shape); [Gaussian wave packets on the Siegel half-plane](../modular-field/gaussian-states.md)).
- **On a phase-space rectangle.** With a Born length λ, ρ keeps a rectangle's area and inverts its aspect ratio, and every rectangle is self-dual for exactly one λ [T][V] (note §5; [the Born length λ](born-length.md)). The note applies this to the universe's radiation and to the box running from the Planck length to the horizon ([the phase-space diameter relation](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md); [the self-dual cosmic box](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-cosmic-box.md)).
- **In the toy models.** In [model I](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/_index.md), the [coset sigma model](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/sigma-models.md) of a metric H and a real structure K, "ρ acts as Z → −1/Z and K(φ) → K(φ + π) = −K(φ). The stabilizer of (1, K₀) in Sp(2,ℝ) is {±1}" ([toy-model note §2](../../papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#2-model-i-a-coset-sigma-model-of-h-k)). But "the twisted element ρ∘(K → −K) maps (1, K₀) to itself", so breaking ρ is physical only once a coupling odd in K is present ([the twisted element](../../symmetry-breaking/twisted-element.md)). In model I and in the complex form of model II, ρ sits inside a continuous U(1), and the polarization is a Goldstone angle [D][V] ([#11 §4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates), [§10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); [the polarization as a Goldstone angle](../../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle.md); checked by [the toy-model scripts](../../method/verification/toy-model-scripts.md)); the real form of model II has a discrete pair of vacua instead ([toy-model note §3](../../papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#3-model-ii-the-self-dual-grossewulkenhaar-model)). The lab notebook's older breaking pattern, ℤ₄ → ℤ₂ = {1, ρ²}, is superseded by that picture ([superseded statements](../../status/superseded.md)).
- **In charge space.** [S-duality](../../electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality.md) acts on the complexified coupling as τ_c → −1/τ_c, "generated by the same [[0,1],[−1,0]] rotation as Born's ρ and our Z → −Z⁻¹: the third appearance of one matrix in this program" ([#11 §11.3](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#113-the-identification-d--structural-math-t); [the symplectic charge lattice](../../electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice.md); flag 67 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending).
