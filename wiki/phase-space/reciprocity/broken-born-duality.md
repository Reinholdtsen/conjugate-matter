*Broken Born duality is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's phrase for a phase space in which all physical probes agree on which directions are space-time: the agreement is a preferred polarization, and it breaks the position–momentum symmetry they call Born duality. In their metastring work they made the neutral metric η dynamical and described space-time as a choice of Lagrangian subspace, but they wrote no potential for η and treated the breaking as kinematical, not as spontaneous symmetry breaking. The program's central idea therefore has a literature precedent. Since version 2.2 the program's checkpoint credits them, and claims as its own only the reading of the breaking as spontaneous, with η as the order parameter.*

**Status:** Established — literature, live-searched citations [S] (#11 ref. [26]; the worklog's prior-art search) — the credit was added to #11 §3 in version 2.2 (2026-10-20).

## Key facts

- **The statement.** #11 §3 quotes them: "if all probes agree on what we mean by space-time, then we have broken Born duality — there is a preferred choice of polarization, the space-time one" [26S, p. 3]. "They treat the breaking as kinematical and call the choice 'a dynamical question'."
- **What they did and did not do.** The worklog's prior-art search (chunk 1, every citation retrieved [S]): in arXiv:1307.7080 "both elements (η, J) of the chiral structure are dynamical. The solutions are labelled by bi-Lagrangians and spacetime is a derived dynamical concept"; arXiv:1405.3949 "ties fixed η to locality". The worklog's verdict: "They have priority on the idea and must be cited. They write no potential and do no symmetry-breaking analysis, and call Born's breaking kinematical, not spontaneous."
- **The program's addition.** #11 §3: "This program's addition is to read it as *spontaneous* breaking, with η as the order parameter". #11 P2′: "That η should be dynamical rather than fixed was argued independently in metastring theory [26S]; the reading of η as the order parameter of broken reciprocity is this program's." See [the hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core) and [the order parameter](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter).
- **No earlier explicit model.** The same search found "no existing action has Born reciprocity exactly symmetric and spontaneously broken with η or K as order parameter", with the caveat that the search coverage "is incomplete, so this is evidence of absence, not proof" (worklog, chunk 1). The toy models were built to supply one ([toy models of broken reciprocity](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models)).
- **Neighbors.** Toy-model note §1: Freidel, Rudolph and Svoboda suggest the choice "might be subject to a dynamical selection rule"; Born sigma models (Marotta–Szabo) choose the polarization by gauging; "Smolin breaks a triality down to Born duality, which stays unbroken". A mechanism that picks one section over its partner remains open in the literature ([double field theory](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory)).
- **References.** #11 [26][S] L. Freidel, R. G. Leigh, D. Minic, "Metastring theory and modular space-time," JHEP 06 (2015) 006, arXiv:1502.08005; the toy-model note's [1a–c] adds arXiv:1307.7080 and arXiv:1405.3949. The same authors are credited for [the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) since #11 version 2.1.
- **History.** The popular article #10 describes Born's question as having "spent eighty-eight years in a drawer"; it predates both credits.
