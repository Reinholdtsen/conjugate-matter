*The field content of the broken vacuum is the list of fields the program's geometry leaves in play once the vacuum carries a definite polarization: the ordinary metric, the momentum graviton X, the ten moduli of [the Lagrangian Grassmannian](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian) (the dark-energy field τ plus nine more), and matter. The Debt-1 notes settled what X is — a massive spin-2 field. Whether the nine extra moduli survive, or are removed by the constraints that usually eliminate redundant Goldstone modes (inverse-Higgs constraints), was never worked out. The question is open, and it is not on the checkpoint's numbered list of open problems.*

**Status:** Open [O] — #5 §D and §F item 4; not carried into #11 §14 — the X part was resolved by #6 (a massive spin-2 field); the count of surviving moduli has not been computed ([open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems)).

## Key facts

- **The list [partial].** #5 §D, "Field content on the broken vacuum":

  ```text
  h_μν (10, graviton) ⊕ X_μν (10, momentum graviton) ⊕ Λ(d) σ-modes (10: τ plus nine) ⊕ matter
  ```

  with the nine "of which inverse-Higgs constraints likely eliminate most [O]". X is the real part of [the modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z).
- **X, resolved.** #5 §D posed a three-way classification of X (healthy, auxiliary or ghost); #6 replaced it: the four would-be Goldstones of the broken dual diffeomorphisms "are eaten", and X "becomes a **massive spin-2** with its five polarizations" (#6 §3). See [why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain) and [the gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs).
- **The nine heavy moduli, open [O].** #5 §F item 4: "inverse-Higgs accounting of the nine heavy moduli". The item does not appear in #11 §14 or in the handoff's open list; this wiki keeps it open.
- **A dependent sketch, unreconciled.** #1 §8's polarization-genesis sketch needs the nine moduli to exist and decay: "at reheating, the heavy Grassmannian moduli (the nine non-Maslov directions) decay". #5 expects inverse-Higgs constraints to eliminate most of them. No document reconciles the two. See [polarization-genesis and the 5.4 : 1 ratio](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis).
- **τ's place.** That τ is the light direction, "with the other nine directions heavy, is assumed [P]" (#1 §2) — see [τ as the Maslov phase](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase).
- **Signs of health.** #5 §D: "the Siegel target metric Tr(Y⁻¹dZ Y⁻¹dZ̄) is positive-definite", and "the DFT precedent (graviton + B-field + dilaton, ghost-free) shows doubled-geometry kinetic operators can be healthy [T]" — see [the Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain) and [double field theory](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory).
