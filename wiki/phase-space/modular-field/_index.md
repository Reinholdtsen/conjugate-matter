*The modular field is the program's name for a single complex matrix field, Z = X + iY, that describes every phase-space metric compatible with the fixed symplectic form ω. Those metrics form a classical space of mathematics, the Siegel domain, and the symplectic group acts on it by fractional-linear maps. The program reads the imaginary part Y as the metric of spacetime and the real part X as a new field, the local tilt of the split between position and momentum; reciprocity acts on Z as Z → −Z⁻¹, and the dark-energy field τ is a phase on the domain's boundary. The mathematics is standard, and the physical readings are the program's derived claims.*

**Stands:** the program's claims [the modular field Z](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z), [reciprocity as modular inversion](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion) and [τ as the Maslov phase](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase) [D]; the standard mathematics of [the Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain) and [the Lagrangian Grassmannian](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian) · **Died:** nothing yet · **Open:** [the field content of the broken vacuum](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content)

## Map

[The Siegel domain Sp(2d, ℝ)/U(d)](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain) comes first: the space itself, its parametrization H_Z by a complex symmetric matrix Z, and battery test T2. It is standard mathematics, with machine-checked identities.

[The modular field Z = X + iY](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z) is the program's reading of that space as a field, alive and derived [D]: Im Z = g is the metric, X = 0 is the vacuum slice where the compatibility theorem's solutions sit, and Re Z = X is the momentum graviton.

[Reciprocity as modular inversion Z → −Z⁻¹](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion) shows how the founding symmetry acts on the field, with the self-dual vacuum Z = i and the circle |Z| = 1 that the cosmology uses. It is derived [D] and machine-checked for one coordinate.

[The Lagrangian Grassmannian Λ(d)](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian) returns to standard mathematics: the compact space of all position/momentum splittings, of dimension 10 at d = 4, which is also the boundary of the Siegel domain. It comes before τ because τ is defined on it.

[τ as the Maslov phase of the boundary](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase) identifies the dark-energy field with the winding angle of that boundary: the identification is derived [D], the topology is literature [T], and τ's lightness is a postulate [P].

[Open: field content of the broken vacuum](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content) closes the section with what is not settled: the momentum graviton has been classified, but whether the nine heavy directions of Λ(d) survive has not been worked out.
