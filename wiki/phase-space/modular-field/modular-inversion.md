*Modular inversion is the map Z → −Z⁻¹, "take minus the reciprocal", applied to [the modular field Z](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z). The program shows that [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity), the quarter-turn exchanging position and momentum, acts on Z in exactly this way. Its fixed point Z = i, a value of the field that the exchange leaves unchanged, is the program's self-dual vacuum; on the vacuum slice the map sends the metric g to its inverse, and the circle |Z| = 1 is the program's relation a·ã = 1 between our scale factor and that of the conjugate description. The claim is derived; it is shown explicitly for one coordinate (d = 1), where a script checks it.*

**Status:** Alive — derived [D] (#11 §6) — the d = 1 case is machine-checked in `verify/toy_coset.py` (see [the toy-model scripts](/wiki/method/verification/toy-model-scripts)); general d is not shown explicitly.

## Key facts

- **The claim.** #11 §6: "**ρ: Z → −Z⁻¹** (Möbius; d = 1 check), fixed point Z = i; at X = 0 it is scale-factor duality g → g⁻¹, and the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1 (§10) is the |Z| = 1 locus."
- **The derivation.** #5 §C: "Born reciprocity acts as Z → −Z⁻¹ (Möbius action of the I-rotation; d = 1 check: matrix [[0,1],[−1,0]] gives Z → −1/Z). Fixed point Z = i: the self-dual vacuum of the earlier turns, now as modular geometry." The Möbius action is that of [the Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain).
- **The machine check (d = 1).** `verify/toy_coset.py` prints `PASS  rho acts on Z as Z -> -1/Z with fixed point Z = i`. In model I, "ρ acts as Z → −1/Z and K(φ) → K(φ + π) = −K(φ)" (toy-model note §2; [model I vacua](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/vacua)).
- **Cosmology.** "The cosmological constraint a·ã = 1 is the |Z| = 1 self-dual circle — the minisuperspace duality was the shadow of this modular action" (#5 §C). #11 §10 derives the constraint from ω-rigidity: "a·ã = 1 (ℏ does not run) — the |Z| = 1 self-dual locus" [D]. See [the cosmological constraint](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) and [scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality).
- **The same matrix in charge space.** #11 §11.3: "S-duality acts on the complexified coupling by τ_c → −1/τ_c, generated by the same [[0,1],[−1,0]] rotation as Born's ρ and our Z → −Z⁻¹: the third appearance of one matrix in this program." Whether the two polarizations belong to one modular structure is open — see [one modular structure for both polarizations](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure) and [S-duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality).
