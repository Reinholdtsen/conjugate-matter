*Phase space is the space of positions and momenta taken together, and this section collects its geometry: the symmetry that exchanges the two, the structures that can be laid on phase space, and the single field the program builds from them. Quantum mechanics treats a state's position description and its momentum description as equally complete, related as a waveform is to its spectrum. The program starts from that fact, uses a theorem about which phase-space geometries are compatible with it, and arrives at one complex field whose parts it reads as the metric of spacetime, a second gravitational field and the dark-energy field.*

**Stands:** the established physics of [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity); [the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) (literature, with the program's block proof and machine check) and [its corollaries](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries); the program's claims [the modular field Z](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z), [reciprocity as modular inversion](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion) and [τ as the Maslov phase](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase) · **Died:** nothing · **Open:** [Lorentzian dressing of the Euclidean skeleton](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing), [the field content of the broken vacuum](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content); folded sections on [connection uniqueness](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection#open-connection-uniqueness-under-p2) under [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime) (on the Born connection), [a mechanism that selects one section](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory#open-a-mechanism-that-selects-one-section) (on double field theory), [the Re Z sector of the geometric action (OP1)](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z#open-the-re-z-sector-of-the-geometric-action-op1) (on the modular field Z) and [extensivity, the soccer-ball problem](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/curved-momentum-space#open-extensivity-the-soccer-ball-problem) (on curved momentum space) · **Superseded:** a folded section on [the three-way classification of X](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content#superseded-the-three-way-classification-of-x) · **Conjectures:** chat-only, folded: [the slaved layer is algebraic](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries#chat-only-the-slaved-layer-is-algebraic), [massive X waves](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content#chat-only-massive-x-waves)

## Map

[Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity) comes first because everything else rests on it. All of it is established physics: [the canonical commutator and the uncertainty principle](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/canonical-commutator), [the symplectic form and its group](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form), [the Fourier transform](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/fourier-transform) that carries out [the quarter-turn ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x)](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity), [the difference between a change of description and a symmetry of an action](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/unitary-equivalence), [Lagrangian polarizations](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations), [the Wigner function](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/wigner-function), and [the literature on curved momentum space](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/curved-momentum-space) that asks why momentum space should be flat. It ends with [the Freidel–Leigh–Minic precedent](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality), the earlier literature in which the program's central idea, a preferred polarization breaking the symmetry, already appears, and against which the program claims only the spontaneous reading.

[Born geometry](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry) puts [three structures](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures) on phase space — the symplectic form ω, the neutral metric η and the generalized metric H — and asks which combinations are compatible. Its [compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) is a literature result with a block proof and machine check of the program's own: with ω and η both held rigid, momentum-space geometry is fixed as the inverse of position-space geometry. That left the program with no content of its own and forced its postulate change from [P2](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2) to [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime). The section closes with an open problem, because every result in it is proven only for a positive-definite (Euclidean) metric.

[The modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field) is what remains when only ω is rigid: the compatible metrics form [the Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain), coordinatized by one complex field Z = X + iY. The mathematics there (the Siegel domain, [the Lagrangian Grassmannian](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian)) is standard. The physical readings are the program's derived claims [D]: Im Z is the metric, Re Z is [the momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), ρ acts as Z → −Z⁻¹, and the boundary phase is the dark-energy field τ.

## Boundaries

- How the symmetry is spontaneously broken — the order parameter, the Goldstone angle, the potential, matter locality and the toy models — is [spontaneous breaking of reciprocity](/wiki/symmetry-breaking).
- The dynamics of the fields inside Z live elsewhere: X as a massive spin-2 field in [the momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), τ as quintessence in [dark energy](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy), and the |Z| = 1 locus in [scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality).
- The postulates written in this geometry are [Postulates P1–P5](/wiki/program/postulates).
- The kinematics of the momentum polarization (a second light cone, a maximum force) is [conjugate special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity); the same kind of polarization choice, made in charge space, is [electric–magnetic duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality).
