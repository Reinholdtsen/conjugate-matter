*Phase space is the space of positions and momenta taken together, and this section collects its geometry: the symmetry that exchanges the two, the structures that can be laid on phase space, and the single field the program builds from them. Quantum mechanics treats a state's position description and its momentum description as equally complete, related as a waveform is to its spectrum. The program starts from that fact, uses a theorem about which phase-space geometries are compatible with it, and arrives at one complex field whose parts it reads as the metric of spacetime, a second gravitational field and the dark-energy field.*

**Stands:** the established physics of [Born reciprocity](reciprocity/_index.md); [the compatibility theorem](born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md) (literature, with the program's block proof and machine check) and [its corollaries](born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries.md); the program's claims [the modular field Z](modular-field/modular-field-z.md), [reciprocity as modular inversion](modular-field/modular-inversion.md), [τ as the Maslov phase](modular-field/maslov-phase.md), from [#13](../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md) and unreviewed, [the Born length λ](reciprocity/born-length.md), and, computed and not reviewed, [reciprocity on a closed space](reciprocity/closed-space-reciprocity.md) · **Conjectures:** chat-only, folded: [the slaved layer is algebraic](born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries.md#chat-only-the-slaved-layer-is-algebraic), [massive X waves](modular-field/field-content.md#chat-only-massive-x-waves) · **Open:** [Lorentzian dressing of the Euclidean skeleton](born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing.md), [the field content of the broken vacuum](modular-field/field-content.md); folded sections on [connection uniqueness](born-geometry/born-connection.md#open-connection-uniqueness-under-p2) under [P2′](../program/postulates/p2-prime.md) (on the Born connection), [a mechanism that selects one section](born-geometry/double-field-theory.md#open-a-mechanism-that-selects-one-section) (on double field theory), [the Re Z sector of the geometric action (OP1)](modular-field/modular-field-z.md#open-the-re-z-sector-of-the-geometric-action-op1) (on the modular field Z) and [extensivity, the soccer-ball problem](reciprocity/curved-momentum-space.md#open-extensivity-the-soccer-ball-problem) (on curved momentum space) · **Superseded:** a folded section on [the three-way classification of X](modular-field/field-content.md#superseded-the-three-way-classification-of-x)

## Map

[Born reciprocity](reciprocity/_index.md) comes first because everything else rests on it. All of it but its last two pages is established physics: [the canonical commutator and the uncertainty principle](reciprocity/canonical-commutator.md), [the symplectic form and its group](reciprocity/symplectic-form.md), [the Fourier transform](reciprocity/fourier-transform.md) that carries out [the quarter-turn ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x)](reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md), [the difference between a change of description and a symmetry of an action](reciprocity/unitary-equivalence.md), [Lagrangian polarizations](reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations.md), [the Wigner function](reciprocity/wigner-function.md), [the momentum fibers](reciprocity/momentum-fibers.md) that phase space is made of, flat in general relativity, and [the literature on curved momentum space](reciprocity/curved-momentum-space.md) that asks why momentum space should be flat. The literature ends with [the Freidel–Leigh–Minic precedent](reciprocity/broken-born-duality.md), the earlier literature in which the program's central idea, a preferred polarization breaking the symmetry, already appears, and against which the program claims only the spontaneous reading. The subsection closes with two program claims: [the Born length λ](reciprocity/born-length.md), #13's name for the reference length that puts position and momentum on a common scale, which #13 sets equal to the Planck length, alive and unreviewed; and [reciprocity on a closed space](reciprocity/closed-space-reciprocity.md), what is left of the symmetry if space is closed, computed and not reviewed.

[Born geometry](born-geometry/_index.md) puts [three structures](born-geometry/born-structures.md) on phase space — the symplectic form ω, the neutral metric η and the generalized metric H — and asks which combinations are compatible; string theory's doubled geometry pairs the metric instead with an antisymmetric field, [the Kalb–Ramond B-field](born-geometry/kalb-ramond-field.md). Its [compatibility theorem](born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md) is a literature result with a block proof and machine check of the program's own: with ω and η both held rigid, momentum-space geometry is fixed as the inverse of position-space geometry. That left the program with no content of its own and forced its postulate change from [P2](../program/postulates/original-p2.md) to [P2′](../program/postulates/p2-prime.md). The section closes with an open problem, because every result in it is proven only for a positive-definite (Euclidean) metric.

[The modular field](modular-field/_index.md) is what remains when only ω is rigid: the compatible metrics form [the Siegel domain](modular-field/siegel-domain.md), coordinatized by one complex field Z = X + iY. The mathematics there (the Siegel domain, [the Lagrangian Grassmannian](modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian.md)) is standard. The physical readings are the program's derived claims [D]: Im Z is the metric, Re Z is [the momentum graviton](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), ρ acts as Z → −Z⁻¹, and the boundary phase is the dark-energy field τ.

- The dynamics of the fields inside Z live elsewhere: X as a massive spin-2 field in [the momentum graviton](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), τ as quintessence in [dark energy](../cosmology/dark-energy/_index.md), and the |Z| = 1 locus in [scale-factor duality](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/_index.md).
- The postulates written in this geometry are [Postulates P1–P5](../program/postulates/_index.md).
- The kinematics of the momentum polarization (a second light cone, a maximum force) is [conjugate special relativity](../conjugate-relativity/_index.md); the same kind of polarization choice, made in charge space, is [electric–magnetic duality](../electric-magnetic-duality/_index.md).
