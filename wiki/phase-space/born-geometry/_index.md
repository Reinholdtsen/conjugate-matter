*Born geometry is the study of three structures that can sit together on phase space: the symplectic form ω, which encodes the quantum commutator; a neutral metric η, whose null directions mark out which coordinates count as position and which as momentum; and a positive generalized metric H, which measures lengths in phase space. When the three are compatible they obey a split-quaternion algebra. The section's central result, the compatibility theorem, says that holding both ω and η fixed leaves only metrics in which momentum-space geometry is the exact inverse of position-space geometry — the result that forced the program to let η vary.*

## Map

[The structures ω, η, H and the split-quaternion algebra](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures) comes first because every later page uses its definitions: the operators I = ω⁻¹H, J = η⁻¹H and K = η⁻¹ω, Freidel, Leigh and Minic's name "Born geometry" for the compatible triple, and the program's derived result that ρ preserves ω and H but reverses η.

[Double field theory and the generalized metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory) is string theory's doubled geometry, built on η alone, with its B-field and its section condition. It is established physics and serves as the contrast case: what changes when ω is rigid as well.

[The Born connection](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection) is the unique connection compatible with all three structures, a literature theorem the program's first action used. Whether it stays unique when only ω and H are fundamental is an open sub-question on the same page.

[The compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) is the section's central result: with ω and η both rigid, the positive metrics compatible with both are exactly H = diag(g, g⁻¹). The statement is Freidel–Leigh–Minic's; the block proof, battery test T1 and the round-3 re-derivation are the program's record.

[Corollaries: unimodularity, no B-field, slaved momentum metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries) follows the theorem with its three consequences. The third, g̃ = g⁻¹, contradicted the program as it stood and forced the change from P2 to P2′.

[Open: Lorentzian dressing of the Euclidean skeleton](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing) comes last because it qualifies everything above: the results are proven for a positive-definite metric, and their extension to spacetime signature is #11's open problem 3.

## Boundaries

- The quantum kinematics these structures come from — the commutator, the symplectic form on its own, the Fourier transform and ρ itself — is [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity).
- The space of metrics left when only ω is rigid, and the fields on it, are [the modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field).
- The postulate change the theorem forced is recorded in [original P2](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2), [the nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem) and [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime).
- η as the order parameter of the breaking, with the unreconciled J/K naming, is [the order parameter](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter).
- Actions written from these structures are in [the tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test): [the geometric action S_H (superseded)](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/geometric-action) and [the frame tier](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier).
