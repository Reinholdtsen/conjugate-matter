*A connection is the rule for comparing directions at neighboring points of a space; it is what is needed to define curvature and to write field equations. Freidel, Rudolph and Svoboda showed that a Born geometry — phase space with the three compatible structures ω, η and H of [Born geometry](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures) — has exactly one connection compatible with all three, the Born connection, so its curvature R_B[H] is well defined. The program's first action for the phase-space metric was built on that curvature. Under the corrected postulate only ω and H are fundamental, and whether a unique compatible connection still exists then has not been shown.*

**Status:** Established — literature [T] (#1 §2), cited from model memory [7†] and not yet source-checked (the [† literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery)) — uniqueness for the (ω, H)-only structure is open [O] (#5 §F item 5) and is not among #11's numbered open problems.

## Key facts

- **The theorem [T].** #1 §2: "A theorem of Freidel, Rudolph & Svoboda (2018) establishes the existence and uniqueness of a connection compatible with the full triple (ω,η,H) — the *Born connection*." Its "curvature R_B[H] is therefore well-defined, and a diffeomorphism-style invariant action for H can be written." #11 §5: "a unique compatible connection exists [7†]".
- **The reference, and an unresolved date.** #11 [7]† L. Freidel, F. J. Rudolph, D. Svoboda, "A unique connection for Born geometry," Commun. Math. Phys. (2019), arXiv:1806.05992. #1 and #9 date the result 2018; the difference is unresolved, probably an arXiv year against a journal year, and is left to the † battery. The toy-model note's ref. [2] (arXiv:1706.07089) is a different paper by the same authors.
- **Open [O]: uniqueness for (ω, H) alone.** Under [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime) only ω and H are fundamental. #5 §F item 5 lists "connection uniqueness for the (ω, H)-only structure", noting that Freidel–Rudolph–Svoboda proved it for the full triple and that "the para-Hermitian literature — Svoboda, Marotta–Szabo — is the nearest neighbor." The item is not carried into #11 §14; this wiki keeps it open ([open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems)).
- **Where it is used.** The lab notebook's geometric action S_H was built from R_B[H] — see [the geometric action S_H (superseded)](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/geometric-action). In the tuning test, "J, E, and the Born connection share the first-order tier, so this is the geometrically minimal formulation" (#11 §7.2) — see [the frame tier](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier).
