*The program's main geometric results — [the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem), the parametrization of [the Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain), and the chain of arguments that forces the momentum graviton X to be massive — were proven for a positive-definite phase-space metric, the Euclidean case. Physical spacetime has Lorentzian signature, in which time enters with the opposite sign to space, and extending the results to it is open problem 3 of the checkpoint #11. The working notes expect the extension to rest on the same massive-gravity machinery the chain already uses, and list it as a check rather than a threat to the structure. Nothing has been computed.*

**Status:** Open [O] — #11 §14 item 3 — not computed; first flagged in #6 §3 and carried by #7, #8, #9, #13 and the handoff's open list ([open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems)).

## Key facts

- **The item.** #11 §14 item 3: "Lorentzian dressing of the Euclidean-rigorous skeleton [O]." #11 does not define the phrase; #6 §3 does: "The chain of §1–§3 is the Euclidean-rigorous skeleton" — the shift symmetry, the gauge structure with the multi-graviton no-go, and the mass mechanism of [why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain).
- **The caveat as first recorded.** #6 §3: "the compatibility theorem and Siegel parametrization were derived for positive H. Lorentzian g makes the stabilizer U(1, d−1) and the invariant target metric indefinite — which is precisely the regime where the gauge+mass structure above is not a luxury but the only path to health." The dressing is expected to ride on the same Fierz–Pauli and Hassan–Rosen machinery the chain lands on; #6 marks the point "[Flagged, not glossed.]" At d = 4 the stabilizer is U(1,3) (#9 §10 item 2).
- **How the notes rank it.** #7 §2 works "in the vacuum frame (g₀ = 1), Euclidean skeleton"; #7 §5 lists the dressing among remaining items that "are checks"; #8 §5 calls the Debt-1 residue "none of it existential". The handoff lists it as open item 5 (`HANDOFF.md` §2).
- **Where signature already enters.** The chain uses Lorentzian signature once: ungauged X is excluded "by Lorentz signature" (#11 §7.1).
- **What inherits it.** #13's preamble: "The Euclidean/Lorentzian caveat of the main checkpoint is inherited throughout" — its light cones are Lorentzian objects ([conjugate special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity); [two causal cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-causal-cones)).
