*S-duality is the proposed equivalence between a gauge theory at coupling g and a theory at coupling of order 1/g in which electrically charged particles and magnetic monopoles exchange roles, so that a strongly coupled theory has a weakly coupled description. Montonen and Olive proposed it in 1977; the checkpoint records it as exact in maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory, one of the few theories in which it has been tested, and Seiberg's related dualities in other supersymmetric theories supply the "magnetic quarks" of the dual description. In string theory S-duality sits with T-duality inside a larger U-duality. The program uses S-duality as a structural comparison, because it acts on the coupling by the same matrix as Born reciprocity, and it carries a standing caution: outside special supersymmetric theories the duality is an expectation, not an established symmetry.*

**Status:** Established — [T] — the checkpoint cites Montonen–Olive 1977 and Seiberg 1995 as [18†], from model memory, so the citations await [the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery). [The round-3 audit](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings) checked the citations in the withdrawn [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) of Montonen–Olive, Osborn 1979 and Harvey (hep-th/9603086) and found that they support the exactness caveat (Step 1).

## Key facts

- **The statement** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §11.2 [T]): "The conjecture that every electrically charged particle has a magnetic counterpart is Montonen–Olive duality [17†-adjacent; 18†], exact in maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills; "magnetic quarks" is standard vocabulary via Seiberg duality [18†]" ([the magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family)).
- **The references** (#11 [18]†): C. Montonen, D. Olive, "Magnetic monopoles as gauge particles?" Phys. Lett. B 72, 117 (1977); N. Seiberg, "Electric–magnetic duality in supersymmetric non-abelian gauge theories," Nucl. Phys. B 435, 129 (1995), hep-th/9411149.
- **Action on the coupling** (#11 §11.3): "S-duality acts on the complexified coupling by τ_c → −1/τ_c, generated by the same [[0,1],[−1,0]] rotation as Born's ρ and our Z → −Z⁻¹". τ_c is the complexified gauge coupling, not the dark-energy field τ ([notation](/wiki/notation)). The comparison is developed on [the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice); the phase-space side is [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity) and [reciprocity as modular inversion](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion).
- **The exactness burden** (`review/v3-worklog.md`, round-2 item R1): exact S-duality has been tested only in N = 4 super Yang–Mills theory and special finite N = 2 theories; for theories without supersymmetry there is "no reason to think" it survives quantum corrections, and "the bosonic spectrum mismatches (W spin 1, monopole spin 0). That is an expectation, not a no-go". The audit quotes Harvey: "put to non-trivial tests in finite N = 4 Super Yang-Mills theory and special finite N = 2 theories". The round-2 referee posed the burden as a choice: either reciprocity supplies the rigidity, or the dual sector is approximately supersymmetric (`HANDOFF.md` §6).
- **How #14 answered it** (#14 §2): "Exact electric-magnetic duality is also not assumed"; "the monopole physics used is semiclassical". Under the worklog's decision F2, "S-duality is no longer what fixes α_d" (`review/v3-worklog.md`, 'Findings neither referee raised'), and the earlier conjecture that reciprocity acts on the coupling as modular S is superseded ([Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)).
- **T-, S- and U-duality** (#11 §11.3 [T]): in string theory T- and S-duality are "cohabiting inside U-duality". T-duality relates a string theory on a circle of radius R to one on a circle of radius 1/R in string units, and its doubled geometry is the subject of [double field theory](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory) [T]. The program's open conjecture is an analog of that inclusion for its own two polarizations ([open problem 8](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure)).
