*Conjugate monopoles are hypothetical magnetic monopoles, or other topological solitons, belonging to the program's momentum-polarized matter sector rather than to ours. The checkpoint keeps one statement about them: if they exist, they gravitate, because every kind of matter in the program couples to the same geometry, so their existence could not be without consequence. Their use as the dark matter itself, the superheavy soliton candidate, was retired on 2026-10-19 after its abundance arithmetic failed review. What remains is a statement about what such objects would do, not a claim that they exist.*

**Status:** Alive — [D] — #11 §11.1 calls the ontology "alive and consequence-bearing". It is an argument from postulate P4, with no calculation or script behind it. The dark-matter candidacy it fed is retired (#11 §12).

## Key facts

- **The statement** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §11.1 [D]): "As ontology: alive and consequence-bearing — by P4, conjugate-sector solitons gravitate; "true but irrelevant" is the empty set (a correction supplied by P.R.)."
- **Why they gravitate** (#11 §4, P4): "Matter comes in x-polarized (Standard Model) and p-polarized (χ) representations; both couple universally to H; gauge forces do not cross polarizations" ([P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter)). The universal coupling to H, the phase-space metric, is what makes the objects gravitate.
- **Two wordings, one status** (unresolved). App. A item 5 records P.R.'s phrase as "true-but-irrelevant is measure zero"; §11.1 says "the empty set". The checkpoint does not reconcile the two; the status, alive, is the same under either.
- **The candidacy, retired** (#11 §11.1, §12): "Their candidacy as the dark matter itself is retired in v2.1 (§12)." [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) had tied the ontology to that candidate — "As ontology they're alive (the soliton candidate)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2) — and the candidate is now dead ([Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). Per the root `CLAUDE.md`, "The framework is merely compatible with known PeV-scale topological dark matter (prior art)" ([topological dark matter in the literature](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art)).
- **The laboratory shadow** (#11 §11.1): "Their laboratory shadow is real: Weyl semimetals host catalogued families of momentum-space Berry-curvature monopoles [16†] — right geometry, different flux." Reference [16]†: S.-Y. Xu et al., "Discovery of a Weyl fermion semimetal," Science 349, 613 (2015); B. Q. Lv et al., Phys. Rev. X 5, 031013 (2015). Prediction row 8 names these as "the only shadow" ([row 8](/wiki/predictions/monopole-null)).
- **Localization in momentum** (#11 §12): topological momentum-space localization of individual objects "remains untested, but has no candidate" ([Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)).
- **The other half of the split** is dead: conjugate monopoles do not explain why we find none ([Obituary: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)).
