*The dual superconductor is the leading picture of quark confinement, the observed fact that quarks are never found in isolation. In an ordinary superconductor a condensate of paired electrons expels magnetic field and confines whatever magnetic flux remains to thin tubes. In the dual picture, proposed by 't Hooft and Mandelstam, the vacuum of the strong interaction holds a condensate of magnetic monopoles, which confines the color-electric field between quarks to a string whose energy grows with its length, so separating two quarks costs ever more energy. It is one of the three pieces of established physics that absorbed the program's founding supposition 11, a magnetic counterpart for every electric particle; the program adds no claim of its own here.*

## Key facts

- **The statement** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §11.2): "the dual-superconductor picture of confinement — chromomagnetic monopole condensation squeezing color-electric flux into strings — is the leading mechanism for why free quarks are never seen [T, 't Hooft–Mandelstam†]".
- **The duality behind the name** [T]: exchanging the roles of electric and magnetic fields turns the superconductor's expulsion of magnetic field into an expulsion of electric field, which is the [electric–magnetic duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality) of this section applied to a medium. The condensing objects are chromomagnetic monopoles, magnetic charges of the strong interaction's color field; monopoles in general are the subject of [magnetic monopoles](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles).
- **Magnetic quarks** (#11 §11.2): "magnetic quarks" is "standard vocabulary via Seiberg duality [18†]" ([S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality)).
- **Founding supposition 11** (#11 App. A item 11): "A full magnetic mirror-family, quarks included — alive as established physics (Montonen–Olive, Seiberg, dual superconductor), absorbed as §11 with the polarization identification as the program-native addition" ([the magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family); [founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions)).
- **In the popular article** (#12, 'Dark matter as the other polarization'): "If that picture is correct, magnetic analogs of quarks are not hypothetical inhabitants of a hidden sector; their condensed form is at work inside every proton." The sentence is conditional, and #12 is never a source for a claim's status ([the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
