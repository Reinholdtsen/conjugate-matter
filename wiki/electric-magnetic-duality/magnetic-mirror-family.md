*The magnetic mirror-family is the idea that every electrically charged particle, quarks included, has a magnetic counterpart. It entered the program as P.R.'s founding supposition 11, and the checkpoint records it as established physics: Montonen–Olive duality, Seiberg's magnetic quarks and the dual-superconductor picture of quark confinement already contain it. Dirac's condition accounts for why the counterparts are not seen: a weak electric coupling forces a strong magnetic one, so magnetic particles are heavy and tightly bound. The program's own addition is the observation that choosing which particles count as electric is a polarization, which has its own page.*

**Status:** Established — [T] — "alive as established physics" (#11 App. A item 11). The physics is #11 §11.2, tagged [T] and cited as [17†-adjacent; 18†] and 't Hooft–Mandelstam†, all from model memory and due [the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery).

## Key facts

- **The supposition and its fate** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) App. A item 11): "A full magnetic mirror-family, quarks included — alive as established physics (Montonen–Olive, Seiberg, dual superconductor), absorbed as §11 with the polarization identification as the program-native addition" ([founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions)).
- **The dual spectrum** (#11 §11.2 [T]): "The conjecture that every electrically charged particle has a magnetic counterpart is Montonen–Olive duality [17†-adjacent; 18†], exact in maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills; "magnetic quarks" is standard vocabulary via Seiberg duality [18†]; and the dual-superconductor picture of confinement ... is the leading mechanism for why free quarks are never seen" ([S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality); [the dual superconductor](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/dual-superconductor)). The checkpoint's opening passage dates the idea: "proposed in 1977, named in 1994".
- **Why the mirror is not seen** (#11 §11.2): "Non-observation is structural: Dirac's condition e·g ~ 2π pins the magnetic coupling near 4π/e² ≈ 34 — the magnetic world is our strong-coupling mirror, heavy and bound" ([Dirac quantization and the DSZ pairing](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization)).
- **The convention behind 34** (`review/v3-worklog.md`, round-2 item R2, [V]): the number "is convention-dependent by a factor of 4": "1/(4α) = 34.26 (eg = 2πn) vs 1/α = 137.04 (eg = 4πn)". The figure should not be quoted without its normalization.
- **Not the dead α_d ≈ 34.** The 34 here is ordinary electromagnetism's own magnetic coupling. The dead claim used Dirac quantization to fix a hidden sector's coupling "against our photon" (`HANDOFF.md` §2; [Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)).
- **The same explanatory shape as the hidden metric** (#11 §11.2): "Same explanatory shape as §7: the duality is genuine, and its structure hides its second half." In §7 the hidden half is the second metric, the [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), which the choice of polarization makes massive.
- **Misread in the popular article.** [#12](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) extends the family into a claim that "the momentum polarization carries the entire magnetic mirror-family", which is not a program claim and is recorded as dead ([Obituary: the magnetic identification (popsci-only)](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)).
