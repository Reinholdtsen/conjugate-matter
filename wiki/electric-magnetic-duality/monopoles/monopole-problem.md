*The monopole problem, in the form the program cites (John Preskill, 1979), is the finding that the hot early universe should have produced superheavy magnetic monopoles in unacceptably large numbers, while none has ever been detected. Monopoles and antimonopoles can annihilate one another, but too slowly to remove enough of them. The standard resolution is inflation, a period of rapid expansion after the monopoles form that dilutes them to an unobservable density. The program meets the problem twice: inflation is one of the two reasons its founding claim that conjugate monopoles explain the null result is dead, and round 3 ruled that the strong-coupling annihilation question of its retired soliton candidate is Preskill's calculation, already done for hidden sectors.*

**Status:** Established — [T] — literature. Preskill 1979 is reference [19] of the withdrawn [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate), cited without a tag; the worklog reports its key figure as "verified from the scan", and [the round-3 audit](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings) checked the abstract. The checkpoint uses inflationary dilution as established physics [T] (#11 §11.1).

## Key facts

- **Preskill 1979** (#14 [19]: J. Preskill, Phys. Rev. Lett. 43, 1365 (1979)). The round-3 audit quotes the abstract: "density of such monopoles in the early universe is estimated to be unacceptably large" (Step 1).
- **Annihilation is not enough** (`review/v3-worklog.md`, finding F1): "Preskill (verified from the scan): annihilation cannot reduce n/T³ below ~10⁻¹⁰ at m ≈ 10¹⁶ GeV." #14 §3: "The classic relic analysis [19] found monopole-antimonopole annihilation inefficient for superheavy monopoles in our plasma." The handoff records that #14 v1 had cited Preskill "inverted", a valid round-1 hit that was fixed (`HANDOFF.md` §6; [review rounds 1 and 2](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two)).
- **Inflation** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §11.1): "inflationary dilution already accounts for absence [T]" — one of the two reasons the explanatory half of founding supposition 5 is dead ([Obituary: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation); [inflation, reheating and entropy dilution](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/inflation-and-dilution)). For its hidden sector #14 required that "Inflation must precede the hidden transition, and v must lie below the reheating temperature" (#14 §3).
- **Strong coupling is Preskill's case** (ruling 10, upheld at abstract level): "Annihilation at α_m ≈ 34 is Preskill's Dirac case, already done for hidden sectors". Here α_m ≈ 34 is the magnetic coupling of the retired candidate's monopoles under #14's hypothesis H, a figure that depends on convention by a factor of 4 ([Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)). The audit's wording: "the "strong-coupling" regime alpha_m = 34 is exactly the Dirac-monopole case of Preskill 1979" (Step 4(a), item 3). #14 had left this as its acceptance criterion 3, annihilation "at alpha_m ~ 34, where Sommerfeld enhancement and rapid bound-state formation may matter" (#14 §3); the question is recorded with [the √N topological floor](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor).
- **The hidden-sector version** (#11 §12): "Hidden-sector monopoles avoid overclosing the universe only for symmetry-breaking scales ≲ 100 PeV, or with a period of early matter domination [29S]" — Liveoak–Maharana–Wells 2026 ([topological dark matter in the literature](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art)).
- **The production side** is [the Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism), which sets the minimum number of monopoles a transition makes; the observational side is [the monopole flux bounds](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches).
