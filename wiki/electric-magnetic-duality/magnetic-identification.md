*The magnetic identification is a claim made only in the popular article "The Universe's Other Half" (#12): that the program's momentum-polarized sector is the magnetic side of electromagnetism, dark because Dirac's condition makes its coupling strong, "about 34, against our 1/137", with only topological knots surviving from it as free relics. No paper or note of the program makes this claim. The technical record supports only the observation that choosing electric charges and choosing position are the same matrix operation, together with an open conjecture that they are one structure. The claim conflicts with a dead claim, with the strict separation of the two sectors' forces and with the program's prediction of a permanent null in direct detection, so the wiki records it as dead.*

**Status:** Dead (popsci-only) — no tag; #12 carries none and is never a source for a claim's status. The claim conflicts with the dead "α_d ≈ 34 via Dirac quantization against our photon" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), with P4 taken strictly (#11 §4; round-3 Decision 1) and with prediction row 7 (#11 §13). Home section: [electric–magnetic duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality); listed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard) and with the [popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures).

## Key facts

- **What was claimed** (#12, 'Dark matter as the other polarization'): "The momentum-polarized sector is the magnetic side of electromagnetism, and its darkness has a quantifiable origin: a consistency condition derived by Dirac in 1931 fixes the product of the two kinds of charge, so the weakness of our electric coupling forces the magnetic coupling to be enormous — about 34, against our 1/137." And: "the momentum polarization carries the entire magnetic mirror-family ... of which only the topologically protected knots survive the age of the universe as free relics." #12's closing summary lists "A candidate identity for dark matter's charge: the magnetic half of electromagnetism's own duality" ('What one assumption purchases'; [the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
- **What the record supports** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §11.3): the same-matrix identity, tagged [D — structural; math T], and a conjecture tagged [O] ([the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice); [open problem 8](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure)). #12 calls the unification "The framework's proposal" and gives it no open status.
- **Conflict with a dead claim.** "α_d ≈ 34 via Dirac quantization against our photon (round-1 reviewer's valid kill: can't use our DSZ lattice and then deny coupling to our photon)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2, under "Dead"), where DSZ is the Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger charge pairing ([Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)).
- **Conflict with sector separation.** [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) described the soliton candidate as "dark by sector separation" and not magnetically charged under our photon (`HANDOFF.md` §2), and [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) v3 states: "These objects carry no charge under our photon" (#14 §2). P4 says "gauge forces do not cross polarizations" and has been taken strictly since v2.1, with no kinetic mixing beyond what gravity induces (#11 §4; [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter); [round-3 decisions](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions); [kinetic mixing between sectors](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing)).
- **Conflict with row 7.** The checkpoint requires a permanent null in direct detection, with both remaining dark-sector candidates decoupled from the Standard Model under strict P4, and lists as the kill any confirmed non-gravitational coupling between dark matter and the Standard Model (#11 §13; [row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null)).
- **The number 34** (`review/v3-worklog.md`, round-2 item R2, [V]): "convention-dependent by a factor of 4": "1/(4α) = 34.26 (eg = 2πn) vs 1/α = 137.04 (eg = 4πn)". The checkpoint's own "4π/e² ≈ 34" (§11.2) describes the magnetic coupling of ordinary electromagnetism, not a property of the momentum sector ([the magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family)).
- **The knots** #12 favors are the retired soliton candidate ([Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). #12's transparency argument for a magnetically charged dark sector rests on the same premise and is quarantined with it ([the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
- **What survives.** The established [magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family) of our own electromagnetism, the alive [symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice), and the open conjecture of [open problem 8](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure).
