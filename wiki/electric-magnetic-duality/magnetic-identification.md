*The magnetic mirror is P.R.'s conjecture that momentum matter is similar to position matter with electric and magnetic exchanged: each electrically charged particle of the position-polarized sector has a magnetically charged analog in the momentum-polarized one. It joins two of P.R.'s [founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions): that monopoles live in conjugate space, and that every electric particle has a magnetic counterpart ([the magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family)). It is what an open conjecture of [the checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) would give if one vacuum event made two choices at once, which charges count as electric and which variables count as position ([one enlarged modular structure](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure)). The second version of the withdrawn soliton manuscript stated a related identification, the momentum-polarized sector as the image of the electric sector under [S-duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality), the exchange of electric and magnetic charge. In one form of the conjecture the analogs are magnetic under a photon of their own sector. In the other they carry magnetic charge of our photon, which the postulate separating the two sectors' forces ([P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter)) would have to be restated to allow. The checkpoint records the conjecture as open in §11.4; nothing derives it yet, and no calculation tests it.*

**Status:** Conjecture — [O] — P.R.'s, recorded in #11 §11.4 (v2.3, 2026-10-29) and in the root `CLAUDE.md` ('Since the handoff', 2026-10-29). No derivation or script stands behind it. Until 2026-10-29 this page was an obituary of the shared-photon form as the [popular article #12](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) states it, on the wiki's own reading; flag 60 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` reversed it on Paul's ruling ([the earlier obituary](#the-earlier-obituary-and-its-reversal)). Flag 62, pending, asks whether the shared-photon form would reopen the dead explanation of the monopole null result. Home section: [electric–magnetic duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality); listed with the [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures) and, under open problem 8, among the [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## The conjecture

P.R.'s statement, in the discussion of 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-29: "momentum matter is similar to position matter with electrically charged position particles having magnetically charged momentum particle analogs" (`review/monopoles/README.md`).

#11 §11.4, "The magnetic-mirror conjecture [O] (P.R.; v2.3)":

> P.R.'s conjecture: momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged x-polarized particle having a magnetically charged p-polarized analog. It joins founding suppositions 5 and 11 (Appendix A), and it is what §11.3's conjecture would give if one vacuum event selected both the position polarization and the electric sublattice.

x-polarized matter is ours, made of fields local in position; p-polarized matter is the conjugate matter χ, made of fields local in momentum ([P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter); [χ particles](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles)). The electric sublattice is the set of charges a theory treats as electric. It is a Lagrangian sublattice of [the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice), chosen the way a [Lagrangian polarization](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations) of phase space is chosen, and #11 §11.3 observes that the same matrix generates both dualities [D — structural; math T].

## Where it comes from

- **Founding suppositions 5 and 11.** #11's audit of P.R.'s founding suppositions records "Monopoles live in conjugate space, explaining non-detection" (App. A item 5) and "A full magnetic mirror-family, quarks included" (item 11). Item 11 was absorbed as established physics (Montonen–Olive, Seiberg, dual superconductor), in which the magnetic partners belong to the same theory as the electric particles [T]. Item 5's explanatory half is dead ([Obituary: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)). Since v2.3, item 5's ontology half and item 11 point to §11.4.
- **The soliton manuscript, version 2.** The second version of [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate), the proposal of superheavy monopoles as dark matter, made the S-dual identification its load-bearing conjecture, "load-bearing for alpha_d" (#14 v2 §6): "the momentum-polarized matter sector is the S-dual image of the electric sector: a distinct gauge structure whose complexified coupling is fixed by the reciprocity map, acting as the modular S transformation, to the image of ours" (#14 v2 §2). A full mirror spectrum appeared there only as something the sector "may carry" (§2 (ii)). Its abstract adds that "one vacuum event fixes both the position/momentum asymmetry of observed physics and the electric/magnetic asymmetry of the observed spectrum". It excluded the shared-photon form: "It does not assert that these objects carry magnetic charge under our photon" (the original sets "not" in capitals). #14 v2 is in git history at commit 8d588f6.
- **Round 2.** The second blind review named the coupling derivation the weakest link: "matrix coincidence ≠ physical identity" / "numerology" (`HANDOFF.md` §6; [review rounds 1 and 2](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two)).
- **Decision F2 and its withdrawal.** On 2026-10-19, before #14's third version, Paul adopted a reading in which "the load-bearing conjecture becomes α_e,d = α (or α_e,d free and weak), and S-duality is no longer what fixes α_d" (`review/v3-worklog.md`, 'Findings neither referee raised', F2). Here α_e,d is the dual sector's elementary coupling and α_d its monopoles' coupling. On 2026-10-29 Paul withdrew the decision as "probably confusion on my part" (flag 60). F2's finding, that #14 v2 used both a weak and a strong value of the dual coupling, stands, and #14 itself stays withdrawn ([Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)).
- **The popular article.** #12 states the shared-photon form outright: "The momentum-polarized sector is the magnetic side of electromagnetism", and "the momentum polarization carries the entire magnetic mirror-family — a matter sector as structured as our own" (`popsci/universes-other-half.md`, 'Dark matter as the other polarization'). #12 presents the framework "as if it were confirmed", and the wiki never takes a claim's status from it.

## Two forms

#11 §11.4 distinguishes the two forms by the photon the analogs' magnetic charge belongs to.

### Separate photon

> *Separate photon* (#14 v2's form). The analogs are magnetic under the p-polarized sector's own gauge field, whose coupling is the S-image of ours. Whether that is the sector's elementary coupling or its magnetic one is open; F2's finding is that the choice decides whether the sector's solitons are heavy. This form is consistent with P4 and leaves the absence of our own monopoles to §11.1. A massless photon of the p-sector would be dark radiation, which bounded #14's hidden sector to a hidden-to-visible temperature ratio ξ < 0.760 (round-3 ruling 5).

In this form the analogs carry no charge under our photon, and the sector's darkness rests on the separation of the two sectors' forces, as for the rest of the conjugate sector. #14 v2 put the coupling at "alpha_d = 1/(4 alpha) ~ 34, quoted in the Dirac normalization (the Schwinger convention shifts this by the standard factor of four)" (#14 v2 §2), in the normalization of [Dirac quantization](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization). The number is that of the dead α_d ≈ 34; the derivation differs. #14's first version obtained it by Dirac quantization against our photon, which round 1 killed; v2 restated it with reciprocity acting as the modular S transformation on the coupling (`HANDOFF.md` §2), which round 2 called numerology ([Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)). Flag 60: neither form of the conjecture repeats the dead inconsistency, Dirac's condition taken against our photon while coupling to it is denied.

### Shared photon

> *Shared photon* (the popular article #12's reading). The analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon: the magnetic half of electromagnetism lives in the p-polarization, and by Dirac's condition their existence would quantize electric charge [T, 17†]. P4 as written excludes this form ("gauge forces do not cross polarizations", in the program since #1), so it needs P4 restated. Localized in position at dark-matter density, such objects would exceed the Parker bound at #14's benchmark masses (#14 v2 and v3 §4; not gated). Something must therefore hide them, and neither option has been computed: p-localization, open since v2.0 ("p-local solitons are p-localized" [O]), with the spread in the objects' field profile rather than their quantum state, wider than detectors and than the coherence length of galactic magnetic fields, and decoupled from the photon–baryon plasma before recombination (†); or binding into magnetically neutral composites ("heavy and bound", §11.2). Under P4 as written, App. A item 5's explanation of the null result stays dead; whether this form, with P4 restated, would reopen it is P.R.'s ruling (flag 62).

- **P4.** The lab notebook stated the separation from the start: "Gauge interactions do not connect the two representations" (#1 §1, P4). Round-3 Decision 1 added the strict reading, no kinetic mixing beyond what gravity induces ([round-3 decisions](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions)). Both are postulates [P], and a shared photon would join the two sectors through one gauge field.
- **The Parker bound.** Galactic magnetic fields would lose energy to free magnetic charges moving along them, and the field strengths observed bound the flux of such charges ([the monopole flux bounds](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches)) [T]. For monopoles of our photon localized in position, at #14 v3's withdrawn benchmark mass of 3×10¹³ GeV (#11 §12), #14 found "That exceeds the Parker bound (~1e-15 for M <~ 1e17 GeV) [20] by 7.5 orders of magnitude" at dark-matter density (#14 §4; withdrawn manuscript, not gated). #14 assumed ordinary kinematics throughout: "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" (#14 §1).
- **Hiding.** An object compact in momentum is spread in position by about ħ/Δp, as a narrowband signal is long in time [T]. Whether that spread exceeds a detector, or a galactic field's coherence length, depends on the width Δp, which the record does not fix, and the spread must be in the object's field profile: a particle in a spread-out quantum state is still detected whole, at a rate set by how many there are [T]. That conjugate-sector solitons are localized in momentum at all is the open [topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole). The alternative is binding into magnetically neutral composites.
- **Row 7.** [Row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null) requires a "**Permanent null** (both dark-sector candidates SM-decoupled; P4 strict)" (#11 §13), where SM is the Standard Model. In this form the analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon, which breaks that basis by construction. If they are part of the dark matter, the row's observable null survives only if they are spread in position, which no document analyzes (flag 60).

## What it needs

#11 §11.4 lists what stays open in either form:

> Open in either form: a mechanism by which one order parameter makes both choices (the electric-sublattice clause of acceptance criterion 1, never attempted; the toy models of §14 item 11 contain no gauge field); the round-2 objection that a shared matrix is not a shared physics ("matrix coincidence ≠ physical identity"), since [[0,1],[−1,0]] is the canonical form of any two-dimensional symplectic pairing [T]; round 2's burden that exact S-duality is established only with extended supersymmetry, so either reciprocity supplies the rigidity or the dual sector is approximately supersymmetric (`HANDOFF.md` §6; `review/v3-worklog.md` R1, which ties the burden's size to F2's decision, now withdrawn); and the dual coupling's factor-of-4 convention spread, 1/(4α) or 1/α (`review/v3-worklog.md` R2 [V]; the formula printed beside 34 in §11.2 is flag 28).

- **One order parameter, two choices.** The handoff's open item 1 asked for a toy Lagrangian with "η the order parameter also selecting the electric sublattice" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). The toy models met [acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion) without that clause.
- **Supersymmetry.** The worklog tied the burden to F2: "If F2 is adopted, S no longer carries α_d, and the burden shrinks to the §2 "full mirror spectrum" remark" (`review/v3-worklog.md`, 'Round-2 pending list', R1). F2's decision is withdrawn.
- **The convention.** "1/(4α) = 34.26 (eg = 2πn) vs 1/α = 137.04 (eg = 4πn)" (`review/v3-worklog.md`, 'Round-2 pending list', R2, [V]), where e and g are the electric and magnetic charges and n an integer ([the convention behind 34](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family#the-convention-behind-34)). Which value the conjecture predicts is open.
- **For the shared-photon form**, also: P4 restated, and p-localization in the field profile or a binding into neutral composites that clears detectors, the Parker bound and the plasma before recombination.

## What it would explain

- **Charge quantization.** Dirac's condition makes every electric charge a multiple of a unit fixed by any one magnetic charge of the same photon [T]. In the shared-photon form the analogs' existence would quantize our electric charges (#11 §11.4).
- **The monopole null result, not claimed.** In the shared-photon form the analogs would be our photon's magnetic charges, unseen because they are not localized in position. Under P4 as written, the founding claim that conjugate monopoles explain the null result stays dead ([Obituary: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation); #11 §11.1). Whether this form, with P4 restated, would reopen that claim is flag 62, pending, and the null result lends the conjecture no support either way ([row 8](/wiki/predictions/monopole-null)).

## Popsci-only: the transparency argument

#12 follows its identification with an argument that a magnetically charged dark sector would still be invisible: "A magnetically charged cosmos raises an immediate objection — should it not affect light? The answer is a standard piece of optics. Uniform media do not scatter light; they refract it." The argument uses the mass of the retired soliton candidate, and no script checks it. #14 v2 made a related consistency check for small kinetic mixing between the sectors, now withdrawn: refraction, dispersion or birefringence of light induced by the sector would lie far below observational sensitivity at its benchmark (#14 v2 §4, 'Optics'). The argument is described, with its numbers, on [the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) page.

## The earlier obituary and its reversal

From the wiki's breadth pass (2026-10-21) to 2026-10-29 this page was "Obituary: the magnetic identification (popsci-only)", with status dead. It argued that #12's identification repeated the dead "α_d ≈ 34 via Dirac quantization against our photon" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), contradicted strict P4 and row 7, and that "No paper or note of the program makes this claim". It described its own verdict as "this wiki's reading of those conflicts under the source precedence". Flag 60 found that a verdict of that kind adds a result, which the wiki does not do, and that its premises do not hold:

- #14 v2 made the S-dual identification its load-bearing conjecture, so the sentence "No paper or note of the program makes this claim" held only for the shared-photon form;
- round 1's kill of α_d ≈ 34 struck an inconsistency, Dirac's condition taken against our photon while coupling to it was denied, and the separate-photon and shared-photon forms are its two ways out;
- strict P4 conflicts only with the shared-photon form, and P4 is a postulate [P];
- row 7's basis conflicts with the shared-photon form by construction, but its observable null is at stake only if the analogs are localized in position, which was never analyzed.

Paul accepted flag 60 on 2026-10-29; in the words of its ruling, he "never meant to create an obituary" and called the decision of 2026-10-19 "probably confusion on my part". The page became this conjecture page and moved from [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard) to the conjectures register.
