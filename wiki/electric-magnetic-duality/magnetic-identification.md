*The magnetic mirror is P.R.'s conjecture that momentum matter is similar to position matter with electric and magnetic exchanged: each electrically charged particle of the position-polarized sector has a magnetically charged analog in the momentum-polarized one. It joins two of P.R.'s [founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions): that monopoles live in conjugate space, and that every electric particle has a magnetic counterpart ([the magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family)). It is what an open conjecture of [the checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) would give if one vacuum event made two choices at once, which charges count as electric and which variables count as position ([one enlarged modular structure](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure)). The second version of the withdrawn soliton manuscript stated a related identification, the momentum-polarized sector as the image of the electric sector under [S-duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality), the exchange of electric and magnetic charge. In one form of the conjecture the analogs are magnetic under a photon of their own sector. In the other they carry magnetic charge of our photon, which the postulate separating the two sectors' forces ([P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter)) would have to be restated to allow; that form is also the setting of P.R.'s open conjecture that conjugate monopoles are why no monopole has been found. The checkpoint records the conjecture as open in §11.4; nothing derives it yet, and no calculation tests it.*

**Status:** Conjecture — [O] — P.R.'s conjecture (#11 §11.4; root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'). Its separate-photon form is consistent with P4. Its shared-photon form needs P4 restated and something to hide the analogs, either localization in momentum, with the spread in the field profile and decoupling from the plasma before recombination (†), or binding into magnetically neutral composites, and neither has been computed. That form is the setting of P.R.'s open explanation of the monopole null result ([Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation); flag 62 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). No derivation or script stands behind the conjecture. Home section: [electric–magnetic duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality); listed with the [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures) and, under open problem 8, among the [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## The conjecture

P.R.'s statement: "momentum matter is similar to position matter with electrically charged position particles having magnetically charged momentum particle analogs" (`review/monopoles/README.md`).

#11 §11.4, "The magnetic-mirror conjecture [O]":

> P.R.'s conjecture: momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged x-polarized particle having a magnetically charged p-polarized analog. It joins founding suppositions 5 and 11 (Appendix A), and it is what §11.3's conjecture would give if one vacuum event selected both the position polarization and the electric sublattice.

x-polarized matter is ours, made of fields local in position; p-polarized matter is the conjugate matter χ, made of fields local in momentum ([P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter); [χ particles](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles)). The electric sublattice is the set of charges a theory treats as electric. It is a Lagrangian sublattice of [the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice), chosen the way a [Lagrangian polarization](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations) of phase space is chosen, and #11 §11.3 observes that the same matrix generates both dualities [D — structural; math T].

## Where it comes from

- **Founding suppositions 5 and 11.** #11's audit of P.R.'s founding suppositions records "Monopoles live in conjugate space, explaining non-detection" (App. A item 5) and "A full magnetic mirror-family, quarks included" (item 11). Item 11 was absorbed as established physics (Montonen–Olive, Seiberg, dual superconductor), in which the magnetic partners belong to the same theory as the electric particles [T]. Item 5's explanatory half is P.R.'s open conjecture, in the setting of the shared-photon form below ([Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)). Item 5's ontology half and item 11 point to §11.4.
- **The soliton manuscript, version 2.** The second version of [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate), the proposal of superheavy monopoles as dark matter, made the S-dual identification its load-bearing conjecture, "load-bearing for alpha_d" (#14 v2 §6): "the momentum-polarized matter sector is the S-dual image of the electric sector: a distinct gauge structure whose complexified coupling is fixed by the reciprocity map, acting as the modular S transformation, to the image of ours" (#14 v2 §2). A full mirror spectrum appeared there only as something the sector "may carry" (§2 (ii)). Its abstract adds that "one vacuum event fixes both the position/momentum asymmetry of observed physics and the electric/magnetic asymmetry of the observed spectrum". It excluded the shared-photon form: "It does not assert that these objects carry magnetic charge under our photon" (the original sets "not" in capitals). #14 v2 is in git history at commit 8d588f6.
- **Round 2.** The second blind review named the coupling derivation the weakest link: "matrix coincidence ≠ physical identity" / "numerology" (`HANDOFF.md` §6; [review rounds 1 and 2](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two)).
- **The worklog's finding F2.** The worklog found that #14 v2 used both a weak and a strong value of the dual coupling, and proposed a reading in which the dual sector's elementary coupling α_e,d is weak and its solitons are heavy and strongly coupled (`review/v3-worklog.md`, 'Findings neither referee raised', F2). #14's third version took that reading: "the load-bearing conjecture becomes α_e,d = α (or α_e,d free and weak), and S-duality is no longer what fixes α_d", where α_d is the monopoles' coupling. The decision to adopt it is withdrawn, and the finding stands (§11.4; flag 60). In the conjecture, which coupling is the S-image of ours is open (§11.4), and #14 itself is withdrawn ([Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)).
- **The popular article.** [#12](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) states the shared-photon form outright: "The momentum-polarized sector is the magnetic side of electromagnetism", and "the momentum polarization carries the entire magnetic mirror-family — a matter sector as structured as our own" (`popsci/universes-other-half.md`, 'Dark matter as the other polarization'). No paper or note of the program asserts this form: #14 v2 excludes it, and #11 §11.4 records it as one form of P.R.'s conjecture (flag 60). #12 presents the framework "as if it were confirmed", and the wiki never takes a claim's status from it.

## Two forms

#11 §11.4 distinguishes the two forms by the photon the analogs' magnetic charge belongs to.

### Separate photon

> *Separate photon* (#14 v2's form). The analogs are magnetic under the p-polarized sector's own gauge field, whose coupling is the S-image of ours. Whether that is the sector's elementary coupling or its magnetic one is open; F2's finding is that the choice decides whether the sector's solitons are heavy. This form is consistent with P4 and leaves the absence of our own monopoles to §11.1. A massless photon of the p-sector would be dark radiation, which bounded #14's hidden sector to a hidden-to-visible temperature ratio ξ < 0.760 (round-3 ruling 5).

In this form the analogs carry no charge under our photon, and the sector's darkness rests on the separation of the two sectors' forces, as for the rest of the conjugate sector. #14 v2 put the coupling at "alpha_d = 1/(4 alpha) ~ 34, quoted in the Dirac normalization (the Schwinger convention shifts this by the standard factor of four)" (#14 v2 §2), in the normalization of [Dirac quantization](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization). The number is that of the hidden-sector α_d ≈ 34, which is under review; the derivation differs. #14's first version obtained it by Dirac quantization against our photon, which round 1 found inconsistent; v2 restated it with reciprocity acting as the modular S transformation on the coupling (`HANDOFF.md` §2), which round 2 called numerology ([Under review: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)). Flag 60: the separate-photon and shared-photon forms are the two ways out of the inconsistency round 1 found, Dirac's condition taken against our photon while coupling to it is denied, and neither form repeats it. The hidden sector of #14 whose temperature ratio the quotation bounds is under review, as the program's proposal ([Under review: the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector)).

### Shared photon

> *Shared photon* (the popular article #12's reading). The analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon: the magnetic half of electromagnetism lives in the p-polarization, and by Dirac's condition their existence would quantize electric charge [T, 17†]. P4 as written excludes this form ("gauge forces do not cross polarizations", in the program since #1), so it needs P4 restated. Localized in position at dark-matter density, such objects would exceed the Parker bound at #14's benchmark masses (#14 v2 and v3 §4; not gated). Something must therefore hide them, and neither option has been computed.

#11 §11.4 names the two options: p-localization ("p-local solitons are p-localized" [O]), with the spread in the objects' field profile rather than their quantum state, wider than detectors and than the coherence length of galactic magnetic fields, and decoupled from the photon–baryon plasma before recombination (†); or binding into magnetically neutral composites ("heavy and bound", §11.2). This form is the setting of App. A item 5's explanation of the null result: it requires P4 restated and the hiding conditions above, none of them yet computed (flag 62).

- **P4.** The lab notebook stated the separation from the start: "Gauge interactions do not connect the two representations" (#1 §1, P4). Round-3 Decision 1 added the strict reading, no kinetic mixing beyond what gravity induces ([round-3 decisions](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions)). Both are postulates [P]. A shared photon would join the two sectors through one gauge field, and strict P4 conflicts only with this form (flag 60).
- **The Parker bound.** Galactic magnetic fields would lose energy to free magnetic charges moving along them, and the field strengths observed bound the flux of such charges ([the monopole flux bounds](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches)) [T]. For monopoles of our photon localized in position, at #14 v3's withdrawn benchmark mass of 3×10¹³ GeV (#11 §12), #14 found "That exceeds the Parker bound (~1e-15 for M <~ 1e17 GeV) [20] by 7.5 orders of magnitude" at dark-matter density (#14 §4; withdrawn manuscript, not gated). #14 assumed ordinary kinematics throughout: "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" (#14 §1).
- **Hiding.** An object compact in momentum is spread in position by about ħ/Δp, as a narrowband signal is long in time [T]. Whether that spread exceeds a detector, or a galactic field's coherence length, depends on the width Δp, which the record does not fix, and the spread must be in the object's field profile: a particle in a spread-out quantum state is still detected whole, at a rate set by how many there are [T]. That conjugate-sector solitons are localized in momentum at all is the open [topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole), whose candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles). The kinematics of momentum-polarized matter has never been modeled: #14 assumed ordinary kinematics (root `CLAUDE.md`, "Open from the monopole discussion"). The alternative is binding into magnetically neutral composites.
- **Row 7.** [Row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null) requires a "**Permanent null** (both dark-sector candidates SM-decoupled; P4 strict)" (#11 §13), where SM is the Standard Model. The row names two candidates, while §12 counts three; flag 63 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`, pending, proposes adding "for the third candidate's shared-photon form, a null of position-space searches". In this form the analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon, which breaks that basis by construction. If they are part of the dark matter, the row's observable null survives only if they are spread in position, which no document analyzes (flag 60).
- **The explanation of the null result.** This form is the setting of P.R.'s open explanation of why no monopole of our photon has been found: the analogs would be our photon's magnetic charges, "unseen because they are not localized in position" (flag 62). Neither ground given against the explanation kills it: this form contests the first, wrong duality, and the second, that the null result is expected under several standard histories, lends it no support either way (#11 §11.1, App. A item 5; [Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)).

## What it needs

#11 §11.4 lists what stays open in either form:

- **One order parameter, two choices.** "a mechanism by which one order parameter makes both choices (the electric-sublattice clause of acceptance criterion 1, never attempted; the toy models of §14 item 11 contain no gauge field)". The handoff's open item 1 asked for a toy Lagrangian with "η the order parameter also selecting the electric sublattice" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). The toy models met [acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion) without that clause.
- **A shared matrix.** "the round-2 objection that a shared matrix is not a shared physics ("matrix coincidence ≠ physical identity"), since [[0,1],[−1,0]] is the canonical form of any two-dimensional symplectic pairing [T]".
- **Supersymmetry.** "round 2's burden that exact S-duality is established only with extended supersymmetry, so either reciprocity supplies the rigidity or the dual sector is approximately supersymmetric" (`HANDOFF.md` §6). The worklog ties the burden's size to whether S carries the coupling: "If F2 is adopted, S no longer carries α_d, and the burden shrinks to the §2 "full mirror spectrum" remark" (`review/v3-worklog.md`, 'Round-2 pending list', R1). In the separate-photon form the sector's coupling is the S-image of ours (§11.4), and the root `CLAUDE.md` lists the burden among that form's open items ("Open from the monopole discussion").
- **The convention.** "the dual coupling's factor-of-4 convention spread, 1/(4α) or 1/α" (the formula printed beside 34 in §11.2 is flag 28): "1/(4α) = 34.26 (eg = 2πn) vs 1/α = 137.04 (eg = 4πn)" (`review/v3-worklog.md`, 'Round-2 pending list', R2, [V]), where e and g are the electric and magnetic charges and n an integer ([the convention behind 34](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family#the-convention-behind-34)). Which value the conjecture predicts is open.
- **For the shared-photon form**, also: P4 restated, and p-localization in the field profile or a binding into neutral composites that clears detectors, the Parker bound and the plasma before recombination. The explanation of the null result needs the same conditions. For the explanation and for the dark-matter candidate, the root `CLAUDE.md` also lists "p-localization, and the kinematics of momentum-polarized matter, never modeled (#14 §1 assumed ordinary kinematics)".

## What it would explain

- **Charge quantization.** Dirac's condition makes every electric charge a multiple of a unit fixed by any one magnetic charge of the same photon [T]. In the shared-photon form the analogs' existence would quantize our electric charges (#11 §11.4).
- **The monopole null result, as P.R.'s open conjecture.** In the shared-photon form the analogs would be our photon's magnetic charges, unseen because they are not localized in position. This form is the setting of App. A item 5's explanation of the null result, P.R.'s open conjecture (flag 62; #11 §11.1, §11.4; [Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)). It requires P4 restated and the hiding conditions, none of them computed. The null result is expected under several standard histories, so it lends the explanation no support either way ([row 8](/wiki/predictions/monopole-null)).

## In the literature

Partly anticipated ([the literature check](/wiki/method/literature-check)), a verdict that counts the magnetic dark sectors below as close variants of the separate-photon form; if the placement in momentum alone is the conjecture, the verdict would be related only, the closest work then being a 2021 conference paper of Berglund, Hübsch and Minic in which the two gauge groups of a string theory, both with visible fields and each with a dark counterpart in the same group, are each other's electric–magnetic duals, for gauge groups only [S] ([one enlarged modular structure](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure#in-the-literature)). Dark sectors of magnetically charged matter are published: [Govaerts 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.03526) asks whether dual electrodynamics, written with two gauge potentials, gives "a dark parallel world of purely magnetically charged matter", and [Vento 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.06697) and [García-Moreno and Vento 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02083) make monopoles and a second photon the dark sector [S]. None of these places the magnetic matter in the momentum polarization or pairs each charged particle of ours with an analog. Vento's sector is the [separate-photon form](#separate-photon) without its placement. Govaerts's is the magnetic half of one dual electrodynamics, the [shared-photon form](#shared-photon) in wording, but under the force law he derives, electric charges feel only the fields of electric charges and magnetic charges only those of magnetic charges, unless one new coupling opens a portal between the two, which the panel judge for C4 finds closer in physics to the separate-photon form (flag 65, pending). Beyond these dark sectors, one analog per charged particle is published: [Winn 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4590), restated in his talks of 2025 and 2026, gives every charged particle of the Standard Model a magnetically charged partner under our own photon, through a proposed supersymmetry, which exchanges bosons and fermions and also turns electric charge into magnetic charge: "there are no electrically charged superpartners, only neutral or magnetic charged partners" [S]. He proposes the partners' magnetically neutral bound states as dark matter. That is a close variant of the [shared-photon form](#shared-photon), with one analog per charged particle, our own photon and binding into magnetically neutral composites, the second of the two ways #11 §11.4 names to hide the analogs, but without the momentum polarization. The partners are elementary particles, not solitons; nothing is placed in momentum or phase space; Born reciprocity does not appear; and neither interaction terms nor a Lagrangian are yet proposed ([the written summary of his Lepton-Photon 2025 talk](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1493037/contributions/6549576); flag 73, pending). So one analog per charged particle is published, and the placement of the analogs in the momentum polarization is not. The bridge between the two dualities is a published remark: "electric-magnetic duality is a special case of Born-Reciprocity" ([Gibbons and Herdeiro 2001](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0008052) [S]; [one enlarged modular structure](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure#in-the-literature), flag 67, pending). The [mirror family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family) itself is established physics.

## Popsci-only: the transparency argument

#12 follows its identification with an argument that a magnetically charged dark sector would still be invisible: "A magnetically charged cosmos raises an immediate objection — should it not affect light? The answer is a standard piece of optics. Uniform media do not scatter light; they refract it." The argument uses a superheavy soliton mass, "around 10¹⁶ times the proton's", and no script checks it. #14 v2, a withdrawn manuscript, made a related consistency check for small kinetic mixing between the sectors: refraction, dispersion or birefringence of light induced by the sector would lie far below observational sensitivity at its benchmark (#14 v2 §4, 'Optics'). The argument is described, with its numbers, on [the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) page.
