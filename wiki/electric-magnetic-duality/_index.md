*Electric–magnetic duality is the symmetry of Maxwell's equations under exchange of the electric and magnetic fields: exact in empty space, and extended to matter only if magnetic charges exist alongside electric ones. A particle with magnetic charge alone is a magnetic monopole, a north pole without a south pole; none has ever been detected, and the theory of monopoles and of the dualities that relate them to ordinary charges is established physics. The Comatter program meets this subject three times. One of its founding ideas offers an explanation of the absence of monopoles, that they belong to the program's conjugate, momentum-polarized sector; that explanation is P.R.'s open conjecture. A weaker form of the same idea stands, that such monopoles would gravitate, and P.R. conjectures that they are abundant in momentum space. The program also observes that choosing which particles count as electric is the same mathematical operation as choosing which coordinates count as position. Whether those two choices are one structure is an open conjecture. P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture, also open, is what that conjecture would give if one vacuum event made both choices: each electrically charged particle of ours would have a magnetically charged analog in the program's momentum-polarized matter sector.*

The number 34 appears on several pages with two different meanings: the magnetic coupling of our own electromagnetism, which is established physics ([#11 §11.2](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#112-the-full-dual-spectrum-t)), and the coupling [α_d ≈ 34](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md) of the hidden monopoles of [#14's soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/_index.md), which failed. The pages keep the two meanings apart.

- [The modular group SL(2, ℤ)](modular-group.md) is the algebra the dualities share: the 2 × 2 whole-number matrices of determinant 1, generated by S: z → −1/z and T: z → z + 1 and acting on the upper half-plane. It is the symmetry group of a lattice of electric and magnetic charges, with Sp(2r, ℤ) for several gauge fields, and the duality group of the complexified coupling τ_c, in which S is exact only where S-duality is. The page sets out the matrix of S that #11 finds again in Born reciprocity and in the [modular inversion](../phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion.md) Z → −Z⁻¹, where it belongs to a continuous group rather than this discrete one, and the program's wider use of the word "modular".
- [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](s-duality.md) is the quantum form of electric–magnetic duality: Maxwell's exchange of E and B as a quarter-turn, the complexified coupling τ_c and the generator S: τ_c → −1/τ_c, the Montonen–Olive observation that g → 4π/g swaps the W bosons and the monopoles, the spin mismatch that confines exact duality to supersymmetric theories, Seiberg's magnetic quarks, and T-, S- and U-duality in string theory. It also records the exactness burden that [round 2](../method/review/rounds-one-and-two.md) placed on the soliton manuscript, which worklog item R1 and #14 v3 addressed and [#11 §11.4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#114-the-magnetic-mirror-conjecture-o-pr-v23) lists as open. #14 v2's S-dual identification is the separate-photon form of P.R.'s open conjecture, in which the momentum-polarized sector's coupling is the S-image of ours.

- [Conjecture: the momentum sector as the magnetic mirror](magnetic-identification.md) comes last. It is P.R.'s conjecture [O] ([#11 §11.4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#114-the-magnetic-mirror-conjecture-o-pr-v23); flag 60) that momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged particle of ours having a magnetically charged analog in the momentum-polarized sector. It joins founding suppositions 5 and 11. #14 v2 stated a related identification, the momentum-polarized sector as the S-dual image of the electric sector, and made that identification its load-bearing conjecture; a full mirror spectrum appeared there only as something the sector "may carry". In the conjecture's separate-photon form, #14 v2's, the analogs are magnetic under that sector's own gauge field, which is consistent with P4. In its shared-photon form, the reading of [the popular article #12](../history/documents/popular-articles.md), they carry magnetic charge of our photon. P4 as written excludes that form, so it needs P4 restated. Its analogs break the basis of [row 7](../predictions/direct-detection-null.md) by construction, and localized in position at dark-matter density they would exceed [the Parker bound](monopoles/monopole-searches.md#the-parker-bound-t) at #14's benchmark masses (not gated), so something must hide them: p-localization, localization in momentum, with the field profile spread in position and the objects decoupled from the photon–baryon plasma before recombination (†); or binding into magnetically neutral composites. Neither has been computed. This form is the setting of P.R.'s open explanation of the null result (flag 62). The page folds #12's transparency argument as popsci-only.
