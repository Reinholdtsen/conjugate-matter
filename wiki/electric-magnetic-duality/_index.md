*Electric–magnetic duality is the symmetry of Maxwell's equations under exchange of the electric and magnetic fields: exact in empty space, and extended to matter only if magnetic charges exist alongside electric ones. A particle with magnetic charge alone is a magnetic monopole, a north pole without a south pole; none has ever been detected, and the theory of monopoles and of the dualities that relate them to ordinary charges is established physics. The Broken-Born program meets this subject three times. One of its founding ideas tried to explain the absence of monopoles and died; a weaker form of the same idea survives; and the program observes that choosing which particles count as electric is the same mathematical operation as choosing which coordinates count as position. Whether those two choices are one structure is an open conjecture. A claim found only in a popular article, that the program's second matter sector is the magnetic side of our electromagnetism, is recorded here as dead.*

## Map

The section follows [#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §11, "Polarized charge lattices: monopoles, duality frames, and the recurring matrix", which first appeared in the checkpoint's v2.0. Literature comes first, then the program's three claims in the order of §11.1–§11.3, then the popular article's claim. The number 34 appears on several pages with two different meanings: the magnetic coupling of our own electromagnetism, which is established physics (#11 §11.2), and the coupling [α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling) of the [retired soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate)'s hidden monopoles, which is dead. The pages keep them apart.

Its five pages hold [Dirac quantization and the Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger (DSZ) pairing](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) of charges, [the 't Hooft–Polyakov monopole](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/thooft-polyakov-monopole) of a broken gauge symmetry, [the Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism) by which a cooling universe makes such defects, [the monopole problem](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-problem), and the observational [flux bounds](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches).
