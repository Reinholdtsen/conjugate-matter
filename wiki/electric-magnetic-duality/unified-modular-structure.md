*This open problem asks whether two choices the program treats in parallel are parts of one structure: the vacuum's choice of which phase-space directions count as position (postulate P5), and the choice of which charges count as electric, which is a polarization of the charge lattice. The model the checkpoint points to is string theory, where T-duality and S-duality are both contained in a larger U-duality. It is open problem 8 of the checkpoint, tagged [O], and no calculation toward it exists in the repository. The toy models that met the program's first acceptance criterion did not address the related demand that the order parameter also select the electric charges.*

**Status:** Open — [O]; the U-duality analogy it invokes is [T] — #11 §11.3 and §14 item 8. Listed with the program's [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems); it qualifies [the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice).

## Key facts

- **The item** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §14 item 8): "Charge-lattice polarization: formalize §11.3's unification [O]. (The soliton-spectrum and p-localization items are closed by the §12 obituary.)"
- **The conjecture** (#11 §11.3): "Conjecture [O]: the phase-space polarization (P5) and the charge-lattice polarization are components of one enlarged modular structure — the BBT-native analog of T- and S-duality cohabiting inside U-duality in string theory [T]." BBT is the Broken-Born program ([P5: the vacuum selects a polarization](/wiki/program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection); [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality)).
- **What it would formalize.** The shared matrix [[0,1],[−1,0]] behind Born's ρ, the modular inversion Z → −Z⁻¹ and S-duality's τ_c → −1/τ_c, and the reading of a duality frame as a Lagrangian polarization of charge space ([the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice); [reciprocity as modular inversion](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion)). #11 §15 counts "the §11.3 unification generating new questions" among the program's progressive signs.
- **The electric-sublattice clause, unaddressed.** The first open item of [the handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) asked for a toy Lagrangian with "η the order parameter also selecting the electric sublattice" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), and acceptance criterion (1) of [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) asked for η "selecting both the position polarization and the hidden sector's electric description" (#14 §6). #11 §14 item 11 declares the toy criterion met without that clause, and nothing in the repository yet addresses it ([the acceptance criterion](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion)).
- **The closed sub-items** (unresolved). The parenthesis in item 8 says the soliton-spectrum and p-localization items are "closed by the §12 obituary", while §12 says the momentum-localization loophole "remains untested, but has no candidate". The checkpoint does not reconcile the two ([Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)).
- **The caution on record.** Round 2's weakest link was a coupling derived from the shared matrix, which the referee called "matrix coincidence ≠ physical identity" (`HANDOFF.md` §6). The conjecture asks for a structure behind the coincidence; it is stated, not derived.
