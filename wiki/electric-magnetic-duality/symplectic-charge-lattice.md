*The symplectic charge lattice is the program's observation that the set of allowed electric and magnetic charges has the same geometry as phase space. The Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger (DSZ) pairing of two charges is an antisymmetric whole-number form, like the pairing of position with momentum; charges that can all be described together in one Lagrangian form a "Lagrangian" half of the lattice; and deciding which particles count as electric is therefore a choice of polarization, as the program's vacuum chooses which coordinates count as position. One quarter-turn matrix generates Born reciprocity, the program's modular inversion and S-duality. The checkpoint counts this as a structural unification [D — structural; math T]; whether the two polarizations are one physical structure is open problem 8.*

**Status:** Alive — [D — structural; math T] — #11 §11.3. No script checks the charge-lattice statements. The phase-space side of the shared matrix is machine-checked: `verify/toy_coset.py` confirms that "rho is symplectic and is the quarter turn: rho^2 = -1" and that "rho acts on Z as Z -> -1/Z with fixed point Z = i" ([the toy-model scripts](/wiki/method/verification/toy-model-scripts)).

## Key facts

- **Charge space is symplectic** ([#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §11.3): "The Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger pairing ⟨q, q′⟩ = e₁g₂ − e₂g₁ ∈ 2πℤ is an antisymmetric integer form: the charge lattice is a symplectic space [17†]" ([Dirac quantization and the DSZ pairing](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization); compare [the symplectic form](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form) of phase space).
- **Lagrangian sublattice** (#11 §11.3): "Mutual locality (which charges can share one Lagrangian) is ⟨q,q′⟩ = 0: a Lagrangian sublattice."
- **A duality frame is a polarization** (#11 §11.3): "Choosing a duality frame — which particles are "electric" (fundamental) versus "magnetic" (solitonic) — is choosing a Lagrangian polarization of charge space, in the literature's own words." The phrase "in the literature's own words" carries no citation and is flagged for [the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery) ([conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts)). The phase-space counterpart is [Lagrangian subspaces and polarizations](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations).
- **One matrix, three appearances** (#11 §11.3): "S-duality acts on the complexified coupling by τ_c → −1/τ_c, generated by the same [[0,1],[−1,0]] rotation as Born's ρ and our Z → −Z⁻¹: the third appearance of one matrix in this program" ([S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality); [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity); [reciprocity as modular inversion](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion)).

```text
the matrix [[0, 1], [−1, 0]] generates
  Born reciprocity     ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x)
  modular inversion    Z → −Z⁻¹
  S-duality            τ_c → −1/τ_c
```

- **How the program counts it.** The technical abstract: the charge lattice "furnishes a second arena in which the same polarization choice recurs" (#11 §1). The assessment lists "one structural unification (polarization of phase space and of charge space as one move)" and, among the progressive signs, "the §11.3 unification generating new questions" (#11 §15). Founding supposition 11 records "the polarization identification as the program-native addition" to the established [magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family) (#11 App. A item 11). [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) lists it as alive: "DSZ pairing is symplectic; duality frame choice = Lagrangian polarization, same [[0,1],[−1,0]] generator as Born rotation and S-duality" (`HANDOFF.md` §2).
- **The critiques.** [The round-3 audit](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings): ""Charge space is symplectic" is interpretation" (Step 1). The withdrawn [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) used the identification "only as motivation ... No numerical result below depends on it" (#14 §2), and the audit concluded that the phase-space framework did no work in that proposal (Step 4(d); [the 'framework does no work' critique](/wiki/program/framework-does-no-work)). In round 2 the weakest link was the derivation of the hidden coupling α_d, which leaned on this matrix, and the referee called it "matrix coincidence ≠ physical identity" and "numerology" (`HANDOFF.md` §6; [Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)).
- **What is open.** Whether the phase-space and charge-space polarizations are components of one enlarged structure is a conjecture [O] ([open problem 8](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure)), and no toy model yet selects the electric charges ([the acceptance criterion](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion)). The popular article #12 presents the unification as "The framework's proposal", without its open status, and goes further ([Obituary: the magnetic identification (popsci-only)](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)).
