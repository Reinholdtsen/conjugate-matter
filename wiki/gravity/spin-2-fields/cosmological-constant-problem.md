*The cosmological-constant problem is the mismatch between the energy density of empty space that quantum field theory predicts, from the zero-point fluctuations and loops of matter fields, and the far smaller value that the expansion of the universe indicates. No accepted mechanism explains the gap, and bimetric gravity is no better placed than general relativity to explain it. For the program this page marks a boundary: the program's [vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity) sets to zero only the vacuum energy of the spin-2 interaction potential at the aligned point, and it does not address the vacuum energy of matter loops.*

**Status:** Established — [T] — an unsolved problem of physics, recorded here as literature; the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) cites [22S] (retrieved by live search) for the statement about bimetric theory (#11 §7.3).

## Key facts

- The "old" cosmological-constant problem, as the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) names it (#11 §7.3), is the vacuum energy of matter loops. On it, "bimetric theory is otherwise no better placed than GR [22S]", GR being general relativity.
- The size of the gap [T]: [#12](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles), a popular article with no citation and no verify/ script for the figure, says that "straightforward estimates exceed the measured value by some hundred and twenty orders of magnitude".
- The scope of the program's identity (#11 §7.3): it "cancels the spin-2 interaction potential's vacuum contribution at the aligned point — it does **not** by itself address matter-loop vacuum energy". See [the vacuum-energy identity V(1) = 2T](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity).
- A caution on reading #12: it sets the hundred-and-twenty-orders figure in the same paragraph as the program's cancellation. Per #11 §7.3 the cancellation does not reduce that gap, so the two should not be read together.
- Reference [22][S] (#11): A. Schmidt-May, M. von Strauss, "Recent developments in bimetric theory," J. Phys. A 49, 183001 (2016), arXiv:1512.00021. The [handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff)'s queue asks whether the identity survives comparison with "the Schmidt-May–von Strauss review's no-protecting-symmetry statement" (HANDOFF §8 item 5); see [open: the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison).
- In the program, observed dark energy is assigned to the τ field rather than to the spin-2 sector: "The massive-gravity sector contributes *nothing* to the cosmological constant; dark energy is left entirely to the τ sector" (#8 §2). See [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence).
