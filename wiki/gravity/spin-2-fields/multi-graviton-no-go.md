*The multi-graviton no-go theorem, proved by Boulanger, Damour, Gualtieri and Henneaux, states that several massless spin-2 fields cannot interact with one another consistently: no ghost-free, local coupling with at most two derivatives can mix them. Each massless graviton is consistent on its own, but a theory in which two of them couple to each other is not. In the program the theorem closes one route for the [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton): X has the gauge symmetry of a second massless graviton and must couple to the metric and to [conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter), so it cannot stay massless. Together with the signature obstruction, this leaves a mass as the only consistent option for X.*

**Status:** Established — [T] — a literature result, cited in the checkpoint as [8†], from model memory, and so due a source check in [the literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery). The round-3 audit read the paper's abstract and found that it supports the program's use, with the caveats "2-derivative, local, ghost-free" (`review/round3/referee/audit-notes.md` Step 1).

## Key facts

- Reference [8†] of the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) (#11): N. Boulanger, T. Damour, L. Gualtieri, M. Henneaux, "Inconsistency of interacting, multi-graviton theories," Nucl. Phys. B 597, 127 (2001), hep-th/0007220.
- The abstract, as the round-3 audit quotes it: "no consistent (ghost-free) coupling, with at most two derivatives ... that can mix the various gravitons". The audit checked it as #14's reference [2], the same paper ([round-3 citation audit](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings)).
- #6 §2 [T]: "With the gauge invariance, X is a second **massless** spin-2 — and massless multi-graviton cross-interactions are inconsistent (Boulanger–Damour–Gualtieri–Henneaux). X must couple (to g through the Siegel structure, to χ through the gate), so decoupling is not available." The gauge invariance is X's [dual diffeomorphisms](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/dual-diffeomorphisms); the gate is the [velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate) of [conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter).
- In the checkpoint's chain [D + T] (#11 §7.1): "Massless X is excluded by the multi-graviton no-go [8†]; ungauged X by Lorentz signature." The second exclusion is the [signature obstruction](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli); together they force a mass ([why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain)).
- #8 §4 repeats the exclusion in its summary of the program's answer to Born: X is a massive spin-2 field, and the massless option is excluded by this theorem ([Born's answer](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer)).
- The date: #12 says the theorem was "proved in 2000", while #11 cites the 2001 journal paper. These are the preprint year and the journal year of the same work, not a disagreement of substance.
