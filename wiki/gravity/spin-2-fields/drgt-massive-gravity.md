*dRGT massive gravity, after de Rham, Gabadadze and Tolley, is a theory of a single massive graviton whose nonlinear potential is built so that the Boulware–Deser ghost never appears; it extends the Fierz–Pauli mass term to all orders. Its potential is assembled from the matrix square root √(g⁻¹f) of the dynamical metric g and a fixed reference metric f, in a specific family of combinations [T]. With its two-metric extension by Hassan and Rosen, it is the one known consistent structure for a massive spin-2 field coupled to gravity. The program adopted this class as the health test for its [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton): X's sector is healthy if and only if the potential that gives X its mass lands in it.*

**Status:** Established — [T] — cited in the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) as [9†], from model memory and not yet source-checked. The program's health criterion built on it is tagged "[T: HR 2011]" in [#6](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes) §3 and was applied in [the tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test), which X's potential passed conditionally.

## Key facts

- Reference [9†] (#11): C. de Rham, G. Gabadadze, A. J. Tolley, "Resummation of massive gravity," Phys. Rev. Lett. 106, 231101 (2011), arXiv:1011.1232.
- #6 §3: "Massive spin-2 coupled to gravity has exactly one known consistent structure: the Fierz–Pauli tuning at quadratic order completed by a dRGT/Hassan–Rosen-class potential [T: HR 2011]." The checkpoint states the same: "The unique consistent structure for massive spin-2 is Fierz–Pauli/dRGT/Hassan–Rosen [9†, 10†, 11†]" (#11 §7.1). See [the Fierz–Pauli mass term](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli) and [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric).
- The program's criterion (#6 §3): "X is a massive spin-2 field. The geometric sector is healthy iff the potential generated by the η-selection lands in the Hassan–Rosen class; a generic potential reintroduces the Boulware–Deser ghost." The η-selection sector is the potential through which the vacuum selects its polarization, the program's Higgs sector ([the misalignment potential](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/misalignment-potential)).
- What depended on it (#6 §5): the remaining computation was to "derive the X-potential from the η-selection (J–Z) sector and test it against the dRGT coefficient family". A pass would keep the geometric core; a failure would have left the program only its phenomenological shell, gated self-interacting dark matter ([conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter)) plus Maslov quintessence ([τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence)).
- The outcome: potentials built from metrics fail and potentials built from frames pass; "the theory passes iff formulated in first-order variables" (#1 'Addendum (v0.5)'; #11 §7.2). See [the verdict: a conditional pass](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/conditional-pass).
- In frame variables the ghost-free class has "dRGT structure inherited rather than tuned" (#7 §3); see [the vielbein formulation](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation).
