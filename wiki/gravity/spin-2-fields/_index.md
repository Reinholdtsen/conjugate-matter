*A spin-2 field is a symmetric tensor field, h_μν, whose quanta carry two units of angular momentum; the graviton of general relativity is the massless case. Such a field can be given a mass, or joined by a second one, only in narrow ways, because a generic mass term or coupling adds a ghost, a mode whose energy has the wrong sign. This section collects the established results that define those narrow ways, from the Fierz–Pauli mass term to the ghost-free dRGT and Hassan–Rosen theories, because the program's momentum graviton X is held to them. Everything here is literature; the program's own claims about X are in the neighbouring sections.*

## Map

[General relativity and the graviton](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity) opens the section: the metric, the Einstein–Hilbert action, minimal coupling, linearized diffeomorphisms and the equivalence principle. X is defined by analogy with this graviton, so it comes first. [The Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Boulware–Deser ghost](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli) follows, because every massive theory after it must reduce to that mass term; the page also states the signature obstruction, which excludes a symmetric tensor without gauge symmetry. [The multi-graviton no-go theorem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go) shows that several massless gravitons cannot interact consistently, the result that excludes a massless X.

[dRGT massive gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity) is the ghost-free nonlinear completion of the Fierz–Pauli term with one dynamical metric, and the program took its class as the health criterion for X. [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) makes the second metric dynamical; its potential, a sum of coefficients βₙ times eₙ(√(g⁻¹f)), and its exchange identity are what reciprocity later acts on. [Elementary symmetric polynomials](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/elementary-symmetric-polynomials) gives the algebra of that potential, with battery tests T6 and T7, and [the vielbein formulation and wedge potentials](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation) gives its frame form, ghost-free by construction, which is the form in which the program's tuning test passed.

The last three pages are context for the program's results. [Partially massless gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity) is the bimetric parameter point with an extra gauge symmetry, and it lies inside the program's palindromic branch. [Bimetric cosmology and its instabilities](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/bimetric-cosmology) records the known instabilities of bimetric cosmologies, the heavy-spin-2 escape and the Higuchi bound, which bear on X's open stability at equal Planck masses. [The cosmological-constant problem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/cosmological-constant-problem) records the vacuum-energy problem that the program's vacuum-energy identity does not address.
