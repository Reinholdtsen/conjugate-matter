*Constrained bimetric gravity is the picture of the program's gravity that the tuning test leaves: [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric), a ghost-free theory of two interacting metrics, in which the second frame is not a free metric but is confined to the compact ten-dimensional space [Λ(d)](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian) of polarizations. The confinement makes it a predictive truncation of bimetric theory, with fewer free functions: the light direction of the compact space is the dark-energy field τ, and the massive spin-2 field is the momentum graviton X. Reciprocity then restricts the potential further, to its [palindromic branch](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch). The picture is a structural derivation [D — structural], and the standard bimetric facts it uses come from the literature [T].*

**Status:** Alive — [D — structural] in calc note #7 (`notes/debt1a-iii-tuning-test.md` §4), with bimetric facts [T] — not machine-checked; carried into the technical abstract (§1) and §7 of [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint).

## Key facts

- Calc note #7 §4 (see [The Debt-1 calc notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes)): "J carries only the 10 compact moduli of U(d)/O(d) — not a full second metric. So the 'second vielbein' E₀[J] is confined to a compact 10-dimensional moduli space."
- #7 §4 [D — structural]: the program's geometric sector is "Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity with the second frame constrained to a compact moduli space Λ(d) — a predictive truncation of bimetric theory: fewer free functions, with the light direction of the compact space being τ (the dark-energy field) and the massive spin-2 being X."
- #7 §4 [T]: "Standard bimetric facts then slot in [T]: one massless combination (our graviton, coupling to SM stress) and one massive (X)". SM is the Standard Model; its matter couples through the metric g ([Safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety)).
- Before the tuning test, calc note #6 §3 had stated the picture conditionally: the geometric sector is "bimetric gravity with the second metric's kinetic structure and couplings dictated by modular (Siegel-domain) symmetry rather than chosen by hand — if the dRGT tuning emerges from the symmetry", adding that "the modular structure explains which bimetric" (dRGT is de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley; [dRGT massive gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity)). The tuning test met the condition in frame variables ([The verdict: a conditional pass](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/conditional-pass)).
- #11 §1 (technical abstract): the chain of results "forces X to be a massive spin-2 field in the interchange-symmetric branch of Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity [10†, 11†] with the second frame confined to Λ(4)." The notes write Λ(d); #11 sets d = 4, where the lab notebook gives the dimension "d(d+1)/2 = 10 for d=4" (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §2).
- The light direction: [τ is not eaten](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten) [D — leading order]; τ is [the Maslov phase](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase) of the boundary.
- How far the truncation goes: after the palindrome and the flat-vacuum condition, calc note #8 §3 finds that the five-function freedom of the bimetric potential "collapses to two numbers" ([Spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity)).
