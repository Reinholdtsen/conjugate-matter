*The tuning test asked whether the potential that gives the [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton) its mass is free of the Boulware–Deser ghost, the extra negative-energy mode that a generic mass term for a spin-2 field carries. Ghost-free mass terms form a narrow, known family: the Fierz–Pauli term at quadratic order, completed nonlinearly by the dRGT (de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley) and Hassan–Rosen potentials. Built from metric variables, the program's potential misses that family twice; built from frame variables, it lands in the family by construction. The verdict, a conditional pass, leaves the program's gravity as Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity with the second frame confined to a compact space.*

## Map

Every page here is the program's own material, most of it derived in calc note #7 (`notes/debt1a-iii-tuning-test.md`) and restated in [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §7.2, where the test is tagged [D][V]. The established theories it is measured against are in [Spin-2 fields](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields). The pages run from the object under test, through its two halves, to the verdict and the picture that results.

[The misalignment potential](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/misalignment-potential) comes first because it is the object under test: the energy cost of a mismatch between the polarization J and the modular field Z, measured by the anticommutator {J, I[Z]}, whose block form battery test T3 checks. [The metric tier fails twice](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/metric-tier-failures) is the negative half: built from metrics, the potential gives a mass term without the Fierz–Pauli trace term and a nonlinear structure outside the Hassan–Rosen family, both machine-checked (T4, T7). [The frame tier: the symplectic vielbein](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier) is the positive half: built from frames, the potential is a wedge polynomial and ghost-free by construction, a structural claim that rests on the literature.

[The verdict: a conditional pass](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/conditional-pass) states the outcome (fail in second-order variables, pass in first-order ones) and what a failure would have left of the program. [Constrained bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric) is the picture that follows: bimetric gravity whose second frame is confined to the ten compact moduli of Λ(d), with τ as its light direction and X as its massive spin-2 field.

The last two pages concern where the passing potential should come from. [The geometric action S_H and the v0.1 action (superseded)](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/geometric-action) records the lab notebook's action, written when η was still frozen; it is superseded as a form, but S_H is the action from which the ghost-free class should be derived. [Open: deriving the wedge sector from S_H](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/wedge-reduction) is that derivation, item 1 of #11 §14 and one of the three items on which the word "theory" waits.
