*Gravity in the Broken-Born program involves two spin-2 fields, the symmetric tensor fields of the kind that carry gravity: the spacetime metric of general relativity, and a second tensor X, the momentum graviton, which carries the geometry of momentum space. A symmetry of the program's phase-space geometry gives X the gauge symmetry of a second graviton, and established consistency results then leave X one option, a mass. This section sets out the established theory of massless and massive spin-2 fields first, then the program's results on X, which were worked out as [Debt 1](/wiki/history/debts), the program's name for the missing field equation of the phase-space metric. Those results are structural derivations, partly machine-checked, and the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) states that the word "theory" still waits on its first three open problems.*

## Map

[Spin-2 fields](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields) comes first because it is the established literature that X is held to: general relativity's graviton, the Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Boulware–Deser ghost, the no-go theorem for several massless gravitons, dRGT massive gravity and Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity with their algebra and frame form, the partially massless point, bimetric cosmology and the cosmological-constant problem. Its pages are literature; where a page states how the program uses a result, it says so.

[The momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton) holds the program's chain of results on X = Re Z: its shift symmetry and dual diffeomorphisms, why it must be massive, how the choice of polarization gives it a mass, why τ is not eaten, its spectrum and its safety for ordinary matter, the open questions, the chat-only results, and the program's answer to Born's question. These are program claims; the checkpoint tags the chain [D + T] (§7.1) and the spectrum [D] (§7.5).

[The tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test) asks whether the potential that gives X its mass avoids the Boulware–Deser ghost. Potentials built from metrics fail twice, and each failure has a check in [the verification battery](/wiki/method/verification/battery) (tests T4 and T7); potentials built from frames pass, which the checkpoint calls a conditional pass. The section also keeps the superseded v0.1 action and the open derivation of the passing potential from the geometric action.

[The palindromic branch](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch) comes last because it acts on the potential that passed: reciprocity forces βₙ = β₄₋ₙ, a flat aligned vacuum then forces the wedge sector's vacuum energy to zero, and a comparison with the partially massless literature is owed before that identity can be called new.
