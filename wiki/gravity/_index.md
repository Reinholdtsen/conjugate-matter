*Gravity in the Comatter program involves two spin-2 fields, symmetric tensor fields of the kind that carry gravity: the spacetime metric of [general relativity](spin-2-fields/general-relativity.md), and a second tensor X, the [momentum graviton](momentum-graviton/_index.md), which carries the geometry of momentum space. A symmetry of the program's phase-space geometry gives X a gauge symmetry, a redundancy in the description of the kind that keeps the ordinary graviton massless, so X behaves as a second graviton. Established results exclude a [second massless graviton](spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go.md) that interacts with the first. They also exclude a field of this kind whose kinetic term, the part of the theory that governs how it propagates, lacks the gauge symmetry, because such a field carries a [ghost](spin-2-fields/ghosts.md), a mode with negative energy that makes a theory unstable. That leaves X one option, a mass. Whether the potential that gives X its mass is free of ghosts was the program's [tuning test](tuning-test/_index.md): potentials written in terms of metrics fail it, and potentials written in terms of [frames](spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation.md), the sets of reference axes from which a metric is built, pass it conditionally. [Reciprocity](../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md) then restricts that potential to a [palindromic branch](palindromic-branch/_index.md), whose coefficients read the same forwards and backwards. On that branch, requiring a flat vacuum in which the two metrics coincide forces the vacuum energy of the spin-2 interaction potential to zero. This section sets out the established theory of massless and massive spin-2 fields first, then the program's results on X, worked out as [the field-equation problem](../history/main-problems.md), the program's name for the missing field equation of the phase-space metric. Those results are structural derivations, partly machine-checked, and the [checkpoint](../history/documents/checkpoint.md) states that the word "theory" still waits on its first three open problems.*

## Map

[Spin-2 fields](spin-2-fields/_index.md) comes first because it is the established literature that X is held to. It runs from [general relativity's graviton](spin-2-fields/general-relativity.md) and its gauge symmetry, the [Planck units](spin-2-fields/planck-units.md) that set its coupling, [the equivalence principle](spin-2-fields/equivalence-principle.md) and the [Yukawa range](spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces.md) of a massive mediator, through [ghosts and perturbative instabilities](spin-2-fields/ghosts.md), [the Fierz–Pauli mass term](spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli.md), whose special weight keeps a massive spin-2 field free of ghosts, [the signature obstruction](spin-2-fields/signature-obstruction.md), which requires the gauge-invariant kinetic term, and [the multi-graviton no-go theorem](spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go.md), to the ghost-free nonlinear theories, [dRGT massive gravity](spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity.md) (de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley) and [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric.md), with their algebra and frame form. It ends with the literature behind the program's later results: [the partially massless point](spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity.md), [bimetric cosmology](spin-2-fields/bimetric-cosmology.md) and the cosmological-constant problem. Its pages are literature; where a page states how the program uses a result, it gives the program's tags.

[The momentum graviton X](momentum-graviton/_index.md) holds the program's chain of results on X = Re Z: its shift symmetry and dual diffeomorphisms, why it must be massive, how the choice of [polarization](../phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations.md) gives it a mass, why [τ is not eaten](momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten.md), its spectrum, its safety for ordinary matter and the laboratory bound on its range, the open questions, X in a closed universe, the chat-only results, and the program's answer to [Born's question](../program/borns-question.md). These are program claims; the checkpoint tags the chain [D + T] ([§7.1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#71-the-chain-d--t)) and the spectrum [D] ([§7.5](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#75-spectrum-d)).

[The tuning test](tuning-test/_index.md) asks whether the potential that gives X its mass avoids the Boulware–Deser ghost. Potentials built from metrics fail twice, and each failure has a numerical check in [the verification battery](../method/verification/battery.md): their mass term lacks the Fierz–Pauli trace term (test T4), and their nonlinear structure lies outside the Hassan–Rosen family (test T7, which checks the power sum p₄). Potentials built from frames pass, which calc note [#7](../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md) calls a conditional pass; the checkpoint tags the test [D][V] ([§7.2](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#72-the-tuning-test-dv)). The section also keeps the superseded v0.1 action and the open derivation of the passing potential from the geometric action.

[The palindromic branch](palindromic-branch/_index.md) comes last because it acts on the potential that passed: reciprocity forces βₙ = β₄₋ₙ, a flat aligned vacuum then forces the wedge sector's vacuum energy to zero, and a comparison with the partially massless literature is still needed before that identity can be called new.
