*The palindromic branch is the family of [Hassan–Rosen](../spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric.md) bimetric potentials whose five coefficients read the same forwards and backwards, βₙ = β₄₋ₙ. The program arrives there because [Born reciprocity](../../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md), on the broken vacuum, acts as an exchange of the potential's two frames, and a potential invariant under that exchange must be palindromic. Inside the branch a four-line algebraic identity shows that a flat aligned vacuum gives the spin-2 interaction sector zero vacuum energy. Both results stand as program derivations. Whether the identity is new is open: the branch also contains the known [partially massless point](../spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity.md), and a different cancellation of vacuum energy by exchange symmetry is already in the literature.*

## Map

[The palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](palindrome.md) comes first. It takes the frame-tier potential that passed [the tuning test](../tuning-test/_index.md), a Hassan–Rosen potential of two frames with five coefficients, and shows what reciprocity does to it: on the broken vacuum reciprocity exchanges the two frames, the exchange reverses the order of the five terms, and an invariant potential must have βₙ = β₄₋ₙ. The page sets out the potential and the standard algebra of the exchange, [calc note #8](../../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md)'s one-sentence argument that reciprocity is the exchange [D], and what follows: the interchange-symmetric branch, the parameter count, the parities and the [equal Planck masses](../spin-2-fields/planck-units.md#equal-planck-masses-in-the-bimetric-sector). The branch itself is known in the literature, where it contains the partially massless point. [#8](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md)'s early claim that the literature had treated the branch only as "an ad hoc simplification" is kept there as a superseded section.

[The vacuum-energy identity V(1) = 2T](vacuum-energy-identity.md) follows from the palindrome at the aligned point, where the two frames coincide. The page works through #8's four lines: the values of the [elementary symmetric polynomials](../spin-2-fields/elementary-symmetric-polynomials.md) at the unit matrix and their linear coefficients, the identity V(1) = 2T, the flatness condition V(1) = T, and the conclusion T = 0 and V(1) = 0. It is tagged [D][V]; [battery tests T5 and T6](../../method/verification/battery.md) check the arithmetic (T5 in exact integers, T6 numerically), not the palindrome or the flatness condition. Its scope excludes the vacuum energy of matter loops ([the cosmological-constant problem](../spin-2-fields/cosmological-constant-problem.md)), and the page lists the result's earlier names, from "Λ theorem" to "identity".

[Open: the partially-massless comparison (OP2)](pm-comparison.md) closes the section. It is the literature check that the identity's novelty waits on: a convention-careful comparison with [partially massless bimetric gravity](../spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity.md) [24S], whose parameter point lies inside the branch at α = 1; with Hossenfelder's exchange-symmetric bimetric theory [25S], which cancels vacuum energy by a different mechanism; and with the Schmidt-May–von Strauss review's no-protecting-symmetry statement [22S]. It also asks whether reciprocity is the origin of the partially massless symmetry. It is open problem 2 of [the checkpoint](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) and part of item 1 of [the current queue](../../status/open-problems.md), and the earlier "standalone result" claim is kept there as a superseded section.

All three are program pages, the last step of the program's results on [gravity and the momentum graviton](../_index.md). The established physics they use, Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity, elementary symmetric polynomials, partially massless gravity and the cosmological-constant problem, is in [Spin-2 fields](../spin-2-fields/_index.md).
