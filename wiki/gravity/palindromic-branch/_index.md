*The palindromic branch is the family of Hassan–Rosen bimetric potentials whose five coefficients read the same forwards and backwards, βₙ = β₄₋ₙ. The program arrives there because Born reciprocity acts on its broken vacuum as an exchange of the two frames, and a potential invariant under that exchange must be palindromic. Inside this branch a short algebraic identity shows that a flat aligned vacuum gives the spin-2 sector zero vacuum energy. Both results stand as program derivations; whether the identity is new is open, because the branch also contains the known partially massless point.*

**Stands:** [the palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome), [the vacuum-energy identity V(1) = 2T](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity) · **Died:** nothing yet · **Open:** [the partially massless comparison (open problem 2)](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison)

## Map

[The palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome) comes first. It is the constraint that reciprocity places on the frame-tier potential that passed [the tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test), derived in the Debt-1 notes and tagged [D], and it identifies the program's gravity sector with the interchange-symmetric branch of bimetric gravity.

[The vacuum-energy identity V(1) = 2T](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity) follows from the palindrome at the aligned point: with a flat aligned vacuum it forces T = 0 and V(1) = 0. It is tagged [D][V], with the integer identity checked by battery test T5, and its scope excludes the vacuum energy of matter loops.

[Open: the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison) closes the section. The branch contains the partially massless point, Hossenfelder's exchange-symmetric bimetric theory cancels vacuum energy by a different mechanism, and a convention-careful comparison is owed before the identity can be presented as new. It is open problem 2 of the checkpoint and part of item 1 of the current queue.

All three are program pages, the last step of the program's results on [gravity and the momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity). The established physics they use, Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity, elementary symmetric polynomials, partially massless gravity and the cosmological-constant problem, is in [Spin-2 fields](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields).
