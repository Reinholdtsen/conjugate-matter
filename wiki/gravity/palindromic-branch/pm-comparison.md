*The partially-massless comparison is a literature check the program owes: a detailed, convention-careful comparison of its palindromic branch and its vacuum-energy identity with partially massless bimetric gravity and with Hossenfelder's exchange-symmetric bimetric theory. It is open problem 2 of the checkpoint and part of the first item of the current work queue. Until it is done, the identity is presented as adjacent to known results rather than as new. A second question rides on it: whether reciprocity is the origin of the partially massless symmetry.*

**Status:** Open — [O] — [#11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §14 item 2; listed with the program's [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems) and in item 1 of the current queue (`CLAUDE.md`, "Since the handoff"). No calculation for it is in the repository yet.

## Key facts

- The item (#11 §14 item 2): "**PM-adjacency:** detailed comparison of the palindromic branch with partially-massless bimetric [24S]; is reciprocity the origin of the PM symmetry? Convention-careful check of the vacuum-energy identity against [24S, 25S] — the queued novelty audit [O]." PM means partially massless.
- What it qualifies: [the palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome) and [the vacuum-energy identity V(1) = 2T](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity), both alive.
- First comparison (#11 §7.4 (a)): the [partially massless point](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity) β₀ = 3α⁻²β₂, β₁ = β₃ = 0, β₄ = 3α²β₂ lies inside the palindromic branch at α = 1. It sits at Λ ≠ 0 with c undetermined, while the program's identity selects a different point of the same branch (β₁ ≠ 0 generically, β₀ = −4β₁ − 3β₂).
- Second comparison (#11 §7.4 (b)): Hossenfelder's exchange-symmetric bimetric theory, "where twin-sector source terms cancel identically [25S]". The program's identity "is sector-internal (no twin matter required) and, to our current knowledge, distinct". Reference [25][S]: S. Hossenfelder, "A bi-metric theory with exchange symmetry," Phys. Rev. D 78, 044015 (2008), arXiv:0807.2838.
- Third comparison (HANDOFF §8 item 5): "the Schmidt-May–von Strauss review's no-protecting-symmetry statement" [22S]; see [the cosmological-constant problem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/cosmological-constant-problem).
- The downgrade (#11 §7.4): the novelty claim went "from 'likely standalone result' to 'requires detailed comparison with the PM and exchange-symmetric literature'". The checkpoint calls this "the selection-versus-derivation bias check of §2 operating as intended" ([claim tiers and selection bias](/wiki/method/claim-tiers)).
- History of the claim: #8 §2 flagged the literature check and called the result, if unnoticed, "a standalone publishable observation"; #8 §5 listed the check as item (v) of Debt 1's open residue; #9 §5 and §10 item 8 kept it open, while #9 §11 counted the identity among "two results that stand on their own". #11 §15 now counts "one identity awaiting a novelty verdict".
- The queue: [the handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) ranked this comparison fifth (HANDOFF §8 item 5). Since the handoff, items 5–7 together form item 1 of the current queue: this comparison, [the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery) and [the phase-space-diameter note](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter) (`CLAUDE.md`).
