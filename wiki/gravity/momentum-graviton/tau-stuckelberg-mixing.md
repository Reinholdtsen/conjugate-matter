*This open problem asks how strongly the dark-energy field τ mixes, through the potential, with the Stückelberg modes: the would-be Goldstone fields that the momentum graviton X absorbs when it becomes massive. At leading order τ is not among the absorbed modes ([τ is not eaten](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten)), but the matrix that mixes the two sectors has not been computed. Calc note #8 and the superseded v1 paper (#9) expect the mixing to be small because τ lives on a compact space; that expectation is not backed by a calculation. It is item 6 of the open problems in [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §14 [O].*

**Status:** Open — [O] — item 6 of #11 §14; logged in `notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md` §3 and §5; not computed, no `verify/` script. Listed in [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).

- Calc note #8 §3 (see [The Debt-1 calc notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes)): "(Residual τ–Stückelberg mixing through the potential: logged, expected small by compactness.)"
- #8 §5 lists it among the "Open residue, none of it existential" of Debt 1, as "(iii) τ–Stückelberg mixing matrix".
- The superseded v1 paper, #9 §10 item 5: "τ–Stückelberg mixing matrix; expected small by compactness [O]."
- What it qualifies: the leading-order result [τ is not eaten](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten) [D — leading order], and through it the identification of τ as the light field of [constrained bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric). The modes in question are those absorbed in [the gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs).
