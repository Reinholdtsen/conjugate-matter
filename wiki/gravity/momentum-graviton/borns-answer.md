*Born's answer is the program's summary of what its gravity results say about the question Max Born raised in 1938, which calc note #8 states as "what is the dynamics of momentum-space geometry?" ([Born's question](/wiki/program/borns-question)). In this program, momentum-space geometry is carried by X = Re Z, a massive spin-2 field that changes sign under reciprocity, with two free parameters and no vacuum energy from its interaction potential. Conjugate matter couples to X through the dual stress, while ordinary matter feels it only at gravitational strength and over a short range, which the program gives as the reason no second metric has been observed. The statement stands as a structural result: [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §15 holds back the word "theory" until open problems 1–3 are settled, and the founding supposition that momentum space has its own gravity is recorded as "vindicated, transformed".*

**Status:** Alive — a summary statement with no tag of its own; it assembles results tagged [D + T], [D — structural], [D] and [D][V] (listed below) — calc note #8 §5 calls its open residue "none of it existential", but #11 §15 makes "theory" wait on §14 items 1–3.

## Key facts

- #11 §7, closing: "Born's answer, as this program states it: momentum-space geometry is dynamical — a reciprocity-odd massive spin-2, its mass born of the polarization choice, its dynamics the reciprocity-symmetric branch of bimetric gravity with the second frame confined to compact Λ(4), its wedge vacuum energy zero by identity. Momentum space gravitates, massively and invisibly to everything not polarized its way — which is simultaneously why the symmetry is real and why its second metric was never seen."
- Calc note #8 §4 (see [The Debt-1 calc notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes)): "Momentum-space geometry is carried by X = Re Z, the reciprocity-odd partner of the metric. It is a massive spin-2 field … Its self-consistent dynamics is Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity restricted to the reciprocity-symmetric branch with the second frame confined to the compact moduli space Λ(d): two free parameters, vacuum energy exactly zero by the palindrome theorem, coupled to conjugate matter through the dual stress (the gate) and to ordinary matter only at gravitational strength and short range."
- Where each part is derived: massive, never massless — [Why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain) [D + T]; the mass from the polarization choice — [The gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs) [D — structural]; the second frame confined to Λ(4) — [Constrained bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric) [D — structural]; the reciprocity-symmetric branch — [The palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome) [D][V]; two parameters and odd parity — [Spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity) [D]; short range — [Safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety) [D + T].
- On the vacuum energy: #8's "palindrome theorem" is the identity #11 §7.3 states [D][V] ([The vacuum-energy identity V(1) = 2T](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity)). #11 limits its scope: it "does not by itself address matter-loop vacuum energy", and its novelty awaits [the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison).
- The question as a debt: the lab notebook's first open problem was "The field equation for H beyond minisuperspace — Born's 88-year debt" (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §11). Calc note #8 §5 declares "Debt 1: structurally paid", with an open residue, "none of it existential" ([The three debts](/wiki/history/debts)).
- #11 Appendix A, item 10: "Momentum space has its own gravity — vindicated, transformed: dynamical, massive, reciprocity-odd (§7)." ([Founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions))
- What remains before "theory": #11 §15, "the word 'theory' still waits on items 1–3" of §14: [deriving the wedge sector from S_H](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/wedge-reduction), the partially-massless comparison, and [the Lorentzian dressing](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing).
