*The gravitational Higgs mechanism is how the momentum graviton X acquires its mass in this program. The vacuum value of the modular field, ⟨Z⟩ = i·g, sits at X = 0, and the [dual-diffeomorphism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/dual-diffeomorphisms) shifts move it, so the vacuum's selection of a polarization spontaneously breaks those gauge symmetries. As gauge bosons do in the ordinary [Higgs mechanism](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-and-higgs), X absorbs the would-be Goldstone modes of the broken symmetry and becomes a massive spin-2 field with five polarizations; its mass M_X is the polarization-breaking scale. The mechanism is a structural derivation [D — structural], and the sources fix no numerical value for M_X.*

**Status:** Alive — [D — structural] in calc note #6 (`notes/debt1a-ii-X-classification.md` §3); step (iii) of [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §7.1's chain, tagged [D + T] — not machine-checked. Whether the resulting mass term is free of ghosts is [the tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test).

## Key facts

- Calc note #6 §3 [D — structural] (see [The Debt-1 calc notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes)): "The mass cannot be written by hand (shift symmetry) — but it doesn't need to be. The vacuum ⟨Z⟩ = i·g sits at X = 0 and is moved by the shifts: the polarization selection (η-emergence) spontaneously breaks the local dual diffeomorphisms." The polarization selection is postulate [P5](/wiki/program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection); η-emergence is [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime).
- #6 §3: "Gravitational Higgs mechanism: the four would-be Goldstones π_μ of the broken λ-gauge are eaten; X becomes a massive spin-2 with its five polarizations, mass M_X = the polarization-breaking scale."
- #11 §7.1, step (iii): "The polarization choice spontaneously breaks the dual diffs; X eats the four Goldstones and emerges massive, M_X = the polarization-breaking scale. The unique consistent structure for massive spin-2 is Fierz–Pauli/dRGT/Hassan–Rosen [9†, 10†, 11†]." dRGT is de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley ([dRGT massive gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity)); the † marks memory citations not yet checked against the source.
- What is eaten is worded two ways, and no document reconciles them: #6 counts "the four would-be Goldstones π_μ", while calc note #8 says the Higgsing "eats the vector π_μ and the longitudinal scalar of the λ-gradient sector" (`notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md` §3). #11 §7.1 follows #6.
- The dark-energy field is not among the eaten modes: [τ is not eaten](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten) [D — leading order].
- The value of M_X: #11 §7.5 gives M_X ∝ 2(β₁+β₂) and fixes no number ([Spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity)).
- The name: the label "gravitational Higgs" is used in #6 §3, in the superseded v1 paper (#9 §5) and in the withdrawn soliton manuscript (#14 §1; [The soliton candidate (retired)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate)). #11 §7.1 describes the mechanism without the label.
