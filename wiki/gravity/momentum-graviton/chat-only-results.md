*These are two results from the program's original claude.ai conversation that no project document derives; the [handoff document](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) lists them in its §4, among the results that exist only in the chat, so that they are not lost. The first says gravity has two independent charges: ordinary stress-energy is the source of the massless graviton, and dual stress, which measures how energy is structured across momentum, is the source of the momentum graviton X. The second compares the program's field equations with those of general relativity: the slaved momentum metric is algebraic, the dual Newtonian limit is a Yukawa potential in momentum, which is the velocity gate, and X waves are massive with a frequency floor. Both are conjectures: no artifact derives them and no script checks them.*

**Status:** Conjecture (chat-only) — no tags in the source — recorded only in `HANDOFF.md` §4; no derivation in any artifact, no `verify/` script. Listed in [Conjectures: chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures).

## Key facts

- Two gravitational charges (`HANDOFF.md` §4, verbatim): "ordinary stress-energy (sources massless graviton) vs dual stress 'how energy is structured across momentum' (sources X); independent numbers; equivalence principle intact; whether invariant length λ and dual charge lock together = 'dual equivalence principle' open question."
- The field-equation comparison, which `HANDOFF.md` §4 titles "GR-vs-conjugate equation comparison" (GR being general relativity), verbatim: "slaved layer g̃ = g⁻¹ is algebraic (Einstein's equation does double duty); dual Newtonian limit = Yukawa in momentum = the gate; X-waves massive, 5 polarizations, frequency floor."
- Parts with a documented counterpart: the slaved momentum metric g̃ = g⁻¹ is corollary 3 of the compatibility theorem (calc note #5, `notes/debt1a-geometric-core.md` §A; [Corollaries: unimodularity, no B-field, slaved momentum metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries)); X's coupling to conjugate matter through the dual stress T̃ is in §E of the same note ([Dual stress T̃](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress)); the gate as X exchange is calc note #6 §4 ([The gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism)); and a massive X "with its five polarizations" is #6 §3 ([The gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs)).
- Parts with no counterpart in any artifact: dual stress as an independent second gravitational charge, the "dual equivalence principle", the dual Newtonian limit as a Yukawa potential in momentum, and the frequency floor of X waves.
- The invariant length λ is the one of conjugate special relativity ([The invariant-length shell and Planck closure](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell)), where the equivalence principle is read as the dual relativity principle ([The equivalence principle as dual relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/equivalence-principle)).
- The ordinary-gravity side of the comparison is [General relativity and the graviton](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity) [T].
