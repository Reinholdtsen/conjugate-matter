*This result says that the [dark-energy field τ](../../cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence.md) is not one of the modes the [momentum graviton X](_index.md) absorbs when it becomes massive. τ is an angle on [the Lagrangian Grassmannian](../../phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian.md) Λ(d), the compact space of all the ways of splitting phase space into position and momentum: it is the [Maslov phase](../../phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase.md) of the vacuum's [polarization](../../phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations.md). The modes X absorbs belong to a different sector, the gauge directions of the broken [dual diffeomorphisms](dual-diffeomorphisms.md), which shift X; constant shifts of X do not generate the compact directions in which τ lies. So τ survives the [gravitational Higgs mechanism](gravitational-higgs.md) as the light field the program's [dark-energy](../../cosmology/dark-energy/_index.md) sector needs. [Calc note #6](../../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md) flagged the point as open; calc note [#8](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md) derived it at leading order [D — leading order]. How strongly τ still mixes with the absorbed modes through the potential has not been computed and is [an open problem](tau-stuckelberg-mixing.md).*

**Status:** Alive at leading order — [D — leading order] in calc note #8 ([`notes/beta-coefficients.md` §3](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#3-spectrum-parameters-selection-structure-d--t)), carried by [checkpoint #11](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§7.5](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#75-spectrum-d), a section tagged [D] — an argument from where each mode comes from, not a computation of the mixing; not machine-checked. The residual mixing is [Open: τ–Stückelberg mixing (OP6)](tau-stuckelberg-mixing.md), item 6 of [#11 §14](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems).

## The statement

Calc note #8 ([the field-equation calc notes](../../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md)), [§3](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#3-spectrum-parameters-selection-structure-d--t):

> **τ is not eaten** [D — leading order]: the Higgsing of the dual diffeomorphisms eats the vector π_μ and the *longitudinal scalar of the λ-gradient sector*. τ is a J-modulus on the compact Λ(d), a different sector with different origin; it survives as the light field. (Residual τ–Stückelberg mixing through the potential: logged, expected small by compactness.)

## The two kinds of mode

### What X absorbs

When the vacuum selects a polarization, it breaks the local shifts of X, δX_μν = ∂₍μλ_ν₎, and X absorbs the would-be Goldstone modes of that broken gauge symmetry, becoming a massive spin-2 field ([the gravitational Higgs mechanism](gravitational-higgs.md)). These modes belong to the gauge parameter λ_μ. Calc note [#6](../../../notes/x-classification.md) calls them "the four would-be Goldstones π_μ of the broken λ-gauge" ([§3](../../../notes/x-classification.md#3-the-mass-mechanism-and-the-endpoint-d--structural)); calc note #8 calls them "the vector π_μ and the *longitudinal scalar of the λ-gradient sector*" ([§3](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#3-spectrum-parameters-selection-structure-d--t)), and no document reconciles the two wordings. Written as explicit fields, would-be Goldstone modes of this kind are [Stückelberg fields](../../symmetry-breaking/stuckelberg-fields.md) [T] ([Goldstone modes and the Higgs mechanism](../../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-and-higgs.md)), and #11 calls them "the Stückelberg sector".

### What τ is

Under the corrected postulate [P2′](../../program/postulates/p2-prime.md) the vacuum chooses a real structure J, which fixes which directions of phase space count as position, and the possible choices form the Lagrangian Grassmannian: "the vacuum spontaneously selects a real structure J (an involution anticommuting with I), and η ≡ HJ. The space of H-compatible real structures on (ℝ^{2d}, I) is {J} ≅ U(d)/O(d) = Λ(d)" ([#5 §B](../../../notes/geometric-core.md#b-the-inconsistency-and-the-forced-repair-d--structural)). Λ(d) is compact, of dimension "d(d+1)/2 = 10 for d=4" ([#1 §2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core)).

τ is one coordinate on that space, the angle around its Maslov circle ([#1 §2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core)):

```text
τ ≡ f · arg det²(·)
```

[#11 §6](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field) places it on the boundary of the Siegel domain: "the dark-energy field τ is the Maslov phase of the boundary variable" ([τ as the Maslov phase of the boundary](../../phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase.md)). A coordinate on the space of J's is what #8 calls "a J-modulus", and the moduli of J together make up what #11 calls the "compact J-sector".

The two kinds of mode therefore have different origins. The absorbed modes come from the gauge parameter of the shifts of X, which move X = Re Z through the non-compact [Siegel domain](../../phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain.md). τ is a coordinate of the choice of J: changing it moves the polarization around the Maslov circle of the compact Λ(d).

## The argument

Calc note #6 stated the expectation and left it as a check: "(The Λ(d) moduli, τ included, are not the eaten modes — they carry the compact phase directions, which constant shifts do not generate; verifying τ survives un-eaten is flagged [O].)" ([§3](../../../notes/x-classification.md#3-the-mass-mechanism-and-the-endpoint-d--structural)).

Calc note #8 settled it at leading order with the same distinction: the absorbed modes are those of "the λ-gradient sector", while "τ is a J-modulus on the compact Λ(d), a different sector with different origin; it survives as the light field" ([§3](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#3-spectrum-parameters-selection-structure-d--t)). The argument rests on where each mode comes from. The modes a gauge field absorbs are the would-be Goldstone modes of its own broken gauge symmetry, here the dual diffeomorphisms [T]; a mode from another sector can enter only through mixing, and that is what "leading order" sets aside.

#8 states what is set aside in the same passage. Through the potential the two sectors can still mix, and #8 records that mixing only as "logged, expected small by compactness". No calculation backs the expectation: the τ–Stückelberg mixing matrix is open item 6 of [#11 §14](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) ([Open: τ–Stückelberg mixing (OP6)](tau-stuckelberg-mixing.md)).

## Why it matters

τ is the program's dark-energy field: "τ is hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle" ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](../../cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence.md)). That role needs τ to be light. The [lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) assumed it from the start: "that τ is the *lightest* modulus, with the other nine directions heavy, is assumed **[P]**" ([#1 §2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core)). The tuning test's picture builds the same split into its geometry, [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](../spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric.md) with the second frame confined to Λ(d), "with the light direction of the compact space being τ (the dark-energy field) and the massive spin-2 being X" ([#7 §4](../../../notes/tuning-test.md#4-the-picture-that-emerges-constrained-bimetric-gravity-d--structural); [constrained bimetric gravity](../tuning-test/constrained-bimetric.md)).

If τ had been among the modes X absorbs, it would have become part of the massive spin-2 field instead of a light field of its own [T]. The result shows, at leading order, that the mechanism giving X its mass leaves τ alone. It does not explain why τ is light: the lightness itself remains the postulate above, and the origin of τ's potential is open ([Open: the origin of the ρ-preserving potential](../../symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential.md)).

## What remains open

- **The residual mixing.** The τ–Stückelberg mixing matrix, open item 6 of [#11 §14](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) ([OP6](tau-stuckelberg-mixing.md)).
- **The other nine moduli.** At d = 4, Λ(d) has ten directions: "Λ(d) σ-modes (10: τ plus nine, of which inverse-Higgs constraints likely eliminate most [O])" ([#5 §D](../../../notes/geometric-core.md#d-quadratic-spectrum-and-the-ghost-question-partial); [inverse-Higgs constraints](../../symmetry-breaking/inverse-higgs-constraints.md)). #6 places all the Λ(d) moduli outside the absorbed modes; what happens to the nine is "(4) inverse-Higgs accounting of the nine heavy moduli" in calc note #5's open list ([§F](../../../notes/geometric-core.md#f-status-after-tonight)), a question no later document takes up ([Open: field content of the broken vacuum](../../phase-space/modular-field/field-content.md)).
- **Lorentzian signature.** The mass mechanism belongs to what calc note #6 calls "the Euclidean-rigorous skeleton" ([§3](../../../notes/x-classification.md#3-the-mass-mechanism-and-the-endpoint-d--structural)); the extension to spacetime signature is open item 3 ([Open: Lorentzian dressing of the Euclidean skeleton (OP3)](../../phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing.md)).

The calc notes write Λ(d), counting its dimension at d = 4; #11 writes Λ(d) in [§6](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field) and Λ(4) in [§7](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton). At d = 4 these are the same space.
