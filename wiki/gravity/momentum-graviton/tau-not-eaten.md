*This result says that the dark-energy field τ is not one of the modes the momentum graviton X absorbs when it becomes massive. τ is a coordinate on the compact space [Λ(d)](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian) of polarizations, its [Maslov phase](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase), while the absorbed modes come from the broken dual-diffeomorphism shifts, and constant shifts of X do not generate the compact directions. So τ survives the [gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs) as the light field that the program's dark-energy sector needs. Calc note #6 flagged the point as open [O]; calc note #8 derived it at leading order [D — leading order], and the residual mixing of τ with the absorbed modes through the potential is an open problem.*

**Status:** Alive at leading order — [D — leading order] in calc note #8 (`notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md` §3), carried by [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §7.5 [D] — not machine-checked. The residual mixing is [Open: τ–Stückelberg mixing](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-stuckelberg-mixing), item 6 of #11 §14.

## Key facts

- Calc note #6 §3, flagging the point (see [The Debt-1 calc notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes)): "(The Λ(d) moduli, τ included, are not the eaten modes — they carry the compact phase directions, which constant shifts do not generate; verifying τ survives un-eaten is flagged [O].)"
- Calc note #8 §3, "τ is not eaten [D — leading order]": "the Higgsing of the dual diffeomorphisms eats the vector π_μ and the longitudinal scalar of the λ-gradient sector. τ is a J-modulus on the compact Λ(d), a different sector with different origin; it survives as the light field. (Residual τ–Stückelberg mixing through the potential: logged, expected small by compactness.)"
- #11 §7.5 [D]: "τ un-eaten (compact J-sector, not the Stückelberg sector)."
- Its role: in [constrained bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric) the compact space's light direction is "τ (the dark-energy field)" (calc note #7 §4). τ's dynamics as dark energy are on [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence).
- τ is one of ten moduli: calc note #5 lists "Λ(d) σ-modes (10: τ plus nine, of which inverse-Higgs constraints likely eliminate most [O])" (`notes/debt1a-geometric-core.md` §D). What happens to the other nine is [Open: field content of the broken vacuum](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content).
- The lab notebook's v0.6 addendum records "τ confirmed un-eaten at leading order (it lives in the compact J-sector, not the Stückelberg sector)".
