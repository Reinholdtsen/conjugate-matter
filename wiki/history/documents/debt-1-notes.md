*The Debt-1 calc notes are four working notes, artifacts #5 to #8, on the most severe of the lab notebook's open problems: what equations govern the geometry of momentum space. The first, written overnight, showed that the original postulates left the program with no content beyond [general relativity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity) and one model of [self-interacting dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter), repaired them, and gathered the geometry into a single [modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z); on the way it found the mechanism of the [velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate). The second showed that the field carrying momentum-space geometry must be a [second graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), and a massive one. The third tested whether that field avoids the [Boulware–Deser ghost](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli), the instability that generic massive-graviton theories carry, and found that it does when the field's potential is written in [frame variables](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier), sets of local reference axes from which a metric is assembled; written in metric variables it fails, and the note argues that the frame formulation is the natural one. The fourth used [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity) to constrain the remaining coefficients, leaving two free parameters, and to cancel the sector's vacuum energy. Together they closed Debt 1 in structure; their results are now stated in checkpoint #11 §5–§8, and the battery checks their explicit algebra.*

**Status:** Record, final for its scope — the notes tag their own results ("[D — rigorous]", "[D — structural]", "[D + T]"); the battery T1–T7 in `verify/battery.py` checks the explicit algebra [V], which #11 §2 calls [Tier 1](/wiki/method/claim-tiers#tier-1-explicit-algebra) — `HANDOFF.md` §3 marks all four "final", and #8 "final — closes Debt 1 (structural)"; the root `CLAUDE.md` files them with the audit trail, "superseded as reference but kept". The files are unchanged since their import on 2026-10-19 (commit 8d588f6; moved to `notes/` in 008c3ee).

## What the notes are

Debt 1 was the first item of [the lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s open problems: "The field equation for H beyond minisuperspace — Born's 88-year debt" (#1 §11), H being the [generalized metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures) on phase space. It is the question [Born's question](/wiki/program/borns-question) leaves: if position and momentum are symmetric, what is the dynamics of the geometry of momentum space? See [the three debts](/wiki/history/debts).

The work came in four chunks, each a working note: Chunk A (`notes/debt1a-geometric-core.md`, #5), A-ii (`notes/debt1a-ii-X-classification.md`, #6), A-iii (`notes/debt1a-iii-tuning-test.md`, #7) and A-iv (`notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md`, #8). The notes are undated; they follow the Debt-3 notes in `HANDOFF.md` §3's creation order, and the notebook's addenda v0.3 to v0.6 summarize them, one each. #5 calls itself an "Overnight working note", and its addendum is headed "overnight".

Each note ends with a list of what it settled and what it left open, and the next note takes up the first open item. The chain therefore reads as one argument: the axioms are repaired (#5), the new field is classified (#6), its potential is [tested](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test) (#7), and the potential's coefficients are fixed (#8).

## Their standing

[The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) marks all four "final" and describes each: #5 "compatibility theorem proof; P2→P2′ inconsistency + repair; Siegel/modular field identification; gate mechanism = X-exchange (pays Debt 2's mechanism)"; #6 "shift symmetry; X = second graviton; massless/ungauged excluded; massive spin-2 via Higgsed dual diffs"; #7 "{J,I[Z]} blocks computed; metric tier fails FP twice; frame tier (HR wedge class) passes; constrained-bimetric picture", FP being Fierz–Pauli and HR [Hassan–Rosen](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric); #8 "palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ; V(1) = 2T ⇒ Λ_wedge = 0; parameters 5→2; spectrum/parities" (`HANDOFF.md` §3).

The wiki treats the calc notes as "final for their own scope" (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`). Their results are restated, with later tags and corrections, in #11 §5 (the compatibility theorem), §6 (the modular field), §7 (the massive momentum graviton) and §8 (the gate's mechanism), and the wiki cites #11 first ([the checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint)).

## #5, debt1a: the geometric core

"Debt 1, Chunk A — the geometric core, linearized groundwork", subtitled "(plus an unplanned payment on Debt 2)". Status: "two rigorous results, one axiom correction forced, one mechanism identified, one decisive computation defined but not completed."

- **§A, the compatibility theorem [D — rigorous].** With ω and η both in standard form, the positive H satisfying (η⁻¹H)² = +1 and (ω⁻¹H)² = −1 "are exactly" H = diag(g, g⁻¹), with a short block proof and its corollaries: det H = 1, no Kalb–Ramond analog, and "The momentum metric is slaved: g̃ = g⁻¹ pointwise" ([the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem); [its corollaries](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries)).
- **§B, the inconsistency and the forced repair [D — structural].** Under the original P2, with ω and η rigid, the compatible polarizations are "**discrete** — the 2^d coordinate-type splittings, GL(d)-rigid": no [Lagrangian Grassmannian](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian), no [Maslov circle](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase), "**no τ field**", and no geometric origin for the gate. "Call this **Branch R** (rigid): BBT reduces to GR + one bespoke SIDM model", BBT being the Broken-Born theory, GR general relativity and SIDM self-interacting dark matter. The repair, P2′: "Freeze ω only — that is genuinely ℏ — and let **η be emergent: the order parameter itself.**" The "Program-nontriviality theorem": "BBT has content beyond GR + SIDM **iff** η is emergent." See [Original P2 (superseded)](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2), [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime) and [the nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem). The word "discrete" is queried in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 1: for d ≥ 2 such polarizations form continuous families, though the conclusion of no Maslov circle survives.
- **§C, the modular field [D — structural; math facts standard].** Positive H compatible with ω alone form the Siegel domain, coordinatized by Z = X + iY: "**Im Z = Y = g**", "**Re Z = X**, a *new symmetric tensor*: the momentum graviton", reciprocity "as Z → −Z⁻¹", the [cosmological constraint](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) "the |Z| = 1 self-dual circle", and Λ(d) as the Shilov boundary carrying τ ([the modular field Z](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z); [the Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain); [reciprocity as modular inversion](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion)). "Born's debt, restated exactly: *the field equation for momentum-space geometry is the Re Z sector of S_H.*" The identification of a·ã = 1 with |Z| = 1 is queried in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 23.
- **§D, the quadratic spectrum [partial].** The field content of the broken vacuum, with "Λ(d) σ-modes (10: τ plus nine, of which inverse-Higgs constraints likely eliminate most [O])", and the decisive check posed as a three-way classification of X: healthy, auxiliary or ghost ([Open: field content of the broken vacuum](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content)).
- **§E, Debt 2's mechanism [D — structural].** Exchange of X between the dual stresses of two χ quanta "yields a four-point amplitude that depends on the **incoming relative momenta** of the pair": "gate = momentum-graviton exchange". The gate's shape stays open ([the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism); [dual stress](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress); [Open: the gate shape](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape)).
- **§F, the record after the night.** Under the note's heading "Paid": the theorem, the repair, the reframing of Born's debt, Debt 2's mechanism, unimodularity and the modular unification. "Now precisely open": the X classification ("*do this next*"), the gate shape, the origin of τ's potential under P2′, the "inverse-Higgs accounting of the nine heavy moduli", and connection uniqueness for the (ω, H)-only structure ([the Born connection](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection#open-connection-uniqueness-under-p2)).

## #6, debt1a-ii: the classification of X

"Debt 1, Chunk A-ii: The classification of X". Status: "X is forced to be a massive spin-2 field, with exactly one known consistent structure available, and the massless option excluded by theorem. One tuning question remains, now precisely posed."

- **§1, shift symmetry [D — rigorous].** The rigid Sp(2d, ℝ) contains Z → Z + B, so constant shifts of X are exact symmetries: X is "axion-like", and "'auxiliary X' (the soft outcome 2) is off the table" ([shift symmetry](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/shift-symmetry)).
- **§2, gauge structure and the no-go [D + T].** Local dual diffeomorphisms act as δX_μν = ∂₍μλ_ν₎, "a *second copy of linearized diffeomorphisms*"; hence "**the symplectic partner of the graviton is another graviton.**" Without the gauge invariance no ghost-free kinetic term exists; with it, X is a second massless graviton, excluded by the Boulanger–Damour–Gualtieri–Henneaux theorem. "**Therefore X must be massive.**" See [dual diffeomorphisms](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/dual-diffeomorphisms), [the symplectic partner of the graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/symplectic-partner) and [the multi-graviton no-go theorem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go). The phrase "η antisymmetric-pairing vs ω" is queried in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 15, since η is the symmetric structure.
- **§3, the mass mechanism [D — structural].** The polarization choice "**spontaneously breaks the local dual diffeomorphisms**": "the four would-be Goldstones π_μ of the broken λ-gauge are eaten", and X becomes a massive spin-2 with "mass M_X = the polarization-breaking scale" ([the gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs)). Massive spin-2 "has exactly one known consistent structure", the Fierz–Pauli and Hassan–Rosen class, so the geometric sector "is healthy iff the potential generated by the η-selection lands in the Hassan–Rosen class" ([why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain)). A signature caveat is "Flagged, not glossed": the chain is "the Euclidean-rigorous skeleton" ([Open: Lorentzian dressing](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing)).
- **§4, consequences, "with one honest downgrade".** The gate comes from the χ quanta's extent on the momentum fiber, and "Wavepacket overlap of Gaussian profiles gives exactly e^{−(p₁−p₂)²/2μ²} — the postulated gate, with **μ = the χ quantum's own p-width**"; but generic vertices give power-law tails, so "row 1's 'exponential cliff' weakens to 'sharp knee at v\*, with the tail shape (Gaussian vs rational) a diagnostic of the vertex completion.'" ([the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism); [row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee)). The relation of this exponent to the notebook's e^{−k²/μ²} is queried in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 17. The note also logs "A new door, deliberately left closed today": X-condensate dark matter ([the X-condensate candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate)).
- **§5, the record.** The single remaining computation: derive the X-potential from the η-selection sector "and test it against the dRGT coefficient family", dRGT being [de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley massive gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity). "Pass ⇒ the geometric core stands and 'theory' becomes the defensible word."

## #7, debt1a-iii: the tuning test

"Debt 1, Chunk A-iii: The tuning test". Status: "computed. Outcome: conditional pass — the condition identified, both branches computed or classified, and the failure mode exhibited explicitly."

- **§1, the object.** The X mass must come from "a misalignment potential penalizing incompatibility between the polarization J and the modular field Z", measured by the anticommutator {J, I[Z]} ([the misalignment potential](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/misalignment-potential)).
- **§2, the metric tier [D — rigorous].** Block multiplication gives {J₀, I[Z]} = [[S, A], [A, S]] with S = Y + XY⁻¹X − Y⁻¹ and A = [X, Y⁻¹]. Any positive quadratic invariant then gives "m²Tr(h²) — a graviton mass **without** the −(Tr h)² companion", which violates Fierz–Pauli at linear order; and the nonlinear structure Tr(g²) + Tr(g⁻²) lies outside the Hassan–Rosen family. "Every metric-tier (second-order, H-coupled) misalignment invariant tested lands off the tuning" ([the metric tier fails twice](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/metric-tier-failures); [the Fierz–Pauli mass term](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli)). Three statements of this section are queried in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`: item 3, the zero set of the potential; item 4, a missing −h² in the expansion of S; and item 14, which square-root eigenvalues p₄ refers to. Items 4 and 14 leave the conclusion unchanged; item 3 asks whether the larger zero set affects the claims about the vacuum structure.
- **§3, the frame tier [D — structural, resting on T].** In vielbein variables the ghost-free potentials are wedge polynomials, and the symplectic vielbein E with H = EᵀE lives "on the same geometric tier" as J. Wedge couplings of (E, E₀[J]) are "*precisely* the Hinterbichler–Rosen class: ghost-free by construction". "**Verdict of the tuning test:** the theory *fails* in second-order (metric) variables and *passes* in first-order (frame) variables" ([the frame tier](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier); [vielbein formulation and wedge potentials](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation); [the verdict: a conditional pass](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/conditional-pass)).
- **§4, constrained bimetric gravity [D — structural].** "**Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity with the second frame constrained to a compact moduli space Λ(d)** — a *predictive truncation* of bimetric theory" ([constrained bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric)).
- **§5, the record.** Remaining: the βₙ coefficients, "the last computation", and the checks that τ is not eaten, the Lorentzian dressing, and the couplings of ordinary matter against graviton-mass bounds. "**Program-level note:** three structural tests in a row (multi-graviton no-go, signature obstruction, tuning test) could each have killed the geometric sector outright".

## #8, debt1a-iv: the β coefficients

"Debt 1, Chunk A-iv: The β coefficients — closing the debt". Status: "computed. Reciprocity fixes the potential's structure, cancels its vacuum energy by theorem, and cuts the free parameters from five to two. Debt 1 is structurally paid."

- **§1, the palindrome [D].** Reciprocity acts on the Hinterbichler–Rosen sector as the exchange of the two frames, which forces "the **palindromic constraint**" βₙ = β₄₋ₙ, the "interchange-symmetric branch" of bimetric theory: "**its ℤ₂ is Born reciprocity.**" ([the palindrome](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome)).
- **§2, the vacuum-energy theorem [D — rigorous, four lines].** With eₙ(1) = C(4, n) and the tadpole T, the palindrome gives V(1) = 2T; a flat aligned vacuum needs V(1) = T, so "**T = 0 and V(1) = 0 exactly.**" The massive-gravity sector "contributes *nothing* to the cosmological constant". The note flags its novelty "to verify" ([the vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity)). `review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 6 notes that §2's wording, which counts the flatness condition as one condition, is the accurate one, against "by symmetry rather than tuning" elsewhere.
- **§3, spectrum and parameters [D + T].** "Parameter count: five βₙ → palindrome (−2) → T = 0 (−1) → **two**: the Fierz–Pauli mass combination m_FP² ∝ β₁ + 2β₂ + β₃ = 2(β₁+β₂), i.e. **M_X**, and one orthogonal shape parameter". The massless graviton is reciprocity-even and X reciprocity-odd; safety for ordinary matter "is by **range**, not decoupling"; graviton-mass bounds from gravitational waves "apply to the even eigenstate, which is exactly massless"; and τ is not eaten [D — leading order]. See [spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity), [safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety) and [τ is not eaten](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten). Its "five-function freedom" is queried in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 8, since the βₙ are constants; item 5 records that #9 and #11 later wrote "M_X ∝ 2(β₁+β₂)" where this note has m_FP².
- **§4, the answer to Born.** "Momentum-space geometry is carried by X = Re Z, the reciprocity-odd partner of the metric. It is a **massive spin-2 field**", with "two free parameters, vacuum energy exactly zero by the palindrome theorem" ([Born's answer](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer)).
- **§5, the record.** "**Debt 1: structurally paid.** Open residue, none of it existential": the [wedge reduction](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/wedge-reduction) from S_H, Lorentzian dressing, [τ–Stückelberg mixing](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-stuckelberg-mixing), the [dual-stress enhancement](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress-enhancement), and the literature check on Λ = 0 ([the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison)). "**Debt 2:** mechanism paid (X-exchange, μ = fiber width); shape completion open". "**Debt 3:** closed (negative)".

- **The compatibility theorem's tag.** "[D — rigorous]" in #5; #11 since v2.1 tags it [T][V], the statement being Freidel, Leigh and Minic's and the block proof and machine check the program's ([broken Born duality](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality)).

- **"Exactly one known" structure.** #6 §3 keeps the qualifier; #11 §7.1 writes "The unique consistent structure" (`review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 7).
- **The safety argument.** #5 §E and #6 §4 combined X's short range with a weak coupling through ordinary matter's dual stress ("suppressed" in #5, "unenhanced" in #6); #7 §4 required the massless combination to dominate the couplings of ordinary matter; #8 §3 rests on range alone, with couplings of gravitational strength: "safety is by **range**, not decoupling". #8's form is #11's ([safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety#superseded-earlier-forms-of-the-safety-argument)).
- **The graviton-mass bound.** #7 used it as a check on a mixing angle; #8 applies it to the exactly massless even eigenstate, #11's [row 6](/wiki/predictions/massless-graviton) ([spectrum and parity](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity#superseded-the-graviton-mass-bound-as-a-mixing-angle-check)).
- **Counts and wording.** #7 counts "three structural tests in a row", #11 §15 "four consecutive consistency tests", and #11 governs. What X eats is "the four would-be Goldstones π_μ" in #6 but "the vector π_μ and the *longitudinal scalar of the λ-gradient sector*" in #8; #11 §7.1 follows #6, and no document reconciles the two. #6 §5 makes a pass the point where "'theory' becomes the defensible word"; #11 §15 says the word "still waits on items 1–3" ([conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts)).
