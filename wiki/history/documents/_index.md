*The program's record is a set of documents: a lab notebook and the calc notes that followed it, later working notes on a closed universe, on momentum gravity as a velocity-dependent force, on the dark-matter gate's mechanism, on a literature check of P.R.'s conjectures and on the published work of Minic and coauthors on dark matter as the dual of visible matter, two paper-length checkpoints, two popular articles, a short paper on [conjugate special relativity](../../conjugate-relativity/_index.md), a [withdrawn manuscript](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md) with the console used to review it, a handoff note, and the files written since the move to this repository. [`HANDOFF.md`](../../../HANDOFF.md) numbers the artifacts carried over from claude.ai as [#1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md)–[#15](../../../review/skeptic-review-console.html), and the wiki uses those numbers. Some documents are current references, some are kept only as the audit trail, and one was withdrawn; where two disagree, a fixed order of precedence decides which one the wiki follows.*

**Record pages only:** this section describes documents and the disagreements between them, and makes no physics claims of its own. What stands, what is open, what is conjecture, what is superseded, what is under review and what is dead is registered in [claims that stand](../../status/alive.md), [open problems and the queue](../../status/open-problems.md), [conjectures](../../status/conjectures.md), [superseded statements](../../status/superseded.md), [claims under review](../../status/under-review.md) and [the graveyard](../../status/graveyard.md). The withdrawn manuscript [#14](../../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) is described with [#14's soliton model (failed)](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/_index.md).

## Map

The pages start with the two files written to orient a new Claude Code session, follow the artifacts in their creation order (the lab notebook, the calc notes, the checkpoints), then take the popular articles, and end with the disagreements between all of them. Each page says what its document is, its versions and dates, its standing in the precedence order, what it covers section by section with links to the pages that draw on each part, and what in it is superseded or flagged.

- [CLAUDE.md and the README](project-instructions.md) comes first because the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) is the most current source: its section "Since the handoff" ranks first in the precedence order. The page gives the file's sections, the queue as it stands and the points where the file and the repository disagree. The README is a one-line description from the first commit, and it says more than the record holds.
- [The handoff document](handoff.md) is [`HANDOFF.md`](../../../HANDOFF.md), written on 2026-10-19 to carry the project from claude.ai to Claude Code. The page goes through its sections and records where every item of its §2 and every entry of its §8 queue stands now. Its soliton candidate and queue are superseded. Of the four failed claims its §2 lists, three are P.R.'s open conjectures, and the fourth, [the coupling α_d ≈ 34](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md), is under review.
- [The lab notebook (#1) and its addenda](lab-notebook.md) is the first formalization, version 0.1 of September 2026. The page takes v0.1 section by section, then the addenda v0.2–v0.6 that recorded each result and correction, and lists what the body, never brought up to date, still states as live. It is superseded as a reference and kept as the audit trail.
- [The clumping calc notes (#2–#4)](clumping-notes.md) are the three notes that tested momentum clumping and closed the clumping problem with a negative result: the particle island, the [wave island](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md) and the [ultra-faint-dwarf](../../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md) endpoint, with their numbers, where the results went and where the notes differ. They are final for their scope. Their results stand, and the founding image they tested is P.R.'s open conjecture.
- [The field-equation calc notes (#5–#8)](field-equation-notes.md) are the four notes that took the field-equation problem from the [compatibility theorem](../../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md) to the [palindrome](../../gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome.md) and recorded the gate's mechanism as found on the way (not derived, according to flag 55, pending). The page maps the [battery](../../method/verification/battery.md) tests T1–T7 onto the notes and records what has changed in them since. They are final for their scope.
- [The checkpoint: #9 to #11 v2.5](checkpoint.md) covers the paper-length consolidations: v1.0 ([#9](../../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md)), now superseded, and [#11](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md), the current reference for the full program, through versions 2.0, 2.1, 2.2, 2.3, 2.4 and 2.5 commit by commit, with #11 section by section, the inconsistencies inside it and the flags that concern it.
- [The popular articles (#10, #12)](popular-articles.md) are a narrative of the first week and a tour of the framework written as if it were confirmed. The page goes through both section by section, links the claims only they make, and sets [#12](../../../popsci/universes-other-half.md)'s closing list against the record. Neither is ever a source for a claim's status.
- [Conflicts between sources](source-conflicts.md) closes the section: where the documents disagree, how the precedence order resolves each case, and which cases no document settles, stated as unresolved. Suspected errors inside single statements are kept apart, in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md).

## The order of precedence

When documents disagree, the wiki follows the most current, in this order ([`wiki/CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md)):

1. the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md), section "Since the handoff";
2. checkpoint #11 (now v2.5), version 2 of the toy-model note and the phase-space-diameter note (now v1.2, [`wiki/papers/phase-space-diameter.md`](../../papers/phase-space-diameter.md));
3. the review adjudications, [`review/round5-adjudication.md`](../../../review/round5-adjudication.md), [`review/round4-adjudication.md`](../../../review/round4-adjudication.md) and [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../../review/round3-adjudication.md);
4. [#13](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md);
5. [`HANDOFF.md`](../../../HANDOFF.md), whose §2 soliton candidate and §8 queue are superseded;
6. the notes: the calc notes [#2](../../../notes/stream-stability.md)–#8 are final for their own scope, the closed-universe note ([`notes/closed-universe.md`](../../../notes/closed-universe.md), 2026-10-27, not reviewed) is current for its scope, the velocity-gated-gravity and gate-mechanism notes ([`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md), [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../../notes/gate-mechanism.md), 2026-10-28, not reviewed) are current for theirs, the literature-check note ([`notes/literature-check.md`](../../../notes/literature-check.md), 2026-11-02, not reviewed) is current for its scope, the dual-sector note ([`notes/dual-sector.md`](../../../notes/dual-sector.md), 2026-11-06, not reviewed) is current for its scope, the published work of Minic and coauthors on dark matter as the dual of visible matter, where it qualifies the literature-check note, #1 and #9 are superseded as reference, and #14 is withdrawn;
7. the popular articles. #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and is never a source for a claim's status.

The cases the order settles, and those it does not, are on [Conflicts between sources](source-conflicts.md).
