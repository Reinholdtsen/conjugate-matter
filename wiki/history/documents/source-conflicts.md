*The program's documents were written over several weeks, and the later ones correct, re-tag or withdraw what the earlier ones said, so the same question can get different answers in different files. This page collects those disagreements between documents, and between two statements of the same document. Most are settled by a fixed order of precedence in which the most current document wins. The rest are cases where documents of equal standing disagree, or where no document addresses the point; for those the page reports both readings as unresolved. Suspected errors inside a single statement, such as a slip in a formula or a wrong section pointer, are listed separately ([what this page does not list](#what-this-page-does-not-list)).*

**Status:** Record — untagged. Current to 2026-10-30. The order of precedence is set in [`wiki/CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), with the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) "Since the handoff" first; each entry names the documents that disagree and the document or rule that settles the point, or says that nothing does.

## How disagreements are settled

The wiki follows the most current document, in this order ([`wiki/CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md); [Documents](_index.md#the-order-of-precedence)):

1. the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md), section "Since the handoff" ([CLAUDE.md and the README](project-instructions.md));
2. checkpoint [#11](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) (v2.5), version 2 of the toy-model note and the phase-space-diameter note (v1.2) ([the checkpoint](checkpoint.md); [toy models](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md); [the phase-space diameter relation](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md));
3. the review adjudications, [`review/round5-adjudication.md`](../../../review/round5-adjudication.md), [`review/round4-adjudication.md`](../../../review/round4-adjudication.md) and [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../../review/round3-adjudication.md);
4. [#13](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md) ([conjugate special relativity](../../conjugate-relativity/_index.md));
5. [`HANDOFF.md`](../../../HANDOFF.md), whose §2 soliton candidate and §8 queue are superseded ([the handoff document](handoff.md));
6. the notes: the calc notes [#2](../../../notes/stream-stability.md)–[#8](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md) are final for their own scope, the closed-universe note [`notes/closed-universe.md`](../../../notes/closed-universe.md) (2026-10-27, not reviewed) is current for its scope, a closed universe ([a closed Comatter universe](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md)), the velocity-gated-gravity note [`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md) and the gate-mechanism note [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) (2026-10-28, not reviewed) are current for theirs, momentum gravity as a velocity-dependent force and the gate's mechanism ([X between ordinary bodies](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/ordinary-bodies.md); [the gate mechanism](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)), the literature-check note [`notes/literature-check.md`](../../../notes/literature-check.md) (2026-11-02, not reviewed) is current for its scope, which of P.R.'s conjectures are already published ([the literature check of P.R.'s conjectures](../../method/literature-check.md)), the dual-sector note [`notes/dual-sector.md`](../../../notes/dual-sector.md) (2026-11-06, not reviewed) is current for its scope, the published work of Minic and coauthors on dark matter as the dual of visible matter, where it qualifies the literature-check note ([dark matter as the dual Standard Model](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-standard-model.md)), [#1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md) and [#9](../../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md) are superseded as reference, and [#14](../../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) is withdrawn ([the lab notebook](lab-notebook.md); [the clumping notes](clumping-notes.md); [the field-equation notes](field-equation-notes.md));
7. the popular articles, never a source for a claim's status ([the popular articles](popular-articles.md)).

Rules that go with the order:

- **A claim's status comes from the highest-ranked source.** Where a lower-ranked document gives another status, the wiki follows the higher-ranked one and states the evidence against the claim in full ([the graveyard](../../status/graveyard.md); [claims under review](../../status/under-review.md); [negative results and dead claims](../../method/negative-results.md)).
- **Tags are never upgraded.** A claim carries the tag of the highest-ranked source. Where a document marks a statement as machine-verified but no `verify/` script checks it, or checks only part of it, the wiki tags the unchecked part [D] ([provenance tags](../../method/provenance-tags.md)).
- **Two statements inside one document.** Where a top-ranked document says two different things, the wiki follows the more current or more specific statement, states the other, and lists the case here.
- **Unresolved** means that documents of equal rank disagree, or that no document addresses the point. The page that holds the topic states both readings, and so does this one.

## What this page does not list

[`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) lists suspected errors inside single statements of the source documents, found while the wiki was built and since: arithmetic, formulas, section pointers and citations, each quoted faithfully on the wiki's pages, with executed checks in [`review/wiki-build-checks/`](../../../review/wiki-build-checks/); those still open are under [pending rulings](../../status/open-problems.md#pending-rulings). A flagged statement can recur in several documents that agree with one another, and is then an error, not a conflict. The reading of [a·ã = 1](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint.md) as the |Z| = 1 locus (item 23) was such a case until [the phase-space-diameter note](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md) read the constraint differently; that disagreement is listed below. Where an entry below concerns a flagged statement, it names the item.

The flags file and [`review/wiki-build-checks/README.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-checks/README.md) cite conflict numbers from the inventory compiled for the wiki's build, which a reboot later cleared (recorded on [the timeline](../timeline.md)). The numbers that survive in the repository are C20, C21, C27, C28, C29 and C54. The entries below give C20, C21, C27, C28 and C29; C54, a date in #1 (item 18), concerns a single statement and is left to the flags file.
