*The clumping calc notes are three short working notes, artifacts [#2](../../../notes/stream-stability.md) to [#4](../../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md), that tested the program's [founding image](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md): dark matter clumping by velocity rather than in space. The first showed that, under the program's [contact-range interaction](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests), which acts only over microscopic distances, dark matter made of particles cannot clump this way. The second found that dark matter light enough to behave as a wave can, but only as an option, except in one scenario where clumping becomes compulsory in the smallest galaxies. The third showed that this scenario would have drained those galaxies of their dark matter, which observation rules out. Together they closed the clumping problem with a negative result. [Checkpoint #11](checkpoint.md) [§9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) states their results as the tests of the founding image, which is P.R.'s open conjecture. The notes are final for their own scope; of their numbers, only #2's shortfall ratios are checked by a script in the gate.*

**Status:** Record, final for its scope — the notes carry few tags of their own ([#3](../../../notes/wave-island.md)'s rate is "[D, heuristic coefficients]", and #3 and #4 cite their literature as [T]); [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) tags the particle-island result [D] and the wave-island result [D + T] — the only clumping numbers a `verify/` script covers are #2's shortfall ratios, reproduced since 2026-10-28 in section D of [`verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py`](../../../verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py) ([the velocity-gated-gravity script](../../method/verification/velocity-gated-gravity-script.md)); #3 and #4 have no script. The files were imported on 2026-10-19 (commit 8d588f6; moved to `notes/` in 008c3ee) and renamed by subject on 2026-10-28, when the [three main problems](../main-problems.md) in their text took their subject names (c12da1b).

## What the notes are

The clumping problem was open problem 3 of [the lab notebook](lab-notebook.md)'s list: "Stream-instability growth rate γ vs. orbital phase-mixing: determines whether Prediction 3's granularity survives in Milky-Way-like halos. [O]" ([#1 §11](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#11-open-problems-in-order-of-severity)). The notebook had predicted that the gated self-interaction of [conjugate matter](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/_index.md) would make a halo fragment into streams in velocity space, and the growth rate of that instability was "the theory's most important undone calculation on the astrophysics side" ([#1 §5.2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#52-collective-behavior-clumping-in-momentum-space-d-at-mean-field-level-o-beyond)). It was the first of [the three main problems](../main-problems.md) to be closed.

The work was done in three chunks, each written up as a working note with a status line: Chunk A ([`notes/stream-stability.md`](../../../notes/stream-stability.md), #2), Chunk B ([`notes/wave-island.md`](../../../notes/wave-island.md), #3) and Chunk C ([`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md), #4). The notes are undated. They follow v0.1 of the notebook, whose v0.2 addendum summarizes them, and precede the field-equation notes. They share the notebook's scattering benchmark, σ₀/m = 30 cm²/g with m_χ = 70 MeV and v\* = 120 km/s (#2; [#1 §5.1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#51-the-scattering-law-d)), and its numbering of the predictions table, in which rows 3 and 4 are [velocity granularity](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md) and [Fornax friction](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md) ([the scattering benchmark](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark.md)).

## #2: the particle island

"The clumping problem, Chunk A: Linear stability of the conjugate sector's velocity-space fragmentation". Status: "calculation complete. Result: negative on the particle island, with a structural corollary."

- **Setup.** The nonrelativistic mean-field (Vlasov) limit of the gated interaction, with a single-particle energy that includes the gated attraction of co-moving neighbors, coupling g ≡ 4πα̃/m_φ² = √(πσ₀/m²) and the position range collapsed to contact. Linearizing about a homogeneous Maxwellian distribution gives "An eigenvalue problem (the 'potential' lives on momentum space), not a scalar dispersion relation." See [the gated Vlasov model](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/gated-vlasov-model.md) and [Vlasov stability and Landau damping](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/vlasov-stability.md).
- **Two limits.** For a broad gate, which the note assigns to the "dwarf regime", the static threshold is gn/(mσ²) ≥ 1; below it perturbations are Landau-damped, and "There is no race with orbital stirring below threshold — the flocking never starts." The notebook's framing of growth against phase-mixing was therefore misplaced ([superseded](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/gated-vlasov-model.md#superseded-racing-stream-growth-against-orbital-stirring)). For a narrow gate, the Milky Way (MW) and cluster regime, only co-moving particles count, and the "Unified criterion" is ε_gate ≳ m · min(σ, v\*)². The narrow-gate substitution σ → v\* is flagged: it "is the threshold of a cold stream that already exists, not of a smooth halo", and in the Milky Way it overstates the shortfall about 3×, with the verdict unchanged ([`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 58; [`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md` §4](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md#4-what-the-obituary-covers); [the narrow-gate form, flagged](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion.md#the-narrow-gate-form-flagged)).
- **The numbers.** At the benchmark, where "g is *determined* — no freedom remains", the ratio of the two sides is "∼ 10⁻²⁸" in a dwarf core and "∼ 10⁻³¹" in the local Milky Way halo: "**Fails by 28–31 orders of magnitude.**" Collisions do not help: "equilibrium is Maxwellian to one part in 10³⁰". See [the mean-field criterion and its 10²⁸–10³¹ shortfall](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion.md).
- **The corollary.** Holding σ₀/m fixed and asking what mass would satisfy the criterion gives m_crit "≈ 10⁻¹³ GeV ∼ 0.1 meV for MW conditions", where the occupation per mode is N_occ "∼ 10²⁰ ≫ 1": "Collective velocity-space dynamics and classical-particle dark matter are mutually exclusive within this framework." See [the classicality theorem](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem.md) and [occupation number](../../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number.md). The corollary evaluates the broad-gate form at Milky Way conditions, which the note treats as narrow-gate; [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 19 records this, with the conclusion unchanged.
- **Consequences.** On the particle island rows 1–2 "**survive** — they are collisional", and rows 3 and 4 are "**withdrawn**". On the wave island the collective sector "is reborn in field language", with "a cliff-analog" surviving "as (v\*/σ)³ suppression rather than an exponential". The next chunk is the wave-island rate.

## #3: the wave island

"The clumping problem, Chunk B: Gated condensation on the wave island". Status: "rate derived, environments evaluated, verdict reached. Coefficients heuristic at O(1)–O(10) level; calibration against Levkov–Panin–Tkachev (LPT) numerics is a flagged to-do."

- **Setup.** On the wave island, "m ≲ 0.1 meV", the halo is "an incoherent classical field", and relaxation runs by wave kinetics with Bose enhancement, the mechanism Levkov, Panin and Tkachev established for gravity ([wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](../../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics.md); [the wave island](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md)).
- **The rate [D, heuristic coefficients].** Γ_self ≈ c g²ρ² min(σ, v\*)⁴/(m³σ⁶) with c = O(5–15). With a narrow gate it is "suppressed by (v\*/σ)⁴ — **a power law, not an exponential**". Two corollaries follow: the **island discriminant**, "The cliff's *sharpness* distinguishes the islands observationally", and a **gravity floor**: the gated channel dominates the gravitational one above a coupling "∼ 10⁻³¹ GeV⁻² (MW numbers) — an easy floor", and for m ≫ 10⁻²⁰ eV "the gated channel is the only game" ([the gated condensation rate](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate.md); [row 3](../../predictions/island-discriminant.md)).
- **Scenario A, the gated channel as the core-maker.** Fitting dwarf cores spends the coupling, and the ratio of the condensation rate to the orbital rate is then about 10⁻² in classical dwarf cores and the inner Milky Way, 4×10⁻⁵ at the solar circle and ∼0 in clusters, but "∼ 40" in ultra-faint dwarfs (UFDs). "In UFDs, condensation is then mandatory and halo-wide", and with an attractive interaction the condensate has a critical mass, M_crit ≈ 3.6 M_P/(m√g) [T], above which it collapses ([Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md); [attractive condensates](../../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates.md)).
- **Scenario B, cores from elsewhere.** With the coupling free, the stream condition leaves a window X ∈ [5×10⁻¹¹, 1] at m = 0.1 meV with Milky Way numbers, "ten orders of magnitude": "**Verdict B: streams are *tunable*, not mandatory.**" The note calls this "a degeneration flag" in Lakatos's terms ([Scenario B](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b.md); [Lakatos's methodology](../../method/lakatosian-methodology.md)).
- **"Bonus: the relic-abundance problem solves itself".** A field this light is produced by [misalignment](../../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism.md), like an [axion](../../cosmology/dark-energy/axion-like-fields.md#wave-χ-produced-like-an-axion), so "Open problem [#5](../../../notes/geometric-core.md) (cannibalism-safe relic abundance) is moot on the wave island"; "The 5.4:1 question remains" ([relic abundance by misalignment on the wave island](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic.md)). "Open problem #5" is item 5 of [#1 §11](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#11-open-problems-in-order-of-severity), not of [#11 §14](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems).
- **Standing results.** "Row 3 as written (Gaia/GD-1 granularity in MW-like halos)" fails "on both islands as a *mandatory* signature". Next: Chunk 3c, "Completes the clumping problem", then the field-equation problem.

## #4: the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint

"The clumping problem, Chunk C: The ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint — closing the clumping problem". Status: "complete. Verdict: Scenario A falsified; the clumping problem closed."

- **Sharpened inputs.** Segue 1 has "M(< 38 pc) ≈ 6×10⁵ M_⊙ ⇒ mean core density ρ ≈ 100 GeV/cm³ (not the conservative 20 used in 3b), σ ≈ 3.7 km/s, t_dyn ≈ 10 Myr". The rate ratio becomes "≈ 660", the condensation time "≈ 1.5×10⁴ yr", and over 10 Gyr "the system has ~6×10⁵ condensation times": "There is no timescale refuge."
- **The attractive endpoint.** Under the Scenario-A normalization, M_crit ≈ 2.9×10⁻³⁰ (m/eV)^{−7/4} M_⊙, which crosses the core mass of an ultra-faint dwarf at m ≈ 5×10⁻²¹ eV. For heavier fields, "the bulk of the wave island", the condensate reaches criticality almost at once and cycles through collapse, ejecting mass faster than the escape velocity: "**the condensate is a mass drain.**" Lighter fields are excluded independently, by ultra-faint-dwarf kinematics (Dalal and Kravtsov, "bound ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV; verify exact figure before publication") and the Lyman-α forest ([bounds on fuzzy dark matter](../../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds.md)).
- **The data.** "Observed UFDs are the densest, most DM-dominated, oldest systems known", DM being dark matter; Segue 1 sits "in an intact, *dense* halo". No parameter direction rescues the scenario ([dwarf galaxies](../../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md)).
- **The verdict.** "**Scenario A is falsified by the existence of ultra-faint dwarfs.**" With the other two chunks, the clumping problem closes: "**Nowhere in the viable parameter space of BBT is velocity-space clumping a mandatory phenomenon.** It is forbidden for classical-particle χ, fatal where it would have been mandatory (Scenario A), and merely optional elsewhere", BBT being Broken-Born theory, the program's earlier name ([the net theorem](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem.md)). "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands. The Fornax mechanism goes with them" ([old row 3](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md); [old row 4](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md)). "The surviving wave island is roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV with Scenario-B couplings."
- **Next.** "The field-equation problem, Chunk 1a": linearize the geometric action "with ω, η frozen", importing the spectrum of linearized [double field theory](../../phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory.md). The field-equation work that followed began instead by showing that with η frozen the program has no content of its own, and made η emergent ([the field-equation calc notes](field-equation-notes.md)).

## Where the results went

The lab notebook's v0.2 addendum recorded the outcome first: "The clumping problem is settled, and the answer went against the theory", with rows 3–4 struck and the island discriminant added (#1). The program v1.0 restated it in its §7, "(the clumping problem: closed, negative)", and [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) carries it, with the particle-island result tagged [D], the wave-island result [D + T] and the net theorem stated as "forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)". §9 records the founding image as P.R.'s open conjecture, and states that "Results (i)–(iii) stand as results" ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md)). Scenario A and the image's two predictions, old rows 3 and 4, are under review ([claims under review](../../status/under-review.md)). The salvage is [row 3, the island discriminant](../../predictions/island-discriminant.md), and the quarantined remainder is [Scenario B](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b.md).

The handoff lists the result among the failed claims of its §2, and it marks the result's central number machine-verified: "impossible for particle DM (criterion fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ ✓)" ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen)). When the handoff was written no `verify/` script computed that number. [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) tags it [D], and the wiki follows #11 ([`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 16). A recomputation made while building the wiki, [`review/wiki-build-checks/stream_stability_check.py`](../../../review/wiki-build-checks/stream_stability_check.py), reproduces the shortfall but is not part of the gate. Since 2026-10-28 its arithmetic has been in the gate, as section D of [`verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py`](../../../verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py), which reproduces both ratios; the flag's addendum says that "The ✓ is now backed by a script", and [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)'s tag is [D].

The momentum-gravity note of 2026-10-28 (computed, not reviewed) records the scope of these tests: #2's interaction is contact-range, "the force is contact-range" being one of its robustness reasons, and the results say that velocity clumping is required nowhere, not that it cannot happen. A long-range attraction among χ gated in relative velocity is a different model, which the clumping problem never tested ([`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md` §4](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md#4-what-the-obituary-covers); [the scope of the tests](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests)).

## Where the notes differ

- **The power of the wave-island suppression.** #2 estimated "(v\*/σ)³"; #3, #4 and #11 have (v\*/σ)⁴, and the fourth power governs ([the cubic suppression, superseded](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate.md#superseded-the-cubic-suppression-of-calc-note-2)).
- **The wave island's upper edge.** #2's consequences give "m_χ ≲ meV", while its corollary and #3's setup give 0.1 meV; #4 and the notebook's addendum write 10⁻⁴ eV.
- **The occupation number.** #3 writes N_occ = ρ/(m⁴σ³) in its setup and a "peak occupation N_occ ≈ (2π)^{3/2}ρ/(m⁴σ³)" in its rate, and does not reconcile the two ([occupation number](../../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number.md)).
- **Scenario B's fuzzy end.** #3's first source of cores, "fuzzy-end quantum pressure at m ∼ 10⁻²² eV", lies below the lower edge of the wave island that #4 sets, 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV. Neither note addresses it.
- **The exponential cliff.** The notes were written before the field-equation notes softened the cliff into a knee, so they still speak of an exponential suppression on the particle island ([the gated scattering law](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law.md)).
- **Numbering.** "Rows 3 and 4" are the notebook's old rows, and "Open problem #5" is the notebook's item 5; [#9](../../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md) and #11 number both lists differently ([history of the predictions table](../../predictions/table-history.md)).

The unreconciled points are listed on [conflicts between sources](source-conflicts.md).

## What they left open

- **The coefficient c.** #3's calibration against Levkov–Panin–Tkachev numerics was "a flagged to-do"; [#9 §10](../../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md#10-open-problems) lists "LPT coefficient calibration" and [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) writes "coefficients [O]" ([calibration of c](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate.md#open-calibration-of-the-coefficient-c)).
- **Rows 1 and 2 in wave language.** #2: "Rows 1–2 must be re-derived in wave language" ([rows 1 and 2 in wave language](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate.md#open-rows-1-and-2-in-wave-language)).
- **The exact light-mass bound**, which #4 asks to be verified before publication.
- **The topological loophole**, not raised in the notes: [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) and the handoff leave untouched the localization in momentum of individual topological objects, whose candidate is P.R.'s untested conjecture of [conjugate monopoles localized in momentum](../../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md) ([Open: the topological loophole](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole.md)).
