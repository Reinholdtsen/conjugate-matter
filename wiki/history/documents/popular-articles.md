*The popular articles are two plain-language essays about the program, written on claude.ai for general readers. "The Angle of the Mirror" (#10) is a field report on the first week: the bet that Born's symmetry between position and momentum is exact but hidden, the two founding ideas the calculations killed, and the structure that survived. "The Universe's Other Half" (#12) is a tour of the whole framework written, in its own opening words, "as if it were confirmed. It is not." Neither has been revised since the import into this repository, so both still present the superheavy soliton candidate as alive and read the dark-energy data more favorably than the checkpoint now does. They are kept for their exposition, and neither is ever a source for a claim's status.*

**Status:** Record, never a status source — untagged — rank 7, last, in the wiki's precedence order (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`); both files are unchanged since the import of 2026-10-19 (8d588f6).

## Key facts

- **#10, "The Angle of the Mirror."** Subtitle: "What happened when an engineer's hunch and an AI's mathematics spent a week stress-testing an 88-year-old symmetry — and killed their own favorite idea". `HANDOFF.md` §3: "final (journey article)". Its sections, "The wager", "The killing fields", "What refused to die", "The referee is already on the field", "Dividends and daydreams" and "The method is the message", are the only narrative of the first week. Its slogan: "dark matter is the mirror-world's stuff; dark energy is the angle of the mirror." It calls itself "unreviewed, unpublished, and wrong in whatever places expert scrutiny will eventually find."
- **The two deaths it narrates.** Velocity flocking, the founding image ([obituary](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)), and the idea that "the conjugate universe's gravitational collapse is what powers our accelerating expansion" ([obituary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion)): "This one lost its two favorites in the first week". Where #10 says data "already executed two of the founding ideas", #11 Appendix A item 9 records the second death as "dead twice (w = −2 falsified; corrected dictionary removed the mechanism)", partly by the program's own mathematics.
- **#12, "The Universe's Other Half."** `HANDOFF.md` §3: "final (destination article)", "the framework presented as if confirmed; wave/spectrum analogy spine; includes magnetic identification, transparency argument, magnetic-quark/dual-superconductor section, dual SR, equivalence-principle unification" (SR: special relativity). Its register, "serious-scientist plain language", is the one `HANDOFF.md` §7 asks for in science writing; its claims are a separate matter.
- **Why neither is a status source.** #12 opens: "What follows presents the broken-Born framework as if it were confirmed. It is not." Both articles were written before #11 v2.1. They present the topological knots, superheavy solitons, as a live dark-matter candidate ([retired](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)), and they read the surveys' hints of weakening dark energy as "exactly the required weakening" (#10) and "the required behavior" (#12); v2.1 then recorded the phantom crossing in Data Release 2 of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument, which puts the prediction under pressure ([DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing)). #10's "Every load-bearing equation in this program has been verified by machine" goes beyond #11 §2, where the battery covers the explicit algebra of Tier 1 and "a verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes" ([verification checks arithmetic, not the model](/wiki/method/verification/arithmetic-not-model)).
- **Claims found only in the articles** ([chat-only and popsci-only conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures)). #12's magnetic identification makes the momentum-polarized sector "the magnetic side of electromagnetism", with a coupling "about 34, against our 1/137"; it conflicts with the dead claim of α_d ≈ 34 via Dirac quantization against our photon and with strict P4, and it is recorded as dead ([Obituary: the magnetic identification](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)). The 34 itself depends on convention by a factor of 4: "1/(4α) = 34.26 (eg = 2πn) vs 1/α = 137.04 (eg = 4πn)" (`review/v3-worklog.md`, R2 [V]). The transparency argument, "Uniform media do not scatter light; they refract it", puts the residual effects on light "some forty orders of magnitude below the sharpest astronomical measurements"; no script checks it, strict P4 excludes its premise, and the mass it assumes, "at the masses natural for these solitons — around 10¹⁶ times the proton's", is that of the retired soliton candidate. #12's "constant is impossible" also goes beyond #11 row 4, where a constant w = −1 kills only the τ sector ([row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift)).
- **"What one assumption purchases"** (#12's closing list), against the record. The primacy of space over momentum: [the hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core), qualified in v2.2 by [the Goldstone angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle). The symmetry's invisibility: the mass of X ([why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain)). "The quietness of empty space": [the vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity), whose novelty is open. Velocity-gated dark matter: [the χ-particle candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles), one of two. Dark energy "obligated to decrease": [τ as thawing quintessence](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence) with [the no-phantom bound](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound), under pressure from the data. The maximum force and the equivalence principle: #13, tagged [D], with "The factor of 4 is not fixed here [O]" ([the maximum force](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force)). The magnetic identity: popsci-only and dead. The turnaround: [the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce), a long-horizon prediction. #11 rests on five postulates, P1–P5, not on #12's "single assumption" ([Postulates P1–P5](/wiki/program/postulates)).
