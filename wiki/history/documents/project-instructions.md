*The root `CLAUDE.md` is the repository's file of standing instructions for Claude Code sessions. It says what the program is, maps the documents to their folders, records what has changed since the program moved from claude.ai, defines [the gate](/wiki/method/verification/the-gate) that must pass before work is called finished, and sets the method and the working arrangement with P.R. Its section "Since the handoff" is the most current statement of the program's status, and it ranks first when the documents disagree. The README is the repository's one-line description from its first commit, and it presents as explained what the record holds as open conjectures.*

**Status:** Record — untagged (the file defines the provenance tags but applies none) — its section "Since the handoff" is rank 1 in the wiki's precedence order (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`). The file was created at the move in commit 008c3ee (2026-10-19) and has been revised after each decision since; its history is in `git log -- CLAUDE.md`. The README has not changed since the initial commit, 6b8f599 (2026-10-18).

## What the file is

When the chat's files were restructured into this repository, the root `CLAUDE.md` was written beside them as the starting point for a fresh session. [The handoff document](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) had asked for this arrangement in its suggested layout: "HANDOFF.md (this file; point CLAUDE.md at it)" (`HANDOFF.md` §9).

The file's parts, in order, are a statement of what the program is; the instruction to read `HANDOFF.md` first; the layout of the folders, with the read order and the section "Since the handoff"; the gate; the method; and the conventions for working with P.R. The method and the conventions were carried over from the handoff's §5 and §7. "Since the handoff" and the method's later bullets are what the repository added.

The file is a working document, not a paper, and it carries no provenance tags of its own. Its authority in the wiki comes from its currency: it has been revised after each decision since the move, so where it states a status, that status is the current one.

## The program statement and the first instruction

The file opens with what the program is: a speculative physics research program built on spontaneously broken Born reciprocity, developed with Claude on claude.ai in September–October 2026 and continued in this repository, "a Lakatosian research program, not a theory". Until 2026-10-26 it was the Broken-Born program (BBT, for Broken-Born theory, the name the lab notebook used). The records and all file names keep the old name, while current documents, the wiki and the site use the new one. See [the research program](/wiki/program), its [hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core) and [Lakatos's methodology of research programmes](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology).

The next paragraph tells a fresh session to read `HANDOFF.md` first, as the project's state at the move from claude.ai: its lists of alive, failed and open claims (§2, with the remark that the list of failed claims is overtaken), the results that exist only in the old chat (§4), the review history and the referees' measured failure mode (§6) and the ranked queue (§8). The handoff's §3 numbers the artifacts, and the layout gives their paths. Parts of the handoff have since been superseded ([the handoff document](/wiki/history/documents/handoff)).

## The README

`README.md` is the repository's initial commit (6b8f599, 2026-10-18), and it has not been edited since. Its whole text is the heading "conjugate-matter" and one line:

> Physics hypothesis explaining dark matter and missing magnetic monopoles

Against the current record, each part of the line claims more than the record holds (its monopole clause is flag 37 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`):

- **The missing monopoles.** The program's explanation of our failure to find magnetic monopoles is P.R.'s open conjecture, not a result. Its setting is the [shared-photon form](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification#shared-photon) of his magnetic-mirror conjecture, which needs P4 restated and conditions that hide the momentum-polarized analogs, none of them yet computed (#11 §11.1, §11.4; flag 62). Two grounds weigh against it, and neither kills it: the relevant duality is electric–magnetic, not position–momentum, a ground that only the shared-photon form contests; and the null result is expected under several standard histories, [inflation](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/inflation-and-dilution) after a unification transition among them, so it lends the explanation no support (flag 59). The README's clause states as the program's result what the record holds as an open conjecture ([Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)).
- **Dark matter.** That dark matter is conjugate matter is P.R.'s core conjecture, alive (#11 Appendix A item 4). The program offers three candidates, [χ particles](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles), [an X-condensate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate) and P.R.'s conjecture of [conjugate monopoles localized in momentum](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles), and discriminating among them is open (#11 §12 and §14 item 9 [O]; [dark-sector candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates)).
- **"Hypothesis".** The root `CLAUDE.md` calls the work "a Lakatosian research program, not a theory", and #11 §15 says the word "theory" "still waits on items 1–3" of its open problems ([assessment](/wiki/program/assessment)).

The wiki does not use the README as a source for anything but its own text.
