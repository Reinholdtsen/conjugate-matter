*`HANDOFF.md` is the note that carried the program from the claude.ai conversation where it was developed to Claude Code and this repository. It was prepared on 2026-10-19 so that a fresh session could continue without the original chat: it gives the scientific state on one screen, numbers the artifacts #1–#15, lists the results that exist only in the chat, and records the method, the review history, the working conventions and a ranked list of next actions. Much of it still stands. Its list of dead claims, to be kept dead, was binding until 2026-10-29, when Paul set the rule that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs: three of the list's four items are now his open conjectures, and the fourth is under review, not dead, until he reviews it. Its superheavy [soliton dark-matter candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), the model of manuscript #14, and its work queue were superseded on 2026-10-19, the queue that replaced it was revised again on 2026-10-19, 2026-10-20 and 2026-10-26, several of its statuses have moved on, its chat-only diameter relation has since been written up in a note that withdraws one of the relation's four statements, and the root `CLAUDE.md` and [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) now rank above it.*

**Status:** Record, superseded in part — untagged (it marks machine-checked results with ✓ rather than [V]) — rank 5 in the wiki's precedence order, with its §2 soliton candidate and §8 queue superseded (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`; root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'), and since 2026-10-29 its §2 instruction to keep the dead claims dead superseded by the root `CLAUDE.md`'s rule on deaths ('Method'). The file was imported on 2026-10-19 (commit 8d588f6, as `import/HANDOFF.md`; moved to the root in 008c3ee). On 2026-10-28 its old numbered labels for the [three main problems](/wiki/history/main-problems), Debt 1–3, became their subject names, and its payment and ledger wording and its file names for the calc notes were rewritten (c12da1b). Two notes dated 2026-10-29 now mark §1's "Honest status" and §2's dead list as overtaken (below). Its account of the state at the move is otherwise as written.

## What the document is

The handoff states its purpose in its first line: "Everything a fresh Claude Code session needs to continue this project. Read this first; the artifacts listed in §3 travel with it. It is self-sufficient — nothing essential lives only in the originating chat session or in claude.ai memory." It mentions a second, running record kept in claude.ai's memory store, and says that if the store is visible, "it corroborates but adds nothing critical beyond this document". The footer dates it: "Handoff prepared 2026-10-19 from the originating claude.ai session."

The document is a snapshot. It was written at the move, when #11 was at version 2.0 and the soliton manuscript #14 at version 2, and before review round 3 had finished. Everything the repository has done since is measured against it: the root `CLAUDE.md` tells a fresh session to read the handoff first, adding of its §2 that "its dead list is overtaken, since none of it is dead after 2026-10-29", and then lists, under "Since the handoff", what has changed ([CLAUDE.md and the README](/wiki/history/documents/project-instructions)).

## §1: what the project is

§1 names the project, the "Broken-Born program" (BBT, Broken-Born theory), "a speculative physics research program developed September–October 2026". It states the [hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core): "Born reciprocity — the (x,p) → (p,−x) symmetry of quantum kinematics — taken as exact and spontaneously broken, with only the symplectic form ω rigid (ℏ-rigidity) and the neutral metric η emergent as the order parameter." It fixes the roles: "Paul (P.R.) — conjecture, direction, veto; Claude — derivations, counterexamples, drafting, verification. Attribution in all papers follows this split" ([the collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration)).

Its "Honest status" reads: "a Lakatosian research program, not a theory. Three blind AI reviews returned verdict B ("flawed but salvageable"). Zero fabricated citations across all checks. The founding intuition (dark matter clumping in momentum space) was falsified by the program's own calculations [overtaken 2026-10-29: Paul did not concur in the death, and the founding image is his open conjecture again, #11 v2.5 §9]; the structural core survived four consecutive consistency tests by being forced onto unique consistent structures each time." The bracket is the handoff's own later note ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)). The count of reviews disagrees with the handoff's own §6 (below). The "four consecutive consistency tests" is also #11 §15's count ([assessment](/wiki/program/assessment)). On "unique consistent structures", without the qualifier "known" that calc note #6 §3 keeps, see flag 7 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`.

## §4: results that exist only in the chat

The section heading, with its capitals set in lower case here, is "Results that exist only in the chat (not in any artifact)". - **The phase-space diameter relation**, "D_x · D_p = h · N^(1/3) per dimension (cosmic time–bandwidth theorem)", with the rectangle "square … exactly at the bounce" and "Conjecture-tier; candidate for a future note".
