*The checkpoint is the program's paper-length statement of everything it has derived, predicted, retracted and left open, with every claim tagged by where it came from. Its first version, #9 ("The Broken-Born Program" v1.0), consolidated the [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) and the calc notes into one document with a plain-language passage opening every section. Its successor, #11, is the current reference for the full program. Version 2.0 added a methodological preface with a machine-verification battery, sections on charge lattices and on the dark-sector candidates, a reference list and an audit of the founding ideas; version 2.1 recorded the outcome of review round 3, retiring the [soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) and putting the [dark-energy prediction](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift) under pressure; version 2.2 recorded the [toy-model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) results; version 2.3 took up three flags, two that Paul accepted and one applied under his instruction to update the repository, restating why the [monopole explanation](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation) is dead, recording his [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification) as open and adding the polarization's defects to the open problems, while a fourth flag, whether one form of that conjecture would reopen the explanation, awaits his ruling. #11 is a working checkpoint, not to be submitted until its citations from memory have been checked against their sources.*

**Status:** Record — #11 v2.3 is rank 2 in the wiki's precedence order and "the current reference for the full program" (root `CLAUDE.md`); #9 is superseded by #11 and kept as the audit trail (`HANDOFF.md` §3: "v1.0 — superseded by #11"; #11 header: "Supersedes v2.2, v2.1, v2.0 and v1.0"). #11's versions are in `git log -- papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`: v2.0 as imported (8d588f6, 2026-10-19), v2.1 (e1c1f67, 2026-10-19) and v2.2 (6ee74d0 and 6606734, 2026-10-19 and 20). v2.3 (2026-10-29) had not been committed when this line was written.

## What the checkpoint is

#11 is titled "The Comatter Program: A Working Checkpoint" (until 2026-10-26, "The Broken-Born Program: A Working Checkpoint") and states its form in the header: "Every section opens with a plain-language passage (italic), followed by the technical record." Every claim carries a provenance tag, [D] derived in the program, [T] established literature, [P] postulate, [O] open or [V] machine-verified, and every reference a mark, [S] for "retrieved by live search during this project" or † for "cited from model memory — verify against the source before external use" ([provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags)). Its footer gives the attribution, "conjecture and direction, P. R.; derivations, counterexamples, verification battery, and drafting, Claude (Anthropic)", and the condition on its use: "Working checkpoint — not for submission until the † audit (open item 10) completes" ([Open: the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery); [the collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration)).

The wiki takes its top-level structure from #11: "The top level follows the program's argument as #11 builds it" (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`). Most pages cite #11 first, and where #11 and an older document differ, #11 governs, unless the root `CLAUDE.md` says otherwise ([conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts)).
