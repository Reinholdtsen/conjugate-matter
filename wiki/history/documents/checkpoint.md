Its first version, #9 ("The Broken-Born Program" v1.0), consolidated the [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) and the calc notes into one document with a plain-language passage opening every section. Its successor, #11, is the current reference for the full program. Version 2.0 added a methodological preface with a machine-verification battery, sections on charge lattices and on the dark-sector candidates, a reference list and an audit of the founding ideas; version 2.1 recorded the outcome of review round 3, in which [#14's model of the topological candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) failed, and put the [dark-energy prediction](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift) under pressure; version 2.2 recorded the [toy-model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) results; version 2.3 restated the grounds against the [monopole explanation](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation), recorded P.R.'s [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification) as open and added the polarization's defects to the open problems; and version 2.4 listed three of P.R.'s founding ideas as open conjectures: [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles) as a third dark-matter candidate, their explanation of the monopole null result, and [conjugate collapse as the source of our accelerating expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion). Version 2.5 lists the founding image, [velocity clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image), as P.R.'s open conjecture, with the calculations that tested it standing; records #14's model as dead; states that two versions of the collapse conjecture failed, the conjecture itself staying open; and lists [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) and two withdrawn prediction rows, [velocity-space granularity](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity) and [Fornax friction](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction), as [under review](/wiki/status/under-review). ## What the checkpoint is

#11 is titled "The Comatter Program: A Working Checkpoint" (until 2026-10-26, "The Broken-Born Program: A Working Checkpoint") and states its form in the header: "Every section opens with a plain-language passage (italic), followed by the technical record." Every claim carries a provenance tag, [D] derived in the program, [T] established literature, [P] postulate, [O] open or [V] machine-verified, and every reference a mark, [S] for "retrieved by live search during this project" or † for "cited from model memory — verify against the source before external use" ([provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags)). Its footer gives the attribution, "conjecture and direction, P. R.; derivations, counterexamples, verification battery, and drafting, Claude (Anthropic)", and the condition on its use: "Working checkpoint — not for submission until the † audit (open item 10) completes" ([Open: the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery); [the collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration)).

The wiki takes its top-level structure from #11: "The top level follows the program's argument as #11 builds it" (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`). Most pages cite #11 first, and where #11 and an older document differ, #11 governs, unless the root `CLAUDE.md` says otherwise ([conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts)).
