*The field-equation calc notes are four working notes, artifacts [#5](../../../notes/geometric-core.md) to [#8](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md), on the most severe of the lab notebook's open problems: what equations govern the geometry of momentum space. The first, written overnight, showed that the original postulates left the program with no content beyond [general relativity](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity.md) and one model of [self-interacting dark matter](../../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter.md), repaired them, and gathered the geometry into a single [modular field](../../phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z.md); on the way it gave a mechanism for the [velocity gate](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md), which a note of 2026-10-28 finds not derived (flag 55, pending). The second showed that the field carrying momentum-space geometry must be a [second graviton](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), and a massive one. The third tested whether that field avoids the [Boulware–Deser ghost](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli.md), the instability that generic massive-graviton theories carry, and found that it does when the field's potential is written in [frame variables](../../gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier.md), sets of local reference axes from which a metric is assembled; written in metric variables it fails, and the note argues that the frame formulation is the natural one. The fourth used [Born reciprocity](../../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md) to constrain the remaining coefficients, leaving two free parameters, and to cancel the sector's vacuum energy. Together they closed the field-equation problem in structure; their results are now stated in checkpoint [#11 §5](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem)–[§8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate), and the battery checks their explicit algebra.*

**Status:** Record, final for its scope — the notes tag their own results ("[D — rigorous]", "[D — structural]", "[D + T]"); the battery T1–T7 in [`verify/battery.py`](../../../verify/battery.py) checks the explicit algebra [V], which [#11 §2](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#2-methodological-preface-provenance-and-verification) calls [Tier 1](../../method/claim-tiers.md#tier-1-explicit-algebra) — [`HANDOFF.md` §3](../../../HANDOFF.md#3-artifact-inventory-creation-order) marks all four "final", and #8 "final — closes the field-equation problem (structural)"; the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) files them with the audit trail, "superseded as reference but kept". The files were imported on 2026-10-19 (commit 8d588f6; moved to `notes/` in 008c3ee) and renamed by subject on 2026-10-28, when the [three main problems](../main-problems.md) in their text also took their subject names (c12da1b).

## What the notes are

The field-equation problem was the first item of [the lab notebook](lab-notebook.md)'s open problems: "The field equation for H beyond minisuperspace — the question Born left open in 1938" ([#1 §11](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#11-open-problems-in-order-of-severity)), H being the [generalized metric](../../phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md) on phase space. It is the question [Born's question](../../program/borns-question.md) leaves: if position and momentum are symmetric, what is the dynamics of the geometry of momentum space? See [the three main problems](../main-problems.md).

The work came in four chunks, each a working note: Chunk A ([`notes/geometric-core.md`](../../../notes/geometric-core.md), #5), A-ii ([`notes/x-classification.md`](../../../notes/x-classification.md), [#6](../../../notes/x-classification.md)), A-iii ([`notes/tuning-test.md`](../../../notes/tuning-test.md), [#7](../../../notes/tuning-test.md)) and A-iv ([`notes/beta-coefficients.md`](../../../notes/beta-coefficients.md), #8). The notes are undated; they follow the clumping notes in [`HANDOFF.md` §3](../../../HANDOFF.md#3-artifact-inventory-creation-order)'s creation order, and the notebook's addenda v0.3 to v0.6 summarize them, one each. #5 calls itself an "Overnight working note", and its addendum is headed "overnight".

Each note ends with a list of what it settled and what it left open, and the next note takes up the first open item. The chain therefore reads as one argument: the axioms are repaired (#5), the new field is classified (#6), its potential is [tested](../../gravity/tuning-test/_index.md) (#7), and the potential's coefficients are fixed (#8).

## #5: the geometric core

"The field-equation problem, Chunk A — the geometric core, linearized groundwork", subtitled "(plus an unplanned result on the gate problem)". Status: "two rigorous results, one axiom correction forced, one mechanism identified, one decisive computation defined but not completed."

- **§A, the compatibility theorem [D — rigorous].** With ω and η both in standard form, the positive H satisfying (η⁻¹H)² = +1 and (ω⁻¹H)² = −1 "are exactly" H = diag(g, g⁻¹), with a short block proof and its corollaries: det H = 1, no [Kalb–Ramond](../../phase-space/born-geometry/kalb-ramond-field.md#frozen-out-when-both-pairings-are-rigid) analog, and "The momentum metric is slaved: g̃ = g⁻¹ pointwise" ([the compatibility theorem](../../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md); [its corollaries](../../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries.md)).
- **§B, the inconsistency and the forced repair [D — structural].** Under the original P2, with ω and η rigid, the compatible polarizations are "**discrete** — the 2^d coordinate-type splittings, GL(d)-rigid": no [Lagrangian Grassmannian](../../phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian.md), no [Maslov circle](../../phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase.md), "**no τ field**", and no geometric origin for the gate. "Call this **Branch R** (rigid): BBT reduces to GR + one bespoke SIDM model", BBT being Broken-Born theory (the program's earlier name), GR general relativity and SIDM self-interacting dark matter. The repair, P2′: "Freeze ω only — that is genuinely ℏ — and let **η be emergent: the order parameter itself.**" The "Program-nontriviality theorem": "BBT has content beyond GR + SIDM **iff** η is emergent." See [Original P2 (superseded)](../../program/postulates/original-p2.md), [P2′](../../program/postulates/p2-prime.md) and [the nontriviality theorem](../../program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem.md). The word "discrete" is queried in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 1: for d ≥ 2 such polarizations form continuous families, though the conclusion of no Maslov circle survives.
- **§C, the modular field [D — structural; math facts standard].** Positive H compatible with ω alone form the Siegel domain, coordinatized by Z = X + iY: "**Im Z = Y = g**", "**Re Z = X**, a *new symmetric tensor*: the momentum graviton", reciprocity "as Z → −Z⁻¹", the [cosmological constraint](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint.md) "the |Z| = 1 self-dual circle", and Λ(d) as the Shilov boundary carrying τ ([the modular field Z](../../phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z.md); [the Siegel domain](../../phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain.md); [reciprocity as modular inversion](../../phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion.md)). "The field-equation problem, restated exactly: *the field equation for momentum-space geometry is the Re Z sector of S_H.*" The identification of a·ã = 1 with |Z| = 1 is queried in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 23.
- **§D, the quadratic spectrum [partial].** The field content of the broken vacuum, with "Λ(d) σ-modes (10: τ plus nine, of which inverse-Higgs constraints likely eliminate most [O])" ([inverse-Higgs constraints](../../symmetry-breaking/inverse-higgs-constraints.md)), and the decisive check posed as a three-way classification of X: healthy, auxiliary or [ghost](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/ghosts.md) ([Open: field content of the broken vacuum](../../phase-space/modular-field/field-content.md)).
- **§E, the gate's mechanism [D — structural].** Exchange of X between the dual stresses of two χ quanta "yields a four-point amplitude that depends on the **incoming relative momenta** of the pair": "gate = momentum-graviton exchange". The gate's shape stays open ([the gate mechanism](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md); [dual stress](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress.md); [Open: the gate shape](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape.md)). [#5 §E](../../../notes/geometric-core.md#e-the-gate-problem--the-mechanism-found-d--structural) is among the text that flag 55 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) lists as depending on [#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate)'s tag: [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) (2026-10-28; two derivations, the second blind; not reviewed) finds that exchanging [#11 §7](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton)'s X cannot produce the gate, and the flag, pending, proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism; [#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate)'s tag is [D — mechanism; shape [O]] ([the gate mechanism](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)).
- **§F, the record after the night.** Under the note's heading "Done": the theorem, the repair, the reframing of the field-equation problem, the gate's mechanism, unimodularity and the modular unification. "Now precisely open": the X classification ("*do this next*"), the gate shape, the origin of τ's potential under P2′, the "inverse-Higgs accounting of the nine heavy moduli", and connection uniqueness for the (ω, H)-only structure ([the Born connection](../../phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection.md#open-connection-uniqueness-under-p2)).

## #6: the classification of X

"The field-equation problem, Chunk A-ii: The classification of X". Status: "X is forced to be a massive spin-2 field, with exactly one known consistent structure available, and the massless option excluded by theorem. One tuning question remains, now precisely posed."

- **§1, shift symmetry [D — rigorous].** The rigid Sp(2d, ℝ) contains Z → Z + B, so constant shifts of X are exact symmetries: X is "axion-like", and "'auxiliary X' (the soft outcome 2) is off the table" ([shift symmetry](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/shift-symmetry.md)).
- **§2, gauge structure and the no-go [D + T].** Local dual diffeomorphisms act as δX_μν = ∂₍μλ_ν₎, "a *second copy of linearized diffeomorphisms*"; hence "**the symplectic partner of the graviton is another graviton.**" Without the gauge invariance no ghost-free kinetic term exists ([the signature obstruction](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/signature-obstruction.md)); with it, X is a second massless graviton, excluded by the Boulanger–Damour–Gualtieri–Henneaux theorem. "**Therefore X must be massive.**" See [dual diffeomorphisms](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/dual-diffeomorphisms.md), [the symplectic partner of the graviton](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/symplectic-partner.md) and [the multi-graviton no-go theorem](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go.md). The phrase "η antisymmetric-pairing vs ω" is queried in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 15, since η is the symmetric structure.
- **§3, the mass mechanism [D — structural].** The polarization choice "**spontaneously breaks the local dual diffeomorphisms**": "the four would-be Goldstones π_μ of the broken λ-gauge are eaten", and X becomes a massive spin-2 with "mass M_X = the polarization-breaking scale" ([the gravitational Higgs mechanism](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs.md)). Massive spin-2 "has exactly one known consistent structure", the Fierz–Pauli and Hassan–Rosen class, so the geometric sector "is healthy iff the potential generated by the η-selection lands in the Hassan–Rosen class" ([why X must be massive](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain.md)). A signature caveat is "Flagged, not glossed": the chain is "the Euclidean-rigorous skeleton" ([Open: Lorentzian dressing](../../phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing.md)).
- **§4, consequences, "with one honest downgrade".** The gate comes from the χ quanta's extent on the [momentum fiber](../../phase-space/reciprocity/momentum-fibers.md#quanta-with-a-width-along-the-fiber), and "Wavepacket overlap of Gaussian profiles gives exactly e^{−(p₁−p₂)²/2μ²} — the postulated gate, with **μ = the χ quantum's own p-width**"; but generic vertices give power-law tails, so "row 1's 'exponential cliff' weakens to 'sharp knee at v\*, with the tail shape (Gaussian vs rational) a diagnostic of the vertex completion.'" ([the gate mechanism](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md); [row 1](../../predictions/self-interaction-knee.md)). The relation of this exponent to the notebook's e^{−k²/μ²} is queried in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 17. [#6 §4](../../../notes/x-classification.md#4-consequences-downstream-d--structural-with-one-honest-downgrade) is also in flag 55's list. The gate-mechanism note writes the overlap out in two ways: as an ordinary potential local in position, of range ħ/μ = 9.87 pm, whose amplitude tends to 1, not 0, at large k and whose tails are power laws even for Gaussian profiles, "contrary to [#6 §4](../../../notes/x-classification.md#4-consequences-downstream-d--structural-with-one-honest-downgrade)"; or as a pair-channel interaction that gives [#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate)'s gate, exactly when the transfer factor is flat, but contains no X. The readings are that note's reconstruction; #6 writes neither down ([`notes/gate-mechanism.md` §3](../../../notes/gate-mechanism.md#3-the-fiber-overlap-read-two-ways); [the fiber overlap, read two ways](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#the-fiber-overlap-read-two-ways)). The note also logs "A new door, deliberately left closed today": X-condensate dark matter ([the X-condensate candidate](../../dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate.md)).
- **§5, the record.** The single remaining computation: derive the X-potential from the η-selection sector "and test it against the dRGT coefficient family", dRGT being [de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley massive gravity](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity.md). "Pass ⇒ the geometric core stands and 'theory' becomes the defensible word."

## #7: the tuning test

"The field-equation problem, Chunk A-iii: The tuning test". Status: "computed. Outcome: conditional pass — the condition identified, both branches computed or classified, and the failure mode exhibited explicitly."

- **§1, the object.** The X mass must come from "a misalignment potential penalizing incompatibility between the polarization J and the modular field Z", measured by the anticommutator {J, I[Z]} ([the misalignment potential](../../gravity/tuning-test/misalignment-potential.md)).
- **§2, the metric tier [D — rigorous].** Block multiplication gives {J₀, I[Z]} = [[S, A], [A, S]] with S = Y + XY⁻¹X − Y⁻¹ and A = [X, Y⁻¹]. Any positive quadratic invariant then gives "m²Tr(h²) — a graviton mass **without** the −(Tr h)² companion", which violates Fierz–Pauli at linear order; and the nonlinear structure Tr(g²) + Tr(g⁻²) lies outside the Hassan–Rosen family. "Every metric-tier (second-order, H-coupled) misalignment invariant tested lands off the tuning" ([the metric tier fails twice](../../gravity/tuning-test/metric-tier-failures.md); [the Fierz–Pauli mass term](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli.md)). Three statements of this section are queried in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md): item 3, the zero set of the potential; item 4, a missing −h² in the expansion of S; and item 14, which square-root eigenvalues p₄ refers to. Items 4 and 14 leave the conclusion unchanged; item 3 asks whether the larger zero set affects the claims about the vacuum structure.
- **§3, the frame tier [D — structural, resting on T].** In vielbein variables the ghost-free potentials are wedge polynomials, and the symplectic vielbein E with H = EᵀE lives "on the same geometric tier" as J. Wedge couplings of (E, E₀[J]) are "*precisely* the Hinterbichler–Rosen class: ghost-free by construction". "**Verdict of the tuning test:** the theory *fails* in second-order (metric) variables and *passes* in first-order (frame) variables" ([the frame tier](../../gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier.md); [vielbein formulation and wedge potentials](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation.md); [the verdict: a conditional pass](../../gravity/tuning-test/conditional-pass.md)).
- **§4, constrained bimetric gravity [D — structural].** "**Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity with the second frame constrained to a compact moduli space Λ(d)** — a *predictive truncation* of bimetric theory" ([constrained bimetric gravity](../../gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric.md)).
- **§5, the record.** Remaining: the βₙ coefficients, "the last computation", and the checks that τ is not eaten, the Lorentzian dressing, and the couplings of ordinary matter against graviton-mass bounds. "**Program-level note:** three structural tests in a row (multi-graviton no-go, signature obstruction, tuning test) could each have killed the geometric sector outright".

## #8: the β coefficients

"The field-equation problem, Chunk A-iv: The β coefficients — closing the problem". Status: "computed. Reciprocity fixes the potential's structure, cancels its vacuum energy by theorem, and cuts the free parameters from five to two. The field-equation problem is solved in structure."

- **§1, the palindrome [D].** Reciprocity acts on the Hinterbichler–Rosen sector as the exchange of the two frames, which forces "the **palindromic constraint**" βₙ = β₄₋ₙ, the "interchange-symmetric branch" of bimetric theory: "**its ℤ₂ is Born reciprocity.**" ([the palindrome](../../gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome.md)).
- **§2, the vacuum-energy theorem [D — rigorous, four lines].** With eₙ(1) = C(4, n) and the tadpole T, the palindrome gives V(1) = 2T; a flat aligned vacuum needs V(1) = T, so "**T = 0 and V(1) = 0 exactly.**" The massive-gravity sector "contributes *nothing* to the cosmological constant". The note flags its novelty "to verify" ([the vacuum-energy identity](../../gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity.md)). [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 6 notes that §2's wording, which counts the flatness condition as one condition, is the accurate one, against "by symmetry rather than tuning" elsewhere; its audit adds that §2's "two tunings" holds only at fixed c = 1, c being the ratio of the two metrics' backgrounds.
- **§3, spectrum and parameters [D + T].** "Parameter count: five βₙ → palindrome (−2) → T = 0 (−1) → **two**: the Fierz–Pauli mass combination m_FP² ∝ β₁ + 2β₂ + β₃ = 2(β₁+β₂), i.e. **M_X**, and one orthogonal shape parameter". The massless graviton is reciprocity-even and X reciprocity-odd (on the parities under [#11 §6](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field)'s Z → −Z⁻¹, see [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 39); safety for ordinary matter "is by **range**, not decoupling"; graviton-mass bounds from gravitational waves "apply to the even eigenstate, which is exactly massless"; and τ is not eaten [D — leading order]. See [spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity.md), [safety by range](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety.md) and [τ is not eaten](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten.md). Its "five-function freedom" is queried in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) item 8, since the βₙ are constants; item 5 records that [#9](../../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md) and #11 later wrote "M_X ∝ 2(β₁+β₂)" where this note has m_FP².
- **§4, the answer to Born.** "Momentum-space geometry is carried by X = Re Z, the reciprocity-odd partner of the metric. It is a **massive spin-2 field**", with "two free parameters, vacuum energy exactly zero by the palindrome theorem" ([Born's answer](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer.md)).
- **§5, the record.** "**The field-equation problem: solved in structure.** Open residue, none of it existential": the [wedge reduction](../../gravity/tuning-test/wedge-reduction.md) from S_H, Lorentzian dressing, [τ–Stückelberg mixing](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-stuckelberg-mixing.md), the [dual-stress enhancement](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress-enhancement.md), and the literature check on Λ = 0 ([the partially-massless comparison](../../gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison.md)). "**The gate problem:** mechanism found (X-exchange, μ = fiber width); shape completion open" (on the mechanism, flag 55, above). "**The clumping problem:** closed (negative)".
