*The three main problems are the three open problems that the program's first write-up, the lab notebook [#1](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md), ranked most severe: the field-equation problem, the gate problem and the clumping problem. Calc notes [#2](../../notes/stream-stability.md) to [#8](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md) were written to close them. The field-equation problem asked for the field equation of momentum-space geometry, the question left open since Born's paper of 1938. The gate problem asked where the velocity gate comes from, the factor that confines the self-interaction of conjugate matter to particles of nearly equal velocity. The clumping problem asked whether that interaction makes dark matter gather into streams of shared velocity, the program's founding image. The clumping problem was closed first, against the program: such clumping is nowhere required. The founding image itself is P.R.'s open conjecture, with those results standing. The field-equation problem was closed in structure: momentum-space geometry is carried by a massive spin-2 field of a known class of bimetric gravity, though the class was selected rather than derived from the program's action. The gate's mechanism, exchange of that field, came out of the first field-equation note; the gate's exact shape is still open. A note of 2026-10-28 finds that exchanging that field cannot produce the gate, and proposes that the gate return to the status of a postulate; the proposal is pending (flag 55).*

## What the main problems were

The lab notebook, #1 ("Version 0.1, September 2026"), set the program out as postulates, an action and a table of predictions ([the lab notebook](documents/lab-notebook.md)). Its summary ends with "three main problems standing between this document and a paper" ([#1 §12](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#12-one-paragraph-summary)): the first three entries of its §11, "Open problems, in order of severity". Quotations from #1 and from the clumping notes on this page are transcribed from their LaTeX.

1. "The field equation for H beyond minisuperspace — the question Born left open in 1938. The action exists (Born connection [T]), but solutions, the section condition on curved backgrounds, and the full ghost analysis do not. [O]" H is the positive generalized metric of postulate [P1](../program/postulates/p1-arena.md), which carries the spacetime metric g and a momentum-space metric g̃ ([the structures ω, η, H](../phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md)). Its action, built from the curvature of [the Born connection](../phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection.md), had been used only in minisuperspace, the reduction of cosmology to a few homogeneous modes ([the v0.1 action](../gravity/tuning-test/geometric-action.md); [minisuperspace](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/friedmann-cosmology.md)). The missing equation would answer what calc note #8 calls "the 88-year question 'what is the dynamics of momentum-space geometry?'" ([§4](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#4-the-field-equation-problem-the-answer-to-born)), the years counted from Born's paper of 1938 ([#11 §3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question); [Born's question](../program/borns-question.md)).
2. "Gate-shape derivation: obtain e^{−k²/μ²} (or its true form) from S_H + S_mix rather than postulating it. The σ(v) cliff is robust to the exact shape; the sub-v\* profile is not. [O]" In v0.1 the self-interaction of [conjugate matter](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/_index.md) χ carries a Gaussian factor in the incoming relative momentum k, where an ordinary force depends on the momentum transfer q; the gate's existence was a postulate [P] ([#1 §3](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#3-action); [the velocity gate](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md)). On the two forms the gate's exponent takes in #1 and calc note #6, see flag 17 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md).
3. "Stream-instability growth rate γ vs. orbital phase-mixing: determines whether Prediction 3's granularity survives in Milky-Way-like halos. [O]" The notebook expected the gated attraction to break a halo's velocity distribution into "discrete velocity streams of width Δv ~ v\*" ([#1 §5.2](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#52-collective-behavior-clumping-in-momentum-space-d-at-mean-field-level-o-beyond)), the founding image of dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md)), behind rows 3 and 4 of its predictions table ([Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md); [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md)). It called the growth rate "the theory's most important undone calculation on the astrophysics side".

The other three entries, [polarization-genesis](../dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis.md), the relic abundance and extensivity, are not among the main problems. The main problems keep their numbers from that list: the heading of addendum v0.2 reads "Addendum (v0.2, after the clumping notes): resolution of open problem 3".

## The clumping problem

### The particle island: no growth to race

Calc note #2 ([`notes/stream-stability.md`](../../notes/stream-stability.md)) took the mean-field, or Vlasov, limit of the gated interaction and asked whether a smooth Maxwellian distribution of velocities is linearly unstable ([Vlasov stability and Landau damping](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/vlasov-stability.md); [the gated Vlasov model](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/gated-vlasov-model.md)). It found a threshold, which [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) writes as g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)²: the attraction among co-moving neighbours must be comparable to the kinetic energy of the [velocity spread](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/velocity-dispersion.md). Below it, perturbations are damped, which changed the question the notebook had posed: "There is no race with orbital stirring below threshold — the flocking never starts. The earlier 'γ vs Ω' framing presumed growth exists; it exists only above this line."

At [the scattering benchmark](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark.md) the fit to [dwarf-galaxy](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md) cores fixes σ₀/m = 30 cm²/g, "hence g is *determined*", and the criterion falls short by 28–31 orders of magnitude (#2; 10²⁸–10³¹ in [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)); "the H-theorem closes the collisional route" ([the mean-field criterion and its shortfall](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion.md)). The corollary, [the classicality theorem](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem.md): meeting the criterion at fixed σ₀/m "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰ — collective momentum-space behavior and classical particles are mutually exclusive" ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); on the regime in which #2 evaluates it, see flag 19 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)). With so many quanta per state, dark matter behaves as a classical wave ([occupation number](../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number.md)).

### The wave island: the race, and where it was required

Calc note #3 ([`notes/wave-island.md`](../../notes/wave-island.md)) took that branch, [the wave island](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md). A wave halo relaxes by Bose-enhanced wave kinetics, the mechanism that the Levkov–Panin–Tkachev (LPT) papers established for gravity ([wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics.md)), and the gate adds a channel:

```text
Γ ≈ c·g²ρ²min(σ, v*)⁴/(m³σ⁶)
```

("Bose-enhanced, LPT-style [13†]; coefficients [O]", [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [the gated condensation rate](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate.md)). Here the race is real: streams form where Γ exceeds the orbital rate Ω = 1/t_dyn. In [Scenario A](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md) the gated channel also makes the cores of classical dwarf galaxies, and "streams lose everywhere by 10²–10⁴ — *except ultra-faint dwarfs*", where condensation becomes mandatory. In Scenario B the cores come from elsewhere, the coupling is free, and "streams are *tunable*, not mandatory" across "ten orders of magnitude", which #3 calls "a degeneration flag" ([Scenario B](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b.md)).

Calc note #4 ([`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md)) followed Scenario A into the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies (UFDs). In Segue 1 the rate ratio is ~660 and condensation takes ~10⁴ yr ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). Because the gated interaction is attractive, the condensate collapses in a bosenova whenever it passes a critical mass, M_crit ≈ 2.9×10⁻³⁰(m/eV)^{−7/4} M_⊙ ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)), ejecting fast waves that leave the galaxy, so the halo is drained ([attractive condensates](../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates.md)). The observed ultra-faint dwarfs are dense and old: "Scenario A is falsified by the existence of ultra-faint dwarfs." The light-mass branch that escapes the collapse is excluded independently ([bounds on fuzzy dark matter](../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds.md)). Scenario A is under review ([Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md)).

### The verdict

Calc note #4 closed the problem with [the net theorem](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem.md):

> "Nowhere in the viable parameter space of BBT is velocity-space clumping a mandatory phenomenon. It is forbidden for classical-particle χ, fatal where it would have been mandatory (Scenario A), and merely optional elsewhere (Scenario B, ten tunable orders of magnitude — a degeneration flag, not a prediction)."

([`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md), 'Verdict and closure of the clumping problem'; BBT is Broken-Born theory, the program's earlier name.) Addendum v0.2 of the notebook reports it as "The clumping problem is settled, and the answer went against the theory" and strikes rows 3 and 4, "permanently" in its words ([Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md); [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md)). The founding image itself is P.R.'s open conjecture, with the results above standing, and Scenario A and the two rows are under review ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested), [Appendix A item 3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates); [claims under review](../status/under-review.md)).

- **The island discriminant.** The self-interaction is cut off as a sharp knee for particle χ and as (v\*/σ)⁴ for wave χ, so the knee's shape identifies the branch ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). It is now [row 3 of the predictions table](../predictions/island-discriminant.md).

- **A quarantined sector.** [#11 §15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment) counts the "tunable wave-island collective sector (quarantined)" among its [degeneration risks](../program/assessment.md).
- **The topological loophole.** The clumping problem tested collective dynamics only; a single topological object localized in momentum "remains untested; its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model); [Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md)).

## The field-equation problem

### From the plan to a restatement

Calc note #4 ends with a plan: linearize S_H on the broken vacuum, import the known spectrum of linearized [double field theory](../phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory.md), run a [ghost](../gravity/spin-2-fields/ghosts.md) check "with ω, η frozen", and extract the equation for the momentum-space block g̃. The plan kept the notebook's postulate P2, under which both the symplectic form ω and the neutral metric η are fixed ([Original P2](../program/postulates/original-p2.md)).

Calc note #5 ([`notes/geometric-core.md`](../../notes/geometric-core.md), an "Overnight working note") changed the plan. Its first result, [the compatibility theorem](../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md), showed that under P2 there was no independent momentum-space geometry for the plan to find: with ω and η both rigid, H = diag(g, g⁻¹), so g̃ = g⁻¹ ([the corollaries](../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries.md)), and "S_H is a functional of g alone: a pure metric theory. No momentum graviton, hence no geometric origin for the χ gate either" ([#5 §B](../../notes/geometric-core.md#b-the-inconsistency-and-the-forced-repair-d--structural)). The note made only ω rigid and η emergent, the [order parameter](../symmetry-breaking/order-parameter.md) ([P2′](../program/postulates/p2-prime.md)), a repair forced by [the nontriviality theorem](../program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem.md): "BBT has content beyond GR + SIDM iff η is emergent", GR being [general relativity](../gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity.md) and SIDM [self-interacting dark matter](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter.md). With only ω rigid, H is described by [the modular field Z = X + iY](../phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z.md), with Im Z the spacetime metric and Re Z = X a new symmetric tensor, the [momentum graviton](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), and the problem became one sector of one field: "The field-equation problem, restated exactly: the field equation for momentum-space geometry is the Re Z sector of S_H" ([#5 §C](../../notes/geometric-core.md#c-the-modular-field-d--structural-math-facts-standard)). The decisive computation, a classification of X at quadratic order as healthy, auxiliary or ghost, was defined and "Not completed tonight" ([§D](../../notes/geometric-core.md#d-quadratic-spectrum-and-the-ghost-question-partial)). #5 tagged the compatibility theorem "[D — rigorous]"; after review round 3 attributed it to Freidel, Leigh and Minic, #11 v2.1 re-tagged it [T][V] ([#11 §5](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem)).

### X is a massive spin-2 field

Calc note #6 ([`notes/x-classification.md`](../../notes/x-classification.md)) found a sharper answer than any of the three outcomes. A constant shift of X is a symmetry, so X is never auxiliary ([shift symmetry](../gravity/momentum-graviton/shift-symmetry.md)). Locally the shifts act as δX_μν = ∂₍μλ_ν₎, so X is a second graviton ([dual diffeomorphisms](../gravity/momentum-graviton/dual-diffeomorphisms.md); [the symplectic partner of the graviton](../gravity/momentum-graviton/symplectic-partner.md)). Massless, it is excluded by [the multi-graviton no-go theorem](../gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go.md) [T], and ungauged by [the Lorentz signature](../gravity/spin-2-fields/signature-obstruction.md). The vacuum's choice of [polarization](../phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations.md) breaks the dual diffeomorphisms, X absorbs the four would-be Goldstone modes ([Goldstone modes and the Higgs mechanism](../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-and-higgs.md)), and it becomes massive, with "mass M_X = the polarization-breaking scale" ([§3](../../notes/x-classification.md#3-the-mass-mechanism-and-the-endpoint-d--structural); [why X must be massive](../gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain.md); [the gravitational Higgs mechanism](../gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs.md)). Calc note [#8 §3](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#3-spectrum-parameters-selection-structure-d--t) words the eaten modes differently, as the vector π_μ and a longitudinal scalar, and no document reconciles the two wordings ([conflicts between sources](documents/source-conflicts.md#gravity-and-symmetry-breaking)). Massive spin-2 coupled to gravity "has exactly one known consistent structure: the Fierz–Pauli tuning at quadratic order completed by a dRGT/Hassan–Rosen-class potential" ([§3](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#3-spectrum-parameters-selection-structure-d--t)), dRGT being de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley ([dRGT massive gravity](../gravity/spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity.md); [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](../gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric.md)). The verdict, "massive spin-2, conditionally healthy", came with the stakes:

> "The single remaining computation of the field-equation problem: derive the X-potential from the η-selection (J–Z) sector and test it against the dRGT coefficient family. Pass ⇒ the geometric core stands and 'theory' becomes the defensible word. Fail ⇒ the Boulware–Deser ghost kills the geometric sector, and BBT survives only as its phenomenological shell (gated SIDM + Maslov quintessence with the geometric story demoted to heuristics)." ([#6 §5](../../notes/x-classification.md#5-the-field-equation-problem-after-this-chunk))

### The tuning test

Calc note [#7](../../notes/tuning-test.md) ([`notes/tuning-test.md`](../../notes/tuning-test.md)) built the mass from a potential that penalizes misalignment between the polarization J and the modular field, through the anticommutator {J, I[Z]} ([the tuning test](../gravity/tuning-test/_index.md); [the misalignment potential](../gravity/tuning-test/misalignment-potential.md)). Built from the metric, it fails twice: at linear order the graviton mass lacks the −(Tr h)² term of the Fierz–Pauli form, and the nonlinear structure lies outside the Hassan–Rosen family; "Every metric-tier (second-order, H-coupled) misalignment invariant tested lands off the tuning" ([§2](../../notes/tuning-test.md#2-explicit-computation-metric-tier-d--rigorous); [the metric tier fails twice](../gravity/tuning-test/metric-tier-failures.md); [the Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Boulware–Deser ghost](../gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli.md)). Built from frames, as [wedge polynomials](../gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation.md) of the symplectic vielbein, it falls in the Hinterbichler–Rosen class, "ghost-free by construction" ([§3](../../notes/tuning-test.md#3-the-frame-tier-d--structural-resting-on-t); [the frame tier](../gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier.md)). The verdict: "the theory *fails* in second-order (metric) variables and *passes* in first-order (frame) variables", a pass conditional on the choice of variables, frames rather than metrics, which the note argues are the natural ones ([the verdict](../gravity/tuning-test/conditional-pass.md)), giving "Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity with the second frame constrained to a compact moduli space Λ(d)" ([constrained bimetric gravity](../gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric.md)).

### The palindrome and the answer

Calc note #8 ([`notes/beta-coefficients.md`](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md)) computed the coefficients βₙ of the wedge potential. [Reciprocity](../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md) acts on it as the exchange of the two frames, which forces [the palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](../gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome.md). Under it the potential at the aligned point is twice the tadpole, V(1) = 2T, while a flat aligned vacuum requires V(1) = T, so T = 0 and V(1) = 0: the wedge sector contributes no vacuum energy ([the vacuum-energy identity](../gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity.md)). The five βₙ reduce to two parameters, the massless graviton is the reciprocity-even mode and X the odd one (on the parities under [#11 §6](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field)'s Z → −Z⁻¹, see flag 39 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)), and τ, the [dark-energy field](../cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence.md), is not absorbed by X at leading order ([spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](../gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity.md); [τ is not eaten](../gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-not-eaten.md)). The note's §4, "The field-equation problem: the answer to Born", gives the answer:

> "Momentum-space geometry is carried by X = Re Z, the reciprocity-odd partner of the metric. It is a massive spin-2 field — massless is forbidden by the multi-graviton no-go, and the mass is generated by the Higgsing of dual diffeomorphisms when the vacuum selects a polarization."

([Born's answer](../gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer.md) sets it out clause by clause.) The note's status line reads "The field-equation problem is solved in structure"; addendum v0.6 is headed "the β coefficients — the field-equation problem closed". The algebra of #5, #7 and #8 is [Tier 1](../method/claim-tiers.md) in [#11 §2](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#2-methodological-preface-provenance-and-verification) and machine-checked [V]: the compatibility theorem (T1), the [Siegel](../phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain.md) parametrization (T2), the blocks of the misalignment potential (T3), the non-Fierz–Pauli mass (T4), the palindrome identity in exact integers (T5) and two facts about symmetric polynomials (T6, T7). In the gate's run of 2026-10-21 every check passed, T5 printing "V(1) - 2T = [0,0,0]" ([`verify/battery.py`](../../verify/battery.py), run by [`scripts/check.sh`](../../scripts/check.sh)).

## The gate problem

### The mechanism

The gate's mechanism came out of the first field-equation note, subtitled "(plus an unplanned result on the gate problem)". Its §E, "The gate problem — the mechanism, found [D — structural]", traces the gate to the modular field. Conjugate matter couples to H universally (postulate [P4](../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md)), and to the X block through its [dual stress T̃](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress.md); "Exchange of X between two such bilinears yields a four-point amplitude that depends on the incoming relative momenta of the pair — the variable no x-local mediator can produce, and exactly the variable the gate was postulated in." The note's summary:

```text
gate = momentum-graviton exchange,    μ ↔ (M_X, M_B) parametrically
```

The shape it left open: "The gate shape is the X propagator's structure on the momentum fibers — open [O]" ([#5 §E](../../notes/geometric-core.md#e-the-gate-problem--the-mechanism-found-d--structural)). Addendum v0.3 records the step as an upgrade of open problem 2: "the mechanism is X-exchange between χ dual-stress bilinears, which produces dependence on incoming relative momenta — the defining property of the gate; the shape awaits the X propagator."

Calc note #6 completed the identification ([§4](../../notes/x-classification.md#4-consequences-downstream-d--structural-with-one-honest-downgrade); [the gate mechanism](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md)). Integrating out the massive X between χ bilinears gives a contact interaction, and the χ quanta are "extended objects on the momentum fiber with width μ, and they exchange X only over their fiber overlap", which the note calls "the dual of how x-extended composites acquire form factors F(qR)": a contact force acts where two waveforms overlap, and the gate acts only where their spectra overlap as well. Gaussian profiles reproduce the postulated Gaussian gate, with μ the quantum's own momentum width in place of #5's relation to M_X and M_B; generic vertices give power-law tails. The notebook's lead prediction was downgraded accordingly: "row 1's 'exponential cliff' weakens to 'sharp knee at v\*, with the tail shape (Gaussian vs rational) a diagnostic of the vertex completion.'" (the note writes v_\* for v\*; [row 1](../predictions/self-interaction-knee.md); [the cliff, superseded](../predictions/self-interaction-knee.md#superseded-the-exponential-cliff-of-v01)). [#8 §5](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#5-what-remains-honest-close-out) records the problem as "The gate problem: mechanism found (X-exchange, μ = fiber width); shape completion open (Gaussian vs rational tail — now also an observable discriminant)", and [#11 §8](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate) tags it "[D — mechanism; shape [O]]".

### The mechanism questioned

[`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) (2026-10-28) asks whether exchanging X produces the gate at all. Two derivations agree, the first by a theory verifier of the momentum-gravity investigation and the second blind and pre-registered; the note has not been reviewed, and results only one derivation computed are marked so in it ([the gate mechanism](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)):

For on-shell χ, any χ–χ–X vertex depends only on the momentum transfer t, so single exchange of X gives an amplitude that depends on the incoming relative momentum k only as a polynomial of degree at most two in k², with velocity scale c: a change of 2.4×10⁻⁷ at 120 km/s, where the gate is 0.61 (note §2; flag 55).
- **The fiber overlap, written out** [D][V; one derivation]. [#6 §4](../../notes/x-classification.md#4-consequences-downstream-d--structural-with-one-honest-downgrade)'s overlap is either an ordinary potential local in position, of range ħ/μ ≈ 10 pm, with power-law tails, or a pair-channel interaction that gives the gate but contains no X. These two readings are the note's reconstruction; the record constructs neither, and its section condition makes X independent of the fiber coordinates (note §3; [the fiber overlap, read two ways](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#the-fiber-overlap-read-two-ways)).
- **Proposed tags** (flag 55, pending). The gate F(k²/μ²) and μ: [P], as [#1 §3](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#3-action) (v0.1) tagged them; the mechanism: [O]; tree-level exchange of [§7](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#7-cosmology-ii-dark-energy-its-drift-and-the-turnaround)'s X as the mechanism: excluded [D][V] (note §5). The text that depends on the tag includes [#5 §E](../../notes/geometric-core.md#e-the-gate-problem--the-mechanism-found-d--structural), [#6 §4](../../notes/x-classification.md#4-consequences-downstream-d--structural-with-one-honest-downgrade), [#8 §5](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md#5-what-remains-honest-close-out) ("**The gate problem:** mechanism found (X-exchange, μ = fiber width)"), [`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen) ("gate = X-exchange") and [§3](../../HANDOFF.md#3-artifact-inventory-creation-order) (#5 "finds the gate's mechanism"). The flag rates itself high.
- **What does not change.** The gate itself is not refuted, the benchmark arithmetic "is contact Born physics and needs no mechanism", and the results of the clumping calculations are unaffected (note §5). The founding image is P.R.'s open conjecture ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)).
