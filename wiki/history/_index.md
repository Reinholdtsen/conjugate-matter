*The history section records how the Broken-Born program developed: when each result, correction and review happened, and which document holds it. The program was developed with Claude on claude.ai in September and October 2026 and moved to Claude Code and this repository on 2026-10-19, so its record has two layers: documents carried over from the chat, most of them undated and known only in the order they were written, and dated, version-controlled files from the move onward. The section makes no physics claims of its own; it says where the claims came from and in what order.*

**Stands:** nothing in this section, which holds records rather than claims ([claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive)) · **Died:** nothing in this section ([the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard)) · **Open:** nothing in this section ([open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems))

## Map

- [Timeline](/wiki/history/timeline) comes first because it answers "when": dated events from Born's 1938 question, through the claude.ai era, to the commits of 2026-10-18 to 2026-10-20 and the next data release of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument, the program's nearest external test.
- [The three debts](/wiki/history/debts) follows because the debts organized the early work. They were the lab notebook's three most severe open problems: the equations for momentum-space geometry (Debt 1), the mechanism and shape of the velocity gate (Debt 2), and the test of momentum clumping (Debt 3). The page records how each was closed or left open.
- [Documents](/wiki/history/documents) closes the section. It maps the artifacts #1–#15 and the files written since the move, gives each one's standing, and states the order of precedence that settles disagreements between them; the cases themselves are on [Conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).
