*The three debts are the three most severe open problems of the program's first write-up, the lab notebook #1; the program named them Debt 1, Debt 2 and Debt 3 and worked through them in its first weeks. Debt 1 asked for the equations that govern the geometry of momentum space, the question Born's 1938 proposal had left open. Debt 2 asked what produces the velocity gate in dark matter's self-interaction and what shape it has. Debt 3 asked whether dark matter really clumps by velocity, the program's founding image. Debt 3 was settled first and went against the program; Debt 1 was settled in structure, with smaller items left open; Debt 2's mechanism was found and its shape is still open. The checkpoint #11 keeps the three names as labels on its §7–§9; the order of the work is on the [Timeline](/wiki/history/timeline).*

**Status:** Record — the debts entered #1 §11 as open problems tagged [O]. Debt 3 was closed negative by calc note #4 (`HANDOFF.md` §3: "closes Debt 3 (negative)"); Debt 1 was closed structurally by calc note #8 ("closes Debt 1 (structural)"); Debt 2's mechanism was found in calc note #5, and its shape is #11 §14 item 4, open.

## Key facts

- **Origin** (#1 §11–§12). The notebook's summary ends with "three named debts standing between this document and a paper". They are open problems 1–3 of its §11, which lists them "in order of severity": (1) "The field equation for H beyond minisuperspace — Born's 88-year debt" [O]; (2) "Gate-shape derivation: obtain e^{−k²/μ²} (or its true form) from S_H + S_mix rather than postulating it" [O]; (3) "Stream-instability growth rate γ vs. orbital phase-mixing" [O]. The numbering is the documents' own: addendum v0.2 is headed "post Debt-3: resolution of open problem 3", and #11 labels §7 (the momentum graviton) "Debt 1", §8 (conjugate matter and the gate) "Debt 2" and §9 (the death of momentum clumping) "Debt 3".
- **Debt 3, settled first and negative** (calc notes #2–#4; [the Debt-3 notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-3-notes)). "Debt 3 is paid, and the answer went against the theory" (#1 addendum v0.2). For particle dark matter the clumping criterion fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark [D]; on the wave side, the one scenario in which clumping was mandatory is falsified by ultra-faint dwarf galaxies. The result is [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem), "Nowhere in the viable parameter space of BBT is velocity-space clumping a mandatory phenomenon" (#4; BBT is the Broken-Born framework), and #11 §9 reports it as the obituary of [the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image). One question survived it: whether a single topological object could be localized in momentum, which "remains untested, but has no candidate" (#11 §12; [the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)).
- **Debt 1, settled in structure** (calc notes #5–#8; [the Debt-1 notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes)). The work ran from the [compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) and the forced repair of P2 to [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime), through X as a second graviton that [must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain), to [the tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test) and [the palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome). Calc note #8: "Debt 1 is structurally paid." The answer it gives to Born is that momentum-space geometry is carried by a massive, reciprocity-odd spin-2 field ([Born's answer](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer)).
- **What "structurally" leaves open.** #8 §5 lists a residue, "none of it existential": the wedge reduction from S_H, Lorentzian dressing, τ–Stückelberg mixing, the dual-stress enhancement and a literature check on the Λ = 0 observation. These are #11 §14 items 1, 3, 6, 5 and 2 [O]. By #11 §15 the word "theory" "still waits on items 1–3": [the wedge reduction](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/wedge-reduction), [the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison) and [Lorentzian dressing](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing) ([Assessment](/wiki/program/assessment)).
- **Debt 2, half settled** (#5 §E; #6 §4). Calc note #5 carries the subtitle "(plus an unplanned payment on Debt 2)": the gate is X exchange between dual-stress bilinears, and #6 identifies μ as the width of a χ quantum on its momentum fiber ([the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism)). #8 §5 records the state: "mechanism paid (X-exchange, μ = fiber width); shape completion open (Gaussian vs rational tail — now also an observable discriminant)". The shape is #11 §14 item 4 [O] ([Open: the gate shape](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape)).
- **The labels across versions.** The program v1.0 (#9) headed its sections "(Debt 1: paid)", "(Debt 2: mechanism paid)" and "(Debt 3: closed, negative)"; #11 keeps only the bare labels "(Debt 1)", "(Debt 2)" and "(Debt 3)" ([the checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint)). `HANDOFF.md` §3 records the same outcomes per artifact: #4 "closes Debt 3 (negative)", #5 "pays Debt 2's mechanism", #8 "closes Debt 1 (structural)".
