The work done on claude.ai in September and October 2026 is almost entirely undated, so those events follow the creation order of the artifacts in [`HANDOFF.md` §3](../../HANDOFF.md#3-artifact-inventory-creation-order). ## How the record is dated

The record has two layers, and 2026-10-26 is dated differently.

- **Before the move.** Artifacts #1–[#15](../../review/skeptic-review-console.html) were written in one conversation on claude.ai and carry almost no dates. #1's header reads "Version 0.1, September 2026". The handoff says the program was "developed September–October 2026" ([`HANDOFF.md` §1](../../HANDOFF.md#1-what-this-project-is)) and dates two events, the first attempt at round 3 ([§6](../../HANDOFF.md#6-review-history-and-the-measured-failure-mode)) and its own preparation (footer). Everything else is ordered by the handoff's inventory, headed "Artifact inventory (creation order)" ([§3](../../HANDOFF.md#3-artifact-inventory-creation-order)), and by the version headers and addenda of the documents. The first popular article, [#10](../../popsci/angle-of-the-mirror.md), describes the work it narrates as "a week" of stress-testing (its subtitle).
- **From the move.** The repository's commits date every later step. Each row gives the time of the commit that recorded the event (local time, −0700) and its hash, so `git show` with the hash displays the change. The time is when the record was committed: an event such as a blind audit ran between the commits that bracket it, and its own time is given only where a document records it.
- **2026-10-26.** The work on the phase-space-diameter note had not been committed when its rows were written: the round-5 pre-registration records that it was not committed, the adjudication gives the reason, "the session had no commit authorization until afterwards", and the note, its script, the review records and the edits to [`scripts/check.sh`](../../scripts/check.sh) and the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) stood uncommitted in the working tree. Those rows give the times of the entries in [`notes/phase-space-diameter-worklog.md`](../../notes/phase-space-diameter-worklog.md), local time like the commits (the dispatch is recorded as "2026-10-26T15:55-07:00"), and their commit column is empty.
