*The status section holds the registers that record where each of the program's claims stands: dead, standing, open, conjectural, or replaced by a corrected version. Each register is a list of links, one line per claim, so the state of the whole program can be read without opening the topic pages. A claim's status is taken from the most current source in a fixed order of precedence, and a claim once recorded as dead is not revived.*

**Stands:** [Claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive) · **Died:** [The graveyard: dead claims](/wiki/status/graveyard) · **Open:** [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems)

## Map

The registers run from the dead claims to the superseded ones.

- [The graveyard: dead claims](/wiki/status/graveyard) comes first because the program reports its negative results prominently. The root `CLAUDE.md` makes that a rule of method, and checkpoint #11 §2 calls negative results "the most reliable Tier-3 items, since the program's incentive gradient ran against them". Each line links an obituary and names the calculation, ruling or decision that killed the claim.
- [Claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive) lists every program claim that currently stands, section by section, with its tag and what checks it. Established literature results are not program claims and are not listed; the dark-energy sector is marked as under pressure.
- [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems) gives #11 §14's eleven open items in order, the open questions raised outside that list, the open ends of the toy models, the history of the register, and the current work queue.
- [Conjectures: chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures) keeps apart the results that exist only in the original claude.ai conversation and the claims found only in the two popular articles, each with its relation to the record.
- [Superseded statements](/wiki/status/superseded) pairs each statement the program replaced — the original P2, the exponential cliff, the version-0.1 action and others — with its replacement, and names the audit-trail documents that still contain the old form.

## How a status is decided

- **One status per page.** The registers track the statuses `dead`, `alive`, `open`, `conjecture` and `superseded`. Pages of established physics (`established`), of method (`method`), of record (`record`) and section indexes (`hub`) are not registered here.
- **Precedence, most current first** (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`):
  1. the root `CLAUDE.md`, section "Since the handoff";
  2. checkpoint #11 v2.2 (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`) and the toy-model note v2 (`papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`);
  3. the review adjudications (`review/round4-adjudication.md`, `review/round3-adjudication.md`);
  4. #13 (`papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md`);
  5. `HANDOFF.md`, whose §2 soliton candidate and §8 queue are superseded;
  6. the notes: the calc notes #2–#8 are final for their own scope, the lab notebook #1 and the program v1.0 (#9) are superseded as reference, and #14 is withdrawn;
  7. the popular articles. #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and is never a source for a claim's status.
- **Dead stays dead.** Claims killed in `HANDOFF.md` §2, in #11 §9 and §12, and in the adjudications are reported as dead on their own obituary pages, and no later framing revives them.
- **Unresolved disagreements are reported, not settled.** Where two sources of equal rank disagree and no document decides the point, the page says so instead of choosing. The cases are collected on [Conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).

## Boundaries

The registers index claims; the topic pages carry the content, and every register line links to one. The dated sequence of deaths and corrections is the [Timeline](/wiki/history/timeline); the documents, their versions and their precedence are under [Documents](/wiki/history/documents). The observational results that could change a status are on [Predictions and kill conditions](/wiki/predictions), and how a claim earns its tag is described under [Method](/wiki/method).
