*The status section holds the registers that record where each of the Comatter program's claims stands: dead, standing, open, conjectural, or replaced by a corrected version. Each register is a list of links, one entry per claim, saying where the claim stands and on what grounds, so the state of the whole program can be read without opening the topic pages. A claim's status is taken from the most current source in a fixed order of precedence. Since 2026-10-29 a claim is recorded as dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs; the deaths recorded before that date stand until he answers, and three of his founding ideas that he contested are open again as his conjectures.*

**Registers only:** every page in this section is a register with status record, so the section makes no claims of its own. The five registers are [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard), [claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive), [open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems), [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures) and [superseded statements](/wiki/status/superseded).

## Map

The registers run from the dead claims to the superseded ones.

- [The graveyard: dead claims](/wiki/status/graveyard) comes first, following the root `CLAUDE.md` rule that negative results are reported prominently ([negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results)). It gives every obituary page with what was claimed, what killed it and the ruling or decision that settled it, then the dead items told inside other pages and the predictions withdrawn from the program's table. Paul's concurrence in each death it lists was asked on 2026-10-29, and a section of its own says where the claims it held until that day went.
- [Claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive) lists every program claim with status alive, in tables grouped by section, each line giving the claim's tags and what verifies it: a script in the gate, a review round, or the derivation, postulate or citation alone. It marks the claims under pressure, chiefly the dark-energy sector.
- [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems) gives the open items of #11 §14 in order, the open questions raised outside that list, the open ends of the toy models, the current work queue, the questions of Paul's concurrence in each recorded death, and the flags awaiting his ruling.
- [Conjectures: P.R.'s, the record's, chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures) opens with the three founding ideas of P.R.'s that #11 v2.4 restored on 2026-10-29 as his open conjectures: [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles), a third dark-matter candidate; their [explanation of the monopole null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation); and [conjugate collapse powering our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion). It keeps apart the results that exist only in the original claude.ai conversation and the claims found only in the two popular articles, each with its relation to the record, and P.R.'s long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ, a conjecture outside the record that a note of 2026-10-28 tested and found decorative at the program's gate width. It also lists the conjectures that the record itself states, among them P.R.'s [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification) (#11 §11.4), whose shared-photon form, the popular article #12's reading, the graveyard held as an obituary until flag 60 reversed it on 2026-10-29, and #14 v2's reciprocity acting as modular S, part of the conjecture's separate-photon form, which the superseded register held until the same day.
- [Superseded statements](/wiki/status/superseded) pairs each statement the program replaced, such as the original P2 and the exponential cliff, with its replacement and with where the replacement is recorded.

## How a status is decided

- **One status per page.** The registers track the program's claims, whose pages have status `dead`, `alive`, `open`, `conjecture` or `superseded`. Pages of established physics (`established`), of method (`method`), of record (`record`) and section indexes (`hub`) are not registered.
- **Status shows in titles.** A dead claim's page is titled "Obituary: …", an open problem's "Open: …", with its number OPn when #11 §14 gives it one, and a superseded statement's "… (superseded)"; chat-only and popsci-only claims carry "(chat-only)" and "(popsci-only)", and prediction pages are titled "Row n: …". Alive and established pages carry no marker.
- **Folded items.** A page sometimes holds a minor item of another status, such as an open sub-question on an established page or a superseded earlier form of an alive claim, in a section of its own headed "Open:", "Superseded:", "Chat-only:", "Popsci-only:" or "Conjecture:". The registers link those sections directly. The graveyard lists the dead items told inside other pages in the same way.
- **Precedence, most current first** (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`):
  1. the root `CLAUDE.md`, section "Since the handoff";
  2. checkpoint #11 v2.4 (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`), the toy-model note v2 (`papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`) and the phase-space-diameter note v1.1 (`papers/phase-space-diameter.md`);
  3. the review adjudications (`review/round5-adjudication.md`, `review/round4-adjudication.md`, `review/round3-adjudication.md`);
  4. #13 (`papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md`);
  5. `HANDOFF.md`, whose §2 soliton candidate and §8 queue are superseded;
  6. the notes: the calc notes #2–#8 are final for their own scope; the closed-universe note (`notes/closed-universe.md`, 2026-10-27) and the two momentum-gravity notes (`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`, `notes/gate-mechanism.md`, 2026-10-28), none of them reviewed, are current for theirs; the lab notebook #1 and the program v1.0 (#9) are superseded as reference, and #14 is withdrawn;
  7. the popular articles. #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and is never a source for a claim's status.
- **Dead needs theory, data and Paul's concurrence.** Since 2026-10-29 a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs, asked for each death separately and never inside a larger change; a failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Method'). Deaths recorded before that date, in `HANDOFF.md` §2, in #11 §9 and §12 and in the adjudications, stand until he answers the concurrence question for them, which was asked on 2026-10-29 for each; the ones he contested are recorded as open or as his conjectures, with the evidence against them stated. Dead claims are reported prominently, on their own obituary pages, and the wiki never declares a death on its own reading ([negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results)). Until 2026-10-29 the rule read "Claims listed as dead in HANDOFF §2 stay dead".
- **Tags carry over exactly.** A register copies each claim's [provenance tags](/wiki/method/provenance-tags) as its sources attach them and never upgrades one. Standing is not confirmation: a machine check confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes.
- **Unresolved disagreements are reported, not settled.** Where two sources of equal rank disagree and no document decides the point, the page says so instead of choosing. The cases are collected on [conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).

## Boundaries

The registers index claims; the topic pages carry the content, and every register entry links to one. The [executive summary](/wiki/summary) condenses the registers onto one page, for a reader who wants the program's standing before its details. The dated sequence of deaths and corrections is [the timeline](/wiki/history/timeline); the documents, their versions and their precedence are under [documents](/wiki/history/documents). The observations that could change a status are on [predictions and kill conditions](/wiki/predictions), and how a claim earns its tag, and how the scripts in the gate check it, is described under [method](/wiki/method).
