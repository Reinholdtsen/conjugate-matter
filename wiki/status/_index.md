*The status section holds the registers that record where each of the Comatter program's claims stands: standing, open, conjectural, replaced by a corrected version, under review or dead. Each register is a list of links, one entry per claim, saying where the claim stands and on what grounds, so the state of the whole program can be read without opening the topic pages. A claim's status is taken from the most current source in a fixed order of precedence.*

**Registers only:** every page in this section is a register with status record, so the section makes no claims of its own. The six registers are [claims that stand](alive.md), [open problems and the queue](open-problems.md), [conjectures](conjectures.md), [superseded statements](superseded.md), [claims under review](under-review.md) and [the graveyard](graveyard.md).

## Map

The registers run from the claims that stand, through the open problems, the conjectures and the superseded statements, to the claims under review and the one dead claim.

- [Claims that stand](alive.md) lists every program claim with status alive, in tables grouped by section, each line giving the claim's tags and what verifies it: a script in the gate, a review round, or the derivation, postulate or citation alone. It marks the claims under pressure, chiefly the dark-energy sector.
- [Open problems and the queue](open-problems.md) gives the open items of [#11 §14](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) in order, the open questions raised outside that list, the open ends of the toy models, the current work queue, the review queue of the thirteen claims under review, and the flags awaiting a ruling.
- [Conjectures: P.R.'s, the record's, chat-only and popsci-only claims](conjectures.md) opens with P.R.'s core conjectures: dark matter as [conjugate matter](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/_index.md), [velocity clumping, the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md), [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md) (a third dark-matter candidate), their [explanation of the monopole null result](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md) and [conjugate collapse powering our expansion](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion.md), two versions of which failed. It keeps apart the results that exist only in the original claude.ai conversation and the claims found only in the two popular articles, each with its relation to the record, and P.R.'s long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ, a conjecture outside the record that a note of 2026-10-28 tested and found decorative at the program's gate width. It also lists the conjectures that the record itself states, among them P.R.'s [magnetic-mirror conjecture](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md) ([#11 §11.4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#114-the-magnetic-mirror-conjecture-o-pr-v23)), whose shared-photon form is the popular article [#12](../../popsci/universes-other-half.md)'s reading, and [#14](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) v2's reciprocity acting as modular S, part of the conjecture's separate-photon form.
- [Superseded statements](superseded.md) pairs each statement the program replaced, such as the original P2 and the exponential cliff, with its replacement and with where the replacement is recorded.
- [Claims under review](under-review.md) lists the claims the calculations or data weigh against, whose final status awaits review: [Scenario A](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md) and the two prediction rows withdrawn after [the clumping problem](../history/main-problems.md#the-clumping-problem), [velocity-space granularity in halos](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md) and [Fornax friction suppression](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md); [six pieces of #14's model](under-review.md#the-pieces-of-14s-model); [two claims the phase-space-diameter note set aside](under-review.md#the-phase-space-diameter); [the naive toy potential](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential.md); and [the symmetric reading of a compact momentum space](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md#under-review-the-symmetric-reading-dv), a folded section of the page on a closed universe. Each entry says what was claimed, what tested it, whether theory, data or both, and where the evidence is.
- [The graveyard: dead claims](graveyard.md) holds one claim, [#14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md). It gives what was claimed, what killed it and the ruling or decision that settled it, then the parts of it told inside other pages and its withdrawal from the program's table. How the program reports its negative results is under [negative results and dead claims](../method/negative-results.md).

## How a status is decided

- **One status per page.** The registers track the program's claims, whose pages have status `alive`, `open`, `conjecture`, `superseded`, `under-review` or `dead`. Pages of established physics (`established`), of method (`method`), of record (`record`) and section indexes (`hub`) are not registered.
- **Status shows in titles.** A dead claim's page is titled "Obituary: …", a claim under review's "Under review: …", an open problem's "Open: …", with its number OPn when [#11 §14](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) gives it one, and a superseded statement's "… (superseded)"; chat-only and popsci-only claims carry "(chat-only)" and "(popsci-only)", and prediction pages are titled "Row n: …". Alive and established pages carry no marker.
- **Folded items.** A page sometimes holds a minor item of another status, such as an open sub-question on an established page or a superseded earlier form of an alive claim, in a section of its own headed "Open:", "Superseded:", "Chat-only:", "Popsci-only:", "Conjecture:" or "Under review:". The registers link those sections directly. The graveyard lists the dead items told inside other pages in the same way.
- **Precedence, most current first** ([`wiki/CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md)):
  1. the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), section "Since the handoff";
  2. checkpoint #11 ([`wiki/papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md)), the toy-model note ([`wiki/papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`](../papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md)) and the phase-space-diameter note ([`wiki/papers/phase-space-diameter.md`](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md));
  3. the review adjudications ([`review/round5-adjudication.md`](../../review/round5-adjudication.md), [`review/round4-adjudication.md`](../../review/round4-adjudication.md), [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md));
  4. [#13](../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md) ([`wiki/papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md`](../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md));
  5. [`HANDOFF.md`](../../HANDOFF.md), whose [§2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen) soliton candidate and [§8](../../HANDOFF.md#8-ranked-next-actions-queue) queue are superseded;
  6. the notes: the calc notes [#2](../../notes/stream-stability.md)–[#8](../../notes/beta-coefficients.md) are final for their own scope; the closed-universe note ([`notes/closed-universe.md`](../../notes/closed-universe.md), 2026-10-27), the two momentum-gravity notes ([`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`](../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md), [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../notes/gate-mechanism.md), 2026-10-28), the literature-check note ([`notes/literature-check.md`](../../notes/literature-check.md), 2026-11-02) and the dual-sector note ([`notes/dual-sector.md`](../../notes/dual-sector.md), 2026-11-06), which qualifies the literature-check note on the published work of Minic and coauthors, none of them reviewed, are current for theirs; the lab notebook [#1](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md) and the program v1.0 ([#9](../../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md)) are superseded as reference, and #14 is withdrawn;
  7. the popular articles. #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and is never a source for a claim's status.
- **The evidence stays with the claim.** A dead claim is reported prominently, on its own obituary page, which says what killed it. A claim the calculations or data weigh against, and whose final status awaits review, has the status `under-review`, with the evidence stated in full; one of them, the symmetric reading, is told in a folded section of a page whose status is alive. An open problem or a conjecture that theory or data weigh against keeps that status, with the evidence stated on its page. The failure of one model or version of an idea is recorded as that failure: #14's model is dead and the candidate it modeled is P.R.'s open conjecture, and two versions of collapse-powered expansion failed while the conjecture is open.
- **Tags carry over exactly.** A register copies each claim's [provenance tags](../method/provenance-tags.md) as its sources attach them and never upgrades one. Standing is not confirmation: a machine check confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes.
- **Unresolved disagreements are reported, not settled.** Where two sources of equal rank disagree and no document decides the point, the page says so instead of choosing. The cases are collected on [conflicts between sources](../history/documents/source-conflicts.md).

## Boundaries

The registers index claims; the topic pages carry the content, and every register entry links to one. The [executive summary](../summary.md) condenses the registers onto one page, for a reader who wants the program's standing before its details. The dated sequence of results and corrections is [the timeline](../history/timeline.md); the documents, their versions and their precedence are under [documents](../history/documents/_index.md). The observations that could change a status are on [predictions and kill conditions](../predictions/_index.md), and how a claim earns its tag, and how the scripts in the gate check it, is described under [method](../method/_index.md).
