*The status section holds the registers that record where each of the Comatter program's claims stands: dead, standing, open, conjectural, replaced by a corrected version, or under review. Each register is a list of links, one entry per claim, saying where the claim stands and on what grounds, so the state of the whole program can be read without opening the topic pages. A claim's status is taken from the most current source in a fixed order of precedence. Since 2026-10-29 the rule has been that a claim is recorded as dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs. That day he answered for the deaths recorded before it: one is dead with his concurrence, twelve are under review, not dead, until he reviews them with Claude, and four of his founding ideas the record had counted dead or retired are open again as his conjectures.*

**Registers only:** every page in this section is a register with status record, so the section makes no claims of its own. The six registers are [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard), [claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive), [open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems), [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures), [superseded statements](/wiki/status/superseded) and [claims under review](/wiki/status/under-review).

## Map

The registers run from the dead claims to the superseded ones, followed by the claims under review, a register added with the status `under-review` after Paul's answers of 2026-10-29.

- [The graveyard: dead claims](/wiki/status/graveyard) comes first, following the root `CLAUDE.md` rule that negative results are reported prominently ([negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results)). Since Paul's answers of 2026-10-29 it has held one claim, [#14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), dead with his concurrence. For it the register gives what was claimed, what killed it and the ruling or decision that settled it, then the parts of it told inside other pages and its withdrawal from the program's table. A section of its own says where the claims it held until that day went.
- [Claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive) lists every program claim with status alive, in tables grouped by section, each line giving the claim's tags and what verifies it: a script in the gate, a review round, or the derivation, postulate or citation alone. It marks the claims under pressure, chiefly the dark-energy sector.
- [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems) gives the open items of #11 §14 in order, the open questions raised outside that list, the open ends of the toy models, the current work queue, Paul's answers to the questions of his concurrence in each death recorded before 2026-10-29 with the review queue of the twelve claims under review, and the flags awaiting his ruling.
- [Conjectures: P.R.'s, the record's, chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures) opens with the four founding ideas of P.R.'s that #11 restored on 2026-10-29 as his open conjectures: in v2.5, [velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image); and in v2.4, three: [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles) (a third dark-matter candidate), their [explanation of the monopole null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation) and [conjugate collapse powering our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion), whose two failed versions are not deaths. - [Superseded statements](/wiki/status/superseded) pairs each statement the program replaced, such as the original P2 and the exponential cliff, with its replacement and with where the replacement is recorded.
- [Claims under review](/wiki/status/under-review) lists the twelve claims, recorded dead before 2026-10-29 without Paul's concurrence, that he chose that day to review with Claude: [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) and the two prediction rows withdrawn after [the clumping problem](/wiki/history/main-problems#the-clumping-problem), [velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity) and [Fornax friction suppression](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction); [six pieces of #14's model](/wiki/status/under-review#the-pieces-of-14s-model); [two claims the phase-space-diameter note set aside](/wiki/status/under-review#the-phase-space-diameter); and [the naive toy potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential). Each entry says what was claimed, what tested it, whether theory, data or both, and where the evidence is; none is dead until he reviews it and concurs.

## How a status is decided

- **One status per page.** The registers track the program's claims, whose pages have status `dead`, `under-review`, `alive`, `open`, `conjecture` or `superseded`. Pages of established physics (`established`), of method (`method`), of record (`record`) and section indexes (`hub`) are not registered.
- **Status shows in titles.** A dead claim's page is titled "Obituary: …", a claim under review's "Under review: …", an open problem's "Open: …", with its number OPn when #11 §14 gives it one, and a superseded statement's "… (superseded)"; chat-only and popsci-only claims carry "(chat-only)" and "(popsci-only)", and prediction pages are titled "Row n: …". Alive and established pages carry no marker.
- **Folded items.** A page sometimes holds a minor item of another status, such as an open sub-question on an established page or a superseded earlier form of an alive claim, in a section of its own headed "Open:", "Superseded:", "Chat-only:", "Popsci-only:" or "Conjecture:". The registers link those sections directly. The graveyard lists the dead items told inside other pages in the same way.
- **Precedence, most current first** (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`):
  1. the root `CLAUDE.md`, section "Since the handoff";
  2. checkpoint #11 v2.5 (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`), the toy-model note v2 (`papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`) and the phase-space-diameter note v1.2 (`papers/phase-space-diameter.md`);
  3. the review adjudications (`review/round5-adjudication.md`, `review/round4-adjudication.md`, `review/round3-adjudication.md`);
  4. #13 (`papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md`);
  5. `HANDOFF.md`, whose §2 soliton candidate and §8 queue are superseded;
  6. the notes: the calc notes #2–#8 are final for their own scope; the closed-universe note (`notes/closed-universe.md`, 2026-10-27) and the two momentum-gravity notes (`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`, `notes/gate-mechanism.md`, 2026-10-28), none of them reviewed, are current for theirs; the lab notebook #1 and the program v1.0 (#9) are superseded as reference, and #14 is withdrawn;
  7. the popular articles. #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and is never a source for a claim's status.
- **Dead needs theory, data and Paul's concurrence.** Since 2026-10-29 the rule has been that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs, asked for each death separately and never inside a larger change; a failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Method'). For the deaths recorded before that date, in `HANDOFF.md` §2, in #11 §9 and §12 and in the adjudications, he answered on 2026-10-29. He concurred in the death of #14's model. He did not concur in the death of the founding image, which is his conjecture again, or in those of the two failed versions of founding supposition 9, which are failed versions of an open conjecture, not deaths. The other twelve, which he chose to review with Claude, have the status `under-review`: "Each is under review, not dead, until he reviews it with Claude" (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'). The ones he contested earlier that day are recorded as open or as his conjectures, with the evidence against them stated. Dead claims are reported prominently, on their own obituary pages, and the wiki never declares a death on its own reading ([negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results)). Until 2026-10-29 the rule read "Claims listed as dead in HANDOFF §2 stay dead".
- **Tags carry over exactly.** A register copies each claim's [provenance tags](/wiki/method/provenance-tags) as its sources attach them and never upgrades one. Standing is not confirmation: a machine check confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes.
- **Unresolved disagreements are reported, not settled.** Where two sources of equal rank disagree and no document decides the point, the page says so instead of choosing. The cases are collected on [conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).

## Boundaries

The registers index claims; the topic pages carry the content, and every register entry links to one. The [executive summary](/wiki/summary) condenses the registers onto one page, for a reader who wants the program's standing before its details. The dated sequence of deaths and corrections is [the timeline](/wiki/history/timeline); the documents, their versions and their precedence are under [documents](/wiki/history/documents). The observations that could change a status are on [predictions and kill conditions](/wiki/predictions), and how a claim earns its tag, and how the scripts in the gate check it, is described under [method](/wiki/method).
