*This register lists the problems the Comatter program has not yet solved and the order in which work on them is queued. Its core is the twelve numbered open problems of [checkpoint #11](../history/documents/checkpoint.md), the program's current reference, which says that none of them is any longer about whether the structure can exist. Around that list it collects the current work queue, the open ends of the [toy models](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md), the open problems raised in the notes, the reviews and the other papers, the open sub-questions recorded on pages about other subjects, and the list of claims under review. [#11](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) says the word "theory" still waits on three of the numbered problems: deriving the interaction potential of the program's second graviton from its geometric action, comparing a vacuum-energy identity with the literature on partially massless gravity, and extending proofs made for positive-definite metrics to the signature of spacetime. The register ends with the 73 numbered flags, suspected errors in the source documents, ten suspected omissions or understatements of published work and one question about a claim's status, recorded while this wiki was built, by the investigations of 2026-10-27 to 2026-10-29, by the two reviewers of #11's version 2.4, by the literature check of 2026-11-02 and by the dual-sector investigation of 2026-11-06. Independent agents, each told to refute a flag, then re-checked the first 43. Of the first 42, 32 were confirmed, 8 depend on a convention or a reading the documents support, 2 are unclear because they rest on model memory, and none was refuted; flag 43, added later, was confirmed by both of its two checks. Flag 44, a minor point of wording raised after the audit, has not yet been audited, and flags 45–58 carry the verdicts of the verifiers who checked them. Flags 59–62, from the monopole discussion of 2026-10-29, carry audit lines of their own and are resolved in #11. Flag 63, raised the same day, notes that two prediction rows still name two dark-matter candidates where #11 counts three. Flags 64–69, from the literature check, point to published work #11 does not cite, or cites for something else, and flag 64 carries an addendum on the published identification of dark matter with the dual of visible matter. Flags 70–73, from the dual-sector investigation, point to published work #11 does not cite, or cites for less than it says. Flags 1–58 and 63–73 await a ruling.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-11-06 and to flag 73, the last item of [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md); the file dates the audit of flags 1–42 and flag 43's own audit 2026-10-22, marks flag 44 not yet audited, dates the audits of flags 45–58 and the flag-27 addendum 2026-10-27, the flag-16 addendum 2026-10-28, the audit lines of flags 59–63 2026-10-29, those of flags 64–69 2026-11-02, and those of flags 70–73 and of the flag-64 addendum 2026-11-06 — no tags of its own; each item keeps its source's tag, and the numbered items carry [O] in [#11 §14](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) — the numbered list is #11's ([`wiki/papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md` §14](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems)), the queue is the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md)'s, which supersedes [`HANDOFF.md` §8](../../HANDOFF.md#8-ranked-next-actions-queue), the list of claims under review is the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md)'s, and the flags are the 73 items of [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), each with its audit verdict where it has one: items 1–58 and 63–73 await a ruling, and items 59–62 are resolved in #11.

## The toy-model open ends

The root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) lists four open ends of the toy Lagrangian: a Monte Carlo with H dynamical; a derivation of the ρ-preserving potential; the strong form of matter locality; and the real Grosse–Wulkenhaar model's one-loop preference for the position-sharp vacuum, "a lead". The first three are the parts of acceptance criterion 1 that [#11 §14 item 11](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) still lists as open. The toy-model note's own "Not shown" list ([`wiki/papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md` §6](../papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#6-summary-and-limitations)) covers the same four and adds two, and its §4 leaves one comparison undone.

1. **A Monte Carlo with H dynamical.** [Open: a Monte Carlo with H dynamical](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/dynamical-h-monte-carlo.md) (folded on [the frozen-H reduction](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/frozen-h-reduction.md#open-a-monte-carlo-with-h-dynamical)) — the ordered ℤ₂ phase has been simulated only with the metric field H frozen; for the full model there is only a classical check that the vacuum is a minimum with all four field directions free.
2. **A derivation of the ρ-preserving potential.** [Open: the origin of the ρ-preserving potential](../symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential.md) — a definite polarization needs a potential that keeps reciprocity as a symmetry but pins the polarization angle; it brings in "a rigid, unordered pair of axes", more fixed structure than postulate [P2′](../program/postulates/p2-prime.md) provides, and nothing derives it.
   - [Open: where the potential comes from](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/bounded-potential.md#open-where-the-potential-comes-from) — the bounded potential V_b that the toy uses is put in by hand and fixes the pair of axes up to sign.
3. **The strong form of matter locality.** [Open: forced locality and the P4 lock](../symmetry-breaking/strong-locality.md) — no mechanism forces matter to interact through a coupling built from H and the order parameter K, so in the toy, locality in position comes from postulate P4, packaged with a momentum-local mirror sector, rather than being derived; the worklog adds that the question is "also open in the literature".
   - [Open: locality is assumed, not derived](../symmetry-breaking/mandatory-breaking.md#open-locality-is-assumed-not-derived) — the argument that the breaking is mandatory runs from locality to a selected polarization; the toy models test the converse and find only its weak form.
4. **The real model's one-loop preference, "a lead".** [Open: the real model's one-loop preference (a lead)](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/real-model-lead.md) — in the real-field Grosse–Wulkenhaar model the first quantum correction gives the vacuum sharper in position the lower free energy, F(+) − F(−) = 2.634 at truncations N = 6, 8 and 10. The selection is explicit, not spontaneous, it holds at fixed bare μ², and "the large-N, renormalized fate of the real model's one-loop selection" is not shown (note §3, §6; round-4 finding F4).
   - [Open: why position rather than momentum](../program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection.md#open-why-position-rather-than-momentum) — the same lead as it bears on postulate P5, which takes our position-tilted vacuum as given.
5. **Whether ρ's SO(2) breaks in the coupled model.** [Open: does ρ's SO(2) break in the coupled model?](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/so2-question.md) — in two dimensions the compact polarization angle cannot order by itself ([the Mermin–Wagner–Coleman theorem](../symmetry-breaking/mermin-wagner-coleman.md)), but model I's metric sector is a hyperbolic sigma model, which can; whether the coupled model breaks ρ's SO(2) without a potential is open (round-4 point C2; note §6).
6. **Anything beyond d = 1.** Both toy models take a phase space with one position and one momentum direction, d = 1, and "anything beyond d = 1" is not shown (note §6). The worklog's untested remark on 3+1 dimensions is listed among the [conjectures](conjectures.md).
7. **The comparison with the exact solution.** [Open: the comparison with the exact solution](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/quantum-restoration.md#open-the-comparison-with-the-exact-solution) — the note names Langmann, Szabo and Zarembo's exact solution of a related model as the check on quantum restoration in the complex model: "The exact solution of the related model [8] is the natural independent check. We have not carried it out." (§4). Round 4's revision 4 had asked for the comparison, yet the adjudication and the worklog record every accepted revision as applied (flag 42, confirmed by the audit). The note's §6 also lists the restoration itself under "Shown (each by executed code)", though it rests on §4's analytic argument and [`verify/toy_gw.py`](../../verify/toy_gw.py) checks only its premise (flag 41, confirmed by the audit). Both flags are listed under the pending rulings below.
