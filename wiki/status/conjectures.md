*This register collects the claims that are not part of the program's derived record. It opens with P.R.'s core conjectures: that dark matter is conjugate matter, and four conjectures, drawn from three of his founding ideas, that have pages of their own, among them the founding image of dark matter clumping in velocity. Some claims exist only in the original claude.ai conversation and are known from a list in [the handoff document](../history/documents/handoff.md); some appear only in the two [popular articles](../history/documents/popular-articles.md); and a few more sit inside the record but are called conjectures by their own sources. No document in the repository derives them and no script checks them, so they are kept apart from the program's results. The exception is the first chat-only item, the phase-space diameter relation, which a note of 2026-10-26 derived and checked; it stays on the list with the statuses that note gives its parts. The popular article "The Universe's Other Half" presents the framework as if it were confirmed and never decides a claim's status. Each line says where the claim is recorded and how it stands against the record.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-11-06 — no provenance tags on the claims, except [O] on the conjectures [#11](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) itself states: the four with pages of their own, the unification of [§11.3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#113-the-identification-d--structural-math-t) and P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture of [§11.4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#114-the-magnetic-mirror-conjecture-o-pr-v23); [`HANDOFF.md` §4](../../HANDOFF.md#4-results-that-exist-only-in-the-chat-not-in-any-artifact) calls two of its items conjecture-tier, [#13 §5](../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md#5-inertia-dualized) calls the dual second law "conjecture-tier", the toy-model worklog calls its [thawing](../cosmology/dark-energy/thawing-and-freezing.md) remark "a claim about the program, not verified here", and the popular articles tag nothing; the parts of the diameter relation carry the tags of the note that wrote it up — no derivation or script in the repository supports these claims, except the diameter relation, derived in [`wiki/papers/phase-space-diameter.md`](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md) and gated in [`verify/phase_space_diameter.py`](../../verify/phase_space_diameter.py); the chat-only list is [`HANDOFF.md` §4](../../HANDOFF.md#4-results-that-exist-only-in-the-chat-not-in-any-artifact), and the popsci-only claims are found only in `popsci/`.

## P.R.'s core conjectures

These are founding ideas of P.R.'s, audited in [#11 App. A](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates) ([founding suppositions](../program/founding-suppositions.md)). No document derives them, and no script checks them as conjectures. The four after the first have pages of their own that state the evidence against them in full.

- [Dark matter is conjugate matter](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/_index.md) — founding supposition 4, "**alive**, P.R.'s core conjecture" ([#11 App. A item 4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates); [the audit's entry](../program/founding-suppositions.md#4-dark-matter-is-conjugate-matter)). Two of [#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates)'s three candidates are conjugate matter, [χ particles](../dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles.md) ("x-clumping, gated self-force", [App. A item 4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)) and the conjugate monopoles below, in a table tagged "[D — framework; each candidate O]". χ "propagates on g (equivalence principle; Bullet-Cluster-safe)" ([#11 §8](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate)): it "falls, orbits, and lenses like ordinary dark matter" ([#1 §4](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#4-consistency-with-everything-already-measured)), which agrees with the data but does not tell it apart from other dark matter. Against the χ form's mechanism, [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) (2026-10-28; two derivations, the second blind; not reviewed) finds that exchanging [#11 §7](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton)'s X cannot produce the gate of [#11 §8](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate), and flag 55 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism; the gate itself is not refuted ([the gate mechanism](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)). The monopole form has not been tested (below).
- [Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md) — founding supposition 3, "Matter clumps in momentum space", open, P.R.'s conjecture [O] ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [App. A item 3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)). The evidence, as [§9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) states it: for particles, the mean-field criterion g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)² "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark" [D], and by the [classicality theorem](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem.md) satisfying it at fixed σ₀/m "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰ — collective momentum-space behavior and classical particles are mutually exclusive"; for the committed wave, Scenario A, condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarf galaxies, whose endpoint drains the halo, "**falsified** by observed UFD density and longevity" [D + T] (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf), a case itself [under review](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md); for the free wave, "optional, never required"; and [the net theorem](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem.md): "velocity-space clumping is nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)". "Results (i)–(iii) stand as results" ([§9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). [App. A item 3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates) puts it as failing "for particle dark matter by 10²⁸–10³¹ (theory) and, where mandatory for wave dark matter (Scenario A, under review), against the faintest dwarfs (data)", and "optional for a free-coupling wave". The tests cover the program's contact-range gate on all three branches, and they say clumping is required nowhere, not that it is impossible; the velocity-gated-gravity note of 2026-10-28 (not reviewed) examined that scope and finds that the results hold for the program as written ([the scope of the tests](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests)). The image's loophole for individual topological objects is untested, and its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles, next ([Open: the topological loophole](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole.md)).

1. **The phase-space diameter relation.** [The phase-space diameter relation](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md), with the bullet itself under [Chat-only: the original statement](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md#chat-only-the-original-statement) — the handoff's bullet makes four claims: the diameter of the universe in position space times its diameter in momentum space equals h · N^(1/3) per dimension, a "cosmic time–bandwidth theorem"; N is "the universe's photon count/entropy (~10⁹⁰, i.e. ~10³⁰ h per dimension)"; the rectangle is "square (D_x = D_p in Planck units) exactly at the bounce"; and its "aspect ratio today ~10⁹²" is "a clock measuring distance from self-duality". The handoff called it "Conjecture-tier; candidate for a future note". The note finds that the bullet defines none of its terms and that its numbers fit one reading, the radiation of today's observable universe (§2). Status: written up; three of the four parts stand as the note corrects them, and one is under review.
   - **The product: alive, as standard physics.** For blackbody radiation filling a sphere, D_x·D_p = κ h N_γ^{1/3} with κ = 1.708, an identity of blackbody statistics [D][V], constant for a fixed comoving region of the radiation [T] (note §3). It holds in standard cosmology with or without reciprocity, so it cannot test the program, and the program's [constraint a·ã = 1](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint.md) fixes the area of one phase-space cell, not the number of cells [D].
   - **The constant: corrected.** The chat's ~10⁹⁰ is the radiation entropy, S = 1.01×10⁹⁰ k_B, beside a photon count N_γ = 1.43×10⁸⁹ [V]. It is not the entropy of the universe, which black holes and the cosmic event horizon dominate [T][S] (note §3, §9).
   - **The square: under review.** [Under review: the square rectangle at the bounce](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce.md). In the reading the chat's numbers fit, the rectangle is square only at about 10¹⁴ times the Planck temperature, before the radiation existed, and at the program's [turnaround](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce.md) it is at its most elongated; no reading makes it square at a bounce, and the note withdraws the claim [D][V] (§1, §6); §6's results stand. Flag 24 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, asks which bounce was meant, and the round-5 adjudication recommends accepting it. The note's inference about the claim's origin, tagged [D, inference], is the record's identification of a·ã = 1 with the circle |Z| = 1 (flag 23), which the measured aspect ratio excludes.
   - **The clock: alive, as a reparametrization.** In Planck units the aspect ratio is 5.2×10⁹² today and grows as a²; its logarithm, 213.5 per axis, is the distance on the Siegel half-plane from the self-dual point Z = i [D][V] ([Gaussian wave packets on the Siegel half-plane](../phase-space/modular-field/gaussian-states.md)). In the note's words, "It reparametrizes ln a and adds no dynamics" (§5). Read as one perturbation mode's squeezing, an aspect ratio of 10⁹² is a squeezing of r = 105.9, inside the literature's 100–120 for today's largest scales, but that mode was round before it left the Hubble radius, not at a bounce ([squeezed cosmological perturbations](../cosmology/early-universe/squeezed-perturbations.md); note §6).
