*This register collects the claims that are not part of the program's derived record. It opens with four of P.R.'s founding ideas, which the record had counted dead or retired and which the checkpoint restored on 2026-10-29 as his open conjectures: three in its version 2.4, on his direction that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs, and the founding image of dark matter clumping in velocity in version 2.5, on his answer to the question of his concurrence in its death. Some claims exist only in the original claude.ai conversation and are known from a list in [the handoff document](/wiki/history/documents/handoff); some appear only in the two [popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles); and a few more sit inside the record but are called conjectures by their own sources. No document in the repository derives them and no script checks them, so they are kept apart from the program's results. The exception is the first chat-only item, the phase-space diameter relation, which a note of 2026-10-26 derived and checked; it stays on the list with the statuses that note gives its parts. The popular article "The Universe's Other Half" presents the framework as if it were confirmed and never decides a claim's status. Each line says where the claim is recorded and how it stands against the record.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-29 and #11 v2.5 — no provenance tags on the claims, except [O] on the conjectures #11 itself states: the four founding ideas restored in v2.4 and v2.5, the unification of §11.3 and P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture of §11.4; `HANDOFF.md` §4 calls two of its items conjecture-tier, #13 §5 calls the dual second law "conjecture-tier", the toy-model worklog calls its [thawing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/thawing-and-freezing) remark "a claim about the program, not verified here", and the popular articles tag nothing; the parts of the diameter relation now carry the tags of the note that wrote it up — no derivation or script in the repository supports these claims, except the diameter relation, now derived in `papers/phase-space-diameter.md` and gated in `verify/phase_space_diameter.py`; the chat-only list is `HANDOFF.md` §4, and the popsci-only claims are found only in `popsci/`.

A claim with a page of its own carries "(chat-only)" or "(popsci-only)" in its title. A smaller claim recorded on a page about another subject sits under a heading "Chat-only: …", "Popsci-only: …" or "Conjecture: …", and the register links that heading. ## P.R.'s conjectures restored in #11 v2.4 and v2.5

On 2026-10-29 Paul directed that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he explicitly concurs: "I think you prematurely killed off the most interesting parts of the comatter conjecture" (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'; the rule is under 'Method'). The same day #11 v2.4 restored three of his founding ideas, "recorded dead or retired without his concurrence", as his open conjectures [O] (#11 header); in the root `CLAUDE.md`'s words, "None was killed by theory and data in general, and he concurred in none of the deaths". Later that day he answered the concurrence questions for the deaths recorded before then, and #11 v2.5 restored a fourth, the founding image, in whose death he did not concur. Each has a page of its own that states the evidence against it in full, and each was listed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard#moved) until that day. No document derives any of them, and no script checks them as conjectures.

- [Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image) — founding supposition 3, "Matter clumps in momentum space", open, P.R.'s conjecture [O] since #11 v2.5: "Matter clumping in momentum space is P.R.'s open conjecture [O] (App. A item 3): he does not concur in recording it dead (2026-10-29). Results (i)–(iii) stand as results" (§9, 'Status (v2.5)'). The evidence, as §9 states it: for particles, the mean-field criterion g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)² "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark" [D], and by the [classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) satisfying it at fixed σ₀/m "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰ — collective momentum-space behavior and classical particles are mutually exclusive"; for the committed wave, Scenario A, condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarf galaxies, whose endpoint drains the halo, "**falsified** by observed UFD density and longevity" [D + T] (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf), a case itself [under review](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a); for the free wave, "optional, never required"; and [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem): "velocity-space clumping is nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)". App. A item 3 puts it as failing "for particle dark matter by 10²⁸–10³¹ (theory) and, where mandatory for wave dark matter (Scenario A, under review), against the faintest dwarfs (data)", and "optional for a free-coupling wave". From v2.0 to v2.4 #11 recorded it as an obituary, "dead as dynamics" (App. A item 3), and the velocity-gated-gravity note of 2026-10-28 found that obituary "not premature": the obituary tested the program's contact-range gate on all three branches, and "it says clumping is required nowhere, not that it is impossible" (root `CLAUDE.md`). The old verdict and the note's finding on it have been history since v2.5. Its loophole for individual topological objects is untested, and its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles, next ([Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)).
- [Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles) — the third dark-matter candidate of #11 §12, "magnetically charged topological solitons of the p-polarized sector, localized in momentum (§11, §11.4)", stable by "topological charge [O]"; in §11.1's words, "P.R. conjectures that they are abundant in momentum space". It needs p-localization and a kinematics of momentum-polarized matter, neither of them modeled (root `CLAUDE.md`), and p-localization is an open problem of #11: "p-local solitons are p-localized" [O] (§14 item 8). It is the candidate of [the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole) that the clumping problem leaves (#11 §9), the momentum-localized form of founding supposition 4 and the conjecture carried by supposition 5's ontology half (App. A); [conjugate monopoles as ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles#conjecture-abundant-in-momentum-space) records it too. 1. **The phase-space diameter relation.** [The phase-space diameter relation](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter), with the bullet itself under [Chat-only: the original statement](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter#chat-only-the-original-statement) — the handoff's bullet makes four claims: the diameter of the universe in position space times its diameter in momentum space equals h · N^(1/3) per dimension, a "cosmic time–bandwidth theorem"; N is "the universe's photon count/entropy (~10⁹⁰, i.e. ~10³⁰ h per dimension)"; the rectangle is "square (D_x = D_p in Planck units) exactly at the bounce"; and its "aspect ratio today ~10⁹²" is "a clock measuring distance from self-duality". The handoff called it "Conjecture-tier; candidate for a future note". The note finds that the bullet defines none of its terms and that its numbers fit one reading, the radiation of today's observable universe (§2). Status: written up; three of the four parts stand as the note corrects them, and one is under review.
   - **The product: alive, as standard physics.** For blackbody radiation filling a sphere, D_x·D_p = κ h N_γ^{1/3} with κ = 1.708, an identity of blackbody statistics [D][V], constant for a fixed comoving region of the radiation [T] (note §3). It holds in standard cosmology with or without reciprocity, so it cannot test the program, and the program's [constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) fixes the area of one phase-space cell, not the number of cells [D].
   - **The constant: corrected.** The chat's ~10⁹⁰ is the radiation entropy, S = 1.01×10⁹⁰ k_B, beside a photon count N_γ = 1.43×10⁸⁹ [V]. It is not the entropy of the universe, which black holes and the cosmic event horizon dominate [T][S] (note §3, §9).
   - **The square: under review.** [Under review: the square rectangle at the bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce). In the reading the chat's numbers fit, the rectangle is square only at about 10¹⁴ times the Planck temperature, before the radiation existed, and at the program's [turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce) it is at its most elongated; no reading makes it square at a bounce, and the note withdraws the claim [D][V] (§1, §6). Recorded dead until 2026-10-29, it has been under Paul's review since the note's version 1.2, in the note's words "under his review, not dead; its results stand". Flag 24 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` asks which bounce was meant; it still awaits Paul's ruling, and the round-5 adjudication recommends accepting it. The note's inference about the claim's origin, tagged [D, inference], is the record's identification of a·ã = 1 with the circle |Z| = 1 (flag 23), which the measured aspect ratio excludes.
   - **The clock: alive, as a reparametrization.** In Planck units the aspect ratio is 5.2×10⁹² today and grows as a²; its logarithm, 213.5 per axis, is the distance on the Siegel half-plane from the self-dual point Z = i [D][V] ([Gaussian wave packets on the Siegel half-plane](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/gaussian-states)). In the note's words, "It reparametrizes ln a and adds no dynamics" (§5). Read as one perturbation mode's squeezing, an aspect ratio of 10⁹² is a squeezing of r = 105.9, inside the literature's 100–120 for today's largest scales, but that mode was round before it left the Hubble radius, not at a bounce ([squeezed cosmological perturbations](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/squeezed-perturbations); note §6).

### Electric–magnetic duality

- [Popsci-only: the transparency argument](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification#popsci-only-the-transparency-argument) — #12 follows its statement that "The momentum-polarized sector is the magnetic side of electromagnetism" with an argument that a magnetically charged dark sector would still be invisible: "Uniform media do not scatter light; they refract it", so that its effects would lie "some forty orders of magnitude below the sharpest astronomical measurements". The argument uses the mass of #14's failed model, and no script checks it. The velocity-gated-gravity note (`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`, 2026-10-28; computed, not reviewed) finds it outside the record, a model "the record does not contain, which the clumping problem never tested", with no field in the record to carry it.
