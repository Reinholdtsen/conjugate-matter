*This register lists the claims under review: twelve claims that the Comatter program's record counted dead before 2026-10-29, without Paul's explicit concurrence, and that he chose on that day to review with Claude rather than concur in their deaths or contest them. Since 2026-10-29 the rule has been that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul concurs, asked for that death alone; his answer for these twelve was "Restore all pending", so none of them is dead until he has reviewed it and concurred. Three concern [momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping), the program's [founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image) of dark matter gathering in velocity rather than in space; six are pieces of the model of a third dark-matter candidate that the withdrawn manuscript [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) built; two are claims that [the note on the size of the universe in position and in momentum](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter) withdrew or excluded; and one is a potential of [the first toy model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model). The calculations and observations that tested them are unchanged and stated in full on each page; only the status word is new. Each entry says what was claimed, what tested it, whether theory, data or both, where the evidence is, and which page holds it.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-29 and #11 v2.5 — no tags of its own; each entry keeps the tags of its page — each claim was tested by the calculation, review ruling or observation named in its entry, and none is dead. The status `under-review` means, in `wiki/CLAUDE.md`'s words, "recorded dead before 2026-10-29 without Paul's concurrence; not dead until he reviews it, with the evidence against it stated in full". The list of twelve is the root `CLAUDE.md`'s ('Since the handoff', 2026-10-29).

## The rule and Paul's answer

**The rule.** The root `CLAUDE.md`, under 'Method': "A claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs." Paul's words of 2026-10-29: "Don't kill them off unless theory and data kill them off and you ask me explicitly that I concur. Do not hide the killing in a massive change." Each death is asked for separately, the answer is recorded with its date, and each death is a change of its own. Until he concurs, a claim against which theory or data weigh is recorded as open, or as his conjecture, with the evidence against it stated, and a failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea.

**Paul's answer.** On 2026-10-29 he answered the concurrence questions for every death recorded before that date (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'; #11 v2.5 header):

- #14's ordinary-kinematics model of the third dark-matter candidate is dead, with his concurrence (#11 §12). It is the one entry in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).
- The founding image, matter clumping in momentum space: he did not concur in its death, and it is his open conjecture again ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)).
- The two failed versions of founding supposition 9, collapse-powered expansion: he did not concur in recording them dead; "they are failed versions of an open conjecture, not deaths" (#11 §10; [Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion)).
- Every other death recorded before 2026-10-29: "Restore all pending". "Each is under review, not dead, until he reviews it with Claude." These are the twelve below.

**What under review means.** The record of each claim stands as it was: what was claimed, how it was tested, what the tests found and what stands. An earlier verdict, such as an obituary, "falsified" or "withdrawn with cause", is kept on the page as history. Each page is titled "Under review: …" with its old title's wording after the colon, and its status line reads: "Under review — recorded dead before 2026-10-29 without Paul's concurrence. On 2026-10-29 he chose to review it with Claude, and until he does, it is not dead (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff')." The root `CLAUDE.md` lists the review first among the open items of the monopole discussion, not queued: "each stays not dead until he reviews it, and a death needs his explicit concurrence, in a change of its own" ([the review queue](/wiki/status/open-problems#the-review-queue)). The [executive summary](/wiki/summary#where-the-program-stands) names the twelve in its account of where the program stands.

## Momentum clumping

The three claims below were built on the founding image, dark matter clumping in velocity, which has been P.R.'s open conjecture since #11 v2.5 ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)). The calculations of [the clumping problem](/wiki/history/main-problems#the-clumping-problem), worked out in [calc notes #2–#4](/wiki/history/documents/clumping-notes) and summarized in #11 §9, tested it on both sides of the particle–wave divide. Each of the three notes now opens with the same status note of 2026-10-29: "none of the deaths that calc notes #2–#4 record has Paul's concurrence, which his rule of that day requires", and "Old rows 3 and 4 and Scenario A are under his review, not dead, until he reviews them with Claude. The calculations stand as results". #11 v2.5 §9: "Results (i)–(iii) stand as results. Scenario A, the committed-wave case of (ii), is under review, not dead, until P.R. reviews it." §13: "Withdrawn and under P.R.'s review, not dead (v2.5): velocity-space granularity in halos; Fornax friction suppression (§9)."

- [Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a). **Claimed:** on the [wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island), where dark matter is light enough to behave as a wave, the gated attraction that condenses it also makes the cores of dwarf galaxies; fitting the cores fixes its coupling, and condensation then becomes mandatory in the [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies). **Tested by:** theory and data. Calc note #4 ([the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/history/documents/clumping-notes#4-the-ultra-faint-dwarf-endpoint)) followed the condensation through. In Segue 1, an ultra-faint dwarf galaxy, the condensation rate is about 660 times the inverse of the galaxy's dynamical time, and condensation takes about 10⁴ years; the attractive condensates pass their critical mass, collapse and drain the halo, and the note's verdict was "Scenario A is falsified by the existence of ultra-faint dwarfs". The data are the observed density and longevity of those galaxies. #11 §9 (ii), tagged [D + T], records the endpoint as "**falsified** by observed UFD density and longevity" (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf), and the escape to a much lighter mass as "closed independently [15†, Lyman-α]". Calc note #3's localized signature in ultra-faint dwarfs, which never became a numbered row, was withdrawn with Scenario A and is under review with it ([the first endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a#the-first-endpoint-a-localized-signature)). **Evidence:** `notes/ufd-endpoint.md` 'Verdict and closure of the clumping problem'; `notes/wave-island.md` 'What pins g? Two scenarios'; #11 §9 (ii). No script checks these numbers.
- [Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity). **Claimed:** row 3 of [the lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s predictions table and one of its three flagship discriminants: the dark matter of a relaxed halo such as the Milky Way's is split into of order ten macro-streams of width about v\*, the gate's velocity scale, whose gravity leaves correlated, long-wavelength perturbations along cold stellar streams (#1 §9). **Tested by:** theory, and on the wave island data through Scenario A. On the particle island the [mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion) for streams fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark, and calc note #2 withdrew the row there; on the wave island streams lose to orbital stirring in Milky-Way-like halos under Scenario A, which makes them mandatory only in the ultra-faint dwarfs, and under [Scenario B](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b), with the coupling free, they are optional. Calc note #4: "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands." **Evidence:** `notes/stream-stability.md` 'Consequences for the predictions table'; `notes/wave-island.md`; `notes/ufd-endpoint.md`; #11 §13.
- [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction). **Claimed:** row 4 of the notebook's table: dynamical friction, the drag that makes a massive body sink toward the centre of a galaxy, is suppressed in condensed dwarf-galaxy cores, so the globular clusters of the Fornax dwarf galaxy stay put (#1 §9). **Tested by:** theory, and on the wave island data through Scenario A. The condensed cores were to come from the same velocity-space condensation as the founding image: on the particle island no core condenses (calc note #2); on the wave island condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarfs, where its endpoint is collapse and a drain of the dark matter rather than a placid core, and elsewhere it is optional. Calc note #4: "The Fornax mechanism goes with them." **Evidence:** the same notes; #11 §13.

## The pieces of #14's model

The standalone manuscript [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) modeled the third dark-matter candidate of #11 v2.0 as superheavy 't Hooft–Polyakov monopoles of a hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, with ordinary kinematics: "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" (#14 §1). [Review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) graded #14 v3 C, and the model is dead, with Paul's concurrence of 2026-10-29: "#14's model is dead, with his concurrence (§12)" (#11 v2.5 header; [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). The six pieces below, recorded dead with it or before it, are under review; the root `CLAUDE.md` names them "six pieces of #14's model (the Kibble normalization, the ψ relic window, the dimension-five operator, the hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, α_d ≈ 34 via our photon, the √N floor)". The candidate #14 modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, is P.R.'s open conjecture ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles)). The [V] numbers are gated in `verify/soliton_obituary.py` ([the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script)), and the rulings are on [round-3 verdict and rulings](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings).

- [Under review: the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector). **Claimed:** the dark sector is a hidden SU(2) gauge force broken to U(1) by an adjoint Higgs field, which leaves heavy W′± bosons, a massless hidden photon and the monopoles, plus a light fermion ψ added so that the W′± can decay; #14 identified this sector with the momentum-polarized half of the program's matter. **Tested by:** theory, in review round 3. It was retired with the candidate by round-3 [Decision 2](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions#decision-2-the-soliton-candidate-retired), which Paul narrowed on 2026-10-29 to #14's model, and the sector itself was not refuted: its minimal form is excluded by [W′ overclosure](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure), "by 10¹⁶–10¹⁷ at the v3 benchmark [V]" (#11 §12), and its low-scale version is [prior art](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art) (Murayama–Shu; Liveoak–Maharana–Wells) that does not depend on Born reciprocity. **Evidence:** `review/round3-adjudication.md` 'Decisions' 2; #11 §12.
- [Under review: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling). **Claimed:** the hidden monopoles' coupling has the value α_d ≈ 34, fixed three ways in turn. **Tested by:** theory. [Round 1](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two#round-1-14-v1) found the first way, [Dirac quantization](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) against our photon, inconsistent: in the handoff's words, the "round-1 reviewer's valid kill: can't use our DSZ lattice and then deny coupling to our photon" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), DSZ being the Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger charge lattice. The third way, hypothesis H, was motivated rather than derived. It was recorded dead as a component of #14 v3 when the model failed (round-3 Decision 2), but no ruling addressed it by itself, and a failed model is not the death of each idea in it. H is therefore under review with the coupling (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'; [how it was retired](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling#how-it-was-retired)). The second way, reciprocity acting on the coupling as modular S, which round 2 called numerology (`HANDOFF.md` §6), has been a conjecture since Paul withdrew the decision of 2026-10-19 that had set it aside (flag 60): the page keeps it as a folded section, [Conjecture: reciprocity acting as modular S](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling#conjecture-reciprocity-acting-as-modular-s), part of the separate-photon form of P.R.'s [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification). **Evidence:** `HANDOFF.md` §2, §6; `review/v3-worklog.md` 'Findings neither referee raised'; `review/round3-adjudication.md` 'Decisions' 2.
- [Under review: the Kibble normalization](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization). **Claimed:** #14's count of the monopoles formed at the hidden transition by the [Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism): the yield n/s = p (T_c/M_Pl)³, the number of monopoles per unit entropy, with T_c the transition temperature and M_Pl the Planck mass. v2 normalized it at p = 1 and v3 at p ≈ 0.023, with p = 1 treated as the upper end. **Tested by:** theory: [round-3 ruling 1](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#rulings-1-to-3-how-many-monopoles-form), upheld. v3's value dropped the factor (1.66√g\*)³, with g\* the number of relativistic degrees of freedom, and one defect per Hubble volume gives "p ≈ 104 [V]" (#11 §12), gated in `verify/soliton_obituary.py` as p = 104.31; ruling 3 added that this count is a floor, not a ceiling. **Evidence:** `review/round3-adjudication.md` 'Rulings' 1 and 3; `verify/soliton_obituary.py`.
- [Under review: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor). **Claimed:** an argument made only in the claude.ai chat, that the Kibble mechanism leaves a net topological charge which annihilation cannot remove; in the handoff's words, "this bounds the relic abundance between Kibble ceiling and √N floor" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), queued as the handoff's §8 item 3. **Tested by:** theory: [round-3 Decision 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions#decision-3-the-n-floor-dropped), "Its premise, Kibble as a ceiling, is inverted (ruling 3)", and "Under decision 2 it has no candidate left to serve"; ruling 10 had also found annihilation at the monopoles' strong coupling already worked out for hidden sectors. The argument was never written into a document, and no number belongs to it. **Evidence:** `review/round3-adjudication.md` 'Decisions' 3; `HANDOFF.md` §2.
- [Under review: the ψ relic window](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window). **Claimed:** #14 v3's mass window for ψ, the light hidden fermion added so that the W′± could decay: light enough for ψ to be a minor part of the dark matter, heavy enough for it to have annihilated before Big Bang nucleosynthesis. **Tested by:** theory: [ruling 4](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#ruling-4-the-ψ-relic-window), upheld. v3 had left out ψ̄ and the second member of the doublet and bracketed an exactly known cross-section by up to a factor of ten; the recount [V] puts 10% of the dark matter at 45–49 GeV and all of it at 146–158 GeV, so "v3's 0.1–0.3 TeV was wrong". [Ruling 6](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#rulings-5-and-6-the-colder-hidden-sector-and-nucleosynthesis), accepted, not re-checked, moved the lower edge: ψ at MeV masses would still be freezing out during nucleosynthesis. **Evidence:** `review/round3-adjudication.md` 'Rulings' 4 and 6; `verify/soliton_obituary.py`.
- [Under review: Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator). **Claimed:** #14 v3's estimate of the [kinetic mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing) ε between the hidden photon and ours, from a dimension-5 operator with an "O(1) coefficient at the Planck scale". **Tested by:** theory: [ruling 7](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#rulings-7-and-8-the-mixing-operator), upheld: the coefficient has no source, and "the literature generates it at one loop from bi-charged states, which sector separation forbids". Ruling 8 found the operator itself in the literature, and Decision 1 then took [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) strictly, with ε ≲ 10⁻¹³ from gravity alone. **Evidence:** `review/round3-adjudication.md` 'Rulings' 7–8 and 'Decisions' 1.

## The phase-space diameter

The [phase-space-diameter note](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter) (`papers/phase-space-diameter.md`, graded B by both referees of [review round 5](/wiki/method/review/round-5) and revised to version 1.1 on 2026-10-26) wrote up and tested the chat-only phase-space diameter relation. Its version 1.2 (2026-10-29): "under Paul's rule that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs, the §6 obituary of 'square exactly at the bounce' is under his review, not dead; its results stand." The numbers of both entries are gated in `verify/phase_space_diameter.py` ([the phase-space-diameter script](/wiki/method/verification/phase-space-diameter-script)).

- [Under review: the square rectangle at the bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce). **Claimed:** a part of the chat-only relation: the universe's position–momentum rectangle is "square (D_x = D_p in Planck units) exactly at the bounce" (`HANDOFF.md` §4), with no bounce named (flag 24 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). **Tested by:** theory, and, for the |Z| = 1 reading, data: the note's §6 [D][V], at every event the record or the literature could mean by "the bounce". In the reading the chat's own numbers fit, the radiation of today's observable universe, the rectangle's aspect ratio in [Planck units](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/planck-units) is 5.2×10⁹² today and grows as a²; it would be square only at a/a₀ = 4.4×10⁻⁴⁷, T = 4.4×10¹⁴ T_P, before the radiation existed, and since [reheating](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/inflation-and-dilution#reheating-t) it has been long by at least 10³⁵. At the program's far-future [turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce) it is at its most elongated. Drawn around the Hubble sphere it is square at T = 0.28 T_P, "dimensional analysis rather than a result"; a [squeezed perturbation mode](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/squeezed-perturbations) matches the 10⁹² but was round before it left the Hubble radius; and at a string-scale bounce the rectangle is still long by 9.5×10³². The one reading that would make the claim exact, the record's |Z| = 1 geometry, contradicts the measured aspect ratio. Round 5 added readings (ruling R14 in `review/round5-adjudication.md`), and none changes the outcome. The note's summary: "**withdrawn**, under P.R.'s review since 2026-10-29 (not dead)" (§9). **Evidence:** `papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §6, §9; `verify/phase_space_diameter.py`.
- [Under review: the self-dual length as a program scale](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-length-obituary). **Claimed:** that λ = √(l_P L), 47–84 μm for L between the Hubble radius and the particle horizon, the one length for which [the self-dual cosmic box](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-cosmic-box) maps onto itself under reciprocity, is a scale of the program, in one of the two places where the note finds that a [Born length](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-length) could enter (§8): the Born length of #13's dual special relativity, or the range of the [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton). **Tested by:** theory and data, the note's §8.1 and §8.2 [D][V][S]. As #13's Born length it makes the [maximum force](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force) c̃ = 1.4×10⁻¹⁷ N, which the Coulomb force in the hydrogen ground state, 8.24×10⁻⁸ N, exceeds by 5.8×10⁹. As X's range: in the program's aligned bimetric vacuum, background ratio u = 1, X acts on ordinary matter as a [Yukawa force](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces) of strength α = 4/3, and torsion-balance tests of the inverse-square law exclude it at 47–84 μm; it would survive only for u < 0.67 (47.1 μm) or u < 0.30 (84.0 μm). Both round-5 referees found the same weakest link, the background ratio that version 1.0 had dropped (ruling R1, upheld in substance), and version 1.1 states u = 1. What stands includes [the laboratory bound on X's range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound), λ_X < 37.7 μm in the aligned vacuum. The note's summary keeps the results, "**excluded**" for both uses (§9); the status under review is the root `CLAUDE.md`'s. **Evidence:** `papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §8.1, §8.2, §9; `verify/phase_space_diameter.py`.

## The toy models

- [Under review: the naive ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential). **Claimed:** the term −μ[Tr(K K_ref)]², which version 1 of [the toy-model note](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) added to [model I](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model) so that its circle of vacua reduces to two vacua exchanged by ρ, the [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity) map, and the breaking of reciprocity survives in 1+1 dimensions. **Tested by:** theory: [round-4 findings F1 and F2](/wiki/method/review/round-4#f1-and-f2-the-potential-runs-away-when-h-moves), both upheld. Once the metric H is dynamical the potential "is unbounded below in model I" (F1), reaching "−8.9 × 10⁶ μ at boost parameter 4" (toy-model note §4), and the intended vacuum (1, K_ref) is a saddle, so the Monte Carlo had simulated a [frozen-H reduction](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/frozen-h-reduction). Version 2 of the note replaced it on 2026-10-20 with [the bounded potential V_b](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/bounded-potential). **Evidence:** `papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md` §4; `review/round4-adjudication.md` 'Rulings' F1–F2; `verify/toy_coset.py`, which checks the runaway and the saddle ([the toy-model scripts](/wiki/method/verification/toy-model-scripts)).
