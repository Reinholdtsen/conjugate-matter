*This register lists the claims under review: thirteen claims that the calculations or data weigh against and whose final status awaits review. Three concern [momentum clumping](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/_index.md), the program's [founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md) of dark matter gathering in velocity rather than in space; six are pieces of the model of a third dark-matter candidate that the withdrawn manuscript [#14](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/_index.md) built; two are claims that [the note on the size of the universe in position and in momentum](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md) withdrew or excluded; one is a potential of [the first toy model](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/_index.md); and one is [the symmetric reading of a compact momentum space](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md#under-review-the-symmetric-reading-dv), which sets the curvature of momentum space equal to ours, in [the closed-universe note](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md). The calculations and observations that tested them are stated in full on each page or section. Each entry says what was claimed, what tested it, whether theory, data or both, where the evidence is, and which page or section holds it.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-30 — no tags of its own; each entry keeps the tags of its page or section — each claim was tested by the calculation, review ruling or observation named in its entry. Under review means that the calculations or data weigh against the claim, and its final status awaits review. The list is the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md)'s ('Since the handoff').

Twelve of the claims have pages of their own, each titled "Under review: …", which state what was claimed, how it was tested, the evidence against it and what stands. The thirteenth has no page of its own: it is told in a folded section, headed "Under review: the symmetric reading [D][V]", of [a closed Comatter universe](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md), a page whose own status is alive, so its status is given by the section's heading, not by the page's frontmatter. The review is listed among the open items, not queued ([the review queue](open-problems.md#the-review-queue)).

## Momentum clumping

The three claims below were built on the founding image, dark matter clumping in velocity, P.R.'s open conjecture [O] ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md)). The calculations of [the clumping problem](../history/main-problems.md#the-clumping-problem), worked out in [calc notes #2–#4](../history/documents/clumping-notes.md) and summarized in [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested), tested it on both sides of the particle–wave divide. They stand as results: [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) says so of its results (i)–(iii), with Scenario A, the committed-wave case of (ii), under review, and [§13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions) lists velocity-space granularity in halos and Fornax friction suppression as withdrawn and under review.

- [Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md). **Claimed:** on the [wave island](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md), where dark matter is light enough to behave as a wave, the gated attraction that condenses it also makes the cores of dwarf galaxies; fitting the cores fixes its coupling, and condensation then becomes mandatory in the [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md). **Tested by:** theory and data. Calc note [#4](../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) ([the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](../history/documents/clumping-notes.md#4-the-ultra-faint-dwarf-endpoint)) followed the condensation through. In Segue 1, an ultra-faint dwarf galaxy, the condensation rate is about 660 times the inverse of the galaxy's dynamical time, and condensation takes about 10⁴ years; the attractive condensates pass their critical mass, collapse and drain the halo, and the note's verdict was "Scenario A is falsified by the existence of ultra-faint dwarfs". The data are the observed density and longevity of those galaxies. [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (ii), tagged [D + T], records the endpoint as "**falsified** by observed UFD density and longevity" (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf), and the escape to a much lighter mass as "closed independently [15†, Lyman-α]". Calc note [#3](../../notes/wave-island.md)'s localized signature in ultra-faint dwarfs, which never became a numbered row, is Scenario A's own prediction; calc note #4 follows it to the endpoint above, and it is under review with the scenario ([the first endpoint](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md#the-first-endpoint-a-localized-signature)). **Evidence:** [`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) 'Verdict and closure of the clumping problem'; [`notes/wave-island.md`](../../notes/wave-island.md) 'What pins g? Two scenarios'; [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (ii). No script checks these numbers.
- [Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md). **Claimed:** row 3 of [the lab notebook](../history/documents/lab-notebook.md)'s predictions table and one of its three flagship discriminants: the dark matter of a relaxed halo such as the Milky Way's is split into of order ten macro-streams of width about v\*, the gate's velocity scale, whose gravity leaves correlated, long-wavelength perturbations along cold stellar streams ([#1 §9](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#9-predictions)). **Tested by:** theory, and on the wave island data through Scenario A. On the particle island the [mean-field criterion](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion.md) for streams fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark, and calc note [#2](../../notes/stream-stability.md) withdrew the row there; on the wave island streams lose to orbital stirring in Milky-Way-like halos under Scenario A, which makes them mandatory only in the ultra-faint dwarfs, and under [Scenario B](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b.md), with the coupling free, they are optional. Calc note #4: "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands." **Evidence:** [`notes/stream-stability.md`](../../notes/stream-stability.md) 'Consequences for the predictions table'; [`notes/wave-island.md`](../../notes/wave-island.md); [`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md); [#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions).
- [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md). **Claimed:** row 4 of the notebook's table: dynamical friction, the drag that makes a massive body sink toward the centre of a galaxy, is suppressed in condensed dwarf-galaxy cores, so the globular clusters of the Fornax dwarf galaxy stay put ([#1 §9](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#9-predictions)). **Tested by:** theory, and on the wave island data through Scenario A. The condensed cores were to come from the same velocity-space condensation as the founding image: on the particle island no core condenses (calc note #2); on the wave island condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarfs, where its endpoint is collapse and a drain of the dark matter rather than a placid core, and elsewhere it is optional. Calc note #4: "The Fornax mechanism goes with them." **Evidence:** the same notes; [#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions).

## The pieces of #14's model

The standalone manuscript [#14](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/_index.md) modeled the third dark-matter candidate of #11 v2.0 as superheavy 't Hooft–Polyakov monopoles of a hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, with ordinary kinematics: "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" ([#14 §1](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md#1-framework)). [Review round 3](../method/review/round-3/_index.md) graded #14 v3 C, and the model failed on its recomputed abundance ([Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md)). The six pieces below are under review; the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) names them "six pieces of #14's model (the Kibble normalization, the ψ relic window, the dimension-five operator, the hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, α_d ≈ 34 via our photon, the √N floor)". The candidate #14 modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, is P.R.'s open conjecture ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md)); #14's model did not treat localization in momentum, so the candidate itself has not been tested. The [V] numbers are gated in [`verify/soliton_obituary.py`](../../verify/soliton_obituary.py) ([the soliton-obituary script](../method/verification/soliton-obituary-script.md)), and the rulings are on [round-3 verdict and rulings](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md).

- [Under review: the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector.md). **Claimed:** the dark sector is a hidden SU(2) gauge force broken to U(1) by an adjoint Higgs field, which leaves heavy W′± bosons, a massless hidden photon and the monopoles, plus a light fermion ψ added so that the W′± can decay; #14 identified this sector with the momentum-polarized half of the program's matter. **Tested by:** theory, in review round 3. It was part of #14's model, which round-3 [Decision 2](../method/review/round-3/decisions.md#decision-2-14s-model-retired) retired, and the sector itself was not refuted: its minimal form is excluded by [W′ overclosure](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure.md), "by 10¹⁶–10¹⁷ at the v3 benchmark [V]" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model)), and its low-scale version is [prior art](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art.md) (Murayama–Shu; Liveoak–Maharana–Wells) that does not depend on Born reciprocity. **Evidence:** [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md) 'Decisions' 2; [#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model).
- [Under review: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md). **Claimed:** the hidden monopoles' coupling has the value α_d ≈ 34, fixed three ways in turn. **Tested by:** theory. [Round 1](../method/review/rounds-one-and-two.md#round-1-14-v1) found the first way, [Dirac quantization](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization.md) against our photon, inconsistent: in the handoff's words, the "round-1 reviewer's valid kill: can't use our DSZ lattice and then deny coupling to our photon" ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen)), DSZ being the Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger charge lattice. The third way, hypothesis H, was motivated rather than derived. It was part of #14 v3, whose model failed (round-3 Decision 2), and no ruling addresses it by itself; H is under review with the coupling (root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), 'Since the handoff'; [hypothesis H](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md#hypothesis-h)). The second way, reciprocity acting on the coupling as modular S, which round 2 called numerology ([`HANDOFF.md` §6](../../HANDOFF.md#6-review-history-and-the-measured-failure-mode)), is a conjecture (flag 60): the page keeps it as a folded section, [Conjecture: reciprocity acting as modular S](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md#conjecture-reciprocity-acting-as-modular-s), part of the separate-photon form of P.R.'s [magnetic-mirror conjecture](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md). **Evidence:** [`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen), [§6](../../HANDOFF.md#6-review-history-and-the-measured-failure-mode); [`review/v3-worklog.md`](../../review/v3-worklog.md) 'Findings neither referee raised'; [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md) 'Decisions' 2.
- [Under review: the Kibble normalization](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md). **Claimed:** #14's count of the monopoles formed at the hidden transition by the [Kibble mechanism](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism.md): the yield n/s = p (T_c/M_Pl)³, the number of monopoles per unit entropy, with T_c the transition temperature and M_Pl the Planck mass. v2 normalized it at p = 1 and v3 at p ≈ 0.023, with p = 1 treated as the upper end. **Tested by:** theory: [round-3 ruling 1](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#rulings-1-to-3-how-many-monopoles-form), upheld. v3's value dropped the factor (1.66√g\*)³, with g\* the number of relativistic degrees of freedom, and one defect per Hubble volume gives "p ≈ 104 [V]" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model)), gated in [`verify/soliton_obituary.py`](../../verify/soliton_obituary.py) as p = 104.31; ruling 3 added that this count is a floor, not a ceiling. **Evidence:** [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md) 'Rulings' 1 and 3; [`verify/soliton_obituary.py`](../../verify/soliton_obituary.py).
- [Under review: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor.md). **Claimed:** an argument made only in the claude.ai chat, that the Kibble mechanism leaves a net topological charge which annihilation cannot remove; in the handoff's words, "this bounds the relic abundance between Kibble ceiling and √N floor" ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen)), queued as the handoff's §8 item 3. **Tested by:** theory, on its premise; the floor itself was never tested. [Round-3 Decision 3](../method/review/round-3/decisions.md#decision-3-the-n-floor-dropped) dropped the calculation queued for it: "Its premise, Kibble as a ceiling, is inverted (ruling 3)", and "Under decision 2 it has no candidate left to serve" (the candidate meant is #14's model, which Decision 2 covers); ruling 10 had also found annihilation at the monopoles' strong coupling already worked out for hidden sectors. The argument was never written up, and no number belongs to it. **Evidence:** [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md) 'Decisions' 3; [`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen).
- [Under review: the ψ relic window](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window.md). **Claimed:** #14 v3's mass window for ψ, the light hidden fermion added so that the W′± could decay: light enough for ψ to be a minor part of the dark matter, heavy enough for it to have annihilated before Big Bang nucleosynthesis. **Tested by:** theory: [ruling 4](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#ruling-4-the-ψ-relic-window), upheld. v3 had left out ψ̄ and the second member of the doublet and bracketed an exactly known cross-section by up to a factor of ten; the recount [V] puts 10% of the dark matter at 45–49 GeV and all of it at 146–158 GeV, so "v3's 0.1–0.3 TeV was wrong". [Ruling 6](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#rulings-5-and-6-the-colder-hidden-sector-and-nucleosynthesis), accepted, not re-checked, moved the lower edge: ψ at MeV masses would still be freezing out during nucleosynthesis. **Evidence:** [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md) 'Rulings' 4 and 6; [`verify/soliton_obituary.py`](../../verify/soliton_obituary.py).
- [Under review: Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator.md). **Claimed:** #14 v3's estimate of the [kinetic mixing](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing.md) ε between the hidden photon and ours, from a dimension-5 operator with an "O(1) coefficient at the Planck scale". **Tested by:** theory: [ruling 7](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#rulings-7-and-8-the-mixing-operator), upheld: the coefficient has no source, and "the literature generates it at one loop from bi-charged states, which sector separation forbids". Ruling 8 found the operator itself in the literature, and Decision 1 then took [P4](../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md) strictly, with ε ≲ 10⁻¹³ from gravity alone. **Evidence:** [`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md) 'Rulings' 7–8 and 'Decisions' 1.

## The phase-space diameter

The [phase-space-diameter note](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md) ([`wiki/papers/phase-space-diameter.md`](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md), graded B by both referees of [review round 5](../method/review/round-5.md) and revised to version 1.1 on 2026-10-26) wrote up and tested the chat-only phase-space diameter relation. The results of its §6 stand. The numbers of both entries are gated in [`verify/phase_space_diameter.py`](../../verify/phase_space_diameter.py) ([the phase-space-diameter script](../method/verification/phase-space-diameter-script.md)).

- [Under review: the square rectangle at the bounce](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce.md). **Claimed:** a part of the chat-only relation: the universe's position–momentum rectangle is "square (D_x = D_p in Planck units) exactly at the bounce" ([`HANDOFF.md` §4](../../HANDOFF.md#4-results-that-exist-only-in-the-chat-not-in-any-artifact)), with no bounce named (flag 24 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)). **Tested by:** theory, and, for the |Z| = 1 reading, data: the note's §6 [D][V], at every event the record or the literature could mean by "the bounce". In the reading the chat's own numbers fit, the radiation of today's observable universe, the rectangle's aspect ratio in [Planck units](../gravity/spin-2-fields/planck-units.md) is 5.2×10⁹² today and grows as a²; it would be square only at a/a₀ = 4.4×10⁻⁴⁷, T = 4.4×10¹⁴ T_P, before the radiation existed, and since [reheating](../cosmology/early-universe/inflation-and-dilution.md#reheating-t) it has been long by at least 10³⁵. At the program's far-future [turnaround](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce.md) it is at its most elongated. Drawn around the Hubble sphere it is square at T = 0.28 T_P, "dimensional analysis rather than a result"; a [squeezed perturbation mode](../cosmology/early-universe/squeezed-perturbations.md) matches the 10⁹² but was round before it left the Hubble radius; and at a string-scale bounce the rectangle is still long by 9.5×10³². The one reading that would make the claim exact, the record's |Z| = 1 geometry, contradicts the measured aspect ratio. Round 5 added readings (ruling R14 in [`review/round5-adjudication.md`](../../review/round5-adjudication.md)), and none changes the outcome. The note's summary gives the claim as "**withdrawn**" and under review (§9). **Evidence:** [`wiki/papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §6](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md#6-where-the-rectangle-is-square), [§9](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md#9-summary); [`verify/phase_space_diameter.py`](../../verify/phase_space_diameter.py).
- [Under review: the self-dual length as a program scale](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-length-obituary.md). **Claimed:** that λ = √(l_P L), 47–84 μm for L between the Hubble radius and the particle horizon, the one length for which [the self-dual cosmic box](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-cosmic-box.md) maps onto itself under reciprocity, is a scale of the program, in one of the two places where the note finds that a [Born length](../phase-space/reciprocity/born-length.md) could enter (§8): the Born length of [#13](../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md)'s dual special relativity, or the range of the [momentum graviton X](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md). **Tested by:** theory and data, the note's §8.1 and §8.2 [D][V][S]. As #13's Born length it makes the [maximum force](../conjugate-relativity/maximum-force.md) c̃ = 1.4×10⁻¹⁷ N, which the Coulomb force in the hydrogen ground state, 8.24×10⁻⁸ N, exceeds by 5.8×10⁹. As X's range: in the program's aligned bimetric vacuum, background ratio u = 1, X acts on ordinary matter as a [Yukawa force](../gravity/spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces.md) of strength α = 4/3, and torsion-balance tests of the inverse-square law exclude it at 47–84 μm; it would survive only for u < 0.67 (47.1 μm) or u < 0.30 (84.0 μm). Both round-5 referees found the same weakest link, the background ratio that version 1.0 had dropped (ruling R1, upheld in substance), and version 1.1 states u = 1. What stands includes [the laboratory bound on X's range](../gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound.md), λ_X < 37.7 μm in the aligned vacuum. The note's summary gives "**excluded**" for both uses (§9); the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) lists the claim under review. **Evidence:** [`wiki/papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §8.1](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md#81-not-the-born-length-of-the-dual-special-relativity-dv), [§8.2](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md#82-not-the-range-of-the-momentum-graviton-and-a-bound-on-it-dvs), [§9](../papers/phase-space-diameter.md#9-summary); [`verify/phase_space_diameter.py`](../../verify/phase_space_diameter.py).

## The toy models

- [Under review: the naive ρ-preserving potential](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential.md). **Claimed:** the term −μ[Tr(K K_ref)]², which the first version of [the toy-model note](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md) added to [model I](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/_index.md) so that its circle of vacua reduces to two vacua exchanged by ρ, the [Born reciprocity](../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md) map, and the breaking of reciprocity survives in 1+1 dimensions. **Tested by:** theory: [round-4 findings F1 and F2](../method/review/round-4.md#f1-and-f2-the-potential-runs-away-when-h-moves), both upheld. Version 2 of the note replaced it on 2026-10-20 with [the bounded potential V_b](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/bounded-potential.md). **Evidence:** [`wiki/papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md` §4](../papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#4-quantum-stability); [`review/round4-adjudication.md`](../../review/round4-adjudication.md) 'Rulings' F1–F2; [`verify/toy_coset.py`](../../verify/toy_coset.py), which checks the runaway and the saddle ([the toy-model scripts](../method/verification/toy-model-scripts.md)).

## The closed universe

[The closed-universe note](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md) ([`notes/closed-universe.md`](../../notes/closed-universe.md), 2026-10-27; computed and checked by independent agents, not reviewed) asks what a closed universe implies for the program. No program document asserts the reading below, so it has no page of its own.
