*This register lists the Comatter program's claims that currently stand: postulates, derived results and predictions that no calculation, review or observation has killed. Each line gives the claim, the tags its sources attach and what verifies it, grouped by the sections of the wiki. Standing is not the same as confirmed: most of these claims are untested by experiment, a machine check confirms the algebra but not whether the algebra describes nature, and the dark-energy sector is under pressure from current data.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-11-06 — each line carries the tags its page gives, copied from the sources; the register adds none and upgrades none — machine checks are the twelve scripts in `verify/`, run by the gate [`scripts/check.sh`](../../scripts/check.sh) (the eleventh, [`verify/literature_check.py`](../../verify/literature_check.py), checks the literature-check note against its record and, in its section G, the duality dictionary's algebra; the twelfth, [`verify/dual_sector.py`](../../verify/dual_sector.py), checks the dual-sector note against its record), and a line with no script rests on derivation [D], postulate [P] or the literature [T]. Under pressure, in the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md)'s words: "The dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3" (Data Release 3).

## How to read the tables

- **Claim** links the claim's page and states the claim in brief, in the order of the wiki's sections. Established literature results, such as [the compatibility theorem](../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md), are not program claims and are not listed; neither are open problems, which are in [open problems and the queue](open-problems.md).
- **Tags** are the [provenance tags](../method/provenance-tags.md) the page's status line gives, as its sources attach them: [D] derived in the program, [T] literature, [P] postulate, [O] open, [V] machine-verified; † marks a citation from memory and [S] one found by live search. A dash-style tag such as [D — structural] is the source's own qualifier.
- **What verifies it** names a script in `verify/` run by [the gate](../method/verification/the-gate.md), a [blind review](../method/review/_index.md) round that re-derived the claim, or *not machine-checked* where the claim rests on its derivation, its postulate or its citations. [#11 §2](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#2-methodological-preface-provenance-and-verification) sets the limit of a script: "a verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes".

## Under pressure

- **The dark-energy sector.** The second data release of the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument](../cosmology/dark-energy/desi.md) favors a phantom crossing above [redshift](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift.md) z ≈ 0.4 (z measures the stretching of light by the expansion of the universe since the light was emitted; larger z means earlier), against the framework's strict w ≥ −1, where w is dark energy's equation of state, its pressure divided by its energy density, and −1 is the value for a cosmological constant ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md); [the phantom crossing](../cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing.md)). It bears on τ's quintessence, the no-phantom bound and row 4.
- **Degeneration risks**, [#11 §15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment): "tunable wave-island collective sector (quarantined); gate tail not yet forced; the novelty demotion of §7.4 as a standing caution, joined in v2.1 by the failure of #14's soliton model (§12) and the §5 re-attribution of the compatibility theorem", and the phantom crossing.
- **Falsification exposure**: [#11 §15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment) names rows 1, 4 and 7 as exposed to falsification in the near term.
- **Row 5's premise.** With the potential as written the turnaround needs a closed universe, and in the program's model class the data disfavour closure at about 2σ, a background fit without supernovae (flag 27 addendum; [a closed Comatter universe](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md#what-the-data-say)).
- **The gate's mechanism.** The gate-mechanism note of 2026-10-28 finds that exchanging [#11 §7](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton)'s X cannot produce [#11 §8](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate)'s gate, and flag 55, pending, proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism ([the gate mechanism](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)). The gate itself is not refuted; rows 1–3 depend on the tag, and whether row 3's island discriminant survives is open.
- **Conjugate special relativity** rests mainly on one document, #13. No review round has examined #13, no `verify/` script checks its derivations, and #11 does not mention it; its standing comes from [`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen) and from the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), which lists #13 among the current papers.
