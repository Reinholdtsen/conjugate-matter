*This register lists the program's claims that currently stand: postulates, derived results and predictions that no calculation, review or observation has yet killed. Each line links to the claim's page, which gives its provenance and what checks it. Standing is not the same as confirmed: most of these claims are untested by experiment, the machine checks confirm algebra but not whether the algebra describes nature, and the dark-energy sector is under pressure from current data.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-20 — each line carries the tag its source gives — machine checks run in the gate `scripts/check.sh` (the scripts in `verify/`), and a claim with no script rests on derivation [D], on postulate [P] or on the literature [T].

## Key facts

- "Alive" means a program claim that currently stands. Established literature results, such as the [compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) [T][V], carry the status established on their own pages and are not listed here.
- The [hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core) is "intact, twice sharpened by forced corrections (P2′; the w-dictionary)" (#11 §15).
- Tier 1 of the checkpoint, its explicit algebra, is machine-verified: "All seven passed [V]" (#11 §2), now `verify/battery.py`. The toy-model results are checked by `verify/toy_coset.py`, `verify/toy_gw.py`, `verify/toy_s4.py` and `verify/toy_s5.py`, and the soliton-obituary numbers by `verify/soliton_obituary.py`. "A verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes" (#11 §2).
- Under pressure: the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi)'s Data Release 2 (DESI DR2) "favors a phantom crossing above z ≈ 0.4, against the framework's strict w ≥ −1", and "the dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3" (Data Release 3; root `CLAUDE.md`).
- #11 §15's degeneration risks: the tunable wave-island collective sector (quarantined), the gate's tail not yet forced, the novelty demotion of §7.4, the soliton obituary and the re-attribution of the compatibility theorem, and the phantom crossing against w ≥ −1.
- Conjugate special relativity rests on #13, which tags its results [D]. No review round has examined #13, no script in `verify/` covers it, and #11 v2.2 does not mention it; its standing comes from `HANDOFF.md` §2 and from the root `CLAUDE.md` listing #13 among the current papers.
- Falsification exposure is "genuine and near-term (rows 1, 4, 7)" (#11 §15).
