*This register lists the statements the program once made and later replaced with corrected versions. A superseded statement differs from a dead claim: the subject survives, but its earlier form is no longer the program's position, often because a calculation or a review forced a repair. Each line names the old statement, what replaced it and where the replacement is recorded. The older documents are kept as an audit trail and still contain the old forms: the [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook), for example, keeps its version-0.1 body with the corrections appended as addenda, so the old forms read there as if they were current.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-30 — no tags of its own; each replacement keeps the tag its source gives — the replacements are recorded in higher-precedence sources: [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint), version 2 of the toy-model note, the review adjudications and the root `CLAUDE.md`.

Two pages have the status superseded. Every other entry is an earlier form recorded on the page of the claim that replaced it, under a heading "Superseded: …" that this register links; the few recorded in a page's body without such a heading are marked "in the body". The documents themselves succeed one another: the lab notebook #1 (version 0.1 with addenda v0.2–v0.6), then the program v1.0 (#9), then checkpoint #11, which "Supersedes v2.4, v2.3, v2.2, v2.1, v2.0 and v1.0". The notes are "superseded as reference but kept" (root `CLAUDE.md`).

A statement and the idea behind it have separate statuses. The heuristic w_eff = −2 − w̃ was superseded by w_eff = −w̃, and the version of collapse-powered expansion that rested on it failed, since it needed w = −2, which the data exclude; the idea is P.R.'s open conjecture [O], through the corrected rule's dual stiff ↦ Λ ([Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion)). The [naive toy potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential), replaced by a bounded one, is under review ([claims under review](/wiki/status/under-review)). P.R.'s conjectures are listed in [the conjectures register](/wiki/status/conjectures), with the evidence on their own pages.
