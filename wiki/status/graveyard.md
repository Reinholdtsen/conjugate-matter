*The graveyard is the register of the Comatter program's dead claims, those that theory and data killed. It holds one: [#14's model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md) of a third dark-matter candidate. The withdrawn manuscript [#14](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) modeled the candidate as superheavy magnetic monopoles of a hidden force, described with ordinary kinematics, and the model failed in review round 3 on its recomputed abundance. The entry comes with the parts of the model told on other pages and its withdrawal from the program's predictions table. The candidate it modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, is P.R.'s open conjecture. The last section says where other kinds of claim are listed.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-30 — no tags of its own; the entry keeps the tags of its obituary page — the death listed rests on the verdict of blind review round 3 and its Decision 2. How the program reports failed calculations and dead claims is described under [negative results and dead claims](../method/negative-results.md).

## Dead claims

### Dark matter: #14's soliton model

Checkpoint [#11](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) v2.0 offered a third dark-matter candidate, topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector, stable by topological charge and, it claimed, localized in momentum by topology rather than by collective dynamics ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates)). The standalone manuscript [#14](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/_index.md) modeled it as superheavy [topological solitons](../symmetry-breaking/topological-defects.md#topological-and-non-topological-solitons-t) of a hidden gauge sector, with ordinary kinematics: "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" ([#14 §1](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md#1-framework)). [Review round 3](../method/review/round-3/_index.md) graded #14 v3 C, and on 2026-10-19 round-3 [Decision 2](../method/review/round-3/decisions.md#decision-2-14s-model-retired) retired the model as a program-native proposal; the manuscript was withdrawn the same day and kept as the audit trail in [`notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md`](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md). Decision 2 covers #14's model, which used ordinary kinematics. The candidate it modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, is P.R.'s open conjecture [O]: #14's model did not treat localization in momentum, so the candidate itself has not been tested ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md)). The six pieces of the model that the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) names, [the hidden sector](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector.md), [the coupling α_d ≈ 34](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md), [the Kibble normalization](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md), [the √N floor](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor.md), [the ψ relic window](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window.md) and [Planck-suppressed mixing](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator.md), are under review ([the pieces of #14's model](under-review.md#the-pieces-of-14s-model)). The rulings are on [round-3 verdict and rulings](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md), and the [V] numbers of the model's failure are gated in [`verify/soliton_obituary.py`](../../verify/soliton_obituary.py) ([the soliton-obituary script](../method/verification/soliton-obituary-script.md)).

- [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md). **Claimed:** the dark matter is superheavy ['t Hooft–Polyakov monopoles](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/thooft-polyakov-monopole.md) of a hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, described with ordinary kinematics, stable because their field configuration cannot unwind, dark because no gauge force of ordinary matter acts on them, and with a mass fixed by the abundance with no free [dilution](../cosmology/early-universe/relic-yield-and-dilution.md#entropy-dilution-t); #14 v3's benchmark was 3×10¹³ GeV. **Killed by:** [the round-3 verdict](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#the-verdict), C, with which the adjudicator agreed. The count of monopoles had been normalized too low; counted correctly, the abundance closes at "M ≈ 2.9–4.4×10¹² GeV [V]", and the benchmark "is overproduced by 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ [V]" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates)). #11: "#14's model fails as a program-native proposal: with ordinary kinematics it is either overproduced or the prior-art PeV-scale scenario, to which the framework adds nothing testable." The version that survives, with a symmetry-breaking scale v ≲ 100 PeV, is [prior art](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art.md) (Murayama–Shu; Liveoak–Maharana–Wells) that does not depend on Born reciprocity (round-3 Decision 2).

### Parts of #14's soliton model

Some dead items have no obituary of their own; they are told in sections of other pages. The items below are the model's own outputs and claims, dead only as parts of it. They fell with the model (rulings 2 and 9 and round-3 Decision 2, as each entry says). The six pieces of the model that the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) names are not among them: they are under review ([the pieces of #14's model](under-review.md#the-pieces-of-14s-model)). Hypothesis H, the third way #14 fixed the coupling α_d ≈ 34, which no ruling addressed by itself, is under review with that coupling (root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), 'Since the handoff'; [hypothesis H](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md#hypothesis-h)). The entries link the section that tells each, the obituary and the ruling.

- **The headline mass band**, in [What v3 had claimed](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction.md#what-v3-had-claimed) on overproduction at the benchmark (alive). **Claimed:** #14 v3's abstract put the monopole mass "at about 1e13 to 4e13 GeV with no dilution required". **Killed by:** [ruling 2](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#rulings-1-to-3-how-many-monopoles-form), upheld: "The M\* band and "no dilution" fail". The corrected numbers stand on the same page [V]. Obituary: [#14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md#the-benchmark-is-overproduced).
- **The gravitational-wave band of the hidden transition**, in [The gravitational-wave band (died with #14's model)](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-thermal-history.md#the-gravitational-wave-band-died-with-14s-model) on the hidden sector's thermal history (record), and in [The hidden transition of #14's model](../cosmology/early-universe/phase-transition-gravitational-waves.md#the-hidden-transition-of-14s-model) on gravitational waves from phase transitions (established). **Claimed:** if the hidden symmetry breaking were first order, it would leave a stochastic gravitational-wave background "with a present-day peak frequency of ~1e5 to 1e7 Hz" ([#14 §4](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md#4-observational-consistency)). **Killed by:** it was a prediction of #14's model and died with it (round-3 Decision 2). Ruling 5 had already moved its upper end to 3.2×10⁷ Hz, and the round-3 referee had found a first-order transition inconsistent with #14's own count of monopoles. Obituary: [#14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md).
- **A further claim of #14 v3**, in [Ruling 9 and the claim it contradicted](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/witten-effect.md#ruling-9-and-the-claim-it-contradicted) on the Witten effect (established): "The decisive constraints therefore act on psi, not on the dark matter itself" ([#14 §2](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md#2-the-hidden-sector-and-its-coupling)). [Ruling 9](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#ruling-9-the-witten-effect), accepted, not re-checked: with mixing and a physical hidden θ the monopoles carry a visible electric charge, which contradicts the claim whenever ε ≠ 0; Decision 1's strict sector separation closed the channel. Obituary: [#14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md#the-other-rulings).

Ruling 13's finding is not listed, because it concerns the manuscript's method rather than a part of the model: [#14 §6](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md#6-status-of-claims-and-acceptance-criteria) stated that "All numerical statements in Secs. 2-4 were computed by executed code rather than by hand", [ruling 13](../method/review/round-3/rulings.md#ruling-13-the-claim-of-executed-code) upheld in substance that this overstates what the code showed ("The code checked the arithmetic of a wrong model"), and the lesson became a rule of method, [verification checks arithmetic, not the model](../method/verification/arithmetic-not-model.md).

## Withdrawn predictions

[#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions) closes the predictions table with its withdrawals: #14's superheavy-soliton model (§12), withdrawn with cause; velocity-space granularity in halos and Fornax friction suppression (§9), withdrawn and under review. The [predictions section](../predictions/_index.md) lists the withdrawals, and [the history of the table](../predictions/table-history.md) records when each left it. This register lists the withdrawal of #14's model. The two under review, [old row 3](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md) and [old row 4](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md), and [Scenario A](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md) with its localized signature in ultra-faint dwarfs, are on [claims under review](under-review.md#momentum-clumping).

## What is not listed here

- **Claims under review.** The calculations or data weigh against these claims, and their final status awaits review: [claims under review](under-review.md).
- **P.R.'s conjectures.** [Velocity clumping, the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md), [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md), [their explanation of the monopole null result](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md), [conjugate collapse powering our expansion](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion.md) and [the magnetic mirror](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md) are open conjectures [O], listed in [conjectures](conjectures.md); each page states the evidence in full. For the founding image, [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) gives this evidence: for particle dark matter the mean-field criterion "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark" [D]; where clumping would be mandatory for wave dark matter, Scenario A, the faintest dwarf galaxies weigh against it; with the coupling free it is "optional, never required". For collapse-powered expansion, two versions failed, the heuristic w = −2 (w is dark energy's [equation of state](../cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence.md#dark-energy-and-the-equation-of-state), its pressure divided by its energy density), which the data exclude, and dual dust, which the corrected dictionary maps to dust; the route that remains is dual stiff ↦ Λ ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)).
- **The founding suppositions.** The present standing of all eleven is on [founding suppositions and their fates](../program/founding-suppositions.md), and the dated events are on [the timeline](../history/timeline.md).
- **Superseded statements.** Earlier forms of surviving claims, replaced by corrected versions, such as the exponential cliff of the scattering law or the original P2, are in [superseded statements](superseded.md).
- **Negative results that stand.** Exclusions the program derived and still holds are standing claims, listed in [claims that stand](alive.md): a massless X is excluded ([why X must be massive](../gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain.md)), potentials built from metrics fail ([the metric tier fails twice](../gravity/tuning-test/metric-tier-failures.md)), the minimal hidden sector is excluded ([W′ overclosure](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure.md)), the soliton benchmark is overproduced ([overproduction at the benchmark](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction.md)), the classical breaking in the complex toy model does not survive quantization ([quantum restoration](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/quantum-restoration.md)), and in the aligned vacuum a range of X above 37.7 μm is excluded ([the laboratory bound on X's range](../gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound.md)).
- **The clumping loophole.** Topological localization of individual objects in momentum space "remains untested; its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates)): [Open: the topological loophole](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole.md).
- **Chat-only and popsci-only claims** are in [conjectures](conjectures.md). Two of the chat-only claims, the √N floor and [the square rectangle at the bounce](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce.md), are under review and are also listed in [claims under review](under-review.md).
