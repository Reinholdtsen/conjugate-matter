*The graveyard is the register of the Broken-Born program's dead claims: ideas the program put forward that its calculations, a [blind review](/wiki/method/review) or an observation then killed. Each entry says what was claimed and what killed it, and links the claim's obituary page and the calculation, review ruling or decision that settled it; dead items told inside other pages, and the predictions withdrawn from the program's table, are listed with them. A claim recorded here is not revived.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-20 — no tags of its own; each entry keeps the tags of its obituary page — every death was decided by the calculation, review ruling or recorded decision named in its entry, except the magnetic identification, a claim found only in a popular article (popsci-only), which no program document addresses and which the wiki records as dead because it conflicts with the record. The root `CLAUDE.md` rule applies: "Claims listed as dead in HANDOFF §2 stay dead." How the program treats its dead claims is described under [negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results).

## Dead claims by section

One entry per obituary page, in the order of the wiki's sections. **Claimed** says what the claim was; **killed by** names what decided its death and links the page that records it.

### Spontaneous breaking of reciprocity

- [Obituary: the naive ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential). **Claimed:** the term −μ[Tr(K K_ref)]², which version 1 of [the toy-model note](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) added to [model I](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model) so that its circle of vacua reduces to two vacua exchanged by ρ, the [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity) map, and the breaking of reciprocity survives in 1+1 dimensions. **Killed by:** [round-4 findings F1 and F2](/wiki/method/review/round-4#f1-and-f2-the-potential-runs-away-when-h-moves), both upheld. Once the metric H is dynamical the potential "is unbounded below in model I", reaching "−8.9 × 10⁶ μ at boost parameter 4" (toy-model note §4), and the intended vacuum (1, K_ref) is a saddle, so the Monte Carlo had simulated a [frozen-H reduction](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/frozen-h-reduction). Version 2 of the note replaced it on 2026-10-20 with [the bounded potential V_b](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/bounded-potential); `verify/toy_coset.py` checks the runaway and the saddle.

### Dark matter: momentum clumping

The founding image and the predictions built on it died in [Debt 3](/wiki/history/debts#debt-3-the-stream-instability), the calculations carried out in [calc notes #2–#4](/wiki/history/documents/debt-3-notes) and summarized in #11 §9 and in [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem): velocity-space clumping is "forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)". No `verify/` script checks any Debt-3 number.

- [Obituary: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image). **Claimed:** a smooth dark-matter halo breaks up into a few streams of particles moving with nearly the same velocity, so that dark matter clumps in velocity rather than in position; [founding supposition 3](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions#3-matter-clumps-in-momentum-space), "Matter clumps in momentum space". **Killed by:** Debt 3, three ways (#11 §9). For particle dark matter the mean-field criterion "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark" [D]; on the [wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island), the one branch in which clumping is mandatory, Scenario A, is falsified by the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies [D + T]; elsewhere clumping is "optional, never required". #11 App. A item 3 records the supposition as "dead as dynamics", and [the handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff#dead) lists it as dead.
- [Obituary: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a). **Claimed:** on the wave island, the gated attraction that condenses wave-like dark matter also makes the cores of dwarf galaxies; fitting the cores fixes its coupling, and condensation then becomes mandatory in the [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies). **Killed by:** calc note #4 ([Debt 3c](/wiki/history/documents/debt-3-notes#4-debt3c-the-ultra-faint-dwarf-endpoint)). The condensates would pass their critical mass, collapse and drain the halo, and "Scenario A is falsified by the existence of ultra-faint dwarfs". #11 §9 (ii), tagged [D + T], records the endpoint as "falsified by observed UFD density and longevity" (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf) and the escape to a much lighter mass as closed independently.
- [Obituary: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity). **Claimed:** row 3 of [the lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s predictions table and one of its three flagship discriminants: the dark matter of a relaxed halo such as the Milky Way's is split into of order ten macro-streams of width about v\*, whose gravity leaves correlated, long-wavelength perturbations along cold stellar streams (#1 §9). **Killed by:** the death of the founding image it rested on. Calc note #2 withdrew the row on the particle island, and calc note #4 on both: "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands." #11 §13 lists "velocity-space granularity in halos" among the predictions withdrawn with cause.
- [Obituary: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction). **Claimed:** row 4 of the notebook's table: dynamical friction, the drag that makes a massive body sink toward the centre of a galaxy, is suppressed in condensed dwarf-galaxy cores, so the globular clusters of the Fornax dwarf galaxy stay put (#1 §9). **Killed by:** the condensed cores came from the same velocity-space condensation as the founding image, and Debt 3 removed them: "The Fornax mechanism goes with them" (calc note #4). #11 §13 lists "Fornax friction suppression (obituary §9)" among the predictions withdrawn with cause.

### Dark matter: the soliton candidate

Checkpoint #11 v2.0 offered a third dark-matter candidate, superheavy [topological solitons](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects#topological-and-non-topological-solitons-t) of a hidden gauge sector, and the standalone manuscript [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) worked it out. [Review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) graded #14 v3 C, and on 2026-10-19 round-3 [Decision 2](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions#decision-2-the-soliton-candidate-retired) retired the candidate; the manuscript was withdrawn the same day and kept as the audit trail in `notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md`. The candidate and the parts built only for it are dead. The rulings are on [round-3 verdict and rulings](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings), and the [V] numbers of the obituary are gated in `verify/soliton_obituary.py` ([the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script)).

- [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary). **Claimed:** the dark matter is superheavy ['t Hooft–Polyakov monopoles](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/thooft-polyakov-monopole) of a hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, stable because their field configuration cannot unwind, dark because no gauge force of ordinary matter acts on them, and with a mass fixed by the abundance with no free [dilution](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/relic-yield-and-dilution#entropy-dilution-t); #14 v3's benchmark was 3×10¹³ GeV. **Killed by:** [the round-3 verdict](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#the-verdict), C, with which the adjudicator agreed. The count of monopoles had been normalized too low; counted correctly, the abundance closes at "M ≈ 2.9–4.4×10¹² GeV [V]", and the benchmark "is overproduced by 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ [V]" (#11 §12). Decision 2 retired the candidate "as a program-native proposal" rather than restore its mass with a free dilution factor. The version that survives, with a symmetry-breaking scale v ≲ 100 PeV, is [prior art](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art) (Murayama–Shu; Liveoak–Maharana–Wells) that does not depend on Born reciprocity (round-3 Decision 2).
- [Obituary: the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector). **Claimed:** the dark sector is a hidden SU(2) gauge force broken to U(1) by an adjoint Higgs field, which leaves heavy W′± bosons, a massless hidden photon and the monopoles, plus a light fermion ψ added so that the W′± can decay; #14 identified this sector with the momentum-polarized half of the program's matter. **Killed by:** retirement with the candidate (round-3 Decision 2). The model itself was not refuted: its minimal form is excluded by [W′ overclosure](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure), and its low-scale version is prior art (Murayama–Shu; Liveoak–Maharana–Wells) that does not depend on Born reciprocity (round-3 Decision 2).
- [Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling). **Claimed:** the hidden monopoles' coupling has the value α_d ≈ 34, fixed three ways in turn. **Killed by:** [round 1](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two#round-1-14-v1), for [attempt 1](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling#attempt-1-dirac-quantization-against-our-photon), [Dirac quantization](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) against our photon; the handoff calls it the "round-1 reviewer's valid kill: can't use our DSZ lattice and then deny coupling to our photon" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), DSZ being the Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger charge lattice. The second form, reciprocity acting on the coupling as [modular S](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/modular-group#the-coupling-conjecture-of-the-soliton-manuscript), was round 2's weakest link ("numerology", `HANDOFF.md` §6) and was set aside; it is filed as [superseded](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling#superseded-reciprocity-acting-as-modular-s). [Attempt 3](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling#attempt-3-hypothesis-h), hypothesis H, was motivated rather than derived and died with #14 v3 (round-3 Decision 2). What survives is general: "A weakly coupled hidden sector always has strongly coupled monopoles, by Dirac quantization [T]" (#11 §12).
- [Obituary: the Kibble normalization](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization). **Claimed:** #14's count of the monopoles formed at the hidden transition by the [Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism), the yield n/s = p (T_c/M_Pl)³, normalized at p = 1 in v2 and p ≈ 0.023 in v3, with p = 1 treated as the upper end. **Killed by:** [round-3 ruling 1](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#rulings-1-to-3-how-many-monopoles-form), upheld. v3's value dropped the factor (1.66√g\*)³, and one defect per Hubble volume gives "p ≈ 104 [V]" (#11 §12), gated in `verify/soliton_obituary.py` as p = 104.31. Ruling 3 added that this count is [a floor, not a ceiling](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization#a-floor-not-a-ceiling).
- [Obituary: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor). **Claimed:** an argument made only in the claude.ai chat, that the Kibble mechanism leaves a net topological charge which annihilation cannot remove; in the handoff's words, "this bounds the relic abundance between Kibble ceiling and √N floor" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). The handoff queued the calculation as its §8 item 3. **Killed by:** [round-3 Decision 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions#decision-3-the-n-floor-dropped): "Its premise, Kibble as a ceiling, is inverted (ruling 3)", and "Under decision 2 it has no candidate left to serve." Ruling 10 had also found annihilation at the monopoles' strong coupling already worked out for hidden sectors. The argument was never written into a document, and no number belongs to it.
- [Obituary: the ψ relic window](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window). **Claimed:** #14 v3's mass window for ψ, the light hidden fermion added so that the W′± could decay: light enough for ψ to be a minor part of the dark matter, heavy enough for it to have annihilated before [nucleosynthesis](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/big-bang-nucleosynthesis#the-ψ-relic-windows-lower-edge). **Killed by:** [ruling 4](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#ruling-4-the-ψ-relic-window), upheld. v3 had left out ψ̄ and the second member of the doublet and bracketed an exactly known cross-section by up to a factor of ten; the recount [V] puts 10% of the dark matter at 45–49 GeV and all of it at 146–158 GeV, so "v3's 0.1–0.3 TeV was wrong". [Ruling 6](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#rulings-5-and-6-the-colder-hidden-sector-and-nucleosynthesis), accepted, not re-checked, moved [the lower edge](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window#the-lower-edge-freeze-out-during-nucleosynthesis): ψ at MeV masses would still be [freezing out](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/thermal-freeze-out#freeze-out-in-a-sector-at-its-own-temperature-t) during Big Bang nucleosynthesis.
- [Obituary: Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator). **Claimed:** #14 v3's estimate of the [kinetic mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing) ε between the hidden photon and ours, from a dimension-5 operator with an "O(1) coefficient at the Planck scale". **Killed by:** [ruling 7](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#rulings-7-and-8-the-mixing-operator), upheld: the coefficient has no source, and "the literature generates it at one loop from bi-charged states, which sector separation forbids". Ruling 8 found the operator itself in the literature. [Decision 1](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions#decision-1-strict-sector-separation) then took [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) strictly, with ε ≲ 10⁻¹³ from gravity alone.

### Cosmology

- [Obituary: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion). **Claimed:** the gravitational collapse of the [conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe) drives the accelerating expansion of ours; [founding supposition 9](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions#9-their-collapse-is-our-acceleration), "Their collapse is our acceleration". **Killed by:** the data, then a derivation. The chat-era translation rule w_eff = −2 − w̃ required w = −2, which is falsified; the corrected [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary), w_eff = −w̃, maps dual dust to dust and so removes the mechanism. The handoff: "first version gave w = −2 (falsified); corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)" ([its dead list](/wiki/history/documents/handoff#dead)); #11 App. A item 9: "dead twice". The corrected dictionary is already in the lab notebook's version 0.1 (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §0, §6).

### Electric–magnetic duality

- [Obituary: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation). **Claimed:** magnetic monopoles exist but belong to the momentum-polarized half of physics, which is why no experiment has found one; the explanatory half of [founding supposition 5](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions#5-monopoles-live-in-conjugate-space-explaining-non-detection). **Killed by:** #11's monopole audit (§11.1, [D]): "the relevant duality is electric–magnetic (E↔B, g → 1/g), not x↔p, and inflationary dilution already accounts for absence [T]". The handoff lists it as dead, "wrong duality; inflation suffices". The other half of the supposition, [conjugate monopoles as ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles), stands.
- [Obituary: the magnetic identification (popsci-only)](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification). **Claimed:** only in the popular article "The Universe's Other Half" (#12): "The momentum-polarized sector is the magnetic side of electromagnetism", dark because Dirac's condition makes its coupling strong, "about 34, against our 1/137". **Killed by:** no ruling, since it was never a program claim. The wiki records it as dead because it conflicts with the dead α_d ≈ 34 via Dirac quantization against our photon, with [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) taken strictly (round-3 Decision 1), and with [row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null), the permanent null in direct detection.

## What is not listed here

- **Superseded statements.** Earlier forms of surviving claims, replaced by corrected versions, such as the exponential cliff of the scattering law or the original P2, are in [superseded statements](/wiki/status/superseded). So is the second form of α_d, which was set aside rather than refuted.
- **Negative results that stand.** Exclusions the program derived and still holds are standing claims, listed in [claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive): a massless X is excluded ([why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain)), potentials built from metrics fail ([the metric tier fails twice](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/metric-tier-failures)), the minimal hidden sector is excluded ([W′ overclosure](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure)), the soliton benchmark is overproduced ([overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction)), and the classical breaking in the complex toy model does not survive quantization ([quantum restoration](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/quantum-restoration)).
- **The Debt-3 loophole.** Topological localization of individual objects in momentum space "remains untested, but has no candidate" (#11 §12): [Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole).
- **Chat-only and popsci-only claims that are not dead** are in [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures). The two that are dead, the √N floor and the magnetic identification, are listed here.
