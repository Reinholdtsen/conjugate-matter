*The graveyard is the register of the Comatter program's dead claims: claims that theory and data killed and in whose death Paul explicitly concurred when asked about that death alone. Since 2026-10-29 that has been the rule, and on that day Paul answered for every death recorded before it. He concurred in one: the death of [#14's model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) of a third dark-matter candidate. The withdrawn manuscript #14 modeled the candidate as superheavy magnetic monopoles of a hidden force, described with ordinary kinematics, and the model failed in review round 3 on its recomputed abundance. It is the one entry here, with the parts of it told on other pages and its withdrawal from the program's predictions table. The other claims this register held until that day have moved: twelve are under review, not dead, until Paul reviews them with Claude; the founding image of dark matter clumping in velocity is his open conjecture again; and the two versions of collapse-powered expansion that failed are recorded as failed versions of an open conjecture, not as deaths. The last section says where each went.*

**Status:** Record, a register current to 2026-10-29 and #11 v2.5 — no tags of its own; the entry keeps the tags of its obituary page — the death listed was decided by review round 3 and its Decision 2, and Paul concurred in it on 2026-10-29: "#14's model is dead, with his concurrence (§12)" (#11 v2.5 header). The rule is the root `CLAUDE.md`'s, under 'Method': "A claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs." He is asked for each death separately, his answer is recorded with its date, each death is a change of its own, and a failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea. The rule it replaced read "Claims listed as dead in HANDOFF §2 stay dead." How the program treats its dead claims is described under [negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results).

## Dead claims

### Dark matter: #14's soliton model

Checkpoint #11 v2.0 offered a third dark-matter candidate, topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector, stable by topological charge and, it claimed, localized in momentum by topology rather than by collective dynamics (#11 §12). The standalone manuscript [#14](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) modeled it as superheavy [topological solitons](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects#topological-and-non-topological-solitons-t) of a hidden gauge sector, with ordinary kinematics: "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" (#14 §1). [Review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) graded #14 v3 C, and on 2026-10-19 round-3 [Decision 2](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions#decision-2-the-soliton-candidate-retired) retired the candidate; the manuscript was withdrawn the same day and kept as the audit trail in `notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md`. On 2026-10-29 Paul narrowed Decision 2 to #14's model, and #11 records the candidate itself, which #14 never modeled in momentum, as his open conjecture ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles)). The same day he concurred in the model's death. In #11 §12's words, "Its retirement was a decision P.R. delegated (round-3 Decision 2); on 2026-10-29 he concurred that the model is dead", and the adjudication's note on Decision 2 says "On 2026-10-29 Paul concurred that #14's model is dead." The six pieces of the model that the root `CLAUDE.md` names, [the hidden sector](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector), [the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling), [the Kibble normalization](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization), [the √N floor](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor), [the ψ relic window](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window) and [Planck-suppressed mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator), are under review, not dead ([the pieces of #14's model](/wiki/status/under-review#the-pieces-of-14s-model)). The rulings are on [round-3 verdict and rulings](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings), and the [V] numbers of the model's failure are gated in `verify/soliton_obituary.py` ([the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script)).

- [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary). **Claimed:** the dark matter is superheavy ['t Hooft–Polyakov monopoles](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/thooft-polyakov-monopole) of a hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, described with ordinary kinematics, stable because their field configuration cannot unwind, dark because no gauge force of ordinary matter acts on them, and with a mass fixed by the abundance with no free [dilution](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/relic-yield-and-dilution#entropy-dilution-t); #14 v3's benchmark was 3×10¹³ GeV. **Killed by:** [the round-3 verdict](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#the-verdict), C, with which the adjudicator agreed. The count of monopoles had been normalized too low; counted correctly, the abundance closes at "M ≈ 2.9–4.4×10¹² GeV [V]", and the benchmark "is overproduced by 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ [V]" (#11 §12). #11: "#14's model fails as a program-native proposal: with ordinary kinematics it is either overproduced or the prior-art PeV-scale scenario, to which the framework adds nothing testable." The version that survives, with a symmetry-breaking scale v ≲ 100 PeV, is [prior art](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art) (Murayama–Shu; Liveoak–Maharana–Wells) that does not depend on Born reciprocity (round-3 Decision 2). **Concurrence:** Paul's, 2026-10-29 (#11 §12; `review/round3-adjudication.md`, Decision 2's note). Retiring the candidate had been a decision he delegated, which he narrowed that day to #14's model; the candidate it modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, is his open conjecture.

### Parts of #14's soliton model

Some dead items have no obituary of their own; they are told in sections of other pages. The items below are the model's own outputs and claims, recorded dead only within its obituary. They fell with the model (rulings 2 and 9 and round-3 Decision 2, as each entry says), and Paul concurred in the model's death on 2026-10-29. The six pieces of the model that the root `CLAUDE.md` names were put to him as deaths of their own, and he chose to review them ([the pieces of #14's model](/wiki/status/under-review#the-pieces-of-14s-model)). Hypothesis H, the third way #14 fixed the coupling α_d ≈ 34, was recorded dead as a component of the model, but no ruling addressed it by itself, and a failed model is not the death of each idea in it. H is under review with that coupling (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'; [how it was retired](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling#how-it-was-retired)). The entries link the section that tells each, the obituary and the ruling.

- **The headline mass band**, in [What v3 had claimed](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction#what-v3-had-claimed) on overproduction at the benchmark (alive). **Claimed:** #14 v3's abstract put the monopole mass "at about 1e13 to 4e13 GeV with no dilution required". **Killed by:** [ruling 2](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#rulings-1-to-3-how-many-monopoles-form), upheld: "The M\* band and "no dilution" fail". The corrected numbers stand on the same page [V]. Obituary: [#14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary#the-benchmark-is-overproduced).
- **The gravitational-wave band of the hidden transition**, in [The gravitational-wave band (died with #14's model)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-thermal-history#the-gravitational-wave-band-died-with-14s-model) on the hidden sector's thermal history (record), and in [The hidden transition of #14's model](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/phase-transition-gravitational-waves#the-hidden-transition-of-14s-model) on gravitational waves from phase transitions (established). **Claimed:** if the hidden symmetry breaking were first order, it would leave a stochastic gravitational-wave background "with a present-day peak frequency of ~1e5 to 1e7 Hz" (#14 §4). **Killed by:** it was a prediction of #14's model and died with it (round-3 Decision 2). Ruling 5 had already moved its upper end to 3.2×10⁷ Hz, and the round-3 referee had found a first-order transition inconsistent with #14's own count of monopoles. Obituary: [#14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).
- **A further claim of #14 v3**, in [Ruling 9 and the claim it contradicted](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/witten-effect#ruling-9-and-the-claim-it-contradicted) on the Witten effect (established): "The decisive constraints therefore act on psi, not on the dark matter itself" (#14 §2). [Ruling 9](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#ruling-9-the-witten-effect), accepted, not re-checked: with mixing and a physical hidden θ the monopoles carry a visible electric charge, which contradicts the claim whenever ε ≠ 0; Decision 1's strict sector separation closed the channel. Obituary: [#14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary#the-other-rulings).

Ruling 13's finding is not listed, because it concerns the manuscript's method rather than a part of the model: #14 §6 stated that "All numerical statements in Secs. 2-4 were computed by executed code rather than by hand", [ruling 13](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings#ruling-13-the-claim-of-executed-code) upheld in substance that this overstates what the code showed ("The code checked the arithmetic of a wrong model"), and the lesson became a rule of method, [verification checks arithmetic, not the model](/wiki/method/verification/arithmetic-not-model).

## How a death is recorded

Since 2026-10-29 the rule has been that a death needs theory and data and Paul's explicit concurrence, asked for that death alone and recorded with the date, and that it is made in a change of its own, never inside a larger one (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Method'). The one death here was decided by blind review, [round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings), and a recorded decision, [Decision 2](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions), and Paul concurred in it on 2026-10-29. Until that day the register listed deaths decided by the program's calculations alone, by blind review alone, and, in one case, by the wiki's own reading of the record, the magnetic identification; flag 60 found that a verdict reached the last way adds a result, which the wiki does not do. [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff#dead) headed its list of the dead "Dead (killed by own calculations — keep dead)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), and a note added under that heading begins "Overtaken, 2026-10-29: none of the four below is dead now."

The fates of all eleven founding suppositions are on [founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions), none of them recorded dead since #11 v2.5, and the dates of the deaths and restorations since the move to this repository are on [the timeline](/wiki/history/timeline).

## Moved

On 2026-10-29 Paul directed that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs, and the same day he answered for every death recorded before then (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff'). The claims this register held until his answers went to three places; on each page the evidence is stated in full.

- **Under review: twelve claims**, recorded here until 2026-10-29 and chosen by Paul for review with Claude ("Restore all pending"): [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) and the two prediction rows withdrawn after [the clumping problem](/wiki/history/main-problems#the-clumping-problem), [velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity) and [Fornax friction suppression](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction); six pieces of #14's model, [the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector), [the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling), [the Kibble normalization](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization), [the √N topological floor](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor), [the ψ relic window](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window) and [Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator); [the square rectangle at the bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce) and [the self-dual length as a program scale](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-length-obituary), from the [phase-space-diameter note](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter); and [the naive ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential). Each is under review, not dead, until he reviews it with Claude, and each is listed, with what tested it, on [claims under review](/wiki/status/under-review).
- **P.R.'s conjecture again: the founding image.** Velocity clumping, founding supposition 3, whose death as dynamics this register recorded until 2026-10-29: Paul does not concur in its death, and #11 v2.5 records it as his open conjecture [O] ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)). The evidence, which #11 §9 says stands as results: for particle dark matter the mean-field criterion "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark" [D]; where clumping would be mandatory for wave dark matter, Scenario A, the faintest dwarf galaxies weigh against it; with the coupling free it is "optional, never required". Round-3 Decision 2 had cited its obituary as precedent, "The program's precedent is the clumping obituary: a dead claim is reported prominently and stays dead", and the adjudication's note on the decision now adds: "The clumping obituary cited above as precedent is no longer a death: he did not concur, and the founding image is his open conjecture again (#11 v2.5 §9)."
- **Not deaths: the two failed versions of collapse-powered expansion**, the heuristic w = −2 and dual dust, listed here from v2.4 as failed versions of a restored conjecture. #11 v2.5 §10: "P.R. does not concur in recording the two versions dead (2026-10-29): they are failed versions of an open conjecture, not deaths." The data exclude w = −2, and the corrected dictionary maps dust to dust; both are told on [Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion).

Earlier the same day four other entries left this register for [the conjectures register](/wiki/status/conjectures): #11 v2.4 restored as P.R.'s open conjectures the dark-matter candidacy of conjugate monopoles ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles)), their explanation of the monopole null result ([Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)) and collapse-powered expansion itself, through the corrected dictionary's dual stiff ↦ Λ; and flag 60 reversed the wiki's own obituary of the magnetic identification, now the shared-photon form of P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture ([Conjecture: the momentum sector as the magnetic mirror](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)).

## What is not listed here

- **Claims under review**, recorded dead before 2026-10-29 without Paul's concurrence: [claims under review](/wiki/status/under-review).
- **Superseded statements.** Earlier forms of surviving claims, replaced by corrected versions, such as the exponential cliff of the scattering law or the original P2, are in [superseded statements](/wiki/status/superseded).
- **Negative results that stand.** Exclusions the program derived and still holds are standing claims, listed in [claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive): a massless X is excluded ([why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain)), potentials built from metrics fail ([the metric tier fails twice](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/metric-tier-failures)), the minimal hidden sector is excluded ([W′ overclosure](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure)), the soliton benchmark is overproduced ([overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction)), the classical breaking in the complex toy model does not survive quantization ([quantum restoration](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/quantum-restoration)), and in the aligned vacuum a range of X above 37.7 μm is excluded ([the laboratory bound on X's range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound)).
- **The clumping loophole.** Topological localization of individual objects in momentum space "remains untested; its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles" (#11 §12): [Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole).
- **Chat-only and popsci-only claims** are in [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures), except two chat-only claims under review, the √N floor and the square rectangle at the bounce.
