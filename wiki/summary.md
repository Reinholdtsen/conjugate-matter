*This page answers six questions about the Comatter program, as its record stands on 2026-10-28. The program starts from [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity), the exchange of position and momentum that [the Fourier transform](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/fourier-transform) carries out. The exchange leaves the basic rules of quantum mechanics unchanged, but not the physics observed, in which interactions are local in position. The program takes reciprocity to be an exact symmetry of the laws of physics that the vacuum, the universe's lowest-energy state, does not share (a symmetry [broken spontaneously](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/spontaneous-symmetry-breaking), in physicists' terms), and follows the consequences into gravity, dark matter, dark energy and electric–magnetic duality. By its own assessment it is a research program, not a theory. Its [founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image), dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, is dead, and so is [one of the three dark-matter candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) it has proposed; two remain. What stands is a set of postulates and theoretical results, most of them untested by observation. No observation in the record favours the program in particular: the weakening of dark energy it requires is shared with other models, and current data pair that weakening with a phase the program forbids. Its nearest decisive test is the next data release of the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi) (DESI).*

**Status:** Record — a summary of the status registers and of [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint), the program's current reference: its predictions, open problems and self-assessment (§13–§15), current to the notes and flags of 2026-10-28. It adds no results: each statement is taken from the record with the tags its source gives, and the pages it links hold each topic's sources and what verifies them. Where this page and a source disagree, the source wins. The table and the first paragraph of each section are the summary; the bullets under them give the detail and the caveats.

**Tags**, as the sources give them: [D] derived in this program, [T] established literature, [P] postulate, [O] open, [V] machine-verified, which means a script reproduces the arithmetic, not that the physics is right. Citations marked † were cited from model memory and are to be verified against the source before any external use; [S] marks a source retrieved by live search ([provenance tags](/wiki/method/provenance-tags)).

## Where the program stands

In its own assessment, a research program, not a theory. Its founding image and one of its three dark-matter candidates are dead. What stands is postulates and theoretical results, most of them untested by observation. Its dark-energy sector is under real pressure from DESI's data. A finding that #11's mechanism for the velocity gate in dark matter's self-scattering (rows 1–3), exchange of the second graviton X, is not derived, confirmed by two independent derivations in a note not yet reviewed, awaits Paul's ruling, which he deferred (flag 55). Its main document, checkpoint #11, has never been reviewed.

- **Dead.** The founding image, dark matter clumping in velocity: with the program's contact-range self-interaction, #11 §9 finds it cannot happen if dark matter is made of particles; it would be fatal if dark matter is a wave whose self-interaction also makes the cores of dwarf galaxies, since the faintest dwarfs would then lose their dark matter, which observation excludes; and it is "optional, never required" if that coupling is left free ([obituary](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)). A re-examination on 2026-10-28 found the obituary not premature: it "says clumping is required nowhere, not that it is impossible" (root `CLAUDE.md`; `notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md` §4, not reviewed). The [superheavy-soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), dark matter made of very heavy magnetic monopoles of a hidden force, was retired after blind review round 3 graded its manuscript C, "fundamentally flawed": even at minimum production, solitons of its benchmark mass would make 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ times the observed amount of dark matter [V]. Every dead claim is in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).
- **Stands.** The hard core and the program's postulates [P]; a second graviton with mass, X [D + T], its early universe open; an identity by which X's interaction potential, the term that couples the program's two metrics and gives X its mass, adds no energy to empty space in a flat aligned vacuum, one in which the potential's two frames coincide, as in the vacuum the program assumes [D][V], whether it is new unaudited ([the vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity)); two toy models of the symmetry breaking [D][V], which show only that the structure is consistent; a laboratory bound on X's range in that aligned vacuum [V][S]; two dark-matter candidates, each open [O]; and the dark-energy field [τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence) [D + T]. In the register's words, "Standing is not the same as confirmed" ([claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive)).
- **Under pressure.** Dark energy's equation of state w, the ratio of its pressure to its density, says how its density changes as space expands: at w = −1, a cosmological constant, the density stays fixed; below −1, called phantom, it would grow. The program requires w never to fall below −1, and DESI's Data Release 2 (DR2) favours a phantom phase in the past, at [redshift](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift) above z ≈ 0.4 (higher redshift means further back in time). The root `CLAUDE.md`: "The dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3", DESI's Data Release 3 ([DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing)). And because τ's potential as written never goes negative (flag 27), the predicted reversal of the expansion (row 5 of #11's predictions table) needs a closed universe, which, in the program's own model class, the data disfavour at about 2σ, two standard deviations, in one agent's fit in a note not reviewed ([a closed Comatter universe](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe#what-the-data-say)).
- **Pending.** #11 says the [velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate), which lets dark-matter particles scatter off each other only when they move at nearly the same velocity, follows from exchanging the second graviton X. Two derivations by AI agents of the momentum-gravity investigation, the second done blind, find that exchanging the X that #11 §7 builds cannot produce it, in the simplest (tree-level) calculation and, in one derivation, beyond it (`notes/gate-mechanism.md`, not reviewed). Flag 55, rated high, proposes tagging the gate and its momentum width [P], its mechanism [O], and tree-level exchange of X as that mechanism excluded [D][V]. In the note's words, "the gate itself is not refuted. What fails is the claim that it has been derived." Paul deferred the ruling on 2026-10-28, so #11 still states the derivation ([what flag 55 proposes](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism#what-flag-55-proposes)).
- **Flagged errors.** Flag 55 is one of 58 numbered suspected errors in the source documents listed in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`, and none has been ruled on. Independent agents told to refute flags 1–42 confirmed 32, found 8 defensible under a convention or reading, left 2 unclear because they rest on model memory, and refuted none; the later flags, except flag 44, carry audits of their own. The file ranks the consequential flags first and calls the rest wording, pointers, dates and references ([pending rulings](/wiki/status/open-problems#pending-rulings)).
- **Who has checked it.** Five rounds of blind review, every referee an AI (Claude models); rounds 3–5 record as a limitation that the referee is of "the same model family as the author", which, in the round-3 pre-registration's words, "does not remove correlated blind spots". Rounds 1–3 graded the dark-matter manuscript #14, since withdrawn (B, then "B near B/C", then C); round 4 the toy-model note (B); round 5 the phase-space-diameter note (B from both referees). On the referees' scale B means "flawed but salvageable" and C "fundamentally flawed"; no round has given an A. The author, Claude, wrote the adjudications of rounds 3–5. No review by a human expert is recorded, and #11 itself, its predictions table, the companion paper #13 and the notes of 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-28 have never been reviewed ([blind review](/wiki/method/review)).
- **What a tag is worth.** "A verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes" (#11 §2). Much of the literature #11's structural results rest on, including the multi-graviton no-go and the bimetric theories behind X, is cited from memory (†), and #11 is "not for submission until the † audit (open item 10) completes" ([the literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery)). #11 puts its own estimate that each novel structural claim is right at "~50–80% pending expert review" ([claim tiers](/wiki/method/claim-tiers)).
- **What it is.** "A Lakatosian research program, not a theory" (root `CLAUDE.md`): in the philosopher Imre Lakatos's sense, a fixed [hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core) whose surrounding claims are revised as tests come in ([Lakatos's methodology](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology)). Paul (P.R.) supplies conjecture and direction and keeps a veto; Claude (Anthropic) supplies derivations, counterexamples, verification and drafting. Paul cannot independently verify most of the technical physics, so the provenance tags, the machine checks and the review forecasts written down in advance are his means of telling sound results from confabulation. Since 2026-10-19 he has delegated some decisions to Claude, among them retiring the [soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) and reading postulate P4 strictly ([the collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration)). #11 says the word "theory" waits on three open theoretical problems [O], OP1–OP3 ([the verdict](/wiki/program/assessment#the-verdict-and-what-theory-waits-on)). The list is #11's own; the record holds open items outside it, among them the early-universe stability of X's two-metric sector and two pieces #11 treats as settled but no document derives (flags 49 and 55).
- **Next.** The work queue holds the comparison with [partially massless gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity) (OP2) and a check of the literature cited from memory (OP10), then, optionally, a sixth review round of the toy-model note "only before any external use" ([the work queue](/wiki/status/open-problems#the-work-queue)). The external clock is DESI's Data Release 3, which [the handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) of 2026-10-19 expected in "late 2026", an unverified date.

## Is anything new?

Possibly, in a few theoretical results, among them that momentum space's geometry becomes a second graviton with mass, and an identity by which that graviton's interaction potential adds no vacuum energy in the aligned vacuum the program assumes, once it is tuned flat; the identity does not by itself address the vacuum energy of matter. None is established: no human expert has assessed them, and #11, itself never reviewed, puts its own estimate that each novel structural claim is right at "~50–80% pending expert review". Literature checks have repeatedly found claimed results already published. The referees' objection that nothing numerical in the dark-matter proposal depended on Born reciprocity ("the framework does no work") is, in the program's own assessment written before the toy models were reviewed, "only partly answered".

- **The program's own count.** #11 §15 counts one observation it says stands whatever the program's fate ([that when geometry is doubled around quantum mechanics' pairing of position with momentum, the graviton's partner is a second graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/symplectic-partner)), one identity awaiting a novelty verdict, one new experimental question (whether the shape of the knee tells particle from wave dark matter), one structural unification (choosing what counts as position and choosing what counts as electric charge as one operation), and a two-way question about what dark matter is. It is a self-assessment in a document never reviewed. Since 2026-10-28 whether the experimental question survives is open (flag 55), and #11 tags the identification behind the unification [D — structural; math T] and the unification itself a conjecture [O], whose formal version is [OP8](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure) ([what the program has produced](/wiki/program/assessment#what-the-program-has-produced)).
- **A concrete model.** Momentum-space geometry becomes a field X: a second graviton with mass that changes sign under reciprocity, governed by a reciprocity-symmetric branch of the published two-metric gravity of [Hassan and Rosen](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) [D + T] ([Born's answer](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer)). The literature behind it is cited from memory (†), its interaction potential was selected rather than derived (OP1), and the record gives its mass no value. Its early universe is open: two-metric cosmologies are unstable while the expansion rate exceeds the second graviton's mass, and in the program's branch, where the two metrics have equal Planck masses, the usual escape from such an instability is closed (`papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §8.2; `notes/closed-universe.md` §6, not reviewed; flag 53).
- **The vacuum-energy identity** [D][V]. In that branch, a flat aligned vacuum forces X's interaction potential to contribute zero vacuum energy. The zero needs one tuning of the model's constants: flatness is itself one condition on them (flag 6). The identity "does not by itself address matter-loop vacuum energy" (#11 §7.3), and after a literature search #11 downgraded its novelty to "requires detailed comparison" with the partially massless and exchange-symmetric two-metric literature, a comparison not yet made ([the comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison)).
- **Spontaneous breaking.** That a preferred choice of position breaks Born's symmetry is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's idea [26S]; #11 §3 counts as the program's addition reading the breaking as spontaneous, with the neutral metric η as its [order parameter](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter) ([the program's addition](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality#the-programs-addition)). The neutral metric marks which directions of phase space count as position; an order parameter takes a definite value once a symmetry breaks, as a magnet's magnetization does. The toy-model note puts its own contribution this way: "The idea has been stated before, but we found no explicit model of ρ itself being spontaneously broken", ρ being reciprocity. Two [toy models](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) give explicit field theories, for one position–momentum pair, in which reciprocity is a symmetry. In the coset model, and in the Grosse–Wulkenhaar model with a complex field, the [polarization](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations), the vacuum's choice of which variables count as position, is a [Goldstone angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle): it can turn at no cost in energy, so a definite polarization needs an added potential [D][V]. In the coset model that potential is put in by hand and the ordered phase is shown only with one field held fixed, and its referee wrote that the model "appears new as a field theory, but it is a simple coset"; in the complex Grosse–Wulkenhaar model the breaking does not survive quantization, by an analytic argument (flag 41). An unexamined lead bears on that claim of no explicit model: Lukierski, Meljanac, Mignemi and Pachoł (2023) break an algebra containing a continuous Born rotation spontaneously, in the Yang model.
- **"The framework does no work."** The round-3 referee found that the phase-space framework did no work in the dark-matter proposal #14, which "stands or falls on that literature, independently of Born reciprocity"; the round-3 adjudication reads every referee across the three rounds on #14 as converging on this objection. The toy models were built to answer it. The program's own assessment, made before their review, is that they answer it "only partly": ordinary matter's locality in position follows from the vacuum only through a coupling, postulate P4, that nothing forces ([the critique](/wiki/program/framework-does-no-work)).
- **Dark matter.** #11 attributes the velocity gate to exchange of X, and tags that mechanism derived and the gate's shape open ("[D — mechanism; shape [O]]"); flag 55, whose ruling Paul deferred, finds that the exchange cannot produce it and that no document writes the derivation down. Whether the program keeps the gate as a postulate, as the flag proposes, or rewrites dark matter's self-interaction as an ordinary force of range about 10 picometres is open [O]. The second option comes from one of the note's two reconstructions of the record's picture; it is ordinary short-range self-interacting dark matter, which "would remove the program-specific content of rows 1–3" (`notes/gate-mechanism.md` §3, §5, not reviewed). Both remaining [candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates), particles of [conjugate matter χ](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles), matter organized around momentum rather than position, and a [condensate of X](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate), are open [O].
- **Already in the literature.** The compatibility theorem and the idea of broken Born duality (both Freidel, Leigh and Minic); the viable, lower-mass version of the [soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) (published topological dark matter); the [self-dual length](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-length-obituary) √(l_P L) ≈ 50 μm, excluded as a program scale; τ's cosine potential, which the lab notebook and #11 leave uncited and flag 54 identifies as the [pseudo-Nambu–Goldstone](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/axion-like-fields) potential of Frieman, Hill, Stebbins and Waga (1995) [S]; the recollapse of a closed universe with oscillating quintessence, on which row 5 now rests (Barrow 1987 †; Tipler et al. [S]; flag 50); #13's maximum force (Low 2006 [S], a lead not queued); and the [phase-space diameter relation](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter) with its one-cell form a·ã = 1, which hold in standard cosmology and so cannot test the program. After round 5 the author recorded that it still underestimates "the extent of prior art". Ideas found only in the original chat, such as momentum-space black holes, are [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures), not results.

#11 Appendix A records the fates of the program's eleven founding ideas, Paul's original suppositions ([founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions)). Eight are alive, most in changed form: a symmetric treatment of position and momentum, now the hard core; a coexisting conjugate universe, now a second description of one state; dark matter as conjugate matter, with its gate under flag 55; the conjugate universe shrinking as ours expands, a·ã = 1, a relation that also holds in standard cosmology; our expansion reversing at the [conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce), which with τ's potential as written needs a closed universe; the two descriptions synchronized rather than causally linked; momentum space with its own gravity, now X; and a [magnetic mirror family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family) for every charged particle, an idea already in the literature [T], cited from memory (†). Two are dead: [dark matter clumping in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image), dead as dynamics (required nowhere, though not shown impossible; a loophole for individual topological objects is untested and has no candidate), and [the conjugate universe's collapse powering our acceleration](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion), which died twice: its first form needed w = −2, which the data exclude, and the mechanism disappeared once the rule translating the conjugate universe's contents into effects on ours, the [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary), was derived properly. One is split: monopoles living in conjugate space are [dead as the reason none has been found](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation) and alive as objects that would gravitate.

## What the data say

No observation in the record favours the program in particular: the weakening of dark energy it requires is shared with other models of changing dark energy, and current data pair it with a phantom phase the program forbids. Elsewhere it agrees with data largely by design, so the agreement does not tell it apart from standard physics, and laboratory gravity tests limit the range of its new force.

- **Against: dark energy.** DESI DR2 favours, in every dataset combination, "a phantom crossing to w < −1 above redshifts z ≃ 0.4" [21S], so the program's bound "stands against the direct reading of the data" (#11 §10). #11 says it survives on two points: non-phantom fits remain compatible (at ~2σ), and w(z) is inferred from distances rather than observed. Flag 43, awaiting Paul's ruling, locates the conflict. Written as the straight-line Chevallier–Polarski–Linder (CPL) form, w(a) = w₀ + wₐ(1 − a), with a the size of the universe relative to today, the program's [thawing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/thawing-and-freezing) corridor, in which w starts near −1 and rises late, falls in the same region of the w₀–wₐ plane as DR2's fits; straight-line fits to τ fields that never fall below −1 reach that region too, so the region alone does not separate them. What separates them is where each straight line crosses w = −1: at z = 2 for the corridor's line, against z ≈ 0.35–0.50 for DR2's fits. τ itself never crosses. The flag's rough Gaussian approximation puts the corridor line outside DR2's 95% region for two dataset combinations, both including cosmic-microwave-background data ([the corridor and DR2's crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/cpl-parametrization#the-corridor-and-dr2s-crossing)). Two leads not queued, Shlivko and Steinhardt (2024) and Hur et al. (2025), bear on reading DESI's fits against w ≥ −1.
- **Shared, not support: the weakening.** Dark energy weakening today is the one departure from a cosmological constant in the program's direction, but continued thawing is "shared with other dynamical-DE models" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), other models of dark energy that changes over time, and DR2's favoured fit pairs it with the phantom phase the program forbids. Earlier readings of DESI as support for the program are [superseded](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing#superseded-the-earlier-readings-of-desi).
- **Against, weakly: a closed universe.** With τ's potential as written, row 5's turnaround needs space to be closed, which nothing in the program derives (flag 27). In the program's own model class the data disfavour closure at about 2σ (a probability of closure of 0.017–0.020). The estimate is a fit [V] made by one AI agent, the note's critic, and not independently verified, using DESI DR2's distances and a compressed summary of the cosmic microwave background, with no supernova data. The note has not been reviewed, and it says a full fit, not yet made, "decides the premise" ([what the data say](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe#what-the-data-say)).
- **Consistent, largely by design.** Under postulate P4 ordinary matter keeps the Standard Model and dark matter couples to gravity as ordinary matter does, so dark matter falls and lenses as ordinary matter does, and the [Bullet Cluster](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/bullet-cluster)'s offset between lensing mass and gas follows (#11 §8: "Bullet-Cluster-safe"). Two agreements are not by design. Ordinary matter also feels X at gravitational strength and "is safe only because X's force is cut off beyond ħ/(M_X c)" (`papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §8.2), so agreement with short-range tests of gravity is the bound below; and the cluster's limit on self-scattering is met, for particle χ, through row 1's zero cluster signal, a prediction whose gate is under flag 55. The long-range graviton is exactly massless, so gravitational-wave bounds on its mass are met for any parameters; no magnetic monopole has been found, an absence #11 credits to inflation [T]; and gamma-ray-burst timing rules out a speed of light that changes linearly with photon energy at the Planck scale [T], as general relativity also expects, though the record cites no paper for this bound. None of these agreements distinguishes the program from standard physics.
- **Bounds.** Torsion-balance tests of gravity at short range put X's range below 37.7 μm, at 95% confidence, in the program's aligned vacuum (background ratio u = 1), so M_X c² > 5.2 meV [V][S]. Both round-5 referees found the bound's coupling formula missing that background ratio; the author's adjudication argued that the program's vacuum has u = 1, and version 1.1 of the note, not reviewed since, states the premise. The bound assumes X adds the only force of this short-range (Yukawa) kind, and whether the aligned vacuum persists as the universe expands is open [O] ([the range bound](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound)).
- **Untested.** The record holds no measurement of the self-scattering knee that rows 1–3 predict ([the data situation](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee#the-data-situation)). The cores of dwarf galaxies enter only as the input that sets the scattering strength, an uncited σ₀/m_χ ≈ 30 cm²/g, and how χ could reach that strength is itself open: #11 calls the "dual-stress enhancement" its "Main open quantitative item" [O] (OP5). No direct-detection result is cited for row 7.
- **Killed by data.** [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a), the wave-dark-matter version in which condensation in velocity was mandatory, would have drained the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies of their dark matter; they are observed to be dense and long-lived, Segue 1 among them.

## What it predicts

The program's predictions are the eleven rows of #11 §13 ([predictions and kill conditions](/wiki/predictions)). No measurement in the record has decided any of them, the table has never been reviewed, and the rows are not machine-checked. #11 names three as testable soon: the knee in dark matter's self-scattering (row 1), dark energy that never goes phantom (row 4) and no direct detection of dark matter (row 7). It calls only rows 3 and 11 "not previously posed", and both are diagnostics: they would tell the program's dark-matter options apart, but no result in them could refute the program. Rows 1–3 carry the program's own dark-matter signature only while it keeps the velocity gate, which flag 55 proposes to keep as a postulate [P]; whether row 3 survives is open either way. Rows 6 and 9 require what general relativity already expects, row 8's null already has the standard explanation, row 7 restates postulate P4, and row 4's thawing is shared with other models of changing dark energy. The program fixes no [Born length](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-length) and gives X's mass no value, so its newest numbers are bounds, not predictions.

## What would falsify it

Rows 1, 2, 4 and 6–9 of #11's table each name a result that would end their claim; rows 3, 5, 10 and 11 name none (below). The nearest test is dark energy in DESI's Data Release 3. Rows 6 and 9 require what general relativity already expects, so a result against them would contradict general relativity as well. No document names an observation that would refute the hard core apart from the rest.

- **Dark energy (row 4).** If DR3 or later data settle on a constant w = −1, the dark-energy sector τ dies; "constant" is not quantified, and the thawing track itself had w ≈ −1 in the past. A robust intrinsic w < −1, one that belongs to dark energy itself rather than to an assumed expansion history, "kills outright". #11 sets that against "kills τ-sector", which reads as more than the dark-energy sector, and the handoff spells it out: "kills the framework outright". The [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s prose and the withdrawn #14 confine the kill to the sector; #11 governs, and no document says why the kill reaches beyond the sector ([how much a violation kills](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound#how-much-a-violation-kills)). DR2 already favours a crossing below −1.
- **The knee (row 1).** Robust self-interaction at speeds far above v\*, or halo data that exclude a knee in the 150–400 km/s band. "Robust" is not quantified, and the gate behind the row waits on flag 55.
- **Direct detection (row 7).** Any confirmed non-gravitational coupling between dark matter and ordinary matter.
- **Rows 2, 6, 8 and 9.** Halo shapes that demand forward-peaked scattering; a nonzero mass attributed to the ordinary graviton; a magnetic monopole of our electromagnetism; a speed of light confirmed to vary linearly with photon energy.
- **What cannot kill.** Rows 3 and 11 are diagnostics; row 5 is long-horizon, with row 4 as its near-term test; row 10's sizes are free, so data only bound them. Better short-range gravity tests tighten the bound on X's range rather than end the program, since X's mass is not predicted.
- **Already killed or withdrawn.** [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a), by the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies; [collapse-powered expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion), twice, its first form needing w = −2, which the data exclude; and the three predictions #11 withdrew "with cause": [velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity), [Fornax friction suppression](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction) and [superheavy-soliton dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), the last by its recomputed abundance.
