*This page answers six questions about the Comatter program, as its record stands on 2026-10-29. The program starts from [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity), the exchange of position and momentum that [the Fourier transform](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/fourier-transform) carries out. The exchange leaves the basic rules of quantum mechanics unchanged, but not the physics observed, in which interactions are local in position. The program takes reciprocity to be an exact symmetry of the laws of physics that the vacuum, the universe's lowest-energy state, does not share (a symmetry [broken spontaneously](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/spontaneous-symmetry-breaking), in physicists' terms), and follows the consequences into gravity, dark matter, dark energy and electric–magnetic duality. By its own assessment it is a research program, not a theory. Its [founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image), dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, is recorded dead. It has proposed three dark-matter candidates: two stand, and the third, [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles), was restored on 2026-10-29 as Paul's conjecture after a model of it built with ordinary kinematics had failed. That day Paul directed that a claim counts as dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs, and the program's checkpoint restored two more of his founding ideas as open conjectures. What stands is a set of postulates and theoretical results, most of them untested by observation. No observation in the record favours the program in particular: the weakening of dark energy it requires is shared with other models, and current data pair that weakening with a phase the program forbids. Its nearest decisive test is the next data release of the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi) (DESI).*

**Status:** Record — a summary of the status registers and of [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint), the program's current reference: its predictions, open problems and self-assessment (§13–§15), current to 2026-10-29: the notes of 2026-10-27 and 2026-10-28; the monopole discussion of 2026-10-29, in which Paul accepted flags 59 and 60 and flag 61 was applied under his instruction; and, later that day, Paul's direction that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs, with #11 v2.4, which restored three of his founding ideas as open conjectures, his ruling on flag 62, and flag 63, raised by the restoration's reviewers on prediction rows 7 and 11 and pending. ## Where the program stands

In its own assessment, a research program, not a theory. Its founding image is recorded dead, and Paul's concurrence in that death was asked on 2026-10-29. Of its three dark-matter candidates, two stand as open candidates, and the third, P.R.'s conjecture of magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space, was restored in #11 v2.4 that day; the one model of it, #14's, built with ordinary kinematics, failed in review round 3. What stands is postulates and theoretical results, most of them untested by observation. Its dark-energy sector is under real pressure from DESI's data, and v2.4 changes nothing there. A finding that #11's mechanism for the velocity gate in dark matter's self-scattering (rows 1–3), exchange of the second graviton X, is not derived, confirmed by two independent derivations in a note not yet reviewed, awaits Paul's ruling, which he deferred (flag 55). Its main document, checkpoint #11, has never been reviewed.

- **Paul's rule (2026-10-29).** A claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs, asked for each death separately and never inside a larger change, and a failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Method'). In his words: "Don't kill them off unless theory and data kill them off and you ask me explicitly that I concur. Do not hide the killing in a massive change." Deaths recorded before that date stand until he answers, and his concurrence was asked that day for each of them; the record holds no answer yet ([the concurrence questions](/wiki/status/open-problems#concurrence-questions-put-to-paul)).
- **Recorded dead.** The founding image, dark matter clumping in velocity: with the program's contact-range self-interaction, #11 §9 finds it cannot happen if dark matter is made of particles; it would be fatal if dark matter is a wave whose self-interaction also makes the cores of dwarf galaxies, since the faintest dwarfs would then lose their dark matter, which observation excludes; and it is "optional, never required" if that coupling is left free ([obituary](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)). A re-examination on 2026-10-28 found the obituary not premature: it "says clumping is required nowhere, not that it is impossible" (root `CLAUDE.md`; `notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md` §4, not reviewed). [#14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), dark matter made of very heavy magnetic monopoles of a hidden force, described with ordinary kinematics, failed after blind review round 3 graded its manuscript C, "fundamentally flawed": even at minimum production, solitons of its benchmark mass would make 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ times the observed amount of dark matter [V]. It is recorded dead, and Paul's concurrence was asked on 2026-10-29; until he answers, the death stands as recorded (#11 §12; root `CLAUDE.md`). Every recorded death is in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).
- **Three founding ideas restored (#11 v2.4).** On Paul's direction #11 v2.4 restored three of his founding ideas, "recorded dead or retired without his concurrence", as his open conjectures [O]; the root `CLAUDE.md` records that "None was killed by theory and data in general". The evidence against each stays in the record ([conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures#prs-conjectures-restored-in-11-v24)):
  - [Magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles) are the third dark-matter candidate again, the one v2.0 proposed: magnetically charged topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector, localized in momentum (#11 §12). #14's model of it failed by its recomputed abundance (above), but #14 "did not model the localization in momentum that v2.0 claimed", so the candidate itself has not been tested. Nothing shows yet that such objects exist or are localized in momentum: p-localization, "p-local solitons are p-localized", is open [O] (§14 item 8).
  - Their [explanation of why no magnetic monopole has been found](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation) is open. Two grounds were given against it, and #11 now finds that neither kills it. The duality that governs magnetic charge is electric–magnetic, not position–momentum, a ground only the shared-photon form of P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture contests, and that form needs postulate P4 restated; and the null result is expected under several standard histories, so it lends the explanation no support either way. The explanation's setting, that form, also needs conditions that would hide the objects, which §11.4 lists and which none of the documents has computed (#11 §11.1, §11.4). #11 recorded it dead from v2.0 to v2.3; flag 62 was ruled on 2026-10-29, option B, by Paul's direction.
  - [Conjugate collapse powering our accelerating expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion) is open. Two versions failed: the heuristic translation rule's w = −2, which the data exclude, and dual dust, which the corrected rule, the [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary), maps to dust, so that collapsing conjugate dust slows our expansion. The corrected dictionary keeps one route: "a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant". Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential, are open (#11 §10). The two failed versions are recorded dead, and Paul's concurrence was asked on 2026-10-29; until he answers, the deaths stand as recorded (#11 §10; root `CLAUDE.md`; [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard#failed-versions-of-a-restored-conjecture)). #11 recorded the idea itself "dead twice" until v2.3.

  P.R.'s core conjecture, that dark matter is conjugate (momentum) matter, was "alive throughout" (root `CLAUDE.md`), and since v2.4 two of the three candidates are conjugate matter: the χ particles and the monopoles localized in momentum ([founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions)).
- **Stands.** The hard core and the program's postulates [P]; a second graviton with mass, X [D + T], its early universe open; an identity by which X's interaction potential, the term that couples the program's two metrics and gives X its mass, adds no energy to empty space in a flat aligned vacuum, one in which the potential's two frames coincide, as in the vacuum the program assumes [D][V], whether it is new unaudited ([the vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity)); two toy models of the symmetry breaking [D][V], which show only that the structure is consistent; a laboratory bound on X's range in that aligned vacuum [V][S]; two dark-matter candidates, χ particles and a condensate of X, each open [O]; and the dark-energy field [τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence) [D + T]. In the register's words, "Standing is not the same as confirmed" ([claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive)).
- **Under pressure.** Dark energy's equation of state w, the ratio of its pressure to its density, says how its density changes as space expands: at w = −1, a cosmological constant, the density stays fixed; below −1, called phantom, it would grow. The program requires w never to fall below −1, and DESI's Data Release 2 (DR2) favours a phantom phase in the past, at [redshift](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift) above z ≈ 0.4 (higher redshift means further back in time). The root `CLAUDE.md`: "The dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3", DESI's Data Release 3 ([DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing)). And because τ's potential as written never goes negative (flag 27), the predicted reversal of the expansion (row 5 of #11's predictions table) needs a closed universe, which, in the program's own model class, the data disfavour at about 2σ, two standard deviations, in one agent's fit in a note not reviewed ([a closed Comatter universe](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe#what-the-data-say)).
- **Pending.** #11 says the [velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate), which lets dark-matter particles scatter off each other only when they move at nearly the same velocity, follows from exchanging the second graviton X. Two derivations by AI agents of the momentum-gravity investigation, the second done blind, find that exchanging the X that #11 §7 builds cannot produce it, in the simplest (tree-level) calculation and, in one derivation, beyond it (`notes/gate-mechanism.md`, not reviewed). Flag 55, rated high, proposes tagging the gate and its momentum width [P], its mechanism [O], and tree-level exchange of X as that mechanism excluded [D][V]. In the note's words, "the gate itself is not refuted. What fails is the claim that it has been derived." Paul deferred the ruling on 2026-10-28, so #11 still states the derivation ([what flag 55 proposes](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism#what-flag-55-proposes)).

- **An open conjecture.** Since 2026-10-29 #11 records P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture, open [O] (§11.4): momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged particle of ours having a magnetically charged analog in the momentum-polarized sector. The second version of the withdrawn soliton manuscript #14 stated a related identification, the momentum-polarized sector as the image of ours under [S-duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality), the exchange of electric and magnetic charge, and called it load-bearing for its dual coupling. ## Is anything new?

Possibly, in a few theoretical results, among them that momentum space's geometry becomes a second graviton with mass, and an identity by which that graviton's interaction potential adds no vacuum energy in the aligned vacuum the program assumes, once it is tuned flat; the identity does not by itself address the vacuum energy of matter. None is established: no human expert has assessed them, and #11, itself never reviewed, puts its own estimate that each novel structural claim is right at "~50–80% pending expert review". Literature checks have repeatedly found claimed results already published. The referees' objection that nothing numerical in the dark-matter proposal depended on Born reciprocity ("the framework does no work") is, in the program's own assessment written before the toy models were reviewed, "only partly answered".

- **The program's own count.** #11 §15 counts one observation it says stands whatever the program's fate ([that when geometry is doubled around quantum mechanics' pairing of position with momentum, the graviton's partner is a second graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/symplectic-partner)), one identity awaiting a novelty verdict, one new experimental question (whether the shape of the knee tells particle from wave dark matter), one structural unification (choosing what counts as position and choosing what counts as electric charge as one operation), and a three-way question about what dark matter is: "#14's superheavy-knot model of the third option was tested and failed, and the option itself is P.R.'s open conjecture". It is a self-assessment in a document never reviewed. From v2.1 until the amendment of 2026-10-29 (1c80d01) the passage read "a two-way question about dark matter's nature with named discriminators; a third option, superheavy knots, was tested and retired." Since 2026-10-28 whether the experimental question survives is open (flag 55), and #11 tags the identification behind the unification [D — structural; math T] and the unification itself a conjecture [O], whose formal version is [OP8](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/unified-modular-structure) ([what the program has produced](/wiki/program/assessment#what-the-program-has-produced)). Since v2.3 #11 also records P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture [O] (§11.4), what the unification would give if one vacuum event made both choices, and OP8 asks for it to be tested.
- **A concrete model.** Momentum-space geometry becomes a field X: a second graviton with mass that changes sign under reciprocity, governed by a reciprocity-symmetric branch of the published two-metric gravity of [Hassan and Rosen](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) [D + T] ([Born's answer](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer)). The literature behind it is cited from memory (†), its interaction potential was selected rather than derived (OP1), and the record gives its mass no value. Its early universe is open: two-metric cosmologies are unstable while the expansion rate exceeds the second graviton's mass, and in the program's branch, where the two metrics have equal Planck masses, the usual escape from such an instability is closed (`papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §8.2; `notes/closed-universe.md` §6, not reviewed; flag 53).
- **The vacuum-energy identity** [D][V]. In that branch, a flat aligned vacuum forces X's interaction potential to contribute zero vacuum energy. The zero needs one tuning of the model's constants: flatness is itself one condition on them (flag 6). The identity "does not by itself address matter-loop vacuum energy" (#11 §7.3), and after a literature search #11 downgraded its novelty to "requires detailed comparison" with the partially massless and exchange-symmetric two-metric literature, a comparison not yet made ([the comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison)).
- **Spontaneous breaking.** That a preferred choice of position breaks Born's symmetry is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's idea [26S]; #11 §3 counts as the program's addition reading the breaking as spontaneous, with the neutral metric η as its [order parameter](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter) ([the program's addition](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality#the-programs-addition)). The neutral metric marks which directions of phase space count as position; an order parameter takes a definite value once a symmetry breaks, as a magnet's magnetization does. The toy-model note puts its own contribution this way: "The idea has been stated before, but we found no explicit model of ρ itself being spontaneously broken", ρ being reciprocity. Two [toy models](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) give explicit field theories, for one position–momentum pair, in which reciprocity is a symmetry. In the coset model, and in the Grosse–Wulkenhaar model with a complex field, the [polarization](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations), the vacuum's choice of which variables count as position, is a [Goldstone angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle): it can turn at no cost in energy, so a definite polarization needs an added potential [D][V]. In the coset model that potential is put in by hand and the ordered phase is shown only with one field held fixed, and its referee wrote that the model "appears new as a field theory, but it is a simple coset"; in the complex Grosse–Wulkenhaar model the breaking does not survive quantization, by an analytic argument (flag 41). An unexamined lead bears on that claim of no explicit model: Lukierski, Meljanac, Mignemi and Pachoł (2023) break an algebra containing a continuous Born rotation spontaneously, in the Yang model.
- **"The framework does no work."** The round-3 referee found that the phase-space framework did no work in the dark-matter proposal #14, which "stands or falls on that literature, independently of Born reciprocity"; the round-3 adjudication reads every referee across the three rounds on #14 as converging on this objection. The toy models were built to answer it. The program's own assessment, made before their review, is that they answer it "only partly": ordinary matter's locality in position follows from the vacuum only through a coupling, postulate P4, that nothing forces ([the critique](/wiki/program/framework-does-no-work)). The objection was made against #14's model, which used ordinary kinematics; the candidate it modeled, restored in v2.4, has had no review.
- **Dark matter.** #11 attributes the velocity gate to exchange of X, and tags that mechanism derived and the gate's shape open ("[D — mechanism; shape [O]]"); flag 55, whose ruling Paul deferred, finds that the exchange cannot produce it and that no document writes the derivation down. ## What the data say

No observation in the record favours the program in particular: the weakening of dark energy it requires is shared with other models of changing dark energy, and current data pair it with a phantom phase the program forbids. Elsewhere it agrees with data largely by design, so the agreement does not tell it apart from standard physics, and laboratory gravity tests limit the range of its new force.

- **Against: dark energy.** DESI DR2 favours, in every dataset combination, "a phantom crossing to w < −1 above redshifts z ≃ 0.4" [21S], so the program's bound "stands against the direct reading of the data" (#11 §10). #11 says it survives on two points: non-phantom fits remain compatible (at ~2σ), and w(z) is inferred from distances rather than observed. Flag 43, awaiting Paul's ruling, locates the conflict. Written as the straight-line Chevallier–Polarski–Linder (CPL) form, w(a) = w₀ + wₐ(1 − a), with a the size of the universe relative to today, the program's [thawing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/thawing-and-freezing) corridor, in which w starts near −1 and rises late, falls in the same region of the w₀–wₐ plane as DR2's fits; straight-line fits to τ fields that never fall below −1 reach that region too, so the region alone does not separate them. What separates them is where each straight line crosses w = −1: at z = 2 for the corridor's line, against z ≈ 0.35–0.50 for DR2's fits. τ itself never crosses. The flag's rough Gaussian approximation puts the corridor line outside DR2's 95% region for two dataset combinations, both including cosmic-microwave-background data ([the corridor and DR2's crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/cpl-parametrization#the-corridor-and-dr2s-crossing)). Two leads not queued, Shlivko and Steinhardt (2024) and Hur et al. (2025), bear on reading DESI's fits against w ≥ −1.
- **Shared, not support: the weakening.** Dark energy weakening today is the one departure from a cosmological constant in the program's direction, but continued thawing is "shared with other dynamical-DE models" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), other models of dark energy that changes over time, and DR2's favoured fit pairs it with the phantom phase the program forbids. Earlier readings of DESI as support for the program are [superseded](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing#superseded-the-earlier-readings-of-desi).
- **Against, weakly: a closed universe.** With τ's potential as written, row 5's turnaround needs space to be closed, which nothing in the program derives (flag 27). In the program's own model class the data disfavour closure at about 2σ (a probability of closure of 0.017–0.020). The estimate is a fit [V] made by one AI agent, the note's critic, and not independently verified, using DESI DR2's distances and a compressed summary of the cosmic microwave background, with no supernova data. The note has not been reviewed, and it says a full fit, not yet made, "decides the premise" ([what the data say](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe#what-the-data-say)).
- **Consistent, largely by design.** Under postulate P4 ordinary matter keeps the Standard Model and dark matter couples to gravity as ordinary matter does, so dark matter falls and lenses as ordinary matter does, and the [Bullet Cluster](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/bullet-cluster)'s offset between lensing mass and gas follows (#11 §8: "Bullet-Cluster-safe"). Two agreements are not by design. Ordinary matter also feels X at gravitational strength and "is safe only because X's force is cut off beyond ħ/(M_X c)" (`papers/phase-space-diameter.md` §8.2), so agreement with short-range tests of gravity is the bound below; and the cluster's limit on self-scattering is met, for particle χ, through row 1's zero cluster signal, a prediction whose gate is under flag 55. The long-range graviton is exactly massless, so gravitational-wave bounds on its mass are met for any parameters; no magnetic monopole has been found, an absence that, by #11 §11.1 since v2.3, several standard histories expect, among them inflation after a unification transition and no grand unification at all, each [T], with further histories listed in flag 59 (†), so that it lends no support either way to P.R.'s explanation of it, open since v2.4 (v2.2 had given inflationary dilution as the account); and gamma-ray-burst timing rules out a speed of light that changes linearly with photon energy at the Planck scale [T], as general relativity also expects, though the record cites no paper for this bound. None of these agreements distinguishes the program from standard physics.
- **Bounds.** Torsion-balance tests of gravity at short range put X's range below 37.7 μm, at 95% confidence, in the program's aligned vacuum (background ratio u = 1), so M_X c² > 5.2 meV [V][S]. Both round-5 referees found the bound's coupling formula missing that background ratio; the author's adjudication argued that the program's vacuum has u = 1, and version 1.1 of the note, not reviewed since, states the premise. The bound assumes X adds the only force of this short-range (Yukawa) kind, and whether the aligned vacuum persists as the universe expands is open [O] ([the range bound](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound)).
- **Untested.** The record holds no measurement of the self-scattering knee that rows 1–3 predict ([the data situation](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee#the-data-situation)). The cores of dwarf galaxies enter only as the input that sets the scattering strength, an uncited σ₀/m_χ ≈ 30 cm²/g, and how χ could reach that strength is itself open: #11 calls the "dual-stress enhancement" its "Main open quantitative item" [O] (OP5). No direct-detection result is cited for row 7. No observation in the record bears on the restored third candidate, whose localization in momentum has not been modeled.
- **Against, by data.** [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a), the wave-dark-matter version in which condensation in velocity was mandatory, would have drained the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies of their dark matter; they are observed to be dense and long-lived, Segue 1 among them. It is recorded dead, with Paul's concurrence asked on 2026-10-29. The data also exclude w = −2, the dark energy that the first version of collapse-powered expansion required; that version failed and is recorded dead, with Paul's concurrence asked on 2026-10-29, and the idea is open through another route (#11 §10).

## What would falsify it

Rows 1, 2, 4 and 6–9 of #11's table each name a result that would end their claim; rows 3, 5, 10 and 11 name none (below). The nearest test is dark energy in DESI's Data Release 3. Rows 6 and 9 require what general relativity already expects, so a result against them would contradict general relativity as well. No document names an observation that would refute the hard core apart from the rest. Since 2026-10-29 a result against a claim does not by itself make the claim dead: theory and data must kill it, and Paul must concur.

- **Dark energy (row 4).** If DR3 or later data settle on a constant w = −1, the dark-energy sector τ dies; "constant" is not quantified, and the thawing track itself had w ≈ −1 in the past. A robust intrinsic w < −1, one that belongs to dark energy itself rather than to an assumed expansion history, "kills outright". #11 sets that against "kills τ-sector", which reads as more than the dark-energy sector, and the handoff spells it out: "kills the framework outright". The [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s prose and the withdrawn #14 confine the kill to the sector; #11 governs, and no document says why the kill reaches beyond the sector ([how much a violation kills](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound#how-much-a-violation-kills)). DR2 already favours a crossing below −1.
- **The knee (row 1).** Robust self-interaction at speeds far above v\*, or halo data that exclude a knee in the 150–400 km/s band. "Robust" is not quantified, and the gate behind the row waits on flag 55.
- **Direct detection (row 7).** Any confirmed non-gravitational coupling between dark matter and ordinary matter.
- **Rows 2, 6, 8 and 9.** Halo shapes that demand forward-peaked scattering; a nonzero mass attributed to the ordinary graviton; a magnetic monopole of our electromagnetism; a speed of light confirmed to vary linearly with photon energy.
- **What cannot kill.** Rows 3 and 11 are diagnostics; row 5 is long-horizon, with row 4 as its near-term test; row 10's sizes are free, so data only bound them. Better short-range gravity tests tighten the bound on X's range rather than end the program, since X's mass is not predicted.
- **Already killed or withdrawn.** [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a), by the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies; and the three predictions #11 withdrew "with cause": [velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity), [Fornax friction suppression](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction) and [#14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), the last by its recomputed abundance. Each stands as recorded, with Paul's concurrence asked on 2026-10-29. [Collapse-powered expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion), listed here until v2.4 as killed twice, is open again: its w = −2 version, which the data exclude, and its dual-dust version failed, and both are recorded dead, with his concurrence asked the same day (#11 §10).
