*The Comatter program takes the exchange of position and momentum, [Born reciprocity](phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md), to be an exact symmetry of the laws of physics that the vacuum breaks spontaneously, and follows the consequences into gravity, dark matter, dark energy and electric–magnetic duality. It is a research program, not a theory: its central ideas are conjectures, and its results are theoretical and mostly untested by observation.*

**Status:** Record, current to 2026-11-06 — a summary of [checkpoint #11](history/documents/checkpoint.md) and [the status registers](status/_index.md); it adds no results. [Tags](method/provenance-tags.md) are as the sources give them; [V] checks the arithmetic, not the physics.

## Where the program stands

A live research program in its own assessment; the word "theory" waits on [three open problems](program/assessment.md#the-verdict-and-what-theory-waits-on). No human expert has reviewed it. Its [blind reviews](method/review/_index.md), all by AI referees, graded other documents no higher than B, "flawed but salvageable"; [#11](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) has had none.

## Is anything new?

None of P.R.'s conjectures is derived. A [literature check](method/literature-check.md) finds the first published in its core, three in part; the two on monopoles were not found, one provisionally.

- **[Dark matter is conjugate matter](dark-matter/conjugate-matter/_index.md)**, organized around momentum, as [particles](dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles.md) with a velocity-gated self-force or monopoles, each open [O]. [Minic and coauthors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.00318) [proposed it first](dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-standard-model.md), in a doubled phase space, coupled beyond gravity in most papers; neither form was found.
- **[Magnetic monopoles are abundant in momentum space](dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md)** [O], a third candidate beside [particles](dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles.md) and [a condensate](dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate.md). Momentum localization is not yet modeled.
- **[That is why none has been found](electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md)** [O]: searches are made in position space. Against it: the duality that governs magnetic charge is electric–magnetic, not position–momentum, and standard histories also expect the null result. It needs P4 restated and a way to hide the objects, not yet computed.
- **[The magnetic mirror](electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md)** [O]: each electrically charged particle of ours has a magnetically charged analog in the momentum sector. The [mirror family](electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family.md) is established [T]; its placement in momentum was not found.
- **[Conjugate collapse drives our accelerating expansion](cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion.md)** [O], if the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated (open). Two versions failed: w = −2 ([pressure over energy density](cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence.md#dark-energy-and-the-equation-of-state)), excluded by data, and dual dust.
- **[Matter clumps in momentum space](dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md)** [O], the founding image. As collective dynamics it falls short by 10²⁸–10³¹ for particles, the faintest dwarf galaxies weigh against it where mandatory for waves, and elsewhere it is optional; a single topological object localized in momentum is untested.

Possibly new derived results, unassessed by experts: [a second graviton with mass](gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer.md) for momentum-space geometry [D + T]; [an identity](gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity.md) by which its potential adds no vacuum energy in a flat aligned vacuum, leaving matter's vacuum energy unaddressed [D][V]; and [toy models](symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md) of spontaneously broken reciprocity [D][V]. Literature checks have moved [others](program/assessment.md) to prior art.

## Core hypotheses still standing

[The hard core](program/hard-core.md): reciprocity is exact and spontaneously broken, with only the [symplectic form](phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form.md) rigid, formalized by [postulates P1–P5](program/postulates/_index.md). They are assumptions [P]: nothing in the record refutes them, and no document names an observation that would refute the core alone. A definite choice of position needs [more than the symplectic form](symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential.md): a potential not yet derived [O]. Of [P.R.'s eleven founding ideas](program/founding-suppositions.md), eight are alive in some form and three are open conjectures.

## What the data say

No observation in the record favours the program specifically.

- **Against:** the program requires dark energy's w ≥ −1, and [DESI](cosmology/dark-energy/desi.md)'s second release [favours w < −1](cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing.md) above [redshift](cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift.md) z ≈ 0.4 (larger z is earlier); non-phantom fits stay compatible at ~2σ. The dark-energy sector is under real pressure.
- **Against, weakly:** with τ's potential as written, the expansion's predicted reversal needs [a closed universe](cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md#what-the-data-say), disfavoured at about 2σ in the program's model class (one fit, not reviewed).
- **Against particle χ, mildly:** two halo fits prefer some self-interaction at cluster speeds ([row 1](predictions/self-interaction-knee.md#the-data-situation); flag 69).
- **Agrees, without telling it apart:** weakening dark energy; dark matter that falls and lenses like ordinary matter.
- **Limit:** short-range gravity tests put [X's range](gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound.md) below 37.7 μm in the aligned vacuum [V][S].

## What it predicts

[Eleven rows](predictions/_index.md), none yet decided by data. Testable soon: [a knee](predictions/self-interaction-knee.md) in dark matter's self-scattering near v\* ~ 10² km/s, [dark energy](predictions/dark-energy-drift.md) that never falls below w = −1, and [no direct detection](predictions/direct-detection-null.md) of dark matter. #11 derives the knee's gate from exchanging X; a later note, not reviewed, finds that exchange cannot produce it ([flag 55](dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#what-flag-55-proposes), pending).

## What would falsify it

- **Dark energy:** a settled constant w = −1 kills the dark-energy sector; a robust intrinsic w < −1 "[kills outright](cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound.md#how-much-a-violation-kills)" ([#11 §13](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions)), and current data lean that way.
- **The knee:** robust self-interaction far above v\*, or a knee excluded across 150–400 km/s.
- **Dark matter:** any confirmed non-gravitational coupling to ordinary matter.
- **Also:** the conditions of [rows 2, 6, 8 and 9](predictions/_index.md).
