*Row 7 predicts that experiments searching for dark matter through any force other than gravity will never see it. Direct-detection experiments look for dark-matter particles striking atomic nuclei in shielded detectors, and colliders look for dark matter produced in particle collisions; both need dark matter to couple to ordinary (Standard Model) matter through some interaction besides gravity [T]. In the program the two remaining dark-matter candidates, χ particles and an X-condensate, have no such coupling, and postulate [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter), taken strictly since 2026-10-19, lets no gauge force act across the two matter sectors, so the expected signal is zero, permanently. One confirmed non-gravitational coupling would kill the row, which is one of the three that #11 lists as the program's near-term falsification exposure.*

**Status:** Alive, strengthened by strict sector separation — it rests on postulate P4 [P] (#11 §4), taken strictly since v2.1 by round-3 Decision 1 — a postulate held by decision, not a derivation; not machine-checked.

## Key facts

- **The row** ([checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §13). Observable: "Direct detection / colliders". Standard expectation: "WIMP-type signal eventually", a weakly interacting massive particle (WIMP) being the classic candidate such experiments target. Requirement: "**Permanent null** (both dark-sector candidates SM-decoupled; P4 strict)". Killed by: "Any confirmed non-gravitational DM–SM coupling", that is, between dark matter (DM) and the Standard Model (SM).
- **P4** (#11 §4): "Matter comes in x-polarized (Standard Model) and p-polarized (χ) representations; both couple universally to H; gauge forces do not cross polarizations. [P; taken strictly since v2.1: no kinetic mixing between the sectors beyond what gravity induces, which vanishes if hidden charge conjugation is exact and otherwise first appears at six loops, ε ≲ 10⁻¹³ [27S].]" Kinetic mixing, of strength ε, is the established route by which a hidden sector's photon could couple to ours [T] ([kinetic mixing between sectors](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing)).
- **The decision** (`review/round3-adjudication.md`, Decision 1): "**Kinetic mixing: strict sector separation.** ε ≲ 10⁻¹³ from gravity alone, and zero if hidden charge conjugation is a gauged symmetry." It is "the program's own postulate P4 ("gauge forces do not cross polarizations") taken at face value", and it "costs nothing and closes three failure channels at once" of the retired soliton proposal ([Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)).
- **An unresolved reading.** The round-3 referee's audit notes report that Gherghetta et al. present 10⁻¹³ as a floor, the "minimum kinetic mixing in any theory with hidden gauge interactions and charge symmetry breaking", consistent with "<~" only in the sense "up to" (`review/round3/referee/audit-notes.md`, citation [9]). #11's "ε ≲ 10⁻¹³" stands, and the reading is unresolved.
- **The candidates** (#11 §12). [The χ-particle candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles) lists a "permanent direct-detection null" among its distinctive handles; [the X-condensate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate) is an "oscillating massive spin-2" field. v1's row read "χ (or X-condensate) has no non-gravitational SM coupling" (#9 §9), and v0.1's "χ has no non-gravitational SM coupling" (#1 §9), where rows 1, 3 and 7 were "the flagship discriminants" ([history of the table](/wiki/predictions/table-history)).
- **Exposure.** #11 §15: "**Falsification exposure:** genuine and near-term (rows 1, 4, 7)."
- **A conflicting popular claim.** The destination article (#12) says "The momentum-polarized sector is the magnetic side of electromagnetism". That conflicts with strict P4 and with this row, and it is dead ([Obituary: the magnetic identification (popsci-only)](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)).
