*Row 11 asks what dark matter is made of, if the program is right: [χ particles](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles), the momentum-polarized counterpart of ordinary matter with a gated self-interaction, or [an X-condensate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate), a coherent oscillation of the massive momentum graviton X filling space. #11 states that the two can be separated using rows 1–3 (the knee, the isotropy of scattering and the shape of the knee's tail) together with signatures of a coherent field. How to make that separation is itself open problem 9 of #11 §14, so the row is a diagnostic still to be worked out rather than a kill condition. #11 counts it among the program's predictions not previously posed.*

**Status:** Open — [O] (#11 §14 item 9); the two-candidate framework is [D] and each candidate [O] (#11 §12) — a diagnostic row with no kill condition and nothing yet to machine-check. Listed in [open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The row** ([checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §13). Observable: "Dark-sector microstructure". Standard expectation: "(not previously posed)". Requirement: "Particles vs condensate, separable via rows 1–3 + coherence signatures". Role: "Diagnostic".
- **The open problem** (#11 §14 item 9): "Discriminating the two remaining dark-sector candidates (§12) [O]."
- **The candidates** (#11 §12, "[D — framework; each candidate O]"):

| Candidate | Origin | Stability | Distinctive handle |
|---|---|---|---|
| χ particles (§8) | p-polarized matter field | assumed | knee at v\*, sharp tail; isotropic scattering; permanent direct-detection null |
| X-condensate | oscillating massive spin-2 (bigravity dark-matter lineage [23†]) | m_X-protected | unifies dark sector with geometry; coherent-field signatures; knee shape wave-like |

- **The discriminators.** [Row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee) is the knee, [row 2](/wiki/predictions/scattering-isotropy) the isotropy of scattering below the gate, and [row 3](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant) the shape of the knee's tail; [row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null)'s permanent null holds for both candidates. The X-condensate's lineage is [bigravity dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/bigravity-dark-matter).
- **Shared question.** "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis) applies to both" (#11 §12; [Open: polarization-genesis and the 5.4 : 1 ratio](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis)).
- **From three candidates to two.** `HANDOFF.md` §2 listed "Dark-sector trilemma discrimination: particles vs X-condensate vs solitons". Round 3 retired the soliton option on 2026-10-19 (Decision 2), and "the trilemma becomes two program-native candidates (gated χ particles; the X-condensate)" (`review/round3-adjudication.md`; [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). Both popular articles predate that and still describe a three-way question.
- **Standing.** #11 §15 counts "predictions not previously posed (rows 3, 11)" among the progressive signs, and its italic summary lists "a two-way question about dark matter's nature with named discriminators". The whole question is set out in [dark-sector candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates).
