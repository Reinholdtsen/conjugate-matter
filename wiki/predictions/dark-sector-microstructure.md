*Row 11 asks what dark matter is made of, if the program is right. The row names two answers: [χ particles](../dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles.md), the momentum-polarized counterpart of ordinary matter, which scatter off each other only through a [velocity gate](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md); or [an X-condensate](../dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate.md), a coherent, space-filling oscillation of the field [X](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), the massive partner of the graviton. [#11](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) states that the two can be told apart using rows 1–3 of its [predictions table](_index.md) (the knee in the self-interaction, the isotropy of scattering and the shape of the knee's tail) together with signatures of a coherent field. How to make that separation is itself open problem 9 of [#11 §14](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems), so the row is a diagnostic still to be worked out, with no kill condition. #11 counts it among the program's predictions not previously posed. The open problem counts a third answer, P.R.'s conjecture that the dark matter is [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md); a model of it with ordinary kinematics, in the withdrawn manuscript [#14](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md), [failed](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md), and that model did not treat localization in momentum, so the candidate itself has not been tested. The row's own wording names two.*

**Status:** Open — [O] ([#11 §14 item 9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems)); the candidate framework is [D] and each candidate [O] ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model)), three candidates, of which the row names two (flag 63, pending) — a diagnostic row with no kill condition and nothing yet to machine-check. Listed in [open problems and the queue](../status/open-problems.md#the-checkpoints-open-items-11-14).

## The row

[Checkpoint #11](../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions), row 11:

| Observable | Standard expectation | Program requirement | Killed by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dark-sector microstructure | (not previously posed) | Particles vs condensate, separable via rows 1–3 + coherence signatures | Diagnostic |

"Microstructure" is what dark matter consists of at the smallest scale on which it has structure: individual particles, or a field oscillating coherently. The open problem behind the row, [#11 §14 item 9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems): "Discriminating the three dark-sector candidates (§12) [O]." The row names two of the three. Flag 63 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, raised by both reviewers of #11's revision, proposes: "Particles vs condensate vs conjugate monopoles, the third untested; separable via rows 1–3 + coherence signatures".

## The candidates

[#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) lists the candidates with their discriminators, tagged "[D — framework; each candidate O]": the framework that produces them is derived, and each candidate is open ([dark-sector candidates](../dark-matter/candidates/_index.md)).

| Candidate | Origin | Stability | Distinctive handle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| χ particles (§8) | p-polarized matter field | assumed | knee at v\*, sharp tail; isotropic scattering; permanent direct-detection null |
| X-condensate | oscillating massive spin-2 (bigravity-DM lineage [23†]) | m_X-protected | unifies dark sector with geometry; coherent-field signatures; knee shape wave-like |
| Conjugate monopoles (P.R.'s conjecture) | magnetically charged topological solitons of the p-polarized sector, localized in momentum (§11, §11.4) | topological charge [O] | untested: p-localization open (§14 item 8); #14's model, with ordinary kinematics, failed (below) |

DM is dark matter. "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis) applies to all three" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model); [Open: polarization-genesis and the 5.4 : 1 ratio (OP7)](../dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis.md)).

- **χ particles** are the p-polarized matter of postulate [P4](../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md). They gravitate like ordinary matter and scatter off each other through a gate in relative momentum, mediated, in [#11 §8](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate), by [exchange of X](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md) (not derived, according to flag 55 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending; [the gate mechanism](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)), which makes their self-interaction strong at low collision speeds and absent at high ones ([conjugate matter and the velocity gate](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/_index.md)). Their stability is assumed.
- **The X-condensate** is a state of the [momentum graviton X](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), the massive, reciprocity-odd spin-2 field of the program's geometry, coupled to ordinary matter at "gravitational-strength" and "Yukawa-cut at 1/M_X" ([#11 §7.5](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#75-spectrum-d); [Yukawa forces](../gravity/spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces.md#the-momentum-graviton-x)). A field that oscillates uniformly through space behaves on average as pressureless matter [T], and the idea's lineage is [bigravity dark matter](../dark-matter/candidates/bigravity-dark-matter.md), Babichev et al. [23†], an entry of #11's reference list that pairs one paper's title with another's arXiv number (flag 20 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)). Its stability is "m_X-protected", stated without an argument ([the three handles](../dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate.md#the-three-handles)).
- **Conjugate monopoles** are P.R.'s conjecture: magnetically charged topological solitons of the p-polarized sector, abundant and localized in momentum, stable by topological charge [O] ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md)). Their entry gives no observational handle, only "untested: p-localization open (§14 item 8); #14's model, with ordinary kinematics, failed" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model); [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md)). The row's requirement does not name them.

## The standard expectation

"(not previously posed)" ([#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions)). [#11 §15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment) counts the row, with [row 3](island-discriminant.md), among its progressive signs: "predictions not previously posed (rows 3, 11)". The general distinction between particle dark matter and dark matter that behaves as a classical wave, because its [occupation numbers](../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number.md) are enormous, is established [T] ([wave dark matter](../dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/_index.md)); what is the program's own is the candidates the row names and the claim that rows 1–3 separate them ([Assessment: a live research program](../program/assessment.md#predictions-not-previously-posed-rows-3-and-11)).

## The proposed discriminators

### Rows 1–3

- **[Row 1: the knee](self-interaction-knee.md).** "Knee at v\* ~ 10² km/s; zero cluster signal (particle χ)" ([#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions)). The knee is the drop in the self-interaction cross-section at the gate's velocity scale v\*. Its zero cluster signal is stated for particle χ; two published fits mildly prefer a nonzero cross-section at cluster speeds, short of the row's kill condition (flag 69 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending).
- **[Row 2: isotropy](scattering-isotropy.md).** "Isotropic below the gate". The argument is a two-body scattering calculation on [the particle island](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem.md#the-particle-island), and the χ row of the candidate table lists "isotropic scattering". No document states isotropy, or its absence, for a condensate.
- **[Row 3: the island discriminant](island-discriminant.md).** "Knee tail: sharp ⇒ particle; (v\*/σ)⁴ ⇒ wave", with σ the halo's [velocity dispersion](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/velocity-dispersion.md#row-3-the-island-discriminant). The particle law is the gate's own cutoff; the wave law was derived in [the clumping problem](../history/main-problems.md) for very light χ, which behaves as a classical wave ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [the two laws](island-discriminant.md#the-two-laws)). The candidate table assigns a "sharp tail" to χ particles and a "knee shape wave-like" to the X-condensate.

Read against the table, the three rows separate the candidates through the knee: a sharp tail with isotropic scattering is the χ-particle signature, a wave-like tail the X-condensate's. Several links in that reading are missing from the record:

- **The condensate's knee is not derived.** The (v\*/σ)⁴ law belongs to wave χ, a regime of the matter field χ. No document derives a knee for a condensate of X, or relates the condensate to wave χ ([caveats and open questions](island-discriminant.md#caveats-and-open-questions)). A wave-like tail would therefore point away from particle χ without, on the record, separating the X-condensate from wave χ.
- **The particle tail may not be sharp.** Whether the gate's tail is Gaussian or rational is open problem 4 of [#11 §14](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems). [Calc note #6](../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md) calls rational gates "power-law-tailed" ([`notes/x-classification.md` §4](../../notes/x-classification.md#4-consequences-downstream-d--structural-with-one-honest-downgrade)), so if the gate is rational, both branches have power-law tails, and no source compares them ([Open: the gate shape (OP4)](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape.md)). [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) (2026-10-28, not reviewed) adds that under its reading R1 of the fiber overlap "the particle tail is a power law, the same functional class as the wave island's. Whether the discriminant survives is open" ([§5](../../notes/gate-mechanism.md#5-what-follows-for-the-record); flag 55, pending; [row 3's caveats](island-discriminant.md#caveats-and-open-questions)).
- **Rows 1 and 2 have no wave form.** [Calc note #2](../history/documents/clumping-notes.md) asked for rows 1–2 to be "re-derived in wave language" ([`notes/stream-stability.md`](../../notes/stream-stability.md), 'Consequences for the predictions table'); the (v\*/σ)⁴ law answers this for the knee's tail only ([Open: rows 1 and 2 in wave language](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate.md#open-rows-1-and-2-in-wave-language)).

### Coherence signatures

The second half of the requirement, "coherence signatures", matches the X-condensate's handle "coherent-field signatures". A coherent field oscillates in phase over a region, like a single standing wave, rather than as a gas of independent quanta [T]. No document says what the signatures are, at what scale they would appear, or how they would be observed ([the X-condensate candidate](../dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate.md#the-three-handles)).

### What does not discriminate

- **Direct detection.** [Row 7](direct-detection-null.md)'s permanent null is stated for two candidates, "both dark-sector candidates SM-decoupled" ([#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions)), SM being the Standard Model, and for those two it holds whichever is right; [§12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) counts three. Flag 63 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) (pending) notes that the third is decoupled in the separate-photon form of P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture, while in the shared-photon form, which needs P4 restated, it carries magnetic charge of our photon, so the null would rest on its being spread in position (flag 60).
- **Gravity.** χ particles and the X-condensate gravitate like ordinary dark matter, and by P4 "conjugate-sector solitons gravitate" ([#11 §11.1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#111-the-monopole-audit-revised-d)).
- **Abundance.** The 5.4 : 1 question applies to all three, and none has a mechanism that produces the ratio.

## Why it is open

Telling the candidates apart needs calculations the record does not contain. For the X-condensate, no document computes how the oscillation would be produced, its amplitude, its lifetime or its coherence signatures ([what is open](../dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate.md#what-is-open)). For χ particles, [the gate's shape](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape.md) and [the dual-stress enhancement](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress-enhancement.md) that would make their self-interaction large are open problems 4 and 5 of [#11 §14](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems). For conjugate monopoles, localization in momentum is open, "p-local solitons are p-localized" [O] ([#11 §14 item 8](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems)), and the kinematics of momentum-polarized matter has never been modeled (root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), 'Since the handoff'). The row records the question and the observables that would answer it; how the answer would be read off is open problem 9 ([open problems and the queue](../status/open-problems.md)). The current work queue in the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) does not include it.

## The data situation

The row has no data of its own. Its discriminators are rows 1–3, whose data situations are on their pages: the knee's band and the cluster limits on [row 1](self-interaction-knee.md#the-data-situation), isotropy on [row 2](scattering-isotropy.md#the-data-situation), the knee's tail on [row 3](island-discriminant.md#the-data-situation). No coherence signature has been specified, so none has been looked for.

## Superseded: v2.0's three-way row

This section records the question in v2.0's form, with superheavy knots and compact-object lensing in the row. Open problem 9 is three-way, the third candidate being P.R.'s conjecture in a form no model has tested (above), while the row keeps v2.1's two-way wording.

In v2.0 the question had three answers. #11 v2.0 listed a third candidate, superheavy [topological solitons](../symmetry-breaking/topological-defects.md#topological-and-non-topological-solitons-t) of a p-local gauge sector, and [the handoff](../history/documents/handoff.md) listed among the open items "Dark-sector trilemma discrimination: particles vs X-condensate vs solitons" ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen), item 8). As imported into the repository (commit 8d588f6), v2.0's row 11 required "Particles / condensate / knots separable via rows 1–3 + compact-object lensing + coherence signatures", and its open problem 9 read "Trilemma discrimination program (§12) [O]". The lensing clause matches the solitons' handle in v2.0's candidate table, "compact-object probes (lensing) if heavy".

[Round 3](../method/review/round-3/_index.md) of [blind review](../method/review/_index.md) found the abundance estimate of #14's model of the solitons [misnormalized](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md), and [Decision 2](../method/review/round-3/decisions.md) retired that model, which used ordinary kinematics, as a program-native proposal ([`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md), 'Decisions'):

> Consequence: the trilemma becomes two program-native candidates (gated χ particles; the X-condensate), plus the remark that the framework is compatible with known PeV-scale topological dark matter.

#11 v2.1 rewrote the row as "Particles vs condensate, separable via rows 1–3 + coherence signatures" and the open problem as "Discriminating the two remaining dark-sector candidates (§12) [O]" (commit e1c1f67). #14's model failed ([Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md)). The row keeps v2.1's wording; [#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) lists the third candidate as P.R.'s conjecture, untested because #14 used ordinary kinematics, and the open problem reads "Discriminating the three dark-sector candidates (§12) [O]".

Both popular articles predate v2.1 and describe three answers: [#12](../../popsci/universes-other-half.md) says the framework "narrows the answer to three candidates" ('Dark matter as the other polarization'), and [#10](../../popsci/angle-of-the-mirror.md) that "Dark matter's identity, meanwhile, has narrowed to a three-way question with named experimental discriminators" ('Corollaries and daydreams'). They are not sources for the question's status ([the popular articles](../history/documents/popular-articles.md)).
