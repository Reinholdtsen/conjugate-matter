*The predictions table is the Broken-Born program's list of observations that could kill it. Each row names an observable, what standard physics expects of it, what the program requires, and the result that would end the claim; [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) introduces its table with "A theory is its list of ways to die." The current table, #11 §13, has eleven rows: the self-interaction of dark matter (rows 1–3), dark energy and the fate of the expansion (4–5), the graviton (6), three permanent nulls (7–9), the early universe (10) and the nature of dark matter (11). Each row has a page here that states the test; the derivations behind the rows live in the sections on the physics they test.*

**Stands:** [row 1, the knee](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee), [row 2, isotropy](/wiki/predictions/scattering-isotropy), [row 3, the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant), [row 4, dark energy (under pressure)](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift), [row 5, the fate of the expansion](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion), [row 6, the massless graviton](/wiki/predictions/massless-graviton), [row 7, the direct-detection null](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null), [row 8, the monopole null](/wiki/predictions/monopole-null), [row 9, photon dispersion](/wiki/predictions/photon-dispersion), [row 10, the early step in G_eff](/wiki/predictions/early-universe-step) · **Died:** no page in this section; the three predictions withdrawn with cause are listed at the end of the Map · **Open:** [row 11, particles or condensate](/wiki/predictions/dark-sector-microstructure)

## Map

Every row page states a claim of the program. The established physics a test relies on (self-interacting dark matter, the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI), the phantom divide, graviton-mass bounds, monopole searches) has its own pages in the sector sections, linked from each row. #11 §15 names rows 1, 4 and 7 as the program's near-term falsification exposure, and rows 3 and 11 as predictions not previously posed.

[History of the predictions table](/wiki/predictions/table-history) comes first because the row numbers changed. It traces the table from the lab notebook's ten rows (v0.1) through the ten of v1 to the eleven of #11, with the two rows withdrawn after Debt 3, the softening of row 1 from a cliff to a knee, the details dropped along the way, and the lab notebook's per-sector parameter counts. Read it before citing a row number from an older document.

Rows 1–3 test the gated self-interaction of [conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter). [Row 1: the knee in σ_T(v)](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee) requires strong self-scattering in slow systems, none in clusters, and a knee at v\* ~ 10² km/s. [Row 2: isotropic scattering below the gate](/wiki/predictions/scattering-isotropy) requires collisions below the gate to scatter equally in all directions, unlike light-mediator models. [Row 3: the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant) reads the knee's tail to tell particle dark matter from wave dark matter; it came out of the calculation that killed velocity clumping.

Rows 4 and 5 test the dark-energy field τ of [cosmology](/wiki/cosmology). [Row 4: thawing dark energy, never phantom](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift) is the program's nearest trial, with DESI's Data Release 3 (DR3) as the decisive test; Data Release 2 (DR2) already favors a phantom crossing against it. [Row 5: the fate of the expansion](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion) identifies a future turnaround with the conjugate universe's bounce; it is long-horizon, and row 4 is its near-term proxy.

[Row 6: the exactly massless graviton](/wiki/predictions/massless-graviton) tests the spectrum of [gravity and the momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity): bounds on the graviton mass from gravitational waves apply to the reciprocity-even combination, which is exactly massless.

Rows 7–9 are permanent nulls. [Row 7: a permanent null in direct detection](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null) follows from strict sector separation (postulate P4). [Row 8: no monopoles of our sector](/wiki/predictions/monopole-null) keeps a null whose original rationale died, with Berry-curvature monopoles in crystals as the only laboratory analog. [Row 9: no linear photon dispersion](/wiki/predictions/photon-dispersion) rests on a symmetry argument that exists only in the lab notebook.

[Row 10: an early step in G_eff](/wiki/predictions/early-universe-step) records two optional early-universe imprints of the dual gravitational sector, bounded by free parameters rather than able to kill the program.

[Row 11: particles or condensate](/wiki/predictions/dark-sector-microstructure) comes last because it uses rows 1–3: telling the two remaining [dark-sector candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates) apart is open problem 9 of #11 §14.

## Boundaries

- The derivation behind each row lives in its sector: rows 1–3 in [conjugate matter and the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter) and [momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping); rows 4 and 5 in [dark energy](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy) and [scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality); row 6 in [the momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton); row 7 in [P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter); row 8 in [electric–magnetic duality and charge lattices](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality); row 9's residual symmetry ρ² in [the order parameter](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter), with #13's reading of the row in [conjugate special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity); row 10 in [scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality) and [early-universe cosmology](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe); row 11 in [dark-sector candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates).
- Withdrawn predictions keep their obituaries with the claims they tested, indexed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard). Open problem 9 is also listed in [open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).
- Predictions about the program's own work are not observational tests and live elsewhere: the pre-registered verdicts of blind review are in [pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/pre-registration), and the toy models' pre-registered predictions are in [success criteria S0–S5](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/success-criteria).
- What the table's state says about the program as a whole is in [Assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment).
