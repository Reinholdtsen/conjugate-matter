*Row 10 describes two possible imprints of the program's dual, momentum-side gravitational sector on the early universe. The first is a step in the effective strength of gravity, G_eff: the dual sector adds to the effective Planck mass a term that falls as the sixth inverse power of the cosmic scale factor, a⁻⁶, so it matters only at early times, before the light elements formed in Big Bang nucleosynthesis. The second is negative dark radiation: if the dual space is closed, its curvature appears in our expansion as radiation with a negative sign, which lowers the effective number of neutrino species, N_eff. The step is optional and the dark radiation conditional on a closed dual space, with sizes set by free parameters (knobs), so a null result bounds the knobs rather than killing the program; #11 names the cosmic microwave background (CMB) experiment CMB-S4 as the probe.*

**Status:** Alive, optional and knob-bounded — [D] (the lab notebook's minisuperspace calculation, #1 §6; #11 §10's dictionary is [D — corrected en route]) — not machine-checked; #11 calls the G_eff step optional.
