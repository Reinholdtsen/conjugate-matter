*Row 5 is the program's prediction for the far future of the cosmic expansion. With a constant dark energy, standard cosmology expands forever toward an empty de Sitter universe. In the program, the expansion halts and reverses once the potential energy V(τ) of the dark-energy field τ becomes negative; through the [cosmological constraint](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) a·ã = 1, which ties our scale factor a to the scale factor ã of the conjugate description, the same event is the conjugate universe's bounce, "our maximum is their minimum". The turnaround itself lies beyond any near-term observation, so #11 names [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift) as its near-term test; whether the potential as the documents write it can go negative is an unresolved point.*

**Status:** Alive, long horizon — the bounce identity [D], the supporting fits [T] — it follows from the constraint a·ã = 1 [D] (#11 §10) and is not machine-checked. Whether V = V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)] takes the negative values the turnaround needs is unresolved ([Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign)).

## Key facts

- **The row** ([checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §13). Observable: "Fate of expansion". Standard expectation: "Eternal de Sitter". Requirement: "Turnaround = conjugate bounce". Kill condition: "(Long horizon; near-term test is row 4)".
- **The identity** (#11 §10). "**Constraint [D]:** ω-rigidity fixes the comoving phase-space cell: a·ã = 1 (ℏ does not run) — the |Z| = 1 self-dual locus." "The turnaround is the conjugate bounce by constraint identity." The lab notebook puts it as: the same event, "read through aã = 1, is the conjugate frame's bounce: our maximum is their minimum" [D] (#1 §7 item 3). See [the turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce).
- **The mechanism** (#1 §7 item 3): "A cosine potential is negative over half its range." Once τ passes V = 0, "the effective vacuum energy is negative, expansion halts, and slow contraction begins." v1's row read "Turnaround after V(τ) < 0 = conjugate bounce" (#9 §9); #11's row drops "after V(τ) < 0".
- **The fits** [T]. #11 §10 cites "high posterior preference for a future anti-de Sitter transition and collapse in negative-minimum quintessence fits [19S, 20S, 21S]". The lab notebook quotes the figure: quintessence fits to the 2025 data of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) "with a potential possessing a negative minimum find a **93.8% preference for a future transition to anti-de Sitter space with eventual cosmological collapse**" (Gialamas et al. 2025, arXiv:2506.21542, #11's [19S]). The 93.8% figure appears only in #1.
- **Unresolved: the sign of V(τ).** #11 §10, #9 §8 and #1 §3 write the potential as V = V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)]. The turnaround needs V(τ) < 0, #1 §7 says the cosine potential is negative over half its range, and the fits cited use a potential with a negative minimum. No source settles whether the potential as written takes negative values; see [Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign) and [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence).
- **The over-constraint.** The turnaround time t_turn is one of three observables (w₀, wₐ, t_turn) fixed by two parameters (f, τᵢ) (#11 §10), with V₀ "fixed by Ω_DE" (#1 §7 item 4); see [the dark-energy over-constraint](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/over-constraint).
- **Founding supposition 7.** "Our expansion reverses at their bounce" is "**alive, corrected**" in #11 App. A: one event with two descriptions, live at DESI's Data Release 3 ([Founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions); [the conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe)).
