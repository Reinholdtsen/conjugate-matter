*The Broken-Born program is a speculative physics research program built on one symmetry of quantum mechanics. Position and momentum are related as a waveform and its spectrum, and quantum kinematics is unchanged when the two are exchanged; in 1938 Max Born asked why the rest of physics treats them so unequally. The program takes the exchange as an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum breaks spontaneously, as the aligned spins of a magnet break the rotational symmetry of the laws that govern them, and follows the consequences into gravity, dark matter, dark energy and electric–magnetic duality. Its founding image, dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, was killed by the program's own calculations; what stands is a chain of structural results, two dark-matter candidates and a dark-energy prediction under pressure from current data. In its own words it is a Lakatosian research program, not a theory.*

## The program in brief

The checkpoint's technical abstract (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md` §1, version 2.2), with the tags it carries:

- **The symmetry and its breaking.** Born reciprocity, ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x), is an exact symmetry of quantum kinematics, implemented unitarily by the Fourier transform (#11 §3). The program takes it as exact and spontaneously broken. Consistency forces only the symplectic form ω to be rigid, with the neutral metric η emergent as the order parameter: postulate [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime) [P], a correction forced by the [nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem) [D].
- **One geometric field.** The geometric sector organizes into a [modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field) Z = X + iY on the Siegel domain Sp(2d, ℝ)/U(d): Im Z is the spacetime metric, Re Z = X a second symmetric tensor, reciprocity acts as Z → −Z⁻¹, and the Lagrangian Grassmannian Λ(d), home of the dark-energy field τ, is the Shilov boundary.
- **A massive second graviton.** A chain of structural results — a [compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) [T][V], the [multi-graviton no-go](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go) [8†], a signature obstruction, and an explicit [tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test) [V] — forces X to be a [massive spin-2 field](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton) in the interchange-symmetric branch of [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) [10†, 11†], with the second frame confined to Λ(4).
- **Zero wedge vacuum energy.** A four-line [identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity) [V] shows that a flat aligned vacuum forces the wedge sector's vacuum energy to vanish exactly; the branch is adjacent to, and contains, the partially-massless parameter point [24S]. The identity does not by itself address matter-loop vacuum energy (#11 §7.3).
- **Dark matter.** [Conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter) survives as gated self-interacting dark matter whose gate is X-exchange; collective [momentum-space clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping) is proven non-mandatory everywhere and falsified where it would have been mandatory. A third candidate, superheavy topological solitons of a hidden gauge sector, was [retired](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) after an execution-verified review found its abundance estimate misnormalized, "leaving two candidates: particles and a condensate".
- **Charge lattices.** The electric–magnetic [charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice), with its Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger symplectic pairing [17†], is a second arena in which the same polarization choice recurs.
- **Dark energy, under pressure (v2.1).** τ is [hilltop quintessence](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence) with w ≥ −1 strictly. The Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument's Data Release 2 (DESI DR2) favors a [phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing) above z ≈ 0.4, so "the dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3" (Data Release 3; root `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Toy models (v2.2).** In an explicit ρ-symmetric coset model of (H, K), and in the complex self-dual Grosse–Wulkenhaar model, ρ sits inside a continuous U(1), so the polarization is a [Goldstone angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle) [D][V]. A definite polarization needs a ρ-preserving potential, whose derivation is [open](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential). The referees' standing demand for an explicit symmetric action is [met in their wording](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion).

Bracketed numbers such as [8†] are entries in #11's reference list, collected with the other bibliographies on [References](/wiki/references).

## Reading a page

- **Shape.** Every page opens with an italic passage for a reader without physics, then a **Status:** line (the status in words, the tags, and what verifies the claim or where it was decided), then the technical record and a list of sources.
- **Status.** Each page has one: `established` (literature), `alive` (a program claim that currently stands), `conjecture`, `open`, `dead` (killed, and stays dead), `superseded`, `method`, `record` or `hub` (a section index). Titles mark status: `Obituary: …` for a dead claim, `Open: … (OPn)` with the checkpoint's open-item number where it has one, `… (superseded)`, `… (chat-only)`, `… (popsci-only)`, and `Row n: …` for a prediction.
- **Provenance tags** (#11's header): **[D]** derived in this program; **[T]** established literature; **[P]** postulate; **[O]** open; **[V]** machine-verified. Citation marks: **[S]**, retrieved by live search during the project; **†**, cited from model memory, to be verified against the source before external use. Pages copy tags exactly from their sources and never upgrade one; an explanation of established physics written from general knowledge is [T], and any citation it adds is †. See [Provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags).
- **Variants in the sources.** `HANDOFF.md` marks machine checks with ✓; #13 qualifies its tags ("[D — structural]"); the toy-model note carries no tags, because it was written for blind review, and uses † for the Hermitian adjoint rather than as a citation mark.
- **What [V] means.** A script in `verify/` reproduces the arithmetic. It does not show that the physics is right: "a verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes" (#11 §2). The scripts run in `scripts/check.sh`; see [Verification](/wiki/method/verification).
- **When sources disagree.** The wiki follows a fixed precedence, most current first: the root `CLAUDE.md` ("Since the handoff"), then #11 v2.2 and the toy-model note v2, the review adjudications, #13, `HANDOFF.md`, the notes, and the popular articles last. The popular article #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and never decides a status. See [Status of the program's claims](/wiki/status) and [Conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).

## Boundaries

The wiki explains and indexes the program's documents — the papers, notes, review records and `verify/` scripts — and adds no results. Every number on a page is copied from a source document or a `verify/` output, and when the wiki and a source disagree, the source wins (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`). The documents themselves are described under [Documents](/wiki/history/documents); the check that keeps the wiki's links, sources and page shapes consistent is part of [the gate](/wiki/method/verification/the-gate).
