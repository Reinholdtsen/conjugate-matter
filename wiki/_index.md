*The Comatter program is a speculative physics research program built on one symmetry of quantum mechanics, [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity). Position and momentum are related as a waveform and its spectrum, and the basic rules of quantum mechanics, before any force law is specified (its kinematics), are unchanged when the two are exchanged, with one change of sign. In 1938 Max Born asked [why the rest of physics treats them so unequally](/wiki/program/borns-question). The program takes the exchange as an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum, the universe's lowest-energy state, [breaks spontaneously](/wiki/symmetry-breaking), as the aligned spins of a magnet break the rotational symmetry of the laws that govern them, and follows the consequences into [gravity](/wiki/gravity), [dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter), [dark energy](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy) and [electric–magnetic duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality). Its founding image, [dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image), was ruled out by the program's calculations, and a later review retired [one of its three dark-matter candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary). What stands is a chain of structural results on its geometry and gravity, the [two remaining dark-matter candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates) (particles and a condensate), and a [dark-energy prediction](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift) under pressure from current data. In its own words it is a [Lakatosian](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology) research program, not a theory: in the philosopher Imre Lakatos's sense, a body of work built on a fixed core assumption, whose surrounding claims are revised as tests come in, and judged by whether those revisions keep producing new predictions. Until 2026-10-26 it was called the Broken-Born program (BBT, for Broken-Born theory), the name its earlier documents use. The [executive summary](/wiki/summary) gives the program's standing on one page: its status, what may be new in it, which core hypotheses stand, what the data say, what it predicts and what would falsify it.*

## The program in brief

Checkpoint #11's technical abstract (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md` §1, version 2.3), point by point with the tags it carries, followed by three later additions the abstract leaves out: the pressure from current dark-energy data recorded in v2.1 (§10), the toy models of v2.2 (§4, §10, §14 item 11), and P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture and the polarization's defects, added in v2.3 (§11.4, §14 item 12).

- **The symmetry and its breaking.** Born reciprocity, ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x), is an exact symmetry of quantum kinematics, implemented unitarily by the [Fourier transform](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/fourier-transform) (#11 §3), a statement about flat space; on a closed space the exchange holds only locally ([reciprocity on a closed space](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/closed-space-reciprocity); flag 47 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). The program takes it as exact and spontaneously broken. Consistency forces only the [symplectic form](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form) ω, the antisymmetric pairing of position and momentum whose rigidity is the constancy of ℏ, to be rigid, with the [neutral metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures) η, the structure that splits [phase space](/wiki/phase-space) (positions and momenta taken together) into position and momentum, emergent as the [order parameter](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter): postulate P2′ [P], a correction forced by the [nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem) [D].
- **One geometric field.** The geometric sector organizes into a [modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field) Z = X + iY on the [Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain) Sp(2d, ℝ)/U(d), the space of all phase-space metrics compatible with ω: Im Z is the spacetime metric, Re Z = X a second symmetric tensor, reciprocity acts as Z → −Z⁻¹, and the [Lagrangian Grassmannian](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian) Λ(d), the space of all ways to split phase space into position and momentum and the home of the dark-energy field τ, is the domain's distinguished (Shilov) boundary.
- **A massive second graviton.** A chain of structural results — a [compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) [T][V], the [multi-graviton no-go](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go) [8†], a [signature obstruction](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/signature-obstruction), and an explicit [tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test) [V] — forces X to be a [massive spin-2 field](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), a second graviton with a mass, in the interchange-symmetric branch of [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) [10†, 11†], with the second frame confined to Λ(4).
- **Zero wedge vacuum energy.** A four-line [identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity) [V] shows that a flat aligned vacuum forces the wedge sector's vacuum energy to vanish exactly; the wedge sector is the frame-built interaction potential that gives X its mass. The branch is adjacent to, and contains, the [partially-massless](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity) parameter point [24S]. The identity does not by itself address matter-loop vacuum energy (#11 §7.3).
- **Dark matter.** [Conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter), the counterpart of ordinary matter organized around momentum, survives as gated self-interacting dark matter: its self-interaction acts only between particles moving at nearly the same velocity ([the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate)). #11 derives the gate from exchange of the momentum graviton X; a note of 2026-10-28 finds that exchanging X cannot produce it, and flag 55 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` proposes counting the gate a postulate and its mechanism open, pending Paul's ruling ([the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)). Collective [momentum-space clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping) is proven non-mandatory everywhere and falsified where it would have been mandatory; a long-range attraction gated in relative velocity, outside the record, would be decorative ([Conjecture: a long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/long-range-gated-attraction)). A third candidate, superheavy [topological solitons](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects#topological-and-non-topological-solitons-t) of a hidden gauge sector, was retired after an execution-verified review found its abundance estimate misnormalized, "leaving two candidates: particles and a condensate".
- **Charge lattices.** The electric–magnetic [charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice), with its [Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) symplectic pairing [17†], is a second arena in which the same polarization choice recurs: choosing which charges count as electric is the same mathematical operation as choosing which coordinates count as position.
- **Dark energy, under pressure (v2.1).** τ is [hilltop quintessence](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence), a dark-energy field whose energy density may fall as space expands but never rise: its equation of state w, the ratio of pressure to energy density, obeys w ≥ −1 strictly. The Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument's Data Release 2 (DESI DR2) favors a [phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing), with w below −1 further back than [redshift](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift) z ≈ 0.4, so "the dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3" (Data Release 3; root `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Toy models (v2.2).** In an explicit ρ-symmetric [coset model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model) of the phase-space metric H and the real structure K, and in the complex self-dual [Grosse–Wulkenhaar model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar), ρ sits inside a continuous U(1), so the polarization is a Goldstone angle [D][V] rather than a discrete choice. A definite polarization needs a ρ-preserving potential, whose derivation is [open](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential). The referees' standing demand for an explicit symmetric action is met in their wording. In #11's words, "None of this tests the cosmology; it only shows the structure is consistent" (§10).
- **The magnetic mirror and the polarization's defects (v2.3).** #11 §11.4 records P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture [O]: "momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged x-polarized particle having a magnetically charged p-polarized analog", x-polarized matter being ours, organized around position, and p-polarized matter organized around momentum ([Conjecture: the momentum sector as the magnetic mirror](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)). In its separate-photon form the analogs are magnetic under a photon of their own sector; in its shared-photon form they carry magnetic charge of our photon, which needs postulate [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) restated. The [explanation of the monopole null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation) stays dead on grounds v2.3 restates (§11.1; flag 59), and whether the shared-photon form would reopen it is P.R.'s ruling, pending (flag 62). §14 item 12 adds that the polarization manifold has π₁(Λ(4)) = ℤ and π₂(Λ(4)) = ℤ₂ [T], with the maps behind them gated in `verify/closed_universe.py` section F, for d = 4 and a global U(4) acting on the polarization; selecting the polarization can then leave strings of τ and, where the order parameter ranges over the whole manifold, point defects of the monopole type. Whether a transition produced them, and their fate, are open [O] ([OP12](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects#open-point-defects-of-the-polarization-op12)).

Bracketed numbers such as [8†] are entries in #11's reference list, collected with the other bibliographies on [References](/wiki/references). The entry behind [24S] has no authors, and its title is a description rather than the paper's title (flag 9 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`, confirmed by the flags' audit).

## Reading a page

- **Shape.** Every page opens with an italic passage for a reader without physics, then a **Status:** line (the status in words, the tags, and what verifies the claim or where it was decided), then the technical record and a list of sources.
- **Status.** Each page has one: `established` (literature), `alive` (a program claim that currently stands), `conjecture`, `open`, `dead` (killed, and stays dead), `superseded`, `method`, `record` or `hub` (a section index). Titles mark status: `Obituary: …` for a dead claim, `Open: … (OPn)` with the checkpoint's open-item number where it has one, `… (superseded)`, `… (chat-only)`, `… (popsci-only)`, and `Row n: …` for a prediction. A minor item of another status inside a page, such as an open sub-question on a standing claim, gets its own section headed `Open: …`, `Superseded: …`, `Chat-only: …`, `Popsci-only: …` or `Conjecture: …`, and the registers link that section.
- **Provenance tags** (#11's header): **[D]** derived in this program; **[T]** established literature; **[P]** postulate; **[O]** open; **[V]** machine-verified. Citation marks: **[S]**, retrieved by live search during the project; **†**, cited from model memory, to be verified against the source before external use. Pages copy tags exactly from their sources and never upgrade one; an explanation of established physics written from general knowledge is [T], and any citation it adds is †. See [Provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags).
- **Variants in the sources.** `HANDOFF.md` marks machine checks with ✓, and one of them, on the clumping shortfall, has no script behind it (flag 16 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`); #13 qualifies its tags ("[D — structural]"); the toy-model note carries no tags, because it was written for blind review, and uses † for the Hermitian adjoint rather than as a citation mark.
- **What [V] means.** A script in `verify/` reproduces the arithmetic. It does not show that the physics is right: "a verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes" (#11 §2). The scripts run in `scripts/check.sh`; see [Verification](/wiki/method/verification).
- **When sources disagree.** The wiki follows a fixed precedence, most current first: the root `CLAUDE.md` ("Since the handoff"), then #11 v2.3, the toy-model note v2 and the phase-space-diameter note v1.1, the review adjudications, #13, `HANDOFF.md`, the notes, and the popular articles last. The popular article #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and never decides a status. Disagreements that no source settles are stated, not resolved. See [Status of the program's claims](/wiki/status) and [Conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).
- **Suspected errors in the sources.** Where the wiki's verifiers believe a source states something wrong, the page quotes the source as written and points to a numbered flag in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` ("see flag 23"); no page corrects a source. The file holds 62 flags. Flags 1–43 were re-checked by independent agents told to refute them. Of flags 1–42, 32 were confirmed, 8 depend on a convention or a reading the documents support, 2 are unclear because they rest on model memory, and none was refuted; flag 43 was confirmed by two independent checks. Flag 44, a minor point of wording raised after the audit, is not yet audited. Flags 45–54 came from the closed-universe investigation of 2026-10-27, each checked by two fresh-context verifiers; flags 55–58 came from the momentum-gravity investigation, and flags 59–62 from the monopole discussion of 2026-10-29; their audit lines say who found and who checked each. On 2026-10-29 Paul accepted flags 59 and 60, and flag 61 was applied under his instruction to update the repository. The other flags still await his ruling, among them flag 62, which asks whether one form of P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture would reopen the dead monopole explanation, and on 2026-10-28 he deferred the ruling on flag 55. The [pending rulings](/wiki/status/open-problems#pending-rulings) list them with their verdicts and flag 44's audit status, with the four flags of the monopole discussion last. A flag that involves arithmetic has an executed check in `review/wiki-build-checks/`, which is not part of `verify/` or the gate; for flags 27 (addendum), 45–58 and 61 the arithmetic is in `verify/closed_universe.py`, `verify/gate_mechanism.py` and `verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py`, which the gate runs.

[Executive summary](/wiki/summary) — the program on one page, current to 2026-10-29: where it stands, whether anything in it is new, which core hypotheses still stand, what the data say, what it predicts and what would falsify it, each answer linked to the pages that hold the record. It comes first for a reader who wants the bottom line before the argument.
2. [The research program](/wiki/program) — the question Born asked, the hard core, the postulates P1–P5, the conjugate universe, the founding suppositions and their fates, the referees' main critique ("the framework does no work") and the program's self-assessment. It comes first among the sections because every later section follows from the postulates.
3. [Phase-space geometry](/wiki/phase-space) — the arena the postulates are written in: Born reciprocity and Born geometry as established physics, including the compatibility theorem, then the program's modular field Z. Its theorems are proven for positive-definite metrics, and their extension to the signature of spacetime is open.
4. [Spontaneous breaking of reciprocity](/wiki/symmetry-breaking) — the mechanism of the hard core: established symmetry-breaking theory, then the program's order parameter, the Goldstone angle, the open origin of the potential, matter locality, and the two toy models that realize the breaking; since #11 v2.3 it also holds the open question of the defects that selecting the polarization could leave (OP12).
5. [Gravity and the momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity) — established massless and massive spin-2 physics, then the results of [the field-equation problem](/wiki/history/main-problems), the program's name for its missing field equation for momentum-space geometry: why X must be massive, the tuning test of the potential that gives X its mass, and the palindromic branch with its vacuum-energy identity.
6. [Dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter) — established halo and wave-dark-matter physics, then conjugate matter and its velocity gate (the gate problem), the death of momentum clumping, and the candidates: two standing, one retired.
7. [Cosmology](/wiki/cosmology) — Friedmann cosmology, the constraint a·ã = 1 between our scale factor and the conjugate one, the duality dictionary, dark energy as the polarization angle τ under pressure from DESI DR2, and early-universe physics as background.
8. [Electric–magnetic duality and charge lattices](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality) — monopoles and dualities as established physics, then the program's monopole audit, the symplectic charge lattice, the conjecture of one modular structure for both polarizations, and P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture, whose shared-photon form the wiki recorded as dead until 2026-10-29.
9. [Conjugate special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity) — special relativity and the maximum-force literature as the established template, then the kinematics of the momentum polarization from #13: a second light cone, in time and momentum, whose limiting rate of change of momentum is a maximum force. It follows the checkpoint's sectors because it is a separate paper, which #11 v2.3 does not mention; #13 tags most of its results [D], and no review or script has checked them.
10. [Predictions and kill conditions](/wiki/predictions) — one page per row of #11 §13: the standard expectation, the framework's requirement and the result that would kill it, with the history of the table. The tests come after the sectors whose derivations they test.

## Boundaries

The wiki explains and indexes the program's documents — the papers, notes, review records and `verify/` scripts — and adds no results. Every number on a page is copied from a source document or a `verify/` output, and when the wiki and a source disagree, the source wins (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`). The documents themselves are described under [Documents](/wiki/history/documents); the check that keeps the wiki's links, sources and page shapes consistent is part of [the gate](/wiki/method/verification/the-gate).
