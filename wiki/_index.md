*The Comatter program is a speculative physics research program built on one symmetry of quantum mechanics, [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity). Position and momentum are related as a waveform and its spectrum, and the basic rules of quantum mechanics, before any force law is specified (its kinematics), are unchanged when the two are exchanged, with one change of sign. In 1938 Max Born asked [why the rest of physics treats them so unequally](/wiki/program/borns-question). The program takes the exchange as an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum, the universe's lowest-energy state, [breaks spontaneously](/wiki/symmetry-breaking), as the aligned spins of a magnet break the rotational symmetry of the laws that govern them, and follows the consequences into [gravity](/wiki/gravity), [dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter), [dark energy](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy) and [electric–magnetic duality](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality). Its central ideas are P.R.'s conjectures: that dark matter is [conjugate (momentum) matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter), that [magnetic monopoles are abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles), that [each charged particle has a magnetic analog there](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification), that [matter clumps in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image), and that [conjugate gravitational collapse drives our accelerating expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion). In its own words it is a [Lakatosian](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology) research program, not a theory: in the philosopher Imre Lakatos's sense, a body of work built on a fixed core assumption, whose surrounding claims are revised as tests come in, and judged by whether those revisions keep producing new predictions. Until 2026-10-26 it was called the Broken-Born program (BBT, for Broken-Born theory), the name its earlier documents use. The [executive summary](/wiki/summary) gives the program's standing on one page.*

## The program in brief

Checkpoint #11's account of the program (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`), point by point with the tags it carries.

- **The symmetry and its breaking.** Born reciprocity, ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x), is an exact symmetry of quantum kinematics, implemented unitarily by the [Fourier transform](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/fourier-transform) (#11 §3), a statement about flat space; on a closed space the exchange holds only locally ([reciprocity on a closed space](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/closed-space-reciprocity); flag 47 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). The program takes it as exact and spontaneously broken. Consistency forces only the [symplectic form](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form) ω, the antisymmetric pairing of position and momentum whose rigidity is the constancy of ℏ, to be rigid, with the [neutral metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures) η, the structure that splits [phase space](/wiki/phase-space) into position and momentum, emergent as the [order parameter](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter): postulate P2′ [P], a correction forced by the [nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem) [D].
- **One geometric field.** The geometric sector organizes into a [modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field) Z = X + iY on the [Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain) Sp(2d, ℝ)/U(d), the space of all phase-space metrics compatible with ω: Im Z is the spacetime metric, Re Z = X a second symmetric tensor, reciprocity acts as Z → −Z⁻¹, and the [Lagrangian Grassmannian](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian) Λ(d), the space of all ways to split phase space into position and momentum and the home of the dark-energy field τ, is the domain's distinguished (Shilov) boundary.
- **A massive second graviton.** A chain of structural results — a [compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) [T][V], the [multi-graviton no-go](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go) [8†], a [signature obstruction](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/signature-obstruction), and an explicit [tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test) [V] — forces X to be a [massive spin-2 field](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), a second graviton with a mass, in the interchange-symmetric branch of [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) [10†, 11†], with the second frame confined to Λ(4).
- **Zero wedge vacuum energy.** A four-line [identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity) [V] shows that a flat aligned vacuum forces the wedge sector's vacuum energy to vanish exactly; the wedge sector is the frame-built interaction potential that gives X its mass. The branch is adjacent to, and contains, the [partially-massless](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity) parameter point [24S]. The identity does not by itself address matter-loop vacuum energy (#11 §7.3).
- **Dark matter.** That dark matter is [conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter), the counterpart of ordinary matter organized around momentum, is P.R.'s core conjecture, as χ particles or as conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, two of the program's three candidates (#11 App. A item 4). The three are [χ particles](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles), whose self-interaction acts only between particles moving at nearly the same velocity ([the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate)); [a condensate of X](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate); and, as P.R.'s conjecture, [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles), topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector, whose localization in momentum is open (§14 item 8). #11 derives the gate from exchange of X; a note of 2026-10-28, not reviewed, finds that exchanging X cannot produce it ([the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate); flag 55). [Momentum-space clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping), P.R.'s founding image and open conjecture, as collective dynamics "is proven non-mandatory everywhere and falsified where it would have been mandatory" (§1).
- **Charge lattices and the magnetic mirror.** The electric–magnetic [charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice), with its [Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) symplectic pairing [17†], is a second arena in which the same polarization choice recurs. P.R.'s [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification) [O] (§11.4): "momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged x-polarized particle having a magnetically charged p-polarized analog". Its shared-photon form, in which the analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon, is the setting of P.R.'s [explanation of the monopole null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation); it needs postulate [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) restated and conditions that would hide the objects, none computed, and the null result is expected under several standard histories. Selecting the polarization can also leave strings of τ and point defects ([OP12](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects#open-point-defects-of-the-polarization-op12)).
- **Dark energy, under pressure.** τ is [hilltop quintessence](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence), a dark-energy field whose energy density may fall as space expands but never rise: its equation of state w, the ratio of pressure to energy density, obeys w ≥ −1 strictly. The Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument's Data Release 2 (DESI DR2) favors a [phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing), with w below −1 further back than [redshift](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift) z ≈ 0.4 (z measures the stretching of light by the expansion of the universe since the light was emitted; larger z means earlier), so "the dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3" (Data Release 3; root `CLAUDE.md`).
- **Conjugate collapse.** P.R.'s conjecture that [conjugate gravitational collapse powers our accelerating expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion) [O] rests on the dictionary's dual stiff ↦ Λ: a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant. Two versions failed, the heuristic w = −2, which the data exclude, and dual dust, which maps to dust (§10).
- **Toy models.** In an explicit ρ-symmetric [coset model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model) of the phase-space metric H and the real structure K, and in the complex self-dual [Grosse–Wulkenhaar model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar), ρ sits inside a continuous U(1), so the polarization is a Goldstone angle [D][V] rather than a discrete choice. A definite polarization needs a ρ-preserving potential, whose derivation is [open](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential). In #11's words, "None of this tests the cosmology; it only shows the structure is consistent" (§10).

Bracketed numbers such as [8†] are entries in #11's reference list, collected with the other bibliographies on [References](/wiki/references). The entry behind [24S] has no authors, and its title is a description rather than the paper's title (flag 9 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`).

## Reading a page

- **Shape.** Every page opens with an italic passage for a reader without physics. A page that is not a section index then has a **Status:** line (the status in words, the tags, and what verifies the claim), the technical record and a list of sources; a section index instead gives its section's status and a map.
- **Status.** Each page has one: `established` (literature), `alive` (a program claim that currently stands), `conjecture`, `open`, `under-review` (the calculations or data weigh against the claim, and its final status awaits review), `dead` (killed by theory and data), `superseded`, `method`, `record` or `hub` (a section index). Titles mark status: `Obituary: …`, `Under review: …`, `Open: … (OPn)` with the checkpoint's open-item number where it has one, `… (superseded)`, `Conjecture: …`, `… (chat-only)`, `… (popsci-only)`, and `Row n: …` for a prediction. A minor item of another status inside a page gets its own section headed `Open: …`, `Superseded: …`, `Chat-only: …`, `Popsci-only: …`, `Conjecture: …` or `Under review: …`, and the registers link that section.
- **Provenance tags** (#11's header): **[D]** derived in this program; **[T]** established literature; **[P]** postulate; **[O]** open; **[V]** machine-verified. Citation marks: **[S]**, retrieved by live search during the project; **†**, cited from model memory, to be verified against the source before external use. Pages copy tags exactly from their sources and never upgrade one; an explanation of established physics written from general knowledge is [T], and any citation it adds is †. See [Provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags).
- **Variants in the sources.** `HANDOFF.md` marks machine checks with ✓, and one of them, on the clumping shortfall, was unbacked when the wiki was built (flag 16 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`); #13 qualifies its tags ("[D — structural]"); the toy-model note carries no tags, because it was written for blind review, and uses † for the Hermitian adjoint rather than as a citation mark.
- **What [V] means.** A script in `verify/` reproduces the arithmetic. It does not show that the physics is right: "a verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes" (#11 §2). The scripts run in `scripts/check.sh`; see [Verification](/wiki/method/verification).
- **When sources disagree.** The wiki follows a fixed precedence, most current first: the root `CLAUDE.md` ("Since the handoff"), then #11, the toy-model note and the phase-space-diameter note, the review adjudications, #13, `HANDOFF.md`, the notes, and the popular articles last. The popular article #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed and never decides a status. See [Status of the program's claims](/wiki/status) and [Conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).
- **Suspected errors in the sources.** Where the wiki's verifiers believe a source states something wrong, the page quotes the source as written and points to a numbered flag in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` ("see flag 23"); no page corrects a source. The file holds 73 flags, each with its audit verdict where it has one; [pending rulings](/wiki/status/open-problems#pending-rulings) lists them and says which are resolved.

## Boundaries

The wiki explains and indexes the program's documents — the papers, notes, review records and `verify/` scripts — and adds no results. Every number on a page is copied from a source document or a `verify/` output, and when the wiki and a source disagree, the source wins (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`). The documents themselves are described under [Documents](/wiki/history/documents); the check that keeps the wiki's links, sources and page shapes consistent is part of [the gate](/wiki/method/verification/the-gate).
