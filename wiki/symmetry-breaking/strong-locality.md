*This open problem asks whether anything forces matter to be local in position, instead of leaving it as an input. In the toy model the vacuum's order parameter fixes a pair of candidate directions, and a suitable coupling makes matter local in whichever one the vacuum marks as position, but nothing in the model requires matter to use that coupling. Without such a link, the line a matter field lives on and the sign of the order parameter are two independent choices. In the toy the link is supplied by postulate P4, which places ordinary matter in the position-polarized representation and its mirror image in the momentum-polarized one; the toy therefore explains locality in position only as part of a package that includes a momentum-local mirror sector, and does not derive it.*

**Status:** Open — [O] (#11 §14 item 11: "locality of matter beyond the weak (Yukawa-like) form, which in the toy reduces to P4") — named among the toy work's open ends in the root `CLAUDE.md` ("the strong form of matter locality"); the worklog adds that it is "also open in the literature". Listed at [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The open item [O].** #11 §14 item 11: "Still open [O]: a Monte Carlo with H dynamical; a derivation of the potential; locality of matter beyond the weak (Yukawa-like) form, which in the toy reduces to P4." ([Acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion)). It qualifies the weak form at [Locality from an (H, K)-built coupling](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/matter-coupling).
- **Not achieved.** "Strong form (locality forced, not chosen): not achieved. It is also open in the literature." (worklog, chunk 4, 'S5 conclusion').
- **Two independent orders.** "(ii) Which member is 'position' is the spontaneously broken ρ, but matter's section is locked to K's sign only through the K-covariant coupling. Without that lock, matter's choice of section and K's sign are independent orders." (worklog; see [the section condition](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/section-condition)).
- **The lock is P4.** "The lock is P4 (x-polarized matter, and its ρ-image, p-polarized matter). So in the toy, the program's explanation of 'forces fade with distance' amounts to this: our matter is the x-polarized species. The explanatory gain is that x-locality comes packaged with a p-local mirror sector, the program's dark-matter claim. It does not derive x-locality from nothing." (worklog; [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter); [Conjugate matter and the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter)).
- **Where the pair comes from.** "(i) the pair {L+, L−} is the eigenline pair of K. It is spontaneously selected only in the continuous version (3+1, angle φ a Goldstone); with the τ-type potential it is fixed by K_ref." (worklog). The 3+1 case has not been computed, and the note covers only d = 1 ([the Goldstone angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle); [Open: the origin of the ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential)).
- **Not shown in the note.** "a mechanism forcing the (H, K)-coupling of matter;" and "anything beyond d = 1." (toy-model note §6).
- **The literature.** From the worklog's survey of how a section could be selected: "Open: a mechanism that picks L over L̃. Each candidate in the literature either selects the pair only, or uses outside input: which modes are light, distance-dependent interactions, or a curved position space." (chunk 4, 'S5 literature').
- **Bearing on the main critique.** "The round-3 critique ('the framework does no work') is only partly answered. The toy makes the program's structures cohere (η as order parameter; τ as the polarization angle), but matter's x-locality still enters through the P4-type coupling." (worklog, 'Assessment'; [The 'framework does no work' critique](/wiki/program/framework-does-no-work)).
