*The turnaround is a far-future event the program predicts: our universe stops expanding and begins to contract, which happens if the potential energy of the dark-energy field τ becomes negative. Because our scale factor and that of the conjugate universe are reciprocals, the moment our universe is largest is the moment its momentum-space description is smallest, so the same event, read in momentum variables, is a contracting universe reaching its minimum size and rebounding. The program states this as one event with two descriptions, not as two linked events. It is a long-horizon prediction whose near-term proxy is the dark-energy drift that survey data are testing now, and the potential becoming negative is itself an unresolved point in the sources.*

**Status:** Alive — [D] for the bounce identity, [T] for the cited fits (#1 §7 item 3) — it follows from [the cosmological constraint](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) by identity and is not machine-checked; long horizon ([row 5](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion)).

## Key facts

- **Statement.** #11 §10: "The turnaround is the conjugate bounce by constraint identity." The handoff: "eventual negative V ⇒ turnaround = conjugate bounce (one event, two descriptions)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2).
- **The identity.** #1 §7 item 3 [D]: the same event, "read through a ã = 1, is the conjugate frame's bounce: our maximum is their minimum." See [the conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe).
- **The trigger.** #1 §7 item 3: once τ's potential energy is negative, "the effective vacuum energy is negative, expansion halts, and slow contraction begins." The documents write τ's potential as V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)], which is never negative for V₀ > 0, and no document explains how V becomes negative: [Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign). The general physics of recollapse is on [quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence).
- **The fits [T].** #11 §10 cites "high posterior preference for a future anti-de Sitter transition and collapse in negative-minimum quintessence fits [19S, 20S, 21S]". The lab notebook quotes a figure from the first of these, a "93.8% preference for a future transition to anti-de Sitter space with eventual cosmological collapse" (Gialamas et al. 2025, arXiv:2506.21542, #11 ref [19S]); the figure appears only in #1 and has not been rechecked.
- **Row 5.** #11 §13: standard expectation "Eternal de Sitter"; requirement "Turnaround = conjugate bounce"; killed by "(Long horizon; near-term test is row 4)". See [row 5: the fate of the expansion](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion) and [row 4: thawing dark energy, never phantom](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift).
- **Founding supposition 7.** "Our expansion reverses at their bounce" is "**alive, corrected**" and "live at DESI DR3" (#11 App. A item 7), that is, at the third data release (Data Release 3) of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument. See [founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions).
- **Its timing.** The turnaround time t_turn is the third observable of [the dark-energy over-constraint](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/over-constraint). A chat-only conjecture says that the universe's position–momentum rectangle is square exactly at the bounce: [the phase-space diameter relation](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter).
