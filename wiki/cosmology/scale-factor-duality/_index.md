*Scale-factor duality, in this program, is the reciprocal relation [a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) between the scale factor a of our universe, which multiplies the distances between distant galaxies, and the scale factor ã of its momentum-space description, the [conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe): as one grows, the other shrinks by the same factor. The relation follows from holding Planck's constant fixed, and it is the cosmological case of a fact familiar from signal analysis, that stretching a waveform compresses its spectrum. Read through this relation, energy in the conjugate description acts on our expansion in a translated form, the far-future halt of our expansion is the same event as the conjugate universe's [bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce), and one founding idea of the program, P.R.'s open conjecture that the conjugate universe's collapse drives our acceleration, has failed in two versions and keeps one route through the derived translation. The same stretching keeps fixed the area of phase space that the universe's radiation occupies, a relation of standard cosmology for which a·ã = 1 is the statement about a single cell; and a box reaching from the Planck length to the horizon is mapped onto itself by reciprocity for one reference length of about 50 micrometres, [a length that fails when tested as a scale of the program's own](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-length-obituary).*

## Map

[Friedmann cosmology and minisuperspace](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/friedmann-cosmology) is established physics and comes first: the metric of a uniform expanding universe written with a lapse, gravity reduced to the scale factor and the lapse, the Friedmann equation as the constraint that varying the lapse produces, how each kind of fluid thins as the universe grows (the stiff fluid among them), and a summary of redshift. It ends with the program's extension, the lab notebook's dual gravity term, which adds an a⁻⁶ correction to the [effective Planck mass](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/planck-units#an-effective-planck-mass); no document derives that term from the program's geometric action (flag 49 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`).

[Redshift](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift) is the second page of established physics: 1 + z = a₀/a_e, the stretching of every wavelength by the ratio of scale factors, with the momentum of every free particle falling as 1/a. In wave terms, stretching a waveform compresses its spectrum by the same factor. The page derives the relation and its conditions, sets out what a region that expands with the universe conserves (its phase-space density, its photon number since electron–positron annihilation and its entropy since reheating), explains redshift as a clock and a distance, and lists the program's uses: z as the coordinate of the dark-energy data, in which the second data release of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) favours a crossing of w = −1 above z ≃ 0.4, with flag 43 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` on the crossing redshift; temperatures and gravitational-wave frequencies falling as 1/a in the early universe; and the popular article #12's reading of redshift as a contracting momentum spectrum, which the wiki takes as a gloss on a·ã = 1.

The next five pages are the program's own. Their results are derived in the [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) (#1 §6–§7) and restated in the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) (#11 §10); none of the derivations is machine-checked except the dictionary's algebra, which section G of [the literature-check script](/wiki/method/verification/literature-check-script#section-g-the-algebra-behind-flag-66) checks in the gate. The published literature holds parts of two of them: the dictionary, as a map of fluid variables, is the scale-factor duality of string cosmology with sources, and a contracting partner universe that drives our acceleration is published, though not as the momentum description of our own universe (flags 65 and 66 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`, pending; [the literature check](/wiki/method/literature-check)).

[The cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) is the program's central cosmological result [D]. Rigidity of the [symplectic form](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form) fixes the volume of a comoving phase-space cell, so the two scale factors are reciprocals; the derivation needs only ω, so it survived the change from the [original P2](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2) to [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime). The documents identify the relation with the |Z| = 1 locus of the [modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z), a structural reading (`review/wiki-build-flags.md` item 23, pending). The phase-space-diameter note reads the constraint as the one-cell form of the diameter relation and, for wave packets, as det H = 1 along the whole slice X = 0, with |Z| = 1 the set of square rectangles and Z = i the self-dual point. The page also sets the program's usage beside the scale-factor duality of string cosmology.

[The duality dictionary w_eff = −w̃](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary) follows from the constraint [D — corrected en route]; as a map of fluid variables it is the scale-factor duality of string cosmology with sources (flag 66, pending). Conjugate-side energy with equation of state w̃ acts on our expansion like energy with −w̃, because its energy is counted in the dual frame's shrinking volume before it is spread over ours. The page derives the rule step by step, explains each entry of the table, and records in a folded section the superseded heuristic −2 − w̃, which omitted that volume factor.

[Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion) sits beside the dictionary that tested it. It is P.R.'s founding supposition 9, an open conjecture [O]. Two versions failed: on the heuristic rule, collapsing conjugate matter acted on us like dark energy with w = −2, which the data exclude, and on the derived rule dual dust acts as ordinary dust, which decelerates the expansion. The derived rule keeps one route, dual stiff ↦ Λ: a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant. Whether it is, and how the route relates to τ's potential, are open.

[The turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce) applies the constraint to the far future: if τ's potential turns negative, our expansion halts and reverses, and since ã = 1/a our maximum is the conjugate description's minimum, one event with two descriptions [D]. Its premise conflicts with the potential as the documents write it, V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)], which is never negative for V₀ > 0; no document resolves the conflict (flag 27 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). The flag's addendum of 2026-10-27 adds that a closed universe turns around with that potential, so the turnaround needs k = +1. The phase-space-diameter note adds that the diameter relation's rectangle is at its most elongated at a turnaround, not square. Its test is [row 5](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion), a long-horizon row whose near-term proxy is [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift).

[Open: dual kinetic energy versus τ's potential](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/dual-kinetic-link) records a gap in the notebook's motivation for dark energy: the dictionary suggests conjugate-side kinetic energy as its source, the program implements it as the potential energy of the field τ in our own description, and no document derives one from the other. #11 §10 lists the same question, how the route of the collapse conjecture relates to τ's potential, as open.

The next four pages come from the phase-space-diameter note, `papers/phase-space-diameter.md` (version 1.1 of 2026-10-26 applied the round-5 revisions), which writes up a conjecture from the program's early chat sessions and asks how the universe's size in position is related to its size in momentum. Every number the note computes is checked by [the phase-space-diameter script](/wiki/method/verification/phase-space-diameter-script), and the note went through [round 5](/wiki/method/review/round-5) of blind review, verdict B from both referees.

[The phase-space diameter relation](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter) is the conjecture in its corrected form, and it is standard physics. For blackbody radiation filling a fixed comoving region, the region's diameter times the radiation's momentum spread is D_x·D_p = κ h N_γ^{1/3}, with κ = 1.708 and N_γ the photon count, an identity of blackbody statistics [D][V]; the expansion stretches the first factor and compresses the second, so the area stays fixed [T]. The constraint a·ã = 1 is the same statement for one cell, so the relation holds with or without reciprocity and cannot test the program. What changes is the rectangle's aspect ratio, which grows as a² and, in Planck units, measures the radiation's distance from the square shape that reciprocity leaves unchanged. The page quotes the original chat-only statement in a folded section.

[Under review: the square rectangle at the bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce) records the clause of the chat claim that the note withdraws. The rectangle, "square … exactly at the bounce" in the chat's summary, is square for the radiation only at about 10¹⁴ times the Planck temperature, before the radiation existed, and at its most elongated at the turnaround; the Hubble-sphere, two-region, squeezed-mode, string-cosmology and |Z| = 1 readings fail as well, and the claim is withdrawn [D][V].

[The self-dual cosmic box](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-cosmic-box) pairs a different set of sizes: the box from the Planck length l_P to a large length L in position, and from ħ/L to the Planck momentum in momentum. Reciprocity maps it onto itself for one Born length, λ = √(l_P L), 47–84 μm for L between the Hubble radius and the particle horizon. The length is in the literature (Cohen, Kaplan and Nelson 1999; Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman, Leigh and Minic 2023; and others), its closeness to the dark-energy scale is the Friedmann equation restated, and with λ fixed at √(l_P L_Λ), L_Λ being the de Sitter radius, a box that follows the Hubble radius approaches exact self-duality as the universe approaches empty de Sitter space.

[Under review: the self-dual length as a program scale](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-length-obituary) closes that group. The note finds that this length fails in both places it could enter the program: as the Born length of its dual special relativity it would give a maximum force of 1.4×10⁻¹⁷ N, which every atom exceeds, and as the range of the momentum graviton X in the program's aligned bimetric vacuum it is excluded by torsion balances. What stands is a bound: in that vacuum X's range is below 37.7 μm, M_X c² > 5.2 meV ([the laboratory bound on X's range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound)).

[A closed Comatter universe](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe) closes the section. It comes from the closed-universe note, `notes/closed-universe.md` (2026-10-27), on what follows if space is finite and positively curved; its numbers are checked by [the closed-universe script](/wiki/method/verification/closed-universe-script), and it has not been reviewed. Nothing in the program derives closure, but with τ's potential never negative only a closed universe turns around, so the turnaround of the conjugate-bounce page needs k = +1; data fitted with the program's own class of dark-energy models disfavour closure at about 2σ. If the universe is closed, the data give only lower bounds on its largest size; momentum space stays flat and only becomes discrete, finest at the turnaround, and a compact momentum space would be a new postulate. The page also records the topology of the polarization, for d = 4 and a global U(4) acting on it: two texture classes on the three-sphere [D], argued from π₃(Λ(4)) = ℤ₂, the third homotopy group of the space of polarizations Λ(4); strings of τ, which exist on the light Maslov circle too; and, where the order parameter ranges over the whole space of polarizations, point defects. Whether a transition produced them, and their fate, are [open](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects#open-point-defects-of-the-polarization-op12) (OP12), and section F of the script gates the maps behind the homotopy groups. The note's gravity-sector and reciprocity results have their own pages, [the momentum graviton in a closed universe](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/closed-universe) and [reciprocity on a closed space](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/closed-space-reciprocity).

## Boundaries

Dark energy itself — the field τ, its equation of state and the survey data that test it — is in [Dark energy](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy), including the open conflict over [the sign of V(τ)](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign). The conjugate universe as a concept, one physical state with two descriptions, is [the conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe), and the founding suppositions behind these pages are on [founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions). The modular geometry whose |Z| = 1 locus the documents identify with a·ã = 1 (an identification queried in flag 23 of `review/wiki-build-flags.md`) is [reciprocity as modular inversion](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion), and the wave-packet geometry behind the aspect ratio's distance from self-duality is [Gaussian wave packets on the Siegel half-plane](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/gaussian-states). The early-universe consequence of the conjugate gravity term, a step in the effective strength of gravity, is [row 10](/wiki/predictions/early-universe-step), with its background physics in [early-universe cosmology](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe), where the observed squeezing of [cosmological perturbations](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/squeezed-perturbations) also sits. The cosmological solutions of the full two-metric theory are [bimetric cosmology](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/bimetric-cosmology). The Born length, the first of the two places where the phase-space-diameter note tested the self-dual length, has its own page, [the Born length](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-length), and so does [the maximum force](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force) it sets; the bound on the momentum graviton that stands is [the laboratory bound on X's range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound), in [the momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton).
