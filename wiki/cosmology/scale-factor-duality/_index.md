*Scale-factor duality, in this program, is the reciprocal relation a·ã = 1 between the scale factor a of our universe, which multiplies the distances between distant galaxies, and the scale factor ã of its momentum-space description: as one grows, the other shrinks by the same factor. The relation follows from holding Planck's constant fixed, and it is the cosmological case of a fact familiar from signal analysis, that stretching a waveform compresses its spectrum. Read through this relation, energy in the conjugate description acts on our expansion in a translated form, the far-future halt of our expansion is the same event as the conjugate universe's bounce, and one early idea of the program, that the conjugate universe's collapse drives our acceleration, did not survive.*

## Map

[Friedmann cosmology and minisuperspace](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/friedmann-cosmology) is established physics and comes first: the metric of a uniform expanding universe written with a lapse, the Friedmann equation, how each kind of fluid thins as the universe grows, and redshift. The pages after it are the program's own. Its results are derived in the [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) (#1 §6–§7) and restated in the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) (#11 §10); the last two pages are an open gap and a conjecture from the chat.

[The cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint) is the program's central cosmological result [D]. Rigidity of the symplectic form fixes the volume of a phase-space cell, so the two scale factors are reciprocals; geometrically the relation is the |Z| = 1 locus of the modular field.

[The duality dictionary w_eff = −w̃](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary) follows from the constraint [D]: conjugate-side energy with equation of state w̃ acts on our expansion like energy with −w̃. It replaced an earlier heuristic rule, −2 − w̃.

[Obituary: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion) sits beside the dictionary that killed it. The heuristic rule gave w = −2, which is falsified, and the corrected dictionary then removed the mechanism, since conjugate dust acts on us as dust.

[The turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce) applies the constraint to the far future: if dark energy's potential turns negative, our expansion reverses, and by a·ã = 1 that event is the conjugate universe's bounce. Its test is [row 5](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion), a long-horizon row.

[Open: dual kinetic energy versus τ's potential](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/dual-kinetic-link) records a gap in the notebook's motivation for dark energy: the dictionary suggests conjugate-side kinetic energy as its source, the program implements it as the potential energy of the field τ, and no document derives one from the other.

[The phase-space diameter relation (chat-only)](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter) closes the section. It is a conjecture from the original chat, D_x·D_p = h·N^{1/3} per dimension, with no document or script behind it; a note on it is in the current work queue.
