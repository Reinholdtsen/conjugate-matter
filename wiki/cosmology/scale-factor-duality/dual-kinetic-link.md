*This open problem is a gap between two explanations of dark energy that stand side by side in the program's lab notebook. The first comes from the duality dictionary: kinetic energy in the conjugate, momentum-space description of the universe acts on our expansion like a cosmological constant, so the notebook names it the natural source of a near-constant dark energy. The second is the explanation the program uses: dark energy is the potential energy of the field τ, held near the top of its potential. No document derives the second from the first, so the step from the motivation to the identification of dark energy with τ is asserted rather than derived.*

**Status:** Open — untagged; not among #11 §14's open problems — the gap was found by reading #1 §6 and §7 side by side when the sources were inventoried for this wiki, and no document addresses it. Listed on [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The motivation.** #1 §6, after the dictionary table: "the natural source of a near-constant dark energy is **kinetic energy in the dual frame**". It rests on the table's entry "Kinetic/stiff energy (w̃ = +1)" ↦ "Cosmological constant (w = −1)" of [the duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary). The same paragraph then names τ, "the one the symmetry breaking already handed us", as the obvious candidate.
- **The implementation.** #1 §7: "Identify dark energy with the Maslov modulus τ sitting near the top of its cosine potential (hilltop axion quintessence)." A field near the top of its potential with w close to −1 carries its energy almost entirely as potential energy [T]; see [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence) and [quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence).
- **The gap.** The first statement concerns kinetic energy in the conjugate description (w̃ = +1); the second concerns potential energy in ours (w close to −1). No document derives either from the other.
- **In the current checkpoint.** #11 §10 keeps both ingredients as separate statements — "Dual stiff ↦ Λ" in the dictionary and "τ is hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle" under dark energy — and does not connect them.
- **Related open problems.** Where τ's potential comes from is open ([Open: the origin of the ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential)), and so is its sign ([Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign)).
