*This page records a dead claim: that the gravitational collapse of the conjugate universe, the momentum-space description of our own, is what drives the accelerating expansion of our universe. It was one of the program's founding suppositions. Written down with the heuristic translation rule of the early discussion, it required dark energy with equation of state w = −2, a value the data exclude. With the translation rule derived properly, collapsing conjugate matter acts on our expansion as ordinary matter does, slowing it rather than accelerating it, so the mechanism disappeared. The claim died twice and stays dead.*

**Status:** Dead, twice — untagged — killed first by data (w = −2 is falsified) and then by the corrected [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary) (`HANDOFF.md` §2; #11 App. A item 9); listed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).

## Key facts

- **What was claimed.** Founding supposition 9: "Their collapse is our acceleration" (#11 App. A item 9). The handoff lists it among the dead as "Conjugate collapse powers our expansion" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). See [founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions).
- **How it was tested first.** The development discussion translated conjugate-side fluids with the heuristic w_eff = −2 − w̃, "which identified densities across frames without the constraint's volume Jacobian" (#1 §6). On that rule, "first version gave w = −2 (falsified)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). A value below −1 is phantom dark energy; see [the phantom divide](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide).
- **Why it failed the second time.** The derived rule is w_eff = −w̃ (#1 §6; #11 §10), under which dust maps to dust: "corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). The [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) puts it as "the w=−2 pathology is gone because it was an artifact" (#1 §6), and announces the correction at its head: "Rigor changed an answer; that is the point of rigor" (#1 §0).
- **The verdict.** "**dead twice** (w = −2 falsified; corrected dictionary removed the mechanism)" (#11 App. A item 9). The first death was observational; the second came from the corrected calculation. The popular article #10 says that data "already executed two of the founding ideas"; #11 App. A item 9 attributes this claim's second death to the corrected dictionary, not to data ([the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
- **What survives.** The corrected dictionary itself, including its entry "Dual stiff ↦ Λ" (#11 §10). Founding supposition 6, "promoted to theorem" as [the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint). Founding supposition 7, "Our expansion reverses at their bounce", "**alive, corrected**" as [the turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce). The program's dark energy is now the field τ: [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence).
- **Where it is filed.** Its home section is [Scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality); the register of dead claims is [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard); how the program treats dead claims is [negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results).
