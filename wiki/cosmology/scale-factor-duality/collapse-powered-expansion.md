*Conjugate collapse powering our expansion is P.R.'s conjecture that the gravitational collapse of the [conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe), the momentum-space description of our own, is what drives the accelerating expansion of our universe. It is the ninth of the program's [founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions). Two versions of it have failed. Written with the heuristic translation rule of the early discussion, it required dark energy with equation of state w = −2, a value the data exclude; with the rule derived properly, the [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary), collapsing conjugate matter acts on our expansion as ordinary matter does, slowing it. The derived dictionary keeps one route: energy that is all kinetic on the conjugate side acts on our expansion as a [cosmological constant](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/cosmological-constant#dual-kinetic-energy-acts-as-a-λ), so a conjugate universe whose contents are dominated by kinetic energy as it contracts would act on ours as one. Whether it is, and how that route relates to the program's dark-energy field [τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence), are open. The record called the idea dead twice until 2026-10-29, when Paul directed that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs.*

## The conjecture

Founding supposition 9 of P.R.'s eleven, as #11's audit words it: "Their collapse is our acceleration" (#11 App. A item 9). "Their" refers to the [conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe). The program describes one universe twice, once in position variables and once in momentum variables, and the conjecture is that the contraction of the second description is the source of the acceleration of our expansion, which standard cosmology attributes to dark energy. The [popular article](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) #10 words it as "the conjugate universe's gravitational collapse is what powers our accelerating expansion" ('The killing fields'); the popular articles are never a source for a claim's status.

#11 v2.4 records it in §10, beside the dictionary:

> **Founding supposition 9 [O] (P.R.; v2.4).** "Their collapse is our acceleration" failed in two versions: the heuristic dictionary's w = −2, which the data exclude, and dual dust, which the corrected dictionary maps to dust. The corrected dictionary keeps a route: dual stiff ↦ Λ, so a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant, and the lab notebook already names "kinetic energy in the dual frame" as "the natural source of a near-constant dark energy" (#1 §6). Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential, are open. The two failed versions stay recorded dead until P.R. answers the concurrence question put to him on 2026-10-29.

Its audit gives the same fate in one line: "**open**, P.R.'s conjecture [O] (v2.4)" (App. A item 9).

## Where it comes from

- **The contraction is a theorem.** By [the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint), the conjugate scale factor ã shrinks exactly as ours grows, in the way a waveform's spectrum narrows by the same factor as the waveform is stretched. That is founding supposition 6, "Conjugate universe shrinks as ours expands", "**promoted to theorem**" (#11 App. A item 6). The conjecture concerns what the contraction does to our expansion, not whether it happens.
- **The translation is the dictionary.** How energy on the conjugate side acts on our expansion is set by the [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary): a dual-frame fluid with equation of state w̃ contributes w_eff = −w̃ (#11 §10, "[D — corrected en route]").
- **The idea is older than the documents.** It belongs to the development discussion on claude.ai that preceded the [lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook), and no document states it in more detail than the sentences quoted on this page.
- **Its return.** On 2026-10-29 Paul objected that ideas of his had been recorded dead prematurely, "I think you prematurely killed off the most interesting parts of the comatter conjecture", and named this one among them: "Also, conjugate gravitational collapse drives positional expansion acceleration." The root `CLAUDE.md` holds his words in full in the entry "2026-10-29, later the same day" ('Since the handoff'), which lists this idea among his founding ideas that are open conjectures again. #11 v2.4 returned it to open as his conjecture, with two other founding ideas "recorded dead or retired without his concurrence", conjugate monopoles abundant in momentum space and their explanation of the monopole null result (#11 header, v2.4 change note).

## Two versions that failed

### With the heuristic rule: w = −2

Turning the idea into a prediction needs a rule for how energy on the conjugate side acts on our expansion. The development discussion used a heuristic rule, w_eff = −2 − w̃, where w̃ is the equation of state of a fluid in the conjugate frame and w_eff the equation of state with which it acts on us. The lab notebook describes the heuristic as one "which identified densities across frames without the constraint's volume Jacobian" (#1 §6).

The collapsing content is matter, which cosmology models as dust, w̃ = 0 (the handoff's "dual dust"). On the heuristic rule it acts on our expansion like a fluid with w = −2 (the wiki's reading of the step: setting ρ_eff = ρ̃ with ã = 1/a gives w_eff = −2 − w̃, and w̃ = 0 gives −2; the derivation is on [the duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary#superseded-the-earlier-heuristic-rule)). The handoff's record: "first version gave w = −2 (falsified)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2).

An equation of state below −1 is phantom: an energy density that grows as the universe expands ([the phantom divide](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide)) [T]. A value as low as −2 is far outside what observations of the expansion allow [T]. The documents record the verdict as "falsified" (`HANDOFF.md` §2; #11 App. A item 9) and as a value "which the data exclude" (#11 §10), and they cite no particular measurement; the popular article #10 says the required dark-energy strength was one "the data ruled out decades ago" ('The killing fields').

### With the corrected rule: dual dust acts as dust

Deriving the dictionary with the constraint's volume Jacobian replaced the heuristic with w_eff = −w̃ (#1 §6; #11 §10, "[D — corrected en route]"). Under the derived rule:

```text
dual dust:   w̃ = 0   ⇒   w_eff = 0
```

Collapsing conjugate matter acts on our expansion as ordinary dust. Dust has positive energy density and no pressure, so in the acceleration equation of [Friedmann cosmology](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/friedmann-cosmology) it decelerates the expansion [T]. In this version the conjugate side's collapse cannot be the source of our acceleration: "corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). The lab notebook: "dual *matter* no longer masquerades as exotic energy (dust maps to dust — the w=−2 pathology is gone because it was an artifact)" (#1 §6).

The first failure was observational: a definite prediction, w = −2, contradicted by data. The second was mathematical: once the rule was derived correctly, dual dust gave no acceleration to test. Both versions take the conjugate universe's content to be dust. The corrected dictionary has other entries, and #11 §10 records that one of them keeps a route.

## The route that remains: dual kinetic energy as a cosmological constant

The corrected dictionary's first entry is "Dual stiff ↦ Λ" (#11 §10). In the lab notebook's table, "Kinetic/stiff energy (w̃ = +1)" on the conjugate side acts on our expansion as a "**Cosmological constant** (w = −1)" (#1 §6; [the table](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary#the-table)). A stiff fluid is what a uniform scalar field becomes when its energy is all kinetic [T] ([fluids and how they thin](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/friedmann-cosmology#fluids-and-how-they-thin-t)). In its own frame it thins fastest of all fluids, as ã⁻⁶, so as the conjugate universe contracts its density grows. Its energy per dual comoving volume then grows as a³, the rate at which our comoving volume grows, so the density it contributes on our side stays constant, as a cosmological constant's does [D — corrected en route] ([the derivation](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary#the-derivation)).

#11 §10 draws the route from this entry: "dual stiff ↦ Λ, so a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant". Kinetic energy is positive, and a positive cosmological constant makes the expansion accelerate [T] ([acceleration](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/cosmological-constant#acceleration)). On this route the conjugate universe's contraction, with contents dominated by kinetic energy, would be the source of our acceleration.

The lab notebook drew the same consequence from the table when it first derived it: "the natural source of a near-constant dark energy is **kinetic energy in the dual frame**" (#1 §6). It announced the correction at its head with "The correction is what makes the dark-energy sector work" (#1 §0). #11 §10 cites the first remark for the route.

The route carries the conjecture's tag, [O]. The dictionary entry is derived; that the conjugate universe's contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts is not.

## What is open

- **Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated** (#11 §10). The root `CLAUDE.md` lists it among the open items recorded on 2026-10-29, not queued ('Since the handoff').
- **How the route relates to τ's potential** (#11 §10; root `CLAUDE.md`, the same list). The program's dark energy is the field τ, "hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle, V = V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)]" (#11 §10), whose dark energy is potential energy in our own description ([τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence)). The route places dark energy in kinetic energy in the conjugate description. The lab notebook named τ "The obvious candidate for a free-rolling field" (#1 §6) and then identified dark energy with τ "sitting near the top of its cosine potential" (#1 §7). No document derives one from the other: [Open: dual kinetic energy versus τ's potential](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/dual-kinetic-link).
- **Paul's concurrence** in the recorded deaths of the two failed versions, asked on 2026-10-29; until he answers, they stand as recorded (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff').
- **A test.** No row of the [predictions table](/wiki/predictions) (#11 §13) tests the conjecture. The dark-energy rows test τ: [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift), thawing and never phantom, and [row 5](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion), the turnaround.
- **The dictionary's scope.** The dictionary is derived for homogeneous fluids in a homogeneous universe and is not machine-checked ([scope and caveats](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary#scope-and-caveats)).
