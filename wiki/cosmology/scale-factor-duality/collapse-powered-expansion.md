*Conjugate collapse powering our expansion is P.R.'s conjecture that the gravitational collapse of the [conjugate universe](../../program/conjugate-universe.md), the momentum-space description of our own, is what drives the accelerating expansion of our universe. It is the ninth of the program's [founding suppositions](../../program/founding-suppositions.md). Two versions of it have failed. Written with the heuristic translation rule of the early discussion, it required dark energy with [equation of state](../dark-energy/quintessence.md#dark-energy-and-the-equation-of-state) w = −2 (w is dark energy's pressure divided by its energy density), a value the data exclude; with the rule derived properly, the [duality dictionary](duality-dictionary.md), collapsing conjugate matter acts on our expansion as ordinary matter does, slowing it. The derived dictionary keeps one route: energy that is all kinetic on the conjugate side acts on our expansion as a [cosmological constant](../dark-energy/cosmological-constant.md#dual-kinetic-energy-acts-as-a-λ), so a conjugate universe whose contents are dominated by kinetic energy as it contracts would act on ours as one. Whether it is, and how that route relates to the program's dark-energy field [τ](../dark-energy/tau-quintessence.md), are open.*

**Status:** Conjecture — [O] — P.R.'s conjecture, founding supposition 9 ([#11](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); [App. A item 9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)). Two versions failed, w = −2, which the data exclude, and dual dust, which the corrected dictionary maps to dust ([below](#two-versions-that-failed)); the route that remains is dual stiff ↦ Λ. Nothing derives the conjecture and no script tests it; the dictionary entry its route rests on, dual stiff ↦ Λ, is [D — corrected en route] ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)), and section G of [the literature-check script](../../method/verification/literature-check-script.md#section-g-the-algebra-behind-flag-66) checks its algebra; as a map of fluid variables the dictionary is the scale-factor duality of string cosmology with sources, and its use in our own Friedmann equation is the program's step (flag 66, pending). Home section: [scale-factor duality](_index.md); listed with the [conjectures](../../status/conjectures.md).

## The conjecture

Founding supposition 9 of P.R.'s eleven, as #11's audit words it: "Their collapse is our acceleration" ([#11 App. A item 9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)). "Their" refers to the [conjugate universe](../../program/conjugate-universe.md). The program describes one universe twice, once in position variables and once in momentum variables, and the conjecture is that the contraction of the second description is the source of the acceleration of our expansion, which standard cosmology attributes to dark energy. The [popular article](../../history/documents/popular-articles.md) [#10](../../../popsci/angle-of-the-mirror.md) words it as "the conjugate universe's gravitational collapse is what powers our accelerating expansion" and reports it as killed ('The killing fields', 'The method is the message'); the popular articles are never a source for a claim's status.

[#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology) records it beside the dictionary, as P.R.'s founding supposition 9 [O]:

> "Their collapse is our acceleration" failed in two versions: the heuristic dictionary's w = −2, which the data exclude, and dual dust, which the corrected dictionary maps to dust. The corrected dictionary keeps a route: dual stiff ↦ Λ, so a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant, and the lab notebook already names "kinetic energy in the dual frame" as "the natural source of a near-constant dark energy" ([#1 §6](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary)). Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential, are open.

Its audit gives the same standing, "**open**, P.R.'s conjecture [O]" (App. A item 9).

## Where it comes from

- **The contraction is a theorem.** By [the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](cosmological-constraint.md), the conjugate scale factor ã shrinks exactly as ours grows, in the way a waveform's spectrum narrows by the same factor as the waveform is stretched. That is founding supposition 6, "Conjugate universe shrinks as ours expands", "**promoted to theorem**" ([#11 App. A item 6](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)). The conjecture concerns what the contraction does to our expansion, not whether it happens.
- **The translation is the dictionary.** How energy on the conjugate side acts on our expansion is set by the [duality dictionary](duality-dictionary.md): a dual-frame fluid with equation of state w̃ contributes w_eff = −w̃ ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology), "[D — corrected en route]").
- **The idea is older than the documents.** It belongs to the development discussion on claude.ai that preceded the [lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md), and no document states it in more detail than the sentences quoted on this page.

## Two versions that failed

Both failures are results of the record, and they are stated in full below: w = −2 is excluded by data, and dust maps to dust.

### With the heuristic rule: w = −2

Turning the idea into a prediction needs a rule for how energy on the conjugate side acts on our expansion. The development discussion used a heuristic rule, w_eff = −2 − w̃, where w̃ is the equation of state of a fluid in the conjugate frame and w_eff the equation of state with which it acts on us. The lab notebook describes the heuristic as one "which identified densities across frames without the constraint's volume Jacobian" ([#1 §6](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary)).

The collapsing content is matter, which cosmology models as dust, w̃ = 0 (the handoff's "dual dust"). On the heuristic rule it acts on our expansion like a fluid with w = −2 (the wiki's reading of the step: setting ρ_eff = ρ̃ with ã = 1/a gives w_eff = −2 − w̃, and w̃ = 0 gives −2; the derivation is on [the duality dictionary](duality-dictionary.md#superseded-the-earlier-heuristic-rule)). The handoff's record: "first version gave w = −2 (falsified)" ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen)).

An equation of state below −1 is phantom: an energy density that grows as the universe expands ([the phantom divide](../dark-energy/phantom-divide.md)) [T]. A value as low as −2 is far outside what observations of the expansion allow [T]. The documents record the verdict as "falsified" ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen); [#11 App. A item 9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)) and as a value "which the data exclude" ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)), and they cite no particular measurement; the popular article #10 says the required dark-energy strength was one "the data ruled out decades ago" ('The killing fields'). The literature-check note gives a measurement for [#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)'s statement: the 2018 results of the Planck satellite, with supernovae and baryon acoustic oscillations and with the [evolution parameter](../dark-energy/cpl-parametrization.md#what-w-and-wₐ-mean) wₐ, which measures how fast w has changed as the universe expanded, fixed at 0, give a constant equation of state w₀ = −1.028 ± 0.031 (w₀ is the value of w today), consistent with a cosmological constant ([Aghanim et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209) [S]; [`notes/literature-check.md` §6](../../../notes/literature-check.md#6-conjugate-collapse-drives-our-acceleration-c5-partly-anticipated); flag 65, pending). It does not bear on the route that remains.

### With the corrected rule: dual dust acts as dust

Deriving the dictionary with the constraint's volume Jacobian replaced the heuristic with w_eff = −w̃ ([#1 §6](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary); [#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology), "[D — corrected en route]"). Under the derived rule:

```text
dual dust:   w̃ = 0   ⇒   w_eff = 0
```

Collapsing conjugate matter acts on our expansion as ordinary dust. Dust has positive energy density and no pressure, so in the acceleration equation of [Friedmann cosmology](friedmann-cosmology.md) it decelerates the expansion [T]. In this version the conjugate side's collapse cannot be the source of our acceleration: "corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)" ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen)). The lab notebook: "dual *matter* no longer masquerades as exotic energy (dust maps to dust — the w=−2 pathology is gone because it was an artifact)" ([#1 §6](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary)).

The first failure was observational: a definite prediction, w = −2, contradicted by data. The second was mathematical: once the rule was derived correctly, dual dust gave no acceleration to test. Both versions take the conjugate universe's content to be dust. The corrected dictionary has other entries, and [#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology) records that one of them keeps a route.

## The route that remains: dual kinetic energy as a cosmological constant

The corrected dictionary's first entry is "Dual stiff ↦ Λ" ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)). In the lab notebook's table, "Kinetic/stiff energy (w̃ = +1)" on the conjugate side acts on our expansion as a "**Cosmological constant** (w = −1)" ([#1 §6](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary); [the table](duality-dictionary.md#the-table)). A stiff fluid is what a uniform scalar field becomes when its energy is all kinetic [T] ([fluids and how they thin](friedmann-cosmology.md#fluids-and-how-they-thin-t)). In its own frame it thins fastest of all fluids, as ã⁻⁶, so as the conjugate universe contracts its density grows. Its energy per dual comoving volume then grows as a³, the rate at which our comoving volume grows, so the density it contributes on our side stays constant, as a cosmological constant's does [D — corrected en route] ([the derivation](duality-dictionary.md#the-derivation)).

[#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology) draws the route from this entry: "dual stiff ↦ Λ, so a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant". Kinetic energy is positive, and a positive cosmological constant makes the expansion accelerate [T] ([acceleration](../dark-energy/cosmological-constant.md#acceleration)). On this route the conjugate universe's contraction, with contents dominated by kinetic energy, would be the source of our acceleration.

The lab notebook drew the same consequence from the table when it first derived it: "the natural source of a near-constant dark energy is **kinetic energy in the dual frame**" ([#1 §6](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary)). It announced the correction at its head with "The correction is what makes the dark-energy sector work" ([#1 §0](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#0-epistemic-scope)). [#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology) cites the first remark for the route.

The route carries the conjecture's tag, [O]. The dictionary entry is derived; that the conjugate universe's contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts is not.

## What is open

- **Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated** ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)). The root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md) lists it among the open items, not queued ('Since the handoff').
- **How the route relates to τ's potential** ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md), the same list). The program's dark energy is the field τ, "hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle, V = V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)]" ([#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)), whose dark energy is potential energy in our own description ([τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](../dark-energy/tau-quintessence.md)). The route places dark energy in kinetic energy in the conjugate description. The lab notebook named τ "The obvious candidate for a free-rolling field" ([#1 §6](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary)) and then identified dark energy with τ "sitting near the top of its cosine potential" ([#1 §7](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#7-cosmology-ii-dark-energy-its-drift-and-the-turnaround)). No document derives one from the other: [Open: dual kinetic energy versus τ's potential](dual-kinetic-link.md).
- **A test.** No row of the [predictions table](../../predictions/_index.md) ([#11 §13](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions)) tests the conjecture. The dark-energy rows test τ: [row 4](../../predictions/dark-energy-drift.md), thawing and never phantom, and [row 5](../../predictions/fate-of-expansion.md), the turnaround.
- **The dictionary's scope.** The dictionary is derived for homogeneous fluids in a homogeneous universe, and the gate checks its algebra only there ([scope and caveats](duality-dictionary.md#scope-and-caveats); [section G of the literature-check script](../../method/verification/literature-check-script.md#section-g-the-algebra-behind-flag-66)). In a contracting universe, anisotropy grows like a stiff fluid: it comes to dominate contents with w < 1, keeps a fixed ratio to stiff contents, and only contents with w > 1 contract isotropically ([`notes/literature-check.md` §6](../../../notes/literature-check.md#6-conjugate-collapse-drives-our-acceleration-c5-partly-anticipated)). A contracting conjugate universe whose contents have w̃ < 1 would then be stiff-like in scaling but anisotropic, in the reading of one verifier of the literature check.

## In the literature

Partly anticipated ([the literature check](../../method/literature-check.md)). Henry-Couannier's Dark Gravity (on arXiv from 2006; the acceleration driven by the conjugate side from 2010) pairs our universe with a conjugate one whose scale factor is the inverse of ours; in his 2018 proceedings the conjugate side contracts as ours expands and, after a permutation of the two sides' regimes, drives our recent acceleration without a cosmological constant [S] (read through a web fetch; the proceedings exist only as a PDF, which the code checks cannot read). There the partner is a time-reversed twin, not the momentum description of our own universe, and its driver is cold matter, the version the [corrected dictionary](duality-dictionary.md) maps to dust. The verdict counts the partner's identity as part of the conjecture; if that identity is counted as framework, Dark Gravity could be called an anticipation of the conjecture's founding sentence, though never of the route that remains, a reading the panel judge for this conjecture states and does not adopt, since Dark Gravity differs in physical claim. The literature check's first-round verifier for this conjecture finds an anticipation arguable only for the founding sentence read as a statement about how the two scale factors move: Henry-Couannier describes the driver as the dominance of the conjugate side's matter, not as its collapse, and in the verifier's calculation that side's contribution to the equations of our expansion weakens as the side contracts, so the contraction does not power our acceleration ([`notes/literature-check.md` §2](../../../notes/literature-check.md#2-the-verdicts)). Collapse in one description of a universe as accelerated expansion in another is published for inflation ([Gasperini and Veneziano 1993](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9309023), 2003; [Buonanno, Damour and Veneziano 1999](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9806230)) and for present dark energy: "the effect of dark energy can equivalently be seen as collapse of space in a conformally connected universe" ([Mukherjee, Jassal and Lochan 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02835)) [S]. There the dark energy is assumed, and the collapse is a second description of it, not its source; with a cosmological constant, the conformally connected universe collapses during the matter era and expands again, after a bounce, through the era of dark energy [S]. The same paper pairs a contracting description whose content is all kinetic energy, a stiff fluid, with an exponentially expanding one that mimics the era of dark energy: the pairing of the route that remains [S], but as one exact solution of Brans–Dicke gravity, a theory in which a scalar field sets the strength of gravity, at a chosen value of its parameter, with a separate clock in each description, and not as a dictionary. Not found: the collapsing partner as the momentum description of our own universe, and with it the route that remains as the program derives it, through the corrected dictionary, derived with one clock shared by the two descriptions, in which the conjugate side's energy enters our own Friedmann equation (flag 65, pending).
