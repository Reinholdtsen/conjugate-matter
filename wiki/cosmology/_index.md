*Cosmology is the study of the universe as a whole: how it expands, what it contains and how the expansion will end. The expansion is tracked by the scale factor, the number that multiplies the distances between distant galaxies. In the Comatter program, the scale factor of our universe and that of its momentum-space description, the [conjugate universe](../program/conjugate-universe.md), are exact reciprocals, so one grows as the other shrinks, in the way a waveform's spectrum narrows when the waveform is stretched. Dark energy, which accelerates the expansion, is identified with a field: [the angle τ](dark-energy/tau-quintessence.md) that sets how the vacuum divides [phase space](../phase-space/_index.md), the space of positions and momenta taken together, into position and momentum. The field's energy density may fall as space expands but never rise. That requirement is the one the most recent survey data lean against, so dark energy is where the program faces its nearest observational test. This section sets out the established cosmology the claims use, then the claims in the order the program derived them.*
