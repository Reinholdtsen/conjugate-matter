*Cosmology is the study of the universe as a whole: how it expands, what it contains and how the expansion will end. In the Broken-Born program, the scale factor of our universe and that of its momentum-space description, the [conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe), are exact reciprocals, so one grows as the other shrinks, in the way a waveform's spectrum narrows when the waveform is stretched. Dark energy, which accelerates the expansion, is identified with a field: the angle τ that sets how the vacuum divides phase space into position and momentum, whose energy density may fall as space expands but never rise. That last requirement is the one the most recent survey data lean against, so dark energy is where the program faces its nearest observational test. This section sets out the established cosmology the claims use, then the claims in the order the program derived them.*

## Map

The section has three subsections. The first two each open with established physics and then give the program's own claims; the third is established physics only.

[Scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality) comes first, because the rest of the section is written in its terms. It starts from [Friedmann cosmology](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/friedmann-cosmology), the standard description of an expanding universe, and then gives the program's results in derivation order: the reciprocal relation a·ã = 1 between the two scale factors, the dictionary that translates energy in the conjugate description into its effect on our expansion, the [obituary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion) of the founding idea that the conjugate universe's collapse drives our acceleration, and the reading of a far-future turnaround as the conjugate universe's bounce. It ends with an open gap in the motivation for dark energy and a chat-only conjecture.

[Dark energy](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy) comes second. Its first three pages are established physics: quintessence and the equation of state w, the phantom divide at w = −1, and the baryon-acoustic-oscillation survey of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI). The program's claims follow: the dark-energy field τ, its safety from fifth-force tests, the bound w ≥ −1, the record of the DESI release that favours a crossing below −1, the over-constraint that ties the model's parameters together, and the open question of whether τ's potential can become negative at all.

[Early-universe cosmology](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe) is literature only: the thermal history of the radiation era and its count of light species, inflation and the dilution of relics, and gravitational waves from phase transitions. It is background for [row 10](/wiki/predictions/early-universe-step) of the predictions table and for the review that retired the [soliton dark-matter candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).
