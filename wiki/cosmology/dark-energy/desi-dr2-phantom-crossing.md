*DESI DR2's phantom crossing is the finding, reported in 2025 from the second data release (Data Release 2, DR2) of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI), that the dark-energy equation of state favoured by the data lies above −1 at low redshift and crosses below −1, into the phantom regime, at redshifts above about 0.4, in every combination of datasets analysed. It is the measurement that put the program's dark-energy sector under pressure. The program requires dark energy never to be phantom, so its requirement stands against the direct reading of these data. It survives on two stated points: fits without a phantom phase remain compatible at about two standard deviations, and the equation of state is inferred from distance measurements rather than observed. The next data release is the decisive test.*

**Status:** Record — the measurement is cited [21S] in #11 §10, "Under pressure (v2.1)" — raised by the round-3 referee, accepted as ruling 11 and written into #11 v2.1; per the root `CLAUDE.md` the sector is "under real pressure ahead of DR3", DESI's Data Release 3.

## Key facts

- **What DESI reports.** In every dataset combination "the favored w(z) shows a phase of w > −1 at low redshifts and a phantom crossing to w < −1 above redshifts z ≃ 0.4", and this preference "is thus rather independent of parametrization choices made in the analysis" (#11 §10, quoting [21S]: DESI Collaboration, "DESI DR2 Results II", arXiv:2503.14738). The survey is described on [DESI baryon acoustic oscillations](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi).
- **The checkpoint's reading.** "The framework's strict w ≥ −1 therefore stands against the direct reading of the data. It survives on two points: non-phantom quintessence fits remain compatible [19S], and w(z) is inferred from distances rather than observed, as [21S] also notes. DR3 is the decisive test (§13, row 4)." The fits are "non-phantom compatible at ~2σ" (#11 §10). See [the no-phantom bound w ≥ −1](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound) and [the phantom divide](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide).
- **How it entered the record.** The round-3 referee, reviewing the soliton manuscript (#14 v3, since withdrawn), added a "Dark-energy aside": "'Never-phantom ... consistent with hints' is at best partially true; the DESI-preferred solution is phantom-crossing" (`review/round3/referee/audit-notes.md` Step 4(c) item 11). Ruling 11 accepted the finding: "The parent framework's 'never phantom' needs the checkpoint's own nuance: non-phantom fits remain compatible at ~2σ." Decision 4 then gave #11 §10 "the DESI DR2 phantom-crossing nuance (ruling 11)". See [round-3 rulings](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings) and [round-3 decisions](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions).
- **The readings it replaced.** The handoff called the framework consistent with the drift hints of DESI's first two data releases (`HANDOFF.md` §2); #9 §8 said "the DESI survey's hints of weakening dark energy are exactly the drift this sector requires"; #1 §7 item 2 called thawing "the *sign pattern DESI reports*"; the withdrawn #14 §4 said "currently consistent with hints of weakening dark energy". #11 §10's own italic opener still says DESI's hints "are exactly the required drift": it was not updated, and the "Under pressure" paragraph governs.
- **Where it stands.** Root `CLAUDE.md`: "DESI DR2 favors a phantom crossing above z ≈ 0.4, against the framework's strict w ≥ −1 (§10). The dark-energy sector is under real pressure ahead of DR3." #11 §15: "the current release already leans against its dark-energy prediction (§10)", and the crossing is listed among the degeneration risks. See [assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment).
- **The three outcomes of Data Release 3.** The handoff's external clock: "robust constant w = −1 kills the τ sector; robust intrinsic w < −1 kills the framework outright; continued thawing drift keeps it alive", with the credit for survival shared with other dynamical dark-energy models (`HANDOFF.md` §2). The popular article #10 calls these "Three doors". The test card is [row 4: thawing dark energy, never phantom](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift).
