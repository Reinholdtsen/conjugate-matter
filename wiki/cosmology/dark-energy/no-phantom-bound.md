*The no-phantom bound is the program's requirement that the dark-energy equation of state w never fall below −1, the value of a cosmological constant. It holds because the program's dark-energy field τ is an ordinary (canonical) scalar field, whose energy density can fall or hold steady as space expands but never rise, while w < −1, the phantom regime, would make it rise. The bound makes the dark-energy sector a sharp test: a robust measurement of an intrinsic w < −1 would kill the program's framework outright. The second data release of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) favours exactly such a crossing at earlier times, so the bound stands, under pressure.*

**Status:** Alive, under pressure — [D] in #1 §7 item 1, and part of #11 §10's [D + T] paragraph — its test is [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift), and DESI's Data Release 2 leans against it ([DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing)).

## Key facts

- **Statement.** #11 §10: "strictly w ≥ −1". #1 §7 item 1: "**w ≥ −1 always.** τ is a canonical scalar; the null energy condition holds; phantom crossing is impossible. **[D]**" The physics is [the phantom divide and the null energy condition](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide); the field is [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence).
- **What counts.** The kill applies to w < −1 "as an intrinsic equation of state, not an artifact of an assumed ΛCDM background" (#1 §7 item 1).
- **Row 4.** #11 §13 sets the test against DESI's Data Release 3 (DR3): requirement "Thawing, w₀ > −1, wₐ < 0, never phantom; drift persists in DESI DR3"; killed by "DR3+ constant −1 (kills τ-sector); robust intrinsic w < −1 (kills outright) — DR2 already favors a crossing above z ≈ 0.4 (§10)". The handoff: "robust intrinsic w < −1 kills the framework outright" (`HANDOFF.md` §2).
- **How much dies.** #1 §7 item 1 said a robust w < −1 "falsifies this sector outright"; #11 row 4 and the handoff make it fatal to the whole framework. The higher-ranked #11 governs.
- **Under pressure.** #11 §10, "Under pressure (v2.1)": "The framework's strict w ≥ −1 therefore stands against the direct reading of the data. It survives on two points: non-phantom quintessence fits remain compatible [19S], and w(z) is inferred from distances rather than observed, as [21S] also notes." #11 §15 lists "DESI DR2's phantom crossing against §10's strict w ≥ −1" among the program's degeneration risks.
- **The popular gloss.** The popular article #12: phantom behaviour "would require the orientation's kinetic energy to be negative, and kinetic energy is the square of a rate of change; a square of a real quantity cannot be negative" (explanation only, not a status source).
