*The dark-energy over-constraint is a counting argument: the program's dark-energy model has fewer free numbers than the quantities it predicts, so the data can test the model against itself. The model has three free numbers, the height V₀ and scale f of τ's potential and τ's initial value τᵢ, and the height is fixed by the measured amount of dark energy, which leaves two. Those two must account for three observables: today's equation of state w₀, its rate of change wₐ, and the time of the future turnaround, t_turn. One relation among the three is therefore a prediction, which #11 counts among the program's progressive signs, although the third observable lies in the far future.*

**Status:** Alive — [D] (#1 §7 item 4), within #11 §10's [D + T] paragraph — a counting result, not machine-checked; it cannot be tested until a turnaround time is measured, which row 5 marks as long-horizon.

## Key facts

- **Statement.** #11 §10: "two parameters (f, τᵢ) for three observables (w₀, wₐ, t_turn) — one over-constraint."
- **Derivation.** #1 §7 item 4: "**Parameter economy.** V₀ is fixed by Ω_DE; the observables (w₀, wₐ, t_turn) are then functions of two numbers, (f, τᵢ). Three measurements, two parameters: one over-constraint. **[D]**" The potential is that of [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence), and w₀ and wₐ are the parameters described on [quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence).
- **Parameter count.** The lab notebook's per-sector table gives dark energy "f, τᵢ (2; V₀ fixed by Ω_DE)" against "w₀, wₐ, t_turn (3)" (#1 §10). See [history of the predictions table](/wiki/predictions/table-history).
- **In the assessment.** #11 §15 counts the dark-energy over-constraint among the progressive signs: "one over-constraint (DE sector)". See [assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment) and [Lakatos's methodology of research programmes](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology).
- **Its reach.** w₀ and wₐ are the near-term observables of [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift). t_turn is the time of [the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce), whose row is "(Long horizon; near-term test is row 4)" (#11 §13 row 5; [row 5](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion)). A turnaround also needs V(τ) to become negative, which the sources leave unresolved: [Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign).
