*Dark energy is the name for whatever makes the expansion of the universe accelerate; in the standard cosmological model it is a cosmological constant, an energy density that stays the same as space expands. The Broken-Born program identifies it instead with a field, τ, the angle that sets how the vacuum divides phase space into position and momentum. As a field, τ can change, and the program requires the change to go one way: dark energy may weaken, but its density may never rise as space expands, the behaviour called phantom. The second data release of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) favours a phantom phase at earlier times, so the requirement stands against the direct reading of the data, and the next release is the decisive test.*

## Map

The first three pages are established physics; the six after them are the program's claims and the record of the data that test them.

[Quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence) comes first: dark energy made of a slowly evolving scalar field, its equation of state w, the difference between thawing and freezing models, hilltop and axion-like potentials, the w₀–wₐ summary of a drifting w, and the recollapse that follows if the vacuum energy turns negative.

[The phantom divide and the null energy condition](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide) explains why w = −1 separates two regimes, why an ordinary scalar field cannot reach w < −1, and what a crossing would require.

[DESI baryon acoustic oscillations](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi) describes the survey whose data releases test the program: how a ruler printed by early-universe sound waves gives distances, and why w(z) is inferred from those distances rather than observed.

[τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence) begins the program's claims [D + T]: dark energy is the polarization angle τ, with potential V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)], sitting near the top and thawing. The toy models show that the structure is consistent, and nothing more.

[Fifth-force safety of τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-fifth-force) checks that so light a field does not produce a detectable force between ordinary bodies: as an approximate Goldstone field it couples only through its rate of change [D].

[The no-phantom bound w ≥ −1](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound) is the sector's sharp requirement: τ is a canonical scalar, so w never falls below −1, and a robust intrinsic w < −1 would kill the framework outright.

[DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing) is the record of the measurement that put the bound under pressure: the survey's preferred w(z) crosses below −1 above z ≃ 0.4. It also records how the finding entered the program through the round-3 review, and the three possible outcomes of the next data release.

[The dark-energy over-constraint](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/over-constraint) counts parameters: two free numbers must fit three observables, one of them the far-future turnaround time.

[Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign) closes the section with an unresolved conflict: the potential as written is never negative, yet the turnaround needs it to be.
