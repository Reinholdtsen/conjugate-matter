*Dark energy is the name for whatever makes the expansion of the universe accelerate; in the standard cosmological model it is a [cosmological constant](cosmological-constant.md), an energy density that stays the same as space expands. The Comatter program identifies it instead with a field, [τ](tau-quintessence.md), the angle that sets how the vacuum divides [phase space](../../phase-space/_index.md), the space of positions and momenta taken together, into position and momentum. As a field, τ can change, and the program requires the change to go one way: dark energy may weaken, but its density may never rise as space expands, the behaviour called [phantom](phantom-divide.md). The second data release of the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument](desi.md) (DESI) favours a phantom phase at earlier times, so the requirement stands against the direct reading of the data and the sector is under real pressure; the next release is the decisive test. This section holds the established physics of dynamical dark energy, the program's τ sector, the record of the data that test it, and one unresolved conflict in its documents.*

**Stands:** the established physics of [the cosmological constant Λ](cosmological-constant.md), [de Sitter space](de-sitter-space.md), [quintessence and the equation of state](quintessence.md), [thawing and freezing quintessence](thawing-and-freezing.md), [axion-like fields and pseudo-Goldstone bosons](axion-like-fields.md), [the CPL parametrization w(a) = w₀ + wₐ(1 − a)](cpl-parametrization.md) (w is dark energy's equation of state, its pressure divided by its energy density; w₀ is the value of w today, and wₐ measures how fast w has changed as the universe expanded), [the phantom divide and the null energy condition](phantom-divide.md) and [DESI baryon acoustic oscillations](desi.md); the program's claims [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](tau-quintessence.md) ([D + T], under real pressure), [fifth-force safety of τ](tau-fifth-force.md) ([D], [lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) only), [the no-phantom bound w ≥ −1](no-phantom-bound.md) ([D + T], under real pressure) and [the dark-energy over-constraint](over-constraint.md) ([D], a count); and the record of [DESI DR2's phantom crossing](desi-dr2-phantom-crossing.md), the measurement that puts the sector under pressure · **Conjectures:** the related [conjugate collapse powers our expansion](../scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion.md), P.R.'s open conjecture, is in [scale-factor duality](../scale-factor-duality/_index.md) · **Open:** [the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](potential-sign.md), an unresolved conflict between the potential and the turnaround (in [open problems](../../status/open-problems.md)) · **Superseded:** no page of its own here; folded sections on [the potential's origin in the rigid ω](tau-quintessence.md#superseded-the-potentials-origin-in-the-rigid-ω) and [the earlier readings of DESI](desi-dr2-phantom-crossing.md#superseded-the-earlier-readings-of-desi) (both in [superseded statements](../../status/superseded.md))

## Map

The first eight pages are established physics, written from general knowledge and tagged [T]; the six after them are the program's claims, the record of the data that test them, and an open conflict. The program's claims are stated in [checkpoint #11](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology), in a paragraph tagged [D + T], and were first set out in [§7](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton) of [the lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md); fifth-force safety is argued in the notebook's §4 only. No `verify/` script tests these claims; [`verify/closed_universe.py`](../../../verify/closed_universe.py) checks the separate arithmetic of [a closed Comatter universe](../scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md).

The literature comes first.

- [The cosmological constant Λ](cosmological-constant.md) is the baseline: a constant term in Einstein's equations that acts as an energy density of empty space, with w = −1 exactly, the dark energy of the standard model ΛCDM. The page shows why it does not thin as space expands, how a positive Λ makes the expansion accelerate toward de Sitter space while a negative one halts and reverses it, how surveys test for a departure from w = −1, and why the vacuum energy of quantum fields acts as a Λ. It is the standard expectation of [row 4](../../predictions/dark-energy-drift.md) and [row 5](../../predictions/fate-of-expansion.md), and the program uses it in four ways: τ in its place, dual kinetic energy acting as a Λ, the constant terms of the bimetric potential, and the negative effective Λ that the documents' turnaround needs.
- [De Sitter space](de-sitter-space.md) is the spacetime of a universe that contains only a positive Λ: a constant Hubble rate, exponential expansion with Λ = 3H², maximal symmetry and a horizon around every observer, with anti-de Sitter space as the counterpart for a negative Λ. It is the far future that row 5 calls "Eternal de Sitter", the background on which a massive spin-2 field must satisfy the [Higuchi bound](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/bimetric-cosmology.md#the-higuchi-bound), and, in two dimensions, the space of values of the field K in [model I](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/_index.md).
- [Quintessence and the equation of state](quintessence.md) introduces dark energy as a scalar field: the equation of state w, why a field with mostly potential energy has w near −1, the field equation with its expansion damping, short summaries of the thawing and freezing classes, of hilltop and axion-like potentials and of the two-parameter form of the CPL page, and recollapse when the vacuum energy turns negative or, with positive energy, in a closed universe. It ends with the fits of quintessence models to recent data that [#11](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) cites, live-searched [S].
- [Thawing and freezing quintessence](thawing-and-freezing.md) gives the two classes in full. A thawing field is held near w = −1 by the expansion and departs from it late; a freezing field moves early and slows toward −1. The page shows why a thawing field's initial velocity drops out, places hilltop models in the thawing class, and locates each class in the w₀–wₐ plane, with the corridor wₐ ≈ −1.5(1 + w₀), for which no document gives a reference. It sets DESI's second data release against the class, with flag 43 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) on where the corridor lies relative to the release's crossing, and states the thawing drift of τ that row 4 requires.
- [Axion-like fields and pseudo-Goldstone bosons](axion-like-fields.md) explains the potential τ is given: a field that is an angle, kept light and coupled only through its derivatives by a shift symmetry, with the periodic potential V₀[1 + cos(φ/f)] left by a small explicit breaking. It covers the axion itself and axion-like fields as dark matter and as dark energy, and the program's uses: τ's hilltop potential and fifth-force safety, the toy models' pseudo-Goldstone polarization angle, the momentum graviton X called "axion-like" for its exact shift symmetry, and the wave-island field χ produced "like an axion".
- [The CPL parametrization w(a) = w₀ + wₐ(1 − a)](cpl-parametrization.md) gives the two-number Chevallier–Polarski–Linder form in which surveys report a changing w and the program states its requirement: a first-order expansion in the scale factor, the [redshift](../scale-factor-duality/redshift.md) form and its early-time limit, the meaning and sign of w₀ and wₐ, the decorrelated pivot form, and what two numbers cannot represent. It places thawing models in the w₀–wₐ plane, with the program's corridor wₐ ≈ −1.5(1 + w₀), for which no document gives a reference, and shows how a fit expresses a phantom crossing, w₀ > −1 with w₀ + wₐ < −1, the shape that DESI's second data release favours. A fit in that region does not by itself show a crossing: the page reports flag 43 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), which places the program's corridor there too and finds the difference from the release's crossing in the slope.
- [The phantom divide and the null energy condition](phantom-divide.md) explains why w = −1 separates dark energy that weakens from dark energy that strengthens, shows that a canonical scalar obeys the null energy condition because ρ + p is a square, lists what a phantom phase or a crossing would need (a [ghost](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/ghosts.md#phantom-fields-t), several fields, non-minimal couplings, dark-sector interactions), and separates an intrinsic phantom from an inferred one, the distinction the program's kill condition depends on.
- [DESI baryon acoustic oscillations](desi.md) describes the survey whose releases test the program: the sound-horizon ruler and its Fourier form, how the ruler gives distances across and along the line of sight, why w(z), the equation of state at redshift z (the stretching of light by the expansion of the universe since the light was emitted; larger z means earlier), is inferred from those distances by fitting a model rather than observed, and the data releases from Data Release 1 (DR1) to DR3 as the documents cite them.

The program's claims follow.

- [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](tau-quintessence.md) is the central claim [D + T]: dark energy is τ, the angle around the Maslov circle of the space of polarizations, with action S_τ and potential V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)], sitting near the maximum and thawing along the corridor wₐ ≈ −1.5(1 + w₀). The page traces τ to the corrected postulate [P2′](../../program/postulates/p2-prime.md), gives the tags of each part of the potential, records that the [wedge sector](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation.md) of the [momentum graviton](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md) adds no competing vacuum energy, sets out the pressure from DESI's Data Release 2 (DR2), with flag 43 on where the corridor lies relative to DR2's crossing, and shows what the [toy models](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md) establish: τ's circle is their [Goldstone angle](../../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle.md), which shows consistency and nothing more. A folded section records the superseded v0.1 account of the potential's origin.
- [Fifth-force safety of τ](tau-fifth-force.md) checks that so light a field produces no detectable force between ordinary bodies: as a [pseudo-Goldstone field](../../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-and-higgs.md) it couples only through its derivatives, with no tree-level [Yukawa coupling](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces.md#the-field-τ-no-coupling-so-no-yukawa-force) [D]. The page explains the standard protection of axion-like fields, records that the argument exists only in the lab notebook's first version, and contrasts the momentum graviton X, which is safe by its short range instead.
- [The no-phantom bound w ≥ −1](no-phantom-bound.md) is the sector's sharpest commitment: τ is a canonical scalar, so whatever the potential its energy density never grows as space expands, and w never falls below −1 while that density is positive. The page gives the three-step derivation, the distinction between an intrinsic and an inferred phantom phase, the kill condition with the documents' differing statements of how much it kills (#11: "kills outright"; the handoff: the whole framework), and the pressure from DR2.
- [DESI DR2's phantom crossing](desi-dr2-phantom-crossing.md) is the record of the measurement: in every dataset combination the favoured w(z) is above −1 at low redshift and crosses below it above z ≃ 0.4. The page sets out the two points on which the sector survives, how the finding entered the program through the [round-3](../../method/review/round-3/_index.md) review, the italic sentence of [#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology) that still reads DESI's hints as "exactly the required drift", and the three outcomes of Data Release 3 (DR3). A folded section lists the earlier, superseded readings of DESI.
- [The dark-energy over-constraint](over-constraint.md) counts parameters: with V₀ fixed by the measured dark-energy density, two numbers (f, τᵢ) must fit three observables (w₀, wₐ, t_turn), one relation that [#11 §15](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment) counts as a progressive sign. Its third observable is long-horizon and rests on the open sign problem; with the potential as written a turnaround needs a closed universe, where the curvature becomes a third input and the over-constraint moves rather than vanishing (flag 46).
- [Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](potential-sign.md) closes the section with an unresolved conflict in the documents: V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)] is never negative for V₀ > 0, yet the documents' turnaround and the fits they cite for it need negative vacuum energy. None of the three documents that write the potential discusses it; the wiki's build recorded it as flag 27 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, with an executed check kept outside [the gate](../../method/verification/the-gate.md). The page lists what depends on it and records the conflict without choosing a side. The flag's addendum of 2026-10-27, from the unreviewed closed-universe note, finds that with this potential a closed universe still turns around, τ oscillating past its minimum and curvature reversing the expansion, while flat and open universes never do ([a closed Comatter universe](../scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md)).
