*Dark energy is the name for whatever makes the expansion of the universe accelerate; in the standard cosmological model it is a cosmological constant, an energy density that stays the same as space expands. The Broken-Born program identifies it instead with a field, [τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence), the angle that sets how the vacuum divides phase space into position and momentum. As a field, τ can change, and the program requires the change to go one way: dark energy may weaken, but its density may never rise as space expands, the behaviour called [phantom](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide). The second data release of the [Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi) (DESI) favours a phantom phase at earlier times, so the requirement stands against the direct reading of the data and the sector is under real pressure; the next release is the decisive test. This section holds the established physics of dynamical dark energy, the program's τ sector, the record of the data that test it, and one unresolved conflict in its documents.*

## Map

The first three pages are established physics, written from general knowledge and tagged [T]; the six after them are the program's claims, the record of the data that test them, and an open conflict. The program's claims are stated in [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §10, in a paragraph tagged [D + T], and were first set out in §7 of [the lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook); fifth-force safety is argued in the notebook's §4 only. No `verify/` script tests the cosmology.

The literature comes first.

- [Quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence) introduces dark energy as a scalar field: the equation of state w, why a field with mostly potential energy has w near −1, the field equation with its expansion damping, the thawing and freezing classes, hilltop and axion-like potentials, the Chevallier–Polarski–Linder form w(a) = w₀ + wₐ(1 − a) with the thawing corridor the program uses, and recollapse when the vacuum energy turns negative. It ends with the fits of quintessence models to recent data that #11 cites, live-searched [S].
- [The phantom divide and the null energy condition](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide) explains why w = −1 separates dark energy that weakens from dark energy that strengthens, shows that a canonical scalar obeys the null energy condition because ρ + p is a square, lists what a phantom phase or a crossing would need (a ghost, several fields, non-minimal couplings, dark-sector interactions), and separates an intrinsic phantom from an inferred one, the distinction the program's kill condition depends on.
- [DESI baryon acoustic oscillations](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi) describes the survey whose releases test the program: the sound-horizon ruler and its Fourier form, how the ruler gives distances across and along the line of sight, why w(z) is inferred from those distances by fitting a model rather than observed, and the data releases from Data Release 1 (DR1) to DR3 as the documents cite them.

The program's claims follow.

- [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence) is the central claim [D + T]: dark energy is τ, the angle around the Maslov circle of the space of polarizations, with action S_τ and potential V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)], sitting near the maximum and thawing along the corridor wₐ ≈ −1.5(1 + w₀). The page traces τ to the corrected postulate [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime), gives the tags of each part of the potential, records that the wedge sector of the [momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton) adds no competing vacuum energy, sets out the pressure from DESI's Data Release 2 (DR2), and shows what the [toy models](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) establish: τ's circle is their [Goldstone angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle), which shows consistency and nothing more. A folded section records the superseded v0.1 account of the potential's origin.
- [Fifth-force safety of τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-fifth-force) checks that so light a field produces no detectable force between ordinary bodies: as a pseudo-Goldstone field it couples only through its derivatives, with no tree-level Yukawa coupling [D]. The page explains the standard protection of axion-like fields, records that the argument exists only in the lab notebook's first version, and contrasts the momentum graviton X, which is safe by its short range instead.
- [The no-phantom bound w ≥ −1](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound) is the sector's sharpest commitment: τ is a canonical scalar, so whatever the potential its energy density never grows as space expands, and w never falls below −1 while that density is positive. The page gives the three-step derivation, the distinction between an intrinsic and an inferred phantom phase, the kill condition with the documents' differing statements of how much it kills (#11: the whole framework), and the pressure from DR2.
- [DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing) is the record of the measurement: in every dataset combination the favoured w(z) is above −1 at low redshift and crosses below it above z ≃ 0.4. The page sets out the two points on which the sector survives, how the finding entered the program through the [round-3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) review and #11 v2.1, the italic sentence of #11 §10 that still reads DESI's hints as "exactly the required drift", and the three outcomes of Data Release 3 (DR3). A folded section lists the earlier, superseded readings of DESI.
- [The dark-energy over-constraint](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/over-constraint) counts parameters: with V₀ fixed by the measured dark-energy density, two numbers (f, τᵢ) must fit three observables (w₀, wₐ, t_turn), one relation that #11 §15 counts as a progressive sign. Its third observable is long-horizon and rests on the open sign problem.
- [Open: the sign of V(τ) and the turnaround](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign) closes the section with an unresolved conflict in the documents: V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)] is never negative for V₀ > 0, yet the turnaround and the fits cited for it need negative vacuum energy. No document discusses it; the page lists what depends on it and records the conflict without choosing a side.
