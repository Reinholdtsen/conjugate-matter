*The sign problem is a conflict in the program's documents about the potential energy V(τ) of the dark-energy field. They write it as V = V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)], and since a cosine never goes below −1, this potential is never negative when V₀ is positive. Yet the program's far-future turnaround, in which our expansion halts and reverses, requires the potential to become negative, and the published fits the program cites for that event use potentials with a negative minimum. No document addresses the conflict, so the turnaround, its reading as the conjugate bounce, and the turnaround time in the over-constraint all rest on an unresolved point until an executed check settles it.*

**Status:** Open — untagged; not among #11 §14's open problems — the conflict was found when the sources were read side by side for this wiki, no document resolves it, and it needs an executed check. Listed on [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems) and [conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).

## Key facts

- **The potential as written.** #11 §10: "V = V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)]". #1 §3 writes the same potential, with "Its *magnitude* V₀ is a free parameter here. **[P/O]**"; #9 §8 writes it with "V₀ fixed by Ω_DE". Because cos(τ/f) ≥ −1, V ≥ 0 whenever V₀ > 0 [T].
- **Statements that need V < 0.** #11 §10's italic opener says dark energy "eventually goes negative — at which point our expansion halts and reverses". #1 §7 item 3: "A cosine potential is negative over half its range." #9 §9 row 5: "Turnaround after V(τ) < 0"; #1 §9 row 6 has the same requirement.
- **The cited fits.** #11 §10 cites "high posterior preference for a future anti-de Sitter transition and collapse in negative-minimum quintessence fits [19S, 20S, 21S]", and #1 §7 item 3 describes them as fits "with a potential possessing a negative minimum". The general physics of recollapse is on [quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence).
- **What depends on it.** [The turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce) and its test, [row 5: the fate of the expansion](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion); the t_turn leg of [the dark-energy over-constraint](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/over-constraint); and the popular articles' account of the eventual reversal ([the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
- **What would settle it.** No document reconciles the two statements. Settling the sign needs an executed check under [the iron rule](/wiki/method/verification/iron-rule); this page records the conflict and does not choose a side.
- **The claim it qualifies.** The potential belongs to [τ: hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-quintessence). Where that potential comes from is a separate open problem: [Open: the origin of the ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/rho-preserving-potential).
