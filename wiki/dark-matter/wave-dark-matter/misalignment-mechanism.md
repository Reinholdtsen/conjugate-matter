*The misalignment mechanism is a way of producing dark matter as a coherent field rather than as a thermal gas of particles. In the early universe a very light scalar field starts at some value away from the minimum of its potential and stays frozen there while the universe expands faster than the field can oscillate; once the expansion rate falls below the field's mass, the field oscillates about its minimum, and the energy of the oscillation then dilutes with the expansion as cold matter does. It is the standard production route for axions. The program borrows it as the production channel for [the wave branch](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island) of conjugate matter, a channel the particle branch still lacks.*

**Status:** Established — [T], explained here from general knowledge — the program applies it only on the wave island (`notes/debt3b-wave-island.md`); the abundance ratio Ω_χ : Ω_b = 5.4 : 1 stays open for both dark-sector candidates (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md` §12).

## Key facts

- **The mechanism [T].** Expansion damps the field's motion while the expansion rate exceeds its mass; afterwards the field oscillates coherently, and the relic density depends on the mass and on the initial displacement of the field. No thermal contact with ordinary matter is needed, which is why the mechanism suits a field with only gravitational and self couplings.
- **The program's use.** "A field this light with only gravitational + self couplings is produced by misalignment, like an axion. Open problem #5 (cannibalism-safe relic abundance) is moot on the wave island: misalignment is the mechanism, and Ω_χ trades against (m, χ_i) in the standard way" (Debt 3b, 'Bonus'). The note does not define χ_i; in the standard treatment the relic density depends on the field's initial value [T].
- **What it leaves open.** "The 5.4:1 question remains (polarization-genesis), but the *production channel* is no longer exotic" (Debt 3b). [The checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) lists among the Debt-3 salvage "free relic abundance via misalignment on the wave island" (#11 §9), and keeps "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis)" open for both candidates (#11 §12); see [polarization-genesis](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis).
- **A numbering note.** Debt 3b's "Open problem #5" is item 5 of [the lab notebook's](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) open problems (#1 §11, "Relic abundance without cannibalism"), not item 5 of the checkpoint's list, which is [the dual-stress enhancement](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress-enhancement).
- **The particle side, still open.** For particle χ, "A sub-GeV, strongly self-coupled, gravitationally-only-connected sector cannot freeze out thermally without cannibalism problems; the abundance must be set asymmetrically" (#1 §5.3); see [relic abundance mechanisms](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/relic-abundance).
