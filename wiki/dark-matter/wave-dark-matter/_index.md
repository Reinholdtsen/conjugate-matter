*Wave dark matter is [dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter) made of particles so light that, at the densities found in galaxies, enormous numbers of them share each quantum state, so that a halo behaves as a classical wave field rather than as a gas of particles. The waves of such a field scatter off one another and pile into the lowest-energy states, building a dense coherent core called a [Bose star](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics); if the particles also attract one another, the core has a maximum mass and collapses when it exceeds it. This section is established literature. The Broken-Born program uses it on [the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island), the light-mass branch of its test of [momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping).*

## Map

All five pages are established physics, in the order the wave-island calculation uses them: when dark matter is a wave, how a wave halo relaxes, where an attractive condensate ends, how such a field is made, and how light it may be.

[Occupation number: particles or waves](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number) comes first. The occupation number N_occ = ρ/(m⁴σ³), the number of particles in a cube one de Broglie wavelength on a side, decides whether dark matter behaves as particles or as a classical wave, and sets the Bose enhancement of a wave halo's relaxation. The program's [classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) turns on it: collective velocity-space behavior needs masses at which N_occ ~ 10²⁰ (on the regime in which Debt 3a evaluates that number, see flag 19 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). The page records the two normalizations of Debt 3b and the three statements of the wave island's upper edge.

[Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics) describes how a wave halo relaxes: two-wave scattering conserves both the number of quanta and their energy, which drives quanta into the lowest modes and builds a Bose-star core. It gives the Levkov–Panin–Tkachev gravitational rate Γ_gr ≈ 8πG²ρ²Λ/(m³σ⁶) and the t^{1/2} slowdown of core growth, the anchor of the program's [gated condensation rate](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate), whose coefficients remain uncalibrated against that anchor.

[Attractive condensates: critical mass and the bosenova](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates) explains why a condensate whose particles attract one another has a maximum mass, M_crit ≈ 3.6 M_P/(m√g), and the collapse-and-regrowth cycle above it. Under [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a), now dead, the cycle would have drained the [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies); the page shows where Scenario A's scaling M_crit ∝ m^{−7/4} comes from and the two branches it divides the wave island into.

[The misalignment mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism) explains how such a light field is produced coherently in the [early universe](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe), frozen by the expansion and then oscillating as cold matter, without thermal contact with ordinary matter. The wave island borrows it for [its relic abundance](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic); [the 5.4 : 1 ratio](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis) of dark to ordinary matter stays open for both [dark-sector candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates).

[Bounds on fuzzy dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds) closes the section with the observations that limit how light wave dark matter can be: stellar heating in ultra-faint dwarfs (Dalal–Kravtsov, a bound the notes give as ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV and flag for verification) and the Lyman-α forest, for which the record gives no number or reference. These bounds set the wave island's lower edge and close Scenario A's light-mass escape. The page also records the unresolved conflict with [Scenario B](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b)'s fuzzy-core option at m ~ 10⁻²² eV.
