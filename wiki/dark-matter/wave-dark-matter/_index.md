*Wave dark matter is dark matter made of particles so light that, at the densities found in galaxies, enormous numbers of them share each quantum state, so that a halo behaves as a classical wave field rather than as a gas of particles. The waves of such a field scatter off one another and pile into the lowest-energy states, building a dense coherent core called a Bose star; if the particles also attract one another, the core has a maximum mass and collapses when it exceeds it. This section is established literature. The program uses it on [the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island), the light-mass branch of its test of momentum clumping.*

## Map

All five pages are established physics, in the order the wave-island calculation uses them.

[Occupation number: particles or waves](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number) comes first. The occupation number N_occ = ρ/(m⁴σ³) decides whether dark matter behaves as particles or as a wave, and the program's [classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) turns on it.

[Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics) describes how a wave halo relaxes and condenses into Bose-star cores, the Levkov–Panin–Tkachev mechanism that anchors the program's [gated condensation rate](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate).

[Attractive condensates: critical mass and the bosenova](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates) gives the maximum mass of a condensate whose particles attract one another, and the collapse-and-regrowth cycle that follows — the endpoint that would have drained the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies in [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a), now dead.

[The misalignment mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism) explains how such a light field is produced coherently in the early universe, which the wave island borrows for [its relic abundance](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic).

[Bounds on fuzzy dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds) closes the section with the observations that set a lower limit on the mass of wave dark matter, the wave island's lower edge.
