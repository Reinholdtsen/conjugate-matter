*Wave dark matter is [dark matter](../_index.md) made of particles so light that, at the densities found in galaxies, enormous numbers of them share each quantum state, so that a halo behaves as a classical wave field rather than as a gas of particles. The waves of such a field scatter off one another and pile into the lowest-energy states, building a dense coherent core called a [Bose star](wave-kinetics.md); if the particles also attract one another, the core has a maximum mass and collapses when it exceeds it. This section is established literature. The Comatter program uses it on [the wave island](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md), the light-mass branch of its test of [momentum clumping](../momentum-clumping/_index.md).*

## Map

All five pages are established physics, in the order the wave-island calculation uses them: when dark matter is a wave, how a wave halo relaxes, where an attractive condensate ends, how such a field is made, and how light it may be.

[Occupation number: particles or waves](occupation-number.md) comes first. The occupation number N_occ = ρ/(m⁴σ³), the number of particles in a cube one de Broglie wavelength on a side, decides whether dark matter behaves as particles or as a classical wave, and sets the Bose enhancement of a wave halo's relaxation. The program's [classicality theorem](../momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem.md) turns on it: collective velocity-space behavior needs masses at which N_occ ~ 10²⁰ (on the regime in which calc note [#2](../../../notes/stream-stability.md) evaluates that number, see flag 19 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)). The page records the two normalizations of calc note [#3](../../../notes/wave-island.md) and the three statements of the wave island's upper edge.

[Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](wave-kinetics.md) describes how a wave halo relaxes: two-wave scattering conserves both the number of quanta and their energy, which drives quanta into the lowest modes and builds a Bose-star core. It gives the Levkov–Panin–Tkachev gravitational rate Γ_gr ≈ 8πG²ρ²Λ/(m³σ⁶) and the t^{1/2} slowdown of core growth, the anchor of the program's [gated condensation rate](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate.md), whose coefficients remain uncalibrated against that anchor.

[Attractive condensates: critical mass and the bosenova](attractive-condensates.md) explains why a condensate whose particles attract one another has a maximum mass, M_crit ≈ 3.6 M_P/(m√g), and the collapse-and-regrowth cycle above it. Under [Scenario A](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md), a claim under review, the cycle would have drained the [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](../halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md); the page shows where Scenario A's scaling M_crit ∝ m^{−7/4} comes from and the two branches it divides the wave island into.

[The misalignment mechanism](misalignment-mechanism.md) explains how such a light field is produced coherently in the [early universe](../../cosmology/early-universe/_index.md), frozen by the expansion and then oscillating as cold matter, without thermal contact with ordinary matter. The wave island borrows it for [its relic abundance](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic.md); [the 5.4 : 1 ratio](../candidates/polarization-genesis.md) of dark to ordinary matter stays open for all three [dark-sector candidates](../candidates/_index.md).

[Bounds on fuzzy dark matter](fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds.md) closes the section with the observations that limit how light wave dark matter can be: stellar heating in ultra-faint dwarfs (Dalal–Kravtsov, a bound the notes give as ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV and flag for verification) and the Lyman-α forest, whose limit on this mass the record gives without a number or reference. These bounds set the wave island's lower edge and close Scenario A's light-mass escape. The page also records the unresolved conflict with [Scenario B](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b.md)'s fuzzy-core option at m ~ 10⁻²² eV.

## Boundaries

The program's gated version of this physics — the condensation rate, Scenarios A and B, and the ultra-faint-dwarf test of Scenario A — is in [The wave island](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md), part of [Momentum clumping](../momentum-clumping/_index.md).
