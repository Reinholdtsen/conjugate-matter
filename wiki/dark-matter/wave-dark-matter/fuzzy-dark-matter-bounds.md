*The bounds on fuzzy dark matter are observational lower limits on the mass of wave dark matter at its light extreme, where the particles are so light that their de Broglie wavelength is comparable to the core of a small galaxy. Two kinds of observation set them. In a fuzzy halo the interfering dark-matter waves form density fluctuations that heat stars over time, so the compact, cold stellar populations of [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies) exclude the lightest masses; and fuzzy dark matter suppresses small-scale structure, which the Lyman-α forest — the absorption lines of intergalactic hydrogen in quasar spectra — would reveal. In the Broken-Born program these bounds set the lower edge of [the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island) and close the light-mass escape from the falsification of [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a).*

**Status:** Established — [T], with the Dalal–Kravtsov citation † (from model memory) — the [Debt-3 notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-3-notes) flag its figure "verify exact figure before publication", no source cites a Lyman-α paper or gives a Lyman-α number, and both await [the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery).

## Fuzzy dark matter [T]

When dark matter is light enough, its de Broglie wavelength in a galaxy reaches the scale of the galaxy's own structure, and the halo must be described as a wave ([occupation number](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number)). At the light extreme, near the m ~ 10⁻²² eV that the Debt-3 notes associate with "fuzzy-end quantum pressure" (`notes/debt3b-wave-island.md`), the wavelength is comparable to the cores of dwarf galaxies. Three features follow from the wave nature:

- **Quantum pressure.** A wave cannot be confined to a region smaller than its wavelength without an energy cost, the Fourier relation between a waveform's extent and its spectrum's width. This opposes gravitational collapse below a [Jeans](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/vlasov-stability#the-static-threshold-and-the-jeans-instability)-like scale and suppresses small structures.
- **Solitonic cores.** The center of each halo holds a coherent ground-state core, a Bose star ([wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics)), whose size grows as the particle mass falls.
- **Granules.** Outside the core, waves of different momenta interfere, and the density is a pattern of granules of about one de Broglie wavelength that reshuffle continually.

Each feature becomes more pronounced as the mass falls, and each gives an observational bound.

## Bounds from ultra-faint dwarfs [T]

[Ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies) are small, cold, old and dominated by dark matter. In a fuzzy halo the granules act on the stars as fluctuating masses: each passing granule gives the stars a small gravitational kick, and over billions of years the kicks add up, heating the stellar system and making it expand. The lighter the particle, the larger and heavier the granules and the stronger the heating. A compact, cold stellar population that has survived for most of the age of the universe therefore excludes the lightest masses. Dalal and Kravtsov turned the sizes and the stellar kinematics of ultra-faint dwarfs into such a bound, [the checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint)'s reference [15]†; Debt 3c's parenthesis names the argument "granule-heating arguments".

## Bounds from the Lyman-α forest [T]

Light from a distant quasar passes through clouds of neutral hydrogen in intergalactic space on its way to the observer. Each cloud absorbs at the Lyman-α wavelength of hydrogen, shifted by the cloud's own [redshift](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/redshift), so the quasar's spectrum shows a forest of absorption lines whose pattern traces the density of the intergalactic gas along the line of sight. Its small-scale fluctuations measure the matter power spectrum on scales where fuzzy dark matter's quantum pressure suppresses it. The observed small-scale power excludes the lightest masses. No source in the program's record cites a Lyman-α analysis or gives its number.

## How the program uses the bounds

### The light branch of Scenario A

[Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) of the wave island, now dead, had two branches. The light one, m ≲ 5×10⁻²¹ eV, is where the condensate of an [attractive self-interaction](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates) would grow as a stable core rather than drain the halo. Debt 3c closes it with these bounds:

> **Branch (ii), m ≲ 5×10⁻²¹ eV:** M_crit ≳ M_core; the condensate grows as a stable soliton — ordinary fuzzy-DM phenomenology with an attractive perturbation. But this mass range is independently excluded: UFD sizes and stellar kinematics (granule-heating arguments, Dalal & Kravtsov 2022 [T — bound ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV; verify exact figure before publication]) and Lyman-α forest limits already forbid wave DM this light.

(`notes/debt3c-ufd-endpoint.md`, 'The attractive endpoint: criticality and the drain'; DM is dark matter and UFD an ultra-faint dwarf.) Its summary in 'The data': "Branch (ii) is excluded before we arrive."

### The wave island's lower edge

The same bound fixes the bottom of the surviving wave island: "The surviving wave island is roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV with Scenario-B couplings" (Debt 3c, 'Verdict and closure of Debt 3'). [The lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s addendum v0.2 states the same range without a citation.

### In v1 and the checkpoint

[Program v1](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) keeps the figure and the flag: "The light-mass escape is independently excluded (UFD heating bounds ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV, Dalal & Kravtsov 2022 [T — verify figure]; Lyman-α)" (#9 §7). [The checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) keeps the conclusion and drops the figure: "the light-mass escape is closed independently [15†, Lyman-α]" (#11 §9). ## An unresolved conflict: Scenario B's fuzzy cores

[Scenario B](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b), the surviving branch of the wave island, lets dark-matter cores come from "fuzzy-end quantum pressure at m ~ 10⁻²² eV, or baryonic feedback" (`notes/debt3b-wave-island.md`, 'What pins g? Two scenarios'). The fuzzy option sits at a mass below the lower edge that Debt 3c later sets with these bounds, 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV. Neither note addresses the difference, and [the record of conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts) lists it as unresolved. Baryonic feedback, the other option, is unaffected.
