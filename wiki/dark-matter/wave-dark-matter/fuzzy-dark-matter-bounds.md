*Fuzzy dark matter is wave dark matter at the light extreme: particles so light that their de Broglie wavelength is comparable to the size of a small galaxy. Two kinds of observation limit how light such particles can be. In a fuzzy halo the interfering dark-matter waves form density fluctuations that heat stars over time, so the compact, cold stellar populations of ultra-faint dwarf galaxies exclude the lightest masses; and fuzzy dark matter suppresses small-scale structure, which the Lyman-α forest — the absorption lines of intergalactic hydrogen in quasar spectra — would reveal. In the program these bounds set the lower edge of [the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island) and close its light-mass escape.*

**Status:** Established — [T], with the Dalal–Kravtsov citation † (from model memory) — the [Debt-3 notes](/wiki/history/documents/debt-3-notes) flag its figure "verify exact figure before publication", no source cites a Lyman-α paper, and both await [the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery).

## Key facts

- **The program's statement.** The light branch of [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a), m ≲ 5×10⁻²¹ eV, "is independently excluded: UFD sizes and stellar kinematics (granule-heating arguments, Dalal & Kravtsov 2022 [T — bound ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV; verify exact figure before publication]) and Lyman-α forest limits already forbid wave DM this light" (`notes/debt3c-ufd-endpoint.md`). Here UFD means ultra-faint dwarf and DM dark matter.
- **The wave island's lower edge.** "The surviving wave island is roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV with Scenario-B couplings" (Debt 3c, 'Verdict and closure of Debt 3').
- **In v1 and the checkpoint.** [v1](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) (#9 §7): "The light-mass escape is independently excluded (UFD heating bounds ≈ 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV, Dalal & Kravtsov 2022 [T — verify figure]; Lyman-α)." The checkpoint keeps the conclusion and drops the figure: "the light-mass escape is closed independently [15†, Lyman-α]" (#11 §9).
- **The reference.** The checkpoint's [15]†: N. Dalal, A. Kravtsov, "Excluding fuzzy dark matter with sizes and stellar kinematics of ultrafaint dwarf galaxies," Phys. Rev. D 106, 063517 (2022), arXiv:2203.05750. The Lyman-α limit has no reference and no number in any source.
- **Still to verify.** v1 lists "Dalal–Kravtsov exact bound [O]" among its literature verifications (#9 §10 item 8), and the checkpoint requires every † reference to be source-checked before external use (#11 §14 item 10).
- **Unresolved.** [Scenario B](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b) allows cores from "fuzzy-end quantum pressure at m ~ 10⁻²² eV" (`notes/debt3b-wave-island.md`), a mass below this lower edge; neither note addresses the conflict.
