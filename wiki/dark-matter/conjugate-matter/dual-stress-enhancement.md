*The dual-stress enhancement is the unexplained gap between how strongly conjugate matter scatters off itself and how weakly ordinary matter couples to the same carrier. Both kinds of matter couple to the momentum graviton X through their [dual stress](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress). Fitting the cores of dwarf galaxies needs a self-scattering cross-section that [the checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) calls nuclear-scale, while ordinary matter must feel X only at gravitational strength. Something must make conjugate matter's dual stress far more effective than ordinary matter's, and what it is, and by how much, has not been worked out.*

**Status:** Open — [O] (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md` §14 item 5) — the checkpoint calls it the "Main open quantitative item" of the conjugate sector (§8); no calculation exists. Listed in [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The open item.** The checkpoint: "Main open quantitative item: the dual-stress enhancement (nuclear-scale σ₀ with gravitational SM couplings) [O]" (#11 §8), where SM is the Standard Model; and "Dual-stress enhancement parametrics [O]" (#11 §14 item 5).
- **Where it was logged.** "[Parametrics of the χ dual-stress enhancement — why σ₀ can be nuclear-scale while SM couplings stay gravitational — logged as the main open quantitative item.]" (`notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md` §3); the Debt-1 close-out repeats it as "(iv) dual-stress enhancement parametrics (gate strength vs gravitational SM coupling)" (§5).
- **The strength to be explained.** σ₀ is the low-velocity cross-section of [the gated scattering law](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law), fixed by dwarf cores at "σ₀/m_χ ≈ 30 cm²/g" in [the scattering benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark) (#1 §5.1).
- **The weak side.** "couplings to x-polarized (SM) matter run through the SM's dual stress, which is unenhanced" (`notes/debt1a-ii-X-classification.md` §4). Ordinary matter's safety now rests on range: "X-mediated forces on the SM are Yukawa-cut at 1/M_X" (`notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md` §3); see [safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety).
- **In v1 and the handoff.** [v1](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) (#9 §6): "The main open quantitative item: the dual-stress enhancement parametrics — why σ₀ can be nuclear-scale while SM–X couplings stay gravitational [O]." [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) lists it with the gate shape among the open items (`HANDOFF.md` §2, Open 6).
- **What it qualifies.** The [gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism) explains why the self-interaction depends on relative momentum, not why it is strong. The benchmark's σ₀ is fitted to dwarf cores; the open problem is why a value that large is compatible with gravitational-strength couplings of ordinary matter to the same X.
