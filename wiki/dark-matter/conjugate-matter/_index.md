*Conjugate matter, written χ, is the program's mirror-polarized matter: the counterpart of ordinary matter in which the roles of position and momentum are exchanged. That [dark matter](../_index.md) is conjugate matter is P.R.'s core conjecture ([#11 App. A item 4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)), in two forms, χ particles and [conjugate monopoles localized in momentum](../candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md), two of the program's three [candidates](../candidates/_index.md), each of them open. Minic and coauthors proposed the identification first, in another setting and with neither form ([the Minic precedent](dual-standard-model.md)). χ gravitates exactly like ordinary matter, so it falls, orbits and bends light as dark matter does, but its own self-interaction acts only between particles moving at nearly the same velocity. In signal terms, an ordinary contact force acts where two waveforms overlap; conjugate matter's force acts only where their spectra overlap as well. That restriction is the velocity gate, the subject of the program's [gate problem](../../history/main-problems.md). This section holds its microphysics: what the gate is, the quantity through which it couples, the mechanism the record gives for it, exchange of the momentum graviton X, what kind of field could carry such a gate at all, the scattering law it implies, the benchmark that fixes its strength, and the two questions left open, the shape of its cutoff and the source of its strength. The mechanism is a structural derivation, and a note of 2026-10-28 finds that exchanging X cannot produce the gate; its proposal that the gate be counted a postulate and its mechanism open is flag 55, pending. Two scripts recompute the arithmetic of the later notes; the record's own derivations are not machine-checked.*

The pages run from what the gate is, through how it arises and what it predicts, to what is open.

- [Dark matter as the dual Standard Model: the Minic precedent](dual-standard-model.md) comes first, because the published proposal precedes the program's own statement of the identification: Djordje Minic and coauthors have proposed since 2019 that dark matter is the dual of the Standard Model in a doubled, Born-reciprocal formulation of string theory. The page sets their proposal beside the program's, element by element: the same identification in substance, or a close variant on a stricter reading of the conjecture; another phase space; couplings beyond gravity in most of their papers, beyond leading order in the string length and of a strength no paper computes; and neither of the program's two forms. It then lists where else their papers meet the program, among them a symmetry breaking tied to the choice of polarization placed in the vacuum, what was not found, and the related work outside the group.
- [The velocity gate](velocity-gate.md) comes next. It gives the self-interaction amplitude M(k, q) = 4πα̃m_χ/(q² + m_φ²)·F(k²/μ²) and its symbols, explains why a force local in position depends, in the Born approximation, only on the momentum transfer q, while the gate F depends on the incoming relative momentum k, and records two unreconciled ways the sources write the Gaussian. It summarizes what the gate does and what is open, and keeps the lab notebook's v0.1 Gaussian postulate as a superseded section.
- [Dual stress T̃](dual-stress.md) defines the quantity through which matter couples to X, the momentum-side counterpart of stress-energy, built from momentum derivatives of the χ bilinear. It comes before the mechanism because the record's mechanism is exchange of X between two dual stresses. The page sets the definition beside stress-energy as the charge of gravity [T], records how ordinary matter's dual stress dropped out of the argument for its safety, and keeps [the handoff](../../history/documents/handoff.md)'s chat-only reading of the dual stress as a second gravitational charge.
- [The gate mechanism: X exchange over fiber overlap](gate-mechanism.md) gives the record's derivation of the gate step by step: integrating out the massive X leaves a contact interaction, which acts only over the overlap of two χ quanta's wavepackets along the [momentum fiber](../../phase-space/reciprocity/momentum-fibers.md#quanta-with-a-width-along-the-fiber), with μ the width of a χ quantum in momentum. It sets out the standard background (integrating out a heavy carrier, form factors, wavepacket overlap), the correspondence with the form factor of a nucleus under the exchange of position and momentum, the downgrade that rational tails force, and the reading of μ in [conjugate special relativity](../../conjugate-relativity/_index.md); superseded and chat-only forms of the mechanism are folded in. The page then reports the gate-mechanism note of 2026-10-28 (two derivations, the second blind; not reviewed): each χ–χ–X vertex depends only on the momentum transfer, so exchanging the X of [#11 §7](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton) gives a dependence on the relative momentum that is a polynomial with velocity scale c, 2.4×10⁻⁷ at 120 km/s where the gate is 0.61; #6's fiber overlap, written out in the note's two readings, is either an ordinary short-range potential of range about 10 pm or a pair-channel interaction that contains no X. Flag 55, pending, proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism; [#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate) tags the mechanism [D].
- [What could carry a relative-velocity gate](gate-carriers.md) asks the general question behind the mechanism and behind P.R.'s long-range variant: can any field carry an interaction that switches off above a relative speed of about 120 km/s? Exchange of a field of spin J gives a polynomial of degree J in the relative Lorentz factor, which grows with speed on the scale c; no-go theorems close massless higher spins and finitely many massive ones; a tower of every spin needs cancellations between terms about 10^5421169 times the net; a medium gates velocity through the medium, not relative velocity; and a massless continuous-spin particle has the right shape but a threshold that falls with distance.
- [The gated scattering law σ_T(v)](scattering-law.md) is the observable consequence: σ_T(v) = σ₀F(v²/v\*²), with a knee at the gate velocity v\*, isotropic scattering below it, and a tail whose shape diagnoses the vertex. After [the transfer cross-section](../halo-astrophysics/transfer-cross-section.md) [T], it shows how the lab notebook obtained its law, the two signatures it claimed, the downgrade from an exponential cliff to a knee with row 1 before and after, the three predictions-table rows that test the law, and its form when χ is a classical wave. The v0.1 cliff is kept as a superseded section.
- [The scattering benchmark](benchmark.md) fixes the gate's strength from dwarf-galaxy cores, giving m_χ ≈ 70 MeV·α̃^{2/3}(σ₀/m ÷ 30 cm²g⁻¹)^{−1/3}. It explains where the input comes from, how the relation follows with the assumption m_φ ~ m_χ, the Born caveat at α̃ ~ 1, and the coupling that the dwarf cores determine. At that coupling the clumping calculation finds that collective clumping of χ particles fails under the program's [contact-range interaction](../momentum-clumping/founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests), which acts only over microscopic distances. The lab notebook's predictions above the gate, which rest on a Gaussian tail and were never machine-checked, are kept as a superseded section.
- [Open: the gate shape (OP4)](gate-shape.md) asks whether the gate cuts off as a Gaussian or with a power-law tail. The answer lies in the X propagator on the momentum fibers and the χ quantum's profile there, neither computed, and the gate-mechanism note finds that such a propagator has no meaning within [#11 §7](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton) and that the Gaussian-against-rational split is not a dichotomy of gates in k (flag 55); it is observable in the knee's tail, which rows 1 and 3 read, and the checkpoint counts it among the program's [degeneration risks](../../program/assessment.md).
- [Open: the dual-stress enhancement (OP5)](dual-stress-enhancement.md) asks why χ scatters off itself with a nuclear-scale cross-section while ordinary matter couples to the same X only at gravitational strength. The checkpoint calls it the main open quantitative item; no source quantifies the gap or proposes a mechanism.
