*The dual Standard Model is a proposal made by Djordje Minic and coauthors since 2019: every particle of the Standard Model has a dual partner in a doubled, [Born-reciprocal](../../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md) formulation of string theory, and the dual partners are the dark matter. In its central identification it is P.R.'s core conjecture, that [dark matter is conjugate matter](_index.md), or a close variant of it if the conjecture includes the program's single phase space and coupling by gravity alone; either way it was published before the program, as a proposal, and it is not derived. The two part ways in the space the dual lives in, a doubled phase space rather than the momentum side of one; in how the dual sector meets ours, beyond gravity in most of the group's papers, though only beyond leading order in the string length and with a strength no paper computes; and in everything that gives the program's dark matter an observable shape: neither of its two forms was found, in the group's papers or outside them. The group's earlier papers also contain much of the program's geometric setting, though not its central move, the reading of the choice of position as a spontaneously broken symmetry with an order parameter. Prior art says who stated an idea first; it does not bear on whether the idea is right.*

**Status:** Established — a proposal of the literature, with live-searched citations [S], summarized from [`notes/dual-sector.md`](../../../notes/dual-sector.md) (read and verified by independent agents and weighed by a panel; not reviewed). [The dual-sector script](../../method/verification/dual-sector-script.md) checks the note's quotations, citations, dates and counts against its record; it does not test the agents' judgments of how close a paper comes to the conjecture.

## The proposal

The identification is written as a proposal in [Minic 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.00318) (§6.2): "The proposal here is that the dual sector (as already indicated in the previous section) should be interpreted as the dark matter sector, which is correlated to the visible sector via the dark energy sector". [Berglund, Hübsch and Minić 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.15610) state it in their abstract: "dark matter, on the other hand, comes from the degrees of freedom dual to the visible matter". [Edmonds, Minic and Takeuchi 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.12763) (§4), with SM for the Standard Model: "Thus the entire SM would have a dual SM (which we propose, describes the dark matter sector)." [Berglund, Hübsch and Minic 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06086) (§6.4) say that "the dual particles are conjugate to the Standard Model particles in analogy with the canonical fact that momenta p are conjugate to coordinates q". The group restates it through April 2026 ([Hübsch and Minic 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06207); [Hübsch and Minic 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19418)). Every statement is a proposal ("could be interpreted", "we propose", "candidates"); none is derived.

**When.** The earliest public statement found is Minic's invited talk at the 10th Mathematical Physics Meeting in Belgrade on 9 September 2019. Its handwritten slides are public ([the talk's slides](https://www.mphys10.ipb.ac.rs/slides/Minic.pdf)), and slide 5 reads "Each particle of the Standard Model has a 'dual' particle", then "dual particles → dual Standard Model (hidden sector?)" (read from the images of the slides, which set the text in capitals). The first written statement is Minic 2020, the talk's proceedings, posted on arXiv on 29 February 2020. A paper of Freidel, Leigh and Minic published in October 2019 ([doi:10.1142/S0217751X19410045](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0217751X19410045)) is closed access and unread; it could hold an earlier written statement, though not an earlier public one. The group's earlier papers proposed something narrower: that non-local quanta of a quantum-gravity theory make up dark matter ([Chang, Minic, Sun and Takeuchi 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.04361); [Edmonds, Farrah, Minic, Ng and Takeuchi 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.04388): "We expect that such non-local excitations (as part of a covariant non-commutative formulation, as implied by the metastring [131, 132]) can serve as unusual dark matter quanta."). The program's first written statement of its own conjecture is the [lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) of September 2026; a restatement by Hübsch and Minic posted on 14 October 2026 ([arXiv:2610.19522](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19522)) is later.

**What it contains.** One dual partner for every field of the Standard Model, among them a dual photon; the papers state no charges of the duals. The dual sector gravitates, and in the group's construction the size of the dual space sets Newton's constant and its curvature the cosmological constant ([Berglund, Hübsch and Minić 2019](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08269)).

## What "dual" means there

The group uses "dual" in three senses, and the dark sector is built in the second.

- **Momentum, rescaled, in one phase space.** In the group's account of quantum mechanics the dual coordinate is x̃ = p/ε, the momentum p divided by a scale ε, with λε = ℏ for a length λ, ℏ being the reduced Planck constant ([Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01829)). This is the program's sense of "conjugate": the other [Lagrangian polarization](../../phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations.md) of one phase space, exchanged with position by [Born reciprocity](../../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md).
- **A doubled phase space at the string length.** Where the dark sector is built, x̃ is a second coordinate, the difference of a string's left- and right-moving coordinates, which fails to commute with x at the scale λ², λ the fundamental (string) length, and has a momentum p̃ of its own: a phase space of twice the dimension of the program's, as in [double field theory](../../phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory.md). Two agents computed the doubling from the papers' brackets, and [the dual-sector script](../../method/verification/dual-sector-script.md#what-it-checks) checks the algebra. The dark fields are backgrounds of p̃, which become "dark matter" fields on ordinary spacetime "after integrating over the dual spacetime" (Hübsch and Minic 2024 §3.3).
- **Conjugacy in field space.** "That is, the dual fields do not commute with Standard Model fields, and the two form an effective Heisenberg algebra." (Berglund, Hübsch and Minic 2023, §6.4). The authors induce it from the commutator of x and x̃ and call the reasoning beyond the rules of effective field theory.

The group says the first sense becomes the second: the description with x̃ = p/ε "will appear in what follows in the metastring formulation of string theory" (Hübsch and Minic 2024 §3.2). The program works only in the first: its conjugate matter χ is matter in the other polarization of one phase space (x, p), with the [symplectic form](../../phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form.md) rigid ([P2′](../../program/postulates/p2-prime.md), [P4](../../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md)).

## How the dual sector meets ours

- **At leading order, only gravity.** "These fields are in general correlated, due to their non-commutativity. However, to first order in the fundamental length, we can treat them as independent." (Berglund, Hübsch and Minic 2023, §6.3). In Minic 2020 (§5) the effective action at zeroth order in λ is a sum of the visible and dual Lagrangians on one metric.
- **Beyond it, couplings beyond gravity.** Minic 2020 (§5): "dark matter quanta should be detectable through their particular metaparticle entanglement to visible matter", a metaparticle being, in the group's account, a particle and its dual together. Berglund, Hübsch and Minić 2021 write mixing terms between visible and dual fields: "This correlation becomes invisible in effective field theory", and it vanishes as λ → 0, but "Note that with a particular double scaling, this term can be finite!" (§4); in their dual quantum electrodynamics (QED), "In the dual QED sector, we should find a dual photon that is correlated to the visible photon, and that is distinct from the dark photon of effective field theory." (§5). No paper computes the strength of any of these terms.
- **Gravity alone below a very high energy, in one paper.** Hübsch and Minic 2024 (§5.3) put the mixing above a decoupling energy "below which the dark and visible matter interact as required, only gravitationally", about 4.5×10¹⁴ GeV. Their papers of 2026 do not repeat it, and none says which position is current.
- **A galactic regularity.** The group's older model, Modified Dark Matter, ties a galaxy's dark matter to its baryons through an acceleration scale a₀ of the order of cH₀, the speed of light times the Hubble constant; in its own papers this is an observed regularity fitted in ordinary general relativity, "Despite its appearance, Eq. (18) does not modify General Relativity (GR) since the derivation of the right-hand-side is based on GR" ([Edmonds et al. 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00662)), and the later papers offer it as evidence for the dual sector.

The program's sectors couple by gravity alone: no gauge force crosses the polarizations ([P4](../../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md)), except in the [shared-photon form](../../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md#shared-photon) of the magnetic mirror, in which the momentum sector's magnetic analogs would carry magnetic charge of our own photon, a form that needs P4 restated.

**Detection.** Both explain why dark matter has not been detected. Minic 2020 (§5): "If the quanta of modified dark matter are metaparticles, this may explain why, so far, dark matter detection experiments have failed to detect dark matter particles." But the group expects detection, through the entanglement, through correlated events at accelerators, and in tests "at colliders, dark matter direct detection experiments and indirect detection experiments" (Edmonds et al. 2017), where the program requires a [permanent null](../../predictions/direct-detection-null.md) in every non-gravitational experiment.

## Further overlaps with the program

The group's earlier papers also contain much of the program's geometric setting, though not its central move. None of these overlaps is one of P.R.'s other conjectures, and #11 credits several of them to the same authors in another form.

- **The split made dynamical.** Freidel, Leigh and Minic call general relativity's split of phase space kinematical and propose to make it dynamical: "the purely kinematical splitting of phase space into space and momentum space has to be made dynamical" ([Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.3949), §1.3). Their formulation does not contain the split, and the solutions of string theory carry it.
- **A breaking in the vacuum.** [Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman, Leigh and Minic 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00802) place a symmetry breaking tied to the choice of polarization in the vacuum, "The symmetry breaking happens at the level of the vacuum sector, and thus a choice of ground state fixes a value of" the parameter that says which commuting combination of x and x̃ is spacetime, and "We now propose that the expansion of the Universe can act as a mechanism that selects the decoupling frame". The broken symmetry is a dual Lorentz symmetry that is not a canonical transformation; it is not ρ, the program's [Born rotation](../../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md#the-map), which preserves the symplectic form ω. Berglund, Hübsch and Minic 2023 (§4.3) say that within a sector of fixed non-zero p̃ that group "is effectively spontaneously broken".
- **The space of choices.** The space U(d)/O(d) of choices of η that preserve ω and the vacuum is named in Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2016, with no potential on it: the manifold over which P2′'s order parameter ranges ([the Lagrangian Grassmannian](../../phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian.md)). The structure η = HJ of P2′ is theirs from 2013 ([Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2014, Phys. Lett. B](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.7080)), and that the other polarization is equally available is stated about the theory, not as a choice of vacuum ([Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman, Leigh and Minic 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.10821); Berglund, Hübsch and Minic 2023). None of these papers gives a potential, an order parameter or a [Goldstone angle](../../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle.md). The spontaneous breaking of the exact, ω-preserving ρ with η as order parameter, the program's [hard core](../../program/hard-core.md) and its [toy models](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md), was not found ([broken Born duality](../../phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality.md); flag 70, pending).
- **The compatibility theorem.** The positive form of [the compatibility theorem](../../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md), that the positive generalized metric compatible with ω and η is H = diag(q, q⁻¹) with q positive, is stated in group language in Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2016 (§2), without the block proof; the proof and battery test T1 are the program's (flag 71, pending).
- **The inverse scaling behind a·ã = 1.** A symplectic map that "squeezes the modular cell along the momentum direction and stretches it in the space direction" (Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2016 §1.4), its tie to the cosmic scale factor (Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman, Leigh and Minic 2021), and a conversion of our volume into the dual one through the uncertainty relation δx δx̃ ∼ λ² ([Hur, Jejjala, Kavic, Minic and Takeuchi 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20854)) are related to the program's [cosmological constraint](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint.md) a·ã = 1, by which the scale factor ã of the momentum-space description shrinks as our scale factor a grows. The program derives the constraint from the rigidity of ω in one phase space (flag 72, pending).
- **Dark energy.** Hur et al. 2025 publish both signs of 1 + w: under the group's earlier ultraviolet–infrared relation "Clearly, w(a)<-1 for all a as anticipated", w(a) being w as a function of the scale factor, and the model is disfavoured by the second release of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument ([DESI](../../cosmology/dark-energy/desi.md)); under an alternative, w(a) ≥ −1. The sign follows from an assumed relation, so the group makes no fixed prediction where the program requires w ≥ −1 ([row 4](../../predictions/dark-energy-drift.md)). Berglund, Hübsch and Minić 2019 integrate the doubled gravitational action over x̃, and "the curvature of the canonically conjugate dual space provides for a positive cosmological constant to leading order": a published treatment of the dual Einstein–Hilbert term that #11 does not derive (flag 49), with the opposite assignment to [#11 §10](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)'s "dual curvature ↦ negative dark radiation" (flag 72, pending).
- **Newton's constant and curved momentum space.** Newton's constant "can be considered as a universal conversion factor between the seemingly different concepts of space and momentum space" (Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2014 §1.2), beside [#13](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md)'s [maximum force](../../conjugate-relativity/maximum-force.md), which is built from the same constant; they state no maximum force. And "not only will space-time become curved, but momentum space as well" (Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2014, Phys. Lett. B), an idea they trace to Born, beside [curved momentum space](../../phase-space/reciprocity/curved-momentum-space.md); the program's form of it, [the momentum graviton X](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), a massive spin-2 field odd under reciprocity, has no counterpart in the group (flag 72, pending).
- **Two descriptions and a toy model.** The map from a string's coordinates to their duals "corresponds to the string Fourier transformation" ([Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.08005) §2), beside the program's [one state in two descriptions](../../program/conjugate-universe.md), in the doubled setting. Minic 2020 (§6) uses the Grosse–Wulkenhaar model for the radiative stability of the cosmological constant, a model the program's [toy model II](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/_index.md) uses in its self-dual form; no paper of the group breaks the model's Langmann–Szabo symmetry, an exchange of x and p, spontaneously.
- **The shape of the monopole explanation.** The group's explanation of dark matter's non-detection by the non-local or dual nature of its quanta has the shape of P.R.'s conjecture that [conjugate monopoles explain the null result](../../electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md) of monopole searches, applied to dark matter rather than to monopoles.

## What was not found

- A self-interaction of the dual sector that depends on relative velocity or on the overlap of momenta, the program's [velocity gate](velocity-gate.md): none of the 41 full texts swept mentions relative velocity, and the one interaction law between metaparticles the group gives is static. A cross-section that falls with velocity has ordinary published mechanisms, among them finite-size dark matter, whose cross-section "decreases with the velocity" ([Chu, Garcia-Cely and Murayama 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.00075)); what is not found is the gate's mechanism.
- Magnetically charged objects localized in momentum, or in the dual coordinate, as dark matter: [P.R.'s conjecture of monopoles abundant in momentum space](../candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md).
- The identification joined to a permanent null. Hübsch and Minic 2024 join it to coupling by gravity alone at every accessible energy, but name an astrophysical signature of the dual sector, and the group expects detection.
- The spontaneous breaking of the exact, ω-preserving ρ with η as order parameter and a potential (above).
- Any of P.R.'s other conjectures in the group's papers: the overlaps above are with the program's setting and results, and the nearest published form of one of those conjectures is outside the group (below).

## Outside the group

No one outside the group has published the identification itself. [Ran 2025](https://doi.org/10.55277/researchhub.unu7fof1.1), an independent report posted on 24 December 2025, puts the origin of dark matter in the coupling of a quantum state's space–time and momentum–energy descriptions: "Here we show that dark matter arises naturally as a structural consequence of the coupling between these two dynamical sectors", "not a new particle species"; it never makes dark matter the counterpart of ordinary matter. Others place dark matter in another dual space coupled to ours by gravity: dark matter of negative mass that "is assumed to reside in" a dual de Sitter space with imaginary time "and therefore is principally invisible" ([Hauser and Dröscher 2019](https://doi.org/10.1515/zna-2018-0559), read in its abstract only); a sector of a dual isospin spacetime ([Nishimura 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.13131)). None of these makes dark matter the counterpart of ordinary matter, and none of the dual spaces is the momentum side of one phase space. A sweep of conference abstracts, slides and proceedings from 2015 to 2026 found the identification, in its phase-space sense, only in the group's own talks, and neither form; talks on mirror matter state a dual of ordinary matter as dark matter under parity, and Winn's (below) under electric–magnetic duality.

Nearer to another of P.R.'s conjectures, [Winn 2012](https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4590) and his talks of 2025 and 2026 ([Lepton-Photon 2025](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1493037/contributions/6549576)) give every charged particle of the Standard Model a magnetically charged partner under our own photon, and propose the partners' magnetically neutral bound states as dark matter. For the shared-photon form of [the magnetic mirror](../../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md) this is a partial overlap, the strongest of Winn's, with one analog per charged particle, our own photon and hiding by binding, but without the placement in momentum, the conjecture's defining clause (flag 73, pending). The partners are elementary particles, not solitons, and nothing is placed in momentum or phase space. His "Dual (effectively dark) sector particle" for each particle of the Standard Model is related to the identification as well, under an electric–magnetic, chirality and spin duality rather than in phase space.

## Which reading of the conjecture

Whether the published identification is P.R.'s conjecture itself or a close variant depends on what the conjecture includes. If any dual or conjugate counterpart of ordinary matter counts, its core is published. If the conjecture includes the other polarization of the program's single phase space at ℏ and coupling by gravity alone, the group's proposal is a close variant, and the core is published only in a weaker sense. No source can settle which reading is the conjecture, and the record leaves it open ([the literature check](../../method/literature-check.md); [`notes/literature-check.md` §2](../../../notes/literature-check.md#2-the-verdicts)).

## Limits

"Not found" means not found by these searches. Not read: the paper of October 2019, which is closed access, a 2026 book of Hübsch and Minić beyond its chapter abstracts, and Freidel, Leigh and Minic's 2015 essay "Modular spacetime". Talks of 2018 and 2019 with no surviving material could hold a public statement earlier by up to nine months, without changing who published it. Literature not in English was not searched.
