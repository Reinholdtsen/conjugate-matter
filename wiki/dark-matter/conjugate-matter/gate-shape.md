*The gate shape is the exact form of the function F that switches conjugate matter's self-interaction off at large relative velocity. A Gaussian F falls off faster than any power of the velocity; a rational F falls off as a power law. Which one holds depends on how the momentum graviton X propagates along the momentum directions of phase space, and that has not been computed. The question is open, and it is observable, because the tail of the knee in the scattering law is the same function.*

**Status:** Open — [O] (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md` §14 item 4) — no calculation exists; the checkpoint counts "gate tail not yet forced" among the program's degeneration risks (§15). Listed in [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The open item.** [The checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint): "Gate shape (X propagator on momentum fibers; Gaussian vs rational — now observable) [O]" (#11 §14 item 4). It qualifies [the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate), whose mechanism is derived and whose shape is not.
- **Where the shape lives.** "The gate *shape* is the X propagator's structure on the momentum fibers — open [O]" (`notes/debt1a-geometric-core.md` §E). The propagator is that of the massive [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton).
- **The two candidates.** "Wavepacket overlap of Gaussian profiles gives exactly e^{−(p₁−p₂)²/2μ²}"; but "tree-level exchange with generic vertices yields rational (power-law-tailed) gates; the Gaussian requires the Gaussian fiber profile" (`notes/debt1a-ii-X-classification.md` §4). The same note ties the Gaussian case to "the metastring's modular vertex structure" (§5). See [the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism).
- **Why it is observable.** The Debt-1 close-out: "shape completion open (Gaussian vs rational tail — now also an observable discriminant)" (`notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md` §5). In [the gated scattering law](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law) the tail "(Gaussian vs rational) diagnoses the vertex completion" (#11 §8), which is what [row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee) would measure.
- **What depends on it.** [The lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s cluster prediction, "σ_T/m (1500 km/s) ≈ 30 e^{−156} ≈ 0" (#1 §5.1), assumes the Gaussian tail; see [the scattering benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark). The particle side of [row 3, the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant), is a sharp cutoff; the sources do not ask what a rational tail would do to that diagnostic.
- **Its history.** In v0.1 the gate was postulated and the shape listed as open: "obtain e^{−k²/μ²} (or its true form) from S_H + S_mix rather than postulating it. The σ(v) *cliff* is robust to the exact shape; the sub-v\* profile is not" (#1 §11 item 2). Since the v0.4 downgrade, the steepness of the cutoff, which v0.1 took as robust, is part of the open question.
- **Its weight in the assessment.** "gate tail not yet forced" is one of the degeneration risks in the checkpoint's [assessment](/wiki/program/assessment) (#11 §15).
