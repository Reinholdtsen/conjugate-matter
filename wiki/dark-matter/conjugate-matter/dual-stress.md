*The dual stress, written T̃, is the quantity through which matter couples to the momentum graviton X, as the stress-energy tensor is the quantity through which matter couples to the ordinary graviton. Stress-energy is built from spacetime derivatives of a matter field and describes how energy and momentum are distributed in space; the dual stress is built from momentum derivatives of the field, and so reflects how the field is structured across momentum. Exchange of X between the dual stresses of two conjugate particles produces [the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate). The calc notes define the dual stress in words, with no explicit formula, and why it is strong for conjugate matter but weak for ordinary matter is an open problem.*

**Status:** Alive — [D — structural] (`notes/debt1a-geometric-core.md` §E) — a structural definition with no explicit formula in any source, not machine-checked; its enhancement for conjugate matter is open (#11 §14 item 5).

## Key facts

- **Definition.** "Conjugate matter couples to H universally (P4); its coupling to the X-block is through the **dual stress** T̃ — moments involving momentum-derivatives of the χ bilinear. Exchange of X between two such bilinears yields a four-point amplitude that depends on the **incoming relative momenta** of the pair — the variable no x-local mediator can produce, and exactly the variable the gate was postulated in" (the first [Debt-1 calc note](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes), `notes/debt1a-geometric-core.md` §E). H is the generalized metric of phase space and P4 is [the polarized-matter postulate](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter).
- **In the checkpoint.** [The checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) states the gate as "delivered by X-exchange between dual-stress bilinears" (#11 §8); the derivation is on [the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism).
- **Ordinary matter's dual stress.** For the Standard Model (SM), the first Debt-1 note says "X is massive and couples to x-polarized matter only through its (suppressed) dual stress; short-range, fifth-force-safe — to be verified when the propagator exists [O]" (§E); the second, that "couplings to x-polarized (SM) matter run through the SM's dual stress, which is unenhanced" (`notes/debt1a-ii-X-classification.md` §4).
- **The argument moved to range.** The last Debt-1 note rests ordinary matter's safety on range instead: "safety is by **range**, not decoupling: X-mediated forces on the SM are Yukawa-cut at 1/M_X" (`notes/debt1a-iv-beta-coefficients.md` §3). See [safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety).
- **The open quantity.** The same note: "[Parametrics of the χ dual-stress enhancement — why σ₀ can be nuclear-scale while SM couplings stay gravitational — logged as the main open quantitative item.]" (§3); see [the dual-stress enhancement](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress-enhancement).
- **A chat-only reading.** [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) records, as a result that exists only in the old chat, "Two gravitational charges: ordinary stress-energy (sources massless graviton) vs dual stress 'how energy is structured across momentum' (sources X)" (`HANDOFF.md` §4); see [the chat-only page](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/chat-only-results). It is a conjecture, not a derived result.
