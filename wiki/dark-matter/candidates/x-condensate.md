*The X-condensate candidate is the proposal that dark matter is a coherent, spatially uniform oscillation of X, the program's momentum graviton: the massive spin-2 field that the broken reciprocity adds to gravity. If it holds, dark matter and the hidden half of the geometry are one field rather than two separate things. The idea was logged during the Debt-1 work as a direction deliberately left unopened, became one of three dark-matter candidates in version 2.0 of the checkpoint, and has been one of two since the third was retired. The record holds its profile and its history, not a calculation; like the χ-particle candidate, its abundance is unexplained.*

**Status:** Alive — one of two candidates; #11 §12 tags the pair "[D — framework; each candidate O]". Its lineage, [bigravity dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/bigravity-dark-matter), is cited from model memory [23†]. Nothing on this page is machine-checked.

## Key facts

- **The profile** (#11 §12, in the columns origin, stability, distinctive handle): "**X-condensate** | oscillating massive spin-2 (bigravity-DM lineage [23†]) | m_X-protected | unifies dark sector with geometry; coherent-field signatures; knee shape wave-like". DM there is dark matter.
- **The field it is made of.** X is [the momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton). "The polarization choice spontaneously breaks the dual diffs; X eats the four Goldstones and emerges massive, M_X = the polarization-breaking scale" (#11 §7.1 [D + T]; [the gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs)). #11 fixes no value for the mass: "Five βₙ → two parameters (M_X ∝ 2(β₁+β₂); one cubic shape)" (#11 §7.5 [D]; [spectrum and parameters](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity)).
- **Where it came from** (`notes/debt1a-ii-X-classification.md` §4): "A new door, deliberately left closed today: homogeneous oscillations of a massive spin-2 X are themselves a dark-matter candidate (cf. bigravity dark matter)." The note adds that the program "may not need χ as a separate field — X-condensate dark matter would unify the geometric sector with the dark sector. Logged as an open direction, not pursued. [O]"
- **Status history.** The lab notebook's addendum v0.4 records "A new open direction: massive-X condensate as the dark matter itself"; the first consolidation, #9, lists it as an open problem: "X-condensate as the dark matter itself (bigravity-DM-style), potentially eliminating χ and unifying the dark sector with the geometry [O — direction, deliberately unopened]" (#9 §10 item 7). #11 v2.0 made it one of three candidates; since v2.1 it is one of two ([the retired soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)).
- **Its tests.** The null in [row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null) covers "both dark-sector candidates". [Row 11](/wiki/predictions/dark-sector-microstructure) separates "Particles vs condensate" "via rows 1–3 + coherence signatures"; it is a "Diagnostic", and the discrimination is open, #11 §14 item 9 [O].
- **What is open.** "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis) applies to both" (#11 §12; [polarization-genesis](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis)). The cosmological stability of a heavy spin-2 field at the equal Planck masses that reciprocity imposes is also open ([stability at equal Planck masses](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/equal-planck-mass-stability)).
- **Not the same condensate.** On the wave island of Debt 3, wave χ can condense into Bose stars ([wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics)). Those are condensates of χ; the X-condensate is a state of X.
