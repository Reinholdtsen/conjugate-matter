*The χ-particle candidate is the proposal that [dark matter](../_index.md) consists of particles of [conjugate matter χ](../conjugate-matter/_index.md): matter in the momentum-polarized representation, the mirror image of ordinary, position-polarized matter. χ gravitates exactly like ordinary matter, so it falls, orbits and bends light like standard cold dark matter, but no gauge force of ordinary matter acts on it. Its own self-interaction works only between particles with nearly equal velocities — the [velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md) — which predicts strong self-scattering in slow [dwarf galaxies](../halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md), none in fast galaxy clusters, and a knee in between. It is one of the program's three [dark-matter candidates](_index.md), and one of the two forms of P.R.'s core conjecture that dark matter is conjugate matter; its stability is assumed rather than derived, and its abundance is unexplained.*

**Status:** Alive — one of three candidates; [#11 §12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) tags the table of candidates "[D — framework; each candidate O]". Its gravitational behaviour is [D] ([`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §4](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#4-consistency-with-everything-already-measured)); its gate is "[D — mechanism; shape [O]]" ([#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate)), a mechanism that [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) (2026-10-28; two derivations, the second blind; not reviewed) finds not derived: flag 55 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism ([the gate mechanism](../conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)). [`verify/gate_mechanism.py`](../../../verify/gate_mechanism.py) recomputes the benchmark's σ₀/m and reruns that note's arithmetic and algebra, and does not test its physics ([the gate-mechanism script](../../method/verification/gate-mechanism-script.md)); nothing else on this page is machine-checked.

## The candidate

[Checkpoint #11](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) answers the question it poses in plain terms, "what is dark matter, fundamentally?", with a table of three candidates, each described by its origin, what makes it stable, and the observable that would single it out. The χ row reads:

| Candidate | Origin | Stability | Distinctive handle |
|---|---|---|---|
| **χ particles** (§8) | p-polarized matter field | assumed | knee at v\*, sharp tail; isotropic scattering; permanent direct-detection null |

The tag on the table, "[D — framework; each candidate O]", separates two things. The framework that produces χ, a second matter representation coupled to gravity and to nothing else of ours, is derived from [the postulates](../../program/postulates/_index.md). Whether dark matter is in fact made of χ is open. The other rows are [the X-condensate](x-condensate.md) and P.R.'s [conjugate monopoles](momentum-space-monopoles.md), magnetically charged topological solitons of the same p-polarized sector, localized in momentum; all three share the open question of [the 5.4 : 1 abundance ratio](polarization-genesis.md).

## Dark matter as conjugate matter

χ is one form of P.R.'s founding supposition 4, "Dark matter is conjugate matter", which #11 records as "**alive**, P.R.'s core conjecture": as χ particles (x-clumping, gated self-force) or as conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, two of the three candidates of [§12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) ([App. A item 4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates); [founding suppositions and their fates](../../program/founding-suppositions.md#4-dark-matter-is-conjugate-matter)). Conjugate matter is matter of the momentum-polarized representation (next section). The two forms differ in where they are localized. χ particles cluster in position, as cold dark matter does, and their self-interaction is gated in relative velocity. The conjugate monopoles would be localized in momentum by their topology; that they are is an open conjecture, which the one model built of it, [#14](../../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md)'s, never tested ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](momentum-space-monopoles.md)). The other candidate, the X-condensate, would be a state of the geometric field X rather than conjugate matter ([#11 §12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model): "unifies dark sector with geometry").

## The field χ

χ is introduced by postulate [P4](../../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md): "Matter comes in x-polarized (Standard Model) and p-polarized (χ) representations; both couple universally to H; gauge forces do not cross polarizations" [P]. Here H is [the generalized metric](../../phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md) on phase space, and a polarization is the choice of which half of phase-space coordinates counts as position ([Lagrangian polarizations](../../phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations.md)). Ordinary matter is local in position; χ is matter organized the other way round, local in momentum, hence "conjugate matter" ([conjugate matter and the velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/_index.md)).

#11 takes P4 strictly: "no kinetic mixing between the sectors beyond what gravity induces, which vanishes if hidden charge conjugation is exact and otherwise first appears at six loops, ε ≲ 10⁻¹³ [27S]" ([#11 §4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates); [round-3 Decision 1](../../method/review/round-3/decisions.md)). No Standard Model (SM) force reaches χ.

[The lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) leaves the spin open: "χ (take a complex scalar or Dirac fermion; nothing below depends on spin) propagates on g — it gravitates exactly like ordinary matter — but its self-interaction is routed through the g̃ block" ([#1 §3](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#3-action)). On the program's vacuum, H splits into a spacetime metric g and a momentum-space metric g̃ ([#1 §3](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#3-action)). χ moves on the first and interacts with itself through the second. That sentence divides the two things this page says of χ. "Local in momentum" describes its polarization and its self-interaction, whose gate the notebook calls "the imprint of momentum-space locality" ([#1 §3](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#3-action)). Its clustering in position follows from g: χ particles cluster in position, the "x-clumping" of [#11 App. A item 4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates). [#13](../../papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md) labels conjugate quanta differently, and no document reconciles the two descriptions ([the invariant length against the checkpoint's kinematics](../../conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell.md#open-the-invariant-length-against-the-checkpoints-kinematics)).

## How χ gravitates [D]

P4 couples both kinds of matter universally to H, and on the vacuum that means to the same spacetime metric g. In the notebook's form of the postulate, "the equivalence principle holds across polarizations" ([#1 §1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#1-postulates), P4; [the equivalence principle](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/equivalence-principle.md)). χ therefore follows the same free-fall trajectories as ordinary matter, and its energy curves spacetime in the same way. The notebook draws the consequence [D]: "χ couples to g universally: it falls, orbits, and lenses like ordinary dark matter. Bullet Cluster lensing-centroid behavior is automatic (and see Prediction 1 for the scattering side)" ([#1 §4](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#4-consistency-with-everything-already-measured)).

[The Bullet Cluster](../halo-astrophysics/bullet-cluster.md) is a collision of two galaxy clusters in which the mass seen by gravitational lensing sits with the galaxies, offset from the hot gas that collided and slowed [T]. The dark matter passed through with little scattering. For χ the lensing half is the gravitational coupling above; the scattering half is the gate, which is closed at cluster speeds (next section). [#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate) keeps both: "χ propagates on g (equivalence principle; Bullet-Cluster-safe)". The established background is on the Bullet Cluster's own page and on [self-interacting dark matter](../halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter.md).

## How χ scatters off itself: the gate and the knee

The self-interaction is where χ differs from cold dark matter. [#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate) gives its nonrelativistic amplitude [D — mechanism; shape [O]]:

```text
M(k, q) = 4πα̃ m_χ / (q² + m_φ²) · F(k²/μ²)
```

Here q is the momentum transfer, k "the incoming *relative* momentum", α̃ the coupling, m_χ the χ mass and m_φ the mass of the carrier of the momentum transfer. The first factor is an ordinary exchange amplitude in the transfer q, contact-like in position when the mediator is heavy (m_φ ≳ m_χ, [#1 §5.1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#51-the-scattering-law-d)). The second, the gate F, cuts the interaction off once the two particles move too fast relative to each other. Its argument, the relative momentum k, is "the variable no x-local mediator produces" ([#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate)). The scale μ is "the χ quantum's intrinsic momentum-fiber width" ([the velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md)).

In [#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate) the mechanism is exchange of [the momentum graviton X](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md) "between dual-stress bilinears". [Calc note #6](../../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md) explains why it depends on k: momentum-polarized quanta are extended along the [momentum fiber](../../phase-space/reciprocity/momentum-fibers.md#quanta-with-a-width-along-the-fiber) with width μ, "and they exchange X only over their fiber overlap" ([#6 §4](../../../notes/x-classification.md#4-consequences-downstream-d--structural-with-one-honest-downgrade); [the gate mechanism](../conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md)). Two particles with very different velocities have wavepackets that do not overlap in momentum, much as two signals in separate frequency bands share no spectral overlap. [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) finds that this account does not hold for the X that [#11 §7](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton) builds: each vertex depends only on the momentum transfer, so exchanging X gives a velocity dependence with the speed of light as its scale, and the fiber overlap, written out in the two ways the note reconstructs, gives either an ordinary short-range potential or an interaction that contains no X (§§2–3; [the fiber overlap, read two ways](../conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#the-fiber-overlap-read-two-ways)). The gate itself is not refuted; flag 55, pending, proposes returning it to a postulate ([the gate mechanism](../conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)).

The consequence for the [momentum-transfer cross-section](../halo-astrophysics/transfer-cross-section.md) is "σ_T(v) = σ₀F(v²/v\*²): robust knee at v\*" ([#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate); [the gated scattering law](../conjugate-matter/scattering-law.md)). Below the knee velocity v\*, χ scatters with cross-section σ₀; above it, scattering is suppressed. The shape of the tail above the knee, "(Gaussian vs rational)", "diagnoses the vertex completion", and the scattering is "isotropic below the gate versus Yukawa forward-peaking" ([the light-mediator limit](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces.md#the-light-mediator-limit-forward-peaking-and-the-coulomb-logarithm)). The benchmark, set by fitting the cores of dwarf galaxies ([#1 §5.1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#51-the-scattering-law-d)), is "m_χ ≈ 70 MeV·α̃^{2/3}(σ₀/m ÷ 30 cm²g⁻¹)^{−1/3}" ([the scattering benchmark](../conjugate-matter/benchmark.md)). Why σ₀ can be nuclear-scale while χ's couplings to ordinary matter stay gravitational is open: "Main open quantitative item: the dual-stress enhancement (nuclear-scale σ₀ with gravitational SM couplings) [O]" ([#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate); [the dual-stress enhancement](../conjugate-matter/dual-stress-enhancement.md)).

| [7](../../predictions/direct-detection-null.md), direct detection and colliders | "**Permanent null** (both dark-sector candidates SM-decoupled; P4 strict)" | "Any confirmed non-gravitational DM–SM coupling" |

DM is dark matter. Row 7's basis names two candidates, while §12 lists three (flag 63 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, proposes wording that counts all three). Rows 1 and 2 are the gate seen in halo dynamics: strong scattering in dwarf galaxies, where velocities are low, and none in clusters, where they are high. Two published fits bear on row 1 at cluster speeds: [Kaplinghat, Tulin and Yu 2016](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.03339), fitting halos from dwarfs to clusters, prefer a cross-section falling from about 2 cm²/g on galaxy scales to about 0.1 cm²/g on cluster scales, and [Sagunski et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.12515) find 0.19 ± 0.09 cm²/g in clusters, a mild preference over cold collisionless dark matter [S]. Both favour a nonzero value at cluster speeds, where row 1 gives particle χ none, and the first also a cross-section that falls with velocity, as the knee does, which the second finds consistent with its data; neither is the robust self-interaction at v ≫ v\* that kills the row ([the data situation](../../predictions/self-interaction-knee.md#the-data-situation); flag 69, pending). Row 7 follows from strict P4: with no gauge force crossing polarizations, a detector built of ordinary matter can register χ only through gravity. [#11 §15](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment) counts rows 1 and 7 among the program's near-term falsification exposure.

## Particle χ and wave χ

The table's "sharp tail" fixes which kind of χ the candidate is. The clumping problem found that χ behaves in one of two regimes, set by its mass ([momentum clumping](../momentum-clumping/_index.md)). Heavy χ is a gas of classical particles, the particle island; very light χ has so many particles per quantum state that it acts as a classical wave, the [wave island](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md), "3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV" in the notebook's addendum v0.2. The [classicality theorem](../momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem.md) separates them: collective momentum-space behaviour "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰ — collective momentum-space behavior and classical particles are mutually exclusive" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); flag 19 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) queries the gate regime in which that bound was evaluated, and finds the conclusion unchanged).

The two regimes give different knees. For particle χ the tail is the gate's own cutoff; for wave χ the suppression is "(v\*/σ)⁴" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [row 3](../../predictions/island-discriminant.md)). The candidate row of [#11 §12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) lists the sharp tail, so it describes particle χ, and row 1's "zero cluster signal" is also stated for "particle χ". The wave branch survives in the record with free couplings; its "tunable wave-island collective sector" is "quarantined" as a degeneration risk ([#11 §15](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment); [Scenario B: tunable streams](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b.md)), and the branch's relic abundance comes from misalignment ([relic abundance by misalignment on the wave island](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic.md)). [#11 §12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model)'s X-condensate row carries "knee shape wave-like". No document relates wave χ to the X-condensate.

## What the candidate does not claim

χ clusters in position, as cold dark matter does, not in velocity. The program's founding image is dark matter clumping in momentum space ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](../momentum-clumping/founding-image.md)). The calculations of the clumping problem tested it under the program's [contact-range interaction](../momentum-clumping/founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests), which acts only over microscopic distances, and found it "nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). The committed-wave case is Scenario A, which is under review ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md)). The image itself is P.R.'s open conjecture, and "Results (i)–(iii) stand as results" ([§9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested), whose three results are the particle island, the wave island and the net theorem). #11 describes the χ candidate with a "sharp tail" ([§12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model)), which marks particle χ, and as "x-clumping" ([App. A item 4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)); on the wiki's reading, the candidate so described does not include the founding image. For wave χ with the coupling free, clumping in velocity stays "optional, never required" ([§9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (ii)). Founding supposition 4, "Dark matter is conjugate matter", is "**alive**, P.R.'s core conjecture", with χ as one of its two forms ([above](#dark-matter-as-conjugate-matter)).
