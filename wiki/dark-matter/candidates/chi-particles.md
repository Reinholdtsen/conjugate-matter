*The χ-particle candidate is the proposal that dark matter consists of particles of conjugate matter χ: matter in the momentum-polarized representation, the mirror image of ordinary, position-polarized matter. χ gravitates exactly like ordinary matter, so it falls, orbits and bends light like standard cold dark matter, but no gauge force of ordinary matter acts on it. Its own self-interaction works only between particles with nearly equal velocities — the velocity gate — which predicts strong self-scattering in slow dwarf galaxies, none in fast galaxy clusters, and a knee in between. It is one of the program's two remaining dark-matter candidates; its stability is assumed rather than derived, and its abundance is unexplained.*

**Status:** Alive — one of two candidates; #11 §12 tags the pair "[D — framework; each candidate O]". Its gravitational behaviour is [D] (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §4); its gate is "[D — mechanism; shape [O]]" (#11 §8). Nothing on this page is machine-checked.

## Key facts

- **The profile** (#11 §12, in the columns origin, stability, distinctive handle): "**χ particles** (§8) | p-polarized matter field | assumed | knee at v\*, sharp tail; isotropic scattering; permanent direct-detection null".
- **The field.** Postulate [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter): "Matter comes in x-polarized (Standard Model) and p-polarized (χ) representations; both couple universally to H; gauge forces do not cross polarizations" [P; taken strictly since v2.1]. The lab notebook: "χ (take a complex scalar or Dirac fermion; nothing below depends on spin) propagates on g — it gravitates exactly like ordinary matter — but its self-interaction is routed through the g̃ block" (#1 §3).
- **Equivalence principle and lensing** [D] (#1 §4): "χ couples to g universally: it falls, orbits, and lenses like ordinary dark matter. Bullet Cluster lensing-centroid behavior is automatic". #11 §8 keeps it: "χ propagates on g (equivalence principle; Bullet-Cluster-safe)".
- **The gate** (#11 §8, [D — mechanism; shape [O]]): the nonrelativistic amplitude "M(k,q) = 4πα̃m_χ/(q² + m_φ²)·F(k²/μ²), k the incoming *relative* momentum", "delivered by X-exchange between dual-stress bilinears, with μ the χ quantum's intrinsic momentum-fiber width" ([the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate), [the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism)). Then "σ_T(v) = σ₀F(v²/v\*²): robust knee at v\*" ([the gated scattering law](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law)), with the benchmark "m_χ ≈ 70 MeV·α̃^{2/3}(σ₀/m ÷ 30 cm²g⁻¹)^{−1/3}" ([the scattering benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark)).
- **Its tests** (#11 §13). [Row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee): "Knee at v\* ~ 10² km/s; zero cluster signal (particle χ)". [Row 2](/wiki/predictions/scattering-isotropy): "Isotropic below the gate". [Row 3](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant): "Knee tail: sharp ⇒ particle; (v\*/σ)⁴ ⇒ wave". [Row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null): a "**Permanent null**", since both candidates are decoupled from the Standard Model and P4 is strict.
- **What it no longer claims.** χ clusters in position, not in velocity. Founding supposition 4, "Dark matter is conjugate matter", is "**alive, modified**: x-clumping, gated self-force; now one of two remaining candidates (§12)" (#11 App. A item 4; [founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions)). Velocity clumping is dead ([obituary](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)).
- **What is open.** "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis) applies to both" candidates (#11 §12; [polarization-genesis](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis)). Telling χ from [the X-condensate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate) is [row 11](/wiki/predictions/dark-sector-microstructure): "Particles vs condensate, separable via rows 1–3 + coherence signatures".
