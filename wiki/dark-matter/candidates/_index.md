*The dark-sector question asks what [dark matter](../_index.md) is made of, within the program's framework. [The checkpoint](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) gives three answers, each open: particles of [conjugate matter χ](../conjugate-matter/_index.md), the mirror-polarized counterpart of ordinary matter; a coherent oscillation of X, [the massive momentum graviton](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md) that the broken symmetry adds to gravity; and, P.R.'s conjecture, [magnetic monopoles of the conjugate sector, abundant in momentum space](momentum-space-monopoles.md) and held there by topology. Two of the three, the particles and the monopoles, are conjugate matter, which is P.R.'s core conjecture about dark matter. Version 2.0 of the checkpoint proposed the third answer. The withdrawn manuscript [#14](../../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) then modeled it as [superheavy monopoles of a hidden gauge sector](soliton-candidate/obituary.md) with the kinematics of ordinary particles, and a review that re-ran every number in code found the model's abundance estimate [misnormalized](soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md), so the model failed. It never treated the monopoles as localized in momentum, so the candidate itself has not been tested. All three answers share one unsolved question: what sets the observed ratio of dark to ordinary matter, 5.4 : 1.*

**Stands:** the established physics of [relic abundance mechanisms](relic-abundance.md), [thermal freeze-out](thermal-freeze-out.md) and [bigravity dark matter](bigravity-dark-matter.md); [the χ-particle candidate](chi-particles.md) and [the X-condensate candidate](x-condensate.md), each [O] as a candidate ([#11 §12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates)), and two results that outlived #14's model, [overproduction at the benchmark](soliton-candidate/overproduction.md) and [W′ overclosure](soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure.md) · **Conjectures:** [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](momentum-space-monopoles.md), the third candidate, P.R.'s · **Open:** [polarization-genesis and the 5.4 : 1 ratio (OP7)](polarization-genesis.md) · **Under review:** six components of #14's model ([claims under review](../../status/under-review.md)): [the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](soliton-candidate/hidden-sector.md), [the coupling α_d ≈ 34](soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md), [the Kibble normalization](soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md), [the √N topological floor (chat-only)](soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor.md), [the ψ relic window](soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window.md) and [Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator.md) · **Died:** [#14's superheavy-soliton model](soliton-candidate/obituary.md)

## Map

[Checkpoint #11](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates) is the current statement of the question. It lists three candidates in a table, each with its origin, what makes it stable and the observable that would single it out. The table is tagged "[D — framework; each candidate O]": the framework that produces the candidates is derived, and each candidate is open. Of the pages below, three are established literature, two are candidates the program claims, one is an open problem, one is the subsection on #14's failed model, and the last is the third candidate, a conjecture.

[Relic abundance mechanisms](relic-abundance.md) comes first because every candidate has to meet it. It is the established physics of how a relic density is set, mechanism by mechanism: the [yield](../../cosmology/early-universe/relic-yield-and-dilution.md#the-yield-t), a summary of [thermal freeze-out](thermal-freeze-out.md), freeze-out in a [sector at its own temperature](../../cosmology/early-universe/hidden-sector-temperature.md), cannibal and asymmetric dark matter and the Sakharov conditions for baryogenesis, and freeze-in. It ends with the program's uses: the argument that χ cannot freeze out, misalignment on the wave island, and the relics of the hidden sector of #14's model.

[Thermal freeze-out](thermal-freeze-out.md) works through the standard mechanism: the Boltzmann equation, why freeze-out comes near T ~ m/20, the s-wave relic formula exactly as [`verify/soliton_obituary.py`](../../../verify/soliton_obituary.py) codes it and the standard relic it is checked against, and freeze-out in a sector at its own temperature. Its program uses are the lab notebook's argument that particle χ cannot freeze out, which leaves the ratio of dark to ordinary matter open; the freeze-out relic coupled to ordinary matter against which [row 7](../../predictions/direct-detection-null.md) predicts a permanent null; and the W′ and ψ relics of #14's model, the first of which survives it.

[The χ-particle candidate](chi-particles.md) is the program's first answer, and one of the two forms of P.R.'s core conjecture that dark matter is conjugate matter ([#11 App. A item 4](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)): particles of the momentum-polarized matter field χ, which no Standard Model force reaches under the strict form of postulate [P4](../../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md). The page sets out why χ falls, orbits and lenses like ordinary dark matter [D], summarizes [the velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md) and [the knee in the scattering cross-section](../conjugate-matter/scattering-law.md), and sets out its tests, rows 1, 2, 3 and 7 of [the predictions table](../../predictions/_index.md), with their kill conditions. It explains why the candidate is particle χ rather than [the wave branch of the clumping problem](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md), what it does not claim, and what is open: its stability is assumed and its abundance unexplained. The gate's microphysics belongs to [conjugate matter and the velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/_index.md).

[Bigravity dark matter](bigravity-dark-matter.md) is established literature placed before the second candidate because it is that candidate's lineage: the massive spin-2 eigenstate of a two-metric theory proposed as the dark matter by Babichev et al. [23†]. The page explains the two mass eigenstates and how the ratio of the [Planck masses](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/planck-units.md#two-metrics-two-planck-masses) sets their coupling to matter, why a uniform oscillation of a massive field behaves as cold matter, the check status of the single citation, and how the program's X differs: forced massive by the broken symmetry, restricted to the [palindromic branch](../../gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome.md), and with equal Planck masses.

[The X-condensate candidate](x-condensate.md) is the program's second answer: a coherent, spatially uniform oscillation of X, which would make dark matter a state of the geometry rather than a separate field. The page sets out what X is (the real part of [the modular field](../../phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z.md), massive, [reciprocity-odd](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity.md), coupled to ordinary matter at gravitational strength with a finite range), what a condensate means here, and the three handles of #11's table, which are stated without derivation. It records the candidate's origin, a door "deliberately left closed" in [the field-equation notes](../../history/documents/field-equation-notes.md).

[Open: polarization-genesis and the 5.4 : 1 ratio (OP7)](polarization-genesis.md) follows the first two candidates because it qualifies them and the third: "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis) applies to all three" ([#11 §12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates)). [The lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) argues that a light, strongly self-interacting sector connected to ordinary matter only by gravity cannot get its abundance from thermal freeze-out, so the ratio Ω_χ : Ω_b = 5.4 : 1 needs an asymmetric mechanism, the program's analog of baryogenesis. The page goes through the notebook's sketch, heavy [Grassmannian](../../phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian.md) moduli decaying with a branching set by the vacuum's tilt angle, and records that a later calc note expects most of those moduli to be eliminated; it is [#11 §14 item 7](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems).

[#14's soliton model (failed)](soliton-candidate/_index.md) comes next. It is the withdrawn manuscript #14's version of the third candidate: superheavy ['t Hooft–Polyakov monopoles](../../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/thooft-polyakov-monopole.md) of a hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, described with ordinary kinematics, [reviewed blind](../../method/review/_index.md) three times, and failed by the third review on its abundance arithmetic. [Round-3 Decision 2](../../method/review/round-3/decisions.md#decision-2-14s-model-retired) retired this model alone as a program-native proposal. The subsection opens with the obituary and records each failed component (six of them under review), the results that survive, and the published low-mass scenario the corrected model duplicates.

[Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](momentum-space-monopoles.md) comes last, because it is the candidate that #14 modeled and did not test. It is P.R.'s conjecture, the third candidate of [#11 §12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates): magnetically charged topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector, localized in momentum and stable by topological charge [O]. The page sets out the conjecture and the table row; where the idea comes from, in founding suppositions 3, 4 and 5, version 2.0's claim and P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture; what tested it and what did not, and the evidence against it; and what it needs, beginning with a derivation that such solitons are localized in momentum at all.

## Boundaries

- The microphysics of χ — the velocity gate, its mechanism, the scattering law and the benchmark — is in [conjugate matter and the velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/_index.md); the particle and wave regimes of χ are in [momentum clumping](../momentum-clumping/_index.md).
- Telling the candidates apart is [#11 §14 item 9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) [O]; the test that separates the first two is [row 11: particles or condensate](../../predictions/dark-sector-microstructure.md). [Row 7](../../predictions/direct-detection-null.md), a permanent null in direct detection, is stated for "both dark-sector candidates" ([#11 §13](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions)); flag 63 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, proposes wording for rows 7 and 11 that counts all three candidates.
- X itself — why it is massive, its spectrum, how it couples to ordinary matter, and the open question of its stability at equal Planck masses — is in [the momentum graviton X](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md); the bimetric theory it lands on is [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](../../gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric.md).
- Relic production for wave χ is [relic abundance by misalignment on the wave island](../momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic.md), and the established mechanism is [the misalignment mechanism](../wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism.md). The heavy moduli of the polarization-genesis sketch belong to [the field content of the broken vacuum](../../phase-space/modular-field/field-content.md).
- Monopole physics in general is in [magnetic monopoles](../../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/_index.md), and the review in which #14's model failed is [review round 3](../../method/review/round-3/_index.md). The magnetic-mirror conjecture to which the third candidate's row refers is [Conjecture: the momentum sector as the magnetic mirror](../../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md), and the gap in the clumping result that the third candidate occupies is [Open: the topological loophole](../momentum-clumping/topological-loophole.md).
