*#14's soliton model was the withdrawn manuscript #14's version of the program's third dark-matter candidate: the proposal that [dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter) consists of superheavy magnetic monopoles of a hidden gauge force, configurations of a hidden field that wrap the space of its vacuum states and cannot unwind, so they would be stable without any further assumption. The manuscript described them with the kinematics of ordinary particles moving in space. Version 2.0 of the checkpoint had listed topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector as a third candidate, and #14 wrote the idea up as a standalone manuscript for [blind review](/wiki/method/review). The third review recomputed every number with executed code and found that the estimate of how many monopoles the early universe forms had been too low by a factor of a hundred to several thousand; counted correctly, superheavy monopoles are made in far too great a number. The model failed on 2026-10-19 and was retired, by a decision Paul had delegated and on 2026-10-29 narrowed to the model. It is recorded dead; Paul's concurrence was asked on 2026-10-29, and until he answers, the death stands as recorded. The model never treated the monopoles as localized in momentum, so the candidate it modeled was not tested; since #11 v2.4 that candidate is P.R.'s open conjecture, [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles). This subsection records the model, each component that failed and why, the results that survive, and the published scenario that the surviving low-mass version duplicates.*

## Map

The record of the episode is the manuscript #14, `notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md`, in three versions, with [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §12, "#14's model of the third candidate: failed (2026-10-19)", and the round-3 adjudication as its current summary and `verify/soliton_obituary.py` as the check on its numbers ([the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script)); flag 33 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` records which of the record's numbers the script does not check. #14 used ordinary kinematics throughout (#11 §12): "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" (#14 §1).

- **Version 1** called the objects "knots" and fixed their coupling by [Dirac quantization](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) against our photon. It is not in the repository. Round 1 of blind review graded it "B / medium" and found the coupling argument self-contradictory; among its valid hits, folded into v2, was the renaming of the "knots" as "π₂ monopole-class" (`HANDOFF.md` §2, §6).
- **Version 2**, "Dark Matter as Superheavy Magnetic Solitons of a Spontaneously Broken Phase-Space Duality", set the hidden coupling by "the reciprocity map, acting as the modular S transformation", to "alpha_d = 1/(4 alpha) ~ 34", took the Kibble normalization at "p = O(1)" and used a benchmark "M = 1e16 GeV". It survives verbatim inside `review/skeptic-review-console.html`, the tool used for rounds 1 and 2. Round 2 graded it "B near B/C / medium" and left a pending list of seven items (`HANDOFF.md` §6).
- **Version 3** (commit 5dbaa82), "Dark Matter as Superheavy Monopoles of a Hidden Gauge Sector Selected by a Spontaneously Broken Phase-Space Duality", was cut from that list in `review/v3-worklog.md`, a chunked log of the round-2 items, new findings and Paul's decisions, closed after round 3 ("This worklog is closed."). v3 placed the monopole mass "at about 1e13 to 4e13 GeV with no dilution required". Round 3, a blind review with code execution, graded it C on 2026-10-19 ([review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3)). It was withdrawn the same day, moved from `papers/` to `notes/`, and given a banner: "Kept as the audit trail; not for circulation."

Of the thirteen pages below, seven are obituaries, two are results that stand [V], two are record and two are established literature ([prior art](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art) and [kinetic mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing)). They open with the model's obituary and the published scenario it reduces to; the components follow, from the hidden sector and its coupling, through the abundance and the particles it forced, to the mixing with ordinary electromagnetism.

- [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) comes first, because the model's failure is the subject of the section. It sets out what #11 v2.0 and the three versions of #14 claimed, #14's ordinary kinematics, how the model was tested in three blind reviews, why the corrected Kibble count overproduces the benchmark 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ times, the thirteen round-3 rulings and the scoring of the pre-registered prediction, Decision 2's three routes, the retirement and its narrowing to the model on 2026-10-29, what survives, what the model did not test, and #14's own self-assessment. [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff)'s framing of the solitons as its current best candidate is folded in as superseded, and [the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)' favoured corner as popsci-only.
- [Topological dark matter in the literature](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art) is the established scenario the corrected model reduces to: [defects](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects) of a hidden sector as relics, set by formation and annihilation; Murayama–Shu's hidden monopoles at 1–10 PeV and Liveoak–Maharana–Wells's overclosure above about 100 PeV [28S, 29S]; annihilation at strong magnetic coupling as Preskill's solved case; the uncited prior art; and why the program claims only that it is "merely compatible".
- [Obituary: the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector) is the sector #14 postulated: an adjoint Higgs field breaking SU(2)_d to U(1)_d, leaving heavy W′±, a massless hidden photon, stable monopoles, and a light fermion ψ. The page explains each piece, why a mirror copy of the Standard Model has no stable monopoles, how v2's unnamed group became v3's sector, and why the model, standard and already published, is dead as the program's own.
- [Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling) starts from the Dirac relation α_m = 1/α_W′ between a hidden sector's electric and magnetic couplings and records the three attempts to fix the value: Dirac quantization against our photon (round 1's valid kill), reciprocity acting as [modular S](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/modular-group#the-coupling-conjecture-of-the-soliton-manuscript) (superseded within #14 by a decision Paul withdrew on 2026-10-29, and folded in as a conjecture, the separate-photon form of P.R.'s open magnetic-mirror conjecture), and hypothesis H (dead with #14's model), with the worklog's F2 reframing, whose decision Paul withdrew on 2026-10-29 while its finding stands, and the factor-of-four convention spread folded in as superseded.
- [Obituary: the Kibble normalization](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization) is the error on which #14's model failed. It derives the [yield](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/relic-yield-and-dilution#the-kibble-yield-t) n/s = p (T_c/M_Pl)³ for one defect per Hubble volume, shows that v3's p ≈ 0.023 drops the Friedmann prefactor (1.66√g\*)³ where the correct causal minimum is p = 104.31, explains why the count is a floor and why the reduced-Planck "factor of 3" was an artefact, and records why v3's own check passed.
- [Overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction) is the corrected result, which stands [V]: the model and inputs behind it, the relation for the closing mass as the gate codes it, the abundance closing at M\* = 2.9–4.4×10¹² GeV and the 3×10¹³ GeV benchmark overproduced 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ times, the [colder-sector](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/hidden-sector-temperature) and Kibble–Zurek figures that only add to it, and the worklog's earlier "point, not a window" as superseded.
- [Obituary: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor) is a bound on the abundance that existed only in the claude.ai chat, between a Kibble "ceiling" and a net-charge floor. Round-3 Decision 3 dropped it because its premise, the Kibble count as a ceiling, is inverted, and because, with #14's model retired, it had "no candidate left to serve"; the strong-coupling annihilation it addressed had already been worked out for hidden sectors (ruling 10).
- [W′ overclosure: the minimal sector is excluded](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure) is a result that survives #14's model [V]: with no light charged fermion the stable, thermal W′± overclose the universe by 10¹⁶–10¹⁷, which forced the addition of ψ. The page gives the gated [freeze-out](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/thermal-freeze-out#the-hidden-sector-of-14s-model) inputs, the annihilation coefficient by detailed balance and the colder-sector check.
- [Obituary: the ψ relic window](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window) is the mass range v3 allowed for ψ. Both edges were wrong: the upper by a miscount of states and an exact cross-section bracketed by a factor of ten, the lower because MeV-scale ψ would freeze out during [nucleosynthesis](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/big-bang-nucleosynthesis#the-ψ-relic-windows-lower-edge).
- [The hidden sector's thermal history](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-thermal-history) is record: the dark radiation that requires a colder hidden sector, ξ < 0.760, its inconsistent use in v3, what a colder sector would need, and the gravitational-wave band a first-order hidden transition would leave, which died with #14's model.
- [Benchmark-conditional arithmetic](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/benchmark-arithmetic) is record: numbers that remain correct as arithmetic at the withdrawn benchmark — densities and spacing, the self-interaction [σ_T/M](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/transfer-cross-section#σ_tm-of-14s-model) ~ 10⁻²⁶ cm²/g, and the counterfactual flux that shows why the hidden sector must be separate from ours.
- [Kinetic mixing between sectors](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing) is the established literature on how a hidden photon can mix with ours: gravity-induced mixing and its protection, no visible magnetic flux while U(1)_d is unbroken, and the leakage channels that strict sector separation closes.
- [Obituary: Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator) comes last: v3's dimension-5 estimate of the mixing, whose order-one coefficient at the [Planck scale](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/planck-units#14s-soliton-model) had no source. With it gone, the program took postulate P4 strictly.

## Boundaries

- General monopole physics is in [magnetic monopoles](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles): [Dirac quantization and the DSZ pairing](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) (DSZ: Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger), [the 't Hooft–Polyakov monopole](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/thooft-polyakov-monopole), [the Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism), [the monopole problem](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-problem) and [the flux bounds](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches). The surviving claim that conjugate-sector monopoles, if they exist, gravitate is [conjugate monopoles as ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles). P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture, that each electrically charged particle has a magnetically charged analog in the momentum polarization, which #11 has recorded as open since v2.3 (§11.4), is [Conjecture: the momentum sector as the magnetic mirror](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification). #14 v2 made a related identification its load-bearing conjecture, for its dual coupling: the momentum-polarized sector as the S-dual image of the electric sector. A full mirror spectrum appeared there only as something the sector "may carry" (flag 60 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`).
- The reviews are in [blind review](/wiki/method/review): [review rounds 1 and 2](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two) and [review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3), with its [pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/round-3/preregistration), [rulings](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings), [decisions and lessons](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions) and [arbitration](/wiki/method/review/round-3/arbitration). - Relic abundances in general are [relic abundance mechanisms](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/relic-abundance). Early-universe background is in [early-universe cosmology](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe): [the radiation era](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/radiation-era), [inflation, reheating and entropy dilution](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/inflation-and-dilution) and [gravitational waves from phase transitions](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/phase-transition-gravitational-waves).
