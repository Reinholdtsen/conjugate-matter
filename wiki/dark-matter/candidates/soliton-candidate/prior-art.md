*Topological dark matter is the established proposal that dark matter consists of topological defects, such as magnetic monopoles, left behind when a symmetry of a hidden sector breaks in the early universe. Murayama and Shu found such defects could be the dark matter at masses of 1–10 PeV, noting that Kibble's simple count of defects underestimates how many form; Liveoak, Maharana and Wells showed in 2026 that hidden monopoles overclose the universe if the symmetry breaks above about 100 PeV, unless a later epoch dilutes them. This low-scale scenario is what remains of the program's retired soliton candidate once its abundance is counted correctly. Nothing in it depends on Born reciprocity, so the program claims compatibility with it and nothing more.*

**Status:** Established — literature, live-searched [28S, 29S] (#11 §12) and checked at abstract level in round 3 (rulings 3 and 10). The program's relation to it is that "The framework is merely compatible with known PeV-scale topological dark matter (prior art)" (`CLAUDE.md`); claiming the scenario as the program's own was rejected as misattribution (round-3 Decision 2, route (a)).

## Key facts

- **Murayama–Shu** [28S]: H. Murayama, J. Shu, "Topological dark matter," Phys. Lett. B 686, 162 (2010), arXiv:0905.1720. As the round-3 referee quotes it: "Kibble mechanism drastically underestimates the production of topological defects ... dark matter of mass 1-10 PeV" (audit notes, Step 4(c) item 1).
- **Liveoak–Maharana–Wells** [29S]: D. Liveoak, A. Maharana, J. D. Wells, "Dark monopoles, bounds on hidden sectors, and cosmological implications," arXiv:2607.20843 (2026). The referee quotes: "for symmetry-breaking scales >~ 100 PeV, the monopole abundance is unacceptably high". The adjudication reads its abstract as: "hidden monopoles overclose for breaking scales ≳ 100 PeV unless diluted, e.g. by early matter domination" (ruling 3). Its Section III also sets one defect per Hubble volume as the "minimal number density" ([the Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism)).
- **The scenario** (#11 §12): "Hidden-sector monopoles avoid overclosing the universe only for symmetry-breaking scales ≲ 100 PeV, or with a period of early matter domination [29S]." "Below that scale, annihilation in the hidden plasma sets the abundance. That is the established topological-dark-matter scenario [28S, 29S], and nothing in it depends on Born reciprocity." The annihilation at strong coupling is a solved case: "Annihilation at α_m ≈ 34 is Preskill's Dirac case, already done for hidden sectors" (ruling 10, upheld at abstract level; [the monopole problem](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-problem)).
- **The referee's conclusion** (Step 5): "Salvage = a different model (v <~ 1e8 GeV, or explicit dilution), which already exists in the literature." In Step 4(d) the referee called #14's proposal "the standard hidden 't Hooft-Polyakov-monopole scenario", which "stands or falls on that literature, independently of Born reciprocity".
- **The decision** (Decision 2): "Route (a), v ≲ 100 PeV, works, but it is Murayama–Shu's and Liveoak–Maharana–Wells's model. Nothing in it depends on Born reciprocity (the referee and the pre-registration agree), so claiming it would add no testable content and would misattribute it." The candidate was retired (route (c)), and "The surviving version is noted as prior art (route a)" ([round-3 decisions](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions)).
- **Prior art #11 does not cite.** The referee also listed Baek–Ko–Park (arXiv:1311.1035) and Khoze–Ro (arXiv:1406.2291), "both: hidden SU(2)->U(1) monopoles + W' + dark photon" (Step 4(c) item 10). The adjudication accepted the list as a to-do; #11 cites Murayama–Shu and Liveoak–Maharana–Wells but neither of these, an open citation gap ([conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts)).
