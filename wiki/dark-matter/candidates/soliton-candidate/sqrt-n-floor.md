*The √N topological floor was an argument, made only in the claude.ai chat that preceded this repository, about how many of the soliton candidate's monopoles could survive annihilation in the early universe. Monopoles and antimonopoles that meet can annihilate, and at the hidden sector's strong coupling, about 34, that annihilation might have been fast enough to deplete them. The floor argument held that the Kibble mechanism leaves a net topological charge that annihilation cannot remove, so the surviving abundance would lie between a Kibble "ceiling" and that floor. It was never written into any document. In round 3 the referee showed that Kibble's count is a minimum, not a ceiling, and that annihilation at this coupling has already been worked out for hidden sectors; with the soliton candidate retired, the planned calculation was dropped.*

**Status:** Dead (chat-only) — dropped by round-3 Decision 3: "Its premise, Kibble as a ceiling, is inverted (ruling 3)." "Under decision 2 it has no candidate left to serve." `CLAUDE.md` records the same: "HANDOFF §8 queue item 3 is dropped." It was never written up and never machine-checked. Home: [the soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate); the decision is in [round-3 decisions](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions); listed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard) and among [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures).

## Key facts

- **The idea** (`HANDOFF.md` §2, open item 2): "Strong-coupling relic freeze-out at α_d ~ 34: Sommerfeld-enhanced annihilation vs. the √N topological-charge floor (Kibble leaves a net-charge residue annihilation cannot remove — chat-only idea, not yet in any paper; this bounds the relic abundance between Kibble ceiling and √N floor)". The handoff's list of chat-only results includes the "**√N topological floor** argument against the Sommerfeld catastrophe" (§4). It gives no formula; the name suggests the random-walk estimate in which N defects formed with uncorrelated charges leave a net charge of order √N (an inference from the name, not a statement of any source).
- **The planned work** (`HANDOFF.md` §8 item 3): "**√N-floor freeze-out estimate** (open item #2) — resolves "Sommerfeld probably fatal" into a number; Preskill-style kinetic calculation; decides whether the soliton candidate's abundance window exists."
- **The question it addressed** (#14 §3): annihilation in the hidden plasma runs "at alpha_m ~ 34, where Sommerfeld enhancement and rapid bound-state formation may matter. Whether the Kibble abundance survives requires a dedicated strong-coupling freeze-out calculation in that plasma, not performed here (acceptance criterion 3)." #14 §6 states that criterion as "the strong-coupling relic freeze-out calculation of Sec. 3, in the hidden plasma".
- **Ruling 10** (upheld at abstract level): "Annihilation at α_m ≈ 34 is Preskill's Dirac case, already done for hidden sectors." The referee: "Acceptance criterion 3 is not open: the "strong-coupling" regime alpha_m = 34 is exactly the Dirac-monopole case of Preskill 1979 (h = 2 pi/e)", whose modern hidden-sector version (Liveoak–Maharana–Wells) excludes symmetry-breaking scales above about 100 PeV (audit notes, Step 4(a) item 3; [the monopole problem](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-problem), [prior art](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art)).
- **Why the premise is inverted** (ruling 3): "Kibble is a floor, not a ceiling; Kibble–Zurek or bubble counting add more" ([the Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism)). The referee also found that, starting from the Kibble minimum, the initial abundance lies "far" below the annihilation attractor, "so annihilation is irrelevant in that case" ([overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction)).
- **Where it went.** Decision 3 removed it from the queue; the current queue is in [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems). The other chat-only results of `HANDOFF.md` §4 are on their own pages, listed in [conjectures](/wiki/status/conjectures).
