*Bigravity dark matter is the proposal, from the literature on bimetric gravity, that dark matter is the massive spin-2 particle of a theory with two interacting metrics. In ghost-free bimetric gravity the two metrics combine into a massless graviton, which carries ordinary gravity, and a massive spin-2 field; Babichev and collaborators proposed in 2016 that this heavy field makes up the dark matter. The program's momentum graviton X is a massive spin-2 field of the same bimetric kind, so this literature is the lineage of the program's X-condensate candidate. The program cites it only for that lineage, from memory, and has not yet checked the source.*

**Status:** Established — literature [T], explained here from general knowledge. The single citation, Babichev et al. [23†], is from model memory and awaits source-checking under [the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery). The program cites it as the "bigravity-DM lineage" of the X-condensate (#11 §12), where DM is dark matter.

## Key facts

- **The reference** (#11 ref [23]†): E. Babichev, L. Marzola, M. Raidal, A. Schmidt-May, F. Urban, H. Veermäe, M. von Strauss, "Bigravitational origin of dark matter," Phys. Rev. D 94, 084055 (2016), arXiv:1607.03497. #11's legend: "† = cited from model memory — verify before external use".
- **The setting** [T]. Bimetric gravity of the [Hassan–Rosen](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) type, the two-metric extension of ghost-free [massive gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/drgt-massive-gravity): "The unique consistent structure for massive spin-2 is Fierz–Pauli/dRGT/Hassan–Rosen [9†, 10†, 11†]" (#11 §7.1).
- **The idea** [T]. The heavy spin-2 mass eigenstate of a bimetric theory as the dark matter (Babichev et al. [23†]).
- **Where the program meets it.** The Debt-1 classification of X found X to be a massive spin-2 field and noted: "A new door, deliberately left closed today: homogeneous oscillations of a massive spin-2 X are themselves a dark-matter candidate (cf. bigravity dark matter)" [O] (`notes/debt1a-ii-X-classification.md` §4). The lab notebook's addendum v0.4 calls the direction "massive-X condensate as the dark matter itself (bigravity-DM-style), potentially unifying the dark sector with the geometry".
- **What the program adds.** In the program, X is not a free addition: it is forced massive by the broken symmetry, with "M_X = the polarization-breaking scale" (#11 §7.1 [D + T]; [why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain)). Reciprocity also "forces equal effective Planck masses (M_f = M_g)" for the two metrics, "which rules out the M_f ≪ M_g escape" — the regime in which a heavy spin-2 field has been shown linearly stable at late times (the v3 worklog, R7; [Open: stability at equal Planck masses](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/equal-planck-mass-stability)).
