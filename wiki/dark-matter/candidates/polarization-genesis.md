*Polarization-genesis is the program's name for a mechanism it does not yet have: the process that would set how much dark matter the early universe made compared with ordinary matter, whose densities stand in the observed ratio Ω_χ : Ω_b = 5.4 : 1. For particle χ the standard route is closed: a light sector that interacts strongly with itself and touches ordinary matter only through gravity cannot get its abundance from thermal freeze-out, so the abundance has to come from an asymmetry, as the abundance of ordinary baryons does. The lab notebook sketched a mechanism in which heavy fields decay into both kinds of matter, with the split set by the vacuum's polarization angle, and called it "a shape, not a mechanism". The problem is open and applies to both remaining candidates.*

**Status:** Open [O] — #11 §14 item 7, "Polarization-genesis: Ω_χ : Ω_b = 5.4 : 1 [O]". The notebook's sketch is tagged "[O, sketched]" (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §8). It qualifies both [the χ-particle candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles) and [the X-condensate candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate), and is listed among [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The problem** (#1 §5.3 [O]): "A sub-GeV, strongly self-coupled, gravitationally-only-connected sector cannot freeze out thermally without cannibalism problems; the abundance must be set asymmetrically — by *polarization-genesis* (§8). The observed Ω_χ/Ω_b = 5.4 is an input awaiting a mechanism, exactly as η_B awaited baryogenesis." The mechanisms named are explained in [relic abundance mechanisms](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/relic-abundance).
- **Its scope now** (#11 §12): "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis) applies to both." The notebook's addendum v0.2 had closed its relic-abundance problem "via misalignment production on the wave island" ([misalignment on the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic)); that closure covers wave χ only, and #11, which outranks the notebook, keeps the ratio open for both candidates.
- **The sketch** (#1 §8 [O, sketched]): "at reheating, the heavy Grassmannian moduli (the nine non-Maslov directions) decay; their branching into x- versus p-polarized quanta is set by the vacuum tilt angle, Ω_χ/Ω_x^(0) ~ tan²θ_pol, after which baryogenesis processes the x-side. A relation between the observed ratio and a Grassmannian angle would be the theory's second nontrivial number. Status: a shape, not a mechanism — the same maturity baryogenesis had in 1966." The moduli live on [the Lagrangian Grassmannian](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian).
- **An unreconciled dependence.** The sketch needs the nine heavy moduli, but the Debt-1 note #5 counts the field content as "Λ(d) σ-modes (10: τ plus nine, of which inverse-Higgs constraints likely eliminate most [O])" (`notes/debt1a-geometric-core.md` §D). No document reconciles the two ([Open: field content of the broken vacuum](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/field-content)).
- **Its lineage.** The notebook ranks it fourth of its open problems ("**Polarization-genesis** mechanism for 5.4 : 1. **[O]**") and makes the fifth, relic abundance, depend on it (#1 §11). #9 §10 item 6 calls it "the program's baryogenesis analog [O]"; the handoff lists it as open item 7, writing the ratio "Ω_DM:Ω_b = 5.4:1" (`HANDOFF.md` §2).
- **A date to check.** The notebook's "1966" for the maturity of baryogenesis is not source-checked; it is flagged in [conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts).
