*The net theorem is the summary result of Debt 3: in every part of the program's parameter space that survives, dark matter clumping by velocity is never a required phenomenon. For particle dark matter it cannot happen; for wave dark matter it was required only under Scenario A, which observation rules out; and with the coupling left free it is merely optional. It closes the test of the program's [founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image) with a negative result, the kind of result the checkpoint counts among its most reliable, because the program's incentives ran against it. It covers collective dynamics only, so whether a single topological object could be localized in momentum is a separate, untested question.*

**Status:** Alive — [D] in #9 §7 ("Three results [D]"); #11 §9 states it as result (iii) without a separate tag, resting on (i) [D] and (ii) [D + T]. Collective dynamics only. Not machine-checked: no `verify/` script covers Debt 3.

## Key facts

- **The verdict** (3c, in which BBT is the Broken-Born framework). "Nowhere in the viable parameter space of BBT is velocity-space clumping a mandatory phenomenon. It is forbidden for classical-particle χ, fatal where it would have been mandatory (Scenario A), and merely optional elsewhere (Scenario B, ten tunable orders of magnitude — a degeneration flag, not a prediction)."
- **The checkpoint's form** (#11 §9 (iii)). "velocity-space clumping is nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)." The technical abstract: "collective momentum-space clumping is proven non-mandatory everywhere and falsified where it would have been mandatory" (#11 §1).
- **The three legs.** Forbidden: [the mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion) fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark, and [the classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) puts any collective behaviour in the wave regime. Fatal: [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) is falsified by ultra-faint dwarf galaxies. Decorative: under [Scenario B](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b) streams are "tunable, not mandatory".
- **What it withdrew** (3c). "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands. The Fornax mechanism goes with them." These are [old row 3](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity) and [old row 4](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction).
- **What survives** (#11 §9). "Salvage: the island discriminant (knee shape: sharp cutoff ⇒ particle χ; (v\*/σ)⁴ ⇒ wave χ) and free relic abundance via misalignment on the wave island" ([row 3, the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant); [misalignment](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic)). 3c's longer list also keeps the cliff (since #11 §8 a knee) with in-gate isotropy, the dark-energy sector and the permanent nulls. The dark-energy sector has since come under pressure from the second data release of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument ([the phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing)).
- **Scope.** The theorem concerns collective dynamics. "The Debt-3 loophole — topological momentum-space localization of individual objects — remains untested, but has no candidate" (#11 §12; [the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)).
- **Weight as evidence** (#11 §2). "Negative results (§9, §7.1, and the §12 obituary) are the most reliable Tier-3 items, since the program's incentive gradient ran against them." ([Negative results](/wiki/method/negative-results).)
