*The topological loophole is the route that Debt 3 leaves open for localization in momentum space: not a collective clumping of many particles, which [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem) rules out, but a single object, such as a topological soliton, whose own structure would localize it in momentum. Debt 3's calculations cover collective dynamics only, so they do not test this case. The candidate that occupied the loophole, superheavy solitons of a hidden gauge sector, was retired on 2026-10-19 after failing review on its abundance arithmetic, so the loophole is logically open but has no candidate. The checkpoint does not say consistently whether it remains a work item.*

**Status:** Open — untested, without a candidate, and with no tag. #11 §12 says it "remains untested, but has no candidate"; #11 §14 item 8 says the "p-localization items are closed by the §12 obituary". The checkpoint does not reconcile the two. Listed among [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems); it qualifies the death of [velocity clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image).

## Key facts

- **The statement** (#11 App. A item 3). Founding supposition 3, "Matter clumps in momentum space", is "dead as dynamics (§9); loophole: topological p-localization of individual solitons untested, and its candidate is retired (§12)" ([founding suppositions](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions)).
- **Why Debt 3 leaves it open.** The net theorem concerns collective velocity-space dynamics. #11 §9's opening passage describes the outcome as leaving "one loophole for individual knots, whose candidate has since been retired (§12)".
- **How the candidate used it** (#11 §12). Version 2.0's third dark-sector candidate, topological solitons of a p-local gauge sector, "also claimed they slip through the Debt-3 loophole (§9), since topology, not collective dynamics, would localize them in momentum."
- **The retirement.** The candidate was "Retired as a program-native candidate" after the round-3 review (#11 §12; [the soliton obituary](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). Per `CLAUDE.md`, "The framework is merely compatible with known PeV-scale topological dark matter (prior art)" ([prior art](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art)).
- **The current wording** (#11 §12, "What survives"). "The Debt-3 loophole — topological momentum-space localization of individual objects — remains untested, but has no candidate."
- **Unresolved.** #11 §14 item 8 ends: "(The soliton-spectrum and p-localization items are closed by the §12 obituary.)" #11 does not reconcile this with §12. Read together, the two say that the logical loophole is untested and that there is no candidate or work item for it ([conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts)).
- **History.** The handoff, written while the soliton candidate was alive, called the loophole "untouched" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). The first consolidation, #9, does not mention a loophole.
