*Momentum clumping is the program's founding picture of [dark matter](../_index.md): instead of gathering into lumps in space, dark matter would gather into a few streams whose members share nearly the same velocity. Whether that happens was [the clumping problem](../../history/main-problems.md), the third of the program's three main problems, and three short notes tested it with standard kinetic theory. For particle dark matter the attraction falls short by 28–31 orders of magnitude. For wave dark matter, the one scenario that made clumping mandatory predicts that the smallest galaxies lose their dark matter, and they have not; with the coupling left free, clumping is merely optional. These results stand, and the founding image is P.R.'s open conjecture. This section holds the conjecture with the evidence against it in full, the calculations, what the tests yielded, and three claims under review. It also holds a later question, the scope of the tests: a note of 2026-10-28 found that they cover the program's own contact-range interaction (which acts only over microscopic distances), that they show clumping is required nowhere rather than impossible, and that a long-range attraction gated in relative velocity, a different model, would be decorative at the program's gate width.*

**Stands:** the established physics of [Vlasov stability](vlasov-stability.md); [the mean-field criterion](instability-criterion.md), [the classicality theorem](classicality-theorem.md), [the gated condensation rate](wave-island/condensation-rate.md), [Scenario B, quarantined](wave-island/scenario-b.md), [relic abundance by misalignment](wave-island/misalignment-relic.md), [the net theorem](net-theorem.md) · **Conjectures:** [velocity clumping, the founding image](founding-image.md) (P.R.'s), [a long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ](long-range-gated-attraction.md) (outside the record; decorative where tested) · **Open:** [the topological loophole](topological-loophole.md), whose candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md) (in the candidates section) · **Under review:** [Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](wave-island/scenario-a.md), [velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](velocity-granularity.md), [Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](fornax-friction.md), in [claims under review](../../status/under-review.md) · **Superseded:** [racing stream growth against orbital stirring](gated-vlasov-model.md#superseded-racing-stream-growth-against-orbital-stirring)

## Map

The clumping problem began as item 3 of the [lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md)'s open problems: the growth rate of the stream instability against orbital phase mixing, which "determines whether Prediction 3's granularity survives in Milky-Way-like halos" ([`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §11](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#11-open-problems-in-order-of-severity)). Three calc notes carried it out: [`notes/stream-stability.md`](../../../notes/stream-stability.md) ("negative on the particle island, with a structural corollary"), [`notes/wave-island.md`](../../../notes/wave-island.md) ("rate derived, environments evaluated, verdict reached") and [`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) ("Scenario A falsified; the clumping problem closed"). [Checkpoint #11](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) is the current summary, tagged [D] on the particle side and [D + T] on the wave side. Since 2026-10-28 [`verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py`](../../../verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py) (section D) has reproduced calc note [#2](../../../notes/stream-stability.md)'s two shortfall ratios, which the handoff had marked machine-verified with no script behind them; the record's tag is [D], and no script covers the other clumping numbers (flag 16 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), addendum; [the velocity-gated-gravity script](../../method/verification/velocity-gated-gravity-script.md)). The [history of the three main problems](../../history/main-problems.md) places the clumping problem among the others. One page below is established physics and one is method; every other page is a claim of the program, alive, under review, open or a conjecture.

[Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](founding-image.md) comes first, because the image is the subject of the section. It records the lab notebook's picture of a halo breaking into discrete velocity streams, "the Jeans problem with the roles of x and v exchanged", with its analogies to superconductivity and to Berry-curvature transport; the two predictions it carried; how the clumping problem tested it; and the three results against it as collective dynamics, stated in full, with their scope.

[Vlasov stability and Landau damping](vlasov-stability.md) is the established kinetic theory the test used: the collisionless Boltzmann equation, linear response about a Maxwellian, Landau damping, the static threshold of an attractive gas and its best-known case, the Jeans instability, and Boltzmann's H-theorem. [The gated Vlasov model](gated-vlasov-model.md) is the method built on it: a Gaussian [velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md) on a contact attraction, whose linear response becomes an eigenvalue problem on momentum space, bracketed by a broad-gate and a narrow-gate limit. It also holds the superseded plan to race stream growth against orbital stirring, which the threshold made moot.

[The mean-field criterion and its 10²⁸–10³¹ shortfall](instability-criterion.md) is the first result: the threshold for streams in both limits, g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)², and its failure by 28–31 orders of magnitude once the coupling is fixed by [dwarf-galaxy cores](../halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md), with collisions excluded by the H-theorem. It also records the handoff's machine-check mark on these numbers, which had no script behind it until the 2026-10-28 port (flag 16 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)), and calc note #2's narrow-gate form of the threshold, which keeps the gate width where a smooth halo keeps its dispersion and overstates the Milky Way shortfall about 3× without changing the verdict (flag 58). [The classicality theorem and the particle island](classicality-theorem.md) follows from it by a scaling argument: meeting the criterion requires masses below about 0.1 meV, and at that mass about 10²⁰ quanta share each quantum state (on the regime in which calc note #2 evaluates these numbers, see flag 19 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)), so, under the program's contact-range interaction, classical particle dark matter scatters but cannot clump, and collective behaviour belongs to waves.

[The wave island](wave-island/_index.md) is the branch the theorem leaves open, with its own pages: the gated condensation rate, Scenario A (under review), Scenario B (quarantined) and the misalignment route to the relic density. [The net theorem](net-theorem.md) then states the outcome: velocity clumping is nowhere mandatory, "forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)". Its salvage, in [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested), is the island discriminant, now [row 3 of the predictions table](../../predictions/island-discriminant.md), and misalignment production on the wave island.

[Conjecture: a long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ](long-range-gated-attraction.md) follows the net theorem because it asks where the theorem's scope ends. [`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md) (2026-10-28, computed, not reviewed) finds that the clumping problem's tests cover the program's contact-range interaction ([the scope of the tests](founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests)), and that an attraction among χ that acts at long range only between particles of similar velocity is a model the record does not contain. No field in the record carries it. At the record's gate width the Lyman-α forest allows ε < 0.057–0.106 of gravity's strength in the record's reading, while velocity-selected clumping needs ε > 1 at every gate width; the force could not make dwarf cores, which would need G̃/G ≈ 3.6×10³¹, so it would be decorative. Gates below about 30 km/s are a corner where N-body runs by one agent found no velocity-selected structure up to ε = 10, with one statistic not adjudicated, and estimates rerun by one agent exclude ε ≈ 2 from 10 km/s up.

[Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](velocity-granularity.md) and [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](fornax-friction.md) record the two predictions the founding image carried: macro-streams perturbing cold [stellar streams](../halo-astrophysics/halo-substructure.md) in the Milky Way, and superfluid-like dwarf cores in which globular clusters do not sink. Both were withdrawn on the results of the clumping problem, and both are under review. [Open: the topological loophole](topological-loophole.md) comes last: a single topological object localized in momentum, which the clumping problem did not test. Its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md). [#14](../../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md)'s model of that candidate, built with ordinary kinematics, failed on its recomputed abundance ([Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md)), so it did not test the loophole. [#11 §14 item 8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) carries p-localization as open [O], for that candidate and for the shared-photon form of P.R.'s [magnetic-mirror conjecture](../../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md#shared-photon).
