*Momentum clumping is the program's founding picture of dark matter: instead of gathering into lumps in space, dark matter would gather into a few streams whose members share nearly the same velocity. Whether that happens was the program's third debt, a calculation it owed, and three short notes carried it out with standard kinetic theory. The answer was no. For particle dark matter the attraction falls short by 28–31 orders of magnitude; for wave dark matter, the one scenario that made clumping mandatory predicts that the smallest galaxies lose their dark matter, and they have not. This section holds the founding claim's obituary, the calculation that killed it, and what survived.*

## Map

Debt 3 began as item 3 of the lab notebook's open problems: the growth rate of the stream instability against orbital phase mixing, which "determines whether Prediction 3's granularity survives in Milky-Way-like halos" (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §11). Three calc notes paid it: `notes/debt3a-stream-stability.md` ("negative on the particle island, with a structural corollary"), `notes/debt3b-wave-island.md` ("rate derived, environments evaluated, verdict reached") and `notes/debt3c-ufd-endpoint.md` ("Scenario A falsified; Debt 3 closed"). Checkpoint #11 §9 is the current summary, and the [history of the three debts](/wiki/history/debts) places Debt 3 among the others. One page below is established physics and one is method; every other page is a claim of the program, alive, dead or open.

[Obituary: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image) comes first, because the death is the subject of the section. It records the lab notebook's picture of a halo breaking into discrete velocity streams, how the program tested it, and why it died.

[Vlasov stability and Landau damping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/vlasov-stability) is the established kinetic theory the test used: the collisionless Boltzmann equation, linear response about a Maxwellian, Landau damping, the Jeans instability and the H-theorem. [The gated Vlasov model](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/gated-vlasov-model) is the method built on it: a Gaussian gate on a contact attraction, whose linear response becomes an eigenvalue problem on momentum space, bracketed by two solvable limits.

[The mean-field criterion and its 10²⁸–10³¹ shortfall](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion) is the first result: the threshold for streams to form, and its failure by 28–31 orders of magnitude once the coupling is fixed by dwarf-galaxy cores. [The classicality theorem and the particle island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) follows from it: meeting the criterion requires masses so small that dark matter behaves as a wave, so classical particle dark matter scatters but cannot clump.

[The wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island) is the branch the theorem leaves open, with its own pages: the gated condensation rate, Scenario A (dead), Scenario B (quarantined) and the misalignment route to the relic density. [The net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem) then states the outcome: velocity clumping is nowhere mandatory. Its salvage, in #11 §9, is the island discriminant and misalignment production on the wave island.

[Obituary: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity) and [Obituary: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction) record the two predictions withdrawn with the founding image. [Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole) comes last: a single topological object localized in momentum, which Debt 3 did not test and which no longer has a candidate.
