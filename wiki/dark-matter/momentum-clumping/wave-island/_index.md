*The wave island is the range of very light masses of [conjugate matter](../../conjugate-matter/_index.md) χ, roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV to 10⁻⁴ eV, in which each quantum state of a dark-matter halo holds so many quanta that the halo behaves as a classical wave field rather than as a gas of particles. There [the gated attraction](../../conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md) can act collectively: as the waves scatter off one another, it moves their power into narrow ranges of momentum, so that a halo condenses into streams and dense cores. [The clumping problem](../../../history/main-problems.md) computed how fast this happens and asked what fixes the strength of the coupling. If the gated channel also makes the cores of [dwarf galaxies](../../halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md) ([Scenario A](scenario-a.md)), condensation is mandatory in the faintest galaxies and drains their dark matter, which observation excludes; Scenario A is under review. If the coupling is free ([Scenario B](scenario-b.md)), streams become an adjustable option rather than a prediction. The light mass has one further consequence: the field can be produced by [misalignment](../../wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism.md), as [axions](../../../cosmology/dark-energy/axion-like-fields.md#as-dark-matter) are.*

## Map

The wave island is the branch that [the classicality theorem](../classicality-theorem.md) leaves open: the coupling needed for collective behavior is reached only where χ is a wave. There, in the words of [`notes/stream-stability.md`](../../../../notes/stream-stability.md), "the collective sector is reborn in field language. The halo is an incoherent wave field; the gated attraction drives condensation in k-space — power flowing into narrow-momentum streams". The notes write the island's upper edge as "≲ meV" ([#2](../../../../notes/stream-stability.md)), "≲ 0.1 meV" ([#3](../../../../notes/wave-island.md)) and 10⁻⁴ eV ([#4](../../../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) and [the lab notebook](../../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md)'s addendum v0.2). This section uses #4's range, "roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV with Scenario-B couplings", which closed the clumping problem; its lower edge is [the bound on fuzzy dark matter](../../wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds.md) that also closed Scenario A's light-mass branch. Every page below is a claim of the program, drawn from [`notes/wave-island.md`](../../../../notes/wave-island.md) and [`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) ([the clumping calc notes](../../../history/documents/clumping-notes.md)) and summarized in [checkpoint #11](../../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§9](../../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (ii), tagged [D + T]. None is machine-checked. The established wave physics they build on is in [wave dark matter](../../wave-dark-matter/_index.md).

- [The gated condensation rate Γ_self](condensation-rate.md) comes first, because everything after it compares against it. The page sets out the wave-kinetic mechanism the rate rests on [T], the coupling and halo quantities it is built from, and the result, Γ_self ≈ c g²ρ² min(σ, v\*)⁴/(m³σ⁶) with c = O(5–15), in its gate-open and gate-narrow forms: a power-law suppression (v\*/σ)⁴ where particles show an exponential one. It then gives the two corollaries, [the island discriminant](../../../predictions/island-discriminant.md) and the gravitational floor, and the race against orbital stirring that frames both scenarios. Folded sections hold calc note #2's superseded cubic estimate and two open items, the calibration of c against Levkov–Panin–Tkachev simulations and the restatement of prediction rows 1 and 2 for waves.
- [Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](scenario-a.md) follows the first answer to what fixes the coupling: the gated channel makes the cores of classical dwarfs, by analogy with [self-interacting dark matter](../../halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter.md). The page gives the environment table in which streams lose everywhere except in ultra-faint dwarfs, the test against [Segue 1](../../halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md) (a rate ratio of about 660 and a condensation time of about 1.5×10⁴ yr), [the critical mass](../../wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates.md) and its two branches, the mass drain by repeated collapse and the light-mass branch excluded in advance, the robustness checks, and what rests on the scenario.
- [Scenario B: tunable streams (quarantined)](scenario-b.md) is the other answer: the cores come from elsewhere and the coupling is free. The page sets out the window for streams in terms of the mean-field parameter X, ten orders of magnitude wide at m = 0.1 meV with Milky Way numbers, explains why a tunable consequence is a degeneration flag in [Lakatos's sense](../../../method/lakatosian-methodology.md), and records an unresolved conflict over its fuzzy-dark-matter option.
- [Relic abundance by misalignment on the wave island](misalignment-relic.md) closes the section with a consequence of the light mass rather than of the gate: a field this light, coupled only through gravity and its own self-interaction, is produced by misalignment, which removes the lab notebook's relic-abundance problem on this branch. The 5.4 : 1 ratio of dark to ordinary matter stays open.

## Boundaries

- The established physics is in [wave dark matter](../../wave-dark-matter/_index.md): [occupation number](../../wave-dark-matter/occupation-number.md), [wave kinetics and Bose stars](../../wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics.md), [attractive condensates](../../wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates.md), [the misalignment mechanism](../../wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism.md) and [the bounds on fuzzy dark matter](../../wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds.md). The ultra-faint dwarfs and Segue 1 as data are in [dwarf galaxies](../../halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md).
- The particle side and the summary verdict are in the parent section, [momentum clumping](../_index.md): [the mean-field criterion](../instability-criterion.md), [the classicality theorem](../classicality-theorem.md) and [the net theorem](../net-theorem.md).
- The particle-versus-wave diagnostic that the clumping problem salvaged is a test, [row 3, the island discriminant](../../../predictions/island-discriminant.md); the ratio of dark to ordinary matter is [Open: polarization-genesis](../../candidates/polarization-genesis.md).
- The χ condensate here is a different object from [the X-condensate candidate](../../candidates/x-condensate.md), a coherent oscillation of the massive [momentum graviton](../../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md). The sources do not connect the two, although [#11 §12](../../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates) lists the X-condensate's [knee](../../conjugate-matter/scattering-law.md) shape as "wave-like". "Soliton" in these notes means a Bose-star core, not the [topological soliton](../../../symmetry-breaking/topological-defects.md#two-meanings-of-soliton-and-the-name-knots) of the third dark-matter candidate, [P.R.'s conjecture of monopoles abundant in momentum space](../../candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md), or of [#14's failed model of it](../../candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md).
- Scenario A is listed in [claims under review](../../../status/under-review.md); the two open items are registered in [open problems](../../../status/open-problems.md).
