*The wave island is the range of very light masses of [conjugate matter](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter) χ, roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV to 10⁻⁴ eV, in which each quantum state of a dark-matter halo holds so many quanta that the halo behaves as a classical wave field rather than as a gas of particles. There [the gated attraction](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate) can act collectively: as the waves scatter off one another, it moves their power into narrow ranges of momentum, so that a halo condenses into streams and dense cores. [The clumping problem](/wiki/history/main-problems) computed how fast this happens and asked what fixes the strength of the coupling. If the gated channel also makes the cores of [dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies) ([Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a)), condensation is mandatory in the faintest galaxies and drains their dark matter, which observation excludes. If the coupling is free ([Scenario B](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b)), streams become an adjustable option rather than a prediction. The light mass has one further consequence: the field can be produced by [misalignment](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism), as [axions](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/axion-like-fields#as-dark-matter) are.*

**Stands:** [the gated condensation rate Γ_self](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate), [Scenario B, quarantined](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b), [relic abundance by misalignment](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic) · **Died:** [Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) · **Open:** folded sections on [calibrating the rate's coefficient c](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate#open-calibration-of-the-coefficient-c) and [rows 1 and 2 in wave language](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate#open-rows-1-and-2-in-wave-language), neither itemized in #11 §14 · **Superseded:** a folded section on [calc note #2's cubic suppression](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate#superseded-the-cubic-suppression-of-calc-note-2)

## Map

The wave island is the branch that [the classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) leaves open: the coupling needed for collective behavior is reached only where χ is a wave. There, in the words of `notes/stream-stability.md`, "the collective sector is reborn in field language. The halo is an incoherent wave field; the gated attraction drives condensation in k-space — power flowing into narrow-momentum streams". The notes write the island's upper edge as "≲ meV" (#2), "≲ 0.1 meV" (#3) and 10⁻⁴ eV (#4 and [the lab notebook](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook)'s addendum v0.2). This section uses #4's range, "roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV with Scenario-B couplings", which closed the clumping problem; its lower edge is [the bound on fuzzy dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds) that also closed Scenario A's light-mass branch. Every page below is a claim of the program, drawn from `notes/wave-island.md` and `notes/ufd-endpoint.md` ([the clumping calc notes](/wiki/history/documents/clumping-notes)) and summarized in [checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) §9 (ii), tagged [D + T]. None is machine-checked. The established wave physics they build on is in [wave dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter).

- [The gated condensation rate Γ_self](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate) comes first, because everything after it compares against it. The page sets out the wave-kinetic mechanism the rate rests on [T], the coupling and halo quantities it is built from, and the result, Γ_self ≈ c g²ρ² min(σ, v\*)⁴/(m³σ⁶) with c = O(5–15), in its gate-open and gate-narrow forms: a power-law suppression (v\*/σ)⁴ where particles show an exponential one. It then gives the two corollaries, [the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant) and the gravitational floor, and the race against orbital stirring that frames both scenarios. Folded sections hold calc note #2's superseded cubic estimate and two open items, the calibration of c against Levkov–Panin–Tkachev simulations and the restatement of prediction rows 1 and 2 for waves.
- [Obituary: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) follows the first answer to what fixes the coupling: the gated channel makes the cores of classical dwarfs, by analogy with [self-interacting dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter). The page gives the environment table in which streams lose everywhere except in ultra-faint dwarfs, the test against [Segue 1](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies) (a rate ratio of about 660 and a condensation time of about 1.5×10⁴ yr), [the critical mass](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates) and its two branches, the mass drain by repeated collapse and the light-mass branch excluded in advance, the robustness checks, and what the kill took with it.
- [Scenario B: tunable streams (quarantined)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b) is the other answer: the cores come from elsewhere and the coupling is free. The page sets out the window for streams in terms of the mean-field parameter X, ten orders of magnitude wide at m = 0.1 meV with Milky Way numbers, explains why a tunable consequence is a degeneration flag in [Lakatos's sense](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology), and records an unresolved conflict over its fuzzy-dark-matter option.
- [Relic abundance by misalignment on the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic) closes the section with a consequence of the light mass rather than of the gate: a field this light, coupled only through gravity and its own self-interaction, is produced by misalignment, which removes the lab notebook's relic-abundance problem on this branch. The 5.4 : 1 ratio of dark to ordinary matter stays open.

## Boundaries

- The established physics is in [wave dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter): [occupation number](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/occupation-number), [wave kinetics and Bose stars](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics), [attractive condensates](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates), [the misalignment mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism) and [the bounds on fuzzy dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds). The ultra-faint dwarfs and Segue 1 as data are in [dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies).
- The particle side and the summary verdict are in the parent section, [momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping): [the mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion), [the classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) and [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem).
- The particle-versus-wave diagnostic that the clumping problem salvaged is a test, [row 3, the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant); the ratio of dark to ordinary matter is [Open: polarization-genesis](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis).
- The χ condensate here is a different object from [the X-condensate candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate), a coherent oscillation of the massive [momentum graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton). The sources do not connect the two, although #11 §12 lists the X-condensate's [knee](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law) shape as "wave-like". "Soliton" in these notes means a Bose-star core, not the [topological soliton](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects#two-meanings-of-soliton-and-the-name-knots) of [the retired candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate).
- Scenario A is listed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard); the two open items are registered in [open problems](/wiki/status/open-problems).
