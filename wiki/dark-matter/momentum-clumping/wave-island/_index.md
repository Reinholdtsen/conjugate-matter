*The wave island is the range of very light χ masses, roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV to 10⁻⁴ eV, in which each quantum state of dark matter holds so many quanta that dark matter behaves as a classical wave field rather than as particles. There the gated attraction can act collectively: it moves the waves' power into narrow ranges of momentum, so that a halo condenses into streams and dense cores. Debt 3 computed how fast this happens and asked what fixes the coupling. If the gated channel also makes the cores of dwarf galaxies (Scenario A), condensation is mandatory in the faintest galaxies and drains them, which observation excludes; if the coupling is free (Scenario B), streams become an adjustable option rather than a prediction.*

**Stands:** [the gated condensation rate Γ_self](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate), [Scenario B, quarantined](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b), [relic abundance by misalignment](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic) · **Died:** [Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) · **Open:** no page of its own; the calibration of Γ_self's coefficient is recorded on [the rate's page](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate)

## Map

The wave island is the branch that [the classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) leaves open. There, in the words of `notes/debt3a-stream-stability.md`, "the collective sector is reborn in field language. The halo is an incoherent wave field; the gated attraction drives condensation in k-space — power flowing into narrow-momentum streams". The island's upper edge differs between the notes: "≲ meV" (3a), "≲ 0.1 meV" (3b) and 10⁻⁴ eV (3c and the lab notebook's addendum v0.2). This section uses 3c's range, "roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV with Scenario-B couplings", which closed Debt 3. The established wave physics these pages build on is in [wave dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter); every page below is a claim of the program.

[The gated condensation rate Γ_self](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate) comes first, because everything after it is a comparison against it: how fast the gated attraction condenses a wave-like halo, the gravitational channel it must beat, and the orbital stirring it races.

[Obituary: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) follows the first answer to what fixes the coupling, the assumption that the same channel makes dwarf-galaxy cores, to its falsification by Segue 1 and the other ultra-faint dwarfs.

[Scenario B: tunable streams (quarantined)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b) is the other answer, a free coupling, under which streams are possible across about ten orders of magnitude but never required; the checkpoint lists it as a degeneration risk.

[Relic abundance by misalignment on the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic) closes the section with a consequence of the light mass rather than of the gate: a standard way to produce the dark-matter density.

## Boundaries

- The established physics is in [wave dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter) (occupation number, wave kinetics and Bose stars, attractive condensates, the misalignment mechanism, the bounds on fuzzy dark matter); the ultra-faint dwarfs and Segue 1 as data are in [dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies).
- The particle side and the summary verdict are in the parent section: [the classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem) and [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem).
- The particle-versus-wave knee diagnostic that Debt 3 salvaged is [row 3, the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant).
- The χ condensate here is a different object from [the X-condensate candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate), a coherent oscillation of the massive momentum graviton. The sources do not connect the two, although #11 §12 lists the X-condensate's knee shape as "wave-like". "Soliton" in these notes means a Bose-star core, not the topological soliton of [the retired candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate).
