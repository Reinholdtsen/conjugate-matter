*Misalignment is a way for a very light field to acquire its relic density in the early universe: the field starts away from the minimum of its potential and later oscillates coherently about it, and those oscillations behave as cold dark matter, as in the standard axion scenario. Debt 3 noted that χ on the [wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island), which couples only through gravity and its own self-interaction, would be produced this way. That removes the lab notebook's difficulty in giving a light, gravity-only sector its density by thermal freeze-out, but only on the wave island. It does not explain why dark matter outweighs ordinary matter by 5.4 to 1, which stays open for both dark-matter candidates.*

**Status:** Alive, on the wave island only — part of Debt 3's salvage in #11 §9 ("free relic abundance via misalignment on the wave island"), with no tag of its own. The 5.4 : 1 question "applies to both" candidates (#11 §12) and is item 7 of #11 §14.

## Key facts

- **The observation** (3b, "Bonus: the relic debt closes itself"). "A field this light with only gravitational + self couplings is produced by misalignment, like an axion. Open problem #5 (cannibalism-safe relic abundance) is moot on the wave island: misalignment is the mechanism, and Ω_χ trades against (m, χᵢ) in the standard way." 3b does not define χᵢ ([the misalignment mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism)).
- **The problem it addresses** (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §5.3, [O]). "A sub-GeV, strongly self-coupled, gravitationally-only-connected sector cannot freeze out thermally without cannibalism problems; the abundance must be set asymmetrically — by polarization-genesis (§8)." ([Relic abundance mechanisms](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/relic-abundance).)
- **What remains** (3b). "The 5.4:1 question remains (polarization-genesis), but the production channel is no longer exotic." #11 §12: "The 5.4 : 1 abundance question (polarization-genesis) applies to both." ([Polarization-genesis](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis); the two candidates are [χ particles](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles) and [the X-condensate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate).)
- **Which problem number 5.** 3b's "Open problem #5" is item 5 of the lab notebook's §11 list, "Relic abundance without cannibalism"; item 5 of #11 §14 is a different problem, the dual-stress enhancement. The notebook's addendum v0.2 says "open problems 3 and 5 close (5 via misalignment production on the wave island)"; #11 §9 states the salvage only for the wave island, and #11 §12 keeps the 5.4 : 1 ratio open.
