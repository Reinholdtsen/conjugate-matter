*Scenario B is the alternative to [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) on the [wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island): the cores of dwarf galaxies are made by something else, such as the quantum pressure of extremely light wave dark matter or feedback from ordinary matter, so the strength of the gated attraction is left free. With the coupling free, it can be set anywhere in a window about ten orders of magnitude wide in which velocity streams form, or outside it, where they do not, so streams are possible but never required. That makes them an option the theory can accommodate rather than a prediction it is committed to, which in Lakatos's terms is a sign of a degenerating research programme. The checkpoint keeps the scenario alive but quarantined, among the program's listed degeneration risks.*

**Status:** Alive but quarantined — #11 §15 lists the "tunable wave-island collective sector (quarantined)" among its degeneration risks. The statement belongs to #11 §9 (ii), which is tagged [D + T] as a whole; the window is computed in `notes/debt3b-wave-island.md` at a single mass. Not machine-checked.

## Key facts

- **The scenario** (3b). "Scenario B — cores come from elsewhere (fuzzy-end quantum pressure at m ~ 10⁻²² eV, or baryonic feedback), leaving g free." Cores and feedback are described under [dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies).
- **The stream condition** (3b). "Writing X ≡ gn/(mσ²) (the Chunk-A mean-field parameter, with X < 1 required so halos aren't modulationally unstable), the stream condition Γ_self ≥ Ω becomes"

  ```text
  X ≥ √(Ωσ²/(c m v*⁴))
  ```

  Here X is [the mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion)'s parameter, not the momentum graviton; a medium is modulationally unstable when small modulations of a uniform wave grow. Γ_self is [the gated condensation rate](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate), and Ω = 1/t_dyn is the orbital stirring rate.
- **The window** (3b, with Milky Way numbers). At m = 0.1 meV the window is "X ∈ [5×10⁻¹¹, 1] — ten orders of magnitude. Verdict B: streams are tunable, not mandatory."
- **The Lakatos reading** (3b). "In Lakatos terms from the earlier discussion: on this branch the granularity prediction is no longer a novel requirement of the program, only an option — a degeneration flag." ([Lakatos's methodology](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology); [the assessment](/wiki/program/assessment); the granularity prediction is [old row 3](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity).)
- **In the checkpoint and the verdict.** #11 §9 (ii): "With coupling free, streams are tunable across ~10 orders: optional, never required." 3c: "merely optional elsewhere (Scenario B, ten tunable orders of magnitude — a degeneration flag, not a prediction)", and "The surviving wave island is roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV ≲ m ≲ 10⁻⁴ eV with Scenario-B couplings." It is the third leg of [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem).
- **Unresolved.** Scenario B's fuzzy-end option, m ~ 10⁻²² eV, lies below 3c's lower edge of the wave island, 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV, which the ultra-faint-dwarf and Lyman-α bounds set ([bounds on fuzzy dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/fuzzy-dark-matter-bounds)). Neither note addresses the mismatch ([conflicts between sources](/wiki/history/documents/source-conflicts)).
