*Fornax friction suppression was the lab notebook's prediction that dark matter in the cores of dwarf galaxies condenses into a superfluid-like state, in which dynamical friction, the drag that makes a massive body sink toward the centre of a galaxy, is suppressed. It would have explained why the globular star clusters of the Fornax dwarf galaxy have not sunk to its centre, the Fornax timing problem, and it was row 4 of the notebook's prediction table. The condensed cores came from the same velocity-space condensation as the [founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image) of velocity clumping, so when Debt 3 killed that picture the row was withdrawn with it, permanently.*

**Status:** Dead — withdrawn on the particle island in `notes/debt3a-stream-stability.md` and on both islands in `notes/debt3c-ufd-endpoint.md` ("The Fornax mechanism goes with them"); #11 §13 lists "Fornax friction suppression (obituary §9)" under "Withdrawn with cause". Killed with the founding image by [the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem). Home section: [momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping); listed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).

## Key facts

- **The row as written** (`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §9, row 4). Observable: "Dynamical friction in dwarf cores". Standard expectation: the globular clusters sink, in a cuspy cold-dark-matter halo. Requirement: "Suppressed in condensed cores", so that Fornax's globular clusters "stay put". Kill condition: "Demonstrated normal friction in cored dwarfs". The physics of the probe is under [halo-substructure probes](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/halo-substructure), and cored dwarfs under [dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies).
- **Its basis** (#1 §5.2, tagged "[D at mean-field level; O beyond]"). A momentum-gated attraction was read as a pairing interaction, and "below a critical velocity dispersion the core of a halo condenses — momentum-space clumping, realized. Consequences: superfluid-like cores in dwarfs and suppressed dynamical friction inside them (the Fornax globular-cluster timing problem is a feature, not a bug)."
- **Withdrawn** (3a, particle island). "Rows 3 (velocity granularity) and 4 (condensed cores / Fornax friction suppression) are withdrawn, and with them the mechanism-level linkage claimed between them" ([the mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion)). 3c, both islands: "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands. The Fornax mechanism goes with them."
- **In the later tables.** #9 §9 and #11 §13 list it as withdrawn with cause. In #9 and #11, row 4 is [thawing dark energy](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift), so this page calls the notebook's row old row 4 ([history of the predictions table](/wiki/predictions/table-history)). Its companion is [old row 3](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity).
