*Velocity clumping is the program's founding picture of dark matter and P.R.'s conjecture: a smooth dark-matter halo would break up into a few streams, each made of particles moving with nearly the same velocity, so that dark matter clumps in velocity instead of in position. The [lab notebook](../../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) described it as the [Jeans problem](vlasov-stability.md), the gravitational instability that makes lumps in space, with the roles of position and velocity exchanged. The clumping calculation tested it as collective dynamics. For particle dark matter the attraction falls short of the threshold by 28 to 31 orders of magnitude. For wave dark matter, the one case in which clumping would be mandatory predicts that the faintest galaxies have lost their dark matter, and they are, in the checkpoint's words, "the most dark-matter-rich objects known". With the coupling left free, clumping is possible but never required. These results stand, and the image is P.R.'s open conjecture.*

**Status:** Conjecture — [O] — P.R.'s founding supposition 3, "Matter clumps in momentum space" ([#11](../../history/documents/checkpoint.md) [§9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [App. A item 3](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)). As collective dynamics it fails for particle dark matter by 10²⁸–10³¹ (theory), fails the test of the faintest dwarf galaxies where it is mandatory for wave dark matter (data), and is optional for a wave with a free coupling ([#11 App. A item 3](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)); the one form the tests did not cover, a single topological object localized in momentum, is untested ([the topological loophole](topological-loophole.md)). The tests' results stand, tagged [D] on the particle island and [D + T] on the [wave island](wave-island/_index.md) ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (i)–(iii); [the evidence](#the-evidence)). [`verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py`](../../../verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py) (section D) reproduces calc note [#2](../../../notes/stream-stability.md)'s two shortfall ratios in the gate; [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)'s tag is [D], and no script covers the other clumping numbers (flag 16 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), addendum; [the velocity-gated-gravity script](../../method/verification/velocity-gated-gravity-script.md)). [Scenario A](wave-island/scenario-a.md), the committed-wave case, is under review. The tests cover the program's contact-range interaction; a long-range attraction gated in relative velocity is a different model ([the scope of the tests](#the-scope-of-the-tests)). ## The conjecture

### The picture

The program began from P.R.'s supposition that "Matter clumps in momentum space" ([#11 App. A](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates), item 3). It stands beside his other open conjectures among the founding ideas: [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md), [their explanation of the monopole null result](../../electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md) and [conjugate collapse powering our expansion](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion.md). In the program's terms, dark matter is [conjugate matter](../conjugate-matter/_index.md), matter polarized toward momentum, and its own force "operates only between particles moving at nearly the same velocity — a gate in relative speed" ([#11 §8](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate), plain-language passage). With an attraction that acts only between particles of similar velocity, the expectation was that dark matter would gather in velocity rather than in position. The popular article [#10](../../../popsci/angle-of-the-mirror.md) states the image as "dark matter as invisible flocks, clumping not in space but in *speed*" ([`popsci/angle-of-the-mirror.md`](../../../popsci/angle-of-the-mirror.md), 'The wager').

The lab notebook made this a mean-field claim in [§5.2](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#52-collective-behavior-clumping-in-momentum-space-d-at-mean-field-level-o-beyond), titled "Collective behavior: clumping in momentum space" and tagged "[D at mean-field level; O beyond]". Its starting point is the energy of one particle in the gas:

```text
ε(p; f) = p²/2m_χ + ∫d³p′ U(p − p′) f̄(p′),     ẋ ⊃ ∇ₚε
```

"The genuinely p-local part of the interaction shifts a particle's energy by the phase-space density of *co-moving* neighbors". Here U is an interaction that depends on the momentum difference p − p′ and f̄ the averaged phase-space density. Because the energy depends on momentum through the neighbours, the velocity ẋ = ∇ₚε acquires a term beyond p/m_χ: "an anomalous velocity, the same structure as Berry-curvature transport in solids". In a crystal, the Berry curvature of an electron band adds an anomalous term to the electron's velocity, proportional to the applied force crossed with the curvature [T]; the notebook asserts the structural analogy without working out the correspondence.

### The instability

The claim proper:

> For attractive U with momentum range μ, the collisionless Boltzmann equation with this mean field is the Jeans problem *with the roles of x and v exchanged*: a smooth Maxwellian f(v) is unstable to fragmentation into **discrete velocity streams of width Δv ~ v\***, each spatially delocalized and internally coherent across kiloparsecs.

In the ordinary Jeans problem, self-gravity depends on the particles' separation in position, and in a uniform gas, perturbations longer than the Jeans length grow into lumps in space ([the Jeans instability](vlasov-stability.md#the-static-threshold-and-the-jeans-instability)) [T]. The notebook's exchanged problem has the attraction acting between particles close in velocity, within the [velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/velocity-gate.md) of width v\*, and expects lumps in velocity: streams spread through space, each with a narrow spread of velocities around its own mean.

### The analogy with superconductivity

The notebook continued: "The BCS analogy is exact in structure: a momentum-gated attraction is precisely the pairing interaction, and below a critical velocity dispersion the core of a halo condenses — momentum-space clumping, realized." BCS is the Bardeen–Cooper–Schrieffer theory of superconductivity, in which an attraction acting only between electrons within a thin shell of momentum near the Fermi surface makes the electron gas unstable to pairing below a critical temperature [T]. In the notebook's analogy, the halo's [velocity dispersion](../halo-astrophysics/velocity-dispersion.md#the-maxwellian-and-the-dynamical-temperature) plays the role of the temperature.

### What it was meant to explain

The notebook drew two consequences: "superfluid-like cores in dwarfs and **suppressed dynamical friction** inside them (the Fornax globular-cluster timing problem is a feature, not a bug)". In its [prediction table](../../predictions/table-history.md) ([`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §9](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#9-predictions)) these became two rows:

- **Row 3**, velocity-space granularity: "Fragmentation into O(10) macro-streams of width ~v\*, coherent over kpc", visible in the perturbations of cold [stellar streams](../halo-astrophysics/halo-substructure.md) ([old row 3](velocity-granularity.md));
- **Row 4**, friction in dwarf cores: "Suppressed in condensed cores; Fornax GCs stay put", with GCs the globular clusters ([old row 4](fornax-friction.md)).

Row 3 was one of the notebook's three "flagship discriminants". Its parameter count credited the two conjugate-matter parameters with "stream granularity scale, friction suppression" among the phenomena covered (§10). The checkpoint's abstract calls velocity clumping the program's "most cherished consequence" ([#11 §1](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#1-two-abstracts)).

## How it was tested

The notebook left one step undone and said so: "The growth rate γ versus orbital phase-mixing remains to be computed; it is the theory's most important undone calculation on the astrophysics side. **[O]**" (§5.2). Its open-problem list carries it as item 3, the stream-instability growth rate, which "determines whether Prediction 3's granularity survives in Milky-Way-like halos" (§11).

That calculation became the clumping problem, one of the program's [three main problems](../../history/main-problems.md), and three short calc notes carried it out ([the clumping calc notes](../../history/documents/clumping-notes.md)):

1. [`notes/stream-stability.md`](../../../notes/stream-stability.md) built [the gated Vlasov model](gated-vlasov-model.md), the notebook's mean-field picture made explicit with a Gaussian gate and a contact interaction, and computed its stability threshold with standard [Vlasov stability theory](vlasov-stability.md). Result: "negative on the particle island, with a structural corollary."
2. [`notes/wave-island.md`](../../../notes/wave-island.md) followed the corollary to very light, wave-like dark matter and computed the gated [condensation rate](wave-island/condensation-rate.md) there. Result: "rate derived, environments evaluated, verdict reached."
3. [`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) followed the one case in which clumping was mandatory, [Scenario A](wave-island/scenario-a.md), to its endpoint in the faintest galaxies. Result: "Scenario A falsified; the clumping problem closed."

## The evidence

#11 sums up the evidence in [App. A item 3](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates): as collective dynamics the image "fails for particle dark matter by 10²⁸–10³¹ (theory) and, where mandatory for wave dark matter (Scenario A, under review), against the faintest dwarfs (data), and it is optional for a free-coupling wave". Its §15: "The founding image failed the program's own calculations for particle dark matter and, where it would have been mandatory for wave dark matter, the test of the faintest dwarf galaxies; P.R. holds it open as his conjecture". The sections below give [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)'s results (i)–(iii) in full; "Results (i)–(iii) stand as results" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)).

### Particles: the attraction falls short by 28–31 orders of magnitude [D]

The threshold for streams is the [mean-field criterion](instability-criterion.md): the attraction energy among co-moving particles must reach the kinetic energy of their random motion, g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)². At the [scattering benchmark](../conjugate-matter/benchmark.md), the coupling is already fixed by fitting dwarf-galaxy cores, and the criterion fails: the ratio is about 10⁻²⁸ in a dwarf core and 10⁻³¹ in the Milky Way's halo near the Sun (#2). Below threshold there is no growth: a ripple in the velocity distribution is Landau-damped, so the race against orbital mixing that the notebook planned never begins. Collisions do not help: "The H-theorem closes the collisional back door: gated collisions still maximize entropy; equilibrium is Maxwellian to one part in 10³⁰" (#2).

The checkpoint's summary: "Mean-field criterion g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ,v\*)² fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark; below threshold there is no growth; the H-theorem closes the collisional route" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (i)).

### The corollary: collective behaviour requires a wave [D]

Meeting the criterion at fixed cross-section per mass "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (i); on the regime in which calc note #2 evaluates that mass, see flag 19 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)): at such masses about 10²⁰ quanta share each quantum state, and dark matter is a classical wave, not a gas of particles. This is [the classicality theorem](classicality-theorem.md), in #11's words: "collective momentum-space behavior and classical particles are mutually exclusive". The notebook's χ was a classical particle, at a benchmark mass of about 70 MeV ([`notes/broken-born-theory.md` §5.1](../../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#51-the-scattering-law-d)); for classical particles the instability does not occur.

### Waves: drained dwarfs where mandatory, optional where free [D + T]

On the [wave island](wave-island/_index.md), the gated attraction can condense the wave field into narrow ranges of momentum, at a rate [Γ_self](wave-island/condensation-rate.md). Whether that makes streams depends on what fixes the coupling ([#3](../../../notes/wave-island.md), 'What pins g? Two scenarios'):

- **Scenario A**, the gated channel also makes the dwarf cores. Then the coupling is fixed, and condensation outruns orbital stirring only in [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](../halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md), where it is mandatory. Because the attraction makes the condensate collapse above a [critical mass](../wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates.md), the endpoint is a cycle of collapses that ejects dark matter from the galaxy. [#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (ii): "If the gated channel also makes dwarf cores, condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarfs (Segue 1: rate ratio ~660; condensation ~10⁴ yr), whose attractive endpoint cycles above M_crit ≈ 2.9×10⁻³⁰(m/eV)^{−7/4} M_⊙ [14†] and drains the halo — **falsified** by observed UFD density and longevity; the light-mass escape is closed independently [15†, Lyman-α]", where UFD stands for ultra-faint dwarf. Segue 1, the test case, is dense and old. Scenario A is under review ([Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](wave-island/scenario-a.md)).
- **Scenario B**, the cores come from elsewhere and the coupling is free. Then "streams are tunable across ~10 orders: optional, never required" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (ii); [Scenario B](wave-island/scenario-b.md)).

### The net theorem

[The net theorem](net-theorem.md): "velocity-space clumping is nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)" ([#11 §9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) (iii)). The notebook's addendum v0.2 opens: "The clumping problem is settled, and the answer went against the theory." Calc note [#4](../../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) withdrew the two predictions the image carried: "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands. The Fornax mechanism goes with them." (#4). [#11 §13](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions) lists both rows as withdrawn and under review ([Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](velocity-granularity.md); [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](fornax-friction.md)).

### The scope of the tests

The tests used the program's own interaction, and that interaction acts on contact. Calc note #2 says "the x-range collapsed to contact (range 1/m_φ, microscopic)" and names "the force is contact-range" as one reason its verdict is robust ([`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md` §4](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md#4-what-the-obituary-covers)). The results cover all three branches of that model: particle χ, wave χ whose coupling also makes the dwarf cores, and wave χ with a free coupling. On those branches, in turn, the net theorem finds clumping "forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)". For particle χ the record's "10²⁸–10³¹" quotes the exponents of the two shortfall ratios; the shortfall factors themselves are 2.6×10²⁷ and 3.5×10³⁰ ([§4](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md#4-what-the-obituary-covers); flag 16, addendum). The results say that velocity clumping is required nowhere. They do not say that clumping cannot happen.

[`notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md`](../../../notes/velocity-gated-gravity.md) (2026-10-28, not reviewed) examined that scope. It finds that the results hold for the program as written, and that momentum gravity does not look like a perturbation of gravity that depends on two bodies' relative velocity. Its results:

- **Between ordinary bodies**, the [momentum graviton X](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md) adds a Yukawa term of range below 37.7 μm in the aligned vacuum (u = 1), unscreened. Its dependence on relative velocity is the O(v²/c²) spin-2 factor, 1 + 2.40×10⁻⁷ at 120 km/s, against 1 + 3.20×10⁻⁷ for ordinary gravity, not a gate [D][V] (§2). A relative-velocity gate acting between all bodies is bounded to |ε| < 8.7×10⁻⁹ for a Gaussian gate of width 0.3–3000 km/s in the e^{−v²/v_a²} convention, which is v\* = 0.2–2100 km/s in the record's [D; data S] (§3), where ε is the gated term's strength relative to gravity ([X between ordinary bodies](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/ordinary-bodies.md)).
- **A long-range attraction among χ, gated in relative velocity,** is a different model. The record does not contain it, and the clumping problem never tested it. No field in the record could carry it (§5). At the record's gate width the Lyman-α forest allows ε < 0.057–0.106 in the record's reading, an estimate from compressed data, while velocity-selected clumping needs ε > 1 at every gate width (§§6–7). The force could not make dwarf cores, which would need G̃/G ≈ 3.6×10³¹, so any clumping it produced would be decorative (§7; [Conjecture: a long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ](long-range-gated-attraction.md); [what could carry a relative-velocity gate](../conjugate-matter/gate-carriers.md)).
- **Narrow gates**, below about 30 km/s: N-body runs (one agent) found no velocity-selected structure up to ε = 10; one statistic shows a small offset the record does not adjudicate (§8). Corrected estimates, not likelihoods and rerun by one agent, exclude ε ≈ 2 from 10 km/s up, and ε ≈ 3 at 5–30 km/s in one of the two readings (§8).

Calc note #2's threshold for narrow gates is itself flagged: its form overstates the Milky Way shortfall about 3×, and the verdict is unchanged (flag 58 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md); [the narrow-gate form, flagged](instability-criterion.md#the-narrow-gate-form-flagged)). The gate-mechanism note, also of 2026-10-28, which finds that exchanging X cannot produce the program's gate (flag 55), leaves the tests' results unaffected and, if anything, strengthened: "a completion whose only velocity dependence is a knee in σ_T(v) leaves the forward amplitude, which drives collective behaviour, ungated" ([`notes/gate-mechanism.md` §5](../../../notes/gate-mechanism.md#5-what-follows-for-the-record); [the gate mechanism](../conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#what-flag-55-proposes)).

## In the literature

Partly anticipated ([the literature check](../../method/literature-check.md)), a verdict that counts Bose condensation of dark matter as a form of the conjecture; restricted to the founding image of several velocity flocks, it would be related only. Dark matter collecting into a single momentum state, a Bose–Einstein condensate, is published: [Riotto and Tkachev 2000](https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0003388), [Sikivie and Yang 2009](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.1106), [Berezhiani and Khoury 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.01019), and [Levkov, Panin and Tkachev 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05857) [S], the last of which #11 cites as [13]† for the [wave island](wave-island/_index.md)'s condensation rate ([§9](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); flag 65, pending). [Guth, Hertzberg and Prescod-Weinstein 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.5930) find that a condensate forms but that attractive interactions do not give it long-range order [S]; the paper treats no attraction acting in velocity, so it does not test the founding image. The founding image was not found: a smooth spread of velocities breaking into several flocks because similar velocities attract. Its nearest published analog is in opinion dynamics, where agents that interact only when their opinions are close end in "a finite number of isolated, noninteracting opinion clusters" ([Ben-Naim, Krapivsky and Redner 2003](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0212313) [S]).
