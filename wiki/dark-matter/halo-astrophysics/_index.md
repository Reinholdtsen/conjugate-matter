*A dark-matter halo is the extended cloud of dark matter in which a galaxy or a galaxy cluster sits; it outweighs the visible stars and gas and sets their orbits. How dense a halo is at its center, how fast its matter moves, and how lumpy it is on small scales all depend on whether dark matter scatters off itself. This section is established literature: the observations and models against which the program's dark-matter claims are tested.*

## Map

All three pages are established physics. They come first in [Dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter) because the program's claims are stated against them.

[Self-interacting dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter) comes first. It defines the quantity the program's [velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate) modifies — the momentum-transfer cross-section per unit mass, σ_T/m — and the standard behavior that [row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee) and [row 2](/wiki/predictions/scattering-isotropy) of the predictions table are measured against: a power-law fall with velocity, forward-peaked when the force is carried by a light particle.

[Dwarf galaxies: cores and ultra-faint dwarfs](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies) follows with the observations the program used. The flat central densities of the classical dwarfs fix the program's [scattering benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark), and the survival of the ultra-faint dwarfs, Segue 1 among them, falsified [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) of the wave island.

[Halo-substructure probes](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/halo-substructure) closes the section with cold stellar streams and dynamical friction, the probes behind the program's withdrawn [old row 3](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity) and [old row 4](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction).
