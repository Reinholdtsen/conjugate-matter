*"The framework does no work" is the referees' objection that the program's phase-space framework, Born reciprocity included, contributed nothing to the results of its dark-matter proposal. It was stated most fully in the third review round. The withdrawn manuscript under review (#14) proposed [superheavy monopoles of a hidden gauge sector](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), and the referee found that every number in it came from standard hidden-sector physics plus one choice of coupling. The program responded by making an explicit model, an action in which reciprocity is a genuine symmetry that the vacuum breaks, its next task. The toy models meet the referees' wording of that demand, but by the program's own assessment they answer the objection only in part: the locality of ordinary matter in position still enters through an assumed coupling rather than following from the vacuum.*

**Status:** Record — untagged — a review finding: predicted as the weakest link in the round-3 pre-registration, returned as the referee's second weakest link (audit notes, Step 4(d) and Step 5), and made the basis of round-3 Decision 4; "only partly answered" by the toy models (toy worklog, 'Assessment against the predictions').

## Key facts

- Pre-registered before the round-3 audit was dispatched: "**Weakest link: the framework does no work.** The candidate in §2–§4 is a standard hidden-sector monopole model. Nothing numerical depends on Born reciprocity, the compatibility theorem or the polarization identification." (`review/round3-preregistration.md`). See [round-3 pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/round-3/preregistration) and [pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/pre-registration).
- The referee's answer to "Does the phase-space framework do any work?" was "No." "The compatibility theorem (true) is never used after Sec. 1; hypothesis H is motivated, not derived (acceptance criterion 1 concedes this)". The proposal "is the standard hidden 't Hooft-Polyakov-monopole scenario ... with one coupling choice (alpha_d = alpha), and it stands or falls on that literature, independently of Born reciprocity." (audit notes, Step 4(d)). See [the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) and [the 't Hooft–Polyakov monopole](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/thooft-polyakov-monopole).
- In the referee's verdict (grade C) the critique is the second weakest link; the first is "the Kibble abundance / mass determination" (Step 5). The adjudication scored the pre-registered weakest link "**Miss on #1** (hit on #2)". See [round-3 rulings](/wiki/method/review/round-3/rulings).
- Decision 2, retiring the soliton candidate: the viable low-mass version "is Murayama–Shu's and Liveoak–Maharana–Wells's model. Nothing in it depends on Born reciprocity (the referee and the pre-registration agree), so claiming it would add no testable content and would misattribute it." See [topological dark matter in the literature](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/prior-art).
- Decision 4: "Every referee across three rounds converges on 'the framework does no work'. The toy action is the only item that can change that, and it is acceptance criterion 1 for anything the program proposes." See [round-3 decisions](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions) and [acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion).
- The toy work split its criteria to match (toy worklog): "The first tier is the referees' letter; the second is what would answer round 3." The second tier includes S5, "the vacuum choice has a consequence for matter". See [success criteria S0–S5](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/success-criteria).
- The outcome. #11 §14 item 11 records acceptance criterion 1 as "Met in the referees' wording". The worklog's assessment: "The round-3 critique ('the framework does no work') is only partly answered. The toy makes the program's structures cohere (η as order parameter; τ as the polarization angle), but matter's x-locality still enters through the P4-type coupling." The toy note: "Nothing here forces that coupling" (§5). See [locality from an (H, K)-built coupling](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/matter-coupling) and [Open: forced locality and the P4 lock](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/strong-locality).
- Also unaddressed: the handoff's version of the criterion has η "also selecting the electric sublattice" (HANDOFF §2). The toy worklog's criteria and #11 §14 item 11 do not take up that clause.
