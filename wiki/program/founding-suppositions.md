*The founding suppositions are the eleven ideas P.R. brought to the program at its start. Appendix A of the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) (#11) lists each with the fate the later work gave it, and its version 2.4, of 2026-10-29, records eight as alive in some form, two as open and one as dead. One became the program's hard core. Others survived in changed form: the conjugate universe became a second description of one state, the idea that momentum space has its own gravity became a heavy, hidden field, and dark matter as conjugate matter, P.R.'s core conjecture, now covers two of the program's three dark-matter candidates, particles that cluster in space like any other dark matter and magnetic monopoles localized in momentum. One was promoted to a theorem, and one was already established physics. Two are open as P.R.'s conjectures, restored on his direction that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and he concurs: that monopoles in conjugate space explain why none has been found, and that the collapse of the conjugate universe powers our accelerating expansion, of which two versions failed. One is recorded dead: the founding image, dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, dead as collective dynamics, with Paul's concurrence asked on 2026-10-29. Since version 2.3, monopoles in conjugate space as objects that exist and a magnetic partner for every electric particle are joined in P.R.'s open [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification). The audit is the record of which parts of the starting picture the calculations kept.*

**Status:** Record — untagged. The audit is #11 Appendix A, added in v2.0 (`HANDOFF.md` §3 row 11) and current to v2.4 (2026-10-29), which changed items 3, 4, 5 and 9; the fates are recorded in the body of #11, whose sections the appendix cites for some of the items. P.R. is Paul, who supplies the program's conjectures and direction ([the collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration)).

## The audit

#11 Appendix A is headed "Audit of founding suppositions (P.R., with fates)". It lists eleven one-line suppositions, each followed by a verdict in bold and, for some, the section of #11 that records the verdict; this page gives the section for each. The suppositions are P.R.'s; the derivations that decided their fates are the program's, under the attribution "conjecture and direction, P.R.; derivations, counterexamples, verification, drafting, Claude (Anthropic)". [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) lists the appendix among the checkpoint's additions in v2.0: "Appendix A supposition audit (11 items with fates)" (`HANDOFF.md` §3 row 11).

The fates use a small vocabulary. "Alive" with a qualifier (transformed, modified, corrected) means the idea survives in an altered form; "promoted to theorem" and "vindicated" mean a derivation turned it into a result; "open", used since v2.4, means the idea is P.R.'s conjecture [O], with the evidence against it stated beside it; "dead" means a calculation or the data killed it. Until v2.4 the vocabulary also had "split", for item 5: one half dead and the other alive. Since 2026-10-29 the program's rule is that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs, and that a failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea; deaths recorded before that date "stand until he answers the concurrence question for them, except those he has contested" (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Method'; [negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results)).

By the fates of v2.4, eight suppositions are alive in some form (items 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 11), two are open (items 5 and 9) and one is recorded dead (item 3, as dynamics). Version 2.4 changed four of them: items 5 and 9, which until v2.3 were split with a dead half (item 5) and dead twice (item 9), are open as P.R.'s conjectures; item 3's loophole has a candidate again; and item 4 names a second form of conjugate matter. The root `CLAUDE.md` records that none of the three ideas restored that day "was killed by theory and data in general, and he concurred in none of the deaths".

## How each fate was decided

### 1. Symmetrize QM around conjugate variables

P.R.'s starting idea was to treat position and momentum, the conjugate variables of quantum mechanics, symmetrically at the foundation. It survived as the program's [hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core) in a sharper form: [the quarter-turn ρ](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity): (x, p) → (p, −x) is an exact symmetry of the laws, the vacuum breaks it, and only the [symplectic form](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form) ω is rigid (#11 §3–§4). How it reached that form, through the repair of the second postulate forced by the [nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem), the credit to [Freidel, Leigh and Minic](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality), and the [toy models](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models)' [Goldstone-angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle) refinement, is told on the hard-core page.

### 2. A coexisting conjugate universe

The second universe became a second description of the same state: the universe described in momentum variables, with a momentum-space scale factor ã that is tied to ours by a·ã = 1 (#11 §10). The transformation is set out on [the conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe).

### 3. Matter clumps in momentum space

This was the program's founding image: dark matter clustering in velocity rather than in space. The clumping problem tested it on both sides of the particle–wave divide (#11 §9; [momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping)):

- **Particles [D].** The [mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion) for collective growth, g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)², "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark". Satisfying it at fixed σ₀/m "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰", so collective momentum-space behaviour and classical particles exclude each other (the [classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem)).
- **Waves [D + T].** If the same coupling also makes the cores of [dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies), condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarfs ("Segue 1: rate ratio ~660; condensation ~10⁴ yr"), where the collapsing condensate would drain the halo. That is "falsified by observed UFD density and longevity" ([Obituary: Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a)).
- **Free coupling.** Left free, velocity streams are "tunable across ~10 orders: optional, never required" ([Scenario B: tunable streams](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b)).

The net theorem: velocity-space clumping is "nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)" ([the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem)). The salvage was a new observable, [the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant). The death is of clumping as collective dynamics, and it stands as recorded: on 2026-10-29 Paul's concurrence was asked for "momentum clumping as collective dynamics (#11 §9: particle dark matter by theory, Scenario A by Segue 1's data)", as for every death recorded before that date (root `CLAUDE.md`, 'Since the handoff').

The audit's loophole, localization of individual topological objects in momentum space, is untested, and since v2.4 it has a candidate again: "its candidate, p-localized conjugate monopoles, is P.R.'s open conjecture (§12; v2.4)" ([Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles); [Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)). From v2.1 to v2.3 the item read "untested, and its candidate is retired (§12)".

### 4. Dark matter is conjugate matter

P.R.'s core conjecture is alive, and v2.4 names two forms of it, "as χ particles (x-clumping, gated self-force) or, since v2.4, as conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, two of the three candidates of §12". The root `CLAUDE.md` records it as "alive throughout (App. A item 4). Its momentum-localized form returns with the next item", the restored third candidate.

- **χ particles.** Conjugate matter χ clusters in position like any dark matter; it "propagates on g (equivalence principle; Bullet-Cluster-safe)" ([the equivalence principle](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/equivalence-principle); [the Bullet Cluster](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/bullet-cluster)), and its self-interaction passes through a gate in relative momentum. Two χ quanta interact by exchanging the massive [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), which they can do only where their momentum-space wavepackets overlap [D — mechanism; shape [O]] (#11 §8; [the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism)). `notes/gate-mechanism.md` (2026-10-28; two derivations, the second blind; not reviewed) finds that exchanging #11 §7's X cannot produce the gate, and flag 55 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism, pending Paul's ruling; the gated self-force itself is not refuted, and #11 is unchanged ([exchanging X cannot produce the gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)). The observable is a knee in the [self-interaction cross-section σ_T(v)](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law) at a velocity v\* ([row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee)). Since v2.1 χ has been a dark-sector candidate beside the [X-condensate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate) (#11 §12; [dark-sector candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates)).
- **Conjugate monopoles localized in momentum.** The third candidate of §12, "magnetically charged topological solitons of the p-polarized sector, localized in momentum", returns in v2.4 as P.R.'s conjecture, with stability by "topological charge [O]" and the handle "untested: p-localization open (§14 item 8); #14's model, with ordinary kinematics, failed" (§12's table; [Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles)).

From v2.1 to v2.3 the fate read "alive, modified: x-clumping, gated self-force; now one of two remaining candidates (§12)", and in v2.0 it ended "now one corner of a trilemma".

### 5. Monopoles live in conjugate space, explaining non-detection

#11 v2.4 records both halves as open: "open in both halves (v2.4)".

- **As an explanation, open: P.R.'s conjecture [O].** [Magnetic monopoles](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles) have never been detected, and the supposition explains this by placing them in conjugate space. #11 §11.1: "As an explanation of the null result: open [O], P.R.'s conjecture (v2.4; flag 62, ruled by P.R.), since neither ground given against it kills it. The relevant duality is electric–magnetic (E↔B, g → 1/g), not x↔p; only the shared-photon form of §11.4 contests this ground, and that form needs P4 restated. The null result is expected under several standard histories, so it lends the explanation no support: inflation after a unification transition dilutes monopoles [T], and without grand unification there are none to dilute, since the Standard Model has no topologically stable monopoles [T]; flag 59 lists further histories (†)." The explanation's setting is §11.4's [shared-photon form](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification#shared-photon), in which the momentum-polarized analogs carry magnetic charge of our own photon, and which, in §11.4's words, "requires P4 restated" and conditions that would hide the objects, "none of them yet computed". P4 is the postulate that no gauge force acts between the two polarizations of matter ([P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter)); the hiding conditions are p-localization, with the objects' field profile spread wider than detectors and than the coherence length of galactic magnetic fields, or binding into magnetically neutral composites ([Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)).
- **The explanation's history.** From v2.0 to v2.3 #11 recorded the explanation as dead. Until v2.3 the second ground read "inflationary dilution already accounts for absence [T]", and the appendix "inflation suffices"; flag 59 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` found that this showed the explanation unnecessary, not false, and Paul accepted the restatement on 2026-10-29, which v2.3 made while leaving the explanation dead. Flag 62 asked the same day whether the shared-photon form reopens it, and Paul ruled it, option B, by his direction: "Don't kill them off unless theory and data kill them off and you ask me explicitly that I concur."
- **As ontology, alive.** If conjugate-sector monopoles exist they have consequences, because "by P4, conjugate-sector solitons gravitate" ([conjugate monopoles as ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles)). P.R. supplied the correction that, in §11.1's wording, existence without consequences is not an option; the appendix words it differently, as discussed below. Since v2.4 this half carries "P.R.'s conjecture that such monopoles are abundant in momentum space (§12)", the restored third dark-matter candidate.
- **Joined with item 11.** Since v2.3 the item ends "Its ontology half, joined with item 11, is P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture (§11.4) [O]" (below, under item 11); until v2.4 it added "whether the conjecture's shared-photon form would reopen the explanation is P.R.'s ruling (flag 62)".

The candidate the item fed is the third dark-matter candidate of v2.0: topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector, which, v2.0 claimed, topology rather than collective dynamics would localize in momentum (#11 §12). The standalone manuscript #14 worked it out as superheavy 't Hooft–Polyakov monopoles of a hidden SU(2) → U(1) sector, with ordinary kinematics: "Throughout, the hidden sector is described by ordinary relativistic quantum field theory" (#14 §1). That model failed. The [round-3 review](/wiki/method/review/round-3) found its abundance misnormalized: "One defect per Hubble volume gives p ≈ 104 [V]", and "the v3 benchmark of 3×10¹³ GeV is overproduced by 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ [V]" (#11 §12; checked in `verify/soliton_obituary.py`, see [the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script); [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). #11 v2.4 adds that #14 "did not model the localization in momentum that v2.0 claimed", so the candidate itself has not been tested: it is P.R.'s open conjecture, while #14's model, in §12's words, "stays recorded dead until he answers the concurrence question put to him on 2026-10-29". Retiring the candidate had been a decision Paul delegated, round-3 Decision 2, which he narrowed on 2026-10-29 to #14's model (`review/round3-adjudication.md`, 'Decisions'). Until v2.3 the appendix read "the knot candidate it fed is retired (§12)".

### 6. Conjugate universe shrinks as ours expands

The rigidity of ω fixes the volume of a comoving cell of phase space, so the position-space scale factor a and the momentum-space scale factor ã satisfy a·ã = 1: "ω-rigidity fixes the comoving phase-space cell: a·ã = 1 (ℏ does not run) — the |Z| = 1 self-dual locus" [D] (#11 §10; derived in [#1](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) §6; on the |Z| = 1 locus, see flag 23 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). A supposition became a derived relation ([the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint)).

### 7. Our expansion reverses at their bounce

The correction is to read the two events as one: through a·ã = 1, our turnaround and the conjugate universe's bounce are the same event in two descriptions, "by constraint identity" (#11 §10; [the turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce)). The turnaround needs the dark-energy potential V(τ) to become negative, a point the documents leave unresolved (see flag 27 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`; [Open: the sign of V(τ)](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign)). The flag's addendum (2026-10-27) finds that with the potential as written the turnaround happens in a closed universe, with V ≥ 0 throughout, and never in a flat or open one ([a closed Comatter universe](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe)). It is a long-horizon prediction ([row 5](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion)); "live at DESI DR3" refers to the near-term proxy, the dark-energy drift of [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift), for which #11 §10 calls DR3 "the decisive test".

### 8. Synchronized, not causal; Fourier/holography

The two descriptions relate as a signal and its spectrum, not as cause and effect. The working content is the [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary), the derived rule w_eff = −w̃ for how energy on the momentum side acts on our expansion [D — corrected en route] (#11 §10). The holographic half is "loosened" and survives as an echo: the dark-energy field τ lives on a boundary, the Shilov boundary of the [Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain) (#11 §6; [τ as the Maslov phase of the boundary](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase)).

### 9. Their collapse is our acceleration

The supposition has the conjugate universe's collapse drive our accelerating expansion. #11 v2.4 records it as open, P.R.'s conjecture [O], with two failed versions and one route left (§10, "Founding supposition 9 [O] (P.R.; v2.4)"):

- **The first version failed by data.** With the early heuristic translation rule, collapsing conjugate matter acted on our expansion as a fluid with w = −2, a value the data exclude.
- **The second failed by the mathematics.** With the derived dictionary, dust on the momentum side acts on our expansion as dust: "dust maps to dust — the w=−2 pathology is gone because it was an artifact" (#1 §6), so collapsing conjugate dust decelerates our expansion rather than accelerating it.
- **The route that remains.** The same dictionary maps stiff content on the momentum side, w̃ = +1, the equation of state of kinetic energy, to a cosmological constant on ours: "dual stiff ↦ Λ, so a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant, and the lab notebook already names 'kinetic energy in the dual frame' as 'the natural source of a near-constant dark energy' (#1 §6)" (#11 §10). Open: "Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential" (§10), the second of which is [Open: dual kinetic energy versus τ's potential](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/dual-kinetic-link).

"The two failed versions stay recorded dead until P.R. answers the concurrence question put to him on 2026-10-29" (§10; App. A item 9); the root `CLAUDE.md` lists them first among the deaths on which his concurrence was asked that day, and the graveyard lists them under [failed versions of a restored conjecture](/wiki/status/graveyard#failed-versions-of-a-restored-conjecture). Until v2.3 the fate read "dead twice (w = −2 falsified; corrected dictionary removed the mechanism)", as the handoff's dead list does: "first version gave w = −2 (falsified); corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). Paul contested that death on 2026-10-29, and v2.4 restored the supposition as his conjecture ([Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion)).

The [popular article](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) #10, written before v2.4, says the data "already executed two of the founding ideas". The audit's record is more specific about this supposition: of its two failed versions only the first failed by the data, the second by the corrected dictionary, and the supposition itself is open.

### 10. Momentum space has its own gravity

The supposition survived in transformed form. The momentum-space partner of the metric, the field X of the [modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z), is a second graviton; two interacting massless gravitons are inconsistent ([the multi-graviton no-go theorem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go)), and X acquires a mass when the vacuum selects its polarization ([the gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs)). #11 §7 states the outcome as Born's answer: "momentum-space geometry is dynamical — a reciprocity-odd massive spin-2, its mass born of the polarization choice, its dynamics the reciprocity-symmetric branch of bimetric gravity with the second frame confined to compact Λ(4), its wedge vacuum energy zero by identity" ([Born's answer: momentum space gravitates](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer)).

### 11. A full magnetic mirror-family, quarks included

The idea already existed in the literature [T] (#11 §11.2). That every electrically charged particle has a magnetic counterpart is Montonen–Olive duality [17†-adjacent; 18†], exact in maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory; "magnetic quarks" is standard vocabulary through Seiberg duality [18†]; and the dual-superconductor picture, in which condensed magnetic charge confines quarks, is "the leading mechanism for why free quarks are never seen [T, 't Hooft–Mandelstam†]" ([the magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family); [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality); [the dual superconductor](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/dual-superconductor)).
