*The founding suppositions are the eleven ideas P.R. brought to the program at its start. Appendix A of the [checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) (#11) lists each with the fate the later work gave it. One became the program's hard core. Others survived in changed form: the conjugate universe became a second description of one state, conjugate matter became a dark-matter candidate that clusters in space like any other, and the idea that momentum space has its own gravity became a heavy, hidden field. One was promoted to a theorem, one split into a dead half and a living half, one was already established physics, and two died, including the founding image of dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space. Since version 2.3 of the checkpoint, the living half of the split one, monopoles in conjugate space as objects that exist, and the established one, a magnetic partner for every electric particle, are joined in P.R.'s open [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification). The audit is the record of which parts of the starting picture the calculations kept.*

**Status:** Record — untagged. The audit is #11 Appendix A, added in v2.0 (`HANDOFF.md` §3 row 11); the fates are recorded in the body of #11, whose sections the appendix cites for some of the items. P.R. is Paul, who supplies the program's conjectures and direction ([the collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration)).

## The audit

#11 Appendix A is headed "Audit of founding suppositions (P.R., with fates)". It lists eleven one-line suppositions, each followed by a verdict in bold and, for some, the section of #11 that records the verdict; this page gives the section for each. The suppositions are P.R.'s; the derivations that decided their fates are the program's, under the attribution "conjecture and direction, P.R.; derivations, counterexamples, verification, drafting, Claude (Anthropic)". [The handoff](/wiki/history/documents/handoff) lists the appendix among the checkpoint's additions in v2.0: "Appendix A supposition audit (11 items with fates)" (`HANDOFF.md` §3 row 11).

The fates use a small vocabulary. "Alive" with a qualifier (transformed, modified, corrected) means the idea survives in an altered form; "promoted to theorem" and "vindicated" mean a derivation turned it into a result; "split" means one part died and another survived; "dead" means a calculation or the data killed it, and the program's rule is that dead claims stay dead ([negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results)).

## How each fate was decided

### 1. Symmetrize QM around conjugate variables

P.R.'s starting idea was to treat position and momentum, the conjugate variables of quantum mechanics, symmetrically at the foundation. It survived as the program's [hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core) in a sharper form: [the quarter-turn ρ](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity): (x, p) → (p, −x) is an exact symmetry of the laws, the vacuum breaks it, and only the [symplectic form](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form) ω is rigid (#11 §3–§4). How it reached that form, through the repair of the second postulate forced by the [nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem), the credit to [Freidel, Leigh and Minic](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality), and the [toy models](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models)' [Goldstone-angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle) refinement, is told on the hard-core page.

### 2. A coexisting conjugate universe

The second universe became a second description of the same state: the universe described in momentum variables, with a momentum-space scale factor ã that is tied to ours by a·ã = 1 (#11 §10). The transformation is set out on [the conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe).

### 3. Matter clumps in momentum space

This was the program's founding image: dark matter clustering in velocity rather than in space. The clumping problem tested it on both sides of the particle–wave divide (#11 §9; [momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping)):

- **Particles [D].** The [mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion) for collective growth, g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)², "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark". Satisfying it at fixed σ₀/m "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰", so collective momentum-space behaviour and classical particles exclude each other (the [classicality theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem)).
- **Waves [D + T].** If the same coupling also makes the cores of [dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies), condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarfs ("Segue 1: rate ratio ~660; condensation ~10⁴ yr"), where the collapsing condensate would drain the halo. That is "falsified by observed UFD density and longevity" ([Obituary: Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a)).
- **Free coupling.** Left free, velocity streams are "tunable across ~10 orders: optional, never required" ([Scenario B: tunable streams](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b)).

The net theorem: velocity-space clumping is "nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)" ([the net theorem](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem)). The salvage was a new observable, [the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant). The audit's loophole, localization of individual topological objects in momentum space, is discussed under the unresolved points below.

### 4. Dark matter is conjugate matter

The modification is stated in the fate: "x-clumping, gated self-force". Conjugate matter χ clusters in position like any dark matter; it "propagates on g (equivalence principle; Bullet-Cluster-safe)" ([the equivalence principle](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/equivalence-principle); [the Bullet Cluster](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/bullet-cluster)), and its self-interaction passes through a gate in relative momentum. Two χ quanta interact by exchanging the massive [momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton), which they can do only where their momentum-space wavepackets overlap [D — mechanism; shape [O]] (#11 §8; [the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism)). `notes/gate-mechanism.md` (2026-10-28; two derivations, the second blind; not reviewed) finds that exchanging #11 §7's X cannot produce the gate, and flag 55 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism, pending Paul's ruling; the gated self-force itself is not refuted, and #11 is unchanged ([exchanging X cannot produce the gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)). The observable is a knee in the [self-interaction cross-section σ_T(v)](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law) at a velocity v\* ([row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee)). Since v2.1 it is one of two dark-sector candidates, with the [X-condensate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate) (#11 §12; [dark-sector candidates](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates)).

### 5. Monopoles live in conjugate space, explaining non-detection

#11 §11.1 gives the two halves of the split [D]:

- **As an explanation, dead.** [Magnetic monopoles](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles) have never been detected, and the supposition explained this by placing them in conjugate space. The objection, as v2.3 states it: "The relevant duality is electric–magnetic (E↔B, g → 1/g), not x↔p; only the shared-photon form of §11.4 contests this ground [O], and that form needs P4 restated. And the null result is expected under several standard histories, so it lends the program no support: inflation after a unification transition dilutes monopoles [T], and without grand unification there are none to dilute, since the Standard Model has no topologically stable monopoles [T]; flag 59 lists further histories (†). Inflation is the standard account, not an observed epoch (v2.3; flag 59). Whether the shared-photon form would reopen the explanation is P.R.'s ruling (flag 62)." P4 is the postulate that no gauge force acts between the two polarizations of matter ([P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter)), and in the [shared-photon form](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification#shared-photon) the momentum-polarized analogs carry magnetic charge of our own photon ([Obituary: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)). Until v2.3 the second ground read "inflationary dilution already accounts for absence [T]", and the appendix "inflation suffices"; flag 59 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` found that this showed the explanation unnecessary, not false, and Paul accepted the restatement on 2026-10-29. Flag 62 is pending, and v2.3 leaves the explanation dead.
- **As ontology, alive.** If conjugate-sector monopoles exist they have consequences, because "by P4, conjugate-sector solitons gravitate" ([conjugate monopoles as ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles)). P.R. supplied the correction that existence without consequences is not an option; the wording is discussed below.
- **Joined with item 11.** Since v2.3 the item ends "Its ontology half, joined with item 11, is P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture (§11.4) [O]; whether the conjecture's shared-photon form would reopen the explanation is P.R.'s ruling (flag 62)" (below, under item 11).

The "knot candidate it fed" was superheavy soliton dark matter, developed into the standalone manuscript #14 and retired in v2.1. The [round-3 review](/wiki/method/review/round-3) found its abundance misnormalized: "One defect per Hubble volume gives p ≈ 104 [V]", and "the v3 benchmark of 3×10¹³ GeV is overproduced by 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ [V]" (#11 §12; checked in `verify/soliton_obituary.py`, see [the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script); [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)).

### 6. Conjugate universe shrinks as ours expands

The rigidity of ω fixes the volume of a comoving cell of phase space, so the position-space scale factor a and the momentum-space scale factor ã satisfy a·ã = 1: "ω-rigidity fixes the comoving phase-space cell: a·ã = 1 (ℏ does not run) — the |Z| = 1 self-dual locus" [D] (#11 §10; derived in [#1](/wiki/history/documents/lab-notebook) §6; on the |Z| = 1 locus, see flag 23 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). A supposition became a derived relation ([the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint)).

### 7. Our expansion reverses at their bounce

The correction is to read the two events as one: through a·ã = 1, our turnaround and the conjugate universe's bounce are the same event in two descriptions, "by constraint identity" (#11 §10; [the turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce)). The turnaround needs the dark-energy potential V(τ) to become negative, a point the documents leave unresolved (see flag 27 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`; [Open: the sign of V(τ)](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign)). The flag's addendum (2026-10-27) finds that with the potential as written the turnaround happens in a closed universe, with V ≥ 0 throughout, and never in a flat or open one ([a closed Comatter universe](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe)). It is a long-horizon prediction ([row 5](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion)); "live at DESI DR3" refers to the near-term proxy, the dark-energy drift of [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift), for which #11 §10 calls DR3 "the decisive test".

### 8. Synchronized, not causal; Fourier/holography

The two descriptions relate as a signal and its spectrum, not as cause and effect. The working content is the [duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary), the derived rule w_eff = −w̃ for how energy on the momentum side acts on our expansion [D — corrected en route] (#11 §10). The holographic half is "loosened" and survives as an echo: the dark-energy field τ lives on a boundary, the Shilov boundary of the [Siegel domain](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain) (#11 §6; [τ as the Maslov phase of the boundary](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase)).

### 9. Their collapse is our acceleration

The supposition had the conjugate universe's collapse drive our accelerating expansion. It died twice (`HANDOFF.md` §2: "first version gave w = −2 (falsified); corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)"):

- **First, by data.** With the early heuristic translation rule, collapsing conjugate matter acted on our expansion as a fluid with w = −2, a value the data exclude.
- **Second, by the mathematics.** With the derived dictionary, dust on the momentum side acts on our expansion as dust: "dust maps to dust — the w=−2 pathology is gone because it was an artifact" (#1 §6), so collapsing conjugate matter decelerates our expansion rather than accelerating it.

The [popular article](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles) #10 says the data "already executed two of the founding ideas". The audit's record is more specific about this supposition: one of its two deaths was mathematical, since the corrected dictionary, not a measurement, removed the mechanism ([Obituary: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion)).

### 10. Momentum space has its own gravity

The supposition survived in transformed form. The momentum-space partner of the metric, the field X of the [modular field](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z), is a second graviton; two interacting massless gravitons are inconsistent ([the multi-graviton no-go theorem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go)), and X acquires a mass when the vacuum selects its polarization ([the gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs)). #11 §7 states the outcome as Born's answer: "momentum-space geometry is dynamical — a reciprocity-odd massive spin-2, its mass born of the polarization choice, its dynamics the reciprocity-symmetric branch of bimetric gravity with the second frame confined to compact Λ(4), its wedge vacuum energy zero by identity" ([Born's answer: momentum space gravitates](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer)).

### 11. A full magnetic mirror-family, quarks included

The idea already existed in the literature [T] (#11 §11.2). That every electrically charged particle has a magnetic counterpart is Montonen–Olive duality [17†-adjacent; 18†], exact in maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory; "magnetic quarks" is standard vocabulary through Seiberg duality [18†]; and the dual-superconductor picture, in which condensed magnetic charge confines quarks, is "the leading mechanism for why free quarks are never seen [T, 't Hooft–Mandelstam†]" ([the magnetic mirror-family](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family); [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality); [the dual superconductor](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/dual-superconductor)).

The program's own addition is the polarization identification [D — structural; math T] (#11 §11.3): the [Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger pairing](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) of electric and magnetic charges makes the charge lattice a symplectic space, choosing which charges count as electric is choosing a [Lagrangian polarization](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations) of it, and the duality is generated by the same matrix [[0,1],[−1,0]] as ρ ([the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice)). Since v2.3 the item adds: "Its placement in the momentum polarization, joined with item 5's ontology half, is P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture (§11.4) [O]." The conjecture: "momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged x-polarized particle having a magnetically charged p-polarized analog" (#11 §11.4). #11 adds that #14 v2 "stated a related identification", the momentum-polarized sector as the S-dual image of the electric sector, which v2 called load-bearing for its dual coupling. In the conjecture's separate-photon form, that of the second version of the withdrawn manuscript #14, the analogs are magnetic under the p-polarized sector's own gauge field, a form consistent with [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter). In its shared-photon form, the popular article #12's reading, the analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon, so that the magnetic half of electromagnetism lives in the momentum polarization; P4 as written excludes that form, so it needs P4 restated, and something must hide the analogs, p-localization or binding into magnetically neutral composites, neither of which has been computed (#11 §11.4). Until 2026-10-29 the wiki recorded #12's identification as a popsci-only obituary; flag 60 reversed that on Paul's ruling ([Conjecture: the momentum sector as the magnetic mirror](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)).

## Points the record leaves unresolved

- **Item 3's loophole.** #11 §12 says topological momentum-space localization of individual objects "remains untested, but has no candidate"; #11 §14 item 8 said from v2.1 to v2.2 that the p-localization items "are closed by the §12 obituary", and since v2.3 it reads "(v2.1 closed the soliton-spectrum and p-localization items with the §12 obituary; §11.4's shared-photon form needs p-localization again.)" #11 does not reconcile the two. Read together, the logical loophole is untested and has no candidate, and the shared-photon form of the magnetic-mirror conjecture needs p-localization again ([Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)).
- **Item 5's wording.** #11 §11.1 calls "true but irrelevant" "the empty set (a correction supplied by P.R.)"; Appendix A quotes P.R. as saying it is "measure zero". The verdict is the same either way: as an explanation of the missing monopoles the idea is dead.
- **Item 5 in the handoff.** `HANDOFF.md` §2 ties the surviving ontology to the soliton candidate ("As ontology they're alive (the soliton candidate)"). Since v2.1 the ontology stands while the candidacy is retired (#11 §11.1, §12).

The dead items, 3 as dynamics, 5 as an explanation and 9, are also on the handoff's list of dead claims ("keep dead", `HANDOFF.md` §2). They stay dead, and [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard) lists them.
