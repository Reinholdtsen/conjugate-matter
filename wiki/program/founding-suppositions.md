*The founding suppositions are the eleven ideas P.R. brought to the program at its start. Appendix A of the [checkpoint](../history/documents/checkpoint.md) ([#11](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md)) lists each with the fate the later work gave it: eight are alive in some form and three are open. One became the program's hard core. Others survived in changed form: the conjugate universe became a second description of one state, and the idea that momentum space has its own gravity became a heavy, hidden field. Dark matter as conjugate matter, P.R.'s core conjecture, is alive and covers two of the program's three dark-matter candidates, particles that cluster in space like any other dark matter and magnetic monopoles localized in momentum. One was promoted to a theorem, and one was already established physics. Three are P.R.'s open conjectures: that monopoles in conjugate space explain why none has been found; that the collapse of the conjugate universe powers our accelerating expansion, two versions of which failed; and that dark matter clumps in velocity rather than in space, the founding image, which the program's calculations found required nowhere. Monopoles in conjugate space as objects that exist and a magnetic partner for every electric particle are joined in P.R.'s open [magnetic-mirror conjecture](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md). The audit is the record of which parts of the starting picture the calculations kept.*

## The audit

[#11 Appendix A](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates) is headed "Audit of founding suppositions (P.R., with fates)". It lists eleven one-line suppositions, each followed by a verdict in bold and, for some, the section of #11 that records the verdict; this page gives the section for each. The suppositions are P.R.'s; the derivations behind their fates are the program's, under the attribution "conjecture and direction, P.R.; derivations, counterexamples, verification, drafting, Claude (Anthropic)". [The handoff](../history/documents/handoff.md) lists the appendix among the checkpoint's additions in v2.0: "Appendix A supposition audit (11 items with fates)" ([`HANDOFF.md` §3 row 11](../../HANDOFF.md#3-artifact-inventory-creation-order)).

The fates use a small vocabulary. "Alive" with a qualifier (transformed, corrected) means the idea survives in an altered form; "promoted to theorem" and "vindicated" mean a derivation turned it into a result; "open" means the idea is P.R.'s conjecture [O], with the evidence against it stated beside it.

Eight suppositions are alive in some form (items 1, 2, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10 and 11), and three are open (items 3, 5 and 9).

## Each supposition

### 1. Symmetrize QM around conjugate variables

P.R.'s starting idea was to treat position and momentum, the conjugate variables of quantum mechanics, symmetrically at the foundation. It survived as the program's [hard core](hard-core.md) in a sharper form: [the quarter-turn ρ](../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md): (x, p) → (p, −x) is an exact symmetry of the laws, the vacuum breaks it, and only the [symplectic form](../phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form.md) ω is rigid ([#11 §3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question)–[§4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates)). How it reached that form, through the repair of the second postulate forced by the [nontriviality theorem](postulates/nontriviality-theorem.md), the credit to [Freidel, Leigh and Minic](../phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality.md), and the [toy models](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md)' [Goldstone-angle](../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle.md) refinement, is told on the hard-core page.

### 2. A coexisting conjugate universe

The second universe became a second description of the same state: the universe described in momentum variables, with a momentum-space scale factor ã that is tied to ours by a·ã = 1 ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)). The transformation is set out on [the conjugate universe](conjugate-universe.md).

### 3. Matter clumps in momentum space

This is the program's founding image: dark matter clustering in velocity rather than in space. The clumping problem tested it on both sides of the particle–wave divide ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [momentum clumping](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/_index.md)):

- **Particles [D].** The [mean-field criterion](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion.md) for collective growth, g·n_gate ≥ m·min(σ, v\*)², "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark". Satisfying it at fixed σ₀/m "forces m ≲ 0.1 meV, where N_occ ~ 10²⁰", so collective momentum-space behaviour and classical particles exclude each other (the [classicality theorem](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem.md)).
- **Waves [D + T].** If the same coupling also makes the cores of [dwarf galaxies](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md), condensation is mandatory only in ultra-faint dwarfs ("Segue 1: rate ratio ~660; condensation ~10⁴ yr"), where the collapsing condensate would drain the halo. That is "falsified by observed UFD density and longevity" (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf), a case under review ([Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md)).
- **Free coupling.** Left free, velocity streams are "tunable across ~10 orders: optional, never required" ([Scenario B: tunable streams](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b.md)).

The net theorem: velocity-space clumping is "nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)" ([the net theorem](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem.md)). The salvage was a new observable, [the island discriminant](../predictions/island-discriminant.md).

The fate is "open", P.R.'s conjecture [O] ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md)). These results stand as results ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). They test the program's contact-range gate on all three branches, and they say clumping is required nowhere, not that it is impossible; the velocity-gated-gravity note of 2026-10-28 (not reviewed) examined that scope and finds that the results hold for the program as written (root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), 'Since the handoff'; [the scope of the tests](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests)).

The audit's loophole, localization of individual topological objects in momentum space, "remains untested; its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles" (§12; [Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md); [Open: the topological loophole](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole.md)). §14 item 8 lists p-localization as open, since the third candidate of §12 and §11.4's shared-photon form need it: "p-local solitons are p-localized" [O].

### 4. Dark matter is conjugate matter

P.R.'s core conjecture is alive, in two forms: as χ particles (x-clumping, gated self-force) or as conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, two of the three candidates of §12 (App. A item 4).

- **χ particles.** Conjugate matter χ clusters in position like any dark matter; it "propagates on g (equivalence principle; Bullet-Cluster-safe)" ([the equivalence principle](../gravity/spin-2-fields/equivalence-principle.md); [the Bullet Cluster](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/bullet-cluster.md)), and its self-interaction passes through a gate in relative momentum. Two χ quanta interact by exchanging the massive [momentum graviton X](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), which they can do only where their momentum-space wavepackets overlap [D — mechanism; shape [O]] ([#11 §8](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#8-conjugate-matter-and-the-gate); [the gate mechanism](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md)). [`notes/gate-mechanism.md`](../../notes/gate-mechanism.md) (2026-10-28; two derivations, the second blind; not reviewed) finds that exchanging [#11 §7](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton)'s X cannot produce the gate, and flag 55 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending, proposes [P] for the gate and μ and [O] for the mechanism; the gated self-force itself is not refuted ([exchanging X cannot produce the gate](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)). The observable is a knee in the [self-interaction cross-section σ_T(v)](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law.md) at a velocity v\* ([row 1](../predictions/self-interaction-knee.md)). χ is a dark-sector candidate beside the [X-condensate](../dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate.md) ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates); [dark-sector candidates](../dark-matter/candidates/_index.md)).
- **Conjugate monopoles localized in momentum.** The third candidate of §12, "magnetically charged topological solitons of the p-polarized sector, localized in momentum", is P.R.'s conjecture, with stability by "topological charge [O]" and the handle "untested: p-localization open (§14 item 8); never modeled" (§12's table; [Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md)).
- **In the literature.** The identification is published, as a proposal that none of its authors derives ([dark matter as the dual Standard Model](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-standard-model.md)). Djordje Minic first stated it in an invited talk in Belgrade on 9 September 2019, whose [slides](https://www.mphys10.ipb.ac.rs/slides/Minic.pdf) are public, and in writing in [Minic 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2003.00318), posted on 29 February 2020: "The proposal here is that the dual sector (as already indicated in the previous section) should be interpreted as the dark matter sector" (§6.2) [S]. He and coauthors restate it in papers to April 2026, among them [Berglund, Hübsch and Minić 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.15610) [S]. Their "dual" has three senses: in their account of quantum mechanics, momentum, rescaled, in one phase space at ℏ, the program's sense; where the dark sector is built, a coordinate of a doubled phase space at the string length, with a momentum of its own; and, in one review, dual fields that do not commute with ours. So their model doubles phase space where the program polarizes one. The literature check's verdict, anticipated, would be partly anticipated if strict P4 and ordinary momentum, rather than the dual coordinate at the string scale, are counted as part of the conjecture, or if its gloss, matter *organized around momentum*, is read as a clause about dynamics, dark matter that interacts or is localized in momentum; which reading is P.R.'s conjecture is open ([which reading](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-standard-model.md#which-reading-of-the-conjecture)). "Correlated" is the group's own word for how its dual sector meets ours: at leading order in the string length the two sectors are independent; beyond it they are coupled beyond gravity, with a strength no paper computes; and in [Hübsch and Minic 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.06207) (§5.3) they interact "only gravitationally" below a decoupling energy of about 4.5×10¹⁴ GeV, a statement their papers of 2026 do not repeat. The group explains why dark-matter searches have seen nothing so far and expects detection, where the program requires a [permanent null](../predictions/direct-detection-null.md). The identification joined to coupling by gravity alone at accessible energies is therefore published once, and never joined to a permanent null. The program's conjugate matter couples to ours by gravity alone in every form but the [shared-photon form](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md#shared-photon) of the monopole candidate, and neither of its two forms was found in the literature, inside the group or outside it ([the literature check](../method/literature-check.md); flag 64, pending, with its addendum). Copies of ordinary matter coupled to ours mainly or only by gravity, mirror and twin matter, are published without reciprocity, and no one outside the group was found to state the identification itself. [#11 §3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question) credits the preferred polarization that breaks Born duality to Freidel, Leigh and Minic and counts as its own the spontaneous reading. [Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman, Leigh and Minic 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00802) place a symmetry breaking tied to the choice of polarization "at the level of the vacuum sector", for a dual Lorentz symmetry that is not a canonical transformation and so not ρ; what was not found is the spontaneous breaking of the exact, ω-preserving ρ, with the neutral metric η, the structure that marks which directions count as position, as [order parameter](../symmetry-breaking/order-parameter.md) (flag 70, pending; [broken Born duality](../phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality.md)).

### 5. Monopoles live in conjugate space, explaining non-detection

#11 records the supposition as "**open in both halves**" ([App. A item 5](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)). In the ontology half, the statement that such objects gravitate is "alive and consequence-bearing" ([§11.1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#111-the-monopole-audit-revised-d)), and what is open is the conjecture attached to it, that they are abundant in momentum space ([the statement](../electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles.md#the-statement)).

- **As an explanation, open: P.R.'s conjecture [O].** [Magnetic monopoles](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/_index.md) have never been detected, and the supposition explains this by placing them in conjugate space. In [#11 §11.1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#111-the-monopole-audit-revised-d) neither ground given against it kills it: "The relevant duality is electric–magnetic (E↔B, g → 1/g), not x↔p; only the shared-photon form of §11.4 contests this ground, and that form needs P4 restated. The null result is expected under several standard histories, so it lends the explanation no support: inflation after a unification transition dilutes monopoles [T], and without grand unification there are none to dilute, since the Standard Model has no topologically stable monopoles [T]; flag 59 lists further histories (†)." An expected null result shows the explanation unnecessary, not false (flag 59 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)). The explanation's setting is §11.4's [shared-photon form](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md#shared-photon) (flag 62), in which the momentum-polarized analogs carry magnetic charge of our own photon, and which, in §11.4's words, "requires P4 restated" and conditions that would hide the objects, "none of them yet computed". P4 is the postulate that no gauge force acts between the two polarizations of matter ([P4](postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md)); the hiding conditions are p-localization, with the spread in the objects' field profile rather than their quantum state, wider than detectors and than the coherence length of galactic magnetic fields, and decoupled from the photon–baryon plasma before recombination (†), or binding into magnetically neutral composites ([Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md)).
- **As ontology, alive.** If conjugate-sector monopoles exist they have consequences, because "by P4, conjugate-sector solitons gravitate" ([conjugate monopoles as ontology](../electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles.md)). P.R. supplied the correction that, in §11.1's wording, existence without consequences is not an option; the appendix words it differently, as discussed below. This half carries "P.R.'s conjecture that such monopoles are abundant in momentum space (§12)", the third dark-matter candidate.
- **Joined with item 11.** The item ends "Its ontology half, joined with item 11, is P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture (§11.4) [O]" (below, under item 11).

The candidate the item fed is the third dark-matter candidate of v2.0: topological solitons of the momentum-polarized sector, which topology rather than collective dynamics would localize in momentum ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). It has never been modeled: [a withdrawn paper on monopole dark matter](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) set out to, but described its monopoles with ordinary kinematics, at points in space, a different object, and its model failed on its own terms ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates)). The candidate is P.R.'s open conjecture.

### 6. Conjugate universe shrinks as ours expands

The rigidity of ω fixes the volume of a comoving cell of phase space, so the position-space scale factor a and the momentum-space scale factor ã satisfy a·ã = 1: "ω-rigidity fixes the comoving phase-space cell: a·ã = 1 (ℏ does not run) — the |Z| = 1 self-dual locus" [D] ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); derived in [#1](../history/documents/lab-notebook.md) [§6](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary); on the |Z| = 1 locus, see flag 23 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)). A supposition became a derived relation ([the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint.md)).

### 7. Our expansion reverses at their bounce

The correction is to read the two events as one: through a·ã = 1, our turnaround and the conjugate universe's bounce are the same event in two descriptions, "by constraint identity" ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); [the turnaround as the conjugate bounce](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce.md)). As the documents describe it, the turnaround needs the dark-energy potential V(τ) to become negative, a point they leave unresolved (see flag 27 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md); [Open: the sign of V(τ)](../cosmology/dark-energy/potential-sign.md)). The flag's addendum (2026-10-27) finds that with the potential as written the turnaround happens in a closed universe, with V ≥ 0 throughout, and never in a flat or open one ([a closed Comatter universe](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md)). It is a long-horizon prediction ([row 5](../predictions/fate-of-expansion.md)); "live at DESI DR3" refers to the near-term proxy, the dark-energy drift of [row 4](../predictions/dark-energy-drift.md), for which [#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology) calls DR3 "the decisive test".

### 8. Synchronized, not causal; Fourier/holography

The two descriptions relate as a signal and its spectrum, not as cause and effect. The working content is the [duality dictionary](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary.md), the derived rule w_eff = −w̃ for how energy on the momentum side acts on our expansion [D — corrected en route] ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)), which as a map of fluid variables is the scale-factor duality of string cosmology with sources (flag 66 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending). The holographic half is "loosened" and survives as an echo: the dark-energy field τ lives on a boundary, the Shilov boundary of the [Siegel domain](../phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain.md) ([#11 §6](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field); [τ as the Maslov phase of the boundary](../phase-space/modular-field/maslov-phase.md)).

### 9. Their collapse is our acceleration

The supposition has the conjugate universe's collapse drive our accelerating expansion. #11 records it as open, P.R.'s conjecture [O], with two failed versions and one route left ([§10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology); [Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion.md)):

- **The first version failed by data.** With the early heuristic translation rule, collapsing conjugate matter acted on our expansion as a fluid with w = −2, a value the data exclude.
- **The second failed by the mathematics.** With the derived dictionary, dust on the momentum side acts on our expansion as dust: "dust maps to dust — the w=−2 pathology is gone because it was an artifact" ([#1 §6](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary)), so collapsing conjugate dust decelerates our expansion rather than accelerating it.
- **The route that remains.** The same dictionary maps stiff content on the momentum side, w̃ = +1, the equation of state of kinetic energy, to a cosmological constant on ours: "dual stiff ↦ Λ, so a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant, and the lab notebook already names 'kinetic energy in the dual frame' as 'the natural source of a near-constant dark energy' ([#1 §6](../../notes/broken-born-theory.md#6-cosmology-i-the-constraint-and-the-corrected-dictionary))" ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)). Open: "Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential" ([§10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)), the second of which is [Open: dual kinetic energy versus τ's potential](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/dual-kinetic-link.md).

The [popular article](../history/documents/popular-articles.md) [#10](../../popsci/angle-of-the-mirror.md) says the data "already executed two of the founding ideas". The audit is more specific about this supposition: of its two failed versions only the first failed by the data, the second by the corrected dictionary, and the supposition itself is open.

### 10. Momentum space has its own gravity

The supposition survived in transformed form. The momentum-space partner of the metric, the field X of the [modular field](../phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z.md), is a second graviton; two interacting massless gravitons are inconsistent ([the multi-graviton no-go theorem](../gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go.md)), and X acquires a mass when the vacuum selects its polarization ([the gravitational Higgs mechanism](../gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs.md)). [#11 §7](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#7-the-massive-momentum-graviton) states the outcome as Born's answer: "momentum-space geometry is dynamical — a reciprocity-odd massive spin-2, its mass born of the polarization choice, its dynamics the reciprocity-symmetric branch of bimetric gravity with the second frame confined to compact Λ(4), its wedge vacuum energy zero by identity" ([Born's answer: momentum space gravitates](../gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer.md)).

### 11. A full magnetic mirror-family, quarks included

The idea already existed in the literature [T] ([#11 §11.2](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#112-the-full-dual-spectrum-t)). That every electrically charged particle has a magnetic counterpart is Montonen–Olive duality [17†-adjacent; 18†], exact in maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory; "magnetic quarks" is standard vocabulary through Seiberg duality [18†]; and the dual-superconductor picture, in which condensed magnetic charge confines quarks, is "the leading mechanism for why free quarks are never seen [T, 't Hooft–Mandelstam†]" ([the magnetic mirror-family](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-mirror-family.md); [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](../electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality.md); [the dual superconductor](../electric-magnetic-duality/dual-superconductor.md)).

The program's own addition is the polarization identification [D — structural; math T] ([#11 §11.3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#113-the-identification-d--structural-math-t)): the [Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger pairing](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization.md) of electric and magnetic charges makes the charge lattice a symplectic space, choosing which charges count as electric is choosing a [Lagrangian polarization](../phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations.md) of it, and the duality is generated by the same matrix [[0,1],[−1,0]] as ρ ([the symplectic charge lattice](../electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice.md)); that electric–magnetic duality is a case of Born reciprocity, in the phase space of the electromagnetic field, is a published remark (flag 67 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending). The item ends: "Its placement in the momentum polarization, joined with item 5's ontology half, is P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture (§11.4) [O]." The conjecture: "momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged x-polarized particle having a magnetically charged p-polarized analog" ([#11 §11.4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#114-the-magnetic-mirror-conjecture-o-pr-v23)). #11 adds a related identification, the momentum-polarized sector as the S-dual image of the electric sector. In its shared-photon form, the popular article [#12](../../popsci/universes-other-half.md)'s reading, the analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon, so that the magnetic half of electromagnetism lives in the momentum polarization; P4 as written excludes that form, so it needs P4 restated, and something must hide the analogs, p-localization or binding into magnetically neutral composites, neither of which has been computed ([#11 §11.4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#114-the-magnetic-mirror-conjecture-o-pr-v23); flag 60 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)).
