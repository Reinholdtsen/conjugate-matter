*The assessment is the checkpoint's own judgement of where the program stands, written in the terms of Imre Lakatos's account of research programmes: a hard core that is kept fixed, auxiliary claims that may be revised, and the question of whether the program keeps producing new predictions (progressive) or only accommodates failures (degenerating). Checkpoint #11 v2.2 finds the hard core intact and sharpened twice by corrections the program's own mathematics forced. It lists the signs of progress and the risks of degeneration side by side, and it notes that the nearest tests come soon, with the current dark-energy data already leaning against one prediction. Its verdict is that this is a live research program, and that the word "theory" waits on three open problems.*

**Status:** Record — untagged (a self-assessment, not a derivation) — #11 §15, as amended in v2.1 after the round-3 review; it supersedes the v1 assessment (#9 §11).

## Key facts

- Hard core (#11 §15): "(spontaneously broken reciprocity, ω-only rigidity) intact, twice sharpened by forced corrections (P2′; the w-dictionary)." See [the hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core), [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime), [the duality dictionary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary) and [Lakatos's methodology](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology).
- Progressive signs (#11 §15): "forced convergence onto consistency islands; predictions not previously posed (rows 3, 11); an unsolicited identity (§7.3); one over-constraint (DE sector); the §11.3 unification generating new questions." The DE sector is the dark-energy sector; the identity of §7.3 is [D][V], battery test T5 ([the verification battery](/wiki/method/verification/battery)). See [row 3](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant), [row 11](/wiki/predictions/dark-sector-microstructure), [the vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity), [the dark-energy over-constraint](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/over-constraint) and [the symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice).
- Forced convergence: #11 §15 counts "four consecutive consistency tests" that "each forced the theory onto the unique consistent structure available". The v1 paper names the chain "no-go → Higgs → HR class → palindrome" (#9 §11), HR being Hassan–Rosen. The Debt-1 note counted three ("multi-graviton no-go, signature obstruction, tuning test") and called repeated forced convergence onto consistency islands "the opposite of degeneration" (#7 §5); #11, #9 and HANDOFF §1 count four, and #11 governs. See [the multi-graviton no-go theorem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go), [the gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs), [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric), [the tuning test](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test) and [the palindrome](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome).
- Degeneration risks (#11 §15): "tunable wave-island collective sector (quarantined); gate tail not yet forced; the novelty demotion of §7.4 as a standing caution, joined in v2.1 by the §12 soliton obituary and the §5 re-attribution of the compatibility theorem; DESI DR2's phantom crossing against §10's strict w ≥ −1." DESI DR2 is Data Release 2 of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument. See [Scenario B](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b), [Open: the gate shape](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape), [Open: the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison), [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), [the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem) and [DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing).
- Falsification exposure (#11 §15): "genuine and near-term (rows 1, 4, 7)". See [row 1](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee), [row 4](/wiki/predictions/dark-energy-drift) and [row 7](/wiki/predictions/direct-detection-null).

The v1 paper had claimed "at least two results that stand on their own"; #11 counts one, plus the identity awaiting its novelty verdict. See [the symplectic partner of the graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/symplectic-partner).
