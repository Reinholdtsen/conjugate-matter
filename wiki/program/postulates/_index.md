*The postulates are the five axioms from which the Broken-Born program derives its results. Physics lives on phase space, the space of positions and momenta together (P1); of its structures only the symplectic form, which encodes the constancy of Planck's constant, is fixed in advance (P2′); the laws are unchanged by the quarter-turn that exchanges position and momentum (P3); ordinary matter is organized around position and conjugate matter around momentum, with only gravity acting between them (P4); and the vacuum selects one of the two organizations (P5). The first version of the second axiom fixed one structure too many, and the program's own geometry showed that this reduced it to general relativity plus one model of dark matter; the corrected axiom, P2′, replaced it.*

## Map

Checkpoint #11 states the postulates in §4 and records in §5 why the second one changed. Every page here is the program's own: the five postulates are tagged [P], and the nontriviality theorem is [D]. The structures they name are established mathematics, defined in [Born geometry](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry). The pages run in #11's order, with the original P2 and the theorem that removed it placed just before P2′.

- [P1: the phase-space arena](/wiki/program/postulates/p1-arena) comes first because every other postulate refers to its structures: phase space with a symplectic form ω and a positive generalized metric H.
- [Original P2: both structures rigid (superseded)](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2) is the lab notebook's first rigidity axiom, which fixed both ω and the neutral metric η. It is kept as the record of what the mathematics rejected.
- [The nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem) is the argument that rejected it: the program has content beyond general relativity plus self-interacting dark matter only if η is emergent.
- [P2′: only the symplectic form is rigid](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime) is the corrected axiom, with η chosen by the vacuum as the order parameter, refined in v2.2 by the toy models.
- [P3: a reciprocity-invariant action](/wiki/program/postulates/p3-reciprocity) is the symmetry axiom itself, and the reason the referees asked for an explicit action.
- [P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) places ordinary matter and conjugate matter in opposite polarizations with gravity in common, read strictly since v2.1.
- [P5: the vacuum selects a polarization](/wiki/program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection) closes the list with the breaking: ours is the position-tilted vacuum, and the mirror vacuum is equally lawful.
