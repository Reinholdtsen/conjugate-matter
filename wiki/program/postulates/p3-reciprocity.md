*P3 is the program's symmetry axiom: the action, the single expression from which the field equations follow, is unchanged by the Born rotation, the quarter-turn of phase space that sends position to momentum and momentum to minus position, generated by the complex structure I = ω⁻¹H built from the two structures of P1. In ordinary quantum mechanics that exchange is a change of description, like switching from a waveform to its spectrum: a unitary map between equivalent representations of the commutation relations. P3 claims more, a symmetry of a specific action, which is the kind of symmetry a vacuum can break spontaneously. The referees asked for an explicit action with that property, and the toy models supplied one.*

**Status:** Alive — [P] — a postulate of #11 §4; the coset toy model realizes it in an explicit action whose invariance under ρ is checked symbolically (`verify/toy_coset.py`, [the toy-model scripts](/wiki/method/verification/toy-model-scripts)), which shows that the structure is consistent, not that P3 holds in nature.

## Key facts

- Statement (#11 §4):

  ```text
  P3  The action is invariant under the Born rotation generated by
      I = ω⁻¹H.                                                       [P]
  ```

- Lab-notebook statement (#1 §1, v0.1): "**P3 (Reciprocity).** The action is invariant under the Born rotation ρ: (x,p) → (p,−x), the ℤ₄ generated by the complex structure I of §2. **[P]**" See [Born reciprocity](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity).
- How ρ acts on the structures (#1 §2, [D]): "R preserves ω (it is symplectic), preserves H (it is orthogonal), and **flips the sign of η**, hence flips J and K while preserving I." See [the structures ω, η, H](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures).
- Change of description versus symmetry. The withdrawn manuscript #14 stated the distinction the program must meet: "as usually encountered, rho is a unitary equivalence between representations of the canonical commutation relations, not a Noether symmetry of a specified action. The framework's claim is that rho is a genuine symmetry of an underlying action, spontaneously broken" (#14 §1). See [unitary equivalence versus Noether symmetry](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/unitary-equivalence).
- The referees' demand (rounds 1–2, as the toy worklog records it): "an action in which ρ is a genuine Noether symmetry, spontaneously broken, with η the order parameter". The worklog's criterion S1 requires that "S is invariant, not merely related to its image by a unitary equivalence". See [acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion) and [success criteria S0–S5](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/success-criteria).
- The toy realization, model I (toy note §2): "Sp(2,ℝ) acts by H → SᵀHS, K → S⁻¹KS, and the action is invariant (checked for generic symplectic S, so for ρ). There is a separate ℤ₂, K → −K." See [model I: action and symmetries](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/action-and-symmetries). #11 §14 item 11 records the criterion as "Met in the referees' wording": "ρ is a symmetry of an explicit action, and η = ωK is the order parameter".
- The separate ℤ₂ has a consequence: the vacuum keeps the twisted element ρ∘(K → −K), so "Breaking ρ is physical only once a coupling odd in K is present" (toy note §2). See [the twisted element](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/twisted-element).
