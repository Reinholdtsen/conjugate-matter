*In the current program the conjugate universe is a second description of our universe rather than a second world: the description of the single physical state in momentum variables, related to the ordinary description the way a sound's spectrum is related to its waveform. It began as one of the program's founding suppositions, a second universe organized around momentum and existing alongside ours. The two descriptions are synchronized rather than causally linked, so an event in one is the same event read in the other; this is how the program reads its predicted far-future halt and reversal of our expansion as a bounce of the conjugate universe. The idea that observers might exist in the momentum description appears only in the popular articles and has no status in the program.*

**Status:** Alive, transformed — untagged in #11 Appendix A; the relations that carry it, the cosmological constraint a·ã = 1 and the corrected duality dictionary, are [D] — the fate is recorded in #11 App. A items 2 and 8, and the relations are derived in #11 §10; the "other observers" speculation is popsci-only and has no status.
