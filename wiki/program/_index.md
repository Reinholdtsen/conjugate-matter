*The Comatter program, called the Broken-Born program (BBT) until 2026-10-26, is a speculative research program in theoretical physics, developed by P.R. with Claude (Anthropic) since September 2026. It starts from [Born reciprocity](../phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md), the exchange of position and momentum. Quantum mechanics relates a state's position description to its momentum description as a waveform to its spectrum, and the exchange, with one change of sign, leaves the rules of quantum kinematics, which fix how position and momentum relate before any forces are specified, exactly unchanged. The program takes the exchange to be an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum, the universe's lowest-energy state, breaks spontaneously. A cooled magnet is the standard picture: the laws governing its atoms single out no direction, but its magnetized state points in one. The program follows the consequences for gravity, dark matter and dark energy. Its calculations find its founding image, [dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md), required nowhere: for particle dark matter the collective pull falls short by thirty orders of magnitude, and where clumping would be mandatory for wave dark matter, the faintest dwarf galaxies weigh against it. The image is P.R.'s open conjecture. A third dark-matter candidate, [magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](../dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles.md), is P.R.'s open conjecture and has never been modeled, beside [two other candidates](../dark-matter/candidates/_index.md): particles of [conjugate matter](../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/_index.md), the counterpart of ordinary matter organized around momentum, and a condensate, a coherent oscillation of the [momentum graviton](../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md), a second, massive gravitational field. In the project's own words its status is a [Lakatosian](../method/lakatosian-methodology.md) research program, not a theory: in the philosopher Imre Lakatos's account, a body of work built around a fixed core assumption and judged by whether its revisions lead to new predictions, some of them confirmed.*

## Map

The section follows the frame of [checkpoint #11](../history/documents/checkpoint.md) ([`wiki/papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md)): its two abstracts ([§1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#1-two-abstracts)), the question ([§3](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question)), the postulates ([§4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates)–[§5](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem)), the assessment ([§15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment)) and the audit of founding suppositions ([Appendix A](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)). The [home page](../_index.md) gives #11's technical abstract with its tags. Born's question is established physics; everything after it is the program's own claim or its own record. The repository's one-line README ("Physics hypothesis explaining dark matter and missing magnetic monopoles") is out of date (see flag 37 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)): it states as the program's result an explanation of the missing monopoles that the record holds as P.R.'s [open conjecture](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md), and the dark-matter candidates are all open.

- [Born's question](borns-question.md) comes first because the program is an answer to it. Quantum kinematics is unchanged when position and momentum are exchanged, yet the form of the Hamiltonian, the locality of interactions, the mass shell and [general relativity](../gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity.md)'s [flat momentum fibers](../phase-space/reciprocity/momentum-fibers.md) all favour position. The page sets out the symmetry, where the asymmetry lives, the earlier work on the question, and how far the program's answer reaches.
- [The hard core](hard-core.md) is the program's protected assumption, its answer to the question: Born reciprocity is exact and spontaneously broken, only the [symplectic form](../phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form.md) ω is rigid, and the [neutral metric](../phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md) η is the [order parameter](../symmetry-breaking/order-parameter.md). The page explains the structures ω, η and H, why only ω is rigid, what is the program's own, the [toy models](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md)' v2.2 refinement (a [Goldstone angle](../symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle.md) that needs a potential, and a [twisted element](../symmetry-breaking/twisted-element.md) that leaves the breaking without physical effect unless matter couples to the sign of the order parameter), the statement's history and its open ends. It stands, tagged [P].
- [Postulates P1–P5](postulates/_index.md) state the hard core as five axioms. The section includes the original P2, which proved too rigid, and the [nontriviality theorem](postulates/nontriviality-theorem.md) that forced its replacement by [P2′](postulates/p2-prime.md).
- [The conjugate universe](conjugate-universe.md) follows the postulates because it is their reading of the founding picture of a second universe. In the current program it is the same state described in momentum variables, tied to ours by [the constraint a·ã = 1](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint.md) and [the duality dictionary](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary.md), with the far-future turnaround as one event in two descriptions.
- [Founding suppositions and their fates](founding-suppositions.md) is a record: the eleven suppositions P.R. brought to the program, each with the fate [#11 Appendix A](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates) assigns it and the calculation or argument that decided it, from "the hard core" to "open": eight are alive in some form and three are open as P.R.'s conjectures.
- [The 'framework does no work' critique](framework-does-no-work.md) is the referees' main objection, stated most fully in [review round 3](../method/review/round-3/_index.md) against [a withdrawn paper on monopole dark matter](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md): nothing numerical in it depended on Born reciprocity. It is why the program retired that paper's model rather than claim a version of it that does not depend on the framework, and it set the toy models as the next task; they meet the referees' wording but answer the objection only in part.
- [Assessment: a live research program](assessment.md) closes the section with [#11 §15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment)'s Lakatosian self-assessment: the hard core intact and twice sharpened, the progressive signs and the degeneration risks, the near-term falsification rows 1, 4 and 7, and the open problems the word "theory" waits on.
