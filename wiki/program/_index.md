*The Broken-Born program (BBT) is a speculative research program in theoretical physics. It takes Born reciprocity, the exchange of position and momentum under which quantum kinematics is exactly invariant, to be an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum breaks spontaneously, and it follows the consequences for gravity, dark matter and dark energy. Its own calculations killed its founding image, [dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image), and after a later review it retired a third dark-matter candidate, [superheavy solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary); [two candidates remain](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates), particles and a condensate. In the project's own words its status is a Lakatosian research program, not a theory.*

## Map

The section follows the frame of checkpoint #11 (`papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`): its two abstracts (§1), the question (§3), the postulates (§4–§5), the assessment (§15) and the audit of founding suppositions (Appendix A). Born's question is established physics. Everything after it is the program's own claim or its own record. The repository's one-line README ("Physics hypothesis explaining dark matter and missing magnetic monopoles") is out of date: the monopole explanation is [dead](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation), and both remaining dark-matter candidates are open.

- [Born's question](/wiki/program/borns-question) comes first because the program is an answer to it: quantum kinematics treats position and momentum alike, yet the known interactions are local in position. It is established physics, with the program's framing.
- [The hard core](/wiki/program/hard-core) is the program's protected assumption, its answer to the question: Born reciprocity is exact and spontaneously broken, only the symplectic form ω is rigid, and the neutral metric η is the order parameter. It stands, tagged [P].
- [Postulates P1–P5](/wiki/program/postulates) state the hard core as five axioms. The section includes the original P2, which the program's own geometry showed to be too rigid, and the nontriviality theorem that forced its replacement by P2′.
- [The conjugate universe](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe) follows the postulates because it is their reading of the founding picture of a second universe: in the current program it is a second description of one state, synchronized with ours rather than causally linked.
- [Founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions) is a record: the eleven suppositions P.R. brought to the program, each with the fate #11 Appendix A assigns it, from "the hard core" to "dead twice".
- [The 'framework does no work' critique](/wiki/program/framework-does-no-work) is the referees' main objection, stated most fully in review round 3. It set the toy models as the program's next task, and they answer it only in part.
- [Assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment) closes the section with #11 §15's Lakatosian self-assessment: what is intact, what counts as progress, what risks degeneration, and which tests come first.
