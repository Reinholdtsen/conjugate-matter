*This glossary is an alphabetical list of the terms used in the Broken-Born program's documents, from the program's own vocabulary (the velocity gate, the wave island, the momentum graviton) to the established physics it draws on (Lagrangian polarizations, the Fierz–Pauli mass term, the Kibble mechanism). Each entry says in plain words what the term means and links the wiki page that defines it. Several words mean different things in different documents; those entries are marked as collisions and gathered in a list near the end. Mathematical symbols are on the Notation page.*

**Status:** Record. A register compiled from the documents; it makes no claims of its own and carries no provenance tags. Each term's status and tags are on the page it links, and where an entry and that page differ, the page and its sources govern.

## Key facts

- **Entries.** Each entry gives the meaning in plain words and then links the defining page. Program terms and established physics sit side by side in one alphabet; the linked page says which a term is.
- **Status in titles.** Linked page titles show status: "Obituary: …" marks a dead claim, "Open: …" an open problem, and "(superseded)", "(chat-only)" and "(popsci-only)" the other cases. Established physics and standing program claims carry no marker. The [Status](/wiki/status) section holds the registers.
- **Collisions.** The words most likely to mislead are the gate, soliton, condensate, island and verdict C. Each is marked *Collision* in its entry, and the full list is in the Collisions section below.
- **Banned metaphors.** Where older documents say "two ledgers" or "the conjugate ledger" (#11 §10 and Appendix A), the wiki says "two descriptions". `HANDOFF.md` §7 and the project instructions rule out ledger and bookkeeping metaphors; the wiki quotes such phrases only as history.
- **Documents.** "#1" to "#15" are the artifact numbers of `HANDOFF.md` §3: #1 the lab notebook, #2–#4 the Debt-3 calc notes, #5–#8 the Debt-1 calc notes, #9 the first consolidation, #10 and #12 the popular articles, #11 the checkpoint, #13 conjugate special relativity, #14 the withdrawn soliton manuscript and #15 the skeptic review console. The toy-model note, `papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`, came later. [Documents](/wiki/history/documents) maps each to its file.
- Mathematical symbols are on [Notation](/wiki/notation), and works cited on [References](/wiki/references).

## A

- **Acceptance criterion 1.** The demand, adopted after the first blind review, for an explicit action in which ρ is a genuine Noether symmetry, spontaneously broken, with η as the order parameter. The coset toy model meets it in the referees' wording. A Monte Carlo with H dynamical, a derivation of the potential and the strong form of matter locality remain open, and the handoff's clause that η also select the hidden sector's electric description is unaddressed. See [Acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion).
- **Aligned point; flat aligned vacuum.** The configuration of the bimetric sector in which the two frames coincide (𝕏 = 1). A flat aligned vacuum requires V(1) = T; with the identity V(1) = 2T this forces T = 0 and V(1) = 0. See [The vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity).
- **Anchor.** A value from outside the paper, such as a textbook identity or a published number, that a verification script checks its formulas against before trusting them. A check that reproduces the paper's own number is circular; round 3 caught a wrong Kibble normalization that a circular anchor had passed. See [Independent anchors](/wiki/method/verification/independent-anchors).
- **Anti-de Sitter transition.** In quintessence fits with a negative-minimum potential, a future in which the effective vacuum energy turns negative, expansion halts and slow contraction begins. In the program the same event, read through a·ã = 1, is the conjugate bounce. See [Row 5: the fate of the expansion](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion).
- **Appendix A.** *Collision.* In `HANDOFF.md`, the code of the seven-test verification battery; in checkpoint #11, the audit of the eleven founding suppositions. See [The verification battery T1–T7](/wiki/method/verification/battery) and [Founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions).
- **Arithmetic, not the model.** The rule that a verification script confirms the arithmetic of the model it encodes, not whether the model's physics is right. The soliton candidate passed such a script with a wrong physics input. See [Verification checks arithmetic, not the model](/wiki/method/verification/arithmetic-not-model).

## B

- **Baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO).** The imprint of early-universe sound waves on the distribution of galaxies, a standard ruler from which the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) infers the expansion history. See [DESI baryon acoustic oscillations](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi).
- **Benchmark.** *Collision.* The scattering benchmark fixes conjugate matter's cross-section per mass from dwarf-galaxy cores and gives m_χ ≈ 70 MeV·α̃^{2/3}(σ₀/m ÷ 30 cm²g⁻¹)^{−1/3} (#11 §8). The soliton benchmark was #14 v3's monopole mass of 3×10¹³ GeV, which round 3 found overproduced. See [The scattering benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark), [Overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction) and [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).
- **Benchmark-conditional arithmetic.** Numbers from the withdrawn soliton manuscript that remain correct as arithmetic at its benchmark: the flux, the densities, σ_T/M and the W′ overclosure. See [Benchmark-conditional arithmetic](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/benchmark-arithmetic).
- **Bigravity dark matter.** The published proposal (Babichev et al.) that a massive spin-2 field of bimetric gravity is the dark matter; the lineage of the X-condensate candidate. See [Bigravity dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/bigravity-dark-matter).
- **Bimetric gravity.** Gravity with two dynamical metrics coupled by the ghost-free Hassan–Rosen potential. The program's geometric sector is this theory on its interchange-symmetric branch, with the second frame confined to Λ(4). See [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric).
- **Binder cumulant.** A ratio of moments of an order parameter's distribution, used in simulations to tell ordered from disordered phases; the toy note's ordered run gives 0.667. See [Monte Carlo: an ordered ℤ₂ phase](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/monte-carlo).
- **Blind review.** Review of a manuscript by a fresh AI referee that sees only the manuscript, is told it may contain confabulations, and returns a verdict on an A/B/C scale. See [The blind-review protocol and verdict scale](/wiki/method/review/review-protocol).
- **Born connection.** The unique connection compatible with all three structures ω, η and H, a theorem of Freidel, Rudolph and Svoboda. Whether it stays unique when only ω and H are fundamental is open. See [The Born connection](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection).
- **Born duality.** Freidel, Leigh and Minic's name for Born reciprocity; "broken Born duality" is their phrase for a preferred polarization. See [Broken Born duality](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality).
- **Born geometry.** Phase space carrying a compatible symplectic form ω, neutral metric η and generalized metric H, whose three products form a split-quaternion algebra; the term is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's. See [The structures ω, η, H and the split-quaternion algebra](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures).
- **Born length.** The length λ of #13's conjugate special relativity, whose momentum counterpart is ε = ℏ/λ; #13 sets it to the Planck length. See [The maximum force c̃ = c⁴/G](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force).
- **Born map (3+1 and four-dimensional).** The exchange of position and momentum in #13. The 3+1 version exchanges space and momentum and leaves time shared; the four-dimensional version also exchanges time and energy, and is not yet built. See [Open: the four-dimensional Born map and dual dynamics](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/four-dimensional-born-map).
- **Born reciprocity; Born rotation.** The quarter-turn ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x) of phase space, an exact symmetry of quantum kinematics that the Fourier transform implements. The program's hard core holds that it is an exact symmetry of the laws, broken by the vacuum. See [Born reciprocity ρ](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity).
- **Born sigma model.** A sigma model of Born geometry in which the polarization is chosen by gauging (Marotta and Szabo); a neighbour of the program's coset toy model in the literature. See [Success criteria S0–S5](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/success-criteria).
- **Born's answer.** The program's answer to Born's question: momentum-space geometry is a reciprocity-odd massive spin-2 field, X. See [Born's answer: momentum space gravitates](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer).
- **Born's debt.** The lab notebook's name for the missing field equation for H ("Born's 88-year debt"), later Debt 1. See [The three debts](/wiki/history/debts).
- **Born's question.** Why physics uses only half of quantum kinematics' position–momentum symmetry: interactions are local in position, and general relativity curves space while leaving momentum flat. See [Born's question](/wiki/program/borns-question).
- **Bose star.** A self-gravitating condensate of wave dark matter, formed by kinetic condensation in a halo; the Debt-3 notes call it a soliton. See [Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics).
- **Bosenova.** The collapse of an attractive condensate that has grown past its critical mass; it ejects part of the condensate as fast waves, and the remnant regrows. See [Attractive condensates](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates).
- **Boulware–Deser ghost.** The ghost mode that a generic nonlinear mass term for a spin-2 field brings back; the tuning test's metric-tier potentials have it. See [The Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Boulware–Deser ghost](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli).
- **Branch.** *Collision.* Branch R is the theory under the original P2: general relativity plus one self-interacting dark-matter model (#5 §B). Branch (i) and Branch (ii) are the two mass ranges of the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint in #4. The palindromic, or interchange-symmetric, branch is the family of bimetric potentials with βₙ = β₄₋ₙ (#8). See [Original P2 (superseded)](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2), [Obituary: Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) and [The palindrome](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome).
- **Bullet Cluster.** A pair of colliding galaxy clusters whose separated dark and visible matter limit dark matter's self-interaction. #11 §8 calls conjugate matter "Bullet-Cluster-safe": it propagates on the metric like ordinary matter, and its self-interaction is off at cluster velocities. See [Self-interacting dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter).

## C

- **Canonical commutator.** [x, p] = iℏ, which rules out states sharp in both position and momentum; #11 reads it as forcing any world with local physics to select a polarization. See [The canonical commutator and the uncertainty principle](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/canonical-commutator).
- **Charge lattice; duality frame; Lagrangian sublattice.** The lattice of allowed electric and magnetic charges. The Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger (DSZ) pairing makes it a symplectic space; mutually local charges form a Lagrangian sublattice; and choosing which charges count as electric, a duality frame, is choosing a polarization of charge space. See [The symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice).
- **Chat-only.** Results that exist only in the old claude.ai conversation (`HANDOFF.md` §4) and in no document. They are conjectures, and their page titles end "(chat-only)". See [Conjectures: chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures).
- **Checkpoint.** Document #11, `papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`, now at v2.2: the current reference for the whole program. See [The checkpoint: #9 to #11 v2.2](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint).
- **Claim tiers.** The checkpoint's trust strata: Tier 1, explicit algebra checked by machine; Tier 2, established literature; Tier 3, new structural physics, given an honest prior of about 50–80% per claim. See [Claim tiers and selection bias](/wiki/method/claim-tiers).
- **Classicality theorem.** Meeting the criterion for collective momentum-space behavior forces the dark-matter mass so low that each mode holds an enormous number of quanta, so collective velocity dynamics and classical-particle dark matter exclude each other. See [The classicality theorem and the particle island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem).
- **Cliff.** The lab notebook's exponential cutoff of the self-interaction cross-section at v\*. It was superseded by the knee once the gate's tail was found to depend on the vertex. See [The gated scattering law σ_T(v)](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law).
- **Cold stellar streams.** Thin streams of stars stripped from globular clusters or dwarf galaxies, whose perturbations probe dark-matter substructure; the lab notebook's withdrawn row 3 predicted correlated perturbations of them. See [Halo-substructure probes](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/halo-substructure) and [Obituary: velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity).
- **Compatibility theorem.** With ω and η both rigid, the only positive generalized metrics are H = diag(g, g⁻¹). It is a Born-geometry result of Freidel, Leigh and Minic, tagged [T][V] in #11; the block proof and the machine check are this program's. See [The compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem).
- **Condensate.** *Collision.* It means four things: the χ condensate that gated attraction drives on the wave island (#3); the X-condensate, a coherent oscillation of the massive momentum graviton proposed as the dark matter (#11 §12); the monopole condensate of the dual-superconductor picture of confinement (#11 §11.2); and the condensed oscillator levels of the complex Grosse–Wulkenhaar (GW) model (toy note §3–§4). See [The wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island), [The X-condensate candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate), [The dual superconductor](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/dual-superconductor) and [Quantum restoration in the complex model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/quantum-restoration).
- **Conditional pass.** The tuning test's verdict: the theory fails in second-order (metric) variables and passes in first-order (frame) variables, the formulation the geometry prefers. See [The verdict: a conditional pass](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/conditional-pass).
- **Conjugate bounce.** The turnaround of our expansion, read through a·ã = 1: the same event is the conjugate universe's bounce. See [The turnaround as the conjugate bounce](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/conjugate-bounce).
- **Conjugate matter (χ).** Matter in the p-polarized representation. It gravitates like ordinary matter, but its self-interaction acts only through the velocity gate; it is one of the two dark-matter candidates. See [Conjugate matter and the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter) and [The χ-particle candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles).
- **Conjugate monopoles.** Magnetic monopoles of the conjugate sector. As the explanation of why our sector shows none, the idea is dead; as objects that would gravitate if they exist, it stands. See [Conjugate monopoles as ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles) and [Obituary: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation).
- **Conjugate special relativity.** #13's special relativity of the momentum polarization: a second light cone whose invariant speed is a maximum force, with force boosts, force dilation and an invariant length. See [Conjugate special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity).
- **Conjugate universe; conjugate frame; dual frame.** The momentum-space description of our universe: one state with two descriptions, related as a waveform is to its spectrum. See [The conjugate universe: one state, two descriptions](/wiki/program/conjugate-universe).
- **Consistency islands.** The unique consistent structures that each structural test forced the theory onto (#7 §5; #11 §15); not the particle and wave islands. See [Assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment).
- **Constrained bimetric gravity; predictive truncation.** Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity with the second frame confined to the ten compact moduli of Λ(d). See [Constrained bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric).
- **Cosmological constraint.** a·ã = 1: rigidity of ω fixes the comoving phase-space cell, so our scale factor and the conjugate one are reciprocals. It is the |Z| = 1 locus of the modular field. See [The cosmological constraint a·ã = 1](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/cosmological-constraint).
- **Cosmological-constant problem.** Matter loops predict a vacuum energy far above the observed one. The program's vacuum-energy identity does not address it, and bimetric theory is no better placed on it than general relativity. See [The cosmological-constant problem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/cosmological-constant-problem).
- **Counterfactual flux.** The monopole flux the soliton candidate would have had if its monopoles carried charge under our photon, set against search limits to show why sector separation was needed. See [Benchmark-conditional arithmetic](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/benchmark-arithmetic).
- **Curved momentum space.** Literature in which momentum space has geometry: Snyder's quantized space-time, momenta on a curved group manifold in 2+1-dimensional gravity, and relative locality. See [Curved momentum space](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/curved-momentum-space).
- **Cusp–core problem.** Simulations of cold dark matter predict dense central cusps where many dwarf galaxies show flat cores; the program's gated self-interaction was fitted to make such cores. See [Dwarf galaxies: cores and ultra-faint dwarfs](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies).

## D

- **Dark energy.** The component driving the accelerating expansion; in the program, the field τ, the angle of the vacuum's polarization on the Maslov circle. See [Dark energy](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy).
- **Dark radiation; ΔN_eff.** Extra relativistic species beyond the Standard Model's neutrinos, counted as ΔN_eff. Closed dual curvature appears as negative dark radiation; the retired soliton candidate's hidden photon added positive dark radiation. See [The radiation era: thermal history and N_eff](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/radiation-era).
- **Dead stays dead.** The rule that a claim killed by calculation, data or review is reported prominently as dead and is not revived. See [Negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results) and [The graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).
- **Debt 1, Debt 2, Debt 3.** The lab notebook's three early obligations: the field equation for momentum-space geometry, the origin and shape of the gate, and the stream instability. Debt 1 was paid structurally; Debt 2's mechanism was found and its shape left open; Debt 3 closed negative. See [The three debts](/wiki/history/debts).
- **Definition of done.** `scripts/check.sh`, which runs the verification scripts and the wiki check; work is not finished until it passes. See [The gate: scripts/check.sh](/wiki/method/verification/the-gate).
- **Degeneration flag.** #3's label for an option the framework allows but does not require, such as streams tunable across ten orders of magnitude. See [Scenario B: tunable streams (quarantined)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b).
- **Dimension-five operator.** The operator Φ^a F^a B/M_\*, which mixes the hidden photon with our hypercharge field once the hidden scalar Φ condenses, giving a mixing ε ~ v/M_\*. It exists in the literature, but #14's order-one coefficient at the Planck scale had no source. See [Obituary: Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator).
- **Dirac quantization.** The condition that the product of electric and magnetic charge is quantized (e·g ~ 2π in #11's normalization). It makes the monopoles of a weakly coupled sector strongly coupled. See [Dirac quantization and the DSZ pairing](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization).
- **Double field theory (DFT).** String theory's doubled geometry: a generalized metric with an O(d, d) structure, an antisymmetric Kalb–Ramond B-field and a section condition. It is the program's contrast case. See [Double field theory and the generalized metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory).
- **Dual cone; two causal cones.** In #13, the light cone of momentum space, which bounds |dp/dt| by c̃, alongside the ordinary light cone. See [Two causal cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-causal-cones).
- **Dual diffeomorphisms.** Local shifts of the momentum graviton, δX_μν = ∂₍μλ_ν₎, which form a second copy of linearized diffeomorphisms. See [Dual diffeomorphisms](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/dual-diffeomorphisms).
- **Dual equivalence principle.** A chat-only open question: whether the invariant length λ and the dual gravitational charge are locked together. See [Two gravitational charges and the conjugate field equations (chat-only)](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/chat-only-results).
- **Dual inertia; dual-inertial frame; uniform dual motion.** In #13, frames of constant dp/dt: rest is force-free motion, which is our free fall, and uniform dual motion is constant force. See [Dual inertia: free fall as rest](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/dual-inertia).
- **Dual interval; dual Lorentz group; force boost.** #13's interval ds̃² = c̃²dt² − dp² and the transformations that preserve it, which carry a force-free frame to a uniformly forced one. See [Force boosts and force composition](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts).
- **Dual proper time; force dilation; momentum-spread contraction.** In #13, sustained force slows the internal evolution of conjugate-polarized systems and contracts a forced system's spread in momentum. See [Force dilation](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-dilation).
- **Dual stress.** The momentum-side counterpart of stress-energy, built from momentum derivatives of the χ bilinear; the momentum graviton couples to it. See [Dual stress T̃](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-stress).
- **Dual superconductor.** The picture of quark confinement in which condensed chromomagnetic monopoles squeeze color-electric flux into strings ('t Hooft and Mandelstam). See [The dual superconductor](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/dual-superconductor).
- **Duality.** *Collision.* Born duality is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's word for reciprocity. Electric–magnetic duality, in its exact form S-duality, exchanges electric and magnetic descriptions; string theory's T- and U-duality are the other dualities #11 §11.3 compares with. The Langmann–Szabo duality exchanges positions and momenta in noncommutative field theory. Scale-factor duality relates our scale factor to the conjugate one, and the duality dictionary translates fluids between the two frames. See [Broken Born duality](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality), [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality), [Noncommutative field theory and the Langmann–Szabo duality](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/noncommutative-field-theory) and [Scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality).
- **Duality dictionary.** How a fluid in the dual frame appears in ours: w_eff = −w̃, so dual stiff matter looks like a cosmological constant and dual dust like dust. See [The duality dictionary w_eff = −w̃](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary).
- **Dyon.** A particle carrying both electric and magnetic charge. See [Dirac quantization and the DSZ pairing](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization).

## E

- **Electric sublattice (clause).** The part of acceptance criterion 1, in `HANDOFF.md` §2 and #14's wording, that asks η also to select the hidden sector's electric description. The toy models do not address it. See [Acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion).
- **Elementary symmetric polynomials.** The symmetric functions eₙ of the eigenvalues of √(g⁻¹f) from which the ghost-free bimetric potential is built. See [Elementary symmetric polynomials](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/elementary-symmetric-polynomials).
- **Entropy dilution; early matter domination.** Late entropy injection, or an era dominated by heavy matter, that dilutes relics produced earlier. The superheavy soliton candidate survived only with a free dilution factor of this kind. See [Inflation, reheating and entropy dilution](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/inflation-and-dilution).
- **Equation of state.** The ratio w of pressure to energy density of a cosmic fluid: w = −1 for a cosmological constant, w = 0 for dust, w = +1 for a stiff fluid. See [Quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence).
- **Equivalence principle as dual relativity.** #13's observation that the equivalence of all force-free frames, the dual principle of relativity, is the equivalence principle reached from kinematics. See [The equivalence principle as dual relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/equivalence-principle).
- **Euclidean(-rigorous) skeleton; Lorentzian dressing.** The compatibility theorem and the chain that makes X massive are proven for positive-definite signature (the skeleton); extending them to spacetime signature (the dressing) is open. See [Open: Lorentzian dressing of the Euclidean skeleton](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing).
- **Exclusion conditions; requirements (i)–(iii).** #14's list of observations that would kill the soliton proposal, and its list of additions that consistency forced on it: a light hidden doublet fermion, a colder hidden sector and an unbroken hidden U(1). See [The soliton candidate (retired)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) and [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).

## F

- **Falsification exposure.** #11 §15's term for how directly and how soon data can kill the program; it names rows 1, 4 and 7 of the predictions table as near-term. See [Assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment).
- **Fierz–Pauli mass term.** The unique healthy linear mass term for a spin-2 field; the metric-tier potentials lack its −(Tr h)² term. See [The Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Boulware–Deser ghost](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli).
- **Fifth-force safety.** The argument that a new field produces no detectable force on ordinary matter: X because its range is short, τ because it couples only through derivatives. See [Safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety) and [Fifth-force safety of τ](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/tau-fifth-force).
- **Force composition.** #13's law F₁ ⊕ F₂ = (F₁ + F₂)/(1 + F₁F₂/c̃²), under which forces saturate at c̃ instead of adding without bound. See [Force boosts and force composition](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts).

## S

- **S0–S5.** The six success criteria fixed before the toy models were built: S0–S3 the referees' letter, S4–S5 the spirit. See [Success criteria S0–S5 and the pre-registered predictions](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/success-criteria).
- **S-duality.** Electric–magnetic duality acting on the complexified coupling as τ_c → −1/τ_c, generated by the same matrix [[0, 1], [−1, 0]] as ρ. See [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality).
- **Scale-factor duality.** The map g → g⁻¹ on the vacuum slice, which in cosmology relates our scale factor to the conjugate one through a·ã = 1. See [Scale-factor duality](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality).
- **Scenario A; Scenario B.** #3's two wave-island scenarios. In A the gated channel makes dwarf-galaxy cores, which forces condensation in ultra-faint dwarfs; it is falsified. In B the cores come from elsewhere, and streams are tunable but never required. See [Obituary: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) and [Scenario B: tunable streams (quarantined)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-b).
- **Section condition; strong constraint.** A restriction confining fields to a maximally isotropic subspace, one Lagrangian half, of the doubled space. Double field theory imposes it for closure of the gauge algebra or derives it from string consistency; built from η, it confines matter to one of η's null lines without saying which. See [The section condition: what η fixes](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/section-condition) and [Double field theory and the generalized metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/double-field-theory).
- **Sector separation (strict P4).** No gauge force crosses the polarizations, taken at face value since #11 v2.1: no kinetic mixing beyond what gravity induces. See [P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter).
- **Segue 1.** An ultra-faint dwarf galaxy, among the densest and most dark-matter-dominated systems known; its intact halo falsified Scenario A. See [Dwarf galaxies: cores and ultra-faint dwarfs](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies).
- **Self-dual point; self-dual circle.** Z = i, the fixed point of ρ on the modular field, and the circle |Z| = 1. See [Reciprocity as modular inversion Z → −Z⁻¹](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion).
- **Self-interacting dark matter (SIDM).** Dark matter with non-gravitational self-scattering, characterized by its transfer cross-section per mass. See [Self-interacting dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter).
- **Shift symmetry.** Constant shifts of X are exact symmetries, so the geometry gives X no potential. See [Shift symmetry: X is never auxiliary](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/shift-symmetry).
- **Shilov boundary.** The part of the Siegel domain's boundary that is the Lagrangian Grassmannian Λ(d), where the dark-energy field lives. See [The Lagrangian Grassmannian Λ(d)](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/lagrangian-grassmannian).
- **Siegel domain; Siegel half-space; Siegel disk.** The space Sp(2d, ℝ)/U(d) of complex symmetric matrices Z with positive imaginary part; `HANDOFF.md` §8 refers to its disk model. See [The Siegel domain Sp(2d, ℝ)/U(d)](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/siegel-domain).
- **Sigma model.** A field theory whose field takes values in a curved target space, such as a coset. Non-compact hyperbolic models, unlike compact ones, can break symmetry in two dimensions. See [Coset and hyperbolic sigma models](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/sigma-models).
- **Signature obstruction.** Without a gauge invariance, no Lorentz-covariant symmetric tensor has a ghost-free two-derivative kinetic term, which excludes an ungauged X. See [The Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Boulware–Deser ghost](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli) and [Why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain).
- **Slaved geometry.** The momentum metric fixed as g̃ = g⁻¹ by the compatibility theorem; #13's dual cone belongs to it. See [Corollaries: unimodularity, no B-field, slaved momentum metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries).
- **Soliton.** *Collision.* In the Debt-3 notes (#3, #4), the Bose-star core of a wave-dark-matter halo. In #14 and #11 §12, a topological defect: a 't Hooft–Polyakov monopole of the hidden sector. #11 §11.3 also calls magnetic states "solitonic". See [Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics), [The soliton candidate (retired)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) and [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).
- **Sommerfeld enhancement.** The growth of an annihilation rate at low velocity caused by a long-range attraction. #14 left open whether it, with rapid bound-state formation, would change the monopoles' abundance. See [Obituary: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor).
- **"Spent" coupling.** #2's word for a coupling already fixed by one fit, the dwarf-core cross-section, which leaves no freedom for another. See [The mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion).
- **Split-quaternion algebra.** The algebra of the three products of ω, η and H: one squares to −1, the other two square to +1, and the product of the first two is the third. See [The structures ω, η, H and the split-quaternion algebra](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures).
- **Spontaneous symmetry breaking (SSB).** A symmetric law with an asymmetric ground state. See [Spontaneous symmetry breaking](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/spontaneous-symmetry-breaking).
- **Status words.** Each wiki page has one status: established (literature), alive (a program claim that stands), conjecture, open, dead, superseded, method, record or hub. See [Status of the program's claims](/wiki/status).
- **Strong form; weak form (of S5).** Criterion S5 asks the vacuum's polarization to make matter's interactions local in one variable. The weak form, with matter's coupling to K given, passes; the strong form, with that coupling forced, is open. See [Locality from an (H, K)-built coupling](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/matter-coupling) and [Open: forced locality and the P4 lock](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/strong-locality).
- **Stückelberg fields.** Fields that keep a massive gauge theory gauge-invariant: the modes X eats. τ's residual mixing with them is open. See [Open: τ–Stückelberg mixing](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/tau-stuckelberg-mixing).
- **Symplectic form.** The antisymmetric pairing ω of position and momentum; its rigidity is read as the constancy of ℏ. See [The symplectic form and Sp(2d, ℝ)](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form).
- **Symplectic partner of the graviton.** #6's result: doubling geometry with ω pairs the metric with a second graviton, whereas doubling with η pairs it with a harmless antisymmetric field. See [The symplectic partner of the graviton is another graviton](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/symplectic-partner).
- **Symplectic vielbein.** E, with H = EᵀE. Wedge polynomials of E and the polarization frame E₀[J] form the ghost-free frame tier. See [The frame tier: the symplectic vielbein](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier).
