*This glossary lists, in alphabetical order, the terms a reader meets in this wiki: the Broken-Born program's own vocabulary, such as the velocity gate, the wave island, the momentum graviton and the three debts, and the established physics the program draws on, such as Lagrangian polarizations, the Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Kibble mechanism. Each entry says in plain words what the term means and links the page that defines it, where the term's status and the evidence for it are given. Some words mean different things in different documents or sections; their entries are marked as collisions and gathered in a list near the end, which is followed by the acronyms. Mathematical symbols are on the Notation page and cited works on the References page.*

**Status:** Record. A register compiled from the wiki's pages; it makes no claims of its own and carries no provenance tags of its own. Each term's status, tags and verification are on the page it links, and where an entry and that page differ, the page and its sources govern.

## Using the glossary

- **Entries.** Each entry gives the meaning first and then the page that defines it. Program terms and established physics share one alphabet, and the linked page says which a term is. Closely related terms share an entry and are named together at its head.
- **Order.** Greek letters sort by their English names (α under A, χ under C, ψ under P, ρ under R, τ under T), and 't Hooft sorts under T. Terms that begin with a number or a mark come first, under [Numbers and marks](#numbers-and-marks).
- **Status in titles.** Page titles show status. "Obituary: …" marks a dead claim, "Open: …" an open problem, with its number OPn when #11 §14 lists it, and "Row n: …" a test in the predictions table; "(superseded)", "(chat-only)" and "(popsci-only)" mark the remaining cases. Established physics and standing program claims carry no marker. A minor item of another status inside a page has a heading of its own, such as "Open: …" or "Superseded: …", which the registers under [Status](/wiki/status) link.
- **Collisions.** Words such as *gate*, *soliton*, *island*, *frame* and *dual* mean different things in different places. Their entries are marked *Collision*, and the [Collisions](#collisions) section lists them all.
- **Names kept, metaphors not.** The program's own names keep their wording: "the debts" is the lab notebook's name for its three most severe open problems. Where #11 writes "two ledgers" or "the conjugate ledger" (§10 and Appendix A), the wiki writes "two descriptions". `HANDOFF.md` §7 and the root `CLAUDE.md` record that ledger and bookkeeping metaphors do not work for Paul, and the wiki quotes such phrases only as history.
- **Documents.** "#1" to "#15" are the artifact numbers of `HANDOFF.md` §3: #1 the lab notebook; #2–#4 the Debt-3 calc notes; #5–#8 the Debt-1 calc notes; #9 the first consolidation, v1.0; #10 and #12 the popular articles; #11 the checkpoint; #13 conjugate special relativity; #14 the withdrawn soliton manuscript; #15 the skeptic review console. The toy-model note, `papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`, came after the handoff. [Documents](/wiki/history/documents) maps each to its file.
- Mathematical symbols are on [Notation](/wiki/notation), cited works on [References](/wiki/references), and the status registers under [Status](/wiki/status).

## Numbers and marks

- **#1–#15.** The artifact numbers of the documents carried over from claude.ai, listed under [Using the glossary](#using-the-glossary). See [Documents](/wiki/history/documents).
- **0+1, 1+1, 2+1, 3+1 dimensions.** A count of space dimensions plus one time dimension. The quantum mechanics of a few degrees of freedom is 0+1; the toy models are built in 1+1, where a compact continuous symmetry cannot break spontaneously; in 2+1-dimensional quantum gravity the momenta of a particle live on a curved space; the program's world is 3+1. See [The Mermin–Wagner–Coleman theorem](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/mermin-wagner-coleman) and [Curved momentum space](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/curved-momentum-space).
- **3+1 presentation.** #13's construction in which the position and momentum polarizations share time, so that the dual light cone lies in time and momentum, with the maximum force c̃ as its slope. The fully covariant alternative, the four-dimensional Born map, has not been built. See [Open: the two dual cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-cones-inconsistency).
- **[D], [T], [P], [O], [V]; †, [S].** The provenance tags: derived in the program, taken from the established literature, postulated, open, and checked by machine. The citation marks: † for a reference recalled from model memory and not yet checked against its source, [S] for one retrieved by a live search. Forms such as [D + T], [D][V] or [D — structural] combine or qualify tags. See [Provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags) and its section on [combined and qualified tags](/wiki/method/provenance-tags#reading-combined-and-qualified-tags).
- **✓.** The handoff's mark for a machine-checked result, where later documents write [V]. Flag 16 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` notes one ✓, on the Debt-3 shortfall, with no script behind it. See [The handoff document](/wiki/history/documents/handoff).
- **√N topological floor; Kibble ceiling.** A chat-only argument that the net topological charge left by the soliton candidate's phase transition sets a floor under its relic abundance, below a Kibble "ceiling". It is dead: round 3 showed that Kibble's count is a minimum, not a ceiling, and with the candidate retired the planned calculation was dropped (round-3 Decision 3). See [Obituary: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor).

## A

- **Acceptance criterion 1.** The referees' standing demand for one explicit action, a toy model being enough, in which Born reciprocity is a genuine symmetry that the vacuum breaks, with the neutral metric η as the order parameter. Checkpoint #11 records it as met "in the referees' wording" by model I with a bounded potential. A simulation with all fields dynamical, a derivation of the potential and the strong form of matter locality remain open, and an older clause, that η also select the hidden sector's electric description, is not addressed. See [Acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion).
- **Adjudication.** The author's written ruling on each finding of a blind review, made after the referee reports: disputed numbers are re-run in separate scripts, each finding is upheld, accepted or overturned, and the forecast committed before the round is scored. See [The blind-review protocol](/wiki/method/review/review-protocol#after-the-referee-adjudication).
- **Aligned point; flat aligned vacuum.** The point at which the two frames of the bimetric potential coincide (𝕏 = 1). A flat vacuum there requires V(1) = T, and with the palindrome's identity V(1) = 2T this forces T = 0 and V(1) = 0. See [The vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity).
- **α_d ≈ 34 (alpha-d).** The coupling the soliton candidate gave its hidden monopoles, 1/(4α), with α ≈ 1/137 the fine-structure constant. All three ways of fixing it are dead: Dirac quantization against our photon, killed in round 1; reciprocity acting as the modular S transformation, set aside as numerology; and hypothesis H, withdrawn with #14. It is distinct from the magnetic coupling of our own electromagnetism, which #11 also puts near 34 (flag 28 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md` queries #11's formula for it). See [Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling).
- **Anchor; circular anchor.** A value known independently of the paper being checked, such as a textbook identity or a published number, that a verification script must reproduce before its own numbers are trusted. A check that reproduces the paper's own figure is circular: the soliton manuscript's script anchored its monopole count to the previous draft's figure and passed a wrong normalization. See [Independent anchors](/wiki/method/verification/independent-anchors).
- **Anti-de Sitter transition.** The future event, favoured in published fits of quintessence with a negative-minimum potential, in which the effective vacuum energy turns negative, the expansion halts and contraction begins. #11 §10 cites these fits for the turnaround, which the program reads as the conjugate bounce. See [Row 5: the fate of the expansion](/wiki/predictions/fate-of-expansion) and [anti-de Sitter space](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/de-sitter-space#anti-de-sitter-space-t).
- **Appendix A.** *Collision.* In `HANDOFF.md`, the code of the seven-test verification battery, now `verify/battery.py`; in checkpoint #11, the audit of the eleven founding suppositions. See [The verification battery T1–T7](/wiki/method/verification/battery) and [Founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions).
- **Arbitration (round 3).** The adjudicator's own recomputation of the numbers the round-3 referee disputed, run before the rulings: the Kibble normalization, the mass at which the monopoles would make up the dark matter, and the relic density of the light hidden fermion ψ. Its numbers were carried into the gate. See [Round-3 arbitration and the referee's constants](/wiki/method/review/round-3/arbitration).
- **Arithmetic, not the model.** The rule that a verification script confirms the arithmetic of the model it encodes, not whether that model is the right physics. The soliton manuscript passed such a script with a wrong normalization, which only an independent review caught. See [Verification checks arithmetic, not the model](/wiki/method/verification/arithmetic-not-model).
- **Assessment.** Checkpoint #11's closing judgement of the program in Lakatos's terms (§15): the hard core intact after two forced corrections, the progressive signs, the degeneration risks and the near-term falsification exposure. Its verdict is that the program is a live research program, with the word "theory" held back. See [Assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment).
- **Asymmetric dark matter; Sakharov conditions; baryogenesis.** Dark matter whose density is set by an excess of particles over antiparticles, as the density of ordinary baryons is. Making such an excess from a symmetric start needs the three Sakharov conditions: a process that violates the conserved number, violation of charge conjugation (C) and of charge conjugation and parity combined (CP), and a departure from thermal equilibrium. Baryogenesis, the making of the baryon excess, is the template for the program's missing polarization-genesis. See [Relic abundance mechanisms](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/relic-abundance#asymmetric-dark-matter-and-the-sakharov-conditions-t).
- **Attribution line.** The line the program's papers carry: "conjecture and direction, P.R.; derivations, counterexamples, verification, drafting, Claude (Anthropic)." See [The collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration#the-attribution-line).
- **Audit trail.** Documents kept as the record of how the program reached its present state but superseded as references, such as the lab notebook, the first consolidation #9 and the withdrawn manuscript #14; the wiki cites them for history, not for current status. See [Documents](/wiki/history/documents).
- **Auxiliary field.** A field with no motion of its own. The shift symmetry rules this out for the momentum graviton X, which either propagates or does not exist. See [Shift symmetry: X is never auxiliary](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/shift-symmetry#what-it-rules-out-an-auxiliary-x).
- **Axion-like field.** A field that is an angle: its couplings are unchanged when its value is shifted by a constant, apart from a small breaking that leaves a periodic potential, and the shift symmetry keeps it light. Axions are the standard example, and the program's dark-energy field τ is of this kind, with the potential V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)]. See [Quintessence and the equation of state](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence#axion-like-fields).

## B

- **Baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO).** The imprint of sound waves in the early universe's plasma on the distribution of galaxies: a preferred separation that serves as a ruler at every later epoch. From it the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) measures distances and, by fitting a model, infers the dark-energy equation of state. See [DESI baryon acoustic oscillations](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi).
- **Benchmark.** *Collision.* The scattering benchmark fixes conjugate matter's self-scattering from the cores of dwarf galaxies and gives m_χ ≈ 70 MeV·α̃^{2/3}(σ₀/m ÷ 30 cm²g⁻¹)^{−1/3} (#11 §8). The soliton benchmark was #14 v3's monopole mass of 3×10¹³ GeV, which round 3 found overproduced. See [The scattering benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/benchmark), [Overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction) and [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).
- **Benchmark-conditional arithmetic.** Numbers that #14 v3 computed at its withdrawn benchmark mass and that remain correct as arithmetic: the monopoles' density and spacing, their self-scattering per unit mass, the counterfactual flux and the overclosure by W′ bosons. See [Benchmark-conditional arithmetic](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/benchmark-arithmetic).
- **Big Bang nucleosynthesis (BBN).** The formation of the lightest nuclei, mostly helium-4 with traces of deuterium, helium-3 and lithium-7, in the first minutes of the hot early universe. The helium abundance measures the expansion rate at that time, and so the radiation content and the strength of gravity together. The lab notebook dates row 10's optional step in G_eff "before BBN", and at MeV masses the retired soliton candidate's light fermion ψ would still have been freezing out during it. See [Big Bang nucleosynthesis](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/big-bang-nucleosynthesis).
- **Bigravity dark matter.** The published proposal of Babichev and collaborators, cited from model memory, that the massive spin-2 field of bimetric gravity is the dark matter; the lineage of the program's X-condensate candidate. See [Bigravity dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/bigravity-dark-matter).
- **Bimetric gravity; Hassan–Rosen.** Gravity with two dynamical metrics, each with its own dynamics, coupled by the ghost-free Hassan–Rosen potential, a sum of five terms with constant coefficients β₀ to β₄. About proportional backgrounds it has one massless and one massive spin-2 field. The program's gravity is this theory with the second frame confined to a compact space. See [Hassan–Rosen bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/hassan-rosen-bimetric) and [Constrained bimetric gravity](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/constrained-bimetric).
- **Binder cumulant.** A ratio of moments of an order parameter's distribution, used in simulations to tell ordered from disordered phases; the toy-model note's ordered run gives 0.667. See [Monte Carlo: an ordered ℤ₂ phase](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/monte-carlo).
- **Blind review.** The program's test of a manuscript: a fresh AI referee receives only the manuscript, checks its citations, re-runs its numbers and grades it A, B or C, after the author has committed a forecast of the outcome. Four rounds have run, three on the soliton manuscript #14 and one on the toy-model note. See [Blind review](/wiki/method/review).
- **Born approximation; Born regime.** *Collision.* The weak-coupling approximation of scattering theory, valid when the coupling is weak compared with the ratio of the mediator's mass to the particle's. The name is Max Born's, but the approximation has nothing to do with Born reciprocity. The lab notebook derives the gated scattering law in it and calls it marginal at its benchmark coupling. See [Self-interacting dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter#the-born-regime-and-beyond).
- **Born connection.** The rule for comparing vectors at neighbouring points of phase space that respects all three structures ω, η and H of a Born geometry; a theorem of Freidel, Rudolph and Svoboda shows that, under its conditions, there is exactly one. Whether a unique compatible connection still exists when only ω and H are fundamental is open. See [The Born connection](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection).
- **Born duality; broken Born duality.** Freidel, Leigh and Minic's name for Born reciprocity. "Broken Born duality" is their phrase for a preferred polarization; they made η dynamical but wrote no potential for it, and reading the breaking as spontaneous, with η as the order parameter, is the program's addition. See [Broken Born duality](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/broken-born-duality).
- **Born geometry.** Phase space carrying three compatible structures, the symplectic form ω, the neutral metric η and the generalized metric H, whose pairwise products I, J and K multiply like split quaternions; the name is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's. See [Born geometry](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry) and [The structures ω, η, H and the split-quaternion algebra](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures).
- **Born length.** The length λ of #13's conjugate special relativity, which sets the maximum force c̃ = ℏc/λ²; #13 sets it equal to the Planck length. See [The maximum force](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force#the-born-length).
- **Born map (3+1 and four-dimensional).** The exchange of position and momentum in #13. The 3+1 version exchanges space with momentum and leaves time shared; the four-dimensional version would also exchange time with energy, and it has not been built. See [Open: the four-dimensional Born map and dual dynamics](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/four-dimensional-born-map).
- **Born reciprocity; Born rotation; ρ (rho).** The quarter-turn ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x) of phase space, which leaves the rules of quantum kinematics unchanged and which the Fourier transform carries out on wavefunctions; applied twice it is the half-turn (x, p) → (−x, −p). The program's hard core holds that it is an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum breaks. See [Born reciprocity ρ](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity).
- **Born sigma model.** A sigma model in which Born geometry is the target structure and the polarization is chosen by gauging (Marotta and Szabo); related to, but not the same as, the program's coset toy model. See [Coset and hyperbolic sigma models](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/sigma-models#related-models-in-the-literature).
- **Born's answer.** The program's answer to Born's question: momentum-space geometry is dynamical, carried by X, a massive spin-2 field that changes sign under reciprocity, with two free parameters and no vacuum energy from its interaction potential. See [Born's answer: momentum space gravitates](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/borns-answer).
- **Born's debt.** The lab notebook's name for the missing field equation of the phase-space metric H, "Born's 88-year debt"; later Debt 1. See [The three debts](/wiki/history/debts).
- **Born's question.** Why physics singles out position when quantum kinematics treats position and momentum as equals: interactions are local in position, and general relativity curves space-time while leaving the space of momenta flat. Max Born raised it in 1938. See [Born's question](/wiki/program/borns-question).
- **Bose star; kinetic condensation.** A dense, coherent ground-state core that forms in a halo of wave dark matter when the waves scatter into the lowest-energy states, a kinetic condensation that Levkov, Panin and Tkachev worked out for gravity. The Debt-3 notes call such a core a soliton. See [Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics).
- **Bosenova; attractive condensate; critical mass.** An attractive condensate is a Bose star whose particles also attract one another. Above a critical mass M_crit it collapses, throws off part of its mass as fast waves in a burst called a bosenova, and the remnant regrows. In the dead [Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) this cycle would have drained the ultra-faint dwarf galaxies of their dark matter. See [Attractive condensates: critical mass and the bosenova](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/attractive-condensates).
- **Boulware–Deser ghost.** The extra negative-energy mode that a generic nonlinear mass term for a spin-2 field brings back. The special de Rham–Gabadadze–Tolley and Hassan–Rosen potentials keep it out, and the metric-tier potentials of the tuning test have it. See [The Fierz–Pauli mass term and the Boulware–Deser ghost](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/fierz-pauli).
- **Bounded potential V_b.** The repaired ρ-preserving potential of model I: the [naive potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential) divided by a normalizing factor, so that it stays between −μ and 0. It leaves the vacuum stable with the metric H free to vary, keeps one flat direction that no symmetry protects, and is put in by hand rather than derived. See [The bounded potential V_b](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/bounded-potential).
- **Branch.** *Collision.* Branch R is the theory under the original P2: general relativity plus one model of self-interacting dark matter (#5 §B). Branch (i) and Branch (ii) are the two mass ranges of the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint of Scenario A (#4): the bulk of the wave island, where the condensate is a mass drain, and lighter masses that observations already exclude. The palindromic, or interchange-symmetric, branch is the family of bimetric potentials with βₙ = β₄₋ₙ (#8). See [Original P2](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2#the-collision-branch-r), [Obituary: Scenario A](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a#branch-i-the-condensate-as-a-mass-drain) and [The palindromic branch](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch).
- **Broken-Born program (BBT).** The research program this wiki describes, developed by Paul with Claude since September 2026: Born reciprocity is taken to be an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum breaks spontaneously, and the consequences are followed into gravity, dark matter, dark energy and electric–magnetic duality. It calls itself a Lakatosian research program, not a theory; the lab notebook's title says Broken-Born Theory. See [The Broken-Born program](/wiki) and [The research program](/wiki/program).
- **Bullet Cluster.** A pair of colliding galaxy clusters whose separated dark and visible matter limit how strongly dark matter scatters off itself. #11 §8 calls conjugate matter "Bullet-Cluster-safe": it falls like ordinary matter, and its self-interaction is off at cluster velocities. See [The Bullet Cluster](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/bullet-cluster).

## C

- **Calc notes.** The seven working notes #2–#8, written to close the lab notebook's debts: #2–#4 on Debt 3, whether dark matter forms velocity streams, and #5–#8 on Debt 1, the field equation of momentum-space geometry. They are final for their own scope and kept as the audit trail, and checkpoint #11 now states their results. See [The Debt-3 calc notes (#2–#4)](/wiki/history/documents/debt-3-notes) and [The Debt-1 calc notes (#5–#8)](/wiki/history/documents/debt-1-notes).
- **Cannibal dark matter.** A dark sector whose particles cannot annihilate into anything lighter, so that number-changing self-interactions set its abundance: for example, three particles scatter into two and the rest mass of the third becomes heat. The lab notebook names the "cannibalism problems" of such a sector as the reason particle χ cannot get its abundance from thermal freeze-out. See [Relic abundance mechanisms](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/relic-abundance#cannibal-dark-matter-t).
- **Canonical commutator.** [x, p] = iℏ, the rule that position and momentum do not commute, which rules out states sharp in both. #11 reads it as requiring any world with local physics to select a polarization. See [The canonical commutator and the uncertainty principle](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/canonical-commutator).
- **Charge lattice; duality frame; Lagrangian sublattice.** The lattice of allowed electric and magnetic charges. The Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger (DSZ) pairing makes it a symplectic space, mutually local charges form a Lagrangian sublattice, and choosing which charges count as electric, a duality frame, is choosing a polarization of charge space. See [The symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice).
- **Chat-only.** Said of a result that exists only in the original claude.ai conversation, recorded in `HANDOFF.md` §4 and derived in no document. Such results have the status conjecture, the handoff calls some of them "conjecture tier", and their page titles end "(chat-only)". See [Conjectures: chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures).
- **Checkpoint.** Document #11, `papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md`, now at version 2.2: the program's paper-length statement of everything it has derived, predicted, retracted and left open, and the current reference for the whole program. Its first version was #9 (v1.0). See [The checkpoint: #9 to #11 v2.2](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint).
- **Claim tiers.** Checkpoint #11's ranking of its own statements by how much trust each deserves: Tier 1, explicit algebra checked by machine; Tier 2, established literature; Tier 3, the program's new structural physics, to which #11 gives a prior of ~50–80% per claim pending expert review. Negative results are the most reliable Tier-3 items. See [Claim tiers and selection bias](/wiki/method/claim-tiers).
- **Classicality theorem.** The result that dark matter coupled strongly enough to clump in velocity must be so light that each quantum state holds an enormous number of quanta, so that it behaves as a classical wave: collective velocity dynamics and classical-particle dark matter exclude each other. It splits dark matter into the particle island and the wave island. See [The classicality theorem and the particle island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem).
- **Cliff.** The lab notebook's exponential cutoff of the self-interaction cross-section at the gate velocity v\*. Once the gate's mechanism was found the program kept only the knee, and the shape of its tail became open. See [The gated scattering law σ_T(v)](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/scattering-law).
- **Cold stellar streams.** Long, thin trails of stars pulled off globular clusters or small galaxies by the Milky Way's tides, which lumps of dark matter disturb as they pass. The lab notebook's withdrawn row 3 predicted correlated ripples along them. See [Halo-substructure probes](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/halo-substructure#cold-stellar-streams-t) and [Obituary: velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity).
- **Collapse-powered expansion.** The founding supposition that the gravitational collapse of the conjugate universe drives the acceleration of ours. It is dead twice: the early heuristic translation rule required dark energy with w = −2, which data exclude, and the corrected duality dictionary makes collapsing conjugate matter slow our expansion as ordinary matter does. See [Obituary: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion).
- **Compatibility corollaries.** Three consequences of the compatibility theorem's answer H = diag(g, g⁻¹): det H = 1, which the program reads as the constancy of Planck's constant; no antisymmetric B-field block; and a momentum metric fixed as the inverse of the position metric, g̃ = g⁻¹, the slaved momentum metric. The last contradicted the program as it then stood and forced the change of postulate to P2′. See [Corollaries: unimodularity, no B-field, slaved momentum metric](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries).
- **Compatibility theorem.** With the symplectic form ω and the neutral metric η both held fixed, the only positive metrics H compatible with both are H = diag(g, g⁻¹). The statement is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's; the short block proof and its machine check, battery test T1, are the program's, and round 3 re-derived it independently. ## G

- **Gate, the.** *Collision.* In the physics, the velocity gate (below). In the method, `scripts/check.sh`, the check that must pass before work is called finished. - **Gate mechanism; fiber overlap.** The program's derivation of the velocity gate: two particles of conjugate matter interact by exchanging the heavy momentum graviton X, which acts as a contact interaction, and only where their wavepackets overlap along the momentum fibers. A χ quantum's finite width μ along those fibers cuts off its interaction at large relative momentum, as a nucleus's size cuts off its scattering at large momentum transfer. The derivation is structural. See [The gate mechanism: X exchange over fiber overlap](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism).
- **Gate open; gate narrow; broad gate; narrow gate.** The two regimes of the gated interaction. With the gate open, or broad (halo dispersion σ ≲ v\*), most pairs in a halo are inside the gate, as in dwarf galaxies; with the gate narrow (σ > v\*), as in the Milky Way and clusters, only particles moving together take part, and the wave-island condensation rate falls by the power law (v\*/σ)⁴. See [The gated condensation rate Γ_self](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate#gate-open-and-gate-narrow) and [The mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion).

It depends on how X propagates along the momentum fibers and on the profile of a conjugate quantum, neither of them computed; it is open problem 4 (OP4), and it is observable in the tail of the knee. See [Open: the gate shape (OP4)](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape).
- **Gated Vlasov model.** The simplified description of dark matter used in Debt 3: a smooth gas in position and momentum with a contact attraction acting only within a Gaussian gate, whose linear stability is bracketed between a broad-gate and a narrow-gate limit. See [The gated Vlasov model](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/gated-vlasov-model).
- **General relativity; graviton.** Einstein's theory of gravity, in which the spacetime metric fixes distances and durations and its curvature is gravity. Small ripples of the metric are the graviton, a massless spin-2 field whose gauge symmetry is the linearized form of a change of coordinates. The program keeps general relativity as the long-range gravity of ordinary matter and uses the graviton as the template for X. See [General relativity and the graviton](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity).
- **Generalized metric (H).** The positive metric on phase space, a ruler that measures position and momentum directions together. With the symplectic form it is one of the two fundamental structures under P2′, and the space of its compatible values is the Siegel domain. See [The structures ω, η, H and the split-quaternion algebra](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures) and [P1: the phase-space arena](/wiki/program/postulates/p1-arena).

It is superseded by P2′ and the Debt-1 calc notes; the name S_H survives as the action from which open problem 1 asks for the ghost-free potential to be derived. See [The geometric action S_H and the v0.1 action (superseded)](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/geometric-action).

- **"go".** Paul's word for continuing with the next chunk of work. See [The collaboration](/wiki/method/collaboration).
- **Goldstone angle.** The toy models' finding that the vacuum's polarization is an angle that can turn at no cost in energy rather than a switch with two settings: where ρ sits inside a continuous U(1), the position-sharp and momentum-sharp vacua lie on one family, so a definite polarization needs an added potential. See [The polarization as a Goldstone angle](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-angle).
- **Goldstone mode; pseudo-Goldstone mode; Higgs mechanism.** A Goldstone mode is the massless excitation that appears when the ground state breaks a continuous symmetry; a small explicit term that tilts the family of ground states gives it a small mass, making it a pseudo-Goldstone mode. When the broken symmetry is a gauge symmetry, the gauge field absorbs the would-be Goldstone modes and becomes massive: the Higgs mechanism. See [Goldstone modes and the Higgs mechanism](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/goldstone-and-higgs).

See [The gravitational Higgs mechanism](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/gravitational-higgs).

Gravothermal evolution is the slow redistribution of heat in a self-gravitating halo. See [Obituary: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a) and [Self-interacting dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/self-interacting-dark-matter#gravothermal-evolution-t).

The duality is a genuine symmetry for complex fields and only a covariance for real ones. See [Model II: the self-dual Grosse–Wulkenhaar model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar).

- **Merger band.** Comparable-mass black-hole mergers, which reach forces of the order of the maximum force: the one natural arena #13 names where dual-kinematic corrections could be of order one. No signature has been derived. See [Force boosts and force composition](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts#open-merger-band-corrections).
- **Mermin–Wagner–Coleman theorem.** In two dimensions a compact continuous symmetry cannot break spontaneously; discrete and some non-compact symmetries escape it. It is why the toy models' polarization angle can order only after a potential reduces its symmetry to a discrete one. See [The Mermin–Wagner–Coleman theorem](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/mermin-wagner-coleman).
- **Millicharged particle.** A particle with a tiny electric charge, such as the hidden fermion ψ would acquire through kinetic mixing; strict P4 closes the case. See [Kinetic mixing between sectors](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing).
- **Mirror vacuum.** The vacuum polarized the other way, organized around momentum, which satisfies the same laws as ours. See [P5: the vacuum selects a polarization](/wiki/program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection).
- **Misalignment.** *Collision.* The misalignment mechanism produces a light field displaced from its potential minimum in the early universe, which later oscillates as cold dark matter; it gives wave-island χ its relic abundance. The misalignment potential is the energy cost of a mismatch between the polarization J and the modular field Z; it gives X its mass. See [The misalignment mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/misalignment-mechanism), [Relic abundance by misalignment on the wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/misalignment-relic) and [The misalignment potential](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/misalignment-potential).
- **Model I; model II.** The two toy models: the coset sigma model of a phase-space metric H and a real structure K, and the self-dual Grosse–Wulkenhaar model. Model I's action is unchanged by ρ, while each of its vacua is carried by ρ to a different one; with a bounded potential it meets acceptance criterion 1 in the referees' wording. In model II the complex version's breaking does not survive quantization. See [Model I: the coset sigma model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model), [Model I: action and symmetries](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/action-and-symmetries), [Model I vacua](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/vacua) and [Model II: the self-dual Grosse–Wulkenhaar model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar).
- **Modular field.** The single complex symmetric matrix field Z = X + iY, with values in the Siegel domain, that carries all of phase-space geometry once only ω is rigid: Im Z = g is the spacetime metric and Re Z = X the momentum graviton. See [The modular field Z = X + iY](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-field-z).
- **Modular inversion; Möbius action.** Born reciprocity acting on the modular field as the fractional-linear map Z → −Z⁻¹, whose fixed point Z = i is the self-dual vacuum; on the vacuum slice it sends the metric to its inverse. See [Reciprocity as modular inversion Z → −Z⁻¹](/wiki/phase-space/modular-field/modular-inversion).
- **Momentum clumping.** Dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, the founding image; tested in Debt 3 and dead as dynamics. See [Momentum clumping (Debt 3)](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping) and [Obituary: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image).
- **Momentum fiber.** The space of momenta attached to one point of space-time. General relativity leaves these fibers flat, which Born's question counts as an assumption, and the gate mechanism acts through the overlap of wavepackets along them. See [Born's question](/wiki/program/borns-question#flat-momentum-fibers) and [The gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism).
- **Momentum graviton (X).** X = Re Z, the symmetric tensor field that carries the geometry of momentum space and changes sign under reciprocity: its symmetries make it a second graviton, consistency makes it massive, and ordinary matter feels it at gravitational strength but only over a short range. #12 calls it "the second geometry"; #6 calls it the "symplectic partner of the graviton". See [The momentum graviton X](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton).
- **Momentum-space black holes; velocity traps.** A chat-only conjecture: a black hole's horizon read in the momentum description as the surface where hovering requires the maximum force, with mirror objects trapped in momentum rather than in space ("velocity traps") and "dual Hawking quanta" huge in position space. No document derives it. See [Momentum-space black holes (chat-only)](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/momentum-space-black-holes).
- **Monopole problem.** The hot early universe should have produced superheavy magnetic monopoles in unacceptably large numbers, and annihilation removes too few; the standard remedy is inflation. See [The monopole problem](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-problem).
- **Monte Carlo (MC).** Simulation by random sampling. The toy-model note's Monte Carlo of the frozen-H lattice model reports error bars from four independent seeds. See [Monte Carlo: an ordered ℤ₂ phase](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/monte-carlo).
- **Monte Carlo error-bar rule.** Numbers from a random simulation need error bars from independent runs: a fixed seed makes a number reproducible, not accurate. It was round 4's second lesson. See [The Monte Carlo error-bar rule](/wiki/method/verification/monte-carlo-error-bars).
- **Montonen–Olive duality.** The proposed exact electric–magnetic duality that exchanges electrically charged particles with monopoles, and strong with weak coupling; exact in maximally supersymmetric Yang–Mills theory. See [S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality).
- **Moyal plane; star product.** The noncommutative plane, [x₁, x₂] = iθ, which behaves like the phase space of a quantum particle, and the product of functions that builds that relation in; the setting of the Grosse–Wulkenhaar model. See [Noncommutative field theory and the Langmann–Szabo duality](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/noncommutative-field-theory).
- **Multi-graviton no-go theorem.** Boulanger, Damour, Gualtieri and Henneaux's theorem that several massless spin-2 fields cannot interact with one another consistently; it excludes a massless X. See [The multi-graviton no-go theorem](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/multi-graviton-no-go).
- **Mutual locality.** Two charges are mutually local when their DSZ pairing vanishes, so that they can share one Lagrangian description. See [The symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice).

## N

- **Naive ρ-preserving potential.** The first potential added to model I to pin its polarization, −μ[Tr(K K_ref)]². Once the metric H varies it is unbounded below and its intended vacuum is a saddle (round-4 findings F1 and F2); it is dead and kept as the counterexample that the bounded potential answers. See [Obituary: the naive ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential).
- **Net theorem.** Debt 3's summary result, that velocity clumping is nowhere a required consequence of the program: in #9's words, "forbidden (particles), fatal (wave, committed coupling), decorative (wave, free coupling)". Clumping is impossible for particle dark matter, ruled out by observation where a fixed coupling would make it mandatory for waves, and merely optional where the coupling is free. See [The net theorem: clumping is nowhere mandatory](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/net-theorem).
- **Neutral metric (η).** A symmetric pairing on phase space with as many positive as negative directions, whose null directions mark out which coordinates count as position and which as momentum. Under P2′ it is not fixed but chosen by the vacuum, as the order parameter of the broken symmetry, and ρ reverses its sign. See [The structures ω, η, H and the split-quaternion algebra](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures).
- **Noncommutative field theory.** Field theory on a space whose coordinates do not commute, such as the Moyal plane. See [Noncommutative field theory and the Langmann–Szabo duality](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/noncommutative-field-theory).
- **Nontriviality theorem.** The program says anything beyond general relativity plus one model of self-interacting dark matter only if the neutral metric η is a property of the vacuum rather than fixed background; it forced the change from P2 to P2′. See [The nontriviality theorem](/wiki/program/postulates/nontriviality-theorem).
- **No-phantom bound.** The requirement that the dark-energy equation of state never fall below −1, because τ is an ordinary scalar field obeying the null energy condition; a robust intrinsic w < −1 would kill the framework outright. See [The no-phantom bound w ≥ −1](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/no-phantom-bound).
- **"No timescale refuge".** Calc note #4's finding that condensation in an ultra-faint dwarf takes so small a fraction of the galaxy's lifetime that slowing it does not save Scenario A. See [Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics#no-timescale-refuge) and [Obituary: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a).
- **Null energy condition.** A standard requirement on reasonable matter, which an ordinary scalar field obeys and which keeps its equation of state at w ≥ −1; a phantom phase requires violating it. See [The phantom divide and the null energy condition](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide).

See [The gated condensation rate Γ_self](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/condensation-rate#the-race-against-orbital-stirring) and [The gated Vlasov model](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/gated-vlasov-model).

For reciprocity it is the neutral metric η, written through J or K in different documents, which do not agree on which to call "the real structure". ℤ₂ is the two-element symmetry group. Version 2 of the toy-model note uses the term in place of the earlier "Ising class", since data at one temperature establish order but not a universality class. ## P

- **P1–P5; P2; P2′.** The five postulates. P1: physics lives on phase space with a symplectic form ω and a generalized metric H. P2′: only ω, the constancy of ℏ, is fixed; it replaced the original P2, which also froze η and left the program with no content of its own. P3: the action is invariant under Born reciprocity. P4: ordinary matter is position-polarized and conjugate matter momentum-polarized, with only gravity acting between them. P5: the vacuum selects a polarization. See [Postulates P1–P5](/wiki/program/postulates), [P1](/wiki/program/postulates/p1-arena), [P2′](/wiki/program/postulates/p2-prime), [Original P2 (superseded)](/wiki/program/postulates/original-p2), [P3](/wiki/program/postulates/p3-reciprocity), [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) and [P5](/wiki/program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection).
- **P4 lock.** In the coset toy model, nothing requires matter to couple to the sign of the order parameter; postulate P4, which places ordinary matter in the position-polarized representation and its mirror in the momentum-polarized one, supplies the link. So the toy explains locality in position only as part of a package with a momentum-local mirror sector. See [Open: forced locality and the P4 lock](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/strong-locality#the-lock-is-p4).
- **Palindrome; palindromic branch; interchange-symmetric branch.** The constraint βₙ = β₄₋ₙ that reciprocity, acting as the exchange of the potential's two frames, imposes on the five coefficients of the bimetric potential, so that the list reads the same forwards and backwards; and the family of potentials that satisfy it. See [The palindrome βₙ = β₄₋ₙ](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/palindrome) and [The palindromic branch](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch).
- **Para-Hermitian geometry.** Geometry with a real structure whose two eigenspaces are null for a compatible neutral metric; calc note #5 §F names it as the literature nearest to Born geometry. See [The structures ω, η, H and the split-quaternion algebra](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures).
- **Parker bound; MACRO.** The upper limit on the flux of magnetic monopoles set by the survival of the Galaxy's magnetic field; the extended Parker bound applies the same argument to the young Galaxy's seed field. Direct searches, among them the Monopole, Astrophysics and Cosmic Ray Observatory (MACRO), have found none. See [Monopole flux bounds: Parker and MACRO](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches).
- **Partially massless (PM) gravity.** A spin-2 field on de Sitter space at the one mass where an extra gauge symmetry removes one of its polarizations; and the bimetric parameter point studied as partially massless bimetric gravity, which lies inside the palindromic branch at α = 1. Whether reciprocity is the origin of its symmetry is part of open problem 2 (OP2). See [Partially massless gravity](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/partially-massless-gravity) and [Open: the partially-massless comparison (OP2)](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison).
- **Particle island.** Classical-particle χ, with masses from MeV to GeV, on which the self-interaction knee survives but collective momentum-space behaviour is impossible. See [The classicality theorem and the particle island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/classicality-theorem#the-particle-island).
- **Patch modes; co-movers.** In calc note #2's narrow-gate limit, perturbations confined to a small ball of momentum space, and the particles inside it, which move within the gate width of one another. See [The mean-field criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion#the-narrow-gate-threshold).
- **Permanent null.** A prediction that a search will never find anything: rows 7–9, no non-gravitational dark-matter signal, no monopole of our sector and no linear photon dispersion. See [Predictions and kill conditions](/wiki/predictions).
- **Phantom crossing; phantom divide.** w = −1 divides ordinary dark energy from phantom dark energy, which becomes denser as space expands; an ordinary scalar field cannot cross it. Data Release 2 of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument favours a crossing to w < −1 above redshift z ≈ 0.4, against the program's requirement. See [The phantom divide and the null energy condition](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/phantom-divide) and [DESI DR2's phantom crossing](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing).
- **Phase.** *Collision.* Phase space is the space of positions and momenta; a phase of matter, such as the ordered ℤ₂ phase, is a thermodynamic state; the Maslov phase and the condensate phase β of model II are angles; and a phantom phase is a period with w < −1. See the entries for each.
- **Phase space.** The space of positions and momenta taken together, the program's arena (P1). See [Phase-space geometry](/wiki/phase-space).
- **Phase-space diameter relation.** A chat-only conjecture, D_x·D_p = h·N^{1/3} per dimension: the universe's diameters in position and in momentum multiply to Planck's constant times a large number, a cosmological counterpart of the time–bandwidth product. A note on it is queued. See [The phase-space diameter relation (chat-only)](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter).
- **Planck units; Planck mass; reduced Planck mass.** The length, energy and mass that can be formed from ℏ, c and G: the Planck length l_P = √(ℏG/c³), the Planck energy and the Planck mass. Their energy-to-length ratio, c⁴/G, is also called the Planck force. The Planck mass also sets how strongly a spin-2 field couples to matter, and it is written in two conventions, M_Pl = G^{−1/2} and the reduced M̄_Pl = M_Pl/√(8π), one of which a formula keeps throughout. See [Planck units and the two Planck masses](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/planck-units).
- **Polarization.** *Collision.* In the program, a choice of Lagrangian subspace, that is, of which variables count as position: ordinary matter is x-polarized, conjugate matter p-polarized, and our vacuum x-tilted. In model II the polarization Δ(β) measures how much broader a vacuum is in position than in momentum. The word keeps its ordinary meanings elsewhere, as in the five polarizations of a massive spin-2 field. See [Lagrangian subspaces and polarizations](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/lagrangian-polarizations), [P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter), [P5: the vacuum selects a polarization](/wiki/program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection) and [Complex-model vacua and the polarization Δ(β)](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/complex-vacua).
- **Polarization-genesis.** The program's name for a mechanism it does not yet have, which would set the ratio Ω_χ : Ω_b = 5.4 : 1 of dark to ordinary matter as baryogenesis sets the baryon excess; the lab notebook's sketch is "a shape, not a mechanism". It is open problem 7 (OP7). See [Open: polarization-genesis and the 5.4 : 1 ratio (OP7)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/polarization-genesis).
- **Popsci-only.** Said of a claim found only in the popular articles #10 and #12, of which #12 presents the framework as if it were confirmed; never a source for a claim's status. See [Conjectures: chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures).

## R

- **Radiation era; yield.** The early period in which radiation and fast particles dominated the universe's energy, so that the temperature alone set the expansion rate. A relic's yield, its number density divided by the entropy density, stays fixed once it is no longer made or destroyed. The soliton candidate's central error was a dropped factor of the radiation-era expansion rate. See [The radiation era: thermal history and N_eff](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/radiation-era).
- **Real structure (K).** An operator on phase space that squares to +1, leaving one line in place and reversing another; its two eigenlines are the candidate position and momentum axes. In the toy models K carries the order parameter, with η = ωK; #11 and the toy-model note disagree on which operator to call "the real structure". See [The order parameter: J, η and K](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/order-parameter) and [The structures ω, η, H](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures#which-operator-is-the-real-structure-unreconciled).
- **Reciprocity parity; even and odd combinations.** Reciprocity exchanges the program's two spin-2 frames, so the fields of definite mass are their sum, unchanged by the exchange (reciprocity-even), which is the massless graviton, and their difference, which changes sign (reciprocity-odd), which is X. Row 6 rests on it. The assignment uses reciprocity as the exchange of the frames; under #11 §6's Z → −Z⁻¹ the metric's fluctuation changes sign too (flag 39 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`). See [Spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity#even-and-odd-combinations) and [Row 6: the exactly massless graviton](/wiki/predictions/massless-graviton).
- **Redshift.** The stretching of every wavelength of light by the expansion between emission and observation, written z; the momentum of any freely moving particle falls in the same proportion. See [Friedmann cosmology and minisuperspace](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/friedmann-cosmology#redshift-and-momentum-t).
- **Reduced-model stability rule.** When numerics run on a model with some fields frozen, check the full model's boundedness and Hessian with all fields dynamical, and say in the text that it is a reduction; round 4's first lesson. See [The reduced-model stability rule](/wiki/method/verification/reduced-model-stability).
- **Relic abundance.** The density a particle species retains from the hot early universe, and the mechanisms that set it. See [Relic abundance mechanisms](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/relic-abundance).
- **Review rounds 1–4.** The four blind reviews: rounds 1 and 2 of #14 v1 and v2 (B, and "B near B/C"), round 3 of #14 v3 (C) and round 4 of the toy-model note (B). See [Blind review](/wiki/method/review), [Review rounds 1 and 2](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two), [Review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) and [Review round 4](/wiki/method/review/round-4).
- **ρ-preserving potential (rho).** A potential on the polarization angle, unchanged by ρ and by K → −K, that reduces the continuous symmetry to a discrete one and so makes the polarization definite. It brings in a fixed, unordered pair of axes, more structure than ω alone, and no derivation of it exists. ## V

- **Vacuum; vacuum manifold; stabilizer.** The vacuum is the lowest-energy state of a theory, or of the universe. - **Vacuum-energy identity.** Under the palindrome, the value of the bimetric potential at the aligned point is twice its tadpole, V(1) = 2T, so a flat aligned vacuum forces the spin-2 interaction sector's vacuum energy to zero. See [The vacuum-energy identity V(1) = 2T](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity).

The scattering amplitude depends, through a form factor F(k²/μ²), on the incoming relative momentum, a variable that, according to #11, no carrier local in position produces. In signal terms, an ordinary contact force acts where two waveforms overlap, and the gate acts only where their spectra overlap as well. See [The velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate).

Hinterbichler and Rosen wrote the ghost-free potentials for two spin-2 fields as wedge products of two frames. See [Vielbein formulation and wedge potentials](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation).

## W

- **W′ overclosure; minimal sector.** The result that the simplest version of the soliton candidate's hidden sector, with no particle lighter than its heavy W′± bosons carrying hidden charge, is excluded: the stable W′± would outweigh the observed dark matter 10¹⁶ to 10¹⁷ times. It forced the hidden fermion ψ, and it survives the candidate. See [W′ overclosure: the minimal sector is excluded](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure).
- **Wave dark matter.** Dark matter made of particles so light that many share each quantum state, so that a halo behaves as a classical wave field. See [Wave dark matter](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter).
- **Wave island.** The range of very light χ masses, roughly 3×10⁻¹⁹ eV to 10⁻⁴ eV, in which a halo of conjugate matter is a classical wave field; gated attraction can drive condensation there, so collective clumping is possible but never required. See [The wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island).
- **Wave kinetics.** The theory of how a field of many random-phase waves relaxes as the waves scatter off one another, building Bose-star cores; Levkov, Panin and Tkachev (LPT) worked it out for gravity, and Debt 3 added a gated channel. See [Wave kinetics and Bose-star formation](/wiki/dark-matter/wave-dark-matter/wave-kinetics).
- **Wedge sector; wedge polynomials.** The bimetric potential written as wedge products of the two frames, the Hinterbichler–Rosen form, ghost-free by construction. That the program's potential takes this form was selected, not derived from S_H; deriving it is open problem 1 (OP1). See [Vielbein formulation and wedge potentials](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/vielbein-formulation) and [Open: deriving the wedge sector from S_H (OP1)](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/wedge-reduction).
- **Weyl semimetal; Berry-curvature monopole.** Crystals whose electronic bands carry monopoles of Berry curvature in momentum space: the one laboratory analog of momentum-space monopoles that row 8 names. See [Row 8: no monopoles of our sector](/wiki/predictions/monopole-null) and [Conjugate monopoles as ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles#the-laboratory-shadow-t).
- **Wigner function; Weyl symbol.** A drawing of a quantum state as a distribution over phase space, whose sums over all momenta and over all positions give the position and momentum probabilities; unlike a probability, it can be negative. Weyl symbols turn operators into functions on phase space in the same way. See [The Wigner function and Weyl symbols](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/wigner-function).
- **Withdrawn.** The status of #14 after round 3: "Kept as the audit trail; not for circulation." See [The soliton candidate (retired)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate) and [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).
- **Witten effect.** A monopole in a theory with a nonzero vacuum angle θ acquires an electric charge, θ/2π of the unit charge for each unit of magnetic charge, which makes it a dyon; with kinetic mixing it would give the retired candidate's hidden monopoles a small visible charge (round-3 ruling 9), a channel strict sector separation closed. See [The Witten effect](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/witten-effect) and [Kinetic mixing between sectors](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing).
- **Worklog.** *Collision.* The v3 worklog, `review/v3-worklog.md`, records the revision of #14 between rounds 2 and 3; the toy-model worklog, `notes/toy-lagrangian.md`, records the building of the toy models. See [Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary#the-v3-worklog) and [Toy models of broken reciprocity](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models).

## X

- **X-condensate.** A coherent, spatially uniform oscillation of the massive momentum graviton X, proposed as the dark matter; one of the two remaining candidates, which would make dark matter and the hidden half of the geometry one field. See [The X-condensate candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/x-condensate).
- **x-polarized; p-polarized; x-tilted.** Organized around position, or around momentum. Ordinary matter is x-polarized and conjugate matter p-polarized (P4); our vacuum is x-tilted, and its mirror would be organized around momentum (P5). See [P4: polarized matter and strict sector separation](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) and [P5: the vacuum selects a polarization](/wiki/program/postulates/p5-spontaneous-selection).

## Y

- **Yukawa force; range; Yukawa coupling.** *Collision.* A Yukawa force is the force carried by a massive particle, which dies away beyond a range of order the inverse of the particle's mass. X's force is of this kind, so its gravitational-strength effects are confined to short distances, where experiment does not constrain them. A Yukawa coupling is a different object: the term that joins a scalar field to a fermion and, through the Higgs field's vacuum value, gives the fermion its mass; the toy models use it as an analogy for how a choice of vacuum can make matter's interactions local. See [Yukawa forces and their range](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces), its section on [the name collision](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/yukawa-forces#a-name-collision-the-yukawa-coupling), and [Safety by range: X and ordinary matter](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety).
