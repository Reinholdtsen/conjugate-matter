*The literature battery is the planned check of every reference that the program's papers cite from model memory, marked †, against the published source. A † citation was recalled by Claude rather than retrieved, so its authors, title, journal reference, or the statement it is cited for may be wrong. Checkpoint #11 lists the battery as open problem 10 of its §14 and calls itself "not for submission until the † audit (open item 10) completes". The battery is part of the first item of the current work queue.*

**Status:** Open — [O] (#11 §14 item 10) — no completed battery is recorded in the repository; it gates any submission (#11 footer) and sits in item 1 of the current queue (root `CLAUDE.md`). Listed in [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The open item.** "Literature battery completion; every † reference source-checked [O]." (#11 §14 item 10).
- **Why it gates external use.** #11's footer: "Working checkpoint — not for submission until the † audit (open item 10) completes." `HANDOFF.md` §7: "All †-flagged citations must be source-verified before anything goes external." Root `CLAUDE.md` defines † as "from model memory (verify against the source before any external use)" ([Provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags)).
- **Its scope.** `HANDOFF.md` §8 item 6: "Literature battery completion: every † reference in #11 and #13 source-checked; resolve the gravity-induced kinetic mixing question." The second half was settled by round-3 Decision 1, "Kinetic mixing: strict sector separation", now part of postulate [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter) ([Round-3 decisions and lessons](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions)).
- **What is to be checked.** In #11's reference list, entries [1]–[18] and [23] carry †, and [19]–[22] and [24]–[29] carry [S]. #13's references [1]–[4] all carry †. The lists are collected on [References](/wiki/references); the documents are [the checkpoint](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) and [conjugate special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity).
- **An unreferenced phrase.** #11 §11.3 says that choosing a duality frame "is choosing a Lagrangian polarization of charge space, in the literature's own words", without naming the source of the words ([The symplectic charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice)).
- **Its place in the queue.** Root `CLAUDE.md`: "1. HANDOFF §8 items 5–7: the partially-massless comparison, the † literature battery, the phase-space-diameter note." See [Open: the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison), [the phase-space diameter relation](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter) and [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).
- **Its history.** The v1 paper's open problems ended with "Literature verifications: symmetric-bimetric Λ = 0; LPT coefficient calibration; Dalal–Kravtsov exact bound [O]" (#9 §10 item 8; LPT is Levkov–Panin–Tkachev), and its provenance note asked that "All citations flagged [T — verify] should be checked against sources before any submission" (#9 §11). The Λ = 0 comparison is now #11's open problem 2.
- **Citation checks done so far cover other documents.** The blind referees checked the references of the manuscripts they reviewed: in round 3 those of #14 v3 (predicted "All 23 citations resolve"; outcome "All exist"), in round 4 those of the toy-model note ("All resolve"). Round 4's revisions required that "Every new citation is to be retrieved, not recalled." Neither round covered #11 or #13. See [Review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) and [Review round 4](/wiki/method/review/round-4).
