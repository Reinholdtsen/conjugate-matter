*Review rounds 1 and 2 were the program's first two blind reviews, both of #14, the standalone manuscript proposing superheavy hidden-sector solitons as dark matter. Both ran through the [skeptic review console](/wiki/method/review/skeptic-console): a fresh referee with web search and no code execution. Round 1 graded version 1 B, flawed but salvageable, with medium confidence, and every valid finding was folded into version 2; round 2 graded version 2 "B near B/C" and called the derivation of the hidden coupling numerology. Round 2's findings became the pending list R1–R7, from which version 3 was cut for [round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3). The full reports and adjudications were never imported into the repository: they exist only in the old chat and in two text files Paul holds.*

**Status:** Record — untagged — summarized in `HANDOFF.md` §6; the full adjudications, "with executed arbitration code", exist only in the chat (`HANDOFF.md` §4). The pending list is moot: v3 was cut from it, failed round 3 and was withdrawn, and the candidate is [retired](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary).

## Key facts

- **Round 1** ("fresh Sonnet, web search, blind"): verdict "**B / medium**". Valid hits, "all incorporated in #14 v2": the α_d-from-our-DSZ contradiction (DSZ: the Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger charge pairing), "restated as reciprocity-as-S conjecture"; the Preskill citation inverted (fixed); kinetic mixing unaddressed ("postulate + ε ≲ 10⁻⁵ exclusion added"); "knots" renamed "π₂ monopole-class"; gravitational-wave backgrounds added; and the "toy-Lagrangian demand adopted as acceptance criterion #1" ([acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion)). The first hit is a dead claim: [α_d ≈ 34 via Dirac quantization against our photon](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling), "round-1 reviewer's valid kill" (`HANDOFF.md` §2).
- **Round 1's errors** ("machine-arbitrated"): "GeV→gram conversion off 10³; σ_T/M misread as σ_T (off ~10⁴³); insinuated the compatibility theorem might be false (it is machine-verified)" ([the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem)).
- **Round 2** ("same protocol, on v2"): verdict "**B near B/C / medium**"; weakest link "the α_d derivation ("matrix coincidence ≠ physical identity" / "numerology")". Its errors: it "fed ρ_DM = 1.26×10⁻³ GeV/cm³ instead of 1.26×10⁻⁶ (the manuscript's own number) — with the right input the closure lands within 3%", and "conflated mean vs local density labels". These errors are the evidence for [the measured failure mode](/wiki/method/review/referee-failure-mode).
- **The pending list R1–R7** and its status when v3 was cut (`review/v3-worklog.md`):
  - R1, the supersymmetry-exactness burden for Montonen–Olive duality: ready ([S-duality and Montonen–Olive](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/s-duality)).
  - R2, the coupling convention and running spread: adjudicated [V]. "Convention: 1/(4α) = 34.26 (eg = 2πn) vs 1/α = 137.04 (eg = 4πn)"; the referee's "toward ~6–8" is "not reproduced for the electromagnetic coupling".
  - R3, the extended Parker bound: ready, counterfactual only; "Excess at the 10¹⁶ benchmark: 6.98 orders [V]" ([monopole flux bounds](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches)).
  - R4, gravity-induced kinetic mixing: ready, with the question changed ([kinetic mixing between sectors](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing)).
  - R5, the entropy-dilution caveat: adjudicated [V], "stronger than the referee said".
  - R6, naming an explicit candidate group: ready; "SU(2) → U(1) by an adjoint Higgs".
  - R7, the bimetric cosmological-branch instability: "ready, with a tension", since reciprocity forces equal Planck masses ([Open: stability at equal Planck masses](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/equal-planck-mass-stability)).
- **Pre-registration.** Both rounds were pre-registered and "scored 2 for 2 on verdicts" (`review/round3-preregistration.md`, citing `HANDOFF.md` §6); the pre-registrations themselves are not in the repository ([pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/pre-registration)).
- **Where the records are.** "Paul also holds two referee-report text files (round 1 and round 2 outputs of #15)" (`HANDOFF.md` §3); the worklog: "The round-2 referee report itself is not in the repo." The console page embeds v2 and is kept as the record of the two rounds.
- **The common thread.** Round 3's adjudication: "Every referee across three rounds converges on 'the framework does no work'" ([the 'framework does no work' critique](/wiki/program/framework-does-no-work)).
- **Unresolved count.** `HANDOFF.md` §1 says "Three blind AI reviews returned verdict B", while its §6 records two completed rounds with verdict B and a third left incomplete. No document reconciles the count.
