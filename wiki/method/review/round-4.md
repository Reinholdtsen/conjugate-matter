*Review round 4 was the blind review of the toy-model note, `papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`, which builds two explicit field theories in which Born reciprocity is a symmetry of the action and asks whether the vacuum breaks it. A fresh referee that could execute code reproduced the note's algebra and numerics, but found that the one claim of quantum symmetry breaking rested on a potential that is unbounded below once the field H is allowed to vary. It graded the note B, flawed but salvageable, and the author agreed; the pre-registered forecast had also been B. Every revision was accepted and applied in version 2 of the note on 2026-10-20, and the round added two rules to the project's method.*

**Status:** Record — untagged — `review/round4-adjudication.md`, with the referee's notes in `review/round4/referee/audit-notes.md`; the revisions were applied in commit 6606734 (toy note v2 and #11 v2.2). A round-5 review is optional, "but only before any external use" (`CLAUDE.md` 'Since the handoff').

## Key facts

- **The round and its forecast.** The manuscript was the note "as of commit d9a06ac", the referee "a blind subagent with Python and web", "interrupted once by a rate limit and resumed from its own transcript" ([the review protocol](/wiki/method/review/review-protocol)). "All 9 of its numerical and algebraic indictments rest on executed code"; its citation indictments "rest on retrieved sources (INSPIRE API, arXiv e-print sources), none from memory". The pre-registration, committed "with a neutral subject", predicted B with medium confidence and named as weakest link "in 1+1, the 'spontaneous' breaking of ρ rests on an explicit, rigid background", the fixed pair of axes K_ref. Scored: verdict "**Hit**"; weakest link "**Partial**: right location, missed severity"; arithmetic "**Hit on count, miss on location**"; citations "**Hit**" ([pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/pre-registration)). The verdict: "Referee: B (flawed but salvageable), confidence ~0.75. I agree." "The algebra and numerics of §§2, 3 and 5 reproduced"; "It is repairable."
- **F1–F2, the potential (upheld).** Along the boost family H = SᵀS, K = S⁻¹K₀S the potential −μ[Tr(K K_ref)]² gives V_μ = −4, −56.6, −2983, −8.9×10⁶ at t = 0, 1, 2, 4, so (1, K_ref) is a saddle (Hessian eigenvalues −6.47, −4.40, −4.04 at λ = 1, 10, 100, in the adjudicator's coordinates), and the Monte Carlo simulates a frozen-H reduction, not model I. The referee's bounded variant −μ[Tr(K K_ref)]²/(2Tr KᵀK) gives eigenvalues [0, 0.764, 2, 5.236]: "**This is the repair.** I should have caught F1 myself: I checked the potential only at frozen H." ([Obituary: the naive ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential); [the bounded potential V_b](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/bounded-potential); [the frozen-H reduction](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/frozen-h-reduction)). On the zero mode the sources conflict: the adjudication calls it "the residual diagonal squeeze, which is an exact symmetry"; version 2 of the note says "It is not symmetry-protected, because V_b breaks the squeeze away from K = ±K_ref", and the note governs.
- **F3 and F5 (upheld).** The twisted ρ, ρ∘(K → −K), maps the vacuum (1, K₀) to itself, so "Breaking ρ means something physically only once a coupling odd in K breaks the K → −K symmetry" ([the twisted element](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/twisted-element)). The matter coupling I_K needs H as well as K: "rescaling the eigenline coordinate by 2 doubles I (0.407 → 0.814)" ([locality from an (H, K)-built coupling](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/matter-coupling)).
- **F4 (upheld, "with a consequence the referee did not draw").** In the real Grosse–Wulkenhaar model the two sign-vacua have equal classical action (−2.5) but "F(+) − F(−) = 2.634", and "The lower free energy belongs to diag(a₀, −a₁), the β = π vacuum, which is the one sharper in position". It is "explicit, not spontaneous" and "recorded as a lead, not a result". The adjudication left truncation dependence "unchecked"; version 2 reports the same value "at truncations N = 6, 8, 10" ([Open: the real model's one-loop preference](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/real-model-lead)).
- **F6 and the Monte Carlo control.** F6, "Accepted, not re-checked": the claim that "Increasing the truncation adds only massive levels" ignores mass renormalization; "The no-breaking conclusion is unaffected; the argument must be restated" ([quantum restoration in the complex model](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/quantum-restoration)). Unresolved: the referee and the adjudication write a "2 ln N" mass renormalization, while version 2, `verify/toy_gw.py` and the toy worklog write ln N ("it is a harmonic sum"). The two count different sums (the referee's 2 Σₖ (G₀ₖ + Gₖ₀), the gate's Σₖ G₀ₖ), and no document reconciles them. The control (upheld): the quoted 0.078 / 0.033 / 0.014 at T = 3 did not match the referee's 0.0630 / 0.0318 / 0.0156; the L = 16 value from seed 116 "was a short-run fluctuation"; "**Fixed seeds gave reproducibility, not accuracy; the numbers lacked error bars.**" Version 2 quotes 0.0653 ± 0.0043, 0.0338 ± 0.0013, 0.0155 ± 0.0004; hot starts at L = 64 trap in metastable domains (accepted) ([Monte Carlo: an ordered ℤ₂ phase](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/monte-carlo)).
- **Scope, citations and prior work.** The double-field-theory "pair" holds "only for d = 1" ([the section condition](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/section-condition)); the Mermin–Wagner statement "must be restricted to the compact SO(2) containing ρ", since model I's H-sector "is a hyperbolic SO(1,2) model, which by Duncan–Niedermaier–Seiler can break in 2D" ([Open: does ρ's SO(2) break in the coupled model?](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/so2-question)). Citation fixes: "A. Duncan, not N."; an erratum; Grosse–Wulkenhaar's "eq. 1 is the 4D form (π²)"; one reference is "about 4D"; and one claim "cannot be checked (paywalled)". Omitted prior work, accepted "as citations and reframing": the Langmann–Szabo–Zarembo exact solution, Smolin's triality paper ("Smolin breaks a triality *down to* Born duality, leaving it unbroken"), Marotta–Szabo, Fosco–Moreno, Grosse–Wulkenhaar's statement that the duality is "manifest" in the matrix base, and Royer's parity-operator form of Weyl symbols ([the duality on the matrix base](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/matrix-base-lemma)). The referee's full-text searches found no prior explicit model with the Langmann–Szabo duality spontaneously broken by vacua; "Model I appears new as a field theory, but it is a simple coset".
- **Revisions and lessons.** Revisions 1–6, "all accepted; applied 2026-10-20 in commit 6606734": the bounded potential, stable with H dynamical, and the Monte Carlo presented as the frozen-H reduction with start conditions and error bars; the twisted ρ; the matrix-base duality as a credited lemma, "Goldstone angle" restricted to the complex model, F4 added as a lead; the restoration argument restated; the d = 1 pair and an (H, K)-covariant I_K; and added references (Langmann–Szabo–Zarembo, Smolin, Marotta–Szabo, Fosco–Moreno, Royer), each "to be retrieved, not recalled". Version 2 departs from the list on the zero mode, on ln N in place of 2 ln N, and on the Langmann–Szabo–Zarembo comparison, of which the note says "We have not carried it out." The two lessons went into `CLAUDE.md`: [the reduced-model stability rule](/wiki/method/verification/reduced-model-stability) and [the Monte Carlo error-bar rule](/wiki/method/verification/monte-carlo-error-bars). #11 §14 item 11 records the outcome: "Round-4 blind review: verdict B; v2 applies the accepted revisions" ([acceptance criterion 1](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/acceptance-criterion)).
- **Arbitration and the gate.** The round's files are `review/round4-preregistration.md`, `review/round4-adjudication.md`, the referee's notes, scripts and outputs in `review/round4/referee/`, and the adjudicator's in `review/round4/adjudicator/`. There `arb.py` covers F1–F5 and `ctrl.py` the control run; they stay in `review/` and the gate does not run them. Each of F1–F6 has a check in the gate's toy scripts (F1–F3 in `verify/toy_coset.py`, F4 and F6 in `verify/toy_gw.py`, F5 in `verify/toy_s5.py`), and the corrected control values in `verify/toy_s4.py` ([the toy-model scripts](/wiki/method/verification/toy-model-scripts)).
