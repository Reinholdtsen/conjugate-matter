*Blind review is the program's procedure for testing a manuscript: a fresh AI referee receives the manuscript, with no project files and no conversation history, and is asked to find what is wrong with it and to grade it A, B or C. Before each round the author writes down the expected grade and the weakest point the referee is expected to find, and fixes the forecast before dispatch, by a commit or by a recorded hash, so that the author's judgement can be scored afterwards; after it, the author rules on every finding in a written adjudication, re-running disputed numbers in code. Five rounds have run. The first two reviewed the [soliton dark-matter manuscript](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md) [#14](../../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) with a referee that could search the web but not run code; the third reviewed #14's third version with a referee that executed code, and its verdict failed #14's model and led to the model's retirement; the fourth reviewed the [toy-model note](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md) and led to its version 2; the fifth sent the note on [the phase-space diameter relation](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md) to two referees at once and led to its version 1.1. The referees of rounds 3 to 5 were, as their pre-registrations state, the same model family as the author.*

**Method and record only:** the section holds the review method in force and the record of the five rounds, so it ranks no claims of the program's own; those are tracked in the registers of [claims that stand](../../status/alive.md), [conjectures](../../status/conjectures.md), [open problems and the queue](../../status/open-problems.md), [claims under review](../../status/under-review.md), [superseded statements](../../status/superseded.md) and [the graveyard](../../status/graveyard.md). What the reviews found against a claim is stated in full on the claim's page. [#14's superheavy-soliton model](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md), from round 3, is in the graveyard. Other claims the reviews weighed against are under review: from round 1, [the coupling α_d ≈ 34](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md) in its first form, Dirac quantization against our photon; from round 3 [the Kibble normalization](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md), among others, and [hypothesis H](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling.md#hypothesis-h), which as the third way of fixing that coupling is under review with it; from round 4 [the naive ρ-preserving potential](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential.md); and two claims that round 5's note had itself set aside before the review: it withdrew [the square rectangle at the bounce](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/square-at-the-bounce.md) and excluded [the self-dual length as a program scale](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-length-obituary.md). Both referees of round 5 found the withdrawal standing under every reading they constructed; the round refuted a side claim, that the agreement of two cell counts "holds at no other epoch", and its major ruling restricted [the bound on X's range](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound.md) to the program's aligned vacuum. The section's one open item is optional: a round-6 review of the toy-model note, "but only before any external use" (root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md)).

- [The blind-review protocol and verdict scale](review-protocol.md) comes first because every round is an instance of it. It sets out the five steps a referee follows (verify every citation, re-run every number in code from its own constants, re-derive the mathematics, audit the argument, grade it with the single weakest link named) and how each was carried out in rounds 3 and 4. It gives the A/B/C scale with its two glosses of C and where the records draw the B/C line, the labels the author's adjudication uses (upheld, accepted, overturned), and the protocol's limits.
- [The skeptic review console](skeptic-console.md) is the tool of the first two rounds, a browser page ([#15](../../../review/skeptic-review-console.html)) that sends the manuscript to a fresh model with web search but no code execution. Its referee prompt, quoted as record, defines the A/B/C scale. The page embeds #14 version 2, and the page traces what became of each of that version's claims.
- [Pre-registration and its calibration record](pre-registration.md) is the forecast written and fixed before each round and scored after it: what a forecast contains, the scoring tables of rounds 3 to 5, and the record across rounds (verdicts 2 for 2 in rounds 1–2, a miss in round 3, hits in rounds 4 and 5; the weakest link's location hit in rounds 4 and 5, while its severity in round 4, and the number of small errors and the extent of prior art in round 5, were underestimated). It also records how round 3's forecast reached the referee through a commit subject, the rule that followed, and round 5's forecast kept outside the repository with its hash recorded.
- [The measured referee failure mode](referee-failure-mode.md) is what the rounds showed about where review goes wrong. In rounds 1–2 it was the referees' unaided arithmetic: three confident order-of-magnitude indictments, none surviving. Once referees could run code, the errors that survived were the author's: in round 3 wrong model inputs that a check of arithmetic passed, in round 4 a stability check made on a reduced model and Monte Carlo values without error bars, in round 5 a parameter dropped from a cited formula.
- [Review rounds 1 and 2](rounds-one-and-two.md) are the two console reviews of #14, versions 1 and 2. Only the handoff's summary survives in the repository, and the page says first what is missing. It gives round 1's valid findings and where each went, round 2's weakest link and its pending list R1–R7 with the status each had when version 3 was cut, and the referees' own errors.
- [Review round 3](round-3/_index.md) is the code-executing audit of #14 v3, graded C. Its rulings found the [Kibble normalization](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md) wrong and the benchmark mass overproduced, and its decisions retired #14's model. It has its own pages for the forecast, the rulings, the decisions and lessons, and the arbitration.
- [Review round 4](round-4.md) is the review of the toy-model note: the referee's citation audit and re-derivations, findings F1–F6 (above all, a potential unbounded below once the field H varies), the Monte Carlo control re-run, the forecast scored, the six revisions applied in version 2 and the three places where version 2 departs from them (one a requested comparison it did not make, flag 42 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)), and the two rules the round added to the method.
- [Round 5](round-5.md) is the review of the note on the phase-space diameter relation by two referees at once, both graded B. Its major ruling, R1, found that the note's bound on [the momentum graviton](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/_index.md)'s range had quoted a bimetric coupling formula without the ratio of the two metrics' backgrounds; the ruling upheld the finding in substance, did not uphold one referee's value for the ratio, and restated the bound for the program's aligned vacuum, with the bound at other ratios. The page gives the hashed forecast and its score, the other rulings, the two lessons, version 1.1, which carries out every accepted revision, and version 1.2 (2026-10-29), which lists the note's withdrawal of the square at the bounce as under review, its results standing.
