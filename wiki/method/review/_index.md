*Blind review is the program's procedure for testing a manuscript: a fresh AI referee receives the manuscript, with no project files and no conversation history, and is asked to find what is wrong with it and to grade it A, B or C. Before each round the author writes down and commits the expected grade and the weakest point the referee is expected to find, so that the author's judgement can be scored afterwards. Four rounds have run. The first two reviewed the soliton dark-matter manuscript #14 with a referee that could search the web but not run code; the third reviewed #14's third version with a referee that executed code, and its verdict led to the candidate's retirement; the fourth reviewed the toy-model note. The referees of rounds 3 and 4 were, as their pre-registrations state, the same model family as the author.*

**Stands:** the method in force — [the review protocol and verdict scale](/wiki/method/review/review-protocol), [pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/pre-registration); the records — [the skeptic review console](/wiki/method/review/skeptic-console), [the measured failure mode](/wiki/method/review/referee-failure-mode), [rounds 1 and 2](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two), [round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3), [round 4](/wiki/method/review/round-4) · **Died:** nothing in this section (what the reviews killed has its own obituaries, listed in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard)) · **Open:** nothing in this section (an optional round-5 review of the toy note is in [the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems))

## Map

The section runs from the procedure to its record. The first four pages say how a review is run and what the rounds have shown about referees and authors; the last three are the rounds themselves, in date order. Everything here is the program's own method and history. The physics each round tested or changed is on the topic pages, listed under Boundaries.

- [The blind-review protocol and verdict scale](/wiki/method/review/review-protocol) comes first because every round is an instance of it: a fresh subagent with only the manuscript checks the citations, re-runs every number in code, re-derives the mathematics, audits the concepts and grades A, B or C, naming the single weakest link.
- [The skeptic review console](/wiki/method/review/skeptic-console) is the tool of the first two rounds, a browser page (#15). It comes second because its referee prompt defines the A/B/C scale the protocol uses; its referee could search the web but could not execute code.
- [Pre-registration and its calibration record](/wiki/method/review/pre-registration) is the forecast written and committed before each round and scored after it: verdicts 2 for 2 in rounds 1–2, a miss in round 3, a hit in round 4.
- [The measured referee failure mode](/wiki/method/review/referee-failure-mode) is what the rounds showed about where review goes wrong: in rounds 1–2 the referees' unaided arithmetic, and in round 3 the author's own verification, which checked the arithmetic of a model with a wrong input.
- [Review rounds 1 and 2](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two) are the two console reviews of #14 (versions 1 and 2), graded B and "B near B/C". Their full adjudications exist only in the old chat.
- [Review round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) is the code-executing audit of #14 v3, graded C, which led to the retirement of the soliton candidate. It has its own pages for the forecast, the rulings, the decisions and the arbitration.
- [Review round 4](/wiki/method/review/round-4) is the review of the toy-model note, graded B as forecast, whose accepted revisions produced version 2 of the note and two new rules for the method.
