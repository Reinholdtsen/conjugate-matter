*Review round 3 was the third blind review of #14, the standalone manuscript proposing that dark matter consists of superheavy 't Hooft–Polyakov monopoles of a hidden gauge sector. It ran on 2026-10-19 on version 3, with a fresh subagent as referee that could execute code. The referee checked every citation against its source, recomputed every number in its own scripts, re-derived the manuscript's one theorem, looked for constraints the manuscript had left out, and graded it C, fundamentally flawed: the count of monopoles formed in the early universe, on which the predicted mass rested, used a wrong normalization of the Kibble estimate. The adjudicator recomputed the disputed numbers and agreed. The decisions that followed retired the soliton candidate, made strict sector separation the reading of postulate P4, and set the toy Lagrangian as the next task, and the round wrote two lessons into the program's method.*

**Records only:** the four pages of this section record one review and make no claims of their own. The statuses the round set are kept in the registers: the retired candidate and the parts of it that died, [the soliton candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary), [the Kibble normalization](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization), [the ψ relic window](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window), [Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator) and [the √N floor](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor), in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard); the corrected [overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction) and the [W′ overclosure](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure) among [the claims that stand](/wiki/status/alive); and the queue the round changed in [open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).

The pages take the round in that order, with the numerical evidence last.

- [Round-3 pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/round-3/preregistration) comes first because it was committed before the referee was dispatched. It forecast verdict B, the weakest link "the framework does no work", no surviving numerical indictment and all 23 citations resolving. The page sets out the forecast's reasoning, how each part scored (a miss on the verdict, the first after two hits), and how the commit's subject line leaked the forecast into the referee's view.
