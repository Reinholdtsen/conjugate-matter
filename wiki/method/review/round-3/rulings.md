*The round-3 verdict and rulings are the outcome of the blind audit of #14 v3, the superheavy-monopole dark-matter manuscript: the referee's grade, and the adjudicator's ruling on each of the referee's numbered findings. The referee graded the manuscript C, fundamentally flawed, with medium confidence: its headline mass prediction rested on a Kibble normalization that had dropped a factor of about 4.6×10³, and every downstream number depended on that mass. The adjudicator agreed. Of the thirteen numbered findings one, a complaint about a citation, was overturned, and the rest were upheld or accepted. The citation check found all 23 references real, with nothing fabricated, and the referee's list of omitted constraints was accepted as a to-do.*

**Status:** Record — untagged; #11 §12 tags the corrected numbers [V] — `review/round3-adjudication.md` 'Verdict' and 'Rulings', with the referee's evidence in `review/round3/referee/audit-notes.md`. The numbers of rulings 1, 2, 4 and 5 are gated in `verify/soliton_obituary.py` ([the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script)); the other rulings are not gated.

## Key facts

- **The verdict.** The referee's grade line, `GRADE: C (fundamentally flawed), confidence MEDIUM`, adds "high confidence in each individual finding; medium on the B/C boundary". The headline mass "with no dilution required" is "wrong on two independent counts": the Kibble normalization, and the "neglect of Kibble-Zurek / bubble overproduction with hidden-plasma annihilation"; every downstream number — v, m_W′, the ψ window, the gravitational-wave band and ε — "hangs on it". Weakest link: "the Kibble abundance / mass determination"; second, "the framework does no work" ([the critique](/wiki/program/framework-does-no-work)). "Salvage = a different model (v <~ 1e8 GeV, or explicit dilution), which already exists in the literature." The adjudication: "I agree for v3 as written."
- **Upheld:**
  - 1, the Kibble normalization: p = 104.31 with M_Pl, 0.828 with the reduced M_Pl; "v3's 0.0228 drops (1.66√g\*)³ ≈ 4.6×10³. My error came from misremembering the formula." ([Obituary: the Kibble normalization](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization))
  - 2, the mass band and "no dilution": "At minimum production M\* = 2.9–4.4×10¹² GeV. The 3×10¹³ benchmark is overproduced 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ times." The "reduced-Planck factor of 3" was not a convention spread ([overproduction at the benchmark](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/overproduction)).
  - 3, Kibble is a floor, not a ceiling: Murayama–Shu, Kibble "drastically underestimates the production of topological defects"; Liveoak–Maharana–Wells, hidden monopoles overclose "for breaking scales ≳ 100 PeV unless diluted". The referee's Kibble–Zurek integration (10⁹–10¹² overproduction) was not re-run ([the Kibble mechanism](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism)).
  - 4, the ψ relic window: v3 "omitted ψ̄ and the second doublet member (a factor of 4)"; the recount puts 10% of the dark matter at 45–49 GeV and all of it at 146–158 GeV, against the referee's 28–46 / 96–153 GeV, "to within ×1.6" ([Obituary: the ψ relic window](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window)).
  - 5 (minor), the dark-radiation bound: with the Standard Model value N_eff,SM = 3.044, "the 95% bound is ΔN_eff < 0.286", so ξ < 0.760, not 0.77; the gravitational-wave upper end becomes 3.2×10⁷ Hz ([the hidden sector's thermal history](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-thermal-history)).
  - 7, the dimension-5 operator's "O(1) coefficient at the Planck scale" is unsupported: the literature generates it "at one loop from bi-charged states, which sector separation forbids"; v3's Sec. 2(a) and 2(b) "are in tension" ([Obituary: Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator)).
  - 10 (abstract level), annihilation at α_m ≈ 34 "is Preskill's Dirac case, already done for hidden sectors".
  - 13 (in substance), v3 §6's "all numbers computed by executed code" overstates: "The code checked the arithmetic of a wrong model." ([verification checks arithmetic, not the model](/wiki/method/verification/arithmetic-not-model))
- **Accepted, not re-checked:** 6, ψ at the MeV scale freezes out during Big Bang nucleosynthesis, "T_f ~ m/20, so the lower mass bound should be ≳ 20–30 MeV"; 9, the Witten effect: with kinetic mixing and a physical hidden θ the monopoles carry a visible electric charge ~ εθ/2π · e, which "contradicts v3's 'constraints act on ψ, not the dark matter' whenever ε ≠ 0" ([kinetic mixing between sectors](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kinetic-mixing)). **Accepted:** 11, the best fit of [DESI DR2](/wiki/cosmology/dark-energy/desi-dr2-phantom-crossing) "crosses into phantom (w < −1) above z ≈ 0.4"; 12, minor citation issues, "[4] over-generalized; Zwanziger's 4π vs 2π; Schwinger uncited".
- **Overturned:** 8. The referee's finding was that "Baumgart et al. does not contain the adjoint operator" (its notes mark the reference `DOES NOT SUPPORT`); the adjudicator found the operator in arXiv:0901.0283v2, p. 5, footnote 5 ("an S parameter type operator Tr[Φw_μν]B^μν"). "The referee missed the footnote."
- **Omitted constraints**, "accepted as a to-do; not individually re-checked": re-thermalization by ψ at ε ~ 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁶; supernova SN1987A and Big Bang nucleosynthesis limits on millicharged particles; charged-subcomponent and cosmic-microwave-background bounds; dark acoustic oscillations (ψ kinetically coupled to the hidden photons late enough to leave acoustic oscillations in its density [T]); the Higgs portal (a coupling of the hidden scalar to the Higgs field, "allowed by every stated symmetry"); the reheating mechanism for ξ; collective magnetic-plasma effects; and uncited prior art (Murayama–Shu, Liveoak–Maharana–Wells, Baek–Ko–Park, Khoze–Ro). The referee's notes give, for example, SN1987A excluding millicharges from 10⁻⁹ to a few ×10⁻⁶ for masses below about 200 MeV, and a magnetic-plasma timescale 1/ω_p of 17 yr locally to 9.7×10³ yr at the cosmic mean, "Unevaluated, not an indictment". [Decision 1](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions) later closed three of these channels: "ψ millicharge, the monopoles' Witten-effect charge (ruling 9), and re-thermalization of the hidden sector".
- **The compatibility theorem** "is verified (sympy, including a Groebner basis at d = 2; random tests at d = 3, 4)", but it is "essentially the Born-geometry result of Freidel–Leigh–Minic (arXiv:1502.08005), which is uncited", so its "[D] derived here" tag "should become [T]". #11 v2.1 re-tagged it [T][V] and records that "the positivity step is not needed for C = 0" ([the compatibility theorem](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem)).
- **The citation audit** (referee, Step 1): all 23 references, "37 individual items", "resolve on INSPIRE with matching authors / journal / volume / page / year / arXiv id. No fabricated or mis-numbered reference found." Flagged: Born 1938/1949 only partially verified ("primary text not read"); [4] a "slight over-generalisation"; [10] "cited for more than it says"; the monopole-mass factor f ~ 1.79 is not in [16] and is uncited; Kolb & Turner "not checked page-level"; Zwanziger's "4 pi Z" against the manuscript's "2 pi Z"; Schwinger not cited. Before the round, the v3 worklog had already corrected v2's list: Kibble's title is "Topology of Cosmic Domains and Strings"; the 10⁻¹⁵ Parker value is to be cited from the Particle Data Group's 2024 review or Turner–Parker–Bogdan (1982), "not Parker 1970 alone"; and "Harvey's lectures are conference proceedings, not a journal article" ([monopole flux bounds](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/monopole-searches)). The same discipline for #11's memory-cited references is still open ([Open: the † literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery)).
- **What survives v3**, per the adjudication: the compatibility theorem (as [T]); "the benchmark-conditional arithmetic: flux, densities, σ_T/M, W′ overclosure"; the kinetic-mixing points (a) and (c); and the exclusion of the minimal adjoint-only sector ([W′ overclosure](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure); [the obituary](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)).
