*Verification, in this program, means recomputing the numbers that the documents assert with scripts kept in the repository's `verify/` folder, under the rule that every asserted or disputed number gets executed code, and running those scripts together in one gate, [`scripts/check.sh`](../../../scripts/check.sh), which must pass before work counts as finished. Each script prints a pass or a fail for every check, with the measured value where there is one. The rules behind the scripts were learned from specific failures: AI referees made confident arithmetic errors, a script confirmed the arithmetic of a wrong physical input, a stability check covered only a frozen version of a model, and simulation numbers came without error bars. A passing script shows that the arithmetic is right for the model it encodes, not that the model describes nature. Two scripts check literature searches instead: they confirm that the literature-check note and the dual-sector note report their stored records, not the agents' judgments behind them. One section of the first checks the algebra behind flag 66, on the program's duality dictionary, and one section of the second the bracket behind the dual-sector note's doubled phase space.*

**Method pages only:** this section holds rules and the scripts that apply them, all in force, and makes no physics claims; what stands, what is open, what is under review and what is dead is registered in [claims that stand](../../status/alive.md), [open problems and the queue](../../status/open-problems.md), [claims under review](../../status/under-review.md) and [the graveyard](../../status/graveyard.md).

## Map

The rules come first, in the order the program learned them, each with the episode that produced it and its applications since, the wiki build's executed checks among them. The gate and the scripts it runs follow. None of these pages states physics: each script page says what it checks, what it does not, and which topic pages carry the claims.

- [The iron rule: no arithmetic by eyeball](iron-rule.md) is the founding rule: every asserted or disputed number gets executed code with printed output. It came from review rounds 1 and 2, whose referees, working without code execution, made three confident order-of-magnitude accusations that were all their own unit errors. The page follows the rule into the scripts, the referees' and adjudicators' practice, the drafting of the [soliton manuscript](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md), and the wiki build.
- [Verification checks arithmetic, not the model](arithmetic-not-model.md) is the iron rule's limit, learned in round 3: every number of the soliton manuscript was computed by a gated script, and its [Kibble normalization](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md) was still wrong. All four surviving indictments were inputs or model choices, not arithmetic slips.
- [Independent anchors](independent-anchors.md) is round 3's repair: a script first tests its formulas on a textbook identity or a published value. The page quotes the circular anchor from the retired script and lists the anchors each current script uses, and why parity checks are not anchors.
- [The reduced-model stability rule](reduced-model-stability.md) is round 4's first lesson: the [coset toy](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/_index.md)'s [first potential](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential.md) had been checked only with H frozen and is unbounded below once H moves, so its vacuum was a saddle. The repair is checked over all fields, the texts name the simulation a reduction, and the zero mode's status is disputed between the sources (flag 12).
- [The Monte Carlo error-bar rule](monte-carlo-error-bars.md) is round 4's second lesson: a fixed-seed control value was a short-run fluctuation. The lattice script now averages four independent seeds, and two of its checks are stated in units of the error bar.
- [The gate: scripts/check.sh](the-gate.md) is the definition of done: how it runs, what each script gates, what is not gated, how its script list grew from the battery alone to the scripts below and the wiki check, and a recorded run.
- [The verification battery T1–T7](battery.md) checks the [Tier-1](../claim-tiers.md#tier-1-explicit-algebra) algebra of checkpoint [#11](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md), test by test: what is drawn, what is asserted with what tolerance, which claim it gates, and its recorded output.
- [The soliton-obituary script](soliton-obituary-script.md) recomputes the numbers of the record of [#14's failed soliton model](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md) after four anchors. Its docstring claims every number of that record; the page states which numbers the script does not check (flag 33 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)).
- [The toy-model scripts](toy-model-scripts.md) check the toy-model note's [criteria S1–S5](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/success-criteria.md): the coset sigma model, the [self-dual Grosse–Wulkenhaar model](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/grosse-wulkenhaar/_index.md), the [lattice Monte Carlo](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/monte-carlo.md) and [matter locality](../../symmetry-breaking/matter-coupling.md), each with its method, its limits and its recorded output.
- [The phase-space-diameter script](phase-space-diameter-script.md) recomputes every number that the note on [the phase-space diameter relation](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md) computes, against anchors independent of the note: the CODATA 2018 Planck units, the Particle Data Group's densities and dark-energy scale, a Bose integral, a published horizon and two published torsion-balance limits. The page sets out its blocks in the note's order, the checks the script labels as an identity or as arithmetic of quoted factors, the recomputations by a separate agent and by both referees of [round 5](../review/round-5.md), the checks the round added and the later tightening, and what it leaves unchecked: the literature's own figures, the citations, and the premise u = 1 behind the bound on [X's range](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/range-bound.md).
- [The closed-universe script](closed-universe-script.md) recomputes every number of the closed-universe note of 2026-10-27, which asks what follows for the program if space is closed ([a closed Comatter universe](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md)). Its anchors come first: the CODATA Planck units, the exact solutions of a closed universe of dust and radiation that its integrator must reproduce, the spectrum of the three-sphere, a published mode of the dodecahedral space, published curvature values and a fit calibrated against two published results of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument. Everything else after them is a regression check of the investigation's own numbers, in five blocks: the closed τ histories, the bounds given closure, the bimetric sector, momentum space and the topology of the polarization manifold (section F, flag 61). That block has two winding-number anchors of its own; it computes the maps behind the homotopy groups of [#11 §14 item 12](../../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) and matches the groups to Bott periodicity's table. The page states what the script trims: four histories in place of the 96-history grid, one phase of τ's oscillation at the crunch, and two curvature reductions taken as input. The note has not been reviewed.
- [The velocity-gated-gravity script](velocity-gated-gravity-script.md) recomputes the numbers of the note of 2026-10-28 on momentum gravity and a relative-velocity gate, which examines the scope of the tests of velocity clumping, [the founding image](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md), P.R.'s open conjecture [O] ([X between ordinary bodies](../../gravity/momentum-graviton/ordinary-bodies.md), [Conjecture: a long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ](../../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/long-range-gated-attraction.md), [what could carry a relative-velocity gate](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-carriers.md)). Its anchors are independent of the investigation: CODATA constants, the Einstein–Infeld–Hoffmann and Darwin Lagrangians, textbook spin-2 numerators, [Planck 2018](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209)'s age, exact growth solutions, a closed-form kernel, published torsion-balance ranges, Mercury's perihelion and the Plummer sphere. Then come regression checks in nine blocks, among them the port of calc note [#2](../../../notes/stream-stability.md)'s shortfall into the gate (flag 16's addendum) and the Vainshtein formula of flag 57. The N-body runs of the narrow-gate corner are not rerun: the script checks the quoted numbers against their kept logs. The note has not been reviewed.
- [The gate-mechanism script](gate-mechanism-script.md) reruns the arithmetic and algebra of the gate-mechanism note of 2026-10-28 ([the gate mechanism](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism.md#exchanging-x-cannot-produce-the-gate)): t-channel exchange of a massive spin-2 X with a general vertex, the Dirac case, the numbers at μc = 20 keV, the two readings of the fiber overlap, the vacuum polarization under boosts, the first derivation's checks, and the two derivations side by side. In the note's words it "does not test their physics". Its result is flag 55, pending.
- [The literature-check script](literature-check-script.md) checks the note on [the literature check](../literature-check.md) of P.R.'s conjectures against the record the check left: the verdicts recomputed from the merged findings, the counts, every quotation found again in a repository document or a stored source, every citation against a metadata snapshot, and the pre-registration's score, and in its section G the algebra behind flag 66, the [duality dictionary](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary.md) as scale-factor duality with sources. It checks that the note reports its record, not the agents' judgments of how close a paper comes to a conjecture.
- [The dual-sector script](dual-sector-script.md) checks the note on [dark matter as the dual Standard Model](../../dark-matter/conjugate-matter/dual-standard-model.md), the proposal of Minic and coauthors, against its record: every quotation found again in a repository document or a stored source, every citation against a metadata snapshot, the dates and counts, the pre-registration's score, the quotations of the wiki page and of the flags the note proposes, and in its section F the bracket of the doubled phase space. It checks that the note reports its record, not the agents' judgments of how close a paper comes to a conjecture.

## Boundaries

- What the review rounds found, and the referees' and adjudicators' own scripts, are in [blind review](../review/_index.md), notably [Review round 3](../review/round-3/_index.md), [Review round 4](../review/round-4.md) and [Round 5](../review/round-5.md). Round 5 added two rules to root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../../CLAUDE.md)'s method, to carry every parameter of a formula taken from the literature and to give a claim about how a quantity behaves over time executed code like any number; they are recorded on the round's page.
- The claims each script checks are stated on their topic pages, for example [the compatibility theorem](../../phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md), [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md), [the toy models](../../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md), [the phase-space diameter relation](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md) and [a closed Comatter universe](../../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/closed-universe.md).
- The [V] tag that a machine check earns is defined on [Provenance tags and citation marks](../provenance-tags.md), and the tier it belongs to on [Claim tiers and selection bias](../claim-tiers.md).
- Suspected errors in the source documents found while building this wiki and since, with their executed checks, are recorded in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../../review/wiki-build-flags.md) and [`review/wiki-build-checks/`](../../../review/wiki-build-checks/), outside the gate, except the arithmetic of flags 27 (addendum), 45–58 and 61, which is in `verify/`, the quotations of flags 64–69 and the algebra of flag 66, which [`verify/literature_check.py`](../../../verify/literature_check.py) checks, and the quotations of flag 64's addendum and of flags 70–73, which [`verify/dual_sector.py`](../../../verify/dual_sector.py) checks; the pages quote the sources faithfully and point to the flags.
