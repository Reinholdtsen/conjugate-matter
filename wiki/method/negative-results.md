*The program's rule on negative results is that a claim killed by its own calculations or by review is reported prominently, in an obituary of its own, and is not revived. Checkpoint #11 rates such failures as its most reliable new results, because the program's incentives ran against finding them. The founding image of the program, dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, died this way, and so did a later dark-matter candidate, superheavy solitons of a hidden gauge sector. The wiki gives every dead claim its own obituary page and lists them all in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).*

**Status:** Method, in force — untagged — root `CLAUDE.md` ('Method') and `HANDOFF.md` §7; applied in round-3 Decision 2 (`review/round3-adjudication.md`).

## Key facts

- **The rule.** "Report negative results prominently. Claims listed as dead in HANDOFF §2 stay dead." (root `CLAUDE.md`). `HANDOFF.md` §7: "keep reporting negative results prominently. Honest obituaries are part of the deliverable."
- **Why they count as the most reliable.** "Negative results (§9, §7.1, and the §12 obituary) are the most reliable Tier-3 items, since the program's incentive gradient ran against them." (#11 §2; see [Claim tiers and selection bias](/wiki/method/claim-tiers)). #11 §15 opens its assessment with the same point: "The founding image is dead, killed by this program's own calculations".
- **The dead list at the handoff.** `HANDOFF.md` §2, "Dead (killed by own calculations — keep dead)": momentum-space clumping as dynamics ([Obituary: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image)); "Conjugate collapse powers our expansion" ([its obituary](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion)); conjugate monopoles as the explanation of our monopole non-detection ([its obituary](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation)); and α_d ≈ 34 via Dirac quantization against our photon ([Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling)).
- **Deaths since the handoff.** `HANDOFF.md` §2 still lists superheavy solitons as its current best dark-matter candidate. Root `CLAUDE.md` overrides it: "The superheavy soliton candidate in HANDOFF §2 is dead as a program-native proposal" ([Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). Round 4 killed the naive potential of the first toy model ([its obituary](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential)).
- **The rule applied.** Round-3 Decision 2 retired the soliton candidate rather than rescue it with a free dilution factor: "The program's precedent is the Debt 3 obituary: a dead claim is reported prominently and stays dead." The rejected rescue would have cost the mass prediction, and "the program does not trade falsifiability for survival" (`review/round3-adjudication.md`; [Round-3 decisions and lessons](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions)).
- **Withdrawn predictions stay withdrawn.** The clumping rows of the original predictions table were struck: "Rows withdrawn permanently." (`HANDOFF.md` §2). See [Obituary: velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity), [Obituary: Fornax friction suppression](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction) and [History of the predictions table](/wiki/predictions/table-history).
- **The shape of an obituary.** #11 §12 lays out its obituary as "What was claimed", "What was built and how it was tested", "Why it failed", "Verdict", "What survives" and "Checks". The wiki's obituary pages follow the same order: what was claimed, how it was tested, why it failed, what survives (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`).
- **What a death leaves standing.** An obituary also records survivors. #11 §12: the minimal adjoint-only hidden sector "is excluded on its own terms" [V], and the Debt-3 loophole "remains untested, but has no candidate" ([Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)). The [V] numbers of that obituary are recomputed in the gate by [the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script).
