*The program's rule on negative results is that a failed calculation or a contrary datum is reported in full, where the claim is discussed. [Checkpoint #11](../history/documents/checkpoint.md) ranks negative results as the most reliable of its new structural results. One claim is recorded as dead, [#14's model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md) of a dark-matter candidate, superheavy solitons of a hidden gauge sector. The calculations that tested [the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md) of the program, dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, stand, and the image is P.R.'s open conjecture, as are three other founding ideas, each with the evidence that bears on it stated. Other claims that the calculations or data weigh against are [under review](../status/under-review.md), and their final status awaits review. The wiki gives a dead claim its own obituary page and lists it in [the graveyard](../status/graveyard.md).*

**Status:** Method, in force — untagged — root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) ('Method'), [`HANDOFF.md` §7](../../HANDOFF.md#7-working-conventions-and-user-preferences) and [`wiki/CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md); applied in [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested), [§10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology), [§11.1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#111-the-monopole-audit-revised-d), [§12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model), [§13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions), [§15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment) and [Appendix A](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates), and in round-3 Decision 2 ([`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md)).

## The rule

A failed calculation or a contrary datum is stated in full, where the claim is discussed (root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), 'Method'). A failure is reported where a reader will see it, not in a footnote, and [`HANDOFF.md` §7](../../HANDOFF.md#7-working-conventions-and-user-preferences) makes prominent reporting part of [the calibration](collaboration.md#the-calibration). A claim is recorded as dead only when theory and data kill it and P.R. agrees.

## Why negative results count as the most reliable

#11's methodological preface ranks them first among the program's new structural results (Tier 3): "Negative results (§9, §7.1, and the failure of [#14](../../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md)'s model in §12) are the most reliable Tier-3 items, since the program's incentive gradient ran against them." ([#11 §2](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#2-methodological-preface-provenance-and-verification)). The three named are the tests of [momentum clumping](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/_index.md) ([§9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)), whose results stand while the founding image is P.R.'s open conjecture, the exclusions in [why X must be massive](../gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain.md) ([§7.1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#71-the-chain-d--t)), and the failure of #14's model (§12; [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md)). The tiers, and what "incentive gradient" means, are on [Claim tiers and selection bias](claim-tiers.md#negative-results-the-most-reliable-tier-3-items).

## The negative results in the record

Each result is stated as the sources state it, followed by where the claim stands now. The registers are [conjectures](../status/conjectures.md) and [claims under review](../status/under-review.md).

- **Momentum-space clumping as dynamics.** The founding image was tested three ways in [the clumping problem](../history/main-problems.md). For particle dark matter the [mean-field instability criterion](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion.md) "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark" ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested) [D]), and by the classicality theorem collective momentum-space behavior and classical particles are mutually exclusive. On the [wave island](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/_index.md), the one branch where clumping became mandatory ([Scenario A](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md)) predicted that [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](../dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies.md) would be drained of dark matter, and it was "**falsified** by observed UFD density and longevity" (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf). With the coupling free the effect is "tunable across ~10 orders: optional, never required" ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). The net theorem: "velocity-space clumping is nowhere mandatory — forbidden (particles), fatal (committed wave), decorative (free wave)". The closing calc note's verdict, BBT being Broken-Born theory, the program's earlier name: "Nowhere in the viable parameter space of BBT is velocity-space clumping a mandatory phenomenon." ([`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md)). The handoff marks the 10²⁸–10³¹ shortfall as machine-verified; no `verify/` script checked it until 2026-10-28, and section D of [`verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py`](../../verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py) now reproduces it (flag 16 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), addendum); [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)'s tag is [D]. The tests cover the program's contact-range interaction, which acts only over microscopic distances: they say clumping is required nowhere, not that it cannot happen. The momentum-gravity note of 2026-10-28 (computed, not reviewed) tests a long-range variant, which the record does not contain, and finds it decorative ([the scope of the tests](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md#the-scope-of-the-tests); [Conjecture: a long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/long-range-gated-attraction.md)). "Results (i)–(iii) stand as results." ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested)). The founding image is P.R.'s open conjecture [O] ([Conjecture: velocity clumping, the founding image](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image.md)). Under review are Scenario A ([Under review: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a.md)) and the two prediction rows withdrawn after the clumping problem ([Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md); [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md)).
- **Conjugate collapse powering our accelerating expansion** (Appendix A item 9). Two versions failed: the [heuristic dictionary](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/duality-dictionary.md#superseded-the-earlier-heuristic-rule)'s w = −2 (w is dark energy's [equation of state](../cosmology/dark-energy/quintessence.md#dark-energy-and-the-equation-of-state), its pressure divided by its energy density), "which the data exclude", and dual dust, "which the corrected dictionary maps to dust" ([#11 §10](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#10-cosmology)). [`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen) gives them as "first version gave w = −2 (falsified); corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)". The corrected dictionary keeps a route, dual stiff ↦ Λ; whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential, are open (§10). The idea is P.R.'s open conjecture [O] ([Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion.md)).
- **Conjugate monopoles as the explanation of our monopole non-detection** (Appendix A item 5). Two grounds are given against it, in [`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen) "wrong duality; inflation suffices". First, the relevant duality is electric–magnetic: only the [shared-photon form](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md#shared-photon) of P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture, in which momentum-polarized analogs carry magnetic charge of our own photon, contests this ground, and that form requires [P4](../program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter.md), the postulate that gauge forces do not cross polarizations, restated, and conditions that hide the analogs, "none of them yet computed" ([#11 §11.4](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#114-the-magnetic-mirror-conjecture-o-pr-v23)). Second, the null result is "expected under several standard histories", inflation after a unification transition among them, so it lends the explanation no support ([#11 §11.1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#111-the-monopole-audit-revised-d); flag 59 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md)). Neither ground refutes it, and the explanation is P.R.'s open conjecture [O] (flag 62; [Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation.md)).
- **#14's superheavy-soliton model.** The one claim recorded as dead: the model, built with ordinary kinematics, failed on its recomputed abundance. [Round 3](review/round-3/_index.md) found the [Kibble estimate](../electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism.md) of [defect](../symmetry-breaking/topological-defects.md) production misnormalized: one defect per Hubble volume gives "p ≈ 104 [V]" against the 0.023 that #14 v3 had used, at minimum production the abundance closes at "M ≈ 2.9–4.4×10¹² GeV [V]", and the v3 benchmark "is overproduced by 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ [V]" ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model); [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary.md)). With ordinary kinematics the model "is either overproduced or the prior-art PeV-scale scenario, to which the framework adds nothing testable" (§12). [`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen) lists superheavy solitons as the current best dark-matter candidate; the root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md) overrides it for #14's model. Six pieces of the model are under review: [the Kibble normalization](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/kibble-normalization.md), [the ψ relic window](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/psi-relic-window.md), [Planck-suppressed adjoint mixing](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/dimension-five-operator.md), [the hidden SU(2) → U(1) proposal](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/hidden-sector.md), the coupling α_d ≈ 34 (below) and [the √N floor](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor.md) (root [`CLAUDE.md`](../../CLAUDE.md), 'Since the handoff').
- **Magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space**, the candidate #14's model was built to describe: conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, P.R.'s open conjecture [O] and a third dark-matter candidate ([#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model)). #14's model did not treat localization in momentum, so the candidate itself has not been tested: "#14 used ordinary kinematics throughout", and p-localization is open (§14 item 8). ## How a negative result is reported

- **In the abstract.** #11's plain-language abstract says that following the idea "put its most cherished consequence — dark matter clumping in velocity instead of space — to the program's own tests, which it failed for particle dark matter and, where it would be mandatory for wave dark matter, at the faintest dwarf galaxies; P.R. holds it open as his conjecture (§9)". It reports the failure of #14's model: "A model of it built with ordinary kinematics failed its own abundance arithmetic; the candidate itself has not been tested, and stands beside particles and a condensate as P.R.'s conjecture" ([#11 §1](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#1-two-abstracts)).
- **In the assessment.** [#11 §15](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#15-assessment) opens its assessment with the founding image's tests: "The founding image failed the program's own calculations for particle dark matter and, where it would have been mandatory for wave dark matter, the test of the faintest dwarf galaxies; P.R. holds it open as his conjecture" ([Assessment: a live research program](../program/assessment.md)).
- **In a section of its own.** [#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested), "Momentum clumping: the founding image, tested", states the tests' results and closes by recording the image as P.R.'s open conjecture. The [§12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model) record of #14's model, headed "#14's model of the third candidate: failed (2026-10-19)", is laid out as "What was claimed", "What was built and how it was tested", "Why it failed", "Verdict", "What survives" and "Checks", the last pointing to the script that gates its numbers.
- **In the predictions table.** [#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions) ends by listing what is withdrawn: #14's superheavy-soliton model (§12) and, withdrawn and under review, velocity-space granularity in halos and Fornax friction suppression (§9). See [Under review: velocity-space granularity in halos (old row 3)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity.md), [Under review: Fornax friction suppression (old row 4)](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction.md) and [History of the predictions table](../predictions/table-history.md).
- **Against the founding suppositions.** [#11's Appendix A](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates) gives each of P.R.'s eleven founding suppositions its fate. Items 3 and 9 are "**open**, P.R.'s conjecture [O]", with the evidence against them stated in the items, and item 5 is "**open in both halves**" ([Founding suppositions and their fates](../program/founding-suppositions.md)).
- **In the files.** The withdrawn manuscript #14 v3 was moved from `papers/` to `notes/` with a banner: "Kept as the audit trail; not for circulation." Its gated checks were replaced by [`verify/soliton_obituary.py`](../../verify/soliton_obituary.py), which checks the numbers of #14's failed model; the adjudication notes that this "does not loosen the gate: the gate verifies what current documents claim, and v3 no longer claims anything" ('Decisions'; [the soliton-obituary script](verification/soliton-obituary-script.md)).

## The rescue that was declined

After round 3, #14's soliton model could have been kept by adding a free [dilution factor](../cosmology/early-universe/relic-yield-and-dilution.md#entropy-dilution-t). Decision 2 rejected that route: it would restore the mass "only by adding a free dilution factor of at least 10³–10⁴ (ruling 2), probably far more (ruling 3)", and the mass "would then no longer be a prediction" ([`review/round3-adjudication.md`](../../review/round3-adjudication.md), 'Decisions'; [Round-3 decisions and lessons](review/round-3/decisions.md)). In the terms of [Lakatos's methodology](lakatosian-methodology.md), a free parameter added to save a claim is an ad hoc adjustment (the wiki's reading). Decision 2 covers #14's model, which used ordinary kinematics, so the declined rescue concerns that model; the candidate it modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, is P.R.'s open conjecture. Decision 3 followed: the queued "√N floor" calculation was dropped, because its premise was inverted and "Under decision 2 it has no candidate left to serve" (the candidate meant is #14's model, which Decision 2 covers; [Under review: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor.md)).

The clumping rows were withdrawn on the same terms: "Rows withdrawn permanently." ([`HANDOFF.md` §2](../../HANDOFF.md#2-scientific-state-one-screen)); "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands." ([`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`](../../notes/ufd-endpoint.md)). [#11 §13](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#13-predictions-and-kill-conditions) lists both as withdrawn and under review.

## What a failure leaves standing

The record of a failed test states its scope, so that the parts that survive are not lost with the claim. A failed model or version of an idea is not the failure of the idea.

- **After the clumping problem.** The salvage was "the **island discriminant** (knee shape: sharp cutoff ⇒ particle χ; (v\*/σ)⁴ ⇒ wave χ) and free relic abundance via misalignment on the wave island" ([#11 §9](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#9-momentum-clumping-the-founding-image-tested); [Row 3: the island discriminant](../predictions/island-discriminant.md)).
- **After #14's model.** [#11 §12](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#12-the-dark-sector-question-three-candidates-and-the-failure-of-one-model): "A weakly coupled hidden sector always has strongly coupled monopoles, by Dirac quantization [T]"; the [minimal adjoint-only hidden sector](../dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure.md) "is excluded on its own terms" [V], an exclusion also published (flag 68 in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md), pending); and the clumping loophole "remains untested; its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles (table above)" ([Open: the topological loophole](../dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole.md)). The [V] numbers are recomputed in the gate by [the soliton-obituary script](verification/soliton-obituary-script.md).
- **Founding supposition 5.** That monopoles live in the conjugate space is "**open in both halves**": the explanation is P.R.'s conjecture [O] under the shared-photon form, and the [ontology](../electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles.md), "alive and consequence-bearing", carries his conjecture that such monopoles are abundant in momentum space; joined with item 11 it is his [magnetic-mirror conjecture](../electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification.md) (§11.4) [O] ([#11 Appendix A item 5](../papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#appendix-a-audit-of-founding-suppositions-pr-with-fates)).

## Negative results about the method

The adjudications also record the author's misses. The round-3 adjudication scored its pre-registration as a miss on the verdict, "the first after 2 for 2", and wrote: "Calibration: my confidence in v3 was too high." It upheld the referee's finding that v3's statement "all numbers computed by executed code" overstated what the code showed: "The code checked the arithmetic of a wrong model" (ruling 13). In round 4 the adjudicator wrote of the unbounded potential: "I should have caught F1 myself: I checked the potential only at frozen H." The calibration record is kept on [pre-registration](review/pre-registration.md), and the other two misses became rules: [Verification checks arithmetic, not the model](verification/arithmetic-not-model.md) and [the reduced-model stability rule](verification/reduced-model-stability.md).
