*The program's rule on negative results is that a failed calculation or a contrary datum is reported in full, where the claim is discussed, and, since 2026-10-29, that a claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs. A failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea. [Checkpoint #11](/wiki/history/documents/checkpoint) ranks negative results as the most reliable of its new structural results. [The founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image) of the program, dark matter clumping in velocity rather than in space, is recorded dead, and so is [#14's model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary) of a later dark-matter candidate, superheavy solitons of a hidden gauge sector; Paul's concurrence in these deaths was asked on 2026-10-29. Under the rule, three of his founding ideas that had been recorded dead or retired without his concurrence are open conjectures again. The wiki gives every dead claim its own obituary page and lists them all in [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard).*

**Status:** Method, in force, revised on 2026-10-29 — untagged — root `CLAUDE.md` ('Method'), `HANDOFF.md` §7 and `wiki/CLAUDE.md`; applied in #11 §9 and §12 and in round-3 Decision 2 (`review/round3-adjudication.md`), and since 2026-10-29 in #11 v2.4's restorations (§10, §11.1, §12, Appendix A) and in the narrowing of Decision 2.

## The rule

The root `CLAUDE.md` states the rule in two bullets of its method, as revised on 2026-10-29. The first: "Report negative results prominently: a failed calculation or a contrary datum is stated in full, where the claim is discussed." The second: "A claim is dead only when theory and data kill it and Paul explicitly concurs. Paul, 2026-10-29: "Don't kill them off unless theory and data kill them off and you ask me explicitly that I concur. Do not hide the killing in a massive change."" It goes on:

- "Ask him for each death, separately, and record his answer with the date."
- "Make each death its own change, never part of a larger one."
- "Until he concurs, a claim against which theory or data weigh is recorded as open, or as his conjecture, with the evidence against it stated."
- "A failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea."
- "Deaths recorded before 2026-10-29 stand until he answers the concurrence question for them, except those he has contested."

The rule has two halves. The first is about visibility: a failure is reported in full where a reader will see it, not in a footnote, and `HANDOFF.md` §7 makes it part of Paul's [calibration request](/wiki/method/collaboration#the-calibration), "keep reporting negative results prominently. Honest obituaries are part of the deliverable." The second is about the threshold for a death: theory and data must kill the claim, and Paul must concur, asked about that death alone.

Until 2026-10-29 the bullet read "Report negative results prominently. Claims listed as dead in HANDOFF §2 stay dead.", and its second half was about permanence: once a claim was dead, later work did not bring it back under a new parameter or a new name. The direction came with Paul's statement, which the root `CLAUDE.md` records in full: "I think you prematurely killed off the most interesting parts of the comatter conjecture: that dark matter is conjugate (momentum) matter; and that magnetic monopoles exist in abundance in momentum space. Yes, I know electric/magnetic and space/momentum are different dualities. Also, conjugate gravitational collapse drives positional expansion acceleration. Don't kill them off unless theory and data kill them off and you ask me explicitly that I concur. Do not hide the killing in a massive change." ('Since the handoff', entry "2026-10-29, later the same day"; [timeline](/wiki/history/timeline#pauls-rule-on-deaths-2026-10-29-later)).

## Why negative results count as the most reliable

#11's methodological preface ranks them first among the program's new structural results (Tier 3): "Negative results (§9, §7.1, and the failure of #14's model in §12) are the most reliable Tier-3 items, since the program's incentive gradient ran against them." (#11 §2). The three named are [the death of momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping) (§9), the exclusions in [why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain) (§7.1), and the failure of #14's model (§12; [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)), which §2 called "the §12 obituary" until the amendment of 2026-10-29 (1c80d01). The tiers, and what "incentive gradient" means, are on [Claim tiers and selection bias](/wiki/method/claim-tiers#negative-results-the-most-reliable-tier-3-items).

## The deaths in the record

### Dead at the handoff

`HANDOFF.md` §2 lists them under "Dead (killed by own calculations — keep dead)". Two of the four are still recorded dead; the other two are open conjectures since #11 v2.4 ([below](#restored-on-2026-10-29)).

- **Momentum-space clumping as dynamics.** The founding image was tested three ways in [the clumping problem](/wiki/history/main-problems). For particle dark matter the [mean-field instability criterion](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/instability-criterion) "fails by 10²⁸–10³¹ at the scattering benchmark" (#11 §9 [D]). On the [wave island](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island), the one branch where clumping became mandatory (Scenario A) predicted that [ultra-faint dwarf galaxies](/wiki/dark-matter/halo-astrophysics/dwarf-galaxies) would be drained of dark matter, and it was "**falsified** by observed UFD density and longevity" (UFD: ultra-faint dwarf). Elsewhere the effect is "tunable across ~10 orders: optional, never required" (#11 §9). The closing calc note's verdict, BBT being Broken-Born theory, the program's earlier name: "Nowhere in the viable parameter space of BBT is velocity-space clumping a mandatory phenomenon." (`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`). See [Obituary: velocity clumping, the founding image](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image) and [Obituary: Scenario A and the ultra-faint-dwarf endpoint](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/wave-island/scenario-a). The handoff marks the 10²⁸–10³¹ shortfall as machine-verified; until 2026-10-28 no `verify/` script checked it (flag 16 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`), and section D of `verify/velocity_gated_gravity.py` now reproduces it, with #11 §9's tag [D] unchanged until Paul rules. The obituary covers the program's contact-range interaction: it says clumping is required nowhere, not that it cannot happen; the momentum-gravity note of 2026-10-28 (computed, not reviewed) tests a long-range variant, which the record does not contain, and finds it decorative ([what the obituary covers](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/founding-image#what-the-obituary-covers); [Conjecture: a long-range velocity-gated attraction among χ](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/long-range-gated-attraction)). The death stands as recorded; Paul's concurrence in it was asked on 2026-10-29.
- **"Conjugate collapse powers our expansion".** "first version gave w = −2 (falsified); corrected dictionary removed the mechanism (dual dust ↦ dust)" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). #11 recorded the supposition as "dead twice" (Appendix A item 9) until v2.3. It is now P.R.'s open conjecture ([below](#restored-on-2026-10-29)).
- **Conjugate monopoles as the explanation of our monopole non-detection.** "wrong duality; inflation suffices" (`HANDOFF.md` §2). #11 v2.3 restated the grounds (flag 59 in `review/wiki-build-flags.md`, accepted 2026-10-29): only the [shared-photon form](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification#shared-photon) of P.R.'s magnetic-mirror conjecture, in which momentum-polarized analogs carry magnetic charge of our own photon, contests the duality ground, and that form needs [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter), the postulate that gauge forces do not cross polarizations, restated (#11 §11.4); and the null result is expected under several standard histories, inflation after a unification transition among them, so it lends the program no support (#11 §11.1). v2.3 left the explanation dead, since the root `CLAUDE.md` then kept claims listed dead in `HANDOFF.md` §2 dead and this one is listed there (flag 62). It is now P.R.'s open conjecture ([below](#restored-on-2026-10-29)).
- **α_d ≈ 34 via [Dirac quantization](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/dirac-quantization) against our photon.** Killed by the [round-1](/wiki/method/review/rounds-one-and-two) referee: one "can't use our DSZ lattice and then deny coupling to our photon" (`HANDOFF.md` §2), DSZ being the Dirac–Schwinger–Zwanziger [charge lattice](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/symplectic-charge-lattice). See [Obituary: the coupling α_d ≈ 34](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/alpha-d-coupling). The death stands as recorded, with Paul's concurrence asked on 2026-10-29.

### Killed by review since the handoff

- **#14's superheavy-soliton model.** `HANDOFF.md` §2 still lists superheavy solitons as the current best dark-matter candidate; the root `CLAUDE.md` overrides it: "The superheavy soliton candidate in HANDOFF §2 is **dead** as a program-native proposal", narrowed on 2026-10-29: "this covers #14's model, which used ordinary kinematics; the candidate it modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, is P.R.'s open conjecture, #11 v2.4 §12". [Round 3](/wiki/method/review/round-3) found the [Kibble estimate](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopoles/kibble-mechanism) of [defect](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/topological-defects) production misnormalized: one defect per Hubble volume gives "p ≈ 104 [V]" against the 0.023 that #14 v3 had used, at minimum production the abundance closes at "M ≈ 2.9–4.4×10¹² GeV [V]", and the v3 benchmark "is overproduced by 2.1×10³–1.2×10⁴ [V]" (#11 §12; [Obituary: #14's superheavy-soliton model](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). Several of #14's subsidiary claims died with it; the graveyard lists them. These deaths stand as recorded, with Paul's concurrence asked on 2026-10-29.
- **The naive potential of the first toy model.** [Round 4](/wiki/method/review/round-4) found that the potential version 1 of the [toy-model note](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models) used to pin the polarization "is unbounded below in model I" once the field H is dynamical (ruling F1), so its vacuum (1, K_ref) is a saddle of the full model and the [Monte Carlo](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/monte-carlo) had simulated a [frozen-H reduction](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/frozen-h-reduction) (ruling F2) (`review/round4-adjudication.md`; [Obituary: the naive ρ-preserving potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/naive-potential)). Version 2 replaced it with a [bounded potential](/wiki/symmetry-breaking/toy-models/coset-model/bounded-potential). The death stands as recorded, with Paul's concurrence asked on 2026-10-29.

### Restored on 2026-10-29

Under the rule, #11 v2.4 returned three of P.R.'s founding ideas, "recorded dead or retired without his concurrence", to open conjectures [O] (#11 header). Each keeps the evidence against it:

- **Magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space**, a third dark-matter candidate again (#11 §12). Retiring the candidate was a decision Paul had delegated, round-3 Decision 2, which he narrowed to #14's model. The model failed: with ordinary kinematics it "is either overproduced or the prior-art PeV-scale scenario, to which the framework adds nothing testable" (§12). It never treated the localization in momentum that the candidate needs, since "#14 used ordinary kinematics throughout", and p-localization is open (§14 item 8). See [Conjecture: magnetic monopoles abundant in momentum space](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/momentum-space-monopoles).
- **Their explanation of the monopole null result** (Appendix A item 5), open by Paul's ruling on flag 62, option B: "Neither ground kills the explanation, and Paul does not concur in its death". Against it stand the two grounds: the relevant duality is electric–magnetic, a ground only the shared-photon form contests, and that form needs P4 restated and conditions that hide the analogs, "none of them yet computed" (§11.4); and the null result, "expected under several standard histories", lends it no support (§11.1; flag 59). See [Conjecture: conjugate monopoles explain the null result](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/monopole-explanation).
- **Conjugate collapse powering our accelerating expansion** (Appendix A item 9). Two versions failed, the heuristic dictionary's w = −2, "which the data exclude", and dual dust, "which the corrected dictionary maps to dust", and they "stay recorded dead until P.R. answers the concurrence question put to him on 2026-10-29". The corrected dictionary keeps a route, dual stiff ↦ Λ; whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential, are open (§10). See [Conjecture: conjugate collapse powers our expansion](/wiki/cosmology/scale-factor-duality/collapse-powered-expansion).

The same entry of the root `CLAUDE.md` records that dark matter is conjugate (momentum) matter, P.R.'s core conjecture, was alive throughout, and that its momentum-localized form returns with the first item (Appendix A item 4).

## How a death is reported

- **In the abstract.** #11's plain-language abstract reports the death of the founding image, that following the idea "killed its most cherished consequence — dark matter clumping in velocity instead of space", and the failure of #14's model: "A model of it built with ordinary kinematics failed its own abundance arithmetic; the candidate itself has not been tested, and stands beside particles and a condensate as P.R.'s conjecture" (#11 §1). Until v2.3 it said that the third candidate "was worked out, failed its own abundance arithmetic, and is retired".
- **In the assessment.** #11 §15 opens its assessment with the death of the founding image ([Assessment: a live research program](/wiki/program/assessment)).
- **In a section of its own.** #11 §9 is titled "Obituary: the death of momentum clumping". The §12 record of #14's model, headed since v2.4 "#14's model of the third candidate: failed (2026-10-19)", is laid out as "What was claimed", "What was built and how it was tested", "Why it failed", "Verdict", "What survives" and "Checks", the last pointing to the script that gates its numbers.
- **In the predictions table.** #11 §13 ends: "Withdrawn with cause: velocity-space granularity in halos; Fornax friction suppression (obituary §9); #14's superheavy-soliton model (§12)." Until v2.3 the last item read "superheavy-soliton dark matter (obituary §12)". See [Obituary: velocity-space granularity in halos](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/velocity-granularity), [Obituary: Fornax friction suppression](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/fornax-friction) and [History of the predictions table](/wiki/predictions/table-history).
- **Against the founding suppositions.** #11's Appendix A gives each of Paul's eleven founding suppositions its fate, including "dead as dynamics" (item 3). Item 9's "dead twice" became "**open**, P.R.'s conjecture [O] (v2.4)", and item 5, "**split**" until v2.3 with its explanation half dead, became "**open in both halves** (v2.4)" ([Founding suppositions and their fates](/wiki/program/founding-suppositions)).
- **In the files.** The withdrawn manuscript #14 v3 was moved from `papers/` to `notes/` with a banner: "Kept as the audit trail; not for circulation." Its gated checks were replaced by `verify/soliton_obituary.py`, which checks the numbers of the obituary; the adjudication notes that this "does not loosen the gate: the gate verifies what current documents claim, and v3 no longer claims anything" ('Decisions'; [the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script)).

## The rescue that was declined

After round 3, the soliton candidate could have been kept by adding a free [dilution factor](/wiki/cosmology/early-universe/relic-yield-and-dilution#entropy-dilution-t). Decision 2 rejected that route: it would restore the mass "only by adding a free dilution factor of at least 10³–10⁴ (ruling 2), probably far more (ruling 3)", and the mass "would then no longer be a prediction". It cited the clumping obituary as the precedent for keeping a dead claim dead (`review/round3-adjudication.md`, 'Decisions'; [Round-3 decisions and lessons](/wiki/method/review/round-3/decisions)). In the terms of [Lakatos's methodology](/wiki/method/lakatosian-methodology), a free parameter added to save a claim is an ad hoc adjustment (the wiki's reading). The decision was one Paul had delegated, and on 2026-10-29 he narrowed it to #14's model, "which used ordinary kinematics": the declined rescue concerns that model, and "The candidate #14 modeled, conjugate monopoles localized in momentum, was never tested and is P.R.'s open conjecture again (#11 v2.4 §12)" (Decision 2, "Narrowed by Paul (2026-10-29)"). Decision 3 followed: the queued "√N floor" calculation was dropped, because its premise was inverted and "it has no candidate left to serve" ([Obituary: the √N topological floor (chat-only)](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/sqrt-n-floor)).

The clumping rows were withdrawn on the same terms: "Rows withdrawn permanently." (`HANDOFF.md` §2); "Rows 3 and 4 of the predictions table are permanently withdrawn, on both islands." (`notes/ufd-endpoint.md`).

## What a death leaves standing

An obituary states its scope, so that the parts that survive are not lost with the claim.

- **After the clumping problem.** The salvage was "the **island discriminant** (knee shape: sharp cutoff ⇒ particle χ; (v\*/σ)⁴ ⇒ wave χ) and free relic abundance via misalignment on the wave island" (#11 §9; [Row 3: the island discriminant](/wiki/predictions/island-discriminant)).
- **After #14's model.** #11 §12: "A weakly coupled hidden sector always has strongly coupled monopoles, by Dirac quantization [T]"; the [minimal adjoint-only hidden sector](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/w-prime-overclosure) "is excluded on its own terms" [V]; and the clumping loophole "remains untested; its candidate is P.R.'s conjecture of conjugate monopoles (table above)", where until v2.3 it "has no candidate" ([Open: the topological loophole](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping/topological-loophole)). The [V] numbers are recomputed in the gate by [the soliton-obituary script](/wiki/method/verification/soliton-obituary-script).
- **A split verdict, until v2.4.** Founding supposition 5, that monopoles live in the conjugate space, was recorded from v2.0 to v2.3 as dead as an explanation and alive as [ontology](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/conjugate-monopoles); in v2.3, "as explanation, dead (wrong duality, which only §11.4's shared-photon form contests, and that form needs P4 restated; the null result is expected under several standard histories, so it lends no support — v2.3, flag 59); as ontology, alive and consequence-bearing", where until then the parenthesis read "(wrong duality; inflation suffices)". Since v2.4 the item is "**open in both halves**": the explanation is P.R.'s conjecture [O] under the shared-photon form, and the ontology, "alive and consequence-bearing", carries his conjecture that such monopoles are abundant in momentum space; joined with item 11 it is his [magnetic-mirror conjecture](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification) (§11.4) [O] (#11 Appendix A item 5).

## Negative results about the method

The adjudications also record the author's misses. It upheld the referee's finding that v3's statement "all numbers computed by executed code" overstated what the code showed: "The code checked the arithmetic of a wrong model" (ruling 13). The calibration record is kept on [pre-registration](/wiki/method/review/pre-registration), and the other two misses became rules: [Verification checks arithmetic, not the model](/wiki/method/verification/arithmetic-not-model) and [the reduced-model stability rule](/wiki/method/verification/reduced-model-stability).

## In the wiki

Since 2026-10-29 the wiki's rule is "Dead means killed by theory and data, with Paul's explicit concurrence" (`wiki/CLAUDE.md`, citing the root `CLAUDE.md`'s 'Method'). Dead claims are reported prominently, on their own pages; a claim whose death Paul has not concurred in, and has contested, is recorded as open or as his conjecture, with the evidence against it stated in full; a failed model or version of an idea is not the death of the idea; deaths recorded before 2026-10-29 stand until Paul answers the concurrence question for them; and "The wiki never declares a death on its own reading." Until then the rule was "Dead stays dead": claims killed in `HANDOFF.md` §2, #11 §9 and §12, and the adjudications "are reported as dead, prominently, on their own pages". Each page of a claim recorded dead has status `dead` and a title beginning "Obituary:", follows the order of #11's obituary (what was claimed, how it was tested, why it failed, what survives), and links the claim's home section, the ruling that killed it and [the graveyard](/wiki/status/graveyard). The pages of the restored ideas keep the record of their deaths as history, each with its date or version.

The rule covers claims that the record killed. One obituary rested on the wiki's own reading instead: the page on #12's magnetic identification recorded it as dead although no review or document had killed it. Flag 60 found that such a verdict adds a result, which the wiki does not do; Paul accepted the flag on 2026-10-29, and the page became a conjecture page ([Conjecture: the momentum sector as the magnetic mirror](/wiki/electric-magnetic-duality/magnetic-identification)).
