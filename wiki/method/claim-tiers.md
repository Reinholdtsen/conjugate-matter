*The claim tiers are checkpoint #11's ranking of its own statements by how much trust each deserves. Tier 1 is explicit algebra checked by machine; Tier 2 is established literature, correct at the level of well-worn results; Tier 3 is the program's new structural physics, which #11 rates as plausible, with an "honest prior per claim ~50–80% pending expert review". Alongside the tiers, #11 names one specific risk, the selection-versus-derivation bias: presenting a structure that was chosen from the known consistent options as though the program's premises had forced it. The tiers and the bias check are part of #11's methodological preface, §2.*

**Status:** Method — Tier 1 is the [V] tier; the tiers themselves carry no tag — #11 §2 ("Methodological preface: provenance and verification").

## Key facts

- **Tier 1.** "Tier 1 (explicit algebra): verified this session by an independent numerical/exact-integer battery — seven tests covering the compatibility theorem …" "All seven passed [V]." (#11 §2). The tests are on [The verification battery T1–T7](/wiki/method/verification/battery).
- **Tier 2.** "Tier 2 (literature): correct at the level of well-worn results; exact numbers, conventions, and attributions are flagged † pending source checks." (#11 §2). The source checks are the open [literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery); the marks are defined on [Provenance tags and citation marks](/wiki/method/provenance-tags).
- **Tier 3.** "Tier 3 (novel structural physics): plausibility-tier reasoning; honest prior per claim ~50–80% pending expert review; the selection-versus-derivation bias is acknowledged explicitly where it operates (§7.4)." (#11 §2).
- **Negative results are the most reliable Tier-3 items.** "Negative results (§9, §7.1, and the §12 obituary) are the most reliable Tier-3 items, since the program's incentive gradient ran against them." (#11 §2). The three are [the death of momentum clumping](/wiki/dark-matter/momentum-clumping), the exclusions in [why X must be massive](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/forcing-chain), and [the soliton obituary](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary). See [Negative results and dead claims](/wiki/method/negative-results).
- **What Tier 1 does not cover.** "What no machine can check is whether the algebra is attached to the right physics; that verdict belongs to expert readers and, ultimately, to data." (#11 §2, plain-language passage). Since v2.1: "a verification script confirms arithmetic, not the model it encodes" ([Verification checks arithmetic, not the model](/wiki/method/verification/arithmetic-not-model)).
- **The bias check at work, §7.4.** A literature search found that the partially-massless (PM) parameter point of bimetric gravity lies inside the program's palindromic branch, and #11 downgraded the novelty of its vacuum-energy identity "from "likely standalone result" to "requires detailed comparison with the PM and exchange-symmetric literature," queued as an open item. This is the selection-versus-derivation bias check of §2 operating as intended." See [Open: the partially-massless comparison](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/pm-comparison) and [the vacuum-energy identity](/wiki/gravity/palindromic-branch/vacuum-energy-identity).
- **Where the bias operates, §14 item 1.** "First-principles reduction of the frame-tier wedge sector from S_H (class selected, not derived) [O]." The v1 paper said the class "was selected by minimality + consistency, not derived line-by-line" (#9 §10 item 1). See [Open: deriving the wedge sector from S_H](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/wedge-reduction) and [the frame tier](/wiki/gravity/tuning-test/frame-tier).
