*These are the Comatter program's papers, published here in full: the four documents that are its current references. Checkpoint [#11](broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) states the whole program. The toy-model note and the phase-space-diameter note each work through one question, and [#13](conjugate-special-relativity.md) develops the kinematics of the momentum polarization. The rest of the wiki explains and indexes these documents and adds no results, so where a wiki page and a paper disagree, the paper is the record and the page is corrected. Each paper states its claims as of its latest version; where a later record or a flag qualifies a statement, the wiki page on that topic says so. Three of the papers open every section with a plain-language passage in italics, followed by the technical record with its provenance tags; the toy-model note, written for blind review, has neither.*

**Record pages only:** this section publishes documents as written and makes no claims of its own. Where each claim in them stands is registered in [claims that stand](../status/alive.md), [open problems and the queue](../status/open-problems.md), [conjectures](../status/conjectures.md), [superseded statements](../status/superseded.md), [claims under review](../status/under-review.md) and [the graveyard](../status/graveyard.md).

## Map

Checkpoint #11 comes first, because it states the whole program and the other three work out parts of it. The two notes follow in the order of the wiki sections they serve, and #13, a separate paper that #11 does not mention, comes last, as its section does in the wiki.

- [The Comatter Program: A Working Checkpoint](broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md) is checkpoint #11, the current reference for the full program: the postulates, the compatibility theorem and the modular field, the massive momentum graviton, conjugate matter and the velocity gate, momentum clumping, cosmology, charge lattices, the three dark-matter candidates, the predictions table, the open problems and the audit of the founding suppositions. Its versions and standing are on [The checkpoint](../history/documents/checkpoint.md).
- [Spontaneous Breaking of Born Reciprocity: Two Toy Models](reciprocity-toy-models.md) is the toy-model note: a coset sigma model and the self-dual Grosse–Wulkenhaar model, two explicit models in which reciprocity is spontaneously broken and the polarization is a Goldstone angle. It was graded B in [review round 4](../method/review/round-4.md). The wiki covers it under [Toy models of broken reciprocity](../symmetry-breaking/toy-models/_index.md).
- [The Size of the Universe in Position and in Momentum](phase-space-diameter.md) is the phase-space-diameter note: the size of the universe in position and in momentum, what reciprocity adds, a self-dual length, and a bound on the range of the momentum graviton X. It was graded B in [review round 5](../method/review/round-5.md). The wiki covers it under [The phase-space diameter relation](../cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md).
- [Special Relativity in the Comatter Framework](conjugate-special-relativity.md) is #13: the kinematics of the momentum polarization, with a second light cone, force boosts, force dilation, the mass and length shells and a maximum force. It tags most of its results [D], and no review or script has checked them. The wiki covers it under [Conjugate special relativity](../conjugate-relativity/_index.md).

## Boundaries

The papers are four of the program's documents. The others, among them the lab notebook, the calc notes, the later working notes, the popular articles, the review records and the `verify/` scripts, stay in the repository; [Documents](../history/documents/_index.md) describes all of them, with each one's versions and standing and [the order of precedence](../history/documents/_index.md#the-order-of-precedence) that settles conflicts between them. Every work the papers cite is collected on [References](../references.md), and suspected errors in their statements are numbered flags in [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../../review/wiki-build-flags.md).
