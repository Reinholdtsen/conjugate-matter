*This open problem is an inconsistency inside #13, the paper on the special relativity of the momentum polarization: two of its sections use different momentum-space light cones. Section 1 builds the dual cone in time and momentum, with time shared between the two polarizations and the maximum force c̃ as its slope; section 6 instead calls light's ordinary energy–momentum relation, E = c|p|, the dual null cone, which belongs to a different construction, the four-dimensional Born map that also exchanges time and energy, where the slope is c. Paul raised it on 2026-10-19, while the withdrawn soliton manuscript was being revised, with the instruction to fix the two cones and settle which construction governs before any paper claims more. It is on neither #11's list of open problems nor the current queue, and no document since has taken it up.*

**Status:** Open — no tag in the source (worklog item O3) — raised by Paul on 2026-10-19 in `review/v3-worklog.md`; not in #11 §14, the current queue in `CLAUDE.md` or the round-3 decisions; unresolved. Listed in [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The §1 cone.** The worklog: "§1 uses the 3+1 version (shared time): the cone lives in (t, p) with slope c̃." In #13 this is |dp/dt| ≤ c̃ (§1) and the dual interval ds̃² = c̃²dt² − dp² (§2) ([Two causal cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-causal-cones); [Force boosts and force composition](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts)).
- **The §6 cone.** #13 §6: "our massless dispersion E = c|p| *is* the dual null cone". The worklog: this "belongs to the 4D Born map (t ↔ E): a cone in (E, p) with slope c. Under the 4D map, anything on our light cone is automatically dual-null." That map is unbuilt ([Open: the four-dimensional Born map and dual dynamics](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/four-dimensional-born-map)).
- **What depends on it.** #13 §6 concludes from its identification that the bounds of the Fermi Large Area Telescope "excluding linear photon-dispersion violations [T] constrain both cones at once — every test of our light cone's rigidity has been, unknowingly, a test of theirs." The argument uses the §6 cone; #13 gives no version of it for the §1 cone of slope c̃. #11's own photon-dispersion requirement, "Linear exactly zero (ρ²-protected); quadratic suppressed" (§13 row 9), is stated without reference to #13 ([Row 9: no linear photon dispersion](/wiki/predictions/photon-dispersion)).
- **The dark sector's kinematics.** The worklog item O3 begins: "#14 describes the dark sector entirely in our kinematics: GeV masses, 10⁻³ c drift, an ordinary massless dark photon moving at c. #13 §4 labels conjugate quanta by an invariant length λ instead of a mass, and a λ = 0 quantum would sit on the dual null cone with |dp/dt| = c̃. The papers were never reconciled." The decision for #14's v3 was to "keep the dark sector in ordinary kinematics and say so explicitly"; #14 has since been withdrawn ([Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)). "Whether the dark photon is m = 0, λ = 0, or both is part of #13's open 'dual dynamics' item." The invariant length is on [The invariant-length shell and Planck closure](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell).
- **The instruction.** "Before any paper claims more: fix #13's two cones and settle which construction governs (raised by Paul, 2026-10-19)."
- **Where it stands.** The item appears only in the worklog. It is not among #11 §14's eleven open problems, not in the current queue in `CLAUDE.md` ('Since the handoff'), and not in the round-3 decisions; #13 itself has not been revised since its import.
