*Force dilation is the momentum-space counterpart of time dilation. In special relativity a fast-moving clock runs slow; in the dual kinematics of #13, a system under a large sustained force evolves slowly, by the same square-root formula with force in place of velocity and the maximum force c̃ in place of the speed of light. Its mirror is a contraction of the range of momenta a forced system spans, in place of the contraction of its length. #13 restricts the effect to systems of the conjugate (momentum) polarization, leaves open which physical clocks respond, and states that both effects are unmeasurably small below the forces of black-hole mergers; the result is alive but unreviewed.*

**Status:** Alive, unreviewed — [D — structural; which physical clocks respond is polarization-dependent, O] — no `verify/` script; the clock-response question is open and is missing from #13 §7's open list.

## Key facts

- **Dual proper time.** #13 §3: "The dual proper time of a trajectory p(t) is"

  ```text
  dτ̃ = dt √(1 − (1/c̃²)|dp/dt|²) = dt √(1 − F²/c̃²)
  ```

  It is the proper time of [special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/special-relativity), dτ = dt√(1 − v²/c²) [T], with dp/dt for dx/dt and c̃ for c.
- **What it dilates.** #13 §3: "so sustained force dilates the internal evolution of (conjugate-polarized) systems as sustained velocity dilates ours [D — structural; which physical clocks respond is polarization-dependent, O]." The two polarizations of matter are those of postulate [P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter).
- **Momentum-spread contraction** (untagged; not in §7's fixed list). #13 §3: "The mirror of length contraction: a forced system's extent *in momentum* — its momentum spread — contracts by γ̃ in the force direction." γ̃ is the dual Lorentz factor of [force boosts](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts).
- **Size of the effects.** #13 §3: "Both effects are unmeasurably small below merger-scale forces (§2)." The section's plain passage: "Below 10⁴³ newtons the effect is utterly negligible, which is why nobody has noticed." The estimate at neutron-star forces, γ̃ − 1 ~ 10⁻⁶⁵, is on [Force boosts and force composition](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts); no script reproduces either number.
- **The open clock question.** §7 lists force dilation among the fixed [D] results, but its open list does not carry §3's question of which clocks respond ([Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems)).
- **The popular version.** #12 ("A second speed of light") says "Clocks subjected to sustained force run slow, by the same square-root formula that governs fast clocks", dropping both the restriction to conjugate-polarized systems and the [O]. #13 governs; #12 is never a status source ([The popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
