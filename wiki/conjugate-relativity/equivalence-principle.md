*This is #13's claim that the equivalence principle of general relativity, which puts all freely falling frames on an equal footing, is the principle of relativity of the momentum polarization. Since free fall is the dual state of rest, the dual statement that physics is the same in all force-free frames is the equivalence principle, reached from kinematics rather than from gravitation, and #13 reads Einstein's two relativity principles as the two polarizations of one statement. The same paper lists what the two polarizations share, the time coordinate and the massless graviton, so that both fall identically, and what the breaking makes different: our light cone is held in place by massless gravity, the dual cone's dynamical part by the massive field X, so dual effects are short-ranged. The claim is alive but unreviewed; the popular article #12 states it as settled, which is not its status.*

**Status:** Alive, unreviewed — [D — structural] (#13 §5); the long-range/short-range asymmetry [D — checkpoint §7] (#13 §6) — no `verify/` script; the claim is not on #13 §7's fixed list, and #11 v2.2 does not mention it.

## Key facts

- **The claim.** #13 §5: "The handshake worth recording [D — structural]: *the dual principle of relativity — equivalence of all force-free frames — is the equivalence principle*, arrived at from kinematics rather than gravitation. In this framework Einstein's two relativity principles are the two polarizations of one statement." It rests on the identification of free fall as dual rest ([Dual inertia: free fall as rest](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/dual-inertia)); the equivalence principle itself is on [General relativity and the graviton](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity) [T].
- **The handoff's summary.** `HANDOFF.md` §2, alive and without a machine-check mark: "dual inertial frames = free fall ⇒ equivalence principle as the dual relativity principle".
- **What is shared** (untagged). #13 §6: "Shared: the time coordinate (in the 3+1 presentation used here) and the massless graviton, so both polarizations fall identically." In #11, conjugate matter χ "propagates on g (equivalence principle; Bullet-Cluster-safe)" (§8), both polarizations of matter couple universally to H ([P4](/wiki/program/postulates/p4-polarized-matter)), and the massless graviton is the reciprocity-even state ([Spectrum, parameters and reciprocity parity](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/spectrum-and-parity); [Row 6: the exactly massless graviton](/wiki/predictions/massless-graviton)). Time is shared only in the 3+1 presentation; the construction that exchanges time and energy is [open](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/four-dimensional-born-map).
- **What is broken.** #13 §6: "our null structure is policed by massless gravity, the dual structure's dynamical part by the massive X, so dual-geometric effects are Yukawa-cut in momentum [D — checkpoint §7]." The derived statement in #11 §7.5 is that X's couplings to Standard Model matter are "gravitational-strength and Yukawa-cut at 1/M_X" ([Safety by range](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/fifth-force-safety)); the reading of the dual Newtonian limit as "Yukawa in momentum = the gate" exists only in the chat (`HANDOFF.md` §4; [Two gravitational charges and the conjugate field equations (chat-only)](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/chat-only-results)). #11's wording governs.
- **A different question.** The chat-only "dual equivalence principle", "whether invariant length λ and dual charge lock together" (`HANDOFF.md` §4), is open and separate from this claim.
- **The popular version.** #12 ("A second speed of light"): "In this framework his two relativity principles are one principle, read in the two descriptions." #12 presents the framework as if confirmed and is never a status source ([The popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
