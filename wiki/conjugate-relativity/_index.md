*Conjugate special relativity is Einstein's special relativity rewritten for the momentum polarization, the program's mirror description in which momentum plays the role that position plays for ordinary matter. In quantum mechanics position and momentum are two descriptions of one state, related as a waveform and its spectrum; if the two are equal partners, the speed limit in space has a twin, a limit on how fast momentum can change, and a rate of change of momentum is a force. One working paper, #13, develops the consequences: a maximum force of about 10⁴⁴ newtons, force boosts, force dilation, an invariant length, and free fall as the dual state of rest. The material is alive but unreviewed: no review round and no script has checked it, and the checkpoint #11 does not mention it.*

## Map

Every page in this section rests on one document, #13, `papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md`, titled "Special Relativity in the Broken-Born Framework": seven sections, the first six opening with an italic plain-language passage, and four references, all marked † (cited from model memory and not yet checked against the source). `HANDOFF.md` §3 lists it as "final". Its file history is a single commit, the import of 2026-10-19, so the checkpoint's later revisions never reached it: it still tags the compatibility theorem [D], where #11 v2.1 re-tagged it [T][V]. No blind review round has covered it, it carries no attribution line, and its preamble inherits the checkpoint's Euclidean/Lorentzian caveat ([Open: Lorentzian dressing of the Euclidean skeleton](/wiki/phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing)). #11 v2.2 never mentions the maximum force or dual special relativity, so the section's status rests on `HANDOFF.md` §2, which lists both among the alive results without a machine-check mark, and on `CLAUDE.md`, which lists #13 as a current reference: alive, unreviewed, nothing machine-verified. #13's closing line points readers to the checkpoint for the geometric sector and to the popular article #12 for the framework at large ([the popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)); #12 presents this material as settled and is never a source for status.

Established physics comes first. [Special relativity: the template](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/special-relativity) collects the parts of Einstein's kinematics that #13 copies: boosts and rapidity, the interval and the light cone, simultaneity, time dilation and length contraction, the mass shell, the Galilean limit and the 3+1 split. [Maximum force and maximal acceleration in the literature](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force-conjectures) covers the published conjectures #13 ties itself to, Gibbons and Schiller's maximum force c⁴/4G and Caianiello's maximal acceleration, and the Planck units #13 uses.

The program's claims follow in #13's order. [Two causal cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-causal-cones) is the foundation: the block form H = diag(g, g⁻¹) gives position space and momentum space each a light cone, and the momentum cone bounds |dp/dt|, which is a force. [The maximum force c̃ = c⁴/G](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force) gives that bound a value by setting the Born length equal to the Planck length; the result is a factor of 4 above the literature's c⁴/4G, and the factor is open. [Force boosts and force composition](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts) builds the dual Lorentz group on (t, p): simultaneity that depends on momentum, forces that combine like relativistic velocities and saturate at c̃, and the Galilean limit that makes force look additive, with black-hole mergers as the one arena near the limit. [Force dilation](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-dilation) is the counterpart of time dilation, together with its mirror, a contraction of momentum spread. [The invariant-length shell and Planck closure](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell) is the counterpart of the mass shell: conjugate quanta carry an invariant length, a length times the maximum force at the Planck length is the Planck energy, and the gate scale of the dark-matter sector is read as a dual-relativistic invariant. [Dual inertia: free fall as rest](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/dual-inertia) identifies the dual-inertial frames with free fall and with uniformly accelerated (Rindler) frames, and records dual Newton's laws at conjecture tier. [The equivalence principle as dual relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/equivalence-principle) reads the equivalence principle as the dual principle of relativity, and states what the two polarizations share and what the breaking makes different.

Two open problems follow the claims they qualify. [Open: the two dual cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-cones-inconsistency) records that #13 uses one momentum-space cone in §1 and a different one in §6, and that §6's photon-dispersion argument depends on which governs; Paul raised it on 2026-10-19, and it is scheduled nowhere. [Open: the four-dimensional Born map and dual dynamics](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/four-dimensional-born-map) is the construction that would also exchange time and energy, which #13 calls the natural home of its kinematics and which must exist before the dual field equations can be written. The last page, [Momentum-space black holes (chat-only)](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/momentum-space-black-holes), records a conjecture from the original chat, partly repeated in #12: horizons read as maximum-force surfaces. No document derives it, and its status is conjecture.
