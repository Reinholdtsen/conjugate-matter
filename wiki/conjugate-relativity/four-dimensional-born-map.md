*The four-dimensional Born map is the version of Born's reciprocity transformation that exchanges time with energy as well as position with momentum; #13 names it as the fully covariant construction in which its kinematics belongs, and it has not been built. #13 itself works in a 3+1 presentation, in which space and momentum trade places while time is shared by both polarizations. The map is also a prerequisite for the dual dynamics, the field equations of the momentum polarization ("their Maxwell, their field theory"), which do not exist: only the dual kinematics has been written. Both items are open in #13 §7, and neither is on #11's list of open problems or on the current queue.*

**Status:** Open — [O] — #13 §6 and §7; nothing computed, no `verify/` script; not in #11 §14 or the current queue in `CLAUDE.md`. Listed in [Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems).

## Key facts

- **The item.** #13 §6: "Open [O]: the fully covariant construction with the four-dimensional Born map (t ↔ E), in which the dual "time" direction is our energy axis — the natural home of this paper's kinematics and a required step before the dual dynamics (their Maxwell, their field theory) can be written." §7's open list: "the 4D covariant construction; dual dynamics".
- **What #13 uses instead.** #13 §6's plain passage: "the tidiest version of this construction treats space and momentum as partners while leaving time alone; the fully four-dimensional version, in which energy and time also trade places, is deeper and not yet built." The 3+1 kinematics it qualifies are [Two causal cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-causal-cones), [force boosts](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts) and [force dilation](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-dilation); the transformation being extended is [Born reciprocity ρ: (x, p) → (p, −x)](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity), and energy–time conjugacy is on [Special relativity: the template](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/special-relativity) [T].
- **Already presupposed.** #13 §6's photon-dispersion argument treats E = c|p| as the dual null cone, which belongs to this map (`review/v3-worklog.md`, item O3); #13 §1 uses a different cone ([Open: the two dual cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-cones-inconsistency)).
- **Waiting on it.** #13 §5's dual second law is tagged "[conjecture-tier; the full dual dynamics awaits the formalization of §6]" ([Dual inertia: free fall as rest](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/dual-inertia)). The worklog adds a question for the dual dynamics: "Whether the dark photon is m = 0, λ = 0, or both is part of #13's open 'dual dynamics' item."
- **Related chat-only material.** `HANDOFF.md` §4 records a comparison of general relativity's field equations with the conjugate ones (the slaved metric, the dual Newtonian limit, massive X waves), which no artifact derives ([Two gravitational charges and the conjugate field equations (chat-only)](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/chat-only-results)).
- **Not scheduled.** Neither item is among #11 §14's eleven open problems or in the current queue in `CLAUDE.md` ('Since the handoff').
