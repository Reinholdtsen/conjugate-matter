*The maximum-force conjecture is a proposal within general relativity that no force can exceed c⁴/4G, a combination of the speed of light c and Newton's constant G; Gibbons named it the maximum tension principle, and Schiller took a principle of maximum force as the starting point for deriving general relativity. An older and separate proposal, Caianiello's, is a maximal acceleration obtained from a metric on phase space. Both are proposals rather than settled results, and #13 notes that the maximum-force conjecture is contested. They matter to the program because #13's maximum force, c̃ = c⁴/G, has the same form up to a factor of 4, and #13 offers a reason for such a limit.*

**Status:** Established as literature — [T], with both citations marked † (from model memory, not yet checked against the papers) — the maximum-force conjecture is contested in the general-relativity literature [T], which #13 notes without a source; the † references wait on [the literature battery](/wiki/method/literature-battery).

## Key facts

- **The maximum tension principle [1†].** #13 §1: "A maximum force c⁴/4G was conjectured within general relativity as the "maximum tension principle" [1†], with a companion maximum luminosity c⁵/4G approached by binary black-hole mergers". Reference [1]†: G. W. Gibbons, "The maximum tension principle in general relativity," Found. Phys. 32, 1891 (2002), hep-th/0210109; C. Schiller, "General relativity and cosmology derived from principle of maximum force," Int. J. Theor. Phys. 44, 1629 (2005).
- **Contested.** #13 §1 notes that the conjecture's status is contested in the general-relativity literature [T], without citing the dispute.
- **Maximal acceleration [2†].** #13 §1: "a maximal *acceleration* from a phase-space metric is older still [2†]". Reference [2]†: E. R. Caianiello, "Is there a maximal acceleration?" Lett. Nuovo Cim. 32, 65 (1981). #13 does not state how its maximum force relates to it.
- **Planck units [T].** The Planck length l_P = √(ℏG/c³) and the Planck energy E_Planck = √(ℏc⁵/G) are the length and energy built from ℏ, c and G [T]. #13 §1 writes the first as λ² = ℏG/c³, and #13 §4 obtains the second as l_P · c⁴/G ([The invariant-length shell and Planck closure](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell)).
- **What #13 adds.** #13 §1: "The framework's contribution is a reason: a maximum force is the statement that nothing outruns light in the momentum polarization." Its value, c⁴/G, differs from c⁴/4G by a factor of 4 that "is not fixed here [O]" ([The maximum force c̃ = c⁴/G](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force)); `HANDOFF.md` §2 says it "matches Gibbons–Schiller maximum-force conjecture up to the factor 4".
