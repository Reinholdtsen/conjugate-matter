*Dual inertia is #13's account of rest and uniform motion in the momentum polarization. In ordinary mechanics an inertial body keeps a constant velocity; in the dual kinematics force takes the place of velocity, so the dual-inertial motions are those of constant force: zero force, which in general relativity is free fall, and a constant nonzero force, which is a uniformly accelerated (Rindler) frame. In #13's words, "An astronaut coasting in orbit is, to the momentum polarization, at rest." The identification of dual-inertial frames with free fall is on #13's list of fixed results; the dual laws of motion built on it are marked conjecture-tier.*

**Status:** Alive, unreviewed — the dual-inertial = free-fall identification is in #13 §7's fixed [D] list (untagged in §5); the dual Newton's laws are "conjecture-tier", which is not one of the house tags — no `verify/` script.

## Key facts

- **The dual-inertial class.** #13 §5: "The dual-inertial class consists of trajectories with dp/dt constant: rest (dp/dt = 0, i.e., force-free motion — our free fall) and uniform dual motion (constant force — our Rindler frames)." §7 lists the "dual-inertial = free-fall identification" among the "Kinematic structure fixed [D]".
- **The reading of free fall.** In general relativity a freely falling body follows a geodesic and feels no force, and a Rindler frame is a uniformly accelerated frame [T] ([General relativity and the graviton](/wiki/gravity/spin-2-fields/general-relativity)). #13's "force-free motion — our free fall" uses this reading.
- **The astronaut.** #13 §5's plain passage: "An astronaut coasting in orbit is, to the momentum polarization, at rest." The same passage describes dual uniform motion as constant force, and dual inertia as resistance not to acceleration but to changes in force.
- **Dual Newton's laws (conjecture-tier).** #13 §5: "The dual first law: an undisturbed system maintains its state of force. The dual second law, structurally, makes λ_χ the measure of resistance to force-*change* [conjecture-tier; the full dual dynamics awaits the formalization of §6]." λ_χ is the invariant length of [the invariant-length shell](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell); the formalization is [Open: the four-dimensional Born map and dual dynamics](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/four-dimensional-born-map); the register of conjectures lists the dual second law ([Conjectures: chat-only and popsci-only claims](/wiki/status/conjectures)). The template is Newton's first law, on [Special relativity: the template](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/special-relativity).
- **What follows.** The dual principle of relativity, the equivalence of all force-free frames, is #13's reading of the equivalence principle ([The equivalence principle as dual relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/equivalence-principle)).
- **Basis of the status.** `HANDOFF.md` §2 lists "dual inertial frames = free fall ⇒ equivalence principle as the dual relativity principle" among the alive results, without a machine-check mark; #11 v2.2 does not mention it.
