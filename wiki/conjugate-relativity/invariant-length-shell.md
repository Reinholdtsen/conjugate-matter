*The invariant-length shell is the momentum polarization's counterpart of the mass shell: where every observer agrees on an ordinary particle's mass, #13 proposes that every observer agrees on a conjugate particle's size, an irreducible length λ_χ. Two consequences are drawn. A length times the maximum force is an energy, and at the Planck length it equals the Planck energy exactly, a consistency check #13 calls closure at the self-dual point. And a conjugate particle's size would set the velocity scale of the dark-matter velocity gate, which makes the knee in dark-matter self-interaction a dual-relativistic invariant. The shell is alive but unreviewed, and its description of dark-matter particles by a length is not reconciled with the checkpoint's ordinary treatment of them, which governs.*

**Status:** Alive, unreviewed — the shell [D — structural], citing Born [3†, 4†]; Planck closure [D]; the gate-scale reading [D — structural] — no `verify/` script checks the closure; the λ_χ description of conjugate quanta is unreconciled with #11 §8, which governs for the χ candidate.

## Key facts

- **The shell.** #13 §4: "Our invariant hypersurface is the mass shell, p_μp^μ = m²c². Its Born image is a position-space hyperboloid,"

  ```text
  x_μx^μ = λ_χ²
  ```

  "an **invariant-length shell**: conjugate quanta are characterized by an irreducible extent λ_χ as ours are by mass [D — structural; this recovers Born's 1938–49 program of a self-dual physics with both m and λ fundamental [3†, 4†]]." The mass shell is the template from [special relativity](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/special-relativity); the Born image is the image under [Born reciprocity ρ](/wiki/phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity).
- **Born's program [3†, 4†].** #13 §4's plain passage: "This was Born's original hope in 1938: a physics with a fundamental mass and a fundamental length on equal footing." The references are Born, Proc. R. Soc. Lond. A 165, 291 (1938), and Born, Rev. Mod. Phys. 21, 463 (1949), both from model memory.
- **Planck closure [D].** #13 §4: "The dual rest-energy relation is E = λ c̃ (length × maximum force). At λ = l_P: E = l_P · c⁴/G = √(ℏc⁵/G) — the Planck energy, exactly [D]. The dual E = mc² returns the right corner of physics." §7 lists "E(l_P) = E_Planck" among its numerical anchors; no `verify/` script checks it ([The maximum force c̃ = c⁴/G](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force); Planck units on [Maximum force and maximal acceleration in the literature](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force-conjectures)).
- **The gate scale [D — structural].** #13 §4: "The conjugate quantum's invariant extent fixes its intrinsic momentum-space width μ ~ ℏ/λ_χ — which is precisely the gate scale of the dark-matter sector, v\* = √2 μ/m_χ. The knee in the dark-matter self-interaction curve is, in this reading, a *dual-relativistic invariant of the dark sector* made visible in galactic dynamics [D — structural]." See [the velocity gate](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/velocity-gate), [the gate mechanism](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-mechanism) and [Row 1: the knee in σ_T(v)](/wiki/predictions/self-interaction-knee).
- **Where v\* comes from.** The formula v\* ≡ √2 μ/m_χ is the Gaussian-gate definition of the lab notebook #1 (§5.1), which is superseded as a reference. #11 v2.2 keeps a "robust knee at v\*" with the gate's shape open and does not restate the formula ([Open: the gate shape](/wiki/dark-matter/conjugate-matter/gate-shape)). The relation μ ~ ℏ/λ_χ appears only in #13.
- **Unreconciled kinematics.** #13 labels conjugate quanta by λ_χ while using m_χ in the same §4. #11 §8 treats χ in ordinary kinematics, with mass m_χ, as did the withdrawn soliton manuscript #14 ([Obituary: superheavy conjugate solitons](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/soliton-candidate/obituary)); the v3 worklog, comparing #13 with #14, records: "The papers were never reconciled" (item O3). #11 governs for [the χ-particle candidate](/wiki/dark-matter/candidates/chi-particles), and #13's reading stays an alternative.
- **λ_χ in dynamics.** #13 §5's dual second law makes λ_χ "the measure of resistance to force-*change*" at conjecture tier ([Dual inertia: free fall as rest](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/dual-inertia)). Whether "invariant length λ and dual charge lock together" is a chat-only open question (`HANDOFF.md` §4; [Two gravitational charges and the conjugate field equations (chat-only)](/wiki/gravity/momentum-graviton/chat-only-results)).
