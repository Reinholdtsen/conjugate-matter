*The maximum force is the invariant speed of the momentum-space light cone: since a rate of change of momentum is a force, the dual cone's speed limit is a largest possible force, c̃ = ℏc/λ², where λ is a length that the paper #13 calls the Born length. Setting the Born length equal to the Planck length gives c̃ = c⁴/G, about 1.2 × 10⁴⁴ newtons, which #13 describes as "the scale black holes reach when they collide". The general-relativity literature already contains a conjectured maximum force, c⁴/4G; #13's value is larger by a factor of 4, and the factor is open. The result is alive but unreviewed, and no script checks the number.*

**Status:** Alive, unreviewed — the boxed value carries no tag (#13 §7 lists it under "Numerical anchors"); the factor of 4 is [O] — listed alive in `HANDOFF.md` §2 without a machine-check mark; absent from #11 v2.2; no `verify/` script reproduces 1.2 × 10⁴⁴ N ([the iron rule](/wiki/method/verification/iron-rule)).

## Key facts

- **The Born length.** #13 §1: "λ is the Born length and ε = ℏ/λ its conjugate momentum scale", so that the dual cone of [Two causal cones](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/two-causal-cones) has speed c̃ = ε/(λ/c) = ℏc/λ². The term "Born length" occurs in no other document of the program.
- **The Planck identification** (untagged). #13 §1: "The only length the gravitational sector supplies is the Planck length, λ² = ℏG/c³, giving"

  ```text
  c̃ = c⁴/G ≈ 1.2 × 10⁴⁴ N
  ```

  (boxed in #13 §1; the Abstract gives the same value "at the Planck value of the Born length").
- **The reason offered.** #13 §1: "The framework's contribution is a reason: a maximum force is the statement that nothing outruns light in the momentum polarization."
- **The literature.** #13 §1: "A maximum force c⁴/4G was conjectured within general relativity as the "maximum tension principle" [1†], with a companion maximum luminosity c⁵/4G approached by binary black-hole mergers; a maximal *acceleration* from a phase-space metric is older still [2†]" ([Maximum force and maximal acceleration in the literature](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/maximum-force-conjectures)).
- **The factor of 4 [O].** #13 §1: "The factor of 4 is not fixed here [O]"; §7 repeats "the factor 4" among the open items ([Open problems and the queue](/wiki/status/open-problems)). `HANDOFF.md` §2 says the value "matches Gibbons–Schiller maximum-force conjecture up to the factor 4". The popular articles say "of exactly that form" (#10) and "of exactly this form" (#12); #13's [O] governs, and the popular articles are never a status source ([The popular articles](/wiki/history/documents/popular-articles)).
- **Where forces reach it.** #13 §2 names comparable-mass black-hole mergers as the one natural process that "operates at F ~ c̃ by construction" ([Force boosts and force composition](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/force-boosts)). A chat-only conjecture reads a black hole's horizon as a maximum-force surface ([Momentum-space black holes (chat-only)](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/momentum-space-black-holes)).
- **Planck closure.** #13 §4's dual rest-energy relation, E = λc̃ (length × maximum force), gives the Planck energy at λ = l_P ([The invariant-length shell and Planck closure](/wiki/conjugate-relativity/invariant-length-shell)).
