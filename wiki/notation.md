*This page lists the mathematical symbols of the Comatter program as its documents and this wiki use them: what each symbol means, which document defines it and which wiki page explains it, grouped by subject in the order of the wiki's sections. The program's central objects are written the same way in all the current documents: the reciprocity map ρ, the symplectic form ω, the neutral metric η, the generalized metric H, the modular field Z = X + iY and the dark-energy field τ. Many of their letters also serve other purposes. H is also the Hubble rate, ρ an energy density and X a parameter of the dark-matter calculations; g is a spacetime metric, a coupling constant and a magnetic charge; μ is a width in momentum in the dark-matter sector and a coupling constant in the toy models. The Collisions section near the end lists every letter with more than one meaning or convention, together with the suspected source errors that bear on them.*

**Status:** Record — untagged — a register compiled from the finished wiki pages and the documents they cite. It adds no results and no numbers of its own; each entry names the document that defines the symbol, and where an entry and its document differ, the document governs.

## How to read this page

- **Entries.** Each entry gives the symbol in bold, its meaning, the document that defines it and the wiki page that explains it, linked to the section where that page introduces the symbol. A letter with several meanings appears under each subject that uses it and again under [Collisions](#collisions).
- **Documents.** References such as "[#11 §5](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem)" use the artifact numbers of [`HANDOFF.md` §3](../HANDOFF.md#3-artifact-inventory-creation-order): [#1](../notes/broken-born-theory.md) is the lab notebook, [#2](../notes/stream-stability.md)–[#4](../notes/ufd-endpoint.md) the clumping calc notes (#2, [#3](../notes/wave-island.md) and #4), [#5](../notes/geometric-core.md)–[#8](../notes/beta-coefficients.md) the field-equation calc notes, [#9](../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md) the superseded program v1, #11 the checkpoint, [#13](papers/conjugate-special-relativity.md) conjugate special relativity and [#14](../notes/knot-dark-matter-standalone.md) the withdrawn soliton manuscript. "The toy note" is [`wiki/papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`](papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md), "the worklog" its working record [`notes/toy-lagrangian.md`](../notes/toy-lagrangian.md), "the v3 worklog" [`review/v3-worklog.md`](../review/v3-worklog.md), and "the diameter note" [`wiki/papers/phase-space-diameter.md`](papers/phase-space-diameter.md), the phase-space-diameter note. [Documents](history/documents/_index.md) maps every number to its file.
- **Flags.** "Flag n" is item n of [`review/wiki-build-flags.md`](../review/wiki-build-flags.md), a suspected error in a source document found while this wiki was built or since; [pending rulings](status/open-problems.md#pending-rulings) lists them and says which are resolved. Entries quote the source as written and name the flag; none is corrected here.
- **What is left out.** Symbols that live inside a single derivation, such as integration variables, summation indices and the matrices of one proof, are left to their pages. Words are on the [Glossary](glossary.md), cited works on [References](references.md).

## Conventions the wiki follows

- **Writing.** The wiki writes mathematics in Unicode, as the checkpoint does: H = diag(g, g⁻¹), Z → −Z⁻¹, βₙ = β₄₋ₙ, w ≥ −1. Display equations go in `text` blocks. The lab notebook, the calc notes and #13 write their formulas in LaTeX, and the wiki transcribes them; where a note writes v_\* the wiki writes v\*.
- **The three products of the structures.** With ω and η in standard form, [#11 §5](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem) defines three products of ω, η and H that obey a split-quaternion algebra, and the lab notebook ([#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core)) uses the same definitions ([the operators I, J, K](phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md#the-operators-i-j-k-and-the-split-quaternion-algebra)):

  ```text
  I = ω⁻¹H,   J = η⁻¹H,   K = η⁻¹ω        I² = −1,   J² = K² = +1,   IJ = K
  ```

- **The real structure.** #11's postulate P2′ calls J the real structure and writes η = HJ; the toy note and [#11 §14 item 11](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#14-open-problems) call K the real structure and write η = ωK. Pages on #11's postulates use J, pages on the toy models use K, and the wiki does not choose between them ([Collisions](#collisions)).
- **Reused letters.** Where a page uses a letter in one of its secondary meanings (ρ as a density, H as the Hubble rate, μ as a coupling), it names the meaning in words at first use, and several pages carry a note on their own collisions.
- **GW** on this wiki means Grosse–Wulkenhaar, the second toy model. Gravitational waves are always written out.
- **†** outside a formula is the citation mark: a reference cited from model memory. Inside the toy models' formulas (φ†φ, ψψ†) it is the Hermitian adjoint.

## Phase space

### Coordinates, reciprocity and the symplectic form

- **d** — the number of position coordinates, so that phase space has 2d dimensions: d = 4 physically, d = 1 in the toy models. — [#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core); [toy note §1](papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#1-setting-and-prior-work) — [Phase space and its three structures](phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md#phase-space-and-its-three-structures)
- **𝒫 ≅ ℝ^{2d}; (xᵃ, pₐ)** — flat phase space, and its coordinates: d positions xᵃ and their momenta pₐ. — [#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core) — [Phase space and its three structures](phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md#phase-space-and-its-three-structures)
- **ρ** — Born reciprocity, the Born rotation (x, p) → (p, −x): a quarter-turn of phase space. As a matrix it is [[0, 1], [−1, 0]], symplectic, with ρ² = −1 and ρ⁴ = 1; on the modular field it acts as Z → −Z⁻¹. — [#11 §3](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question), [§6](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field); [toy note §1](papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#1-setting-and-prior-work) — [The map](phase-space/reciprocity/born-reciprocity.md#the-map)
- **R** — the matrix [[0, 1], [−1, 0]] by which ρ acts on tangent vectors in #1. "R preserves ω (it is symplectic), preserves H (it is orthogonal), and flips the sign of η". — [#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core) — [How ρ acts on the triple](phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md#how-ρ-acts-on-the-triple-d)
- **ρ²; ℤ₄** — the phase-space parity (x, p) → (−x, −p), which preserves ω, η and H, and the group of four elements that ρ generates. The lab notebook's breaking pattern ℤ₄ → ℤ₂, with the vacuum keeping {1, ρ²}, is superseded by the Goldstone picture. — [#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core) — [Superseded: the discrete breaking pattern](symmetry-breaking/order-parameter.md#superseded-the-discrete-breaking-pattern)
- **ρ_λ; A** — reciprocity in ordinary units with Born length λ, x′ = (λ²/ℏ)p and p′ = −(ℏ/λ²)x, symplectic for every λ; and the aspect ratio of a phase-space rectangle of extents (L, P) in units of λ, A = (L/λ)/(P/(ℏ/λ)) = ℏL/(λ²P). ρ_λ keeps the area L·P and sends A to 1/A; A = 1 exactly when λ² = ℏL/P, and maps with two Born lengths differ by a squeeze, ρ_λ₁ρ_λ₂⁻¹ = diag((λ₁/λ₂)², (λ₂/λ₁)²). In Planck units today's radiation rectangle has A₀ = 5.2×10⁹². — [diameter note §5](papers/phase-space-diameter.md#5-what-reciprocity-adds-invariant-area-inverted-shape), [§6](papers/phase-space-diameter.md#6-where-the-rectangle-is-square) — [The phase-space diameter relation](cosmology/scale-factor-duality/phase-space-diameter.md); [The self-dual cosmic box](cosmology/scale-factor-duality/self-dual-cosmic-box.md)
- **ψ(x), ψ̃(p); F** — a state's position and momentum wavefunctions, and the Fourier transform that relates them. F implements ρ on wavefunctions: it conjugates x̂ into p̂ and p̂ into −x̂, and applied twice it is parity. — [T]; [toy note §5](papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#5-matter-and-locality) — [Definition](phase-space/reciprocity/fourier-transform.md#definition); [Twice is parity](phase-space/reciprocity/fourier-transform.md#twice-is-parity)
- **x̂, p̂; [x, p] = iℏ** — the position and momentum operators, and the canonical commutator, which [#11 §3](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question) reads as ruling out joint locality in x and p. — [#11 §3](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#3-the-question) — [What the commutator says](phase-space/reciprocity/canonical-commutator.md#what-the-commutator-says)
- **ℏ** — Planck's constant, set to 1 in the toy models' checks. [#11 §4](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates) reads its constancy as the rigidity of ω: "symplectic rigidity ≡ constancy of ℏ". — [#11 §4](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates) — [ℏ as the one rigid structure](phase-space/reciprocity/canonical-commutator.md#second-use-ℏ-as-the-one-rigid-structure)
- **ω; ω(u, v)** — the symplectic form, [[0, 1], [−1, 0]] in d × d blocks, the one structure held rigid under P2′. On vectors u = (u_x, u_p) and v it is ω(u, v) = u_x · v_p − u_p · v_x, a signed area in each position–momentum plane. — [#11 §4](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates); [#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core); [toy note §1](papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#1-setting-and-prior-work) — [Definition](phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form.md#definition)
- **Sp(2d, ℝ); S** — the group of linear maps that preserve ω; Sp(2, ℝ) in the toy models, where it contains ρ. [`verify/toy_coset.py`](../verify/toy_coset.py) uses the generic element S = [[s₁, s₂], [s₃, (1 + s₂s₃)/s₁]]. — [#11 §1](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#1-two-abstracts), [§6](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field); [toy note §2](papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#2-model-i-a-coset-sigma-model-of-h-k) — [The symplectic group](phase-space/reciprocity/symplectic-form.md#the-symplectic-group)
- **W, W_ψ** — the Wigner function of a state ψ, a function on phase space whose two marginals are |ψ(x)|² and |ψ̃(p)|². — [T]; [toy note §5](papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md#5-matter-and-locality) — [Definition](phase-space/reciprocity/wigner-function.md#definition)

### Born geometry

- **η** — the neutral (split-signature) metric, [[0, 1], [1, 0]] in the standard frame: a symmetric pairing with as many positive as negative directions. Rigid under the original P2; under P2′ it is emergent and serves as the order parameter. — [#1 §1](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#1-postulates)–[§2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core); [#11 §4](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates) — [Phase space and its three structures](phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md#phase-space-and-its-three-structures)
- **H** — the positive generalized (phase-space) metric of postulate P1. — [#11 §4](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates) — [P1: the phase-space arena](program/postulates/p1-arena.md)
- **I, J, K** — the three products I = ω⁻¹H, J = η⁻¹H and K = η⁻¹ω. I is a complex structure (I² = −1), itself a quarter-turn of phase space, and the lab notebook calls ρ's group "the ℤ₄ generated by the complex structure I" ([P3](program/postulates/p3-reciprocity.md)); J and K are real structures (J² = K² = +1). In #1, J is the order parameter: "Reciprocity is broken iff ⟨J⟩ ≠ 0 in the vacuum". — [#11 §4](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#4-postulates)–[§5](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem); [#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core) — [The operators I, J, K](phase-space/born-geometry/born-structures.md#the-operators-i-j-k-and-the-split-quaternion-algebra)
- **g; g̃** — the spacetime metric, g = Im Z under P2′, and the momentum-space metric, slaved to g̃ = g⁻¹ when ω and η are both rigid. — [#11 §5](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem)–[§6](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#6-the-modular-field); [#5 §A](../notes/geometric-core.md#a-the-compatibility-theorem-d--rigorous) — [Corollary 3: the slaved momentum metric](phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries.md#corollary-3-the-slaved-momentum-metric)
- **diag(g, g⁻¹); Sym⁺(d)** — the only positive H compatible with both rigid ω and rigid η, and the set of real symmetric positive-definite d × d matrices g. — [#11 §5](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem) — [Statement](phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md#statement)
- **A, B, C; E1, E2** — the blocks of H = [[A, C], [Cᵀ, B]] in the proof of the compatibility theorem, and the round-3 referee's two residual matrices, E1 = (η⁻¹H)² − 1 and E2 = (ω⁻¹H)² + 1. — [#5 §A](../notes/geometric-core.md#a-the-compatibility-theorem-d--rigorous); [#11 §5](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem); [`review/round3/referee/audit-notes.md`](../review/round3/referee/audit-notes.md) — [The proof](phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md#the-proof); [Round 3's independent re-derivation](phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-theorem.md#round-3s-independent-re-derivation)
- **det H = 1; B-field** — the first corollary of the theorem, unimodularity, and the antisymmetric Kalb–Ramond field of string theory and double field theory, which the second corollary excludes: "no Kalb–Ramond analog". — [#5 §A](../notes/geometric-core.md#a-the-compatibility-theorem-d--rigorous); [#11 §5](papers/broken-born-checkpoint-v2.md#5-the-kinematic-core-and-the-compatibility-theorem) — [Corollary 1](phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries.md#corollary-1-det-h--1); [Corollary 2](phase-space/born-geometry/compatibility-corollaries.md#corollary-2-no-kalbramond-analog); [The Kalb–Ramond B-field](phase-space/born-geometry/kalb-ramond-field.md)
- **R_B[H]** — the curvature of the Born connection, in the lab notebook's geometric action. — [#1 §2](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#2-kinematic-core)–[§3](../notes/broken-born-theory.md#3-action) — [The lab notebook's geometric action](phase-space/born-geometry/born-connection.md#the-lab-notebooks-geometric-action-v01-superseded)
- **U(1, d−1)** — the stabilizer when g has Lorentzian signature, written U(1,3) in #9. — [#6 §3](../notes/x-classification.md#3-the-mass-mechanism-and-the-endpoint-d--structural); [#9 §10](../notes/broken-born-program-v1.md#10-open-problems) — [What changes in Lorentzian signature](phase-space/born-geometry/lorentzian-dressing.md#what-changes-in-lorentzian-signature)

## Collisions

Each entry below is a letter or mark with more than one meaning, or one quantity written in more than one convention. The first group are conventions that differ between documents for the same quantity, the second the letters with several meanings, the third the marks and labels. Where a suspected source error bears on an entry, the flag is named, and an arrow (→) points to the pages that discuss the point. Words with several meanings (the gate, soliton, condensate, island, verdict C) are on the [Glossary](glossary.md).
