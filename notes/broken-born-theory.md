## 1. Postulates

**P1 (Arena).** Physics is formulated on phase space $\mathcal P$ carrying a *Born geometry*: a symplectic form $\omega$, a neutral (split-signature) metric $\eta$, and a positive generalized metric $H$, mutually compatible in the sense of §2. **[P]**

**P2 (Rigidity of $\hbar$).** $\omega$ and $\eta$ are non-dynamical; only $H$ carries degrees of freedom. Physically: the symplectic structure *is* the constancy of $\hbar$ — phase-space cells do not stretch. **[P]** This single postulate does triple duty below: it kills the would-be second graviton (no Boulware–Deser ghost, §6), it enforces the cosmological constraint $a\tilde a = \text{const}$ (§7), and it supplies the explicit breaking that gives the dark-energy field its potential (§5).

**P3 (Reciprocity).** The action is invariant under the Born rotation $\rho: (x,p) \to (p,-x)$, the $\mathbb Z_4$ generated by the complex structure $I$ of §2. **[P]**

**P4 (Polarized matter, universal gravity).** Matter comes in two representations: $x$-polarized fields (the Standard Model) and $p$-polarized fields ($\chi$, conjugate matter). Both couple universally to $H$ — the equivalence principle holds across polarizations. Gauge interactions do not connect the two representations. **[P]**

**P5 (Spontaneous breaking).** The vacuum selects a Lagrangian polarization of $\mathcal P$; ours is $x$-tilted. The choice is spontaneous: the mirror vacuum is an equally good solution. **[P]**

## 2. Kinematic core

Work first on flat $\mathcal P \cong \mathbb R^{2d}$ with coordinates $(x^a, p_a)$, $d=4$. In the $(x,p)$ block basis:

$$
\omega = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad
\eta = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \qquad
H = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

(units chosen so a reference length $\lambda$ and momentum $\varepsilon = \hbar/\lambda$ are scaled out). Define the endomorphisms

$$
I \equiv \omega^{-1} H, \qquad J \equiv \eta^{-1} H, \qquad K \equiv \eta^{-1}\omega .
$$

Direct matrix computation gives $I^2 = -\mathbb 1$, $J^2 = +\mathbb 1$, $K^2 = +\mathbb 1$, and $IJ = K$: a **split-quaternion (para-quaternionic) algebra**. A compatible triple $(\omega, \eta, H)$ realizing this algebra is precisely a Born geometry in the sense of Freidel, Leigh & Minic. **[T]**

**The Born rotation.** $\rho$ acts on tangent vectors by $R = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ -1 & 0\end{pmatrix}$. One checks directly: $R$ preserves $\omega$ (it is symplectic), preserves $H$ (it is orthogonal), and **flips the sign of $\eta$**, hence flips $J$ and $K$ while preserving $I$. **[D]**

Consequently:

- The **order parameter** of the theory is $J = \eta^{-1}H$ — equivalently, the choice of which Lagrangian splitting $H$ aligns with. Reciprocity is broken iff $\langle J \rangle \neq 0$ in the vacuum.
- $\rho^2: (x,p) \to (-x,-p)$ preserves *all three* structures. The breaking pattern is $\mathbb Z_4 \to \mathbb Z_2$: **[D]**

$$
\mathbb Z_4 \;\xrightarrow{\ \langle J\rangle\ }\; \mathbb Z_2 = \{\mathbb 1,\ \rho^2\}.
$$

**Connection and curvature.** A theorem of Freidel, Rudolph & Svoboda (2018) establishes the existence and uniqueness of a connection compatible with the full triple $(\omega,\eta,H)$ — the *Born connection*. **[T]** Its curvature $R_B[H]$ is therefore well-defined, and a diffeomorphism-style invariant action for $H$ can be written. Field dependence is restricted to a Lagrangian leaf by a section condition, as in double field theory; consistency of the section condition on general backgrounds is open there and open here. **[O]**

**The vacuum manifold and the Maslov circle.** The space of Lagrangian polarizations of $\mathbb R^{2d}$ is the Lagrangian Grassmannian $\Lambda(d) \cong U(d)/O(d)$, compact, of dimension $d(d+1)/2 = 10$ for $d=4$. **[T]** Its fundamental group is $\pi_1(\Lambda(d)) = \mathbb Z$, generated by the **Maslov circle** via the map $\det{}^2: U(d)/O(d) \to U(1)$. **[T]** The angular coordinate on this circle,

$$
\tau \;\equiv\; f \cdot \arg\det{}^2(\cdot),
$$

is a compact, $2\pi f$-periodic modulus of the vacuum. This field is promoted below to the dark-energy field. Periodicity is topology **[D]**; that $\tau$ is the *lightest* modulus, with the other nine directions heavy, is assumed **[P]**.

## 3. Action

$$
S \;=\; S_H \;+\; S_\tau \;+\; S_x[\text{SM};\, g] \;+\; S_p[\chi;\, g, \tilde g] \;+\; S_{\rm mix},
$$

with:

**Geometric sector.** $S_H = \tfrac{1}{2}M_B^{2}\!\int_\Sigma \sqrt{|H|}\, R_B[H]$ over the section $\Sigma$, with $\omega,\eta$ frozen (P2). On the $x$-tilted vacuum, $H$ decomposes into a spacetime metric $g_{\mu\nu}(x)$ and a momentum-space metric $\tilde g^{\mu\nu}$; the $g$-block reproduces the Einstein–Hilbert action with $M_P$ fixed by $M_B$ and the section volume. **[D, schematic]**

**Polarization sector.** $S_\tau = \int \sqrt{-g}\,\big[\tfrac12 (\partial\tau)^2 - V(\tau)\big]$ with

$$
V(\tau) = V_0\left[1 + \cos(\tau/f)\right].
$$

The potential's *periodicity* is forced by the Maslov topology **[D]**; its *existence* comes from the explicit breaking of Grassmannian rotations by the rigid $\omega$ (P2) — the rigidity is the "instanton" that tilts the circle. Its *magnitude* $V_0$ is a free parameter here. **[P/O]**

**Ordinary sector.** $S_x$ is the Standard Model minimally coupled to $g$. Nothing is modified. **[P4]**

**Conjugate sector.** $\chi$ (take a complex scalar or Dirac fermion; nothing below depends on spin) propagates on $g$ — it gravitates exactly like ordinary matter — but its self-interaction is routed through the $\tilde g$ block. The leading nonrelativistic effective amplitude consistent with the residual $\mathbb Z_2$ and with $p$-polarization is

$$
\boxed{\ \mathcal M(k, q) \;=\; \frac{4\pi\,\tilde\alpha\, m_\chi}{q^2 + m_\phi^2}\; e^{-k^2/\mu^2}\ }
$$

where $q$ is the momentum **transfer** and $k = m_\chi v_{\rm rel}/2$ the incoming **relative** momentum. The Gaussian *gate* $e^{-k^2/\mu^2}$ is the new object: it is the imprint of momentum-space locality, and it depends on $k$, not $q$. An $x$-local Yukawa is the limit $\mu \to \infty$. Deriving the precise gate shape from $S_H + S_{\rm mix}$ is open **[O]**; that the gate exists, with support set by a scale $\mu$ of the $\tilde g$ sector, is the EFT-level content of "conjugate matter interacts in momentum space." **[P]**

Consistent. **[T]**

**Ghost accounting.** There is no second independent metric: $\tilde g$'s cosmological mode is eliminated by the P2 constraint (§7), so no Boulware–Deser sector arises in minisuperspace. **[D]** The statement for full inhomogeneous $H$ is open. **[O]**

## 5. Conjugate-sector phenomenology

### 5.1 The scattering law **[D]**

In Born approximation with a heavy transfer mediator ($m_\phi \gtrsim m_\chi$, contact-like in $x$), the momentum-transfer cross-section from the boxed amplitude is

$$
\boxed{\ \sigma_T(v) \;=\; \sigma_0\, e^{-v^2/v_*^2}, \qquad v_* \equiv \sqrt{2}\,\mu/m_\chi, \qquad \sigma_0 = \frac{16\pi\tilde\alpha^2 m_\chi^2}{m_\phi^4}\ }
$$

Two structural signatures distinguish this from *every* $x$-local mediator model:

1. **An exponential cliff, not a power-law tail.** Yukawa SIDM falls as $v^{-4}$ with a Coulomb-log correction; BBT falls off a cliff at $v_*$. The discriminating band is $v \sim 150\text{–}400$ km/s (large spirals, small groups), where the two shapes diverge by orders of magnitude.
2. **Isotropy inside the gate.** Because the gate depends on $k$ and the contact part is flat in $q$, scattering below $v_*$ is isotropic; light-mediator Yukawa scattering is forward-peaked. Energy- vs momentum-transport efficiencies differ, which feeds into halo relaxation morphology.

**Benchmark.** Fitting dwarf-scale cores wants $\sigma_0/m_\chi \approx 30\ \mathrm{cm^2/g}$ at $v \lesssim v_* \approx 120$ km/s. With $m_\phi \sim m_\chi$ this fixes

$$
m_\chi \approx 70\ \mathrm{MeV} \times \tilde\alpha^{2/3}\left(\frac{\sigma_0/m}{30\ \mathrm{cm^2 g^{-1}}}\right)^{-1/3}, \qquad \mu = \frac{m_\chi v_*}{\sqrt 2} \approx 20\ \mathrm{keV}\ (\tilde\alpha \approx 1).
$$

At $\tilde\alpha \sim 1$ the Born approximation is marginal (expect $O(1)$ corrections); the *shape* claims above survive because they follow from the $k$- vs $q$-dependence, not from the coupling strength. Then the theory *predicts*, with zero remaining freedom:

$$
\sigma_T/m\,(300\ \mathrm{km/s}) \approx 6\times10^{-2}\ \mathrm{cm^2/g}, \qquad \sigma_T/m\,(1500\ \mathrm{km/s}) \approx 30\,e^{-156} \approx 0.
$$

Cluster self-interaction is *identically zero at any future sensitivity* — a sharper statement than any Yukawa model can make, and a standing kill condition.

### 5.2 Collective behavior: clumping in momentum space **[D at mean-field level; O beyond]**

The genuinely $p$-local part of the interaction shifts a particle's energy by the phase-space density of *co-moving* neighbors:

$$
\varepsilon(\mathbf p; f) = \frac{p^2}{2m_\chi} + \int d^3p'\; U(\mathbf p - \mathbf p')\, \bar f(\mathbf p'), \qquad \dot{\mathbf x} \supset \nabla_{\mathbf p}\,\varepsilon
$$

— an anomalous velocity, the same structure as Berry-curvature transport in solids. For attractive $U$ with momentum range $\mu$, the collisionless Boltzmann equation with this mean field is the Jeans problem *with the roles of $x$ and $v$ exchanged*: a smooth Maxwellian $f(v)$ is unstable to fragmentation into **discrete velocity streams of width $\Delta v \sim v_*$**, each spatially delocalized and internally coherent across kiloparsecs. The BCS analogy is exact in structure: a momentum-gated attraction is precisely the pairing interaction, and below a critical velocity dispersion the core of a halo condenses — momentum-space clumping, realized. Consequences: superfluid-like cores in dwarfs and **suppressed dynamical friction** inside them (the Fornax globular-cluster timing problem is a feature, not a bug). The growth rate $\gamma$ versus orbital phase-mixing remains to be computed; it is the theory's most important undone calculation on the astrophysics side. **[O]**

## 6. Cosmology I: the constraint and the corrected dictionary

**The constraint from P2. [D]** Rigidity of $\omega$ means the comoving phase-space cell has fixed volume: with $x$-space scale factor $a$ and $p$-space scale factor $\tilde a$, each mode occupies $(a\tilde a)^3 \times$ (comoving cell) $= \hbar^3$. Hence

$$
a\,\tilde a = \text{const} \equiv 1.
$$

This is not an extra assumption; it is the statement that $\hbar$ does not run.

**Minisuperspace. [D]** With one shared lapse $N$ and dual Planck mass $\tilde M$, substituting $\tilde a = 1/a$ into the dual Einstein–Hilbert kinetic term ($\tilde a \dot{\tilde a}^2 = \dot a^2/a^5$) gives

$$
3H^2\left(M_P^2 + \tilde M^2 a^{-6}\right) = \rho.
$$

The dual gravitational sector is **not** a new fluid and **not** a ghost — $\tilde a$ was never independent — but a stiff-like ($a^{-6}$) modification of the effective Planck mass, relevant only at early times. It predicts a step in $G_{\rm eff}$ before BBN and, conditionally on its one knob, a spectral step in the primordial gravitational-wave background. **[D]**

**The corrected dictionary. [D]** A fluid comoving in the dual frame with equation of state $\tilde w$ has $\tilde\rho \propto \tilde a^{-3(1+\tilde w)}$ and energy $E = \tilde\rho\,\tilde a^3 \propto \tilde a^{-3\tilde w} = a^{3\tilde w}$. Entering our Hamiltonian constraint as $\rho_{\rm eff} = E/a^3 \propto a^{-3(1-\tilde w)}$:

$$
\boxed{\ w_{\rm eff} = -\tilde w\ }
$$

This **supersedes** the heuristic $w_{\rm eff} = -2 - \tilde w$ used earlier in the development (which identified densities across frames without the constraint's volume Jacobian). Consequences of the corrected map:

| Dual-frame content | Appears on our books as |
|---|---|
| Kinetic/stiff energy ($\tilde w = +1$) | **Cosmological constant** ($w = -1$) |
| Cosmological constant ($\tilde w = -1$) | Stiff fluid ($w = +1$, dies as $a^{-6}$) |
| Spatial curvature ($\tilde w = -1/3$) | Dark radiation ($w = +1/3$; closed dual space $\Rightarrow$ *negative* $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$) |
| Dust ($\tilde w = 0$) | Dust ($w = 0$) |

Note what the corrected map does: dual *matter* no longer masquerades as exotic energy (dust maps to dust — the $w=-2$ pathology is gone because it was an artifact), and the natural source of a near-constant dark energy is **kinetic energy in the dual frame**. The obvious candidate for a free-rolling field is the one the symmetry breaking already handed us: $\tau$.

## 7. Cosmology II: dark energy, its drift, and the turnaround

Identify dark energy with the Maslov modulus $\tau$ sitting near the top of its cosine potential (hilltop axion quintessence). Then:

1. **$w \geq -1$ always.** $\tau$ is a canonical scalar; the null energy condition holds; phantom crossing is impossible. **[D]** Any robust measurement of $w < -1$ (as an intrinsic equation of state, not an artifact of an assumed $\Lambda$CDM background) falsifies this sector outright.
2. **Thawing shape.** Hilltop initial conditions give $w \approx -1$ in the past, rising now: $w_0 > -1$, $w_a < 0$, on the standard thawing corridor $w_a \approx -1.5\,(1+w_0)$. This is the *sign pattern DESI reports*. Hilltop/axion potentials of exactly this class have been fit directly to DESI DR1/DR2 by the collaboration and others (Ureña-López et al. 2025, arXiv:2503.20178; DESI 2025). **[T]**
3. **The turnaround.** A cosine potential is negative over half its range. Once $\tau$ rolls past $V = 0$, the effective vacuum energy is negative, expansion halts, and slow contraction begins. Quintessence fits to DESI 2025 data with a potential possessing a negative minimum find a **93.8% preference for a future transition to anti-de Sitter space with eventual cosmological collapse** (Gialamas, Hütsi, Raidal, Urrutia, Vasar, Veermäe 2025, arXiv:2506.21542). **[T]** In BBT the same event, read through $a\tilde a = 1$, is the conjugate frame's bounce: our maximum is their minimum. One event, two descriptions — now as a constraint identity rather than a metaphor. **[D]**
4. **Parameter economy.** $V_0$ is fixed by $\Omega_{\rm DE}$; the observables $(w_0, w_a, t_{\rm turn})$ are then functions of two numbers, $(f, \tau_i)$. Three measurements, two parameters: one over-constraint. **[D]**
