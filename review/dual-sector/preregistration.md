## Predictions (probability that each holds in the final note)

A. Statement and priority
- A1 (0.90) The identification, dark matter as the dual (x-tilde sector) partner of visible matter, is stated in
  substance in Minic 2020 (§5) and Berglund-Hubsch-Minic 2021; "its core is published" stands.
- A2 (0.75) No statement of the identification earlier than Minic 2020's arXiv posting (29 Feb 2020) is found,
  inside or outside the school.
- A3 (0.70) No statement of the identification independent of the Minic school is found.

B. "Their sector correlated with ours"
- B1 (0.75) The school's texts make the dark sector correlated or entangled with visible matter beyond gravity
  (through x/x-tilde noncommutativity, a correlation term, or Modified Dark Matter's acceleration scale) and
  nowhere state that the two couple by gravity alone.
- B2 (0.65) The school predicts no positive signal in standard direct-detection experiments, and Minic 2020
  offers the metaparticle nature as a reason for the null; the school and the program agree on non-detection
  in standard experiments and differ in that the school predicts other non-gravitational correlations.
- B3 (0.60) "Correlated with ours" survives but needs qualifying: its observable content is MDM-type galactic
  phenomenology and string- or Planck-scale effects, not a laboratory-strength coupling.

C. The two forms
- C1 (0.90) No velocity- or relative-momentum-gated self-interaction of the dark sector in the school.
- C2 (0.85) No magnetically charged objects localized in momentum or dual space as dark matter in the school;
  the nearest stay FLM 2014's worldsheet dyons and BHM 2021's E8 Langlands pairing.
- C3 (0.70) The deeper sweep outside the school finds neither form either.

D. What "dual" is; the vacuum; other overlaps
- D1 (0.80) In the dark-matter papers the dual sector lives on x-tilde, coordinates of a doubled phase space
  with string-scale noncommutativity, while the foundations call x-tilde momentum-like; no statement that the
  dark sector is ordinary matter in the momentum polarization at hbar.
- D2 (0.60) The school states no spontaneous-symmetry-breaking mechanism (potential, order parameter) for the
  choice of polarization; its "vacuum selection" is something else or words only. WEAKEST LINK.
- D3 (0.70) Overlap beyond C1 in theme with the program's dark energy (dark energy from the dual space's
  geometry), but no statement that a contracting dual universe drives our acceleration (C5).

E. Outcome
- E1 (0.85) No claim's status changes; flag 64 gets an addendum, not a new verdict; C1 stays "anticipated".
- E2 (0.50) Verdict on the summary line: holds, with qualifications; at least one clause needs rewording (most
