## §3, the question (last part, with the attribution)
Quantum kinematics is exactly invariant under ρ: (x,p) → (p,−x); the Fourier transform implements it unitarily [1†, 2†]. The asymmetry is dynamical: x-local interactions, the momentum-space mass shell, and general relativity's flat momentum fibers — the last an architectural assumption, not a measurement, and false in the one exactly solved quantum gravity (2+1d, where momenta live on a curved group manifold [5†]; curved momentum space has an independent lineage from Snyder [4†] through relative locality [6†]). The program: ρ is exact and spontaneously broken. The breaking is *mandatory*: [x,p] = iℏ forbids joint locality, so any world with local physics must select a Lagrangian polarization. The magnet must point. *Attribution (v2.2):* the idea that a preferred polarization breaks Born duality is Freidel, Leigh and Minic's — "if all probes agree on what we mean by space-time, then we have broken Born duality — there is a preferred choice of polarization, the space-time one" [26S, p. 3]. They treat the breaking as kinematical and call the choice "a dynamical question". This program's addition is to read it as *spontaneous* breaking, with η as the order parameter (§5; the toy models in `wiki/papers/reciprocity-toy-models.md`).

## §8, conjugate matter and the gate

We first postulated the gate; now we know its mechanism: two conjugate particles exchange the massive momentum-graviton, and can only do so where their momentum-space wavepackets overlap. The observational fingerprint is a knee: self-interaction strong in cold dwarf galaxies, off in hot clusters — and the knee's sharpness is itself a diagnostic.*

## §10, cosmology (technical record)
**Notation [T]:** a is the scale factor, the size of the universe relative to its size today, and ã its conjugate. w is a fluid's equation of state, its pressure divided by its energy density; a cosmological constant has w = −1, and below −1 an energy density grows as space expands. w̃ is the same ratio for a fluid in the dual frame, and w_eff the equation of state with which that fluid acts on our expansion. Dark energy's w is summarized as w(a) = w₀ + wₐ(1 − a): w₀ is its value today, and wₐ measures how fast it changes as the universe expands. z is redshift, the stretching of light by the expansion since the light was emitted, 1 + z = 1/a for light received today: larger z means earlier. **Constraint [D]:** ω-rigidity fixes the comoving phase-space cell: a·ã = 1 (ℏ does not run) — the |Z| = 1 self-dual locus. **Dictionary [D — corrected en route]:** with one shared lapse, a dual-frame fluid contributes w_eff = −w̃ (superseding an earlier −2 − w̃; the constraint's Jacobian changed the answer — rigor's job). Dual stiff ↦ Λ; dual Λ ↦ stiff; dual curvature ↦ negative dark radiation; dust ↦ dust; the dual Einstein term is an a⁻⁶ step in G_eff. **Founding supposition 9 [O] (P.R.; v2.4).** "Their collapse is our acceleration" failed in two versions: the heuristic dictionary's w = −2, which the data exclude, and dual dust, which the corrected dictionary maps to dust. The corrected dictionary keeps a route: dual stiff ↦ Λ, so a conjugate universe whose contents are kinetic-dominated as it contracts would act on ours as a cosmological constant, and the lab notebook already names "kinetic energy in the dual frame" as "the natural source of a near-constant dark energy" (#1 §6). Whether the contracting conjugate universe is kinetic-dominated, and how this route relates to τ's potential, are open. P.R. does not concur in recording the two versions dead (2026-10-29): they are failed versions of an open conjecture, not deaths. **Dark energy [D + T]:** τ is hilltop quintessence on the Maslov circle, V = V₀[1 + cos(τ/f)], periodicity by topology; strictly w ≥ −1; thawing corridor wₐ ≈ −1.5(1 + w₀); two parameters (f, τᵢ) for three observables (w₀, wₐ, t_turn) — one over-constraint. ## §11.4, the magnetic-mirror conjecture

**11.4 The magnetic-mirror conjecture [O] (P.R.; v2.3).** P.R.'s conjecture: momentum matter is similar to position matter, each electrically charged x-polarized particle having a magnetically charged p-polarized analog. It joins founding suppositions 5 and 11 (Appendix A), and it is what §11.3's conjecture would give if one vacuum event selected both the position polarization and the electric sublattice. #14 v2 stated a related identification, "the momentum-polarized matter sector is the S-dual image of the electric sector", with reciprocity acting on gauge couplings as S; it called that identification load-bearing for its dual coupling α_d, and a full mirror spectrum appeared there only as something the sector "may carry". On 2026-10-19 P.R. decided that S-duality no longer fixes α_d (`review/v3-worklog.md`, F2). On 2026-10-29 he withdrew that decision as "probably confusion on my part" (flag 60). F2's finding, that #14 v2 used both a weak and a strong value of the dual coupling, stands, and #14 stays withdrawn (§12). The conjecture has two forms:

- *Separate photon* (#14 v2's form). The analogs are magnetic under the p-polarized sector's own gauge field, whose coupling is the S-image of ours. Whether that is the sector's elementary coupling or its magnetic one is open; F2's finding is that the choice decides whether the sector's solitons are heavy. A massless photon of the p-sector would be dark radiation, which bounded #14's hidden sector to a hidden-to-visible temperature ratio ξ < 0.760 (round-3 ruling 5).
- *Shared photon* (the popular article #12's reading). The analogs carry magnetic charge of our photon: the magnetic half of electromagnetism lives in the p-polarization, and by Dirac's condition their existence would quantize electric charge [T, 17†]. P4 as written excludes this form ("gauge forces do not cross polarizations", in the program since #1), so it needs P4 restated. Localized in position at dark-matter density, such objects would exceed the Parker bound at #14's benchmark masses (#14 v2 and v3 §4; not gated). Something must therefore hide them, and neither option has been computed: p-localization, open since v2.0 ("p-local solitons are p-localized" [O]), with the spread in the objects' field profile rather than their quantum state, wider than detectors and than the coherence length of galactic magnetic fields, and decoupled from the photon–baryon plasma before recombination (†); or binding into magnetically neutral composites ("heavy and bound", §11.2). This form is the setting of App. A item 5's explanation of the null result, open since v2.4 (flag 62): it requires P4 restated and the hiding conditions above, none of them yet computed.
