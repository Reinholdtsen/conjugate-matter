## The corrected facts

1. **Date and form of the identification.** Djordje Minic first stated, as a proposal, that dark matter is the dual
   of the Standard Model in an invited talk at the 10th Mathematical Physics Meeting, Belgrade, on 9 September 2019
   (slides public); the first written statement is Minic 2020, posted on arXiv on 29 February 2020 (§5, §6.2: "The
   proposal here is that the dual sector (as already indicated in the previous section) should be interpreted as the
   dark matter sector"). Restated by him and coauthors to April 2026; the paper of 14 October 2026 follows the
   program's lab notebook (September 2026) and is a restatement. Every statement is a proposal; none is derived. The
   sentence of Minic 2020's abstract, "metaparticle quanta (the zero modes of the metastring) as the natural quanta of
   dark matter in this approach", is a narrower claim the group made in 2017 (Edmonds et al., arXiv September 2017),
   not the identification. Note §3.
2. **What their "dual" is.** Three senses: momentum rescaled in one phase space at ℏ (x̃ = p/ε, λε = ℏ) in their
   quantum mechanics, the program's sense; a coordinate of a doubled phase space at the string length, with a
   momentum p̃ of its own, where the dark sector is built; and a conjugacy of fields ("the dual fields do not commute
   with Standard Model fields"). "canonically conjugate dual space" names the doubled coordinate, the difference of
   the string's left- and right-movers, not the momentum side. The literature check's "their model doubles phase
   space where the program polarizes one" stands. Note §4.
3. **The coupling.** "Correlated" is the group's own word. At leading order in the string length the sectors are
   independent ("to first order in the fundamental length, we can treat them as independent"); beyond it, couplings
   beyond gravity (a Berry-phase-like term, mixing terms, a dual photon correlated with ours), of a strength no paper
   computes; Hübsch and Minic 2024 §5.3 state a decoupling energy "below which the dark and visible matter interact
   as required, only gravitationally", about 4.5×10¹⁴ GeV, a statement their 2026 papers do not repeat. So the
   identification joined to coupling by gravity alone at accessible energies is published once (2024), but never
   joined to a permanent null. Note §5.
4. **Detection.** The group explains past nulls ("If the quanta of modified dark matter are metaparticles, this may
   explain why, so far, dark matter detection experiments have failed to detect dark matter particles.") and expects
   detection; the program requires a permanent null (#11 §13 row 7). The literature check's "the school, like the
   program, expects no standard direct-detection signal" holds only for past nulls. Note §6.
5. **The forms.** Neither form was found, inside or outside the group. Note §7.
6. **The vacuum (flag 70).** Freidel, Leigh and Minic call general relativity's split kinematical and propose to make
   it dynamical ("the purely kinematical splitting of phase space into space and momentum space has to be made
   dynamical", 2014 §1.3), with the split carried by the solutions, not the formulation. Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman,
   Leigh and Minic 2021: "The symmetry breaking happens at the level of the vacuum sector, and thus a choice of ground
   state fixes a value of" the parameter that fixes which combination of x and x̃ is spacetime; "We now propose that
   the expansion of the Universe can act as a mechanism that selects the decoupling frame". The broken symmetry is a
   dual Lorentz symmetry that is not a canonical transformation, not ρ; Berglund, Hübsch and Minic 2023 call it
   "effectively spontaneously broken". U(d)/O(d) is named as the space of choices of η compatible with ω and the
   vacuum (Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2016); η = HJ is theirs from 2013 (both elements (η, J) "of the chiral structure
   are dynamical"). That the other polarization is equally available is stated as a symmetry of the theory (2018,
   2023: "we could have picked the dual" polarization). A 2021 thesis (Yargic) speculates that a broken modular gauge
   symmetry "may explain the absence of Born reciprocity between position and momentum variables in the classical
   world". None gives a potential, an order parameter or a Goldstone angle. Not found: the spontaneous breaking of the
   exact, ω-preserving ρ, with η as order parameter and a potential. Note §8.
7. **The compatibility theorem (flag 71).** Its positive form, H = diag(q, q⁻¹), is stated in group language in
   Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2016 §2 ("the group of transformations that preserves the symplectic structure and the
   polarization metric" η, GL(d) = Sp(2d) ∩ O(d, d), with frames "determining the vacuum metric"), without the block
   proof; the proof and battery test T1 stay the program's. Note §9.
8. **Other overlaps (flag 72).** Inverse scalings related to a·ã = 1 (Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2016 §1.4, "squeezes
   the modular cell along the momentum direction and stretches it in the space direction"; Freidel, Kowalski-Glikman,
   Leigh and Minic 2021; Hur et al. 2025, δx δx̃ ∼ λ²); a dual Einstein term with the opposite assignment to #11 §10's
   (Berglund, Hübsch and Minić 2019: "the curvature of the canonically conjugate dual space provides for a positive
   cosmological constant to leading order"; flag 49); Hur et al. 2025 publish both signs of 1 + w ("Clearly, w(a)<-1
   for all a as anticipated", disfavoured by DESI's second release; under an alternative relation, w(a) ≥ −1); Newton's
   constant "can be considered as a universal conversion factor between the seemingly different concepts of space and
   momentum space" (Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2014 §1.2), beside #13's maximum force; curved momentum space, "not only
   will space-time become curved, but momentum space as well" (Freidel, Leigh and Minic 2014, Phys. Lett. B). Note §9.
9. **Outside the group.** No statement of the identification itself. Ran 2025 (independent, December 2025) puts dark
   matter's origin in the coupling of a state's space–time and momentum–energy descriptions; others place dark matter
   in other dual spaces coupled by gravity (Hauser and Dröscher 2019; Nishimura 2022). Note §10.
10. **No claim's status changes.** C1 stays alive, P.R.'s core conjecture; the literature check's verdict for it,
   anticipated, stands under the reading it states; which reading is P.R.'s conjecture stays open.

## Added after round 2 (flag 73)

11. **Winn, outside the group** (note §10; flag 73). David R. Winn (Fairfield University) has proposed since 2012 that
   every charged Standard Model particle has a magnetically charged partner under our own photon (Winn 2012,
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1206.4590: "there are no electrically charged superpartners, only neutral or magnetic charged
   partners"; "The resulting strong magnetic force between any pair-produced monopoles explains the absence of detected
   monopoles heretofore"), with the partners' magnetically neutral bound states as dark matter; restated in talks of
   2025–2026 (Lepton-Photon 2025, https://indico.cern.ch/event/1493037/contributions/6549576). Elementary, not
   solitons; nothing in momentum or phase space; a heuristic without a Lagrangian. Relations: a close variant of C4's
   shared-photon form (one analog per charged particle, our own photon, hiding by binding into magnetically neutral
   composites, §11.4's second route), without the momentum polarization; related to C2 (abundant monopoles, ordinary
   space), C3 (the null explained by binding, not conjugate space), C6 (Bose condensates of sneutrinos) and C1 (a dual
   of every particle as dark matter under an electric–magnetic duality, not phase space). It narrows the literature
   check's C4 "Not found" (`notes/literature-check.md` §2 table and §5): one analog per charged particle is published;
   the placement in momentum is not. C4's verdict, partly anticipated, stands; C2's and C3's, related only, stand.
12. **The conference sweep** (note §10): the identification only in the group's own talks; neither form.
