# Pre-registration: a closed Comatter universe (2026-10-27)

Written before any agent was dispatched. Kept out of every agent folder; never named in a prompt.

Question (Paul): "Presumably a comatter universe is closed. Work out the cosmological consequences wrt
generalized general relativity. How small does momentum space get? How big does position space get?
Anything else of interest."

Working reading: "generalized general relativity" = the program's gravity on the generalized metric H
(H = diag(g, g^-1) in the vacuum, a·ã = 1, massive X in the palindromic Hassan–Rosen branch at u = 1,
τ with V = V0[1 + cos(τ/f)], the dual-frame minisuperspace). "Closed" = k = +1.

## Predictions (confidence in brackets)

P1. With V0[1+cos] (minimum 0) and the wedge identity (no bimetric Λ), the late vacuum energy is 0, so
    a turnaround happens iff k = +1. Flat/open: a → ∞, ã → 0, no conjugate bounce. Row 5 therefore
    becomes a present-day statement about the sign of Ω_k. [0.95]
P2. Data: DESI DR2 + CMB in ΛCDM+Ω_K gives Ω_K ≈ +0.002 ± 0.001 (open side), so closed is disfavoured
    at about 2σ there; in w0wa+Ω_K the constraint loosens and closed sits within about 1σ. [0.6]
P3. a_max ≈ (Ω_m + Ω_τ,dust)/|Ω_k| up to O(1). For |Ω_k| = 1e-3 and thawing w0 in [-0.9, -0.75]:
    a_max in [1e3, 3e4]; t_turn in [1e5, 1e7] Gyr; R_max ≥ 1e5 Gpc (≥ 3e30 m, ≥ 1e65 l_P).
    Data give only lower bounds on a_max and R_max; no upper bound. [0.7, orders of magnitude]
P4. Closure sets no edge to momentum space. Content and grain shrink as 1/a: ã_min/ã0 = 1/a_max ≤ 1e-3;
    lowest momentum quantum ~ħ√3/R_max ≤ 1e-65 kg m/s; CMB temperature at turnaround ≤ 3 mK. In the
    slaved-block reading the conjugate description is an unbounded discrete lattice of spacing ~λ²/R,
    not a closed space. [0.6]
P5. Closure supplies a natural normalization of a·ã = 1 (a = R/λ). Self-dual epoch R = λ is never
    reached after inflation begins for λ = l_P (ã ≤ l_P H_inf/c ~ 4e-6). The a^-6 step and the dual
    curvature term are negligible (≤ 1e-30 relative); row 10's knob is removed in that normalization.
    [0.55 — weakest link]
P6. Bimetric: k drops out of the algebraic y-equation; u = 1 persists in closed slicing; y - 1 ~ (H/M_X)²
    ~ 1e-61 today; Higuchi satisfied today, violated during inflation (pre-existing issue). Possible
    snag at the turnaround, where the branch condition involves Ẏ/ȧ with ȧ = 0. [0.8; snag 0.3]
P7. Global Fourier reciprocity is not a symmetry of quantum kinematics on compact S³ (compact ⇒
    discrete spectrum). Closure breaks global ρ explicitly at scale ħ/R; local (tangent-space) ρ
    survives. A finite exactly self-dual phase space needs λ ~ √(l_P R) ~ 0.3–1 mm, excluded by the
    diameter note's two tests. [0.75]
P8. Finite volume: the selection of a polarization (P5) is strictly approximate; tunnelling and
    rotor-dephasing times exceed the universe's lifetime by absurd margins; nothing observable. [0.8]
P9. Pairing radius R with total mass budget Mc in Planck units gives aspect ratio
    (3π/4)(1 + H²R²/c²), minimal (3π/4) at the turnaround: "square at the bounce" up to an O(1)
    convention factor. It is the closed Friedmann equation at H = 0 and does not match the chat's
    10^90 / 10^92 numbers; the diameter note's obituary stands as worded. [0.8]
P10. In the closed version t_turn depends on Ω_k as well as (f, τ_i): no over-constraint unless Ω_k is
    measured independently. [0.85]
P11. π₃(Λ(4)) = Z₂ (stable range), so a closed universe has Z₂ polarization-texture sectors above the
    heavy-moduli scale; at low energy only the Maslov circle is light and π₃(S¹) = 0. [0.5]

## Weakest links

1. P5: the normalization argument and the reading of the dual frame's curvature k̃.
2. P2: the exact curvature numbers.
3. P3: τ's late-time oscillation treated as dust when m_τ ~ H.

## Expected overall answer

Closure is natural for the program, and in fact needed to keep row 5 with the potential as written.
It costs a ~2σ tension with DESI DR2's curvature, the over-constraint, and global (not local)
reciprocity. It makes row 5 testable now through the sign of Ω_k. The sizes come out only as lower
bounds: position space at least ~1e3 × today's curvature radius at turnaround, momentum space at least
~1e3 × finer than today.
