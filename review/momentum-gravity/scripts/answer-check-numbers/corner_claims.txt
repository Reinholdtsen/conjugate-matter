===== Q1 | [P] model; [D] equations
Reading and model used. The corner model U = -G~ m^2 W(|w-w'|/v*)/r (W = exp(-s^2/2), eps = G~/G, added to gravity, chi only) was integrated as a Hamiltonian H = sum p^2/2m + sum U(x_ij, p_ij), with the gate on the canonical momentum per mass w = p/m: xdot_i = w_i + (eps G m/v*^2) sum_j W_ij (w_i - w_j)/r_ij (anomalous velocity), wdot_i = -G m sum_j (1 + eps W_ij)(x_i - x_j)/r_ij^3. Why: #11 sec. 8 puts the gate on the incoming relative momentum k, a canonical momentum; the Hamiltonian form conserves energy, momentum, angular momentum and phase-space volume, so round 2's mean-field (Vlasov) picture applies; the force-law reading (xdot = w, force at fixed velocity) follows from no Lagrangian (critic D1) and does not conserve phase-space volume, so it was run only as a comparison (eps = 3). The model itself is a hypothetical outside the record (round 2 C16: X massive, lambda_X < 37.7 um).

===== Q6 | [D] with executed scripts; densities [S]
Relaxation and the window. The rms change of each particle's mean-field energy grows as N^-1/2: the ratio N=1000/N=2000 is 1.425 at eps = 0 and 1.369 at eps = 10, against sqrt2 = 1.414. So that evolution is two-body relaxation, not a collective mode. The gate speeds relaxation up: the squared ratio to the control is 6.0 at eps = 10 (H) and 16 for the F reading at eps = 3 (partly from its contraction). The 43-free-fall-time window corresponds to 0.37-0.64 Gyr in a classical dSph core and 0.12 Gyr in Segue 1. A dwarf's 10 Gyr spans 668-1155 such times (3683 in Segue 1). The run therefore excludes velocity-selected instabilities with e-folding times shorter than roughly the window, at the sensitivity of Q4. Slower collisionless instabilities, and anything below the relaxation floor, are not excluded.
