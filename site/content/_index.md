The Comatter program is built on Born reciprocity, the exchange of position and momentum (with one change of sign), which leaves the basic rules of quantum mechanics unchanged. It takes the exchange to be an exact symmetry of the laws that the vacuum, the universe's lowest-energy state, breaks spontaneously, and follows the consequences into gravity, dark matter, dark energy and electric–magnetic duality.

It is a speculative research program, not a theory. Until 2026-10-26 it was called the Broken-Born program, the name its earlier documents use.

**[The executive summary](/wiki/summary)** gives where the program stands on one page: its status, whether anything in it is new, which of its core hypotheses still stand, what the data say, what it predicts and what would falsify it.

[The wiki](/wiki) explains each topic and states where each claim stands (alive, dead, open, superseded or conjectural), with its sources and what verifies it.
